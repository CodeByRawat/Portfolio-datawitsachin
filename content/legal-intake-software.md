Prospective clients often contact several firms and instruct the first one that responds well. Your **legal intake** process decides whether an enquiry becomes a client or a missed opportunity.

## What good intake looks like

1. **Capture** from every channel into one place: web form, chat, phone, email, referrals.
2. **Acknowledge instantly,** with an automated response that sets expectations.
3. **Qualify** with a few smart questions: matter type, urgency, jurisdiction, value.
4. **Check conflicts** before anyone invests time.
5. **Book** a consultation with the right lawyer straight from the confirmation.
6. **Hand off** to the matter system with data already filled in.

## What to automate

- Instant replies and out-of-hours responses
- Qualification and routing by matter type
- Appointment scheduling and reminders
- Engagement letters and e-signature
- Follow-up when a prospect goes quiet

## Intake software, solutions and companies

You'll see three approaches: **software** you configure yourself, **intake companies** (outsourced call and chat teams) and **custom builds**. When comparing, ask about response times, how leads reach your CRM, how conflicts are checked, and what happens to prospect data.

## Compliance points

Prospect information can be confidential even before engagement. Be careful with what chat and AI assistants say: they shouldn't give legal advice, and your intake forms should include appropriate disclaimers. Follow your regulator's advertising and solicitation rules.
