<p class="lede">A short-term rental is a property rented for <strong>short stays (usually under 30 days)</strong> on platforms or by direct booking. To get into short-term rentals: pick a <strong>market with demand and clear regulations</strong>, analyse deals by <strong>nightly rate, occupancy and expenses</strong>, arrange <strong>financing</strong>, furnish for guests, and decide whether to <strong>self-manage or hire a manager</strong>.</p>

## Short-term vs long-term rentals

| | Short-term | Long-term |
|---|---|---|
| Stay length | Nights to weeks | Months to years |
| Income potential | Higher, more variable | Lower, steadier |
| Work | More (cleaning, guests) | Less |
| Regulation | Often strict | Standard landlord law |
| Furnishing | Required | Usually not |

## Steps to get started

1. **Learn the rules** in target cities
2. **Choose a market** (tourism, business travel, hospitals, events)
3. **Analyse deals**: revenue estimates, expenses, financing
4. **Finance** the purchase (conventional, second home, DSCR loans)
5. **Furnish and photograph**
6. **List and price** with dynamic pricing
7. **Manage** yourself or hire a manager

## Risks

Regulation changes, seasonality, oversupply, high-maintenance guests and rising costs. Keep reserves.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I get into short-term rentals?
Choose a market, check regulations, analyse deals, finance and furnish a property, then list and manage it.

### What does short-term rental mean?
A rental for short stays, typically under 30 days.

### How long is a short-term rental?
Usually from one night up to about a month.

### Are short-term rentals a good investment?
They can be, in strong markets with favourable rules, but carry more risk and work.

### Do I need to self-manage?
No; managers handle operations for a fee.

### Can I start short-term rentals without buying?
Yes, through co-hosting or rental arbitrage.
