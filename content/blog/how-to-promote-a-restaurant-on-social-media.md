<p class="lede">To promote a restaurant on social media, focus on <strong>short vertical videos</strong> (Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts) that make food look irresistible and show the <strong>people and story</strong> behind it, post <strong>consistently</strong> with <strong>location tags</strong>, encourage <strong>user-generated content</strong> and reviews, collaborate with <strong>local food creators</strong>, run small <strong>local ads</strong> for events and offers, and always make it easy to <strong>book or order</strong> from your profile.</p>

## Choose platforms

| Platform | Use for |
|---|---|
| **Instagram** | Reels, Stories, highlights (menu, booking), collaborations |
| **TikTok** | Discovery through entertaining food content |
| **Facebook** | Local community, events, ads to broader age groups |
| **Google Business Profile** | Photos, posts, reviews (critical for local search) |
| **YouTube Shorts** | Reuse vertical videos |

## Content ideas that work

- **Dish close-ups:** sizzle, cheese pulls, pouring, plating
- **Chef and staff stories**
- **Behind the scenes:** prep, sourcing, opening routine
- **Menu launches and specials**
- **Customer reactions** and UGC reposts (with permission)
- **"How it's made"** process videos
- **Events and live music**
- **Local partnerships and neighbourhood features**
- **Polls and questions** in Stories
- **Trend-based content** adapted to your brand (see [How to find trending topics](/blog/how-to-find-trending-topics/))

## Filming tips

- Natural light near windows; avoid harsh overhead light
- Vertical 9:16, steady phone or gimbal
- Hook in the first second (the best shot first)
- Short clips, quick cuts to music
- Captions and on-screen text (dish name, price, offer)
- Sound: sizzles and kitchen ASMR perform well
(See [How to make a promotional video](/blog/how-to-make-a-promotional-video/).)

## Posting schedule

3–5 Reels/TikToks per week, daily Stories, weekly Google posts. Consistency beats perfection.

## Local reach tactics

- **Location tags** and local hashtags
- **Collab posts** with neighbouring businesses
- **Respond to comments and DMs** quickly
- **Feature regulars** (with consent)

## Influencer partnerships

Invite local food creators for a tasting, agree deliverables, give them freedom to create authentic content, and track bookings with a unique code.

## Paid social ads

Promote events, new menus and offers to people within a few kilometres; use video creatives and a clear CTA to book or order (see [How to advertise your business on Facebook](/blog/how-to-advertise-your-business-on-facebook/)).

## Turn followers into customers

- Booking and ordering links in bio and highlights
- WhatsApp click-to-chat for reservations
- Exclusive follower offers
- Collect contacts for loyalty and follow-ups

(Broader sales tactics: [How to increase restaurant sales](/blog/how-to-increase-restaurant-sales/).)

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I promote my restaurant on social media?
Post short, appetising videos regularly, show your team and story, use location tags, repost customer content, collaborate with local creators and make booking or ordering easy from your profile.

### What should restaurants post on social media?
Dish videos, specials, behind-the-scenes prep, chef stories, events, customer reactions and local partnerships.

### Which social media is best for restaurants?
Instagram and TikTok for discovery, Facebook for community and events, and Google Business Profile for local search.

### How often should a restaurant post?
Aim for several short videos per week plus daily Stories, keeping a consistent schedule.

### Do restaurants need influencers?
Not necessarily, but local food creators can drive awareness and visits when the partnership is authentic and tracked.

### How do I measure social media results for my restaurant?
Track bookings, online orders, offer redemptions and "how did you hear about us" responses, not just likes.
