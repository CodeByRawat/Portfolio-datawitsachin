<p class="lede">Lawyers get clients mainly through <strong>referrals</strong> (past clients, other lawyers, accountants, financial advisers), <strong>search</strong> (a strong website, Google Business Profile, reviews and practice-area content), <strong>reputation</strong> (speaking, writing, community involvement) and <strong>paid advertising</strong> for high-intent searches, combined with <strong>fast, professional intake</strong>. Many firms lose potential clients simply by responding slowly, so speed and follow-up matter as much as marketing.</p>

## Referral-based strategies

1. **Ask satisfied clients** for referrals and reviews (where permitted)
2. **Referral relationships** with lawyers in other practice areas
3. **Professional partners:** accountants, financial planners, real estate agents, HR consultants
4. **Bar association referral services**
5. **Stay in touch** with past clients (newsletters, check-ins)

## Online strategies

6. **Website** with dedicated practice-area pages, attorney bios, FAQs and clear calls to action
7. **Google Business Profile** and reviews (see [How to create a Google Business page](/blog/how-to-create-a-google-business-page/))
8. **Local SEO and content** answering common legal questions (see [What is SEO?](/blog/what-is-seo/))
9. **Legal directories** (Avvo, Justia, FindLaw, Martindale-Hubbell and local directories)
10. **Video:** short explainers that build trust
11. **Google Ads** for urgent, high-intent searches (see [Are Google Ads worth it?](/blog/are-google-ads-worth-it/))

## Reputation and community

12. **Speaking** at events, chambers and webinars
13. **Writing** for local publications and industry newsletters
14. **Community involvement** and pro bono work

## Intake and conversion

15. **Fast response:** answer calls, chat and forms within minutes; use after-hours answering or AI intake (see [Legal intake software](/blog/legal-intake-software/))

Also: clear consultation booking, fee transparency, and automated follow-ups (see [Legal CRM for law firms](/blog/legal-crm-for-law-firms/)).

## Ethics and advertising rules

Check your jurisdiction's rules on solicitation, testimonials, "specialist" claims, referral fees and required disclaimers before advertising.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do lawyers get clients?
Through referrals, a strong website and Google presence, reviews, practice-area content, directories, networking, speaking and paid search, backed by fast intake.

### How can a new lawyer get clients?
Build referral relationships, join bar referral services, network locally, publish helpful content and set up a professional website and Google Business Profile.

### What is the best way to attract clients to a law firm?
Combine referrals and reputation with search visibility and fast, professional intake.

### Do legal directories help lawyers get clients?
They can, especially for consumer practice areas, though results vary by directory and market.

### Should lawyers use Google Ads?
For many practice areas with urgent needs, yes, if campaigns are tightly targeted and intake is responsive; costs can be high in competitive areas.

### How quickly should a law firm respond to new inquiries?
As quickly as possible, ideally within minutes; many prospective clients contact several firms.
