<p class="lede">Restaurant sales grow in three ways: <strong>more customers</strong>, <strong>higher average spend per customer</strong>, and <strong>more frequent visits</strong>. The highest-impact moves are usually a strong <strong>Google Business Profile with many recent reviews</strong>, <strong>commission-free online ordering</strong> on your own website, <strong>menu engineering</strong> to promote profitable dishes, <strong>staff upselling</strong>, a <strong>loyalty programme</strong> with WhatsApp/email follow-ups, and <strong>events, catering and private dining</strong>.</p>

## Get more new customers

1. **Optimise Google Business Profile:** photos, menu, hours, booking/ordering links (see [How to create a Google Business page](/blog/how-to-create-a-google-business-page/))
2. **Get more reviews** and reply to all
3. **Short-form video** of dishes and kitchen moments (see [How to promote a restaurant on social media](/blog/how-to-promote-a-restaurant-on-social-media/))
4. **Local SEO:** website pages for cuisine + area ("best biryani in Koramangala")
5. **Partner with local businesses** (offices, gyms, hotels)
6. **Delivery apps** for discovery, while steering repeat customers to direct ordering
7. **Local influencer and food blogger visits**
8. **Community events and sponsorships**

## Increase average check

9. **Menu engineering:** highlight high-margin "stars", reposition low-profit items
10. **Upsell and suggestive selling:** starters, drinks, desserts, add-ons
11. **Bundles and combos**
12. **Premium options and specials**
13. **Price review:** small, strategic increases tied to value

## Increase visit frequency

14. **Loyalty programme** (points, stamps, member perks)
15. **Collect customer contacts** (with consent) and send offers via WhatsApp/email (see [How to schedule a WhatsApp message](/blog/how-to-schedule-a-whatsapp-message/))
16. **Weekday promotions** for slow periods (happy hours, set lunches, kids eat free)
17. **Themed nights and events**

## New revenue streams

18. **Own online ordering and delivery/pickup** (avoid high third-party commissions for repeat orders)
19. **Catering and corporate orders**
20. **Private dining, merchandise, meal kits, gift cards**

## How to increase delivery sales

- Professional photos on delivery apps
- Optimised menu for travel (packaging, items that hold up)
- Delivery-only bundles and value meals
- Fast prep times and accurate orders for better ratings
- Promote **direct ordering** with a discount on your website

## How to increase sales without advertising

- Train staff in hospitality and suggestive selling
- Improve speed of service and table turnover
- Ask every happy guest for a review
- Encourage referrals and group bookings
- Use your existing customer list
- Improve the menu and atmosphere

## Measure what matters

Covers per day, average check, table turnover, food cost %, labour cost %, repeat visit rate, online order share and review rating. (Profit basics: [How to run a successful restaurant](/blog/how-to-run-a-successful-restaurant/).)

## Frequently asked questions

### How can I increase restaurant sales?
Attract more customers with Google, reviews and social media; raise average spend with menu engineering and upselling; increase visit frequency with loyalty and follow-ups; and add revenue from online ordering, catering and events.

### How do I increase sales in a slow restaurant?
Run weekday promotions, events, set menus and partnerships, ask customers for reviews and use your customer list to bring people back.

### How can I boost restaurant sales without advertising?
Improve service and upselling, encourage reviews and referrals, use loyalty programmes and contact past customers.

### How do I get new customers to my restaurant?
Optimise Google Business Profile, post food videos, partner with local businesses and influencers, and make online ordering and booking easy.

### How do I increase restaurant delivery sales?
Use great photos, delivery-friendly menus and bundles, fast accurate service, and promote your own direct ordering channel.

### What is a good average check increase strategy?
Menu engineering, combos, add-ons and staff training on suggestive selling.
