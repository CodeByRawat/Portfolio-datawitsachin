<p class="lede">To build a coffee cart or truck, <strong>start with your local health department's rules</strong>, then design a <strong>compact workflow</strong> (order → espresso → milk → handoff), install <strong>hand-wash and ware-wash sinks with fresh and grey water tanks</strong>, plan <strong>enough power</strong> for the espresso machine, use <strong>food-safe surfaces</strong>, and get the build <strong>inspected before opening</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Check the rules first

Ask your health department for mobile unit plan requirements. Common requirements:

- Hand-wash sink with hot water
- Ware-wash (often 3-compartment) sink, unless using a commissary
- Fresh and grey water tank sizes
- Smooth, cleanable surfaces
- Refrigeration for milk
- Plan review and inspection

## Step 2: Layout and workflow

Arrange equipment so one or two baristas can work fast: register → grinder → espresso → milk → handoff. Keep the customer side clear and attractive.

## Step 3: Water system

Fresh water tank, pump, water filter for the espresso machine, water heater for hand-wash sink, and grey water tank (usually larger than fresh).

## Step 4: Power

Commercial espresso machines draw a lot of power. Options:

| Power source | Notes |
|---|---|
| Venue power | Cheapest; check amperage |
| Quiet inverter generator | Common for outdoor events |
| Battery and inverter | Quiet, expensive for high loads |
| Propane espresso machine | Reduces electrical needs |

## Step 5: Materials

Stainless steel counters, sealed wood or laminate exteriors, non-slip flooring for trucks, and secure equipment mounts for travel.

## Step 6: Costs

| Build | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| DIY coffee cart | $5,000 – $15,000 + equipment |
| Professional cart build | $10,000 – $30,000 + equipment |
| Coffee truck build-out | $30,000 – $100,000+ |

## DIY vs buying pre-built

DIY saves money but risks failing inspection. Pre-built carts often come designed to meet health codes.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I build a coffee cart?
Follow health code requirements, plan workflow, install sinks, water and power, and get inspected.

### How much does it cost to build a coffee cart?
Often $5,000 to $30,000 plus equipment.

### What power does a coffee cart need?
Enough for a commercial espresso machine; often venue power or a generator.

### Do coffee carts need sinks?
Usually a hand-wash sink, and often a ware-wash sink.

### Can I build a coffee truck myself?
Yes, but many hire professionals to pass inspection.
