<p class="lede">To start a barbershop: get your <strong>barber licence</strong> (if you'll cut) and the <strong>shop/establishment licence</strong>, write a <strong>business plan</strong>, choose a <strong>location</strong> with good foot traffic and parking, build out stations with <strong>plumbing and electrical</strong>, buy <strong>chairs and equipment</strong>, decide on a <strong>booth rent or commission model</strong>, hire <strong>skilled barbers</strong>, set <strong>prices</strong>, set up <strong>online booking</strong>, and promote the shop through <strong>Instagram, Google and the local community</strong>.</p>

## Steps

1. **Licences:** barber licence, shop licence, business registration, inspections
2. **Business plan:** concept (classic, premium, kids, walk-in), target clients, costs and pricing
3. **Location:** visibility, parking, rent, plumbing access
4. **Build-out and equipment:** barber chairs, stations, mirrors, sinks, sterilisation, waiting area, POS
5. **Model:** booth rent (stable rent income) vs commission (more control, higher upside)
6. **Barbers:** hire for skill and clientele; culture matters
7. **Pricing:** cuts, fades, beard trims, packages, memberships
8. **Online booking** with reminders and deposits (see [How to make a booking website](/blog/how-to-make-a-booking-website/))
9. **Branding:** name, logo, interior design, Instagram aesthetic
10. **Promotion:** Google reviews, Instagram Reels, local sponsorships, grand opening (see [How to grow your salon business](/blog/how-to-grow-your-salon-business/))

## Startup costs

Rent deposits, build-out, chairs and equipment, licences, insurance, branding and working capital; costs vary widely by location and size (see [How much does it cost to open a salon?](/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-open-a-salon/) for similar ranges).

## How much do barbershop owners make?

It depends on chairs, pricing, model and whether the owner cuts; owners can earn from their own chair plus booth rents or commission profit (see [How much do barbers make?](/blog/how-much-do-barbers-make/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a barbershop?
Get licensed, plan the business, choose a location, build out and equip the shop, choose booth rent or commission, hire barbers, set prices, add online booking and promote locally.

### Do I need a barber licence to own a barbershop?
Owners may not need one if they don't cut, but the shop needs proper licensing and licensed barbers; check local rules.

### How much does it cost to open a barbershop?
It varies with rent, build-out and equipment; small shops cost far less than premium build-outs.

### How much does a barbershop owner make?
It depends on chairs filled, pricing, model and whether the owner also cuts hair.

### Is booth rent or commission better for a barbershop?
Booth rent gives predictable income with less control; commission offers more control and upside with more management.

### How do you promote a barber shop?
Instagram cut videos, Google reviews, online booking, local partnerships and community events.
