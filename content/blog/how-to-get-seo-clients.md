<p class="lede">To get SEO clients: pick a <strong>niche</strong>, build <strong>proof</strong> (case studies with traffic and lead growth), package <strong>clear SEO services</strong>, make your <strong>own website rank</strong> for niche keywords, use <strong>free mini-audits</strong> as a lead magnet, do <strong>personalised outreach</strong>, partner with <strong>web designers, developers and agencies</strong>, ask for <strong>referrals</strong>, and send <strong>proposals focused on business outcomes</strong>, then retain clients with transparent reporting.</p>

## Steps to win SEO clients

1. **Niche:** local services, SaaS, e-commerce, healthcare, legal
2. **Proof:** case studies (rankings, traffic, leads, revenue)
3. **Productised services:** audits, local SEO packages, content + links, technical SEO
4. **Your website:** service pages, niche pages, blog that ranks (see [What is SEO?](/blog/what-is-seo/))
5. **Lead magnet:** 5-minute video audits or mini-reports
6. **Outreach:** personalised, problem-focused (see [What is sales prospecting?](/blog/what-is-sales-prospecting/))
7. **Partnerships:** web designers, developers, PPC agencies, accountants
8. **Referrals** from happy clients
9. **Proposals:** goals, strategy, deliverables, timeline, pricing, expected outcomes
10. **Retention:** monthly reporting tied to leads and revenue (see [How to make an SEO report for a client](/blog/how-to-make-an-seo-report-for-a-client/))

## Starting an SEO consulting business or company

Business setup and contracts, pricing models (monthly retainers, projects, audits), tools (Search Console, GA4, Semrush/Ahrefs, rank trackers), delivery SOPs, and hiring writers and link builders as you scale (see [How to start a digital marketing agency](/blog/how-to-start-a-digital-marketing-agency/)).

## SEO pricing

Monthly retainers, one-time audits and project fees, depending on scope, competition and niche.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I get SEO clients?
Choose a niche, build case studies, package services, rank your own site, offer mini-audits, do personalised outreach, partner with web designers and ask for referrals.

### How do I start an SEO consulting business?
Develop SEO skills and proof, set up the business and contracts, define packages and pricing, build tools and SOPs, and market through content, outreach and partnerships.

### How much should I charge for SEO?
It depends on scope, competition and niche; most providers use monthly retainers plus one-time audits.

### Do cold emails work for getting SEO clients?
Personalised, value-led outreach can work; generic mass emails rarely do and may breach anti-spam rules.

### How do I retain SEO clients?
Set realistic expectations, report on leads and revenue, communicate proactively and show progress each month.

### What is the best niche for SEO services?
Niches with high customer value and strong search demand, such as legal, healthcare and home services.
