<p class="lede">To start a bounce house business, buy <strong>commercial-grade inflatables</strong> (bounce houses, combos, water slides), get <strong>liability insurance</strong> and any <strong>state inspections or permits</strong>, learn <strong>safe setup and anchoring</strong>, arrange <strong>storage and a trailer</strong>, set <strong>rental prices with delivery</strong>, take <strong>online bookings</strong>, and market to <strong>parents, schools, churches and event planners</strong>. Many start for <strong>$5,000 to $20,000</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Choose your inflatables

| Type | Notes |
|---|---|
| Bounce houses | Classic, most affordable |
| Combo units (bounce + slide) | Most popular rental |
| Water slides | Big summer demand |
| Obstacle courses | Schools and large events |
| Toddler units | Young kids' parties |
| Interactive games | Corporate and school events |

Buy **commercial-grade** units, not residential ones, for durability and insurance.

## Step 2: Startup costs

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Bounce house (commercial) | $1,500 – $3,000 |
| Combo or water slide | $2,500 – $6,000 |
| Blowers, stakes, sandbags, tarps | $300 – $1,000 |
| Trailer and dolly | $1,500 – $5,000 |
| Insurance | $1,500 – $5,000 per year |
| Website and booking software | $300 – $2,000 |
| Storage | Garage or $100+ per month |

## Step 3: Insurance and permits

Liability insurance is essential. Some states require inflatable inspections, operator training or permits. Check your state and city. More: [Do you need insurance for a bounce house?](/blog/do-you-need-insurance-for-a-bounce-house/).

## Step 4: Safety

Follow manufacturer setup guides, anchor properly, never operate in high winds, supervise capacity rules, and give customers written safety rules.

## Step 5: Pricing

Charge per unit per day (or 8-hour rental), plus delivery by distance, setup on difficult surfaces, and generator rental. Offer weekend and package deals.

## Step 6: Get customers

1. A website with photos, prices and online booking
2. Google Business Profile and reviews
3. Schools, churches, daycares and HOAs
4. Facebook groups for parents
5. Event planners and party rental partners

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a bounce house rental business?
Buy commercial inflatables, get insured, learn safe setup, and market with online booking.

### How much does it cost to start a bounce house business?
Often $5,000 to $20,000.

### Do bounce house businesses need permits?
Some states require inspections or permits; check locally.

### What inflatables rent the most?
Combo units and water slides.

### Can I run a bounce house business from home?
Yes, with storage space and a trailer.
