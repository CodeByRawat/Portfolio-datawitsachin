<p class="lede">To become a mobile mechanic, <strong>learn auto repair</strong> through a trade school or on-the-job training, <strong>work in a shop for a few years</strong>, earn <strong>ASE certifications</strong>, then either join a mobile repair company or <strong>start your own mobile mechanic business</strong> with a tool-equipped van.</p>

## Step 1: Get training

- Auto technology programs at community colleges or trade schools (about 6 months to 2 years)
- Or start as a lube tech or apprentice in a shop and learn on the job

## Step 2: Gain shop experience

Two or more years in a shop teaches diagnostics, speed and the jobs that come up every day. You'll also learn which jobs can be done outside a shop.

## Step 3: Get certified

ASE certifications (brakes, electrical, engine performance and more) need work experience plus tests. Get EPA 609 if you'll work on A/C.

## Step 4: Build your tool kit

Hand tools, power tools, a good diagnostic scanner, jacks and stands, lighting and a reliable van or truck.

## Step 5: Choose how to work

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile repair company | Steady jobs, no marketing | Lower pay share |
| Fleet mechanic | Regular hours | Less variety |
| Own business | Highest earning potential | Marketing, insurance, admin |

## What mobile mechanics make

Many mobile mechanics earn similar to shop technicians, around **$45,000 to $70,000**, and successful owners can earn more because they keep the labour rate and parts markup. Pay varies by area and skill.

## Licenses

You usually don't need a personal mechanic license, but your business may need registration. See [Do you need a license to be a mobile mechanic?](/blog/do-you-need-a-license-to-be-a-mobile-mechanic/)

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I become a mobile mechanic?
Get training, work in a shop, earn ASE certifications, then join a mobile company or start your own.

### Do you need certification to be a mobile mechanic?
Not legally in most states, but ASE certification builds trust.

### How long does it take to become a mobile mechanic?
Usually two to five years including training and shop experience.

### How much do mobile mechanics make?
Often $45,000 to $70,000, and owners can earn more.

### Can I be a mobile mechanic without experience?
It's risky. Get shop experience first so you can diagnose and repair reliably.
