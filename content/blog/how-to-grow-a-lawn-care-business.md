<p class="lede">To grow a lawn care business: <strong>tighten route density</strong>, <strong>raise prices</strong> and drop unprofitable clients, sell <strong>recurring plans with auto-pay</strong>, <strong>upsell add-ons</strong> (fertilisation, aeration, cleanups), win <strong>commercial and HOA contracts</strong>, <strong>hire and train crews</strong> with clear processes, use <strong>lawn care software</strong> for scheduling and billing, and build a <strong>marketing system</strong> that generates steady leads.</p>

## 1. Route density

Concentrate customers in fewer neighbourhoods to cut drive time; decline far-away jobs or charge more for them.

## 2. Pricing

Know your cost per hour, raise prices annually, set minimums and drop the least profitable accounts (see [How much does lawn care cost?](/blog/how-much-does-lawn-care-cost/)).

## 3. Recurring revenue

Sell seasonal/annual plans billed monthly with auto-pay for predictable cash flow.

## 4. Upsells

Fertilisation and weed control, aeration and overseeding, cleanups, mulching, hedge trimming, irrigation checks.

## 5. Commercial and HOA accounts

Larger contracts fill weekdays and improve efficiency (see [How to get commercial landscaping contracts](/blog/how-to-get-commercial-landscaping-contracts/)).

## 6. Crews and training

Hire reliable people, document checklists for each service, set quality standards, and promote crew leaders.

## 7. Software and automation

Scheduling, routing, quoting, invoicing, reminders and review requests in one system. Automate follow-ups and payment reminders.

## 8. Marketing system

Google Business Profile, reviews, website with quote form, referral programme and seasonal campaigns (see [How to get lawn care customers](/blog/how-to-get-lawn-care-customers/)).

## 9. Off-season revenue

Snow removal, holiday lighting, leaf cleanup and landscaping projects.

## 10. Know your numbers

Revenue per crew hour, profit per account, customer retention and cost per lead.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I grow my lawn care business?
Improve route density, raise prices, sell recurring plans, upsell services, win commercial accounts, hire crews and use software.

### How do lawn care businesses scale?
By adding crews with documented processes, software for scheduling and billing, and steady marketing.

### What services increase lawn care profits?
Fertilisation, aeration, overseeding, cleanups and mulching usually have strong margins.

### How do I get commercial lawn care contracts?
Build relationships with property managers and HOAs and submit professional bids with insurance proof.

### When should I hire my first lawn care employee?
When you consistently turn away work or can't keep up with your schedule.

### How can I make money in the off season?
Offer snow removal, holiday lighting, leaf cleanup and landscaping projects.
