<p class="lede">To make a booking website: decide <strong>what customers book</strong> (appointments, classes, rooms, rentals, tours), choose a <strong>platform with booking built in</strong> (Wix Bookings, Squarespace Scheduling/Acuity, WordPress with a booking plugin, an embedded tool like Calendly or Cal.com, or a <strong>custom-built booking system</strong>), set up <strong>services, staff, availability and prices</strong>, add <strong>online payments or deposits</strong>, automatic <strong>confirmations and reminders</strong>, then design pages that make it <strong>easy to book in a few taps</strong> on mobile.</p>

## Step 1: Define your booking model

| Business | Booking type | Key needs |
|---|---|---|
| Clinic, salon, consultant | Appointments with staff | Staff calendars, service durations, buffers |
| Gym, yoga, workshops | Classes/events with capacity | Seats, waitlists, memberships |
| Hotels, rentals, homestays | Nights/dates | Availability calendar, channel sync |
| Tours and activities | Time slots with capacity | Group sizes, guides |
| Equipment/vehicle rental | Items by time | Inventory, deposits |
| Restaurants | Tables | Covers, time slots |

## Step 2: Choose a platform

| Option | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Wix Bookings** | Small service businesses | Built into Wix sites |
| **Squarespace Scheduling (Acuity)** | Appointments, design-led sites | Strong scheduling features |
| **WordPress + plugin** (Amelia, Bookly, WooCommerce Bookings) | Flexibility, owned site | Plugin licences and maintenance |
| **Embedded schedulers** (Calendly, Cal.com, SimplyBook.me, Setmore) | Adding booking to any site | Fast to set up |
| **Industry software** (salon, clinic, fitness, hotel systems) | Vertical features | Often include POS, records |
| **Shopify + booking apps** | Product + booking businesses | App-dependent |
| **Custom booking system** | Unique rules, integrations, scale | Developer build (see [How to build a web app](/blog/how-to-build-a-web-app/)) |

## Step 3: Set up services and availability

- Services with **duration, price, description**
- **Staff** and which services they perform
- **Working hours**, breaks and holidays
- **Buffers** between appointments
- **Lead time** (e.g., no bookings within 2 hours) and **booking window** (e.g., up to 60 days)
- **Capacity** for classes/tours
- **Locations** if multiple

## Step 4: Payments and policies

- Pay in full, **deposit**, or pay at venue
- Stripe, PayPal, Razorpay or platform payments
- **Cancellation and no-show policy** displayed clearly
- Discount codes, packages and memberships

## Step 5: Automate confirmations and reminders

- Instant **email/SMS/WhatsApp confirmation**
- Reminders (24 hours and 2 hours before)
- Reschedule/cancel links
- Post-visit **review request**
(See [How to schedule a WhatsApp message](/blog/how-to-schedule-a-whatsapp-message/) and [Benefits of online booking](/blog/benefits-of-online-booking/).)

## Step 6: Integrations

- **Google/Outlook calendar sync**
- **CRM** for customer history (see [What is a CRM?](/blog/what-is-a-crm/))
- **Google Business Profile "Book" button** (see [How to create a Google Business page](/blog/how-to-create-a-google-business-page/))
- **Instagram/Facebook booking buttons**
- **Accounting and invoicing** (see [How to send an invoice](/blog/how-to-send-an-invoice/))
- **Video meeting links** for online sessions

## Step 7: Design for more bookings

- **"Book now" button** visible on every page (sticky on mobile)
- Booking in **3 steps or fewer**
- Show **prices and durations** clearly
- **Real photos, reviews and staff profiles**
- **Fast loading** and mobile-first layout
- **WhatsApp/chat** for questions
- Clear **location and parking info**

## Common mistakes

- Too many steps or required fields
- No mobile testing
- Calendar not synced, causing double bookings
- No reminders, causing no-shows
- Hidden cancellation policies
- Booking page that doesn't match your brand

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I make a booking website?
Choose a platform with booking (Wix, Squarespace, WordPress plugins or scheduling tools), set services, staff, availability and prices, add payments and reminders, and design pages that make booking quick on mobile.

### What is the best booking system for a small business?
It depends on your industry: Wix Bookings, Acuity, Calendly or SimplyBook.me for appointments; specialised software for salons, clinics, gyms or hotels.

### Can I add online booking to my existing website?
Yes. Embed a scheduling tool or booking widget on your site, or install a booking plugin.

### How much does a booking website cost?
From about $20–$60 per month DIY to a few thousand dollars for a professionally built site; custom systems cost more.

### Should I take deposits for bookings?
Deposits reduce no-shows, especially for long or high-value appointments; state your policy clearly.

### How do I stop double bookings?
Use one central booking system and sync it with staff calendars and other channels.
