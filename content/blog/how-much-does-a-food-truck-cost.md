<p class="lede">A food truck typically costs <strong>roughly $30,000–$100,000 for a used or basic truck</strong> and <strong>$100,000–$200,000+ for a new custom-built truck</strong>, depending on size, kitchen equipment and location. Total <strong>startup costs</strong> also include permits, insurance, commissary fees, wrap/branding, POS, initial inventory and working capital. <strong>Leasing</strong> or starting with a <strong>trailer or food van</strong> can lower upfront costs.</p>

## Truck cost options (rough ranges)

| Option | Typical range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Used food truck | ~$30,000–$100,000 | Inspect engine, kitchen, generator |
| New custom build | ~$100,000–$200,000+ | Designed for your menu |
| Food trailer | Often lower than trucks | Needs towing vehicle |
| Lease/rental | Monthly payments | Lower upfront, less equity |

Prices vary widely by region, condition and equipment.

## Startup cost breakdown

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| Truck/trailer | Biggest cost |
| Kitchen equipment | Grills, fryers, fridges, hoods, fire suppression |
| Generator/power | Quiet generator or battery systems |
| Wrap and branding | Design and installation |
| Permits and licences | Vary by city/state (see [Food truck permits and licences](/blog/food-truck-permits-and-licenses/)) |
| Insurance | Commercial auto, general liability, property |
| Commissary | Monthly rent |
| POS and online ordering | Hardware + subscriptions |
| Initial inventory | Food and packaging |
| Website and marketing | Menu, schedule, catering form |
| Working capital | Several months of expenses |

## Monthly running costs

Food and packaging, fuel and propane, commissary rent, staff wages, insurance, loan payments, maintenance, permits renewals, payment processing and marketing.

## Financing options

Equipment loans, small business loans, leasing, investors, crowdfunding, and starting smaller (trailer or cart) to prove demand.

## Ways to cut costs

Buy a quality used truck with inspection, start with a trailer or cart, keep the menu simple to limit equipment, share commissary space, and focus on catering/events with guaranteed minimums.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a food truck cost?
Roughly $30,000–$100,000 for used or basic trucks and $100,000–$200,000+ for new custom builds, depending on equipment and location.

### How much does it cost to start a food truck business?
The truck plus equipment, permits, insurance, commissary, branding, POS, inventory and working capital; total costs vary widely.

### Is it cheaper to buy or lease a food truck?
Leasing has lower upfront costs; buying builds equity and may cost less long term.

### How much does a food truck permit cost?
It varies by city and state; budget for multiple permits and annual renewals.

### Can I start a food truck with little money?
Consider a food cart, trailer, shared truck or leasing, and start with events and catering.

### How long does it take to pay off a food truck?
It depends on sales, costs and financing; strong catering and event revenue can shorten payback.
