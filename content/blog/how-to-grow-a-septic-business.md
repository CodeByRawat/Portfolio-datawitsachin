<p class="lede">To grow a septic business, <strong>get found on Google</strong>, partner with <strong>realtors and home inspectors</strong>, sell <strong>maintenance plans and reminders</strong>, add higher-value services like <strong>repairs, installation and drain field work</strong>, then add <strong>trucks and trained drivers</strong>. Many septic companies also grow by <strong>buying retiring owners' routes</strong>.</p>

## 1. Get more customers

- Google Business Profile with photos and reviews
- A website listing services, service area and online booking
- Ads in rural areas and local directories
- Truck lettering and yard signs

## 2. Partner with realtors and inspectors

Home sales often require septic inspections. Fast, reliable inspection reports earn steady referrals.

## 3. Build recurring revenue

| Idea | Benefit |
|---|---|
| Pumping reminders | Customers return every few years |
| Maintenance plans | Predictable income |
| Commercial contracts | Restaurants, campgrounds, businesses |

## 4. Add services

- Repairs, risers and filters
- Camera inspections
- Drain field repair and replacement
- New installations for builders
- Grease trap pumping
- Portable toilet rentals

## 5. Add trucks and drivers

When your schedule is full for weeks, add a truck and a trained driver. Keep each truck busy with good routing.

## 6. Buy other routes

Many septic owners are near retirement. Buying their customer list and trucks can grow you quickly.

## 7. Track the right numbers

Jobs per truck per day, revenue per job, disposal cost per gallon, repeat rate and review count.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I grow my septic business?
Get more Google reviews, partner with realtors, add services and add trucks as demand grows.

### How do septic companies get customers?
Google, realtors, inspectors, builders and reminders to past customers.

### Should I add septic installation?
If you have the licence and equipment, it adds high-value jobs.

### When should I buy another pump truck?
When your current truck is fully booked and you can keep a second one busy.

### Can I buy another septic company?
Yes. Buying a retiring owner's routes is a common way to grow.
