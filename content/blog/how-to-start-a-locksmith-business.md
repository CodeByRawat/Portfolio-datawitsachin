<p class="lede">To start a locksmith business, <strong>get trained and experienced</strong> (apprenticeship or locksmith course), get a <strong>state locksmith license if your state requires one</strong>, buy <strong>insurance and a bond</strong>, set up a <strong>mobile van with tools and key machines</strong>, and get calls from <strong>Google, property managers and businesses</strong>. Many locksmiths start mobile for about <strong>$10,000 to $40,000</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Get training and experience

Work under an experienced locksmith or take a locksmith training course. Learn lock picking and bypass, rekeying, installing locks, master key systems, safes and automotive keys. Industry associations offer certifications that build trust.

## Step 2: Check licensing

Some states (for example California, Texas, Illinois, New Jersey, North Carolina and others) require a locksmith license, often with fingerprints and a background check. Other states only need a business license. Check your state and city before you start.

## Step 3: Register, insure and bond

LLC, EIN, business license, general liability insurance, commercial auto, and a surety bond. Customers are trusting you with their homes, so a clean record matters.

## Step 4: Choose your services

| Service | Notes |
|---|---|
| Residential | Lockouts, rekeying, new locks, smart locks |
| Automotive | Car lockouts, key cutting and programming, fobs |
| Commercial | Master key systems, access control, panic bars |
| Safes | Opening, installing and servicing safes |
| Emergency 24/7 | Higher rates, more calls |

Car keys: [How to start a car locksmith business](/blog/how-to-start-a-car-locksmith-business/).

## Step 5: Tools and startup costs

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Used van with shelving | $10,000 – $25,000 |
| Hand tools, picks, lockout kits | $1,000 – $3,000 |
| Key cutting machine | $1,000 – $5,000 |
| Car key programmer | $1,500 – $5,000+ |
| Key blanks and lock inventory | $1,000 – $3,000 |
| License, insurance, bond | $1,000 – $4,000 per year |
| Website and Google profile | $500 – $3,000 |

## Step 6: Set prices

Charge a service call fee plus labour and parts. Add after-hours and emergency surcharges. Give a clear total before starting work: many customers worry about scam locksmiths, so honest upfront pricing wins reviews.

## Step 7: Get customers

1. Google Business Profile with your real address and reviews
2. A fast mobile website with a call button and your service area
3. Property managers, landlords and realtors (rekeys at every move-out)
4. Car dealers and auto repair shops
5. Businesses for commercial locks and access control

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a locksmith business?
Get trained, check your state's licensing, insure and bond the business, equip a van and market on Google.

### How much does it cost to start a locksmith business?
Often $10,000 to $40,000 for a mobile setup.

### Do you need a license to be a locksmith?
In some states yes; in others only a business license.

### Is a locksmith business profitable?
Yes. Margins are good, especially on automotive keys and emergency calls.

### Should I start mobile or with a shop?
Most locksmiths start mobile because overhead is lower.
