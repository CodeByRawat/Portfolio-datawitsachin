<p class="lede">A <strong>landing page conversion</strong> happens when a visitor <strong>completes the action the landing page was built for</strong>: submitting a form, booking a call, starting a trial, making a purchase, downloading a guide or tapping to call or WhatsApp. The <strong>landing page conversion rate</strong> is the percentage of visitors who convert: <strong>conversions ÷ visitors × 100</strong>. If 1,000 people visit and 50 submit the form, the conversion rate is <strong>5%</strong>.</p>

## Landing page conversion examples

| Landing page type | Conversion |
|---|---|
| Lead generation | Form submitted, quote requested |
| Booking | Appointment or call booked |
| Sales | Purchase completed |
| SaaS | Free trial or demo request |
| Event/webinar | Registration |
| Local service | Click-to-call or WhatsApp click |
| App | App store click/install |

## Macro vs micro conversions

- **Macro conversion:** the main goal (lead, sale, booking)
- **Micro conversions:** steps toward it (scrolling to pricing, watching the video, clicking a CTA, starting a form)

Tracking micro conversions shows where visitors drop off.

## How to calculate landing page conversion rate

**Conversion rate = (number of conversions ÷ number of visitors) × 100**

Use consistent definitions: unique visitors or sessions, and one primary conversion. Calculate separately per traffic source (Google Ads, Meta, email, organic), since rates vary greatly by intent.

## What is a good landing page conversion rate?

Benchmarks vary widely by industry, offer, traffic source and definition. As rough guidance:
- Many landing pages convert in the **2–5%** range
- Strong, well-targeted pages often reach **10%+**
- High-intent traffic (branded search, warm email lists) converts much higher than cold social traffic
- Low-commitment offers (free guide) convert higher than high-commitment ones (purchase)

The best benchmark is **your own baseline**: improve it month over month.

## How to track landing page conversions

| Tool | How |
|---|---|
| **Google Analytics 4** | Mark key events (form_submit, generate_lead, purchase) as key events/conversions |
| **Google Tag Manager** | Fire tags on thank-you pages, button clicks, form submissions |
| **Google Ads** | Conversion actions via Google tag or GA4 import; enhanced conversions |
| **Meta Ads** | Meta Pixel + Conversions API standard events (Lead, Purchase) |
| **Call tracking** | Dynamic numbers for phone conversions |
| **CRM** | Track which leads become customers (offline conversion import) |

Use a **thank-you page** or explicit events so every conversion is counted once.

## 15 ways to increase landing page conversion rate

1. **Match the message** to the ad or link that brought visitors
2. **Write a benefit-driven headline** that answers "what's in it for me?"
3. **One clear CTA** with specific text ("Get my free quote")
4. **Put the CTA above the fold** and repeat it
5. **Reduce form fields** to the essentials
6. **Add social proof:** reviews, ratings, logos, case studies near the CTA
7. **Show the product or result** with images or short video (see [What is an explainer video?](/blog/what-is-an-explainer-video/))
8. **Handle objections** with FAQs, guarantees and pricing clarity
9. **Speed up the page** (images, scripts, hosting)
10. **Design mobile-first** with sticky CTAs and tap-to-call
11. **Remove navigation and distractions**
12. **Add trust signals:** contact details, privacy note, secure badges
13. **Offer an alternative low-friction action** (WhatsApp, chat; see [How to create an AI chatbot](/blog/how-to-create-an-ai-chatbot/))
14. **A/B test** headlines, offers and layouts
15. **Follow up instantly** after conversion so leads don't go cold (see [How to automate your sales process](/blog/how-to-automate-your-sales-process/))

## Conversion rate optimisation (CRO) process

1. **Measure** baseline conversion rate by source
2. **Research:** analytics, heatmaps, session recordings, user feedback
3. **Hypothesise:** "Adding reviews near the form will increase submissions"
4. **Test:** A/B test with enough traffic
5. **Implement winners** and repeat

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a landing page conversion?
When a visitor completes the landing page's intended action, such as submitting a form, booking, purchasing, signing up or calling.

### How do you calculate landing page conversion rate?
Divide conversions by visitors and multiply by 100. For example, 40 sign-ups from 800 visitors is a 5% conversion rate.

### What is a good landing page conversion rate?
Often around 2–5% is typical and 10%+ is strong, but it depends heavily on industry, offer and traffic source. Improve against your own baseline.

### How do I track landing page conversions?
Set up conversion events in GA4, Google Ads and Meta Pixel using a thank-you page or form submission events, ideally via Google Tag Manager.

### Why is my landing page not converting?
Common causes are message mismatch with the ad, unclear offer, weak proof, long forms, slow loading, poor mobile experience or the wrong audience.

### What is the difference between conversion rate and click-through rate?
Click-through rate measures how many people click an ad or link; conversion rate measures how many visitors complete the desired action on the landing page.
