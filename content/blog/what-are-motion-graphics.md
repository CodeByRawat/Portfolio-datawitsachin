<p class="lede"><strong>Motion graphics</strong> are animated graphic design: text, shapes, icons, logos, charts, illustrations and interface elements brought to life with movement, timing and sound. Instead of telling a character-driven story like a cartoon, motion graphics are used to <strong>communicate information or a message</strong> clearly and attractively: an explainer video, an animated ad, a logo reveal, a product feature walkthrough, a title sequence or a data visualisation.</p>

If you've watched a short ad where words slide in rhythm with music, a SaaS video where the app interface floats in 3D space, or a news graphic where numbers count up on a chart, you've seen motion graphics. They are everywhere because movement grabs attention in a feed and makes complex ideas easier to follow. This guide explains what motion graphics are, how they differ from animation and VFX, the main styles, how a project is made, the tools and what it costs.

## Motion graphics meaning

The term comes from **"motion graphic design"**: graphic design principles (typography, layout, colour, hierarchy, brand) combined with time-based media principles (timing, easing, rhythm, transitions, sound). A motion designer thinks about:

- **What the viewer should understand** at each second
- **Hierarchy over time:** what appears first, what is emphasised, what leaves
- **Rhythm:** cuts and moves timed to music or voiceover
- **Easing:** how objects accelerate and decelerate so movement feels natural rather than robotic
- **Continuity:** how one scene transforms into the next

Motion graphics usually have **no dialogue-driven characters** and often a **voiceover or on-screen text** carrying the message.

## Motion graphics vs animation vs VFX

| | Motion graphics | Character animation | Visual effects (VFX) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Main purpose** | Communicate a message, information or brand | Tell a story through characters | Create or enhance imagery in live-action footage |
| **Typical content** | Text, logos, icons, UI, charts, shapes, illustrations | Characters acting, lip-sync, emotion | Explosions, environments, compositing, cleanup |
| **Examples** | Explainers, ads, title sequences, social posts, product demos | Cartoons, animated films, game cinematics | Film and TV shots, set extensions, CGI creatures |
| **Common tools** | After Effects, Cinema 4D, Blender, Figma, Rive, Lottie | Toon Boom, Maya, Blender, Moho | Nuke, Houdini, After Effects, Maya |

The boundaries blur: many explainer videos mix motion graphics with simple character animation, and many ads composite motion graphics over live footage.

## Where motion graphics are used

- **Social ads and organic posts:** animated text, product highlights and offers for Reels, TikTok, Shorts and LinkedIn
- **Explainer videos:** how a product or service works in 60–120 seconds (see [How to write a video brief for a product demo](/blog/how-to-write-a-video-brief-for-a-product-demo/))
- **SaaS and app promos:** UI screens animated in 2D or 3D to show features
- **Logo animations and brand idents:** a 3–8 second signature for videos and presentations
- **Title sequences and lower thirds** for YouTube, events, film and TV
- **Data visualisation:** animated charts and infographics for reports and news
- **Product launches and event openers**
- **Website and app UI animation:** micro-interactions, onboarding, animated illustrations (often delivered as Lottie or Rive files)
- **Presentations and internal training**

## Common motion graphics styles

| Style | What it looks like | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **Kinetic typography** | Words animated in time with voice or music | Ads, quotes, announcements |
| **Flat 2D / vector** | Clean shapes, icons and illustrations | Explainers, onboarding, tech brands |
| **3D motion** | Objects, products or UI in three-dimensional space with lighting | Premium launches, hardware, SaaS hero videos |
| **UI animation / screen-based** | Real app screens moving, zooming and transforming | Software demos and promos |
| **Isometric** | 2.5D illustrated scenes | Processes, systems, ecosystems |
| **Collage / mixed media** | Cut-out photos, textures, hand-drawn elements | Fashion, culture, creative brands |
| **Data-driven** | Charts, maps, counters | Reports, finance, journalism |
| **Liquid / shape morphing** | Fluid transitions between shapes | Brand idents, energetic ads |

## How a motion graphics video is made

1. **Brief:** goal, audience, platform, length, key message, call to action, brand assets.
2. **Script:** the words (voiceover or on-screen text). For a 60-second video, roughly 140–160 spoken words.
3. **Storyboard:** frame-by-frame sketches or styleframes showing what happens in each scene.
4. **Style frames / design:** a few fully designed stills that set the look (colour, type, illustration style).
5. **Voiceover and music:** recorded or licensed so animation can be timed to them.
6. **Animation:** building the movement, transitions and timing.
7. **Sound design:** whooshes, clicks and accents that make movement feel physical.
8. **Review rounds:** usually two or three rounds of feedback.
9. **Export:** versions for each platform (16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5), with and without captions.

Approving the **script and storyboard before animation** is the single biggest factor in keeping a project on time and budget, because changes are cheap on paper and expensive once animated.

## How much do motion graphics cost?

Prices vary hugely by region, complexity and who makes them. Rough ranges for a professional freelancer or small studio:

| Project | Typical length | Rough range (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| Animated social post or simple ad | 6–15 s | $150–$800 |
| Logo animation | 3–8 s | $150–$1,500 |
| 2D explainer video | 60–90 s | $1,500–$8,000 |
| SaaS / app promo with UI animation | 20–60 s | $1,500–$10,000+ |
| 3D product animation | 15–45 s | $2,000–$15,000+ |
| Ongoing monthly ad creatives | Several per month | Retainer, often $1,000–$5,000/month |

What drives cost: length, style (3D and custom illustration cost more), number of revisions, voiceover and music licensing, number of aspect-ratio versions, and turnaround time. Templates are cheap but look like everyone else's; custom work matches your brand and message.

## Why businesses use motion graphics

- **Attention:** movement stops the scroll better than static images in most feeds
- **Clarity:** a 60-second animation can explain an abstract product better than a page of text
- **Brand consistency:** fonts, colours and style controlled exactly, unlike live footage
- **Easy to update:** change a price, feature or language without a reshoot
- **Reusable:** one project can be cut into many ads, posts and website loops

## How AI is changing motion design

AI now helps with ideas, scripts, voiceovers, background generation, rotoscoping, upscaling and even short generated clips. But brand-accurate typography, real product UI, precise timing to a message and consistent style across a campaign still depend on a designer's control. The most effective workflow today combines **designed and animated core scenes** with AI-assisted assets where they save time.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are motion graphics?
Motion graphics are animated graphic design elements such as text, logos, shapes, icons, charts and UI, used to communicate a message or information in videos, ads, websites and presentations.

### What is the difference between motion graphics and animation?
Motion graphics animate design elements to communicate information or a brand message, while character animation tells stories through characters acting. Motion graphics are a type of animation focused on design and messaging.

### What software is used for motion graphics?
Adobe After Effects is the most common, alongside Cinema 4D and Blender for 3D, Illustrator and Figma for design, and Rive or Lottie for web and app animations.

### How long does a motion graphics video take?
A short social ad can take a few days; a 60–90 second explainer usually takes two to five weeks including script, storyboard, design, animation and revisions.

### Are motion graphics worth it for small businesses?
Often, yes. A short animated ad or explainer can be reused across your website, social media and ads, and animated content tends to get more attention than static posts.

### What is a motion graphics designer?
A motion graphics designer (motion designer) combines graphic design and animation skills to create animated content such as ads, explainers, logo animations, titles and UI animations.
