<p class="lede"><strong>Event planning</strong> is the process of designing and organising events (weddings, conferences, corporate meetings, parties, festivals) from concept to completion. <strong>Event planners</strong> define goals and budgets, choose venues, hire and manage vendors, build timelines, handle guests and logistics, manage the event on the day, and evaluate results so clients can enjoy a smooth, memorable event.</p>

## The event planning process

1. **Goals and brief:** purpose, audience, date, budget, style
2. **Budget:** allocate across venue, catering, décor, AV, entertainment, staffing, contingency
3. **Venue:** source, visit, negotiate, contract
4. **Vendors:** caterers, photographers, florists, AV, rentals, entertainment, transport
5. **Design:** theme, décor, layout, run of show
6. **Logistics:** timelines, floor plans, permits, insurance, accessibility
7. **Guest management:** invitations, RSVPs, registration, seating
8. **Marketing** (for public events): promotion and ticketing
9. **On-site management:** setup, vendor coordination, problem-solving
10. **Evaluation:** feedback, budget reconciliation, lessons learned

## Daily responsibilities

Client meetings, vendor calls and negotiation, budget tracking, timelines and checklists, site visits, contract reviews, and long days on event dates.

## Types of events

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Social | Weddings, birthdays, anniversaries, showers |
| Corporate | Conferences, product launches, offsites, holiday parties |
| Non-profit | Galas, fundraisers |
| Public | Festivals, concerts, community events |
| Virtual/hybrid | Webinars, online conferences |

## Tools event planners use

Project management, budgeting spreadsheets, CRM, floor plan software, registration/ticketing platforms, e-signatures and communication tools; automation for reminders and attendee emails (see [How to automate tasks with AI](/blog/how-to-automate-tasks-with-ai/)).

## Planner vs coordinator vs manager

Planners design and manage the whole event; coordinators focus on logistics and day-of execution; event managers often oversee larger or corporate events and teams (see [How to become an event planner](/blog/how-to-become-an-event-planner/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### What does an event planner do?
Plans and manages events end to end: goals, budget, venue, vendors, design, logistics, guest management and on-site coordination.

### What is event planning?
The process of designing, organising and executing events to meet a client's goals within a budget.

### What skills do event planners need?
Organisation, communication, negotiation, budgeting, creativity and calm problem-solving.

### Is event planning a stressful job?
It can be, especially near event dates, but strong systems and vendor teams reduce stress.

### Do event planners need certifications?
Not required, but certifications like CMP or CSEP can help in corporate and conference roles.

### How do event planners get paid?
Through flat fees, percentages of budgets, hourly rates or salaries, depending on the role (see [How much do event planners charge?](/blog/how-much-do-event-planners-charge/)).
