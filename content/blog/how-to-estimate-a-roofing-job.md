<p class="lede">To estimate a roofing job: <strong>measure the roof area</strong> (on site, from drone/satellite reports), convert to <strong>roofing squares</strong> (1 square = 100 sq ft), apply a <strong>pitch multiplier</strong> and <strong>waste factor</strong>, list <strong>materials</strong>, add <strong>tear-off, disposal and labour</strong>, then add <strong>overhead and profit</strong>. Present the estimate clearly with options.</p>

## Step by step

1. **Measure:** length × width of each roof plane, or use aerial measurement reports
2. **Adjust for pitch:** steeper roofs have more surface area; apply a pitch factor
3. **Convert to squares:** total sq ft ÷ 100
4. **Add waste:** commonly 10–15% depending on complexity (hips, valleys, dormers)
5. **Materials:** shingles/metal, underlayment, ice and water shield, starter strips, ridge caps, flashing, drip edge, vents, nails
6. **Tear-off and disposal:** layers to remove, dumpster and disposal fees
7. **Labour:** crew hours based on squares, pitch, height and complexity
8. **Extras:** decking replacement, skylights, chimneys, permits
9. **Overhead and profit:** insurance, vehicles, office, marketing plus margin
10. **Present:** clear written estimate with good/better/best options and warranty details

## Example structure

| Line item | Basis |
|---|---|
| Materials | Squares × cost per square + accessories |
| Tear-off | Squares × layers × rate |
| Labour | Squares × labour rate (adjusted for pitch) |
| Disposal | Dumpster/tonnage |
| Overhead + profit | Percentage or markup |

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you estimate a roofing job?
Measure the roof, convert to squares, adjust for pitch and waste, price materials, tear-off and labour, then add overhead and profit.

### What is a roofing square?
100 square feet of roof surface.

### How much waste should I add to a roofing estimate?
Often 10–15%, more for complex roofs with many hips and valleys.

### How do you measure a roof without climbing it?
Use aerial or satellite measurement reports or drones.

### How do roofers calculate labour?
By squares, pitch, height, layers to remove and complexity.

### Should a roofing estimate include options?
Yes; good/better/best options help customers choose and often raise average job value.
