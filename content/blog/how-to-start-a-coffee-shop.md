<p class="lede">To start a coffee shop: define your <strong>concept</strong> (grab-and-go, café with food, specialty roaster, drive-thru, kiosk), write a <strong>business plan and budget</strong>, secure a <strong>high-traffic location</strong>, get <strong>permits and health approvals</strong>, buy <strong>espresso equipment</strong> and choose <strong>coffee and food suppliers</strong>, design a <strong>focused menu with good margins</strong>, hire and train <strong>baristas</strong>, set up <strong>POS, loyalty and online ordering</strong>, and build buzz before <strong>opening day</strong>.</p>

## Steps

1. **Concept:** who your customers are and what experience you offer
2. **Business plan:** costs, sales forecasts, pricing and funding (see [How much does it cost to open a coffee shop?](/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-open-a-coffee-shop/))
3. **Location:** foot or car traffic, visibility, parking, nearby offices/schools, lease terms
4. **Permits:** business licence, food service permit, health inspection, signage and building permits
5. **Equipment:** espresso machine, grinders, brewers, refrigeration, ice machine, dishwasher, furniture
6. **Suppliers:** roaster, milk and alternatives, pastries/food, cups and packaging
7. **Menu and pricing:** focus on high-margin drinks; price to cover cost of goods and labour
8. **Staff:** hire for attitude; train on drinks, speed and hospitality
9. **Tech:** POS, loyalty programme, online ordering and a website (see [How to create a website for your business](/blog/how-to-create-a-website-for-your-business/))
10. **Marketing:** social media teasers, soft opening, local partnerships, Google Business Profile

## Small coffee shop or home start options

Kiosks, carts, coffee trucks and pop-ups need less capital; selling roasted beans online can start from home where cottage/food laws allow.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a coffee shop business?
Define a concept, write a plan, find a location, get permits, buy equipment, choose suppliers, design a menu, hire staff, set up POS and market your opening.

### How do I start a small coffee shop?
Start lean with a kiosk, cart, small footprint café or coffee truck.

### Can I start a coffee business from home?
Roasting and selling beans or packaged coffee may be possible under local food laws; serving drinks usually needs a permitted space.

### Can you open a coffee shop with no money?
It's difficult; options include investors, loans, partners or starting very small with a cart.

### What permits do I need for a coffee shop?
Business licence, food service permit, health inspection and building/signage permits.

### How do coffee shops attract customers?
Great location, quality drinks, fast service, loyalty programmes, social media and online ordering.
