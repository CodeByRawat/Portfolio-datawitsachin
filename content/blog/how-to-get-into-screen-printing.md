<p class="lede">To get into screen printing, <strong>learn the basics</strong> (online courses, videos, local workshops), <strong>practise with a small press or starter kit</strong>, or <strong>get a job at a print shop</strong> to learn fast on real equipment. Master <strong>screen prep, registration, printing and curing</strong>, then start taking small orders.</p>

## Ways to learn

| Option | Pros |
|---|---|
| Online courses and videos | Cheap, flexible |
| Community workshops and makerspaces | Use shared equipment |
| Art school printmaking classes | Strong fundamentals |
| Job at a screen printing shop | Paid, fast, real production |
| Supplier training | Equipment and ink know-how |

## Starter equipment

A beginner kit or single-colour press, a few screens, emulsion, exposure light, squeegees, plastisol or water-based ink, and a heat gun or flash dryer. Upgrade as you take orders.

## Skills to master

1. Artwork and colour separations
2. Coating and exposing screens
3. Registration for multi-colour prints
4. Printing technique (pressure, angle, flood)
5. Curing at the right temperature
6. Screen reclaiming and shop cleanliness

## Practise

Print on scrap fabric and cheap shirts. Test wash durability. Learn from mistakes before charging customers.

## Turning it into income

- Print for friends, teams and local events
- Sell your own designs
- Take small business orders
- Start a side hustle, then grow into a shop

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I get into screen printing?
Learn online or at a workshop, practise with a starter kit, or work at a print shop.

### How do I start silk screen printing?
Get a basic press, screens, emulsion, exposure light, inks and practise.

### Is screen printing hard to learn?
The basics are quick to learn; quality and speed take practice.

### Can I learn screen printing at home?
Yes, with ventilation and water access.

### Is screen printing a good career?
It can be, especially if you start your own shop or specialise.
