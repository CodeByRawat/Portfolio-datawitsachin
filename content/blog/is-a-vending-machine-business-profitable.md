<p class="lede">A vending machine business can be <strong>profitable</strong> when machines are in <strong>high-traffic locations with captive customers</strong>, products are <strong>priced well above wholesale cost</strong>, and restocking routes are <strong>efficient</strong>. Profit is reduced by <strong>location commissions, spoilage, fuel, repairs and card fees</strong>. Weak locations are the main reason vending businesses fail.</p>

## How vending machines make money

Each sale earns the **difference between your sale price and wholesale cost**. Multiply by sales per day and number of machines, then subtract costs.

## Costs

| Cost | Notes |
|---|---|
| Machines | Upfront purchase or financing |
| Inventory | Wholesale snacks and drinks |
| Location commission | Some locations ask for a percentage |
| Card processing fees | Per transaction |
| Fuel and time | Restocking routes |
| Repairs | Coin mechs, readers, refrigeration |
| Insurance and permits | |

## Is vending a good business?

### Pros
- Low labour per machine
- Flexible, part-time friendly
- Scalable machine by machine
- Cash and card income daily

### Cons
- Location dependency
- Theft and vandalism risk
- Time spent restocking
- Competition from big operators

## What makes a vending location profitable

Many daily visitors, long stays (workers, residents), few nearby food options, and 24/7 access. Factories, warehouses and apartment buildings often outperform small offices.

## How to increase profit

Use telemetry to stock best-sellers, add card readers, raise prices carefully, remove underperforming machines, group locations geographically, and upgrade top locations to micro markets.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a vending machine business profitable?
Yes, in the right locations with good product mix and efficient routes.

### Is vending a good business?
It's a solid, scalable side or full-time business for people willing to find great locations.

### Are vending machines passive income?
Semi-passive: you still restock, collect, repair and manage locations.

### What is the most profitable vending machine?
It depends on the location; combo and drink machines in busy workplaces often perform well.

### Why do vending businesses fail?
Poor locations, wrong products and high commissions.

### How many vending machines do I need to make a full-time income?
It depends on profit per machine; owners grow gradually by adding strong locations.
