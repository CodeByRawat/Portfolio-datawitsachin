<p class="lede">To start a hair salon: get the required <strong>cosmetology and salon licences</strong>, choose a <strong>model</strong> (full-service salon, booth rental, salon suite, home or mobile), write a <strong>business plan</strong> with costs and pricing, find a <strong>location</strong>, buy <strong>chairs, stations, shampoo units and products</strong>, decide on <strong>staff pay (commission or booth rent)</strong>, set up <strong>salon software with online booking</strong>, build your <strong>brand and Instagram</strong>, and launch with offers that fill your book.</p>

## Step 1: Licences and regulations

Requirements vary by country/state: cosmetology/barber licence for stylists, salon or establishment licence, business registration, health and safety inspections, and insurance. Check your local cosmetology board or equivalent.

## Step 2: Choose your salon model

| Model | Startup cost | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Salon suite** (rent a private suite) | Low | Fast start, independence | Limited growth without staff |
| **Booth/chair rental** in another salon | Very low | Minimal risk | Less control |
| **Home salon** | Low | Low overhead | Zoning/licensing rules, perception |
| **Mobile salon** | Low–medium | Serve clients at home/events | Travel time |
| **Full-service salon** with staff | Higher | Scalable, brand | Rent, payroll, management |

**Salon suite business:** some entrepreneurs lease a building and rent suites to independent stylists, a real-estate-like model.

## Step 3: Business plan and costs

Startup costs, services and pricing, target clients, staffing model and break-even (see [How much does it cost to open a salon?](/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-open-a-salon/)).

## Step 4: Location

Visibility, parking, foot traffic, plumbing for shampoo stations, electrical capacity, ventilation (especially for nail and chemical services) and lease terms.

## Step 5: Equipment and design

Styling chairs and stations, shampoo bowls, dryers, reception desk, retail display, tools, products, laundry, and an inviting design that photographs well for social media.

## Step 6: Staff and pay structure

| Model | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Commission** | Stylists earn a percentage of services; salon provides products/clients |
| **Hourly + commission** | Base pay plus incentives |
| **Booth/chair rent** | Stylists pay rent and run their own clients |

## Step 7: Software and online booking

Salon software (e.g., Fresha, Vagaro, Boulevard, Square Appointments, GlossGenius and others) for booking, payments, reminders, client notes and marketing. Add a **"Book now"** button to your website, Instagram and Google profile (see [How to make a booking website](/blog/how-to-make-a-booking-website/)).

## Step 8: Brand and marketing

- Name, logo and Instagram aesthetic
- Before/after photos and Reels
- Google Business Profile and reviews
- Grand opening offers and referral rewards
(Growth: [How to grow your salon business](/blog/how-to-grow-your-salon-business/).)

## How to open a salon with no money (or very little)

Start with **booth rental or a salon suite**, buy used equipment, build a client base on Instagram first, pre-sell packages, consider a small business loan or investor, and grow into a larger space.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a hair salon business?
Get licensed, choose a model, write a business plan, find a location, buy equipment, decide staff pay, set up booking software, brand your salon and launch with marketing offers.

### Do I need a licence to open a salon?
Usually yes: stylists need cosmetology licences and salons often need an establishment licence, plus business registration and inspections. Check local rules.

### How can I open a salon with little money?
Start with booth rental or a salon suite, buy used equipment, build clients on social media and pre-sell services.

### What is a salon suite business?
Either renting a private suite as an independent stylist, or owning a facility that rents suites to multiple independent professionals.

### Is owning a salon profitable?
It can be; see how much salon owners make and the factors that affect profit.

### How long does it take to open a salon?
Often a few months, depending on licences, build-out and financing.
