<p class="lede">Demolition companies find work through <strong>general contractors and developers</strong>, <strong>public bid boards</strong> (cities, schools, housing authorities), <strong>insurance and fire/storm damage</strong> work, <strong>remodelers and homeowners</strong>, and <strong>property managers</strong>. A strong <strong>Google presence, website and reputation for safe, on-time work</strong> keeps the jobs coming.</p>

## Where demolition work comes from

| Source | Typical jobs |
|---|---|
| General contractors | Interior strip-outs, selective demolition |
| Developers and builders | Full building teardowns, site clearing |
| Public agencies | Blighted properties, schools, infrastructure |
| Insurance and restoration | Fire, flood and storm damage |
| Homeowners | Sheds, pools, garages, house teardowns |
| Remodelers | Kitchens, bathrooms, basements |
| Commercial property managers | Tenant fit-out strip-outs |
| Environmental contractors | Post-abatement demolition |

## How to get on bid lists

Register with cities, counties, school districts and housing authorities. Get bonded, keep insurance current and learn public bidding requirements.

## Build contractor relationships

Visit general contractors, attend builder association events, respond fast to bid requests and finish on schedule. Repeat GC work is the backbone of many demolition companies.

## Market directly

1. Google Business Profile with project photos
2. A website listing services, service area and a quote form
3. Reviews from past clients
4. Truck and equipment branding
5. Partnerships with dumpster and excavation companies

## Win more bids

Price carefully (labour, equipment, disposal, recycling credits), show safety records, and explain your approach clearly.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I find demolition work?
General contractors, developers, public bids, insurance work, homeowners and property managers.

### How do demolition companies get contracts?
By bidding, building GC relationships and marketing online.

### Where are public demolition bids posted?
On city, county, school district and state bid portals.

### How do I get more demolition jobs?
Respond quickly, bid accurately, finish on time and collect reviews.

### Do demolition companies need bonding?
Often for public and larger commercial work.
