<p class="lede">To start a cleaning business: choose <strong>residential (house) or commercial (contract) cleaning</strong>, register your <strong>business</strong> and get any required <strong>licences</strong>, buy <strong>liability insurance</strong> (and bonding if you'll clean homes), assemble <strong>supplies and equipment</strong>, set <strong>prices</strong> by job type, create <strong>checklists</strong>, set up <strong>booking, scheduling and invoicing</strong>, and win first clients through <strong>Google, referrals and local marketing</strong>. Many cleaning businesses start from home with low startup costs.</p>

## Step 1: Choose your market

| Type | Customers | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Residential/house cleaning** | Homeowners, renters | Recurring weekly/biweekly visits |
| **Move-in/move-out, deep cleans** | Renters, landlords, agents | Higher-ticket one-offs |
| **Vacation rental/Airbnb turnover** | Hosts, property managers | Tight scheduling |
| **Commercial/contract cleaning** | Offices, clinics, gyms, schools | Contracts, after-hours work (see [How to find commercial cleaning contracts](/blog/how-to-find-commercial-cleaning-contracts/)) |
| **Specialty** | Carpets, windows, post-construction | Equipment and skills |

## Step 2: What you need to start

- Business registration and name
- Licences/permits as required locally (see [What licenses are needed to start a cleaning business?](/blog/what-licenses-are-needed-to-start-a-cleaning-business/))
- General liability insurance; workers' comp if you have employees; bonding for home cleaning trust
- Supplies: vacuums, microfibre cloths, mops, eco-friendly products, caddies, PPE
- Transport
- Simple contracts and service checklists

## Step 3: Startup costs

Home-based residential cleaning can often start with a few hundred to a few thousand dollars (supplies, insurance, registration, website); commercial cleaning with larger equipment costs more.

## Step 4: Pricing

- Hourly, per room, flat-rate by home size, or per square foot (commercial)
- Price deep cleans and move-outs higher
- Recurring discounts for weekly/biweekly clients
- Include travel, supplies, labour and profit margin

## Step 5: Systems

- Cleaning checklists per service
- **Booking and scheduling software** with reminders (see [How to make a booking website](/blog/how-to-make-a-booking-website/))
- **Invoicing and online payments** (see [How to send an invoice](/blog/how-to-send-an-invoice/))
- Quality checks and customer feedback
- Field service apps for teams (see [What is field service management software?](/blog/what-is-field-service-management-software/))

## Step 6: Get your first clients

Google Business Profile, a simple website with instant quotes, local Facebook groups and Nextdoor, referrals from real estate agents and property managers, and flyers in target neighbourhoods (see [How to get clients for a cleaning business](/blog/how-to-get-clients-for-a-cleaning-business/)).

## Step 7: Hire and grow

Hire reliable cleaners, train with checklists, run background checks, and focus on recurring clients and commercial contracts for stable revenue.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start my own cleaning business?
Choose residential or commercial cleaning, register the business, get required licences and insurance, buy supplies, set prices, create checklists, set up booking and invoicing, and market locally.

### What do I need to start a cleaning business?
Business registration, any local licences, liability insurance (and bonding for homes), cleaning supplies, transport, contracts, checklists and a way to book and invoice clients.

### How much does it cost to start a cleaning business?
Residential cleaning can start with a few hundred to a few thousand dollars; commercial cleaning usually needs more equipment and insurance.

### Is a cleaning business profitable?
It can be, especially with recurring clients, efficient routes, good pricing and reliable staff.

### How do I start a house cleaning business from home?
Register the business, get insurance, buy supplies, set up online booking and start with referrals, local groups and Google Business Profile.

### How do I start a contract cleaning business?
Target offices and facilities, get commercial insurance and bonding, prepare bids and proposals, and build relationships with property and facility managers.
