<p class="lede">Managing a small law firm well means running it like a business with <strong>reliable systems</strong>: a consistent <strong>intake and conflict check</strong>, <strong>matter management</strong> with deadlines and tasks, organised <strong>documents</strong>, accurate <strong>time tracking, billing and trust accounting</strong>, proactive <strong>client communication</strong>, clear <strong>staff roles</strong>, and <strong>weekly review of key numbers</strong>. Practice management software and automation remove much of the admin burden.</p>

## Choosing practice management software

Compare features (matters, calendar, billing, trust, documents, intake, client portal, integrations), security, ease of use, support and price. When reading **reviews**, look for firms of similar size and practice area, and test with a trial on real workflows. (Options: [Law practice management software](/blog/law-practice-management-software/), [Build vs buy legal software](/blog/build-vs-buy-legal-software/).)

## Staffing and delegation

- Define roles: attorney, paralegal, legal assistant, intake/reception, bookkeeping
- Document processes with **checklists and SOPs**
- Delegate routine tasks; review quality
- Weekly team meetings on matters and priorities

## Client communication standards

Respond within a set time (e.g., one business day), send proactive status updates, explain fees and timelines clearly, and use secure portals.

## Weekly management routine

1. Review upcoming deadlines and court dates
2. Matter status and bottlenecks
3. New leads and consultations
4. Billing, collections and trust balances
5. Cash flow and key metrics (see [How to grow a law firm](/blog/how-to-grow-a-law-firm/))

## Automate repetitive workflows

Intake forms to CRM, engagement letters with e-signature, document generation, deadline reminders, invoice reminders and client updates (see [Legal workflow automation](/blog/legal-workflow-automation/), [Legal document automation](/blog/legal-document-automation/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you manage a small law firm?
Implement consistent systems for intake, matters, deadlines, documents, billing, trust accounting and communication, define staff roles and review key numbers weekly.

### How do you run a law firm efficiently?
Use practice management software, standardise workflows with checklists, delegate, automate repetitive tasks and monitor KPIs.

### What software does a small law firm need?
Practice management with calendaring, billing and trust accounting, document management, intake/CRM and secure communication tools.

### How do I choose law practice management software from reviews?
Prioritise reviews from firms of similar size and practice area, check support and security, and trial it with real workflows.

### What are the biggest challenges of running a small law firm?
Balancing legal work with business management, cash flow, consistent client acquisition and administrative workload.

### Can small law firms use automation?
Yes. Intake, documents, reminders, billing and client updates are common, affordable automation wins.
