<p class="lede"><strong>Account-based marketing (ABM)</strong> is a <strong>B2B strategy</strong> where marketing and sales <strong>choose a specific list of high-value target companies (accounts)</strong> and run <strong>personalised campaigns</strong> to engage the key decision-makers at each one. Instead of attracting as many leads as possible and filtering them, ABM <strong>starts with the accounts you most want to win</strong> and focuses resources on them, turning the traditional marketing funnel upside down.</p>

## How ABM works

1. **Align sales and marketing** on goals and target accounts
2. **Define the ideal customer profile (ICP):** industry, size, revenue, tech stack, triggers
3. **Select target accounts** (from CRM data, intent data, fit scoring)
4. **Map buying committees:** decision-makers, influencers, users, finance, IT
5. **Research each account's** priorities and pain points
6. **Create personalised content and offers**
7. **Orchestrate multi-channel engagement:** ads, email, LinkedIn, direct mail, events, sales outreach
8. **Measure account engagement** and pipeline
9. **Optimise and expand** within won accounts

## Types of ABM

| Type | Accounts | Personalisation | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **One-to-one (strategic ABM)** | A handful of very high-value accounts | Fully bespoke | Custom research report and executive event for one enterprise |
| **One-to-few (ABM lite)** | Clusters of 5–50 similar accounts | By industry or shared challenge | Campaign for regional banks facing the same regulation |
| **One-to-many (programmatic ABM)** | Hundreds or thousands | Light, data-driven, automated | Targeted LinkedIn ads and personalised website messaging by industry |

## ABM vs inbound and traditional lead generation

| | Traditional lead gen | Inbound marketing | ABM |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Starts with** | Broad audience | Content attracting searchers | Named target accounts |
| **Measures** | Lead volume | Traffic, leads | Account engagement, pipeline, revenue |
| **Personalisation** | Low | Medium | High |
| **Best for** | High-volume, low-price sales | Educated buyers searching solutions | High-value, complex B2B deals |

They work well together: inbound attracts and educates, ABM concentrates on key accounts. (See [What is inbound marketing?](/blog/what-is-inbound-marketing/))

## ABM tactics

- **Personalised landing pages and microsites** per account or industry (see [What is a microsite?](/blog/what-is-a-microsite/))
- **Targeted ads** on LinkedIn and programmatic platforms
- **Personalised email and LinkedIn outreach** from sales (see [What is sales prospecting?](/blog/what-is-sales-prospecting/))
- **Account-specific content:** audits, ROI analyses, industry reports
- **Direct mail and gifting**
- **Executive events, roundtables and webinars**
- **Website personalisation** by company (reverse IP)
- **Customer marketing** for expansion and upsell

## ABM example

A cybersecurity company targets 30 mid-sized hospitals. Marketing builds a "hospital ransomware readiness" report, runs LinkedIn ads to CIOs and IT directors at those hospitals, sends a personalised video audit to each, and invites them to a regional roundtable. Sales follows up with tailored proposals. Success is measured by meetings and pipeline from the 30 accounts.

## ABM metrics

- Account coverage (contacts engaged per account)
- Account engagement score
- Meetings and opportunities from target accounts
- Pipeline and revenue influenced
- Deal size and win rate vs non-ABM
- Sales cycle length

## AI and ABM

AI makes ABM scalable: researching accounts, summarising news and filings, scoring intent, drafting personalised messages and content variations, and recommending next best actions. Keep humans reviewing messaging for accuracy and tone. (See [What is agentic marketing?](/blog/what-is-agentic-marketing/))

## Frequently asked questions

### What is account-based marketing?
A B2B strategy where marketing and sales target a defined list of high-value accounts with personalised campaigns aimed at their buying committees.

### What are the three types of ABM?
One-to-one (strategic), one-to-few (clusters of similar accounts) and one-to-many (programmatic, scaled with technology).

### What is the difference between ABM and inbound marketing?
Inbound attracts prospects through content and search; ABM proactively targets specific named accounts with personalised engagement.

### Is ABM only for large companies?
No. Smaller B2B companies can run ABM on a few dozen accounts with LinkedIn, personalised outreach and simple tools.

### How do you measure ABM success?
Through account engagement, meetings, pipeline, revenue, deal size, win rate and sales cycle length for target accounts.

### What tools do you need for ABM?
A CRM, data/enrichment tools, ad platforms like LinkedIn, and optionally ABM platforms, intent data and personalisation tools.
