<p class="lede">A <strong>sellable agency</strong> is one that keeps winning, delivering and retaining clients <strong>without depending on the founder</strong>. To build one: focus on a <strong>niche</strong>, sell <strong>productised, recurring services</strong>, keep <strong>client concentration low</strong>, document everything in <strong>systems and SOPs</strong>, build a <strong>leadership team</strong> that owns sales and delivery, maintain <strong>healthy margins and clean financials</strong>, and use <strong>automation</strong> to make delivery consistent. Buyers pay for predictable, transferable profit.</p>

## What buyers look for

| Factor | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Recurring revenue** | Retainers and subscriptions are predictable |
| **Profit margins** | Healthy net margins after market-rate founder salary |
| **Client diversification** | No client is a huge share of revenue |
| **Retention** | Long average client lifetime |
| **Founder independence** | Team runs sales, delivery and client relationships |
| **Systems and SOPs** | Repeatable, trainable processes |
| **Niche positioning** | Clear market, defensible expertise |
| **Clean financials** | Accurate books, contracts, IP ownership |
| **Growth trend** | Consistent growth and pipeline |

## How to build it

1. **Niche down:** e.g., "Google Ads for dental clinics" or "automation for law firms" (see [How to grow an AI automation agency](/blog/how-to-grow-an-ai-automation-agency/))
2. **Productise services:** fixed scopes, deliverables, pricing tiers
3. **Shift to retainers:** monthly management, reporting, optimisation
4. **Document SOPs** for sales, onboarding, delivery, reporting and offboarding
5. **Hire leaders:** account managers, delivery leads, a sales owner
6. **Remove yourself from delivery**, then from sales
7. **Automate:** onboarding, reporting, task creation, client updates (see [How to make an SEO report for a client](/blog/how-to-make-an-seo-report-for-a-client/))
8. **Track metrics:** MRR, churn, gross margin, utilisation, CAC, LTV (see [What is LTV?](/blog/what-is-ltv/))
9. **Contracts:** written agreements, clear IP and data terms
10. **Plan the exit early:** clean books and a 2–3 year track record

## How agencies are valued (roughly)

Agencies are often valued on a **multiple of adjusted profit (EBITDA/SDE)**, with higher multiples for recurring revenue, growth, niche specialisation and low founder dependence; deals often include earn-outs.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I build a sellable agency business?
Niche down, productise services, build recurring revenue, document SOPs, hire leaders so the business runs without you, diversify clients, keep margins healthy and clean up financials.

### What makes an agency valuable to buyers?
Predictable recurring profit, low client concentration, high retention, founder independence, strong systems and a clear niche.

### How are agencies valued?
Commonly as a multiple of adjusted profit, adjusted for recurring revenue, growth, risk and founder dependence.

### Can a one-person agency be sold?
It's difficult; buyers want businesses that run without the founder, so build a team and systems first.

### What are productised services?
Standardised service packages with fixed scope, process and price, making delivery repeatable and scalable.

### How long does it take to make an agency sellable?
Often two to three years of building recurring revenue, systems and a team.
