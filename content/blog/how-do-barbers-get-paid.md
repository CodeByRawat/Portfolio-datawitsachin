<p class="lede">Barbers usually get paid in one of four ways: <strong>commission</strong> (a percentage of each service), <strong>booth or chair rent</strong> (pay the shop rent and keep everything else), <strong>hourly or salary plus commission</strong>, or as a <strong>shop owner</strong>. On top of that, most barbers earn <strong>tips</strong> and sometimes <strong>commission on product sales</strong>. Booth renters are typically self-employed and handle their own taxes.</p>

## The main pay models

| Model | How it works | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Commission** | Barber keeps a percentage of service revenue | New barbers building clientele |
| **Booth/chair rent** | Fixed rent, barber keeps all service revenue | Barbers with a full book |
| **Hourly/salary + commission** | Guaranteed base plus a share | Chains and franchise shops |
| **Ownership** | Profit from the business | Experienced barbers ready to manage |

## Tips

Tips are a significant part of barber income. Card and app tipping at checkout makes tipping easier and often increases totals.

## Employee vs independent contractor

Commission and hourly barbers are often employees (taxes withheld, shop sets schedule). Booth renters are usually independent contractors who set their own prices and hours and pay self-employment taxes. Classification rules vary by state and country.

## Taxes

Self-employed barbers should track income and expenses, set aside money for taxes and make estimated payments where required.

## Choosing the best model

Commission offers stability and support while building clients; booth rent pays more once you're consistently busy. Owning a shop offers the highest upside with the most risk (see [How much does a barbershop owner make?](/blog/how-much-does-a-barbershop-owner-make/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do barbers get paid?
Through commission, booth rent, hourly or salary plus commission, or shop ownership, plus tips.

### Do barbers get paid hourly?
Some do, especially at chains; many are paid commission or rent a booth.

### What is a typical barber commission split?
Splits vary by shop and experience; check local norms and what the shop provides.

### Do booth renters pay their own taxes?
Usually yes, as independent contractors.

### Do barbers get tips?
Yes, tips are a meaningful part of most barbers' income.

### Which pays more, booth rent or commission?
Booth rent usually pays more for busy barbers; commission is safer while building a client base.
