<p class="lede">To start a moving company: choose your <strong>services</strong> (local residential, long-distance, labour-only, commercial/office, specialty items), register the <strong>business</strong> and get required <strong>licences</strong> (state moving permits; for interstate moves in the US, a USDOT number and MC authority), buy <strong>insurance</strong> (liability, cargo, auto, workers' comp), get <strong>trucks and equipment</strong>, hire and train <strong>movers</strong>, set <strong>pricing</strong> and an <strong>online quote and booking</strong> process, and market with <strong>Google Business Profile, reviews and real estate partnerships</strong>.</p>

## Steps

1. **Services:** start local or labour-only to reduce startup costs
2. **Business setup:** entity, bank account, contracts and bill of lading templates
3. **Licences:** state/local moving licences; interstate authority where required
4. **Insurance:** general liability, cargo, commercial auto, workers' comp
5. **Equipment:** truck(s) (lease or buy), dollies, pads, straps, ramps, tools
6. **Team:** reliable movers, training on lifting safety and furniture protection
7. **Pricing:** hourly for local, weight/distance for long-distance (see [How much does a moving company cost?](/blog/how-much-does-a-moving-company-cost/))
8. **Quotes and booking:** online inventory forms, video surveys, deposits, reminders (see [How to make a booking website](/blog/how-to-make-a-booking-website/))
9. **Marketing:** Google Business Profile, local SEO, reviews, real estate agents, property managers, storage facilities
10. **Grow:** add trucks, packing services, storage and commercial moves

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a moving company?
Choose services, register the business, get licences and insurance, acquire trucks and equipment, hire movers, set pricing, offer online quotes and market locally.

### Do I need a licence to start a moving company?
Usually; requirements vary by state or country, and interstate movers in the US need a USDOT number and operating authority.

### How much does it cost to start a moving business?
Labour-only services can start cheaply; owning trucks, insurance and staff increases costs significantly.

### How do moving companies get customers?
Google searches and reviews, real estate agents, property managers, storage facilities and referrals.

### Is a moving company profitable?
It can be with good pricing, efficient crews, strong reviews and steady demand, especially in peak season.

### What is labour-only moving?
Movers load or unload a customer's rental truck or container without providing transport.
