<p class="lede">To make an invoice template for free, open <strong>Word, Excel, Google Docs or Google Sheets</strong> (or start from their built-in invoice templates), add your <strong>logo and business details</strong>, fields for <strong>client details, invoice number and dates</strong>, a <strong>line-item table</strong> with quantity, rate and amount (using formulas in a spreadsheet), <strong>subtotal, tax and total</strong>, and <strong>payment terms and methods</strong>. Save it as a reusable template, then for each invoice fill it in, update the number, and <strong>export as PDF</strong>.</p>

## Fields to include

- Logo and business name, address, contact, tax ID (if applicable)
- "Invoice" title
- Invoice number, invoice date, due date
- Client name, company, billing address, PO number
- Line items: description, quantity, rate, amount
- Subtotal, discount, tax, total due
- Payment terms and instructions
- Notes/thank-you

(Reference layout: [What does an invoice look like?](/blog/what-does-an-invoice-look-like/))

## Option 1: Google Sheets or Excel (best for calculations)

1. **File › New › From template** and search "invoice", or start blank
2. Create header rows with your details and invoice info
3. Make a table: Description | Qty | Rate | Amount
4. In the Amount column: `=B10*C10` (copy down)
5. Subtotal: `=SUM(D10:D20)`
6. Tax: `=D21*0.18` (replace with your tax rate, or 0)
7. Total: `=D21+D22`
8. Format currency and add borders
9. Save as **"Invoice template"**; duplicate it for each new invoice
10. **File › Download › PDF** to send

## Option 2: Word or Google Docs (best for design)

1. Use a built-in invoice template or insert a table
2. Add your logo and details
3. Type line items and totals (calculate carefully or use Word table formulas)
4. Save as a **template (.dotx)** in Word or "Make a copy" in Google Docs
5. Export as **PDF**

## How to type up an invoice (quick steps)

1. Open your template copy
2. Update the **invoice number** (next in sequence; see [What is an invoice number?](/blog/what-is-an-invoice-number/))
3. Enter today's date and due date
4. Fill in client details and PO
5. Add line items and check totals
6. Save as PDF named like "INV-1045_ClientName.pdf"
7. Send it (see [How to send an invoice](/blog/how-to-send-an-invoice/))

## Free invoice generators and apps

Many invoicing tools offer free plans or free generators: Zoho Invoice, Wave, Invoice Simple-style apps, PayPal and Square invoices, Stripe for payment-linked invoices. Free plans may have limits on clients, branding or features.

## When to switch from templates to software

| Templates work when | Switch to software when |
|---|---|
| You send a few invoices a month | You invoice weekly or more |
| Simple pricing | Recurring invoices, multiple currencies or taxes |
| Payment by bank transfer | You want online card payments |
| No need for tracking | You need reminders, paid/unpaid tracking, reports |
| Manual bookkeeping is fine | You want sync with accounting |

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I make an invoice template?
Create a document or spreadsheet with your business details, client fields, invoice number and dates, an itemised table, totals, taxes and payment terms, then save it as a reusable template.

### How can I make invoices for free?
Use Google Docs/Sheets or Word/Excel templates, or free plans of invoicing apps such as Zoho Invoice or Wave.

### How do I make a simple invoice?
Include your details, client details, invoice number, date, items with prices, total due and how to pay, then save as PDF and send.

### What is the best free invoice template?
Built-in templates in Google Sheets, Excel or Word are good starting points; spreadsheets are best for automatic calculations.

### How do I write a billing invoice for payment?
List what was delivered with prices, show the total due and due date, add payment instructions and send it to the client's billing contact.

### Should I use a template or invoicing software?
Templates suit occasional invoices; software saves time with numbering, online payments, reminders and accounting sync.
