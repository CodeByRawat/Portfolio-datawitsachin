<p class="lede">An <strong>HVAC business</strong> installs, repairs and maintains <strong>heating, ventilation and air conditioning</strong> systems such as furnaces, heat pumps, air conditioners, boilers, ductwork, thermostats and indoor air quality equipment. HVAC companies serve <strong>homes, businesses or new construction</strong> and earn money from <strong>service calls, repairs, system replacements, installations and maintenance agreements</strong>.</p>

## Typical HVAC services

- Furnace, heat pump and AC repair
- System replacement and new installation
- Seasonal tune-ups and maintenance
- Ductwork installation, sealing and cleaning
- Thermostats and smart controls
- Indoor air quality (filters, purifiers, humidifiers)
- Commercial rooftop units and refrigeration

## Types of HVAC companies

| Type | Focus |
|---|---|
| Residential service and replacement | Homeowners; repairs and new systems |
| Commercial service | Offices, retail, restaurants, rooftop units |
| New construction | Builders and contractors |
| Specialty | Refrigeration, controls, geothermal, ductwork |

## How HVAC companies make money

Diagnostic and repair fees, system replacements (the largest tickets), maintenance agreements (recurring revenue), new construction contracts and add-on products.

## Is HVAC a good business?

Demand is steady because heating and cooling are essential, and systems need regular maintenance and eventual replacement. Success depends on technician talent, pricing and marketing (see [How to start an HVAC business](/blog/how-to-start-an-hvac-business/) and [How to run an HVAC business](/blog/how-to-run-an-hvac-business/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is an HVAC business?
A company that installs, repairs and maintains heating, ventilation and air conditioning systems.

### What does HVAC stand for?
Heating, ventilation and air conditioning.

### How do HVAC companies make money?
From repairs, system replacements, installations and recurring maintenance agreements.

### Is an HVAC business profitable?
It can be, particularly with strong replacement sales and a large maintenance agreement base.

### What is the difference between residential and commercial HVAC?
Residential serves homes; commercial serves larger buildings with more complex systems.

### Do HVAC businesses need a licence?
Most states require HVAC or mechanical contractor licences, and EPA 608 certification is needed for refrigerants.
