<p class="lede">In most of the US, <strong>dog groomers don't need a state grooming licence</strong>, but a few states and many cities regulate pet businesses. You'll usually need a <strong>general business licence</strong>, possibly a <strong>local pet-facility or kennel permit</strong>, <strong>zoning approval</strong> for home or shop grooming, and <strong>insurance</strong>. Certification is <strong>optional</strong> but builds trust.</p>

## Are dog groomers regulated?

Grooming is lightly regulated compared with veterinary work. Rules usually come from:

- **State law** in a small number of states that license or inspect pet grooming facilities
- **City or county** animal-facility permits and inspections
- **Zoning rules** for home-based or mobile businesses
- **Health and safety** rules on sanitation, drying equipment and waste

## Licences and permits you may need

| Item | When |
|---|---|
| Business registration | Always |
| General business licence | Most cities |
| Pet facility / kennel permit | Some cities and states |
| Home occupation permit | Grooming from home |
| Vehicle permits | Mobile grooming vans |
| Sales tax permit | If selling products or in taxable states |

## Do you have to be certified to groom dogs?

Usually not. Certification from grooming schools or associations is voluntary but shows skill and safety training, which helps you charge more and win clients' trust.

## Insurance for groomers

Get **general liability**, **care, custody and control (animal bailee) coverage** for pets in your care, **commercial auto** for mobile vans, and **workers' comp** when you hire.

## How to check your rules

Call your city licensing office and county animal services, check your state's department of agriculture, and confirm zoning before setting up at home.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do pet groomers need a license?
Most states don't require a groomer licence, but local business licences and facility permits often apply.

### Are dog groomers required to be licensed?
Only in some states and localities; check state and city rules.

### Do you have to be certified to groom dogs?
No, certification is optional but recommended.

### How much is a dog grooming license?
Local business licence and permit fees vary by city and county.

### Do I need insurance to groom dogs?
Yes; liability and animal bailee coverage protect you if a pet is injured.

### Can I groom dogs without a license at home?
You may still need a business licence and home occupation permit, so check local rules.
