<p class="lede">To bid a concrete job, <strong>measure the area</strong>, calculate <strong>cubic yards</strong> (length × width × thickness ÷ 27, plus waste), add <strong>materials, labour, equipment and disposal</strong>, then add <strong>overhead and profit</strong>. Check your total against a <strong>price per square foot</strong> for similar local jobs.</p>

## Step 1: Measure and inspect

Measure length, width and thickness. Note access for the concrete truck, slope, existing concrete to remove, soil condition and drainage.

## Step 2: Calculate concrete

**Cubic yards = (length ft × width ft × thickness in ÷ 12) ÷ 27**

Example: a 20 × 24 ft driveway at 4 inches = 160 cubic feet ÷ 27 ≈ 5.9 yards. Add 5–10% waste, so order about 6.5 yards.

## Step 3: List all costs

| Cost | Examples |
|---|---|
| Concrete | Price per yard, short-load fees, additives |
| Base | Gravel, compaction |
| Reinforcement | Rebar, mesh, fibre |
| Forms | Lumber, stakes |
| Labour | Crew hours × wage including taxes |
| Equipment | Pump, skid steer, saw, compactor |
| Demolition and disposal | Tear-out and dump fees |
| Finishes | Stamping, colour, sealer |
| Permits | If required |

## Step 4: Add overhead and profit

Overhead covers insurance, trucks, office and marketing. Many contractors add 10%–20% overhead plus 10%–20% profit, but use your own numbers.

## Step 5: Sanity-check per square foot

Compare your total to local per-square-foot prices for similar jobs. Decorative work, tear-outs and difficult access cost more.

## Residential vs commercial bids

| | Residential | Commercial |
|---|---|---|
| Format | Simple written quote | Formal bid based on plans and specs |
| Payment | Deposit + final | Progress billing, retainage |
| Requirements | License, insurance | Bonds, safety plans, certified payroll sometimes |

## What to put in your bid

Scope of work, thickness and reinforcement, finish, removal, timeline, exclusions, payment terms and warranty.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you bid a concrete job?
Calculate yards and all costs, then add overhead and profit and check against local per-square-foot prices.

### How do I calculate concrete for a bid?
Length × width × thickness (in feet) ÷ 27 = cubic yards, plus 5–10% waste.

### How do you bid commercial concrete work?
Do a takeoff from the plans, price to the specs, and include bonds and progress billing terms.

### What should a concrete bid include?
Scope, specs, finish, timeline, exclusions, payment terms and warranty.

### Should I charge per square foot?
It's useful for quoting and checking, but always build your price from actual costs.
