<p class="lede">Running a laundromat well means keeping the store <strong>clean, safe and fully working</strong>, managing <strong>attendants and hours</strong>, staying on top of <strong>preventive maintenance</strong>, watching <strong>utility costs</strong>, setting <strong>competitive prices</strong>, and growing revenue with <strong>wash-and-fold, delivery and commercial accounts</strong>.</p>

## Daily operations

- Open/close and inspect machines
- Clean floors, lint traps, folding tables and restrooms
- Restock soap and vending
- Help customers and handle refunds
- Process wash-and-fold orders

## Staffing

Attendants improve cleanliness and safety and run wash-and-fold. Some stores are unattended with cameras and remote monitoring.

## Maintenance

| Task | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Lint trap cleaning | Multiple times daily |
| Machine checks | Daily |
| Dryer vent cleaning | Regularly |
| Preventive service | Scheduled |
| Water heater checks | Regularly |

Out-of-order machines lose money and customers.

## Key numbers to track

Revenue per machine, turns per day, utility cost per turn, wash-and-fold pounds, repairs and customer reviews.

## Pricing and payments

Review prices against competitors, use card/app systems for flexible pricing and loyalty rewards.

## Growth

Add pickup and delivery with online booking, sign commercial accounts, extend hours and keep upgrading equipment. Learn more in [How much can a laundromat make?](/blog/how-much-can-a-laundromat-make/) and [How to start a laundromat business](/blog/how-to-start-a-laundromat-business/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you run a laundromat business?
Keep it clean, maintain machines, manage staff and utilities, and add services like wash-and-fold.

### Do laundromats need attendants?
Not always, but attendants improve service and enable wash-and-fold.

### How many hours a week does a laundromat owner work?
It varies; owners with managers and remote monitoring work fewer hours.

### Is running a laundromat profitable?
Yes, with good location, pricing and cost control.

### How do I reduce laundromat utility costs?
Use efficient machines, maintain water heaters and check for leaks.

### How do I market a laundromat?
Google Business Profile, signage, loyalty programs and an online booking site for pickup and delivery.
