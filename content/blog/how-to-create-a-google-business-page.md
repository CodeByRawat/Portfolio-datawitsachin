<p class="lede">To create a Google business page, go to <strong>google.com/business</strong> (or search your business name on Google or Maps and choose "Add your business"), sign in with a Google account, enter your <strong>business name, category, location or service area, phone and website</strong>, then <strong>verify</strong> the business (by video, phone/text, email or postcard, depending on what Google offers). Once verified, complete every section (hours, photos, services, products, description) and collect reviews to rank in local search and Google Maps.</p>

## Who can create a Google Business Profile?

Businesses that have **in-person contact with customers** during stated hours: either at a physical location (shop, clinic, restaurant) or by travelling to customers (service-area businesses like plumbers or cleaners). Purely online businesses without customer contact generally aren't eligible. Follow Google's guidelines for representing your business accurately.

## Step-by-step: create your Google Business Profile

1. **Go to google.com/business** and click **Manage now** (or "Add your business" on Google Maps).
2. **Sign in** with a Google account, ideally a business account you control long-term.
3. **Enter your business name** exactly as used in the real world (no keyword stuffing).
4. **Choose a primary category** (e.g., "Dentist", "Plumber"); you can add secondary categories later.
5. **Location:** add an address if customers visit you; otherwise choose **service areas** and hide your address.
6. **Contact details:** phone number and website URL (see [How to create a website for your business](/blog/how-to-create-a-website-for-your-business/)).
7. **Verify:** follow the method Google offers, commonly a **video recording** showing your location, signage and business tools, or phone, text, email or postcard.
8. **Complete your profile** while verification is pending.

Verification can take from minutes to several days (or longer for video reviews).

## Optimise your profile to rank higher

| Section | Best practice |
|---|---|
| **Categories** | Most specific primary category; relevant secondary categories |
| **Description** | 750 characters: what you do, specialities, location, what makes you different |
| **Hours** | Accurate, including holiday hours |
| **Photos and videos** | Exterior, interior, team, work samples; add regularly |
| **Services/products** | List each with descriptions and prices where possible |
| **Attributes** | Accessibility, women-owned, online appointments, etc. |
| **Booking link** | Connect your booking system |
| **Messaging/chat** | Where available, respond quickly |
| **Posts** | Updates, offers and events (keep active) |
| **Q&A** | Add and answer common questions |
| **Reviews** | Ask every happy customer; reply to all |

## How Google ranks local results

Google says local results are based mainly on **relevance** (how well your profile matches the search), **distance** (how close you are to the searcher) and **prominence** (how well known your business is, including reviews, links and articles). A strong website and consistent citations support prominence (see [What are local citations?](/blog/what-are-local-citations/)).

## Getting more reviews

- Share your **review link** (from the profile's "Ask for reviews" option) via text, email or WhatsApp after each job (see [How to schedule a WhatsApp message](/blog/how-to-schedule-a-whatsapp-message/))
- Put a QR code at reception or on invoices
- Never buy reviews or offer incentives against Google's policies
- Reply professionally to every review, including negative ones

## Connect your profile and website

- Link to the most relevant page (homepage or location page)
- Keep **name, address, phone** consistent between profile and site
- Add **LocalBusiness structured data** and an embedded map on your contact page
- Use UTM parameters on the website link to track profile traffic in analytics

## Common problems

- **Suspended profile:** often due to guideline violations (virtual offices, keyword-stuffed names)
- **Duplicate listings:** request removal or merging
- **Someone else claimed your business:** request ownership through the profile
- **Edits by the public:** check and correct suggested changes

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I create a Google business page?
Visit google.com/business, sign in, enter your business name, category, location or service area, phone and website, then verify the business and complete your profile.

### Is a Google Business Profile free?
Yes. Creating and managing a Google Business Profile is free.

### How long does Google Business Profile verification take?
It varies: instant in some cases, a few days for video or postcard verification, and longer if Google needs further review.

### Can I create a Google Business Profile without an address?
Yes, if you serve customers at their locations. Set service areas and hide your address.

### How do I rank higher on Google Maps?
Complete every section accurately, choose the right categories, add photos, collect and respond to reviews, keep information consistent online and link to a strong website.

### Do I still need a website if I have a Google Business Profile?
Yes. A profile shows basics; a website provides detailed services, trust signals, booking and SEO that support your profile's rankings.
