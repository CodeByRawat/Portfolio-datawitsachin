<p class="lede">Yes, pressure washing is a good business to start for many people: <strong>low startup cost</strong>, <strong>high profit margins</strong> (often <strong>40% to 70%+</strong> on jobs), and steady demand from homes and businesses. Owner-operators often make <strong>$50,000 to $100,000+</strong> a year. The downsides are <strong>physical work, weather and seasonality</strong>, and the risk of damaging property.</p>

These are rough ranges; your area and pricing matter most.

## Pros

- Startup costs from about $3,000
- Low material costs mean high margins
- Results are visible, which makes marketing easy (before-and-after photos)
- Repeat yearly customers and commercial contracts
- Easy to start part-time
- Many upsell services: roofs, gutters, decks, windows, sealing

## Cons

| Downside | How to manage it |
|---|---|
| Seasonal in cold areas | Add Christmas lights, snow removal or gutter cleaning |
| Physical work | Hire help as you grow |
| Damage and liability risk | Learn soft washing, carry insurance |
| Many low-price competitors | Compete on reviews, insurance and quality |
| Weather cancellations | Flexible scheduling |

## How much can a pressure washing business make?

| Stage | Rough yearly revenue |
|---|---|
| Part-time | $10,000 – $40,000 |
| Full-time owner-operator | $60,000 – $150,000 |
| Multi-crew company | $250,000+ |

Profit depends on your prices, travel time and how many jobs you do per day.

## Who it's good for

People who like physical, outdoor work, want a low-cost business, are comfortable selling door-to-door and can learn the chemistry of soft washing.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a pressure washing business profitable?
Yes. Margins on jobs are often 40% to 70%+ because materials are cheap.

### How much can a pressure washing business make?
Full-time owner-operators often bring in $60,000 to $150,000 in revenue.

### Is pressure washing hard?
It's physical, and soft washing needs some training to avoid damage.

### Is the pressure washing market saturated?
There's competition, but insured companies with good reviews still win steady work.

### Can you pressure wash year-round?
In warm areas yes; in cold areas it's mostly a spring-to-fall business.
