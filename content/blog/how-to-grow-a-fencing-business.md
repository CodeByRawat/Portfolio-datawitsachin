<p class="lede">To grow a fencing business, <strong>get more leads</strong> (Google reviews, a website with estimate booking, builder and landscaper partnerships), <strong>close more estimates</strong> with fast, clear quotes, <strong>raise prices</strong> as demand grows, then <strong>add trained crews</strong> and systems so you're not doing every job yourself.</p>

## 1. Get more leads

- Google Business Profile with weekly photos and reviews
- A website showing fence styles, service area and online estimate booking
- Yard signs at every finished job
- Builders, landscapers, pool companies and realtors
- HOAs and property managers

## 2. Close more estimates

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Slow quotes | Same-day or next-day quotes |
| Confusing pricing | Clear "good, better, best" options |
| No follow-up | Follow up at 2, 7 and 14 days |
| Price objections | Show quality, warranty and reviews |

## 3. Raise prices

If you're booked weeks ahead, raise prices. Track close rates; if you win nearly every bid, you're too cheap.

## 4. Build crews

Hire a strong lead installer, train with clear standards, and pay crews in a way that rewards quality and speed.

## 5. Add systems

Use estimating and job management software, schedule material deliveries, and keep a job checklist (811 locates, permits, property lines, HOA approval).

## 6. Add services

Automatic gates, ornamental aluminium, commercial and security fencing, fence staining and repairs.

## 7. Track numbers

Leads, close rate, average job size, profit per job, and crew productivity per day.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I grow my fencing business?
Get more leads, quote faster, raise prices and add trained crews.

### How do fence companies get more leads?
Google reviews, a strong website, yard signs and partnerships with builders and landscapers.

### When should I hire another fence crew?
When you're booked out for weeks and can't keep up with estimates.

### How do I close more fence estimates?
Quote fast, offer clear options and follow up.

### What services can fence companies add?
Gates and openers, ornamental fencing, commercial work, staining and repairs.
