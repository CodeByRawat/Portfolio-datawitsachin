<p class="lede">To add a logo to a video, import a <strong>transparent PNG (or vector) logo</strong> into your editor, place it on a <strong>track above the video</strong>, resize and position it (usually a corner), adjust <strong>opacity</strong> if it's a watermark, and set it to last the whole video, or only the intro and outro. For a more professional brand look, use an <strong>animated logo reveal</strong> at the start or end. Every major editor (CapCut, Premiere Pro, iMovie, Canva, DaVinci Resolve) supports this.</p>

## Prepare your logo file

| Format | Use |
|---|---|
| **PNG with transparent background** | Most editors; corner watermarks |
| **SVG / AI / EPS (vector)** | Scaling without quality loss; animation in After Effects |
| **MOV with alpha (ProRes 4444) or WebM with alpha** | Pre-made animated logos over video |
| **JPG** | Avoid: no transparency, a box shows behind the logo |

Make a **white or single-colour version** for dark footage and a dark version for light footage.

## How to add a logo in CapCut (mobile and desktop)

1. Open your project and tap **Overlay › Add overlay** (mobile) or drag the PNG onto a track above the video (desktop).
2. Pinch or drag to **resize and position** it.
3. Drag the overlay's ends to cover the full video or just part.
4. Use **Opacity** to make it subtle (e.g., 60–80%).
5. Optional: add an **In animation** (fade, zoom) for a polished entrance.

## How to add a logo in Adobe Premiere Pro

1. Import the logo (File › Import).
2. Drag it to **V2**, above your footage on V1.
3. Stretch its duration to match the video.
4. In **Effect Controls**, adjust **Position, Scale and Opacity**.
5. To reuse, create a **nested sequence** or a **MOGRT** template, or use an Essential Graphics template.
6. For animated reveals, build in After Effects and bring in via **Dynamic Link** (see [What is Adobe After Effects used for?](/blog/what-is-adobe-after-effects-used-for/)).

## How to add a logo in iMovie (Mac)

1. Drag the PNG logo above the clip in the timeline.
2. Click **Video Overlay Settings** and choose **Picture in Picture**.
3. Resize and move the logo; set the duration.
Note: iMovie has limited control over opacity and transparency handling; for more control use Final Cut Pro or CapCut.

## How to add a logo in Canva

Upload your logo, drag it onto the video page, resize, place it in a corner, set **Transparency**, and apply it to all pages. Brand Kits keep logos ready for every design.

## How to add a logo in DaVinci Resolve

Import the PNG to the Media Pool, drag it onto **V2**, then use **Inspector › Transform** (zoom, position) and **Composite › Opacity**.

## Adding a branding watermark on YouTube

In **YouTube Studio › Customization › Branding › Video watermark**, upload an image (recommended 150×150 px). It appears on your videos as a subscribe button watermark, which you can show at the end, from a custom start time or throughout.

## Where and how big should a logo be?

- **Position:** top-right or bottom-right is most common; avoid areas covered by platform UI (top and bottom in Reels/TikTok)
- **Size:** around 5–10% of the frame width for a watermark
- **Opacity:** 50–80% for watermarks so it doesn't distract
- **Margins:** keep inside title-safe area (about 5% from edges)
- **Duration:** watermark throughout, or logo only in intro/outro for ads

## Logo intro, outro or animated logo?

| Option | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Static corner watermark** | Protecting content, constant brand presence |
| **Logo intro (2–4 s)** | YouTube channels, corporate videos (keep very short on social) |
| **Logo outro with CTA** | Ads and promos: brand + website + action |
| **Animated logo reveal** | Premium brand identity across all videos |

For ads, don't open with a long logo intro: start with the hook and show the logo briefly or at the end.

## Making an animated logo

Common logo animation styles: **reveal through masks, line drawing, shape morphing, particle assembly, 3D rotation, glitch, liquid motion**. They're typically built in After Effects from a vector logo, last **3–8 seconds** and are exported in several versions (with/without background, 16:9 and 9:16, with sound). (Motion design basics: [What are motion graphics?](/blog/what-are-motion-graphics/) and [How to make motion graphics](/blog/how-to-make-motion-graphics/).)

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I add a logo to a video?
Import a transparent PNG logo into your video editor, place it on a layer above the video, resize and position it, adjust opacity and set its duration.

### How do I insert a logo in a video for free?
Use free editors such as CapCut, DaVinci Resolve or Canva's free plan: add the logo as an overlay, then position and resize it.

### What format should my logo be for video?
A PNG with a transparent background for most uses, a vector file for animation, and a video file with an alpha channel for pre-made animated logos.

### Where should I put a watermark on a video?
Usually the top-right or bottom-right corner, small and semi-transparent, away from platform interface elements.

### How do I add an animated logo to my videos?
Create or commission a short logo animation exported with transparency or as an intro/outro clip, then place it at the start or end of your videos.

### How long should a logo intro be?
Two to four seconds for most videos; on social ads, show the logo briefly or at the end rather than delaying the hook.
