<p class="lede">In most US states you <strong>don't need a personal mechanic license</strong>, but your mobile mechanic business usually needs a <strong>local business license</strong>, and some states require <strong>auto repair facility registration</strong> (for example, California's Bureau of Automotive Repair). If you work on A/C you need <strong>EPA 609 certification</strong>. ASE certification is optional but builds trust.</p>

Rules change by state and city, so check with your local licensing office.

## What you may need

| Requirement | Who needs it |
|---|---|
| Business license | Almost every business |
| State auto repair registration | Some states (CA, NY, MI and others have rules) |
| EPA Section 609 certification | Anyone handling A/C refrigerant |
| Sales tax permit | If you sell parts |
| Garage liability insurance | Strongly recommended |
| Commercial auto insurance | For your work vehicle |
| ASE certification | Optional, builds trust |

## Can you open a mechanic shop without a license?

You'll still need a business license, zoning approval and any state repair-shop registration. A shop also needs permits for waste oil and fluids.

## Can you run a mechanic business from home?

You can usually base a mobile business at home for paperwork and storage. Doing repairs in a residential driveway or garage is often against local zoning, especially on customers' cars. Do the work at the customer's location.

## Disposing of fluids

Recycle oil, coolant and batteries properly. Many auto parts stores accept used oil and batteries.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do mobile mechanics need a business license?
Yes, usually a local business license, plus state repair registration in some states.

### Do you need a license to be a mechanic?
Most states don't license individual mechanics, though some require shop registration.

### Do you need a license to open a mechanic shop?
You need a business license, zoning approval and any state repair-shop registration.

### Can I run a mechanic business from home?
You can base a mobile business at home, but repairs at home are often restricted by zoning.

### Do I need ASE certification?
No, but it helps customers trust you.
