<p class="lede">With an esthetician license you can usually open a <strong>skincare studio or day spa</strong> offering <strong>non-medical treatments</strong>. To offer <strong>medical treatments</strong> (injectables, medical lasers), you need a <strong>medical structure</strong>: a physician-owned practice, an <strong>MSO arrangement</strong>, and a <strong>medical director with licensed providers</strong>, depending on your state.</p>

## What estheticians can typically do

- Facials and extractions
- Superficial chemical peels
- Waxing and brow services
- Microdermabrasion (in many states)
- Skincare consultations and product sales

## What usually requires medical licences

- Neurotoxins and fillers
- Medical-grade lasers (in many states)
- Deep peels and medical microneedling (varies)
- Prescription treatments

Scope varies by state, so check your state board.

## Two paths for estheticians

| Path | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| Skincare studio | You own it; non-medical services only |
| Med spa via MSO | You run business operations; physician-owned practice handles medical services |

See [Who can open a medical spa?](/blog/who-can-open-a-medical-spa/).

## Steps to grow into a med spa

1. Build a loyal skincare client base
2. Learn state rules and talk to a healthcare attorney
3. Partner with a medical director and hire licensed injectors
4. Add medical services under the correct structure
5. Market packages and memberships

## Frequently asked questions

### Can an esthetician open a med spa?
Not alone for medical services in most states; a medical director or physician-owned structure is required.

### Can I open a spa with an esthetician license?
Yes, a skincare studio or day spa offering services within your scope.

### Can estheticians do Botox?
No, injectables require licensed medical providers.

### Can estheticians use lasers?
It depends on the state and the device.

### What is an MSO?
A management company owned by non-physicians that supports a physician-owned medical practice.

### How do estheticians add medical services?
By partnering with a medical director and licensed providers under a compliant structure.
