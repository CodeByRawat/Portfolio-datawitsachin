<p class="lede">To start a candle making business, <strong>learn and test your recipe</strong> (wax, wick, fragrance load), create a <strong>brand and packaging</strong>, follow <strong>safety labelling</strong> rules, register the business, set <strong>prices that cover costs and time</strong>, and sell through <strong>markets, Etsy, your own website and wholesale</strong>. You can start from home for <strong>$500 to $3,000</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Learn and test

| Choice | Options |
|---|---|
| Wax | Soy, coconut, paraffin, beeswax, blends |
| Wicks | Cotton, wood; size matters |
| Fragrance | Candle-safe fragrance oils or essential oils |
| Vessels | Jars, tins, ceramics |

Test burn every candle: even melt pool, no tunnelling, safe flame height, good scent throw. Testing is what separates good candle brands.

## Step 2: Safety and labelling

Add warning labels (never leave burning candles unattended, keep away from children and pets, trim wick). Some regions require specific safety labels and allergen information.

## Step 3: Branding and packaging

Pick a niche (luxury, cosy, eco, scents for men, local themes), a name, labels, and gift packaging.

## Step 4: Startup costs

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Wax, wicks, fragrance | $200 – $800 |
| Jars and tins | $100 – $500 |
| Pouring pitcher, melter, thermometer | $50 – $300 |
| Labels and packaging | $100 – $500 |
| Business registration and insurance | $100 – $800 |
| Website or Etsy setup | $0 – $500 |

## Step 5: Pricing

Add materials, packaging, labour and overhead, then multiply for wholesale (often 2×) and retail (often 2× wholesale). Don't underprice.

## Step 6: Sell

- Craft fairs and farmers' markets
- Etsy and your own online store
- Wholesale to boutiques and gift shops
- Custom candles for weddings and businesses

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a candle making business?
Test your candles, build a brand, follow labelling rules, register and sell online and at markets.

### How much does it cost to start a candle business?
Often $500 to $3,000 from home.

### What wax is best for selling candles?
Soy and coconut blends are popular for clean burning and good scent.

### How do I price candles?
Cover materials, labour and overhead, then add wholesale and retail markups.

### Where can I sell candles?
Markets, Etsy, your website and wholesale to shops.
