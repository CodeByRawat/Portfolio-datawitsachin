<p class="lede">A professionally installed garage door opener often costs around <strong>$300 to $800</strong> in total, depending on the <strong>opener type</strong> (chain, belt, screw or wall-mount), <strong>features</strong> (Wi-Fi, battery backup, camera) and <strong>labour</strong>. Labour alone is often <strong>$100 to $300</strong>. Wall-mount (jackshaft) openers and complex installs can cost <strong>$800 to $1,500+</strong>.</p>

These are rough ranges; prices vary by region and brand.

## Cost by opener type

| Type | Notes | Typical installed range |
|---|---|---|
| Chain drive | Strong, louder, budget | Lower |
| Belt drive | Quiet, popular for attached garages | Mid |
| Screw drive | Fewer parts, moderate noise | Mid |
| Wall-mount (jackshaft) | Frees ceiling space, quiet | Higher |

## What affects the price

- Opener type and horsepower
- Smart features (Wi-Fi, app control, camera)
- Battery backup (required in some states for new installs)
- Removing and disposing of the old opener
- Door height (extension rails for tall doors)
- Electrical work (adding an outlet)
- Extra remotes and keypads

## Labour

Most installations take **2 to 4 hours**. Labour is often $100 to $300, more if wiring or extension kits are needed.

## Before installing a new opener

The door must be balanced with working springs. An opener can't fix a bad spring, and a heavy, unbalanced door will wear out a new opener fast.

## DIY vs professional

Many handy homeowners install openers themselves. Hire a pro if springs need work, wiring is needed or you want a warranty on labour. More: [Can you install a garage door yourself?](/blog/can-you-install-a-garage-door-yourself/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How much are garage door openers installed?
Often $300 to $800 total; wall-mount openers can cost more.

### How much is labour to install a garage door opener?
Often $100 to $300.

### Which garage door opener is quietest?
Belt drive and wall-mount openers are usually quietest.

### How long does opener installation take?
Usually 2 to 4 hours.

### Do I need battery backup?
It's useful during outages and required for new installs in some states.
