<p class="lede">The most reliable ways to get real estate leads are your <strong>sphere of influence and referrals</strong>, a <strong>lead-capturing website</strong> (home search, home valuation, neighbourhood guides), a strong <strong>Google Business Profile with reviews</strong>, consistent <strong>local video and social content</strong>, <strong>targeted ads</strong> on Meta and Google, <strong>open houses</strong>, <strong>geographic farming</strong>, and outreach to <strong>expired and FSBO listings</strong> (following local rules). Then <strong>respond fast and follow up automatically</strong>, since most leads aren't ready to transact immediately.</p>

## Free and relationship-based lead sources

1. **Sphere of influence:** past clients, friends, family; stay in touch monthly
2. **Referrals:** ask happy clients and offer thank-you gifts where allowed
3. **Google Business Profile** with reviews (see [How to create a Google Business page](/blog/how-to-create-a-google-business-page/))
4. **Neighbourhood content:** market updates, school guides, "living in" videos
5. **Social media:** Instagram Reels, YouTube tours, TikTok tips
6. **Open houses:** capture visitor details with a sign-in app
7. **Community involvement:** events, sponsorships, local groups
8. **Partnerships:** mortgage brokers, builders, attorneys, movers, divorce and estate attorneys
9. **Geographic farming:** consistent mail and door-knocking in one area
10. **Past client events and annual home value updates**

## Website and online lead generation

11. **Real estate website with IDX listings** and saved searches
12. **Home valuation landing page** ("What's my home worth?") to attract sellers
13. **Neighbourhood and buyer/seller guides** for SEO (see [What is SEO?](/blog/what-is-seo/))
14. **Lead magnets:** first-time buyer guide, relocation guide
15. **Chat/WhatsApp** for quick questions (see [How to create an AI chatbot](/blog/how-to-create-an-ai-chatbot/))

## Paid lead generation

16. **Facebook/Instagram lead ads** with video tours (see [How to advertise for realtors](/blog/how-to-advertise-real-estate/))
17. **Google Ads** for "sell my house [city]" or "homes for sale [neighbourhood]"
18. **Portals** (Zillow, Realtor.com and regional portals) buyer leads
19. **Retargeting** website visitors (see [What is remarketing?](/blog/what-is-remarketing/))
20. **Direct mail** to farm areas and absentee owners

## Seller leads vs buyer leads

| | Buyer leads | Seller (listing) leads |
|---|---|---|
| **Sources** | Portals, IDX search, ads, open houses | Home valuation pages, farming, expired/FSBO, sphere |
| **Value** | Volume, often longer timelines | Listings bring buyer leads and visibility |

(Listings guide: [How to get more real estate listings](/blog/how-to-get-more-real-estate-listings/).)

## Converting leads

Speed matters: respond within minutes, qualify (timeline, budget, financing, motivation), set appointments, and nurture long-term leads with automated email/WhatsApp, new-listing alerts and market updates. (Full guide: [How to convert real estate leads](/blog/how-to-convert-real-estate-leads/).)

## Compliance

Follow do-not-call rules, anti-spam and privacy laws, fair housing advertising rules and your brokerage's policies.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I get real estate leads?
Work your sphere and referrals, build a lead-capturing website with home valuations and search, optimise your Google Business Profile, post local video content, run targeted ads, host open houses and farm a neighbourhood, then follow up quickly.

### What is the best source of real estate leads?
Referrals and repeat clients typically convert best; online leads add volume but need fast, consistent follow-up.

### How do new agents get leads?
Start with their sphere, open houses for other agents' listings, social media content, community involvement and partnerships with lenders.

### How do realtors get buyer leads?
IDX websites, portals, Facebook and Google ads, open houses and social media property content.

### Are paid real estate leads worth it?
They can be if you respond fast, follow up persistently and track cost per closing; quality varies by source.

### How quickly should I respond to real estate leads?
Within minutes if possible; fast responses greatly increase contact and appointment rates.
