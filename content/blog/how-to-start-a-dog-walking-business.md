<p class="lede">To start a dog walking business: <strong>register your business</strong>, get <strong>pet care insurance</strong> (and bonding), define <strong>services and prices</strong> (solo walks, group walks, pet sitting, drop-ins), use <strong>contracts and meet-and-greets</strong>, follow <strong>safety rules</strong> (leads, harnesses, group limits, local regulations), use a <strong>booking app with reminders and walk reports</strong>, and win clients through <strong>neighbourhood marketing, Google, Nextdoor and vet/groomer referrals</strong>.</p>

## Steps

1. **Register the business** and check local licensing (some cities require dog walker permits, especially for parks)
2. **Insurance:** pet care liability, care-custody-control, bonding; check UK/EU-specific cover where relevant
3. **Training:** pet first aid, dog behaviour basics
4. **Services:** 30/60-minute walks, group walks (follow limits), puppy visits, pet sitting, overnight stays
5. **Pricing:** by duration, number of dogs, frequency and location; holiday surcharges
6. **Contracts:** services, cancellations, emergency vet authorisation, key handling
7. **Meet-and-greet** before the first walk
8. **Safety:** secure equipment, no off-lead without permission, heat/cold rules, vaccinations
9. **Booking app** with GPS walk reports, photos, invoicing and reminders (see [How to make a booking website](/blog/how-to-make-a-booking-website/))
10. **Marketing:** Google Business Profile, Nextdoor, flyers at vets and pet shops, reviews, referral discounts

## Starting young (e.g., at 13 in the UK)

Teenagers can start small with neighbours' dogs, with **parental involvement**, clear agreements, appropriate dog sizes and walking only where safe. Formal business registration and insurance may require an adult; check local rules.

## Grow

Hire walkers, add pet sitting and dog daycare (see [How to start a dog boarding business](/blog/how-to-start-a-dog-boarding-business/)), and build recurring weekday routes.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a dog walking business?
Register the business, get insurance, set services and prices, use contracts and meet-and-greets, follow safety rules, use a booking app and market locally.

### Do I need insurance to walk dogs?
Strongly recommended; pet care liability insurance protects you if a dog is injured or causes damage.

### How much should I charge for dog walking?
Base prices on local rates, walk length, number of dogs and frequency; add holiday and short-notice surcharges.

### Do dog walkers need a licence?
Some cities require permits, especially for group walking in parks; check local rules.

### Can a teenager start a dog walking business?
Yes, informally with parental supervision and clear agreements; formal registration may require an adult.

### How do dog walkers find clients?
Neighbourhood flyers, Nextdoor, Google, referrals from vets and groomers, and word of mouth.
