<p class="lede">There are five main ways to get into the Airbnb business: <strong>host your own space</strong>, <strong>buy a rental property</strong>, do <strong>rental arbitrage</strong> (lease and sublet with permission), become a <strong>co-host</strong>, or run an <strong>Airbnb property management</strong> company. Whatever path you pick, check <strong>local short-term rental laws</strong>, run the <strong>numbers</strong>, set up <strong>cleaning and guest systems</strong>, and build <strong>direct bookings</strong> over time.</p>

## 5 ways to start

| Path | Capital needed | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Host your own home or room | Low | Easiest way to learn |
| Buy a property | High | You own the asset and the income |
| Rental arbitrage | Medium | Lease a property and host with landlord permission |
| Co-hosting | Very low | Manage listings for owners for a percentage |
| Property management company | Low–medium | Scale co-hosting into a business |

See [How to start an Airbnb business with no money](/blog/how-to-start-an-airbnb-business-with-no-money/) for the low-capital paths.

## Step 1: Check the rules

Many cities limit short-term rentals with **permits, registration, night caps, occupancy taxes and zoning**. HOAs and leases may ban them. Confirm before you spend money.

## Step 2: Choose a market and property

Look at demand (tourism, business travel, hospitals, universities), seasonality, competition, nightly rates and occupancy, and regulations.

## Step 3: Run the numbers

Estimate revenue (nightly rate × occupancy) minus rent or mortgage, utilities, cleaning, supplies, platform fees, taxes, insurance and maintenance.

## Step 4: Furnish and set up

Comfortable beds, quality linens, fast Wi-Fi, a working kitchen, self check-in (smart lock), good photos and clear house rules.

## Step 5: List, price and operate

Write a strong listing, use dynamic pricing, automate guest messages, schedule cleaners between stays and collect reviews.

## Step 6: Grow

Add properties, build a brand, and take **direct bookings** through your own website to reduce platform fees. Learn more in [How does an Airbnb business work?](/blog/how-does-an-airbnb-business-work/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I get into the Airbnb business?
Host your space, buy a property, do rental arbitrage, co-host or start a management company, after checking local laws.

### How do I start an Airbnb business from scratch?
Choose a market, confirm regulations, run numbers, set up a property, list it and build cleaning and guest systems.

### Is Airbnb a good business to get into?
It can be, in markets with steady demand and friendly regulations, if you manage costs and reviews well.

### Do I need to own a property to start an Airbnb business?
No; arbitrage, co-hosting and property management don't require ownership.

### How much money do I need to start an Airbnb?
It depends on the path; co-hosting needs little, buying needs the most.

### Should I take direct bookings?
Yes, over time; a direct-booking website lowers fees and builds repeat guests.
