<p class="lede">To sell insurance well: get <strong>licensed</strong> and learn your <strong>products</strong> deeply, choose a <strong>niche</strong>, build a steady <strong>prospect pipeline</strong>, run <strong>needs-based conversations</strong> (understand risks and goals before quoting), present <strong>clear options</strong>, handle <strong>objections</strong> honestly, close with a simple next step, and <strong>follow up</strong> consistently. Trust and service, not pressure, drive long-term success and renewals.</p>

## Step 1: Licensing and product knowledge

Get licensed for the lines you'll sell, then learn coverages, exclusions, riders and carrier differences. Clients trust agents who explain clearly.

## Step 2: Pick a niche

Young families (life), seniors (Medicare/health where applicable), homeowners, small businesses, specific professions or communities.

## Step 3: Build a pipeline

Warm market, referrals, partnerships, online leads and events (see [How to get insurance leads](/blog/how-to-get-insurance-leads/)).

## Step 4: Needs-based conversation

1. Build rapport and explain your role
2. Ask about family, assets, income, health, business and goals
3. Identify risks and gaps in current coverage
4. Summarise needs back to the client

## Step 5: Present options

Two or three clear options with coverage, cost and trade-offs; use simple language and visuals.

## Step 6: Handle objections

| Objection | Approach |
|---|---|
| "It's too expensive" | Adjust coverage/deductibles; show the cost of being uninsured |
| "I need to think about it" | Clarify concerns; schedule a specific follow-up |
| "I already have coverage" | Offer a free review to check gaps |
| "I don't trust insurance companies" | Explain claims process and your support role |

## Step 7: Close and follow up

Ask for the decision, complete applications, set expectations, and schedule reviews. Use a CRM and automated reminders (see [How to use a CRM](/blog/how-to-use-a-crm/)).

## Selling health insurance

Understand plan types, networks, deductibles and enrolment periods; follow strict regulatory rules on marketing and enrolment; focus on helping clients compare total costs and doctor networks.

## Is selling insurance hard?

The first year can be tough: building a pipeline and handling rejection. It gets easier with a niche, consistent daily prospecting, referrals and a growing renewal book (see [How do insurance agents make money?](/blog/how-do-insurance-agents-make-money/)).

## Compliance

Follow licensing, suitability, disclosure, advertising and privacy rules; never misrepresent coverage.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I sell insurance?
Get licensed, learn products, choose a niche, build a prospect pipeline, use needs-based conversations, present clear options, handle objections and follow up.

### Is selling insurance hard?
It can be challenging at first, but becomes easier with training, a niche, referrals and consistent prospecting.

### How do I sell health insurance?
Learn plan types and enrolment rules, compare total costs and networks for clients, and follow strict marketing regulations.

### What is the best way to find insurance clients?
Referrals, cross-selling existing clients and partnerships with professionals like real estate agents and mortgage brokers.

### How do insurance agents overcome objections?
By listening, clarifying concerns, adjusting options and explaining value and claims support honestly.

### Do I need a licence to sell insurance?
Yes, in most jurisdictions you need a licence for each line of insurance you sell.
