<p class="lede">To start a solar panel business, pick a model: <strong>solar sales/dealer</strong> (lowest cost, you sell and partner with installers), <strong>full installer</strong> (you design and install systems), or a <strong>niche</strong> like maintenance, cleaning or off-grid systems. Then get <strong>training (such as NABCEP)</strong>, the <strong>licences your state requires</strong> (often electrical or solar contractor), <strong>insurance</strong>, supplier and <strong>financing partners</strong>, and a steady flow of <strong>qualified leads</strong>.</p>

## Choose your business model

| Model | Startup cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Solar sales / dealer | Low | Sell systems, installer partner does the work |
| Residential installer | Medium–high | Full control, needs licensed crew |
| Commercial installer | High | Bigger projects, more expertise |
| Solar maintenance and cleaning | Low | Recurring service work |
| Off-grid and battery storage | Medium | Rural and backup power niche |

## Step 1: Get trained

Learn solar design, electrical basics, roofing and mounting, permits and interconnection, incentives and customer financing. NABCEP certifications are widely respected.

## Step 2: Licences and insurance

Many states require a licensed electrician or a solar/electrical contractor licence for installs, and roofing work may need a roofing licence. Get general liability, workers' comp and commercial auto. Sales-only businesses may need a home improvement salesperson licence in some states.

## Step 3: Suppliers and financing

Open accounts with solar distributors for panels, inverters, batteries and racking. Partner with solar loan and lease providers; most customers finance their systems.

## Step 4: Startup costs

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Training and certification | $1,000 – $5,000 |
| Licences and insurance | $5,000 – $20,000 per year |
| Tools, ladders, safety gear | $5,000 – $20,000 |
| Truck or van | $20,000 – $50,000 |
| Design and CRM software | $100 – $1,000 per month |
| Marketing and lead generation | $2,000 – $20,000+ |

A sales-only model can start for much less.

## Step 5: Get customers

1. A website with savings calculator and quote form
2. Google Business Profile and reviews
3. Referral programs (solar customers love to refer)
4. Roofing and electrical company partnerships
5. Community events and home shows

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a solar panel business?
Choose a model, get trained, get licensed and insured, set up suppliers and financing partners, and generate leads.

### How do I get into the solar energy business?
Start in solar sales or join an installer to learn, then start your own company.

### Do I need a licence to install solar panels?
Many states require an electrical or solar contractor licence; check your state.

### How much does it cost to start a solar business?
From a few thousand for sales-only to $50,000+ for an installer.

### What certification do solar installers need?
NABCEP is the best-known; state licences may also be required.
