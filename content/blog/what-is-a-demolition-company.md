<p class="lede">A demolition company <strong>safely tears down or removes buildings and structures</strong>, from whole houses and commercial buildings to <strong>interior strip-outs</strong> and <strong>selective demolition</strong> for remodels. Demolition contractors handle <strong>permits, utility disconnects, hazardous material checks, heavy equipment, debris hauling and recycling</strong>, and leave a clear site.</p>

## Types of demolition work

| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Residential | Houses, garages, sheds, pools |
| Commercial | Stores, offices, warehouses |
| Interior / selective | Kitchens, bathrooms, office fit-outs |
| Industrial | Plants, tanks, structures |
| Concrete removal | Driveways, slabs, foundations |
| Site clearing | Trees, debris, grading |

## What demolition contractors do

1. **Survey the site** and structure
2. **Check for hazards**: asbestos and lead surveys, abatement before demolition
3. **Get permits** from the city or county
4. **Disconnect utilities**: gas, electric, water, sewer
5. **Plan the method**: machine demolition, hand demolition or selective removal
6. **Demolish safely** with excavators, skid steers and hand tools
7. **Haul debris** and recycle metal, concrete and wood where possible
8. **Clear and grade** the site

## What do demolition workers do?

Demolition workers operate equipment, remove materials by hand, sort debris, set up safety barriers and follow safety plans. See [How to become a demolition worker](/blog/how-to-become-a-demolition-worker/).

## Can a general contractor do demolition?

Often yes, especially interior demolition for their own projects. Larger or hazardous demolition is usually done by specialised demolition contractors, and some areas require a demolition licence or specific permits.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a demolition company?
A contractor that safely tears down structures and clears the site.

### What do demolition contractors do?
Permits, utility disconnects, hazard checks, demolition, debris hauling and site clearing.

### Can a general contractor do demolition?
Often for interior work; larger jobs usually go to demolition specialists.

### Do demolition companies handle asbestos?
They arrange surveys, and licensed abatement contractors remove asbestos before demolition.

### Do demolition companies recycle?
Many recycle metal, concrete and wood.
