<p class="lede">Contractors often charge around <strong>$30 to $60 per square foot</strong> for a pressure-treated wood deck and <strong>$45 to $100+ per square foot</strong> for composite or PVC decks, including materials and labour. Labour alone is often <strong>$15 to $35 per square foot</strong>, and deck builders who charge hourly often bill around <strong>$50 to $100 per hour</strong> per carpenter.</p>

These are rough ranges; prices vary a lot by region and design.

## Cost by material

| Material | Rough installed cost per sq ft |
|---|---|
| Pressure-treated wood | $30 – $60 |
| Cedar or redwood | $40 – $75 |
| Composite | $45 – $90 |
| PVC | $55 – $100+ |
| Exotic hardwood | $60 – $100+ |

## Example

A 12 × 16 ft (192 sq ft) deck:

- Pressure-treated: roughly $6,000 – $11,500
- Composite: roughly $8,500 – $17,000+

## What affects the price

- Size and shape
- Height off the ground (taller decks need more structure)
- Stairs and landings
- Railings (wood, aluminium, cable, glass)
- Multi-level designs
- Removal of an old deck
- Permits and engineering
- Lighting, benches, pergolas
- Site access and soil

## Labour

Labour often makes up 40%–60% of the cost. Complex designs and high decks take more time.

## How deck builders price jobs

Most quote a total price based on materials, labour hours, permits, equipment and profit, often presented as a price per square foot.

## Saving money

Choose a simple rectangle, standard heights, pressure-treated framing with composite boards, and build in the off-season when builders are less busy.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much do contractors charge to build a deck?
Often $30 to $60 per sq ft for wood and $45 to $100+ for composite.

### How much do deck builders charge per hour?
Often $50 to $100 per hour per carpenter.

### What is labour cost to build a deck?
Often $15 to $35 per square foot.

### Is composite decking worth the cost?
Many find it worth it for low maintenance and longer life.

### Does a permit add to the cost?
Yes. Permit fees and inspections add to the total.
