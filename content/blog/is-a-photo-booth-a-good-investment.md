<p class="lede">Yes, a photo booth can be a good investment. A booth costing <strong>$2,000 to $10,000</strong> can often pay for itself in <strong>10 to 25 events</strong>. Busy owner-operators commonly earn <strong>$20,000 to $100,000+</strong> a year, and companies with several booths can earn more. Profit per event is often <strong>$200 to $600</strong> after attendant pay and supplies.</p>

These are rough ranges; your market, pricing and booking volume matter most.

## Profit per event (example)

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| 3-hour package | $600 |
| Attendant pay | −$100 |
| Prints and supplies | −$40 |
| Travel and fuel | −$30 |
| Software, insurance share | −$30 |
| **Profit** | **about $400** |

## Payback example

A $5,000 booth earning $400 profit per event pays back in about **13 events**.

## Yearly income

| Business | Rough yearly revenue |
|---|---|
| Weekend side business (1 booth) | $10,000 – $40,000 |
| Full-time owner (2–3 booths) | $50,000 – $150,000 |
| Multi-booth company | $150,000+ |

## Most profitable booths and add-ons

- 360 video booths (popular and cheap to buy)
- Mirror and premium booths for weddings
- Corporate branded experiences
- Add-ons: extra hours, guest books, custom backdrops

## Risks and costs

Seasonality (busy spring to December), equipment damage, competition and weekend-heavy schedules.

## How to make it more profitable

Sell premium packages, run multiple booths on the same night, target corporate clients, and get on venue preferred vendor lists.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a photo booth a good investment?
Yes. Booths often pay back in 10 to 25 events.

### How profitable is a photo booth business?
Often $200 to $600 profit per event.

### How much can a photo booth business make a year?
From $10,000 part-time to $100,000+ for busy owners.

### What photo booth makes the most money?
360 booths and premium mirror booths often earn the most per booking.

### Is a photo booth business seasonal?
Yes. Weddings and holiday parties drive the busiest months.
