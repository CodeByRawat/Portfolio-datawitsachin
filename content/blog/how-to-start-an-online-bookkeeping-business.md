<p class="lede">An online bookkeeping business serves clients <strong>remotely</strong> using <strong>cloud accounting software</strong>, <strong>secure document portals</strong> and <strong>automated bank feeds</strong>. To start one: build skills, register and insure the business, choose a <strong>niche</strong>, set <strong>monthly packages</strong>, create a <strong>tech stack and automations</strong>, and find clients through <strong>software directories, LinkedIn, referrals and content</strong>.</p>

## Why online bookkeeping works

- Clients anywhere, not just locally
- Cloud software and bank feeds remove paperwork
- Low overhead (work from home)
- Recurring monthly revenue

## Step-by-step

1. **Skills**: bookkeeping basics, software certifications
2. **Business setup**: registration, insurance (professional liability), engagement letters
3. **Niche**: e-commerce, contractors, agencies, restaurants, real estate
4. **Packages**: monthly fee by transaction volume and services
5. **Tech stack**: accounting software, receipt capture, client portal, e-signature, password manager, practice management
6. **Automations**: bank rules, receipt matching, recurring tasks, client reminders, month-end checklists
7. **Marketing**: website, LinkedIn, software partner directories, referrals from accountants

## Automation ideas

| Task | Automation |
|---|---|
| Document collection | Automated reminders and portal uploads |
| Categorisation | Bank rules |
| Receipts | Capture apps that match transactions |
| Month-end close | Checklist templates |
| Reporting | Scheduled report delivery |
| Onboarding | Forms that create tasks automatically |

Automations let you serve more clients without hiring as quickly. Learn general process automation in [How to automate business processes](/blog/how-to-automate-business-processes/).

Use encrypted portals, multi-factor authentication, password managers and clear data policies.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start an online bookkeeping business?
Build skills, register the business, choose a niche, set monthly packages, set up cloud tools and market online.

### Can I do bookkeeping from home?
Yes; most online bookkeepers work remotely from home.

### What software do online bookkeepers use?
Cloud accounting software, receipt capture apps, client portals and practice management tools.

### How do online bookkeepers find clients?
Software partner directories, LinkedIn, referrals and niche content.

### Do I need a certification?
Not legally in most places, but certifications build trust.

### How do I automate bookkeeping work?
Use bank rules, receipt matching, scheduled reports and automated client reminders.
