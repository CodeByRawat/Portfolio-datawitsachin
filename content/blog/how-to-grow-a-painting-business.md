<p class="lede">To grow a painting business: generate <strong>more leads</strong> (Google Business Profile, reviews, SEO, ads, referrals), <strong>close more quotes</strong> with fast professional estimates and follow-up, raise <strong>average job size</strong> with options and upsells, win <strong>commercial and property manager accounts</strong>, and add <strong>crews with strong crew leaders</strong> supported by <strong>systems and software</strong>.</p>

## 1. More leads

Google Business Profile with steady reviews, service and city pages on your website, Local Services Ads, yard signs, Nextdoor, realtors and referral rewards.

## 2. Better close rates

Respond within hours, visit promptly, present good/better/best options, show before/after photos and reviews, and follow up every quote.

## 3. Bigger jobs

Bundle interiors, trim, cabinets, decks and exteriors; offer premium paints and warranties.

## 4. Commercial and repeat clients

Property managers, HOAs, offices and builders provide recurring work.

## 5. Crews and leadership

Hire and train painters, promote crew leaders, document processes and track production.

## 6. Systems

CRM, estimating, scheduling and automated follow-ups and review requests.

## KPIs

Leads per month, close rate, average job size, gross margin per job, crew production rates and review count.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I grow my painting business?
Generate more leads, close more quotes, increase job size, win commercial clients and add crews with systems.

### How do painting companies get more leads?
Google reviews, local SEO, ads, yard signs, referrals and partnerships with realtors.

### How can painters increase their close rate?
Fast responses, professional quotes with options, social proof and consistent follow-up.

### When should a painting company add a crew?
When the schedule is consistently booked weeks out and you have a reliable crew leader.

### Are commercial painting jobs worth it?
They can provide large, repeat work but require bidding and insurance.

### What KPIs should painting businesses track?
Leads, close rate, average job size, margin per job and crew production.
