<p class="lede">To become a medical courier with no experience, you need a <strong>reliable vehicle, clean driving record and background check</strong>, plus training in <strong>HIPAA privacy, bloodborne pathogens and specimen handling</strong> (many companies provide it). Apply to <strong>medical courier companies, labs and hospitals</strong> as an employee or independent contractor.</p>

## What medical couriers do

- Pick up lab specimens from clinics and deliver to labs
- Deliver prescriptions to patients and care homes
- Transport medical equipment, supplies and records
- Keep items at the right temperature
- Follow chain-of-custody and privacy rules
- Run scheduled daily routes and STAT (urgent) deliveries

## Requirements

| Requirement | Notes |
|---|---|
| Driver's license and clean record | Required |
| Reliable vehicle | Often your own car or van |
| Background check and drug test | Common |
| HIPAA training | Protecting patient information |
| OSHA bloodborne pathogens training | For specimens |
| Coolers and temperature control | For specimens and meds |
| Insurance | Commercial auto often required for contractors |

## How to get a medical courier job with no experience

1. Complete HIPAA and bloodborne pathogens courses (many are short and online)
2. Apply to medical courier companies and local delivery companies with medical routes
3. Check labs, hospitals and pharmacies for in-house courier jobs
4. Highlight reliability, punctuality and a clean driving record
5. Start with routes, then pick up STAT work

## Employee vs independent contractor

Employees get steady hours and usually a company vehicle or mileage pay. Contractors use their own vehicle, set more of their schedule, and can later start their own business.

## Starting your own medical courier business

Experienced medical couriers often start their own company and sign contracts with labs, clinics and pharmacies directly. See [How to start a courier business](/blog/how-to-start-a-courier-business/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I become a medical courier with no experience?
Get HIPAA and bloodborne pathogens training, and apply to medical courier companies, labs and hospitals.

### What are medical courier jobs?
Driving specimens, prescriptions, records and equipment between healthcare locations.

### Do medical couriers need certification?
No license is required, but HIPAA and OSHA training are usually expected.

### Do I need my own car to be a medical courier?
For contractor roles, usually yes. Some hospitals provide vehicles.

### Is medical courier a good job?
It offers steady routes and is in demand, especially in areas with many labs and clinics.
