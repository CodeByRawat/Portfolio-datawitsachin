<p class="lede">Several AI tools can <strong>generate videos from text prompts</strong>. The best-known <strong>text-to-video generators</strong> include <strong>Google Veo</strong> (available through Gemini, Google Flow and Google's developer platforms), <strong>OpenAI Sora</strong>, <strong>Runway</strong>, <strong>Kling</strong>, <strong>Luma Dream Machine</strong>, <strong>Pika</strong> and <strong>Adobe Firefly</strong>. A separate category, <strong>AI avatar tools</strong> such as Synthesia and HeyGen, turns a script into a video of a digital presenter. They're powerful for short clips, concepts and B-roll, but brand-accurate ads and product promos still usually need editing, motion graphics and human direction.</p>

The field changes almost monthly: new model versions, features, limits and prices. This guide explains how text-to-video works, the main tool types, what they're good and bad at, how to prompt them, and how businesses use them in real video production.

## How text-to-video AI works

Most text-to-video models are **diffusion-based generative models** trained on large amounts of video and images with descriptions. Given your prompt, the model generates a sequence of frames that match the description and stay (mostly) consistent over time. Newer models also generate **synchronised audio** (sound effects, ambience, even dialogue), accept **reference images** to control style or characters, and allow **image-to-video** (animating a still) and **video-to-video** (restyling footage).

## Why can I only create a few videos a day in Gemini?

Video generation is very expensive to compute, so the Gemini app applies **daily limits on video generations** that depend on your plan (free, AI Pro/Ultra or Workspace tiers) and change over time. When you hit the limit, you'll need to wait for it to reset or use a higher plan or another Google surface such as Flow or the API (which is billed separately). Other tools use credit systems for the same reason.

## What text-to-video AI is good at

- **B-roll and atmosphere:** cityscapes, nature, abstract backgrounds, lifestyle moments
- **Concept and pitch visuals:** testing ideas before a shoot
- **Storyboards and animatics**
- **Short social clips and effects**
- **Stylised visuals** that would be expensive to film
- **Animating stills** (image-to-video) such as product photos or illustrations

## Where it still struggles (especially for brand ads)

- **Exact product accuracy:** logos, packaging text and product details can be distorted
- **Real software UI:** generated screens don't show your actual app
- **Readable on-screen text** inside generated footage
- **Consistency** of characters, products and style across many shots
- **Precise timing** to a script, voiceover or music beat
- **Length:** clips are typically seconds long; longer videos are stitched
- **Brand guidelines:** exact colours, fonts and layouts
- **Rights and policy:** likeness, trademarks and platform rules

That's why most professional AI-assisted promos are **hybrids**: AI-generated B-roll or backgrounds, combined with **real product footage or UI, designed motion graphics and typography**, edited to a script. (See [What are motion graphics?](/blog/what-are-motion-graphics/))

## How to write better text-to-video prompts

Describe the shot like a director:
1. **Subject:** who/what, with specific details
2. **Action:** what happens, in one clear motion
3. **Setting:** place, time of day, weather
4. **Camera:** shot size and movement ("slow dolly in", "aerial tracking shot", "handheld close-up")
5. **Lighting and mood:** "soft morning light", "neon reflections"
6. **Style:** "photorealistic, 35mm film", "3D clay animation", "flat vector animation"
7. **Audio** (if supported): ambience, effects, music mood
8. **Duration and aspect ratio:** 9:16 for social

**Example:** "Close-up of a barista pouring latte art in a sunlit café, slow dolly in, shallow depth of field, warm morning light, photorealistic, gentle café ambience, 9:16."

Generate several variations, keep the best, and use image-to-video with a reference frame for more control.

## Costs

Most tools work on **subscriptions with credits**; longer, higher-resolution or audio-enabled clips use more credits. API access is usually billed per second of generated video. Expect to generate many takes for each usable shot, so budget for iteration.

## Copyright, disclosure and safety

- Check each tool's **commercial use terms**
- Don't generate real people's likenesses, celebrities, or trademarks without rights
- Some platforms require or encourage **AI content labels** for realistic synthetic media
- Many generators add **watermarks or provenance metadata** (such as C2PA content credentials or Google's SynthID); see [Can Google Docs detect AI?](/blog/can-google-docs-detect-ai/) for how SynthID works
- Review for misleading claims in ads

## Frequently asked questions

### Which tool is capable of generating videos from textual prompts?
Text-to-video tools include Google Veo (in Gemini and Flow), OpenAI Sora, Runway, Kling, Luma Dream Machine, Pika and Adobe Firefly. Avatar tools like Synthesia and HeyGen create presenter videos from scripts.

### What is the best AI video generator?
It depends on the job: realistic cinematic clips, stylised animation, avatar presenters or quick social edits. Quality leaders change frequently, so test a few with your own prompts.

### Can AI make a full advert?
AI can generate shots, voiceovers and music, but complete brand ads usually still need human scripting, real product visuals, motion graphics, editing and review for accuracy and policy compliance.

### Why can I only create a few videos on Gemini?
Video generation is computationally expensive, so Gemini applies daily generation limits that depend on your subscription plan.

### Is AI-generated video free?
Some tools offer free trials or limited free generations; serious use generally requires paid plans or credits.

### Can I use AI-generated videos commercially?
Often yes, depending on each tool's terms and your plan. Avoid using real people's likenesses or trademarks without permission, and follow platform disclosure rules.
