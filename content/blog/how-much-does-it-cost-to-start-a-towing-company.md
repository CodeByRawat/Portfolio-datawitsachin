<p class="lede">Starting a towing company usually costs about <strong>$50,000 to $150,000</strong> with one used light-duty truck, and <strong>$150,000 to $400,000+</strong> with a new truck or heavy-duty equipment. The truck and the <strong>insurance</strong> are the biggest costs. You can start for less with roadside assistance only.</p>

These are rough US ranges. Get real quotes in your area before you commit.

## Startup cost breakdown

| Item | Typical range |
|---|---|
| Used light-duty rollback or wrecker | $30,000 – $80,000 |
| New light-duty rollback | $90,000 – $150,000 |
| Heavy-duty wrecker (new) | $250,000 – $700,000+ |
| Insurance (first year, often paid upfront or as a deposit) | $8,000 – $25,000+ per truck |
| Licenses, permits, DOT number, LLC | $500 – $3,000 |
| Equipment (dollies, straps, chains, jump pack, lockout kit, lights) | $3,000 – $10,000 |
| Truck lettering and lights | $1,000 – $5,000 |
| Dispatch software and phone | $50 – $300 per month |
| Website and Google profile setup | $500 – $3,000 |
| Storage yard (if you do impounds) | Rent varies widely by area |
| Working capital (fuel, repairs, 3 months of bills) | $10,000 – $25,000 |

## Sample budgets

| Plan | Total |
|---|---|
| Roadside assistance only (car or pickup + tools) | $5,000 – $20,000 |
| One used rollback, no storage yard | $50,000 – $100,000 |
| New rollback + storage yard | $130,000 – $250,000 |
| Heavy-duty recovery | $300,000+ |

## Ways to lower the cost

- Buy a used truck with service records
- Finance the truck (equipment financing is common in towing)
- Start without a storage yard
- Shop insurance with brokers who specialise in towing
- Begin with motor club and roadside work while you build direct customers

## Ongoing monthly costs

Truck payment, insurance, fuel, maintenance and tires, phone and dispatch software, yard rent and marketing. Track these against your average revenue per call.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does it cost to start a towing company?
Usually $50,000 to $150,000 with one used light-duty truck, more with new or heavy-duty trucks.

### How much is a tow truck?
Used light-duty trucks often cost $30,000 to $80,000; new rollbacks $90,000 to $150,000; heavy wreckers much more.

### How much is tow truck insurance?
Often $8,000 to $25,000+ per truck per year, depending on state, driving record and coverage.

### Can I start a towing business with no money?
Not easily. Start with roadside assistance or drive for another tow company first while you save and build credit for financing.

### Can you finance a tow truck?
Yes. Equipment loans and leases are common, especially with some industry experience.
