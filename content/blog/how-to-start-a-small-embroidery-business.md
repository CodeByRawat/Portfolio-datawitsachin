<p class="lede">To start a small embroidery business, <strong>choose a niche</strong> (custom gifts, team and school wear, business uniforms or online products), buy an <strong>embroidery machine</strong> (a home multi-needle or a commercial single-head machine), get <strong>digitizing software or a digitizer</strong>, source <strong>blanks and supplies</strong>, set your <strong>pricing</strong>, and sell <strong>locally and online</strong>. Many start from home for <strong>$3,000 to $15,000</strong>; commercial setups cost <strong>$15,000 to $50,000+</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Choose your niche

| Niche | Examples |
|---|---|
| Custom gifts | Names on towels, baby items, blankets |
| Teams and schools | Jerseys, caps, jackets |
| Business uniforms | Polos, work shirts, hats with logos |
| Online products | Etsy-style personalised items |
| Patches | Custom patches for brands and clubs |
| Corporate merchandise | Bags, jackets, promotional items |

Business uniforms and teams bring repeat orders.

## Step 2: Choose a machine

| Machine | Rough cost | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Single-needle home machine | $500 – $2,000 | Hobby and testing |
| Home multi-needle (6–10 needles) | $5,000 – $12,000 | Small home business |
| Commercial single-head (12–15 needles) | $10,000 – $20,000 | Serious small business |
| Multi-head commercial | $25,000 – $60,000+ | Bulk orders |

Multi-needle machines save huge time on colour changes.

## Step 3: Digitizing

Designs must be converted to stitch files. Learn digitizing software or outsource digitizing for each logo (often a per-design fee).

## Step 4: Supplies

Blank garments and hats, stabilisers, threads, needles, hoops (including cap frames), scissors and a heat press for patches.

## Step 5: Register your business

Business licence, sales tax permit, and home occupation permit if working from home. Get liability insurance.

## Step 6: Pricing

Price per piece based on stitch count and quantity, plus digitizing fees and the blank item. Set minimums and give bulk discounts.

## Step 7: Find customers

1. Local businesses (uniforms and logo wear)
2. Schools, sports teams and clubs
3. Etsy and your own website
4. Social media with videos of your machine running
5. Events and markets

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a small embroidery business?
Choose a niche, buy a multi-needle machine, learn or outsource digitizing, register the business and find local and online customers.

### How much does it cost to start an embroidery business?
Often $3,000 to $15,000 from home; $15,000 to $50,000+ for commercial setups.

### Can I start an embroidery business from home?
Yes. Many start with a home multi-needle machine.

### What embroidery machine is best for a business?
A multi-needle machine, either home or commercial single-head.

### How do I price embroidery?
By stitch count, quantity and garment, plus digitizing fees.
