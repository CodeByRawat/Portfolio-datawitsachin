<p class="lede">You can start in the Airbnb business with almost no money by <strong>co-hosting</strong> or running <strong>short-term rental property management</strong> for owners, earning a <strong>percentage of booking revenue</strong>. Other low-cost paths include <strong>turnover cleaning</strong>, <strong>renting out a room</strong>, and <strong>partnering with investors</strong>. Rental arbitrage needs some capital for deposits and furniture.</p>

## Low-capital paths

| Path | Startup cost | How you earn |
|---|---|---|
| Co-hosting | Very low | Percentage of revenue |
| STR property management | Low | Management fee |
| Turnover cleaning | Low | Per clean |
| Renting a spare room | Low | Nightly income |
| Investor partnership | Low (sweat equity) | Share of profit |
| Rental arbitrage | Medium | Profit over rent |

## How to land your first co-hosting client

1. Learn the business by studying listings and pricing tools
2. Create a simple service offer (listing setup, pricing, guest messaging, cleaning coordination)
3. Reach out to owners with poor listings, landlords with vacant units, and investors
4. Offer a performance-based fee
5. Deliver results and collect testimonials
6. Build a website showing your results

## What you need

A phone and laptop, pricing and messaging tools, reliable cleaners, a simple management agreement, and knowledge of local rules.

## Grow it

Add owners, standardise systems, hire virtual assistants and cleaners, and register as a business. Read [How does an Airbnb business work?](/blog/how-does-an-airbnb-business-work/) and [How to get into short-term rentals](/blog/how-to-get-into-short-term-rentals/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I start an Airbnb business with no money?
Yes, through co-hosting, property management or cleaning services.

### What is Airbnb co-hosting?
Managing listings for owners in exchange for a share of revenue.

### Does rental arbitrage need money?
Yes, for deposits, first month's rent and furnishing.

### How do co-hosts find property owners?
Outreach to owners and landlords, investor networks, and referrals.

### How much do co-hosts charge?
Usually a percentage of booking revenue, depending on services.

### Do I need a licence to co-host?
Some states require a real estate or property management licence; check local rules.
