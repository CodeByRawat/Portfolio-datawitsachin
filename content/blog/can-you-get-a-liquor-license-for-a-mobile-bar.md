<p class="lede">It depends on your state. Some states offer <strong>caterer's licences, special event permits or mobile licences</strong> that let you sell alcohol at events, while others make it hard. That's why most mobile bars use a <strong>dry-hire model</strong>: the <strong>client buys the alcohol</strong> and the mobile bar provides the <strong>bar, bartenders and service</strong>. Always check your state's alcohol control agency.</p>

This is general information, not legal advice. Laws change and differ by state and county.

## Common ways mobile bars operate

| Model | How it works |
|---|---|
| Dry hire | Client buys alcohol; you serve it |
| Caterer's licence | Licensed to serve alcohol at catered events (where available) |
| Special / temporary event permit | Per-event permission, often for nonprofits or venues |
| Venue's licence | Serving under the venue's licence |
| Brewery/winery partnership | Licensed producers serve at events |

## State differences

States regulate alcohol very differently. Some allow caterer's or mobile permits; others restrict selling alcohol outside licensed premises. Questions like "are mobile bars legal in Texas" or "can you have a mobile bar in Florida" depend on whether you're **selling** alcohol or just **serving** client-supplied alcohol. Check with the state alcohol agency.

## Other requirements

- Alcohol server certification for bartenders
- Liquor liability insurance
- Venue approval and rules
- Health permits if serving food or perishable mixers
- Local business licence

## Tips

- Use clear contracts stating who supplies alcohol
- Check IDs and follow responsible service rules
- Keep insurance certificates ready for venues

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you get a liquor license for a mobile bar?
In some states, through caterer's or special event licences; it varies.

### Can you have a mobile liquor license?
Some states offer mobile or caterer's licences; others don't.

### Are mobile bars legal in Texas?
Mobile bars operate there, often under dry-hire or specific permits; check state rules.

### Can you have a mobile bar in Florida?
Yes, typically under dry-hire or appropriate licences; check state rules.

### What is dry hire?
The client provides the alcohol and you provide the bar and service.
