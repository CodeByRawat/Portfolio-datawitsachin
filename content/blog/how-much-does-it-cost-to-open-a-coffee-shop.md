<p class="lede">Opening a coffee shop can cost from <strong>a modest amount for a coffee cart or kiosk</strong> to <strong>tens of thousands for a small café</strong> and <strong>hundreds of thousands for a full build-out or drive-thru</strong>. The biggest costs are <strong>build-out/renovation, espresso and kitchen equipment, lease deposits and working capital</strong>.</p>

## Cost by format

| Format | Relative cost |
|---|---|
| Coffee cart | Lowest |
| Kiosk | Low–medium |
| Coffee truck | Medium |
| Small café (existing space) | Medium–high |
| Full build-out café | High |
| Drive-thru (new build) | Highest |

## Budget breakdown

- Lease deposit and rent before opening
- Build-out: plumbing, electrical, counters, flooring
- Espresso machine, grinders, brewers, refrigeration, ice machine
- Furniture, décor and signage
- Permits, licences and inspections
- Initial inventory: coffee, milk, food, cups
- POS, loyalty and online ordering
- Hiring and training
- Marketing for opening
- Working capital for the first months

## Ways to open for less

Take over a former café with equipment, lease equipment, start with a cart or kiosk, keep the menu simple, and buy quality used equipment.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does it cost to open a coffee shop?
From a modest amount for a cart to hundreds of thousands for a full build-out or drive-thru.

### How much does it cost to open a small café?
Costs depend on location and condition of the space; reusing an existing café space saves a lot.

### What is the most expensive part of opening a coffee shop?
Build-out and equipment, followed by working capital.

### How much money do you need to open a coffee shop?
Enough to cover setup costs plus several months of operating expenses.

### Can I lease coffee shop equipment?
Yes; leasing espresso machines and equipment reduces upfront costs.

### How can I open a coffee shop cheaply?
Start with a cart or kiosk, take over an existing café, or buy used equipment.
