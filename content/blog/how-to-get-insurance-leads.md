<p class="lede">Insurance agents get the best leads from <strong>referrals and existing clients</strong> (cross-selling and asking for introductions), <strong>partnerships</strong> with professionals who meet buyers at key moments (real estate agents, mortgage brokers, auto dealers, accountants), and <strong>local online visibility</strong>: a quote-ready website, Google Business Profile with reviews, and helpful content. <strong>Paid ads and purchased leads</strong> add volume but require fast follow-up and careful compliance.</p>

## Referral and relationship sources

1. **Ask happy clients for referrals** at renewal and after claims are handled well
2. **Cross-sell** existing clients (auto → home, life, umbrella, business)
3. **Real estate agents and mortgage brokers** (home insurance at purchase)
4. **Auto dealerships**
5. **Accountants and financial planners**
6. **Business associations and chambers** (commercial lines)

## Online lead generation

7. **Website** with quick quote forms, line-specific pages and reviews (see [How to create a website for your business](/blog/how-to-create-a-website-for-your-business/))
8. **Google Business Profile** and review requests (see [How to create a Google Business page](/blog/how-to-create-a-google-business-page/))
9. **Local SEO** for "[insurance type] in [city]"
10. **Educational content and video** answering coverage questions
11. **Social media** for community presence and referrals
12. **Paid search and social ads** (follow insurance advertising rules)

## Other sources

13. **Purchased leads** from lead vendors (exclusive vs shared; respond within minutes)
14. **Community events and seminars** (Medicare, retirement, small business risk)
15. **Niche specialisation** (contractors, restaurants, trucking, seniors)

## Converting leads into clients

- Respond immediately (calls, texts, emails)
- Quote quickly with clear comparisons
- Follow up persistently with a CRM sequence
- Automate renewal reminders and cross-sell campaigns
(See [How to automate your sales process](/blog/how-to-automate-your-sales-process/).)

## Compliance

Follow insurance advertising rules, licensing requirements for each state/region, do-not-call and anti-spam laws, and data privacy.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do insurance agents get clients?
Through referrals, cross-selling, partnerships with real estate agents, mortgage brokers and dealers, local online visibility, content, ads and events.

### How do I generate insurance leads online?
Build a quote-ready website, optimise Google Business Profile and reviews, publish helpful content, use local SEO and run compliant paid ads.

### Are purchased insurance leads worth it?
They can be if you respond fast and follow up consistently; exclusive leads usually convert better than shared leads.

### What is the best source of insurance leads?
Referrals and existing client cross-sells typically convert best and cost least.

### How do I market insurance online?
Website and SEO, Google Business Profile, educational content, social media and targeted ads, within advertising regulations.

### How quickly should I follow up on insurance leads?
Within minutes when possible; speed greatly improves contact and conversion rates.
