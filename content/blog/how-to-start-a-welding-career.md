<p class="lede">To start a welding career, <strong>take a welding program</strong> at a trade school or community college (often <strong>6 to 18 months</strong>) or join an <strong>apprenticeship</strong>, learn the main processes (<strong>stick, MIG, TIG, flux-core</strong>), earn <strong>welding certifications</strong>, and apply for <strong>entry-level welder or helper jobs</strong> in fabrication, construction or manufacturing.</p>

## Step 1: Get training

| Path | Notes |
|---|---|
| Trade school / community college | Structured, 6–18 months |
| Apprenticeship (union or employer) | Paid while learning, 3–5 years |
| On-the-job training | Start as a helper |
| High school vocational programs | Early start |

## Step 2: Learn the processes

| Process | Common uses |
|---|---|
| Stick (SMAW) | Construction, pipe, outdoor repair |
| MIG (GMAW) | Manufacturing, fabrication |
| Flux-core (FCAW) | Structural, outdoor |
| TIG (GTAW) | Precision, stainless, aluminium, aerospace |

Also learn blueprint reading, welding symbols, cutting and safety.

## Step 3: Get certified

Certification tests (often following AWS codes) prove you can make specific welds. Employers may test you on hire. More certs, especially pipe and structural, mean better pay.

## Step 4: Get your first job

Apply to fabrication shops, manufacturers, construction companies, shipyards and pipeline contractors. Be ready for a weld test. Bring your certifications, safety gear and a willingness to work any shift.

## Pay

Entry-level welders often start around **$18 to $25 per hour**, and experienced and specialised welders can earn much more, especially with overtime and travel.

## Career paths

Pipe welder, structural welder, welding inspector (CWI), welding engineer, fabrication shop owner or mobile welder. See [How to start a mobile welding business](/blog/how-to-start-a-mobile-welding-business/) and [How to make money welding](/blog/how-to-make-money-welding/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a welding career?
Train at a welding school or apprenticeship, earn certifications and apply for entry-level welding jobs.

### How long does it take to become a welder?
Often 6 to 18 months of training, longer for apprenticeships.

### Do welders need certification?
Many jobs require passing weld tests or holding certifications.

### Is welding a good career?
Yes. Skilled welders are in demand, and specialisation pays well.

### What welding pays the most?
Pipe, underwater, specialised TIG and travel/shutdown work.
