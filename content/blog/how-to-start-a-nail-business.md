<p class="lede">To start a nail business: get the required <strong>nail technician licence</strong> and business registration, choose a setup (<strong>home studio, mobile, salon suite or storefront nail salon</strong>), buy <strong>professional tools and supplies</strong>, ensure proper <strong>ventilation and sanitation</strong>, set <strong>prices</strong> based on local rates and your costs, build a <strong>portfolio on Instagram and TikTok</strong>, add <strong>online booking</strong>, and win your first clients through friends, referrals and local promotion.</p>

## Step 1: Licensing and rules

Most places require a nail technician (manicurist) licence, plus a business licence and sometimes an establishment licence and inspections. Home-based studios may need zoning approval. Check your local board.

## Step 2: Choose your setup

| Setup | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| **Home studio** | Lowest cost | Zoning, space, perception |
| **Mobile nails** | Serve clients at home/events | Travel time |
| **Salon suite** | Professional, independent | Rent |
| **Booth rental** | Low risk | Less control |
| **Storefront nail salon** | Scalable, walk-ins | Highest cost (see [How much does it cost to open a salon?](/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-open-a-salon/)) |

## Step 3: Equipment and supplies

Nail table, comfortable chairs, LED/UV lamp, e-file, brushes and tools, gels/acrylics/polishes, sterilisation equipment (autoclave or approved disinfectants), disposables, ventilation/dust extraction, pedicure station if offering pedicures.

## Step 4: Safety and sanitation

- Proper **ventilation** for fumes and dust
- **Disinfection and sterilisation** protocols
- Single-use items where required
- Client health checks (allergies, infections)

## Step 5: Pricing

Research local prices, calculate product cost and time per service, and price for profit. Offer clear service menus (gel manicure, acrylic full set, fills, nail art tiers).

## Step 6: Build your brand and portfolio

- Consistent photo style (lighting, background)
- Instagram/TikTok Reels of designs and process
- Highlights for prices, booking, aftercare
- Google Business Profile (see [How to create a Google Business page](/blog/how-to-create-a-google-business-page/))

## Step 7: Online booking and payments

Use booking software with deposits for long appointments and automatic reminders (see [How to make a booking website](/blog/how-to-make-a-booking-website/)).

## Step 8: Get your first clients

Friends and family discounts in exchange for reviews and photos, referral rewards, local collaborations (bridal, photographers, boutiques), and promotion in local groups. (Growth ideas: [How to grow your salon business](/blog/how-to-grow-your-salon-business/).)

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a nail business?
Get licensed, choose a setup, buy professional supplies, ensure ventilation and sanitation, set prices, build an Instagram portfolio, add online booking and promote to your first clients.

### How do I start a nail salon?
Follow the same steps on a larger scale: business plan, location with ventilation and plumbing, equipment, licences, staff, booking software and marketing.

### Can I do nails from home?
Often yes, if local licensing and zoning rules allow it; check your nail board and local regulations.

### How much does it cost to start a nail business?
A home or suite setup can start from a few thousand dollars; a storefront nail salon costs significantly more.

### How do nail techs get clients?
Through Instagram and TikTok portfolios, Google Business Profile, referrals, local collaborations and easy online booking.

### Is a nail business profitable?
It can be, with good pricing, consistent bookings, rebooking and controlled product and rent costs.
