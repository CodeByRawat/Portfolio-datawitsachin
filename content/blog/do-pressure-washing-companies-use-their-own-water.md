<p class="lede">Most pressure washing companies <strong>use the customer's outdoor faucet</strong> for residential jobs. A typical job uses a few hundred gallons, which usually costs the homeowner <strong>only a few dollars</strong>. For commercial sites, new construction or places with no water, many companies bring <strong>their own water tank</strong>.</p>

## How much water does pressure washing use?

A commercial pressure washer uses about **4 to 8 gallons per minute**. Water use depends on job size:

| Job | Rough water use |
|---|---|
| Driveway | 100 – 400 gallons |
| House wash (soft wash) | 100 – 300 gallons |
| Deck or patio | 50 – 200 gallons |
| Large commercial lot | Much more |

At typical US water prices, a residential job usually costs the homeowner a few dollars of water.

## When companies bring their own water

- Commercial lots, gas stations and parking garages
- New construction with no water connection
- Customers with weak water flow or wells
- Vacant properties with water shut off
- Drought restrictions

These companies carry 100–500+ gallon tanks on a truck or trailer, and may charge more for it.

## Tips for pressure washing businesses

- Say in your quote whether you'll use the customer's water
- Check water flow before starting (fill a 5-gallon bucket and time it)
- Bring a buffer tank for low-flow homes
- Follow local rules on wastewater and runoff, especially for commercial work

## Frequently asked questions

### Do pressure washing companies use your water?
For homes, usually yes, from your outdoor faucet.

### How much does it cost me in water?
Usually just a few dollars for a residential job.

### Do I need to be home for pressure washing?
Usually not, as long as the outdoor water is on and gates are open.

### Do pressure washers need hot water?
Not usually. Hot water machines are used for grease and oil.

### What if I don't have an outdoor faucet?
Ask the company to bring a water tank.
