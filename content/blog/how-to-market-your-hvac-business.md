<p class="lede">To market an HVAC business, make it easy for homeowners to <strong>find you, trust you and book you</strong>, especially when their heating or cooling fails. Prioritise a <strong>Google Business Profile with reviews</strong>, <strong>Local Services Ads and search ads</strong> for urgent searches, a <strong>website with online booking</strong>, <strong>seasonal tune-up campaigns</strong>, and <strong>maintenance memberships</strong> that create recurring revenue and repeat customers.</p>

## 15 HVAC marketing strategies

1. **Google Business Profile** with service areas, photos, reviews (see [How to create a Google Business page](/blog/how-to-create-a-google-business-page/))
2. **Local Services Ads** (where available) for "AC repair near me"
3. **Google Search Ads** for emergency and replacement searches
4. **Website** with services, financing, emergency number, online booking (see [How to make a booking website](/blog/how-to-make-a-booking-website/))
5. **Local SEO pages** for each service and town
6. **Seasonal campaigns:** spring AC tune-ups, fall furnace checks
7. **Maintenance plans/memberships:** priority service, discounts, two visits per year
8. **Review requests** after every job
9. **Email/SMS reminders** for maintenance (see [How to schedule a WhatsApp message](/blog/how-to-schedule-a-whatsapp-message/))
10. **Referral programme**
11. **Partnerships:** real estate agents, property managers, home builders
12. **Social media:** tips, before/after installs, technician spotlights
13. **Truck wraps and yard signs**
14. **Financing offers** for system replacements
15. **Fast dispatch and call answering** (24/7 answering service or AI receptionist)

## How to advertise your HVAC business

Combine Google (LSA + search) for demand capture, Meta ads for seasonal offers and memberships, and local channels (mailers, community sponsorships). Track cost per booked job. (See [Are Google Ads worth it?](/blog/are-google-ads-worth-it/).)

## Increase revenue per customer

Maintenance agreements, indoor air quality add-ons, smart thermostats, replacement financing and honest system assessments.

## Software and automation

Field service software for scheduling, dispatch, quotes and invoices (see [What is field service management software?](/blog/what-is-field-service-management-software/)), automated reminders and review requests.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I market my HVAC business?
Optimise Google Business Profile and reviews, run Local Services and search ads, build a website with online booking, run seasonal campaigns and sell maintenance memberships.

### How do I advertise my HVAC business?
Use Google Local Services Ads and search ads for urgent needs, social ads for seasonal offers, and local channels like mailers and truck wraps.

### How do HVAC companies get customers?
Through Google searches, reviews, referrals, maintenance plans, partnerships and seasonal promotions.

### Are HVAC maintenance plans worth offering?
Yes; they create recurring revenue, smooth seasonal demand and increase customer loyalty.

### What is the best time to market HVAC services?
Before peak seasons (spring for cooling, autumn for heating), plus always-on visibility for emergencies.

### How can HVAC companies get more reviews?
Ask at job completion with an automatic text link and make it quick to leave a review.
