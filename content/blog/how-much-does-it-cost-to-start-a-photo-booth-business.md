<p class="lede">Starting a photo booth business typically costs <strong>$2,000 to $15,000</strong>. A lean setup with an <strong>iPad or 360 booth</strong> can start around <strong>$2,000 to $4,000</strong>, while a <strong>DSLR or mirror booth</strong> with a printer, premium backdrops and branding often costs <strong>$8,000 to $15,000+</strong>.</p>

These are rough ranges; prices change by brand and features.

## Sample budgets

| Item | Lean setup | Premium setup |
|---|---|---|
| Booth / camera | $1,200 (iPad or 360) | $8,000 (mirror or DSLR) |
| Lighting | $100 | $500 |
| Printer and first media | $0 (digital only) | $1,500 |
| Software (yearly) | $150 | $600 |
| Backdrops and props | $200 | $1,000 |
| Cases and transport | $150 | $600 |
| Insurance | $400 | $800 |
| Website and marketing | $300 | $2,000 |
| **Total** | **about $2,500** | **about $15,000** |

## Ongoing costs

- Print media (paper and ribbon) per event
- Software subscriptions
- Attendant pay
- Fuel and vehicle
- Insurance renewals
- Repairs and replacement props

## Ways to save

- Start digital-only (no printer)
- Buy used equipment from other operators
- Start with one booth type and add later
- Use your own vehicle

## How fast you earn it back

At $300–$600 profit per event, many booths pay back in 10 to 25 events. See [Is a photo booth a good investment?](/blog/is-a-photo-booth-a-good-investment/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does it cost to start a photo booth business?
Typically $2,000 to $15,000.

### What's the cheapest way to start a photo booth business?
An iPad or 360 booth with digital sharing only.

### Do I need a printer?
Not always; many clients are happy with digital sharing, but prints add value.

### How much is photo booth software?
Often $100 to $1,000 a year.

### How long until a photo booth pays for itself?
Often 10 to 25 events.
