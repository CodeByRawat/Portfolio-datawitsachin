<p class="lede">To start a lawn care business: <strong>choose your services</strong> (mowing, edging, trimming, leaf cleanup, fertilising), <strong>register the business and get insurance</strong>, check <strong>local licence requirements</strong> (especially for pesticide or fertiliser application), buy <strong>reliable equipment</strong>, set <strong>pricing</strong>, use <strong>scheduling and invoicing software</strong>, and win your <strong>first clients</strong> through neighbours, Google, door hangers and a simple website. Many owners start solo with a mower and trailer and grow into crews.</p>

## Step by step

1. **Pick services and a niche:** residential mowing, premium properties, HOAs, or commercial
2. **Register the business:** name, structure (sole proprietor or LLC), tax ID, business bank account
3. **Licences and insurance:** general liability, commercial auto, and any pesticide/fertiliser licence (see [Do you need a license for a lawn care business?](/blog/do-you-need-a-license-for-a-lawn-care-business/))
4. **Equipment:** mower(s), string trimmer, edger, blower, safety gear, truck and trailer
5. **Pricing:** per visit, per square foot or monthly plans (see [How much does lawn care cost?](/blog/how-much-does-lawn-care-cost/))
6. **Software:** scheduling, routing, quotes, invoicing and online payment
7. **Branding and website:** logo, truck signage, a site with a quote form (see [How to create a website for your business](/blog/how-to-create-a-website-for-your-business/))
8. **First customers:** neighbours, Nextdoor, door hangers, Google Business Profile (see [How to get lawn care customers](/blog/how-to-get-lawn-care-customers/))
9. **Systems:** route density, recurring weekly/biweekly schedules, seasonal upsells
10. **Grow:** hire help, add services, add a second crew (see [How to grow a lawn care business](/blog/how-to-grow-a-lawn-care-business/))

## Startup costs

Startup costs range from modest (used push mower and existing vehicle) to much higher with a commercial zero-turn mower, trailer and truck. The main costs are equipment, insurance, registration, marketing and fuel.

## Seasonality

Plan for off-season revenue: leaf cleanup, aeration, overseeding, snow removal, holiday lighting or landscaping (see [How to start a landscaping business](/blog/how-to-start-a-landscaping-business/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a lawn care business?
Choose services, register the business, get insurance and any required licences, buy equipment, set prices, use scheduling software and market locally.

### How much does it cost to start a lawn care business?
It can start small with basic equipment or cost more with commercial mowers, a trailer and a truck.

### Do I need a licence for lawn care?
Basic mowing often only needs a business licence, but applying pesticides or fertiliser usually requires a state licence.

### Is lawn care a profitable business?
It can be, especially with dense routes, recurring customers and add-on services.

### How do I get my first lawn care customers?
Start with neighbours, Nextdoor, door hangers, Google Business Profile and referrals.

### What equipment do I need to start mowing lawns?
A mower, trimmer, edger, blower, safety gear and a way to transport them.
