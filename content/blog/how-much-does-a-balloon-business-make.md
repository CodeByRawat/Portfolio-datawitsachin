<p class="lede">A balloon décor business can make <strong>$500 to $3,000 a month part-time</strong> and <strong>$40,000 to $100,000+ a year full-time</strong>. Balloon materials are relatively cheap, so many installs have <strong>50% to 70% gross margins</strong>. Startup costs are low, often <strong>$500 to $3,000</strong>.</p>

These are rough ranges; prices and demand vary by area.

## Typical pricing

| Service | Typical price range |
|---|---|
| Grab-and-go garland | $75 – $200 |
| Installed garland (per foot) | Priced per foot |
| Balloon arch | $250 – $800+ |
| Backdrop with garland | $400 – $1,500+ |
| Corporate installations | $500 – $5,000+ |

## Profit per install (example)

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Arch installation price | $500 |
| Balloons and supplies | −$100 |
| Delivery and fuel | −$25 |
| **Gross profit** | **about $375** |

Your time for building and setup also needs to be paid.

## Income examples

| Business | Rough yearly revenue |
|---|---|
| Weekend side hustle | $5,000 – $30,000 |
| Full-time solo | $40,000 – $100,000 |
| Studio with team | $100,000+ |

## Costs

Balloons, helium, frames, transport, insurance, storage and marketing.

## How to earn more

- Raise prices as your portfolio grows
- Sell premium backdrops and full event décor
- Book corporate clients
- Add rentals (backdrops, stands, neon signs)
- Do multiple installs per weekend with helpers

Getting started: [How to start a balloon business](/blog/how-to-start-a-balloon-business/). Marketing: [How to market a balloon business](/blog/how-to-market-a-balloon-business/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a balloon business make?
From a few hundred dollars a month part-time to $100,000+ full-time.

### How much does it cost to start a balloon business?
Often $500 to $3,000.

### Is balloon décor profitable?
Yes. Materials are cheap compared with installation prices.

### How do balloon decorators price their work?
By size, design, per foot of garland, plus delivery and setup.

### Do you tip balloon installers?
Tipping isn't required, but it's appreciated for great work.
