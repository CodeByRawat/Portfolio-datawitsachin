<p class="lede">To start a vending machine business, choose a <strong>vending type</strong> (snacks, drinks, combo, healthy or micro markets), <strong>register the business</strong> and get <strong>sales tax and vending permits</strong>, buy <strong>new or used machines with card readers</strong>, secure <strong>high-traffic locations</strong> through a professional pitch, buy stock at <strong>wholesale</strong>, and grow by building a <strong>dense restocking route</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Choose your vending niche

| Type | Notes |
|---|---|
| Snack and drink combo | Flexible, good for smaller locations |
| Drink machines | Simple, high demand in warm spots |
| Healthy vending | Gyms, schools, offices |
| Micro markets | Self-checkout kiosks for larger workplaces |
| Specialty | Coffee, PPE, ice, toys, laundry supplies |

## Step 2: Register and get permits

Register the business, get an EIN, apply for a **sales tax permit**, and check city or county **vending licences** and food handling rules for perishable items. Get liability insurance.

## Step 3: Buy machines

- **New** machines: reliable, warranty, modern cashless readers
- **Used/refurbished**: cheaper, inspect carefully
- Add **cashless card readers**; many customers don't carry cash

## Step 4: Find locations

The best locations have **many people and few food options**: factories, warehouses, offices, apartment complexes, hotels, auto shops, gyms, schools and hospitals.

How to pitch:

1. Walk in and ask for the manager or decision-maker
2. Offer free installation, stocking and service
3. Offer a commission or free product to the location if needed
4. Leave a professional one-page sheet and a website link
5. Sign a simple placement agreement

## Step 5: Stock and price

Buy from wholesale clubs or distributors, track best-sellers, rotate slow items and price competitively for the location.

## Step 6: Operate and scale

Restock on a schedule, use telemetry to see what's sold, keep machines clean and working, and add locations close to your route. ## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a vending machine business?
Choose a niche, register and get permits, buy machines, secure locations, stock products and run a restocking route.

### Can I start a vending machine business with no experience?
Yes; it's beginner-friendly, but learning location selection and product mix is key.

### How do you get into the vending machine business?
Start with one or two machines in strong locations, then reinvest profits into more machines.

### What do you need to start a vending machine business?
A registered business, permits, machines, card readers, locations, inventory and a vehicle.

### Do I need a license for vending machines?
Usually a sales tax permit and sometimes a local vending licence.

### How many vending machines should I start with?
Many owners start with one to three machines to learn before scaling.
