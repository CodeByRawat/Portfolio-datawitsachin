<p class="lede">Car washes can be <strong>quite profitable</strong>, especially <strong>express tunnels and automatic washes in high-traffic locations with strong unlimited membership programmes</strong>, because labour needs are relatively low and memberships create recurring revenue. Annual revenue ranges widely from modest amounts for small self-serve washes to millions for busy tunnels. Profit depends on <strong>volume, pricing, membership count, labour, water and chemical costs, maintenance and debt</strong>.</p>

## What drives car wash revenue

| Driver | Notes |
|---|---|
| Car count | Location and traffic |
| Average ticket | Wash packages and add-ons |
| Memberships | Recurring monthly revenue |
| Fleet accounts | Business vehicles |
| Ancillary sales | Vacuums, detailing, vending |

## Costs

Labour, utilities and water, chemicals, maintenance and repairs, equipment financing, rent/mortgage, insurance, marketing and payment fees.

## What makes car washes profitable

- High-visibility, high-traffic location
- Efficient automated equipment
- Large membership base
- Good customer experience and reviews
- Controlled labour and maintenance

## Risks

Weather seasonality, new competitors, equipment breakdowns, water restrictions, high debt from construction.

## Is a car wash a good investment?

It can be when the location, market demand and financing are sound; buyers should study car counts, membership numbers, equipment condition and competition (see [How much does it cost to buy a car wash?](/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-buy-a-car-wash/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### Are car washes profitable?
They can be, especially automated or express washes with high traffic and many memberships.

### How much do car washes make a year?
Anywhere from modest revenue for small self-serve washes to millions for busy express tunnels.

### Is a car wash a good investment?
Often, with a strong location, efficient operations and recurring membership revenue.

### Why are car wash memberships important?
They provide predictable recurring revenue and increase customer loyalty.

### What are the biggest car wash costs?
Labour, utilities, chemicals, maintenance and financing.

### How profitable is a self-serve car wash?
Self-serve washes have lower labour costs but usually lower revenue; profitability depends on location and maintenance.
