<p class="lede">To start an online clothing business: choose your <strong>model</strong> (own brand, curated boutique, dropshipping or print-on-demand), pick an <strong>e-commerce platform</strong>, source <strong>inventory</strong> or set up suppliers, create <strong>great photos, descriptions and size guides</strong>, set clear <strong>shipping and returns</strong>, and market through <strong>Instagram, TikTok, creators, email and ads</strong>, focusing on repeat customers.</p>

## Business models

| Model | Inventory | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Own brand | Yes | Highest control and margins (see [How to start your own clothing brand](/blog/how-to-start-your-own-clothing-brand/)) |
| Curated boutique | Yes (wholesale) | Curation and styling are your edge |
| Dropshipping | No | Lower margins, less control over quality and shipping |
| Print-on-demand | No | Design-led (see [How to start a t-shirt business](/blog/how-to-start-a-t-shirt-business/)) |

## Platform

Shopify is common for fashion; WooCommerce, Squarespace and Wix also work (see [What is the best website builder?](/blog/what-is-the-best-website-builder/)).

## Product pages that sell clothes

- Multiple photos on models, flat lays, close-ups
- Short videos showing fit and movement
- Detailed size guide and model measurements
- Fabric, care and fit notes
- Reviews with photos
- Clear shipping and returns info

## Returns and shipping

Fashion has high return rates; make policies clear, consider exchanges and store credit, and offer free shipping thresholds.

## Marketing

Instagram/TikTok try-on videos, creators and affiliates, email welcome series, retargeting ads, seasonal drops, and loyalty (see [How to increase e-commerce sales](/blog/how-to-increase-ecommerce-sales/) and [How to improve e-commerce customer experience](/blog/how-to-improve-ecommerce-customer-experience/)).

## From home to scale

Start shipping from home, then use a 3PL as orders grow (see [How to start a small clothing business from home](/blog/how-to-start-a-small-clothing-business-from-home/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start an online clothing business?
Choose a model, set up an e-commerce store, source products, create strong product pages, define shipping and returns, and market on social media and email.

### What is the best platform for an online clothing store?
Shopify is popular for fashion, with WooCommerce, Squarespace and Wix as alternatives.

### How do I start an online boutique with no money?
Begin with print-on-demand or dropshipping, then reinvest profits into inventory.

### How do online boutiques get customers?
Social media content, creators, ads, email marketing and great reviews.

### How do I reduce returns for clothing?
Accurate size guides, fit notes, model measurements, videos and reviews with photos.

### Is an online clothing business profitable?
It can be with strong branding, good margins, controlled returns and repeat customers.
