<p class="lede">A <strong>business consultant</strong> helps companies <strong>improve performance</strong> by assessing how the business operates, identifying <strong>problems and opportunities</strong>, recommending <strong>practical solutions</strong> and often helping <strong>implement</strong> them across strategy, operations, sales and marketing, finance, people and technology.</p>

## What a business consultant does

- Interviews owners and staff, reviews data and processes
- Benchmarks against competitors and best practices
- Identifies bottlenecks, risks and growth opportunities
- Builds recommendations, plans and business cases
- Supports implementation, training and change
- Measures results

## Business consulting services

| Service | Examples |
|---|---|
| Strategy | Growth plans, market entry, pricing |
| Operations | Process improvement, automation (see [How to automate business processes](/blog/how-to-automate-business-processes/)) |
| Sales and marketing | Funnels, CRM, lead generation (see [How to generate sales leads](/blog/how-to-generate-sales-leads/)) |
| Finance | Budgeting, cash flow, profitability |
| HR and organisation | Structure, hiring, culture |
| Technology | Systems selection, digital transformation, AI |

## Consultant vs coach

Consultants diagnose and advise (often implementing); coaches guide clients to their own solutions (see [What is a coaching business?](/blog/what-is-a-coaching-business/)).

## Skills

Analytical thinking, problem-solving, industry knowledge, communication and presentation, project management, change management and trust-building.

## How consultants charge

Hourly/daily rates, project fees, retainers, value-based or performance fees.

## How to get into consulting

1. Build deep expertise in a function or industry
2. Develop analytical and communication skills
3. Join a consulting firm, or start with freelance projects
4. Build case studies and references
5. Specialise and network
(See [How to start a consulting business](/blog/how-to-start-a-consulting-business/).)

## Frequently asked questions

### What does a business consultant do?
Assesses a business, identifies problems and opportunities, recommends solutions and helps implement improvements.

### What are business consulting services?
Advice and implementation support in strategy, operations, sales and marketing, finance, HR and technology.

### How do I get into consulting?
Build expertise and analytical skills, join a firm or take freelance projects, create case studies and network.

### What skills do consultants need?
Analysis, problem-solving, communication, project management and industry knowledge.

### How do business consultants charge?
Hourly or daily rates, fixed project fees, retainers or value-based pricing.

### What is the difference between a consultant and a coach?
Consultants provide expert solutions; coaches help clients develop their own solutions through questions and accountability.
