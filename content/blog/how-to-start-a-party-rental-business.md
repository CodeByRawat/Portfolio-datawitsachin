<p class="lede">To start a party rental business, <strong>choose your inventory</strong> (tables and chairs, tents, bounce houses, linens, décor), <strong>start small from home</strong> with a truck or trailer and garage storage, get <strong>liability insurance and permits</strong>, set <strong>rental prices with delivery fees</strong>, use <strong>online booking</strong>, and market to <strong>parents, event planners, churches and venues</strong>. Many start for <strong>$5,000 to $25,000</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Choose your inventory

| Category | Notes |
|---|---|
| Tables and chairs | Steady demand, easy to start |
| Tents and canopies | High value, need setup skills |
| Bounce houses and inflatables | Popular, need safety training and insurance |
| Linens and décor | Weddings and upscale events |
| Concessions (popcorn, cotton candy) | Easy add-ons |
| Lighting, dance floors, staging | Bigger events |

Start with what your area books most and add over time.

## Step 2: Start from home

Many party rental businesses start from a garage or storage unit with a truck and trailer. Check zoning and HOA rules for storage and customer pickups.

## Step 3: Startup costs

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Tables and chairs | $2,000 – $8,000 |
| Tents | $1,500 – $10,000 |
| Inflatables (each) | $1,500 – $5,000 |
| Trailer | $2,000 – $8,000 |
| Storage | $100 – $500 per month |
| Insurance | $1,000 – $5,000 per year |
| Website and booking software | $500 – $3,000 |

See [Is a party rental business profitable?](/blog/is-a-party-rental-business-profitable/) for profit and payback.

## Step 4: Licences and insurance

LLC, EIN, business licence, sales tax permit (rentals are taxable in many states), general liability, commercial auto, and inflatable inspections where required.

## Step 5: Pricing

Price per item per event (often a 24-hour rental), plus delivery and setup fees by distance, and deposits for large orders. Package deals (tables + chairs + tent) increase order value.

## Step 6: Online booking

Use rental software that shows real-time availability, takes deposits and sends reminders. Customers book online at night when planning parties.

## Step 7: Get customers

1. Google Business Profile and reviews
2. A website with inventory, prices and online booking
3. Event planners, venues and caterers
4. Schools, churches and community events
5. Social media photos of setups

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a party rental business?
Choose inventory, start from home with a trailer, get insured, set prices and use online booking.

### How much does it cost to start a party rental business?
Often $5,000 to $25,000 to start small.

### Can I start a party rental business from home?
Yes, if zoning and storage allow.

### What party rentals are most profitable?
Tents, inflatables and packages often have the best returns.

### Do I need insurance for party rentals?
Yes, especially for inflatables and tents.
