<p class="lede"><strong>OTR stands for "over-the-road"</strong> trucking: <strong>long-haul</strong> driving that covers long distances, often across many states or a whole country, with drivers typically <strong>away from home for days or weeks</strong> at a time and sleeping in the truck's sleeper cab. OTR jobs usually offer <strong>more miles</strong> (and often more pay for mileage-paid drivers) but <strong>less home time</strong> than regional or local trucking.</p>

## OTR vs regional vs local

| | OTR | Regional | Local |
|---|---|---|---|
| Distance | Long-haul, nationwide | Within a region | Within a city/metro |
| Home time | Every 1–3+ weeks | Weekly or more | Daily |
| Pay basis | Often per mile | Per mile or hourly | Often hourly |
| Lifestyle | Living on the road | Mixed | Home every night |

## What OTR drivers do

Plan routes, drive long distances within hours-of-service limits, perform inspections, manage logs (ELD), deliver loads, communicate with dispatch and brokers, and maintain safety.

## Pros and cons

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| More miles and earning potential | Long periods away from home |
| See the country | Irregular sleep, health challenges |
| Often entry point for new drivers | Limited personal time |

## How to become an OTR driver

Get a Class A CDL (or equivalent), meet medical requirements, join a carrier with training if new, and build experience (see [How to become a trucker](/blog/how-to-become-a-trucker/)). Pay details: [How much do truck drivers make?](/blog/how-much-do-truck-drivers-make/)

## Frequently asked questions

### What is OTR in trucking?
Over-the-road: long-haul trucking across long distances, with drivers away from home for extended periods.

### What does OTR mean for truck drivers?
Long trips, sleeping in the truck and home time every one to several weeks.

### Do OTR drivers make more money?
Often, due to more miles, though pay depends on the carrier and pay structure.

### What is the difference between OTR and regional trucking?
Regional routes stay within a region with more frequent home time; OTR covers longer distances with longer stretches away.

### Is OTR good for new drivers?
Many new drivers start OTR to gain experience, as carriers often hire new CDL holders for these roles.

### How long are OTR drivers away from home?
Commonly one to three weeks or more, depending on the carrier and route.
