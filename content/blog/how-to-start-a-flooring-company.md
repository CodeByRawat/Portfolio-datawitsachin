<p class="lede">To start a flooring company, <strong>learn installation</strong> (or partner with an installer), choose your <strong>flooring types</strong>, get your <strong>contractor license where required and liability insurance</strong>, buy tools, open accounts with <strong>suppliers</strong>, and win jobs from <strong>homeowners, builders, remodelers and Google</strong>. Most people start as an installer before opening a store.</p>

## Step 1: Get the skills

Work for a flooring installer or contractor first, or take manufacturer and trade training. Customers judge you on clean, flat, gap-free floors.

## Step 2: Choose your services

| Type | Notes |
|---|---|
| Luxury vinyl plank (LVP) | Most popular today; quick to install |
| Laminate | Budget-friendly, similar install |
| Carpet | Needs stretchers and seaming tools |
| Tile | Higher skill, higher price per sq ft |
| Hardwood (install and refinishing) | Premium work |
| Subfloor prep and removal | Easy add-on revenue |

## Step 3: Pick your business model

- **Installation only:** customer or store supplies material; lowest startup cost
- **Install + supply:** you sell material and earn a markup
- **Flooring store / showroom:** samples, design help and a crew; highest cost
- **Subcontract to stores and builders:** steady work, lower rates

## Step 4: Licenses and insurance

- LLC and EIN
- Business license
- State or local contractor license if required (rules vary by state and job size)
- General liability insurance, plus workers' comp once you hire

## Step 5: Tools and startup costs

Saws, moisture meter, levels, carpet tools, tile tools, a cargo van or trailer, and dust control. An installer can start for roughly <strong>$5,000 to $20,000</strong>; a store usually costs <strong>$100,000+</strong>.

## Step 6: Supplier accounts

Open trade accounts with flooring distributors for better pricing, and keep samples for estimates.

## Step 7: Price your jobs

Quote by square foot for labour, plus material, removal, subfloor prep, trims, stairs and moving furniture. Measure carefully and add waste (often 5–10%, more for tile patterns).

## Step 8: Get clients

1. Google Business Profile with photos of finished floors
2. A website where people can book a free in-home estimate
3. Builders, remodelers, realtors and property managers
4. Flooring stores that need subcontract installers
5. Reviews and referrals

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a flooring installation business?
Learn installation, get licensed and insured, buy tools, open supplier accounts and market to homeowners and contractors.

### Do I need a license to install flooring?
It depends on your state. Some require a contractor license above a certain job size; all require a business license.

### How much does it cost to start a flooring business?
Roughly $5,000 to $20,000 for an installer, $100,000+ for a showroom.

### How do flooring companies get customers?
Google, reviews, referrals, builders, remodelers and subcontracting for flooring stores.

### Can I start a carpet installation business from scratch?
Yes. Learn under an experienced installer, buy carpet tools and start subcontracting for stores.
