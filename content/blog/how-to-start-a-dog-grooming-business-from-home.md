<p class="lede">You can run a dog grooming business from home if <strong>local zoning allows it</strong>. Get a <strong>home occupation permit</strong> and business licence, set up a <strong>dedicated grooming room or garage</strong> with a tub, table, dryer and good drainage, buy <strong>insurance</strong>, set <strong>prices by breed and size</strong>, and use <strong>online booking</strong> and local marketing to fill your schedule.</p>

## Step 1: Check zoning and permits

Many cities allow home businesses but limit customer visits, signs, parking and noise. HOAs and leases may forbid it. Get a **home occupation permit**, **business licence**, and any pet-facility permit. Read [Do pet groomers need a license?](/blog/do-pet-groomers-need-a-license/).

## Step 2: Set up your grooming space

- Separate entrance if possible
- Washable floors and walls
- Raised tub with hot water and good drainage (hair trap)
- Ventilation for dryers and dust
- Secure doors and gates
- Waiting space or drop-off system

Garages and converted rooms are common choices.

## Step 3: Equipment

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| Grooming table with arm | Hydraulic or electric for comfort |
| Bathing tub | Raised, with sprayer |
| High-velocity dryer | Plus stand dryer |
| Clippers and blades | Several blade sizes |
| Shears, brushes, combs | Straight, curved, thinning |
| Nail grinders and clippers | |
| Shampoos and conditioners | |
| Towels, washer and dryer | |

## Step 4: Insurance and policies

Get liability and animal bailee coverage. Write policies for vaccinations, aggressive dogs, late pickups, matted coats and cancellations.

## Step 5: Pricing

Price by **breed, size, coat condition and service** (bath, full groom, nails). Add fees for matting and difficult behaviour.

## Step 6: Get clients

- Google Business Profile with photos
- Instagram before/after posts
- Partner with local vets, dog walkers and trainers
- Referral discounts
- Online booking with reminders and rebooking every 4–8 weeks

## Pros and cons of home grooming

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| No rent | Zoning limits |
| No commute | Less separation of home and work |
| Lower prices possible | Limited space for growth |

As you grow, consider a mobile van or salon. More on owner earnings: [How much does a dog grooming business make?](/blog/how-much-does-a-dog-grooming-business-make/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I run a dog grooming business from home?
Yes, if zoning and HOA rules allow it and you get the right permits.

### Can I run a dog grooming business from my garage?
Many groomers do, with washable surfaces, plumbing, ventilation and a permit.

### What do I need to start dog grooming from home?
A permitted space, tub, table, dryer, clippers, insurance, pricing and a booking system.

### Do I need a license to groom dogs at home?
Usually a business licence and home occupation permit; some areas require pet-facility permits.

### How much does it cost to start a home dog grooming business?
Less than a salon, since there's no rent; equipment and room setup are the main costs.

### How do I get clients for home dog grooming?
Google reviews, social media photos, vet referrals and online booking.
