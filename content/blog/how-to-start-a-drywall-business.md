<p class="lede">To start a drywall business, get <strong>experience hanging and finishing drywall</strong>, choose your focus (<strong>new construction, remodels or repairs</strong>), get <strong>licensed where required and insured</strong>, buy <strong>tools</strong> (lifts, screw guns, taping tools, sanders), and win work from <strong>builders, general contractors and homeowners</strong>. A small drywall business can start for <strong>$5,000 to $20,000</strong> plus a truck.</p>

## Step 1: Get experience

Work on a drywall crew to learn hanging, taping, mudding, finishing levels, texture and repairs. Finishing well takes practice and is what customers notice.

## Step 2: Choose your niche

| Niche | Notes |
|---|---|
| New construction | Volume from builders, competitive pricing |
| Remodels | Better margins, more variety |
| Repairs and patches | Small jobs, great for starting |
| Commercial | Metal studs, bigger contracts, more requirements |
| Texture and finishing | Specialised skill |

## Step 3: Licenses and insurance

LLC, EIN, business license, a contractor license where required (often above a job-size limit), general liability, workers' comp and commercial auto.

## Step 4: Tools and startup costs

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Drywall lift, screw guns, cutting tools | $1,000 – $3,000 |
| Taping and finishing tools (knives, banjo, boxes) | $500 – $5,000 |
| Sanders and dust control | $500 – $2,000 |
| Stilts, ladders, scaffolding | $500 – $2,000 |
| Insurance and licenses | $2,000 – $6,000 per year |
| Truck or van | Varies |

Automatic taping tools are expensive but speed up big jobs.

## Step 5: Pricing

Drywall is often priced **per square foot** of wall and ceiling area, per board, or as a job price for repairs. Include materials, hanging, finishing level, texture, cleanup and difficult areas like high ceilings.

## Step 6: Build a crew

Hangers and finishers often specialise. Start with one partner and add as work grows.

## Step 7: Get work

1. Builders and general contractors
2. Remodelers and handymen
3. Google Business Profile with photos
4. A website with an estimate form
5. Property managers for repairs

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a drywall business?
Get experience, choose a niche, get licensed and insured, buy tools and win work from builders and homeowners.

### How much does it cost to start a drywall company?
Often $5,000 to $20,000 plus a truck.

### Is the drywall business profitable?
Yes, especially remodels, repairs and finishing work.

### How much does a drywall company owner make?
Often $50,000 to $120,000+, depending on crew size.

### How do drywall contractors charge?
Per square foot, per board or per job.
