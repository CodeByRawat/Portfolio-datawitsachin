<p class="lede">To create a website for your business: <strong>define what the site must achieve</strong> (calls, bookings, sales or leads), <strong>register a domain</strong>, <strong>choose a platform</strong> (a website builder like Wix, Squarespace, Shopify or Framer, WordPress, or a custom-built site), <strong>plan your pages</strong>, <strong>write clear copy</strong>, <strong>design for mobile first</strong>, add <strong>contact, booking and WhatsApp</strong> options, set up <strong>SEO and analytics</strong>, then test and launch. You can start <strong>free</strong> on builder plans, but a professional domain and paid plan are worth it for a real business.</p>

## Step 1: Define the website's job

| Business type | Main goal | Key feature |
|---|---|---|
| Local service (dentist, salon, plumber) | Calls and bookings | Click-to-call, booking, WhatsApp, map |
| Shop | Online sales | E-commerce checkout |
| B2B / consultant | Leads | Case studies, contact form, calendar booking |
| Restaurant | Reservations and orders | Menu, booking, delivery links |
| Creator / portfolio | Clients or audience | Work samples, contact |

Write one sentence: "Our website exists to get ___ to ___."

## Step 2: Choose and register a domain

- Short, easy to spell, matches your brand
- .com if available, or a country domain (e.g., .co.uk, .in) for local businesses
- Register through a reputable registrar; **register it in your own name/account**
- Typically ~$10–$40/year

## Step 3: Choose a platform

| Platform | Cost | Ease | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wix** | Free plan / paid | Very easy | Small businesses, DIY |
| **Squarespace** | Paid | Easy | Design-led brands |
| **Shopify** | Paid | Easy | Online stores |
| **WordPress.org** | Free software + hosting | Medium | Flexibility, blogs, plugins |
| **Webflow, Framer** | Free plan / paid | Medium | Modern design, marketing sites |
| **Google Business Profile website/site features** | Free | Very easy | Minimal presence (check current availability) |
| **Custom-built** (e.g., React, Next.js, Astro) | Developer cost | Needs a developer | Speed, unique design, integrations |

## Step 4: Plan your pages

A typical small business website:
- **Home:** what you do, for whom, why choose you, main call to action
- **Services/Products:** one page per main service is better for SEO
- **About:** story, team, credentials
- **Reviews / case studies**
- **Pricing** (if possible)
- **FAQ**
- **Contact:** phone, WhatsApp, form, map, hours
- **Blog/resources** (optional, great for SEO)
- **Privacy policy** and legal pages

## Step 5: Write copy that converts

- **Headline:** what you do + for whom + outcome ("Pain-free dental care in Austin, open Saturdays")
- **Benefits before features**
- **Proof:** reviews, numbers, logos, photos of real work
- **Clear CTAs** on every page ("Book an appointment", "Get a quote")
- **Simple language;** answer questions customers ask on the phone

## Step 6: Design for mobile first

Most visitors arrive on phones:
- Large tap targets, sticky call/WhatsApp button
- Fast loading (compress images, avoid heavy sliders)
- Readable text (16px+), strong contrast
- Short forms
- Real photos over stock where possible

## Step 7: Add the features that bring business

- **Contact forms** connected to email or a CRM (see [What is a CRM?](/blog/what-is-a-crm/))
- **Online booking** (Calendly, Cal.com, Acuity, industry-specific tools)
- **WhatsApp chat button** and automated replies (see [How to schedule a WhatsApp message](/blog/how-to-schedule-a-whatsapp-message/))
- **AI chatbot** for FAQs and lead capture (see [How to create an AI chatbot](/blog/how-to-create-an-ai-chatbot/))
- **Payments or e-commerce**
- **Email sign-up**

## Step 8: Set up SEO basics

- Unique **title tag and meta description** for each page
- One clear **H1** per page; logical headings
- **Local SEO:** name, address and phone consistent everywhere; embed map; create and optimise your **Google Business Profile**
- **Structured data** (LocalBusiness, FAQ)
- **Fast speed and good Core Web Vitals**
- Submit sitemap in **Google Search Console**
- Publish helpful content answering customer questions

## Step 9: Analytics and tracking

- **Google Analytics 4** or a privacy-friendly alternative
- **Google Search Console**
- **Conversion tracking:** form submissions, calls, WhatsApp clicks, bookings
- **Cookie consent** where required by law

## Step 10: Launch checklist

- [ ] All links and forms tested
- [ ] Mobile and desktop checked in several browsers
- [ ] Page speed tested (PageSpeed Insights)
- [ ] SSL (https) active
- [ ] Favicon, social share images
- [ ] Legal pages and cookie consent
- [ ] Analytics and Search Console set up
- [ ] Backups configured
- [ ] Google Business Profile links to the site

## Can I create a business website for free?

Yes: Wix, Webflow, Framer and others offer free plans, and WordPress or static sites can be hosted cheaply or free. Limitations: platform branding, a subdomain (yourbusiness.wixsite.com), limited features and less credibility. For a real business, budget at least for a domain and a paid plan. (Full price breakdown: [How much does a website cost?](/blog/how-much-does-a-website-cost/))

## DIY or hire a professional?

| DIY makes sense if | Hire a professional if |
|---|---|
| Budget is very tight | The website is a key source of customers |
| You need something simple quickly | You need booking, payments, integrations or automation |
| You enjoy design and tech | You want speed, SEO and conversion done right |
| You have time to learn and maintain | Your time is better spent running the business |

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I create a website for my business?
Define the website's goal, register a domain, choose a platform, plan your pages, write clear copy, design for mobile, add contact, booking and WhatsApp options, set up SEO and analytics, then test and launch.

### How do I create a website for my business for free?
Use a free plan from a builder like Wix, Webflow or Framer, or free static hosting. Expect a branded subdomain and limitations; upgrading to a custom domain is recommended.

### What is the best platform to create a business website?
Wix or Squarespace for easy DIY, Shopify for online stores, WordPress for flexibility, Webflow or Framer for modern design, and custom development for speed and unique features.

### How long does it take to build a business website?
A simple DIY site can take a weekend; a professional small business site usually takes two to six weeks depending on content and features.

### What pages should a business website have?
Home, services or products, about, reviews or case studies, FAQ, contact, legal pages and optionally pricing and a blog.

### Do small businesses still need a website?
Yes. A website builds credibility, appears in Google searches, and gives you a place you control to convert visitors into customers, beyond social media profiles.
