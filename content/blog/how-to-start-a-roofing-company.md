<p class="lede">To start a roofing company: gain <strong>roofing experience</strong>, get any <strong>state or local contractor licence</strong> required, <strong>register the business</strong>, buy <strong>general liability and workers' comp insurance</strong> (critical in roofing), open <strong>supplier accounts</strong>, decide on <strong>in-house crews or subcontractors</strong>, learn <strong>estimating</strong>, and market through <strong>Google, reviews, referrals and a strong website</strong>.</p>

## Steps

1. **Experience:** work for a roofing company to learn installs, estimating and sales
2. **Licences:** state/local contractor licence where required; permits per job
3. **Register:** LLC or corporation, EIN, business bank account
4. **Insurance:** general liability, workers' compensation, commercial auto; bonds where required
5. **Equipment:** ladders, safety harnesses, nail guns, compressors, trailer, truck
6. **Crews:** hire your own or start with reliable subcontractors
7. **Suppliers:** open accounts for shingles, metal and accessories; ask about manufacturer certifications
8. **Estimating:** measure accurately and price correctly (see [How to estimate a roofing job](/blog/how-to-estimate-a-roofing-job/))
9. **Insurance claims:** learn how storm damage claims work if you'll do restoration
10. **Marketing:** Google Business Profile, reviews, yard signs, referrals and a website with inspection booking (see [How to market a roofing company](/blog/how-to-market-a-roofing-company/))

## How much do you need to start?

Costs include licensing, insurance (often the biggest), equipment, a truck/trailer, marketing and working capital to buy materials before customers pay. Subcontracting labour reduces upfront costs.

## Getting into roofing with no experience

Start as a labourer or apprentice, learn safety, install and estimating skills, or partner with experienced roofers if you bring sales and business skills.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a roofing company?
Gain experience, get licences and insurance, register the business, set up crews and suppliers, learn estimating and market locally.

### How much do you need to start a roofing company?
It depends on insurance, equipment and working capital; subcontracting reduces startup costs.

### Do I need a licence to start a roofing business?
Many states and cities require a contractor or roofing licence; check local rules.

### Can I start a roofing company with no experience?
It's risky; most owners gain field experience first or partner with experienced roofers.

### How do roofing companies get jobs?
Google reviews, referrals, yard signs, storm restoration, website leads and contractor relationships.

### Is roofing a profitable business?
It can be, with accurate estimating, good crews and steady leads.
