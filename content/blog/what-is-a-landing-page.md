<p class="lede">A <strong>landing page</strong> is a standalone web page designed for <strong>one specific goal</strong>, such as capturing leads, booking calls, selling a product or registering for an event, usually the page people "land" on after clicking an <strong>ad, email or social link</strong>. Unlike a homepage, which introduces everything about a business, a landing page focuses on <strong>one offer and one call to action</strong> with minimal distractions, which is why it typically converts visitors better.</p>

## Landing page vs homepage

| | Landing page | Homepage |
|---|---|---|
| **Goal** | One conversion action | Introduce the business and route visitors |
| **Audience** | Specific campaign traffic | Everyone |
| **Navigation** | Minimal or none | Full menu |
| **Content** | Focused on one offer | Broad overview |
| **Calls to action** | One (repeated) | Several |
| **Typical traffic** | Ads, email, social campaigns | Direct, brand search, referrals |

## Types of landing pages

| Type | Goal | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Lead generation** | Capture contact details | Free guide, quote request, consultation |
| **Booking** | Schedule appointments or calls | Dentist or consultant booking page |
| **Sales** | Sell directly | Product or course sales page |
| **Click-through** | Warm up before checkout or sign-up | SaaS feature page leading to trial |
| **Event/webinar** | Registrations | Webinar sign-up |
| **App download** | Installs | App features with store buttons |
| **Coming soon/waitlist** | Collect interest | Product launch waitlist |

## Anatomy of a high-converting landing page

1. **Headline matching the ad:** same promise, same words (message match)
2. **Subheadline:** who it's for and the outcome
3. **Hero visual or video:** product, result or short explainer (see [What is an explainer video?](/blog/what-is-an-explainer-video/))
4. **Primary CTA above the fold**
5. **Benefits** (not just features), scannable
6. **Social proof:** reviews, testimonials, logos, numbers
7. **How it works:** 3 simple steps
8. **Objection handling:** FAQ, guarantee, pricing clarity
9. **Repeated CTA** after key sections
10. **Short form:** only fields you truly need
11. **Fast loading and mobile-first**
12. **Trust elements:** privacy note, security badges, contact details

(What Google considers a good experience: [Which attributes describe a good landing page experience?](/blog/good-landing-page-experience/))

## Landing page examples

- **Dental clinic ad:** "New patient check-up and clean for $99", before/after photos, reviews, booking widget, WhatsApp button
- **SaaS:** "Automate your invoices in 10 minutes", UI animation, logos, free trial form
- **Coach:** free webinar registration with speaker bio, agenda and countdown
- **E-commerce:** single product page for an ad with video, reviews, bundle offer

## How much does it cost to build a landing page?

| Option | Typical cost |
|---|---|
| DIY with a builder | Builder subscription (~$0–$100+/month) |
| Freelancer | ~$500–$3,000 per page |
| Agency (with copy, design, testing) | ~$2,000–$10,000+ |

Costs rise with custom copywriting, design, animation, integrations (CRM, WhatsApp, booking) and A/B testing. (More: [How much does a website cost?](/blog/how-much-does-a-website-cost/))

## How to improve landing page conversion

- **Match message** to each ad group or audience
- **Test headlines** first; they have the biggest impact
- **Reduce form fields**
- **Speed up the page** (compress images, minimise scripts)
- **Add stronger proof** near the CTA
- **Use a single, specific CTA** ("Get my free quote")
- **Remove navigation and distractions**
- **Add live chat or WhatsApp** for questions (see [How to create an AI chatbot](/blog/how-to-create-an-ai-chatbot/))
- **Follow up automatically:** instant confirmation emails or WhatsApp messages and CRM entry (see [How to automate your sales process](/blog/how-to-automate-your-sales-process/))

Typical conversion rates vary widely by industry and traffic source, often a few percent up to 10%+ for well-targeted offers.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a landing page on a website?
A focused page built for a single goal, such as getting leads or sales, where visitors arrive from an ad, email or link, with one offer and one call to action.

### What is the difference between a landing page and a homepage?
A homepage introduces the whole business and links everywhere; a landing page concentrates on one offer and action, often with no navigation.

### How much does it cost to build a landing page?
DIY tools cost a monthly subscription; freelancers often charge about $500–$3,000 per page; agencies charge more for copy, design and testing.

### Do I need a landing page for ads?
It's strongly recommended. Sending ad traffic to a dedicated, matching landing page usually converts better than sending it to a homepage.

### What makes a good landing page?
A headline matching the ad, clear benefits, strong proof, one call to action, a short form, fast loading and a great mobile experience.

### Can a landing page be part of my website?
Yes. Landing pages can live on your main domain (e.g., yoursite.com/offer) or a subdomain, often hidden from main navigation.
