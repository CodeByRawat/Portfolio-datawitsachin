<p class="lede">Yes, if you use a fireplace or wood stove. Fire safety guidelines recommend an <strong>annual chimney inspection</strong>, with <strong>cleaning when needed</strong>. Burning wood leaves <strong>creosote</strong>, which is flammable and causes <strong>chimney fires</strong>. Chimneys can also be blocked by <strong>nests and debris</strong>, which can push <strong>carbon monoxide</strong> back into your home.</p>

## Why chimneys need sweeping

- **Creosote** builds up and can ignite
- **Blockages** from birds, animals, leaves and debris
- **Damage** like cracked liners, crowns or flue tiles
- **Carbon monoxide** risk from poor venting

## How often?

| Use | Guideline |
|---|---|
| Wood fireplace or stove | Inspect yearly, clean as needed (often yearly) |
| Heavy wood stove use | May need cleaning more than once a year |
| Gas fireplace | Annual inspection, less frequent cleaning |
| Unused chimney | Inspect before using again |

## Signs you need a sweep

- Smoky smell or smoke in the room
- Black, flaky or tar-like deposits in the firebox
- Poor draft or fires that are hard to start
- Animal sounds or debris falling
- Haven't had it checked in over a year

## Can you have a fire before a chimney sweep?

If it's been inspected recently and nothing has changed, usually yes. If it's been more than a year, or you're unsure, have it inspected first.

## Can I sweep it myself?

DIY kits exist, but a professional also inspects for damage you can't see. Many homeowners use a pro at least for the annual inspection.

## Do chimney sweeps work in winter?

Yes, but autumn is the busiest time. Booking in spring or summer is easier.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I really need a chimney sweep?
Yes, if you use your fireplace or stove; yearly inspections help prevent fires.

### How often should a chimney be swept?
Inspect yearly and clean as needed, often once a year for wood burners.

### Do gas fireplaces need a chimney sweep?
They need annual inspections, though less cleaning.

### Can you have a fire before a chimney sweep?
If it was inspected recently and nothing changed, usually yes.

### What happens if you don't clean your chimney?
Creosote builds up, raising the risk of chimney fires and blockages.
