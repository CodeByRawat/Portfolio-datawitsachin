<p class="lede">Pool service is usually priced as a <strong>monthly fee for weekly visits</strong>, either with <strong>chemicals included</strong> or <strong>chemicals billed separately</strong>. Price depends on <strong>pool size, type (chlorine, salt), debris from trees, enclosures, spa add-ons and your local market</strong>. One-time cleanings, openings/closings and repairs are priced separately.</p>

## What's included in weekly pool service

- Skimming and emptying baskets
- Brushing walls and steps
- Vacuuming as needed
- Testing and balancing chemicals
- Checking equipment and filter pressure
- Periodic filter cleaning (sometimes extra)

## Pricing models

| Model | How it works |
|---|---|
| Chemicals included | One predictable monthly price |
| Chemicals extra | Lower base fee plus chemicals used |
| Per visit | For occasional or seasonal service |

## What affects pool service prices

Pool size and depth, attached spa, tree debris, screen enclosure, pool type, equipment condition, access and local labour costs.

## Extra services

Green pool cleanups, filter cleaning, acid washes, openings and closings, equipment repairs and upgrades.

## Choosing a pool service company

Ask what's included, how chemicals are billed, how they report each visit, whether they're insured, and how they handle repairs. Read reviews.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does pool service cost per month?
It depends on pool size, location and whether chemicals are included; most companies quote a monthly fee for weekly service.

### What does monthly pool maintenance include?
Skimming, brushing, vacuuming, chemical balancing, basket emptying and equipment checks.

### Are chemicals included in pool service?
Some companies include them; others bill separately.

### How long does a pool cleaning take?
Routine weekly service often takes a short visit; deep cleanings take longer.

### Is pool service worth it?
For many owners, yes: it saves time and prevents costly chemical or equipment problems.

### How much does a one-time pool cleaning cost?
More than a routine visit, especially for green or neglected pools.
