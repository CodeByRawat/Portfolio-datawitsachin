<p class="lede">Professional carpet cleaning usually costs <strong>about $30 to $75 per room</strong> or <strong>$0.25 to $0.50 per square foot</strong>, so most homes pay <strong>$125 to $300</strong> for a whole-house clean. Stairs, pet treatments, rugs and upholstery cost extra. Prices vary a lot by city.</p>

These are typical US ranges; get a few quotes locally.

## Average carpet cleaning prices

| Service | Typical price |
|---|---|
| Per room | $30 – $75 |
| Per square foot | $0.25 – $0.50 |
| Whole house (3–5 rooms) | $125 – $300 |
| Stairs | $2 – $5 per step |
| Hallway | Often priced as half a room |
| Area rug | $2 – $5 per sq ft (in-plant) |
| Sofa | $100 – $200 |
| Pet odour treatment | $25 – $100+ |
| Stain protectant | $0.10 – $0.25 per sq ft |
| Minimum charge | Often $100 – $150 |

## What changes the price

- Size of the area
- How dirty the carpet is
- Steam (hot water extraction) vs low-moisture cleaning
- Pet urine and heavy stains
- Moving furniture
- Your location

## Watch out for bait pricing

Very low "any 3 rooms" deals often add charges on the day. Ask for the full price, including pre-treatment and furniture moving, before booking.

## How often to clean carpets

Most manufacturers recommend professional cleaning every 12–18 months; homes with pets or kids often need it every 6–12 months.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does professional carpet cleaning cost?
Usually $30 to $75 per room, or $125 to $300 for most homes.

### How much does it cost to get carpets cleaned per square foot?
About $0.25 to $0.50 per square foot.

### How much does rug cleaning cost?
Usually $2 to $5 per square foot for professional in-plant cleaning.

### Is steam cleaning more expensive?
Hot water extraction is the standard and is often similar in price to other methods.

### Is professional carpet cleaning worth it?
Yes for deep dirt, pet stains and odours, and to keep carpet warranties valid.
