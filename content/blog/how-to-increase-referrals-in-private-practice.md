<p class="lede">To increase referrals in private practice: build <strong>genuine relationships with referring providers</strong> (visits, lunch-and-learns, clear speciality info), make <strong>referring effortless</strong> (simple forms, fast scheduling, a direct line), <strong>close the loop</strong> with prompt consult notes, deliver outstanding <strong>patient experience</strong> that earns word-of-mouth and reviews, partner with <strong>community organisations</strong>, and <strong>track referral sources</strong> so you invest in what works.</p>

## Provider referrals

1. **Identify referral sources:** GPs, specialists, dentists, physios, therapists, schools, employers
2. **Introduce yourself** with a one-page summary of services, conditions treated, insurance accepted and availability
3. **Lunch-and-learns** or short case discussions
4. **Make referring easy:** online referral form, fax/e-referral, direct phone line, same-week appointments
5. **Close the loop:** send consult notes quickly and thank referrers
6. **Stay in touch:** periodic updates and new service announcements

## Patient referrals

- Excellent experience: short waits, clear communication, follow-up calls
- Ask satisfied patients to recommend you (follow local healthcare advertising and incentive rules)
- Make it easy to share: website links, review requests
- Collect **Google reviews** (see [How to create a Google Business page](/blog/how-to-create-a-google-business-page/))

## Community referrals

Talks at community centres, gyms, schools, employers; health fairs; partnerships with complementary non-medical providers (fitness, nutrition, wellness).

## Online presence that supports referrals

Referrers and patients check you online. A professional website with clear services, bios, insurance info and online booking reassures them (see [How to make a booking website](/blog/how-to-make-a-booking-website/)).

## Track referrals

Ask every new patient "How did you hear about us?", record referring providers in your EHR/CRM, and review monthly: which sources send the most and best-fit patients.

## Automate follow-ups

Automatic thank-you notes to referrers, appointment confirmations to patients, consult note delivery reminders and review requests (see [Automation in healthcare](/blog/automation-in-healthcare/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I increase referrals to my private practice?
Build relationships with referring providers, make referring easy, send prompt consult notes, deliver great patient experiences, partner with community organisations and track sources.

### How do I get doctors to refer patients to me?
Introduce your services clearly, offer fast access for their patients, communicate outcomes promptly and maintain regular, helpful contact.

### Can I give incentives for referrals in healthcare?
Rules are strict in many jurisdictions (e.g., anti-kickback laws); check regulations before offering anything of value for referrals.

### How do I track referral sources?
Ask every new patient, record the source in your EHR or CRM and review reports monthly.

### Do online reviews affect referrals?
Yes. Patients and referring providers often check reviews before choosing a practice.

### What is the fastest way to get more referrals?
Contact your top potential referrers personally and offer quick appointment access with a simple referral process.
