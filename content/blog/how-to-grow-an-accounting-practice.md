<p class="lede">To grow an accounting practice, increase <strong>revenue per client</strong> with <strong>add-on and advisory services</strong>, move to <strong>packaged and value-based pricing</strong>, focus on <strong>ideal clients</strong> (and let go of unprofitable ones), win <strong>new clients through referrals and niche marketing</strong>, and create <strong>capacity</strong> with standardised processes, automation and the right hires.</p>

## Add-on services for a CPA firm

| Service | Why clients buy it |
|---|---|
| **Tax planning** (not just preparation) | Saves money proactively |
| **Monthly bookkeeping** | Reliable books, recurring revenue |
| **Payroll** | Compliance and convenience |
| **Fractional CFO / advisory** | Strategy, KPIs, growth decisions |
| **Cash flow forecasting and budgeting** | Planning and financing |
| **Bookkeeping clean-up/catch-up** | One-off high-value projects |
| **Entity selection and setup** | New businesses |
| **Sales tax/VAT compliance** | Especially for e-commerce |
| **Technology/app stack consulting** | Automation and integrations (see [What tools do accountants use?](/blog/what-tools-do-accountants-use/)) |
| **Financial dashboards** | Visibility (see [What is business intelligence?](/blog/what-is-business-intelligence/)) |
| **Succession and exit planning** | Business owners preparing to sell |

## Pricing for growth

- **Packages** (Essentials, Growth, CFO) bundling services monthly
- **Value pricing** for advisory based on outcomes
- **Annual price reviews**
- **Scope management** with clear engagement letters

## Client segmentation

Rank clients by revenue, profitability, effort and fit. Serve A-clients exceptionally, upgrade B-clients, reprice or release C-clients. (Profitability measurement concepts: [How to grow a law firm](/blog/how-to-grow-a-law-firm/).)

## Win new clients

Referral partners, niche content, webinars, Google presence (see [How to get accounting clients](/blog/how-to-get-accounting-clients/)).

## Create capacity

- **Standardise** workflows and checklists
- **Automate** document collection, reminders, data capture and reporting (see [What are automated financial systems?](/blog/what-are-automated-financial-systems/))
- **Hire or outsource** bookkeeping and admin
- **Client portal** to reduce email back-and-forth

## Manage the firm

Track revenue per client and per employee, realisation, WIP, AR days, client retention and capacity utilisation; review monthly.

## Frequently asked questions

### How can I grow my accounting practice?
Add advisory and recurring services, switch to packaged or value pricing, focus on ideal clients, win referrals and create capacity with automation and hiring.

### What add-on services can a CPA firm offer?
Tax planning, bookkeeping, payroll, fractional CFO and advisory, cash flow forecasting, clean-up projects, entity setup, sales tax compliance and tech consulting.

### How do accounting firms increase revenue per client?
By bundling services into packages, adding advisory and pricing based on value rather than hours.

### How do I manage an accounting firm efficiently?
Standardise processes, use practice management software, automate repetitive work and track key metrics.

### Should accountants offer advisory services?
Yes; advisory adds value for clients and higher margins for firms, especially for growing businesses.

### When should an accounting firm hire?
When recurring revenue and workload consistently exceed capacity after processes are standardised and automated.
