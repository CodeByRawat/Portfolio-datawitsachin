<p class="lede">You <strong>can</strong> physically stick car tint on a house window, but it's <strong>not recommended</strong>. Automotive film is made for <strong>curved, tempered car glass</strong> and is often thinner, with different adhesives. On home windows it may <strong>peel, bubble or fade faster</strong>, can contribute to <strong>thermal stress cracks</strong> on some glass, and may <strong>void your window warranty</strong>. Use <strong>residential window film</strong> instead.</p>

## Auto film vs residential film

| | Automotive film | Residential film |
|---|---|---|
| Designed for | Curved car glass | Flat home glass |
| Installation | Heat-shrunk to fit curves | Applied flat |
| Size | Narrower rolls | Wider rolls for big windows |
| Goals | Privacy, heat, legal darkness limits | Heat, glare, UV, privacy, energy savings |
| Glass compatibility | Tempered auto glass | Checked for double-pane and low-E glass |
| Warranty | Auto installs | Residential film and window warranties |

## Risks of using car tint at home

- **Thermal stress**: dark, heat-absorbing film on some double-pane or large windows can raise the risk of cracking
- **Window warranty**: some manufacturers void warranties if non-approved film is used
- **Appearance**: seams on wide windows, bubbles and uneven colour
- **Durability**: may not last as long on home glass

## Choosing the right home film

- Heat-rejection and ceramic films for hot climates
- Low-reflective films for a natural look
- Privacy and decorative films for bathrooms and offices
- Safety and security films to hold glass together
- Check film compatibility charts for your glass type

## DIY or professional?

DIY residential film works for small windows. Professionals check glass compatibility, offer film warranties and handle large or hard-to-reach windows.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you use car tint on house windows?
You can, but it's not recommended; residential film is better and safer for home glass.

### Will car tint crack my house windows?
Dark film can increase thermal stress risk on some glass types.

### Does window film void window warranties?
Some manufacturers void warranties for non-approved film; check first.

### What tint is best for house windows?
Residential heat-rejection or ceramic film suited to your glass.

### Can I install home window film myself?
Yes for small windows; pros are better for large or double-pane windows.
