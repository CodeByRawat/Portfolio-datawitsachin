<p class="lede">To start a gutter business, <strong>learn gutter installation</strong> (work with an installer or roofer first), get <strong>licensed where required and insured</strong>, buy a <strong>seamless gutter machine, trailer, ladders and tools</strong>, open <strong>coil and parts supplier accounts</strong>, and get customers from <strong>homeowners, roofers and builders</strong>. Startup costs are often <strong>$20,000 to $60,000</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Learn the trade

Work for a gutter or roofing company to learn measuring, pitching gutters, hangers, downspouts, corners, and ladder safety.

## Step 2: Choose your services

| Service | Notes |
|---|---|
| Seamless aluminium gutters | Most common, made on site |
| Copper and steel gutters | Premium, higher price |
| Gutter guards | High-margin add-on |
| Gutter repair | Small, quick jobs |
| Gutter cleaning | Recurring revenue |
| Fascia and soffit repair | Common add-on |

## Step 3: Licenses and insurance

LLC, EIN, business license, a contractor or home improvement license where required, general liability and workers' comp (ladder work is high-risk).

## Step 4: Equipment and startup costs

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Seamless gutter machine | $8,000 – $15,000 (used cheaper) |
| Trailer or truck rack | $3,000 – $10,000 |
| Ladders and stabilisers | $1,000 – $3,000 |
| Hand tools, crimpers, drills, saws | $500 – $2,000 |
| Coil and parts inventory | $2,000 – $5,000 |
| Insurance and licenses | $3,000 – $8,000 per year |

## Step 5: Pricing

Price per linear foot, plus downspouts, corners, guards, removal of old gutters and difficult access (steep or tall homes). Set a minimum job price.

## Step 6: Get customers

1. Google Business Profile and reviews
2. A website with free estimate booking and photos
3. Roofers (gutters often come off during roof replacement)
4. Builders and remodelers
5. Insurance claims after storms

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a gutter business?
Learn installation, get licensed and insured, buy a seamless machine and tools, and market to homeowners and roofers.

### How much does it cost to start a gutter business?
Often $20,000 to $60,000, mostly for the seamless machine, trailer and ladders.

### What do you need to start a gutter installation business?
Experience, a gutter machine, ladders, tools, insurance and supplier accounts.

### Do I need a license to install gutters?
Some states require a contractor or home improvement license.

### Can I start with gutter cleaning first?
Yes. It's cheaper and builds a customer list for installations.
