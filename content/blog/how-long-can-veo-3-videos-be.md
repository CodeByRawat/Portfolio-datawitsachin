<p class="lede"><strong>Veo 3 generates short clips</strong>: in the Gemini app, each video generation has typically been <strong>about 8 seconds</strong> long (with audio) at the time of writing. To make a <strong>longer video</strong>, you create multiple clips and <strong>extend or stitch them together</strong>, for example with <strong>Google Flow's scene-building tools</strong> or a video editor. Limits on length, resolution and daily generations depend on the model version, the product (Gemini app, Flow, API) and your plan, and Google updates them frequently.</p>

## Veo video length by product (typical, subject to change)

| Where | Clip length per generation | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Gemini app** | ~8 seconds | Daily limits by plan; text-to-video and image-to-video on eligible plans |
| **Google Flow** | Short clips (~8 s) that can be extended and arranged into scenes | Filmmaking tools for longer sequences |
| **Gemini API / Vertex AI** | Short clips, parameters vary by model | Billed per second of video; developer controls |

Newer Veo versions (e.g., Veo 3.1 and later) may change lengths, aspect ratios and features; check Google's current documentation.

## Why are AI video clips so short?

- **Compute cost:** each second of video is expensive to generate
- **Consistency:** longer clips risk drifting characters, objects and physics
- **Quality control:** short clips are easier to direct and regenerate

## How to make Gemini create a video

1. Open the **Gemini app** (eligible plan and region)
2. Choose the **video** option (or ask "create a video of…")
3. Write a detailed prompt: subject, action, setting, camera movement, style, audio
4. Optionally start from an **image** (image-to-video)
5. Generate, review and download

(Prompting tips: [Which AI tools generate videos from text?](/blog/which-ai-tools-generate-videos-from-text/))

### Why can I only create a few videos?
Gemini sets **daily video generation limits** that vary by subscription; higher tiers get more. Limits reset periodically.

## How to make longer AI videos

1. **Plan a storyboard** with shots of 4–8 seconds each
2. Keep a consistent **character/product description** and style in every prompt
3. Use **reference images** for consistency (image-to-video, ingredients features)
4. **Extend** clips in Flow where available
5. **Edit** shots together in CapCut, Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve
6. Add **voiceover, music, captions and motion graphics** for a finished video (see [How to make motion graphics](/blog/how-to-make-motion-graphics/))

## How to make AI video for free

- Free tiers or trials of AI video tools (limits and watermarks apply)
- Google's offers for students or promotions when available
- Open-source video models run locally (requires powerful GPUs)
- Template-based editors with stock footage (Canva, CapCut)

## Can you get unlimited Veo generations?

Not on consumer plans; all plans have limits. Developers pay per second via the API, which allows higher volumes at a cost. Be wary of third-party sites promising "unlimited Veo" access.

## From AI clips to a professional promo

AI clips are great for B-roll and concept shots, but brand promos usually need real product footage or UI, accurate logos and text, consistent style, and edited pacing to a script. A hybrid approach (AI shots + designed motion graphics + editing) gives the best results. (See [How to make a promotional video](/blog/how-to-make-a-promotional-video/).)
