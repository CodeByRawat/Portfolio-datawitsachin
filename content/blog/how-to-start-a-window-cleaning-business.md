<p class="lede">You can start a window cleaning business with <strong>low startup costs</strong>: choose <strong>residential, commercial or both</strong>, register the business, get <strong>liability insurance</strong>, buy <strong>basic squeegee and water-fed pole equipment</strong>, set <strong>per-pane or per-job pricing</strong>, and win your first customers with <strong>door-to-door flyers, Google reviews and recurring service plans</strong>. Growth comes from routes of repeat clients.</p>

## Residential vs commercial window cleaning

| | Residential | Commercial |
|---|---|---|
| Customers | Homeowners | Shops, offices, restaurants |
| Frequency | 2–4 times a year | Weekly or monthly |
| Job size | Larger per visit | Smaller, but recurring |
| Selling | Flyers, reviews, referrals | Walk-in visits to storefronts |
| Risk | Ladders, second floors | Ground-level storefronts are easiest; high-rise needs special training |

Many owners start with **storefront routes** for steady weekly cash and add residential jobs for bigger tickets.

## Step-by-step setup

1. **Pick a name and register** the business (sole proprietorship or LLC).
2. **Get a business licence** if your city requires one (see [Do you need a license to wash windows?](/blog/do-you-need-a-license-to-wash-windows/)).
3. **Buy general liability insurance**; add workers' comp when you hire.
4. **Buy equipment** (below).
5. **Set prices** and a simple quote method.
6. **Set up booking, invoicing and reminders**.
7. **Market locally** and build a route.

## Equipment you need

- Squeegees (several sizes) and channels with rubbers
- Strip washers (applicators) and buckets
- Scrapers and microfibre towels
- Extension poles and a safe ladder with stabilisers
- Water-fed pole system with a pure water filter (for upper windows without ladders)
- Screen-cleaning supplies and track brushes
- Safety gear

Starting with hand tools keeps costs low; a water-fed pole is the biggest upgrade and makes two-storey homes faster and safer.

## Pricing your jobs

Window cleaners usually price **per pane**, **per window**, or **per job** with a minimum charge. Add for inside cleaning, screens, tracks, hard-water stains, high windows and post-construction cleanup. For a full guide, read [How to bid window cleaning jobs](/blog/how-to-bid-window-cleaning-jobs/).

## How to sell window cleaning services

- **Storefronts:** walk in, offer a free first clean or a simple weekly price.
- **Homes:** door hangers in neighbourhoods where you already have a job.
- **Before-and-after photos** on Google Business Profile and social media.
- **Bundle services:** gutter cleaning, screen repair, pressure washing, solar panel cleaning.
- **Recurring plans:** discounts for quarterly or monthly service.
- **Website with online quotes** so homeowners can book at night.

## Growing the business

Build dense routes, raise prices as you get busy, hire and train a helper, add commercial contracts, and track customers' next due dates so you can re-book them automatically. Read [Is a window cleaning business profitable?](/blog/is-a-window-cleaning-business-profitable/) for the numbers behind growth.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a window washing business?
Register the business, get insurance, buy squeegees, poles and a ladder, set prices, and find customers through storefront visits, flyers and reviews.

### How much does it cost to start a window cleaning business?
It is one of the cheapest service businesses to start; hand tools, insurance and marketing are the main costs, with a water-fed pole system as an optional larger purchase.

### Can I start a window cleaning business with no money?
You can start very lean with basic hand tools and free marketing (door-to-door and Google Business Profile), then reinvest early income into better equipment.

### Is residential or commercial window cleaning better?
Commercial storefronts give steady weekly income; residential gives larger jobs. Many businesses do both.

### Can window cleaning be a side hustle?
Yes; evening and weekend residential jobs and early-morning storefront routes fit around a day job.

### What do I need to start a window cleaning business?
A registered business, insurance, cleaning equipment, a safe ladder or water-fed pole, pricing, and a way to take bookings.
