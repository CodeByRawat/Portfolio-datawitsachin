<p class="lede">To start a wedding floral business, <strong>build a portfolio</strong> (styled shoots and smaller weddings), create a <strong>consultation and proposal process</strong>, use <strong>contracts with deposits</strong>, price for <strong>flowers, labour, rentals and delivery</strong>, plan <strong>sourcing and processing</strong> for each wedding, hire <strong>help for install days</strong>, and get on <strong>planner and venue referral lists</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Portfolio

Do styled shoots with photographers and planners, and start with smaller weddings and elopements. Post high-quality photos on Instagram and your website.

## Step 2: Consultations and proposals

Meet couples to understand colours, style, budget and venue. Send a clear proposal listing every item: bridal bouquet, bridesmaid bouquets, boutonnieres, centrepieces, ceremony arch, installations.

## Step 3: Contracts and deposits

Use contracts covering deposits (often non-refundable), payment schedules, substitutions, delivery and setup, rental returns and cancellation.

## Step 4: Pricing

| Cost | Notes |
|---|---|
| Flowers and greenery | Often marked up about 3× |
| Labour and design | Included in markup or separate |
| Hard goods | Vases, stands, mechanics |
| Rentals | Arches, candles, linens |
| Delivery, setup, teardown | Separate fees |

Set a wedding minimum once you're established.

## Step 5: Sourcing and processing

Order flowers 1–2 weeks ahead, receive and condition them days before, and plan a production timeline.

## Step 6: Wedding day

Bring a trained team, a setup checklist, an emergency kit, and plan teardown and rental pickup.

## Step 7: Get bookings

Wedding planners, venues, photographers, bridal shows, Instagram, Pinterest and wedding directories.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a wedding flower business?
Build a portfolio, create proposals and contracts, price carefully and partner with planners and venues.

### How do wedding florists price?
Flowers marked up, plus labour, rentals, delivery and setup fees.

### Do I need a shop to be a wedding florist?
No. Many work from a studio with a cooler.

### How far ahead should flowers be ordered?
Often 1–2 weeks before the wedding.

### How do wedding florists get clients?
Planners, venues, photographers, social media and directories.
