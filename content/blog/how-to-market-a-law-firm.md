<p class="lede"><strong>Legal marketing</strong> is how law firms attract, convert and retain clients: positioning the firm, building visibility (website, SEO, Google Business Profile, content, directories, ads), earning trust (reviews, expertise, reputation) and converting inquiries through fast intake. To market a law firm, <strong>pick clear practice areas and client types</strong>, build a <strong>conversion-focused website</strong>, invest in <strong>local SEO and helpful content</strong>, gather <strong>reviews and referrals</strong>, use <strong>paid search selectively</strong>, and <strong>track cost per client</strong> by channel, all within lawyer advertising rules.</p>

## Step 1: Positioning

Define practice areas, ideal clients, geography and what makes you different (speed, fixed fees, niche expertise, language, results).

## Step 2: Website

- Separate pages per practice area and location
- Attorney bios with credentials
- FAQs answering real client questions
- Clear contact options and consultation booking
- Fast, mobile-friendly, secure
(See [How to create a website for your business](/blog/how-to-create-a-website-for-your-business/).)

## Step 3: Local SEO and content

- Google Business Profile with categories, photos and reviews
- Practice area + city pages
- Articles and videos answering common questions
- Citations in legal and local directories (see [What are local citations?](/blog/what-are-local-citations/))

## Step 4: Reviews and reputation

Ask satisfied clients for reviews where permitted, respond professionally, publish case studies within ethical limits, speak and write in your community.

## Step 5: Paid advertising

- **Google Search ads** for high-intent searches
- **Local Services Ads** (where available for legal categories)
- **Social ads** for awareness and specific practice areas
Track cost per signed case, not just clicks.

## Step 6: Email and referral marketing

Newsletters to past clients and referral partners; regular check-ins with accountants, advisers and other lawyers.

## Step 7: Intake and follow-up

Fast response, intake scripts, consultation scheduling and automated follow-ups (see [Legal intake software](/blog/legal-intake-software/), [Legal workflow automation](/blog/legal-workflow-automation/)).

## Marketing a small law firm

Focus on 2–3 channels: referrals, Google Business Profile + website SEO, and one paid or content channel you can sustain.

## How much do law firms spend on marketing?

It varies widely. Many firms budget a percentage of revenue (commonly cited ranges are in the low-to-high single digits, higher for growth-focused consumer practices like personal injury). Base budgets on target cases and cost per case by channel.

## Measure ROI

Leads by source, consultation rate, signed clients, revenue per client, cost per signed case, and review growth.

## Ethics rules

Follow your jurisdiction's rules on advertising, testimonials, solicitation, specialisation claims and disclaimers.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is legal marketing?
The strategies law firms use to attract, convert and retain clients, including websites, SEO, content, reviews, directories, ads, referrals and intake.

### How do you market a law firm?
Define your positioning, build a strong website, invest in local SEO and content, gather reviews, use targeted ads, nurture referrals and measure cost per client.

### How do you market a small law firm on a budget?
Focus on referrals, Google Business Profile, a well-optimised website and helpful content before adding paid channels.

### How much should a law firm spend on marketing?
It varies; many budget a percentage of revenue and adjust based on cost per signed client.

### Can lawyers advertise on social media?
Yes, within professional conduct rules on advertising and solicitation.

### What is the best marketing for lawyers?
Referrals and local search visibility typically deliver the best return, supported by fast intake.
