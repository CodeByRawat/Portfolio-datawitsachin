<p class="lede">A home maintenance business keeps homes in good shape through <strong>scheduled seasonal visits and repairs</strong>. To start one: define a <strong>service menu and seasonal checklists</strong>, package them into <strong>annual or monthly membership plans</strong>, check <strong>licence rules</strong>, get <strong>insurance</strong>, use <strong>scheduling and billing software</strong>, and market to <strong>busy homeowners, seniors, landlords, realtors and property managers</strong>.</p>

## Typical services

- Seasonal home check-ups (spring/fall)
- HVAC filter changes and smoke detector checks
- Gutter cleaning and minor roof checks
- Caulking, weatherstripping and minor repairs
- Dryer vent cleaning, water heater flushing
- Exterior washing and deck maintenance
- Small handyman repairs found during visits

## Membership plans

| Plan | Includes |
|---|---|
| Basic | Two seasonal visits with checklist |
| Standard | Quarterly visits + small repairs time |
| Premium | Monthly visits, priority scheduling, discounts |

Recurring billing creates predictable revenue and steady schedules.

## Setup steps

Register the business, check licence limits for repair work, get liability insurance, buy tools, build checklists and reports, set up scheduling and auto-billing, and create a website with plan sign-ups.

## Marketing

Target new homeowners, seniors and families, partner with realtors and home inspectors, offer landlord packages, and collect reviews.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a home maintenance business?
Define services and checklists, create membership plans, register and insure, set up scheduling and billing, and market to homeowners and partners.

### What services does a home maintenance company offer?
Seasonal inspections, filter changes, gutter cleaning, caulking, minor repairs and preventive tasks.

### Are home maintenance memberships profitable?
They provide recurring revenue and lead to additional repair work.

### Do I need a licence for a home maintenance business?
Basic maintenance often doesn't, but larger repairs or trade work may; check state rules.

### Who buys home maintenance plans?
Busy homeowners, seniors, landlords and new homeowners.

### How do I market a home maintenance business?
Realtor and inspector partnerships, Google reviews, local social media and a website with plan sign-ups.
