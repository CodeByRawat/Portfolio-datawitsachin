<p class="lede">Pest control is generally considered a <strong>good business</strong>: demand is <strong>steady and recession-resistant</strong>, customers sign up for <strong>recurring service plans</strong>, startup costs are <strong>moderate</strong>, and the business can scale with routes and technicians. The challenges are <strong>licensing and regulations</strong>, <strong>chemical safety and liability</strong>, <strong>seasonality</strong>, and <strong>competition from large national brands</strong>.</p>

## Why pest control works as a business

- Pests are a recurring problem, so customers need ongoing service
- Quarterly/monthly plans create predictable revenue
- Route density makes technicians efficient
- Commercial accounts (restaurants, food facilities, property managers) need regular service by law or standard

## Pros and cons

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Recurring revenue | Licensing and record-keeping rules |
| Moderate startup costs | Chemical handling and liability |
| Steady demand | Seasonal peaks and dips |
| Scalable with routes | Competition from big brands |
| Valuable to buyers when you sell | Hiring and retaining technicians |

## What successful pest control companies do

Focus on recurring plans and retention, build dense routes, deliver excellent customer communication, use software for scheduling and auto-pay, collect reviews, and add specialty services (termites, mosquitoes, wildlife).

## Is it profitable?

Profitability depends on route density, retention, pricing and labour; well-run recurring-plan companies are often attractive acquisition targets.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is pest control a good business?
Yes for many owners, thanks to recurring revenue, steady demand and moderate startup costs.

### Is pest control profitable?
It can be, especially with recurring plans, dense routes and strong customer retention.

### What are the risks of a pest control business?
Regulations, chemical liability, seasonality and competition.

### Is pest control recession-proof?
It's fairly resilient because pest problems continue regardless of the economy.

### Can a pest control business be sold?
Yes; recurring revenue makes pest control companies attractive to buyers.

### Do I need experience to start pest control?
You need training and state licensing; working for an established company first is common.
