<p class="lede">Cleaning businesses win clients by being <strong>easy to find and book</strong> (Google Business Profile, reviews, a website with instant quotes and online booking), by tapping <strong>local word of mouth</strong> (Nextdoor, neighbourhood Facebook groups, referral rewards), and by partnering with people who <strong>regularly need cleaners</strong>: real estate agents, property managers and vacation rental hosts. Converting one-off cleans into <strong>recurring plans</strong> is the key to growth.</p>

## 15 ways to get cleaning clients

1. **Google Business Profile** with photos and reviews (see [How to create a Google Business page](/blog/how-to-create-a-google-business-page/))
2. **Website with instant quote and online booking** (see [How to make a booking website](/blog/how-to-make-a-booking-website/))
3. **Local SEO:** "house cleaning in [area]" pages
4. **Review requests** after every clean
5. **Referral programme:** a discount for each referral
6. **Nextdoor and local Facebook groups**
7. **Real estate agents** (move-in/move-out cleans)
8. **Property managers and landlords**
9. **Airbnb/vacation rental hosts**
10. **Door hangers and flyers** in target neighbourhoods
11. **Before/after photos and short videos** on social media
12. **Local service ads** on Google and Meta
13. **Recurring plan offers** (weekly/biweekly discounts)
14. **Commercial outreach** to offices and clinics (see [How to find commercial cleaning contracts](/blog/how-to-find-commercial-cleaning-contracts/))
15. **Partnerships** with movers, painters and contractors

## How to grow a cleaning business

- Focus on **recurring clients** and retention
- **Route density:** cluster clients geographically
- **Hire and train** with checklists and quality checks
- **Raise prices** annually
- **Add services:** deep cleans, windows, carpets, organising
- **Automate** booking, reminders, invoicing and review requests (see [How to automate your sales process](/blog/how-to-automate-your-sales-process/))

## Convert enquiries into bookings

Respond within minutes, offer instant quotes, make booking simple and follow up on unbooked quotes.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I get clients for my cleaning business?
Optimise Google Business Profile, build a website with instant quotes and booking, ask for reviews and referrals, post in local groups and partner with real estate agents, property managers and rental hosts.

### How can I get customers for a cleaning business fast?
Post in local community groups, offer a first-clean discount, contact real estate agents and property managers, and run small local ads.

### How do cleaning businesses grow?
By building recurring clients, increasing route density, hiring and training staff, adding services and automating admin.

### Are Facebook ads good for cleaning businesses?
They can be, with local targeting, before/after visuals and a clear first-clean offer.

### What is the best marketing for a cleaning business?
Google Business Profile and reviews plus referrals and local community groups typically perform best.

### How do I keep cleaning clients long-term?
Consistent quality, reliable scheduling, clear communication and easy rescheduling and payment.
