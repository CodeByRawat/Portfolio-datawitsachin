<p class="lede">A charcuterie cart business sets up a <strong>decorated cart</strong> at events where guests receive <strong>charcuterie cups or plates</strong>. To start, get a <strong>cart with food-safe surfaces and cold holding</strong>, prepare food in a <strong>licensed kitchen</strong> with the right <strong>health permits</strong>, carry <strong>insurance</strong>, set <strong>per-guest pricing</strong>, and market to <strong>weddings, showers and corporate events</strong>. Startup costs often run <strong>$3,000 to $15,000</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: The cart

- A cart with smooth, cleanable surfaces
- Cold holding (ice wells, refrigerated drawers or chilled trays)
- Canopy or umbrella for outdoor events
- Branding and décor
- Sneeze guard if required

## Step 2: Permits and kitchen

Most areas require food to be prepared in a licensed kitchen and a health permit for serving at events. Some events also need temporary food permits.

## Step 3: Startup costs

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Cart (new or custom) | $1,500 – $8,000 |
| Cold holding equipment | $300 – $2,000 |
| Permits and certification | $200 – $1,500 |
| Cups, picks, packaging | $200 – $800 |
| Insurance | $500 – $2,000 per year |
| Branding and website | $300 – $2,000 |

## Step 4: Pricing

Price per guest with a minimum (for example a set number of guests), plus travel, setup time and add-ons like dessert cups or drinks.

## Step 5: Operations

Prepare cups shortly before events, transport cold, keep foods within safe time limits, and bring staff for larger events.

## Step 6: Get bookings

Wedding planners and venues, bridal shows, corporate events, Instagram and TikTok, and partnerships with party rental and balloon businesses.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a charcuterie cart business?
A mobile cart serving charcuterie cups or plates at events.

### How much does it cost to start a charcuterie cart?
Often $3,000 to $15,000.

### Do I need a permit for a charcuterie cart?
Usually a health permit and a licensed kitchen for prep.

### How do charcuterie carts charge?
Per guest with minimums, plus travel and add-ons.

### What events book charcuterie carts?
Weddings, showers, birthdays and corporate events.
