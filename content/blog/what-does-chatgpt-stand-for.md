<p class="lede"><strong>ChatGPT stands for "Chat Generative Pre-trained Transformer."</strong> "Chat" means it's designed for conversation; "Generative" means it creates new text (and now images, audio and code); "Pre-trained" means it first learned from a huge amount of text before being refined; and "Transformer" is the type of neural network architecture it's built on. ChatGPT was released by OpenAI on <strong>30 November 2022</strong>.</p>

ChatGPT is one of the fastest-adopted consumer products in history, yet most people who use it daily couldn't say what the letters mean. This guide explains each part of the name, how ChatGPT actually works in plain English, how long it's been around, what it's good and bad at, and how it compares with alternatives.

## What does GPT stand for?

| Letter | Word | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **G** | **Generative** | The model *generates* new content, predicting and producing text one piece at a time rather than retrieving fixed answers |
| **P** | **Pre-trained** | Before being adapted for chat, the model is trained on an enormous dataset of text (and other data) to learn language patterns, facts and reasoning |
| **T** | **Transformer** | The neural network architecture, introduced by Google researchers in the 2017 paper *"Attention Is All You Need"*, which uses "attention" to understand how words relate to each other across long passages |

**"Chat"** was added because ChatGPT is the conversational version: the underlying GPT models were fine-tuned to follow instructions and hold back-and-forth dialogue.

So "ChatGPT" literally means: **a conversational, text-generating, pre-trained transformer model.**

## What is ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is an **AI assistant** made by **OpenAI**. You type (or speak) a question or instruction, and it responds in natural language. It can write, summarise, translate, brainstorm, explain concepts, write and debug code, analyse files and data, create images, search the web, and use tools. It runs on OpenAI's GPT family of **large language models (LLMs)**. (Who's behind it: [Who owns ChatGPT?](/blog/who-owns-chatgpt/))

## How does ChatGPT work?

### 1. Pre-training: learning language
The model is trained on a vast collection of text from books, websites, code and other sources. Its task is simple: **predict the next token** (a token is a word or part of a word). By doing this billions of times, it learns grammar, facts, patterns of reasoning and writing styles. This produces a **base model** that's knowledgeable but not yet a helpful assistant.

### 2. Fine-tuning: learning to be helpful
The base model is then trained on examples of good conversations and instructions (supervised fine-tuning), followed by **reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF)** and related methods: people rate responses, and the model is optimised to produce answers that are more helpful, accurate and safe. (See [What is reinforcement learning?](/blog/what-is-reinforcement-learning/))

### 3. Inference: answering you
When you send a message:
1. Your text is broken into **tokens**.
2. The transformer processes all tokens in the conversation (its **context window**) using attention to work out which parts matter for the reply.
3. It predicts the most suitable next token, adds it, and repeats, generating the response token by token. That's why answers appear word by word.
4. Settings and safety systems shape the output.

### 4. Tools and extra capabilities
Modern ChatGPT doesn't rely on the model alone. It can:
- **Search the web** for current information
- **Run code** to analyse data and create files and charts
- **Read uploaded files** (PDFs, spreadsheets, images)
- **Generate images** and handle voice conversations
- **Remember** preferences across chats (memory, if enabled)
- **Reason** step by step before answering with "thinking" models
- **Take actions** in some products through agent features that browse websites and complete tasks (see [Is ChatGPT agent mode safe?](/blog/is-chatgpt-agent-mode-safe/))

### What ChatGPT is not
It isn't a search engine or database that looks up stored answers, and it doesn't "understand" in the human sense. It generates statistically likely, often very useful text, which is why it can sometimes produce confident errors ("hallucinations"). (Deeper background: [What is a neural network?](/blog/what-is-a-neural-network/) and [How is AI created?](/blog/how-is-ai-created/))

## How long has ChatGPT been around?

ChatGPT launched publicly on **30 November 2022**, so by late 2026 it has been around for about **four years**. But the GPT technology is older:

| Year | Milestone |
|---|---|
| **2015** | OpenAI founded as a non-profit AI research lab |
| **2017** | Google researchers publish the Transformer architecture |
| **2018** | OpenAI releases GPT-1 |
| **2019** | GPT-2 (initially withheld over misuse concerns) |
| **2020** | GPT-3, available through an API |
| **Nov 2022** | **ChatGPT launches** (based on GPT-3.5) and reaches 1 million users within days |
| **2023** | GPT-4 released; ChatGPT Plus subscriptions, plugins, image input, Enterprise plan |
| **2024** | GPT-4o (faster, multimodal: text, voice, images); o1 reasoning models; ChatGPT search |
| **2025** | GPT-4.5, o3 reasoning models, agent capabilities, and GPT-5 |
| **2026** | Continued model updates and more agentic, work-focused features |

ChatGPT now has hundreds of millions of weekly users worldwide.

## What does ChatGPT do well?

- Drafting and rewriting emails, articles, reports and social posts
- Explaining complex topics simply
- Summarising long documents and meetings
- Brainstorming ideas and outlines
- Writing, explaining and debugging code
- Analysing spreadsheets and creating charts
- Translating and adjusting tone
- Creating presentations and documents (see [Can ChatGPT make PowerPoints?](/blog/can-chatgpt-make-powerpoints/))
- Acting as a tutor or study partner (see [How to learn AI](/blog/how-to-learn-ai/))

## Limitations

- **Hallucinations:** can state false information confidently; verify important facts
- **Knowledge cutoff:** the model's built-in knowledge stops at a training date (web search helps)
- **Bias:** reflects patterns in training data
- **Privacy:** be careful with personal or confidential information; check your data settings and company policy
- **Not a professional:** not a substitute for qualified legal, medical or financial advice
- **Inconsistent:** the same question can produce different answers

## Common misspellings people search

"Chat GBT", "Chat GTP" and "Chat GPT" all refer to **ChatGPT**. The correct spelling is one word with GPT in capitals: **ChatGPT**.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does ChatGPT stand for?
ChatGPT stands for Chat Generative Pre-trained Transformer: a conversational AI that generates text using a transformer neural network pre-trained on large amounts of data.

### What does GPT stand for in ChatGPT?
GPT stands for Generative Pre-trained Transformer: generative because it creates content, pre-trained because it learns from massive datasets before fine-tuning, and transformer because of its neural network architecture.

### When did ChatGPT come out?
OpenAI released ChatGPT to the public on 30 November 2022.

### How does ChatGPT work in simple terms?
It has learned patterns in language from huge amounts of text, then been fine-tuned to be helpful. When you ask something, it predicts the best response one token at a time, and can use tools like web search and code execution.

### Who created ChatGPT?
ChatGPT was created by OpenAI, the AI company led by CEO Sam Altman, with Microsoft as a major investor and partner.

### Is ChatGPT free?
Yes, there is a free version with usage limits. Paid plans offer higher limits, more capable models and additional features for individuals and businesses.

### Is ChatGPT the same as GPT-5?
No. ChatGPT is the app and assistant; GPT models such as GPT-4o and GPT-5 are the underlying AI models it uses. ChatGPT may use different models depending on your plan and the task.
