<p class="lede">Solar companies <strong>find customers</strong>, <strong>assess the home and energy use</strong>, <strong>design a system</strong>, help arrange <strong>financing</strong> (cash, loan, lease or PPA), handle <strong>permits and utility interconnection</strong>, <strong>install</strong> the panels and inverter, pass <strong>inspection</strong>, and get <strong>permission to operate</strong> from the utility. They make money on the <strong>system price</strong>, and some also earn from <strong>leases and service</strong>.</p>

## Step-by-step process

1. **Lead and consultation**: customer asks for a quote
2. **Site assessment**: roof condition, shade, electrical panel, energy bills
3. **System design**: size, panel layout, inverter and batteries
4. **Proposal**: price, savings, incentives and financing options
5. **Contract and financing**
6. **Permits**: building and electrical permits, HOA approval
7. **Interconnection application**: to the utility
8. **Installation**: racking, panels, wiring, inverter (often 1–3 days)
9. **Inspection**: local building inspection
10. **Permission to operate (PTO)**: utility approval to turn on

## Financing options

| Option | Who owns the system |
|---|---|
| Cash | Homeowner |
| Solar loan | Homeowner |
| Lease | Solar company or financier |
| Power purchase agreement (PPA) | Solar company; homeowner buys power |

## Types of solar companies

- **Installers** design and install
- **Dealers / sales companies** sell and partner with installers
- **Lease/PPA providers** own systems on customers' roofs
- **Manufacturers and distributors** make and supply equipment

## Do energy companies pay for solar?

Under net metering or similar programs, utilities credit or pay homeowners for extra electricity sent to the grid. The rate depends on local rules.

## How solar companies make money

Through the system price (equipment, labour, overhead and margin), lease or PPA payments, battery add-ons and service. More: [Is the solar energy business profitable?](/blog/is-solar-energy-business-profitable/) and [How to start a solar panel business](/blog/how-to-start-a-solar-panel-business/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do solar companies work?
They sell, design, finance, permit and install solar systems, then get utility approval.

### How long does solar installation take?
Installation often takes 1–3 days; the full process can take weeks to months.

### Do energy companies pay for solar energy?
With net metering, utilities credit or pay for excess power.

### What is a solar PPA?
An agreement to buy power from panels the solar company owns.

### How do solar companies make money?
From system sales, leases or PPAs, batteries and service.
