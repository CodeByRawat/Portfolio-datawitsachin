<p class="lede">To start an insurance agency: choose your <strong>lines</strong> (property & casualty, life & health, commercial) and <strong>model</strong> (independent, captive/franchise, or joining a <strong>network/aggregator</strong> for carrier access), obtain the required <strong>licences</strong>, form a <strong>business entity</strong> and agency licence, buy <strong>errors & omissions (E&O) insurance</strong>, secure <strong>carrier appointments</strong>, set up an <strong>agency management system</strong>, and launch a <strong>website and lead generation</strong> plan. Many agents start from home to keep costs low.</p>

## Step 1: Choose lines and model

| Model | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| **Independent agency** | Many carriers, own your book | Harder to get appointments early |
| **Captive/franchise** | Brand, training, leads | Less independence, one carrier |
| **Network/aggregator or cluster** | Carrier access, support | Revenue sharing |

(What an insurance marketing organisation (IMO) is: a wholesale distributor, common in life and annuities, that provides carrier contracts, training and support to agents.)

## Step 2: Licensing

Pre-licensing education and exams for each line (e.g., P&C, life & health), agency/business entity licence where required, continuing education.

## Step 3: Business setup

Entity, tax registration, bank account, E&O insurance, office or home office, compliance procedures and data security.

## Step 4: Carrier appointments

Apply to carriers directly or through networks/aggregators; carriers often look for experience and production potential.

## Step 5: Technology

Agency management system (AMS), comparative rating software, CRM, e-signature, quoting tools, phone system and automation for renewals and follow-ups (see [How to use a CRM](/blog/how-to-use-a-crm/)).

## Startup costs

Licensing and exams, E&O insurance, entity and agency licensing fees, technology subscriptions, website and marketing, and working capital (commission income takes time to build).

## Starting from home

Allowed in many places; check local zoning and licensing rules, and use virtual meeting tools and a professional mailing address if needed.

## Step 6: Website and lead generation

Website with lines offered, quote request forms, reviews and local pages; Google Business Profile; referrals and partnerships (see [How to get insurance leads](/blog/how-to-get-insurance-leads/)).

## Step 7: Grow

Retention, cross-selling and hiring producers (see [How to grow an insurance agency](/blog/how-to-grow-an-insurance-agency/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start an insurance agency?
Choose lines and a model, get licensed, form an entity with an agency licence, buy E&O insurance, secure carrier appointments, set up an agency management system and market your agency.

### Can I start an insurance agency from home?
Often yes, subject to local zoning and licensing rules.

### How much does it cost to start an insurance agency?
Costs include licensing, E&O insurance, technology, marketing and working capital; home-based agencies can start relatively lean.

### How do new agencies get carrier appointments?
Directly from carriers or through networks/aggregators that provide access in exchange for revenue sharing.

### What is an insurance marketing organization (IMO)?
A distributor that contracts with carriers and provides agents with product access, training and support, especially in life and annuities.

### How long does it take to start an insurance agency?
Typically a few months to complete licensing, setup and carrier appointments.
