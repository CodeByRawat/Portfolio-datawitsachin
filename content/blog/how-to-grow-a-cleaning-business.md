<p class="lede">To grow a cleaning business: focus on <strong>recurring clients</strong>, <strong>raise prices</strong> as demand grows, pick a <strong>profitable niche</strong>, <strong>hire and train cleaners</strong> with checklists and quality checks, use <strong>online booking and automation</strong>, collect <strong>reviews and referrals</strong>, pursue <strong>commercial contracts</strong>, and track <strong>retention, profit per job and cost per client</strong>.</p>

## 1. Recurring clients first

Weekly and biweekly clients give predictable revenue; convert one-off deep cleans into recurring plans.

## 2. Pricing

Charge for value and time; raise prices annually; charge more for deep cleans, move-outs and last-minute bookings.

## 3. Choose a niche

Residential recurring, move-in/move-out, Airbnb turnovers, offices, medical facilities or post-construction.

## 4. Hiring and training

Hire for reliability and attitude, run background checks, train with checklists and shadowing, and pay well to reduce turnover.

## 5. Quality control

Room checklists, photo confirmations, inspections and quick fixes for complaints.

## 6. Online booking and automation

Instant quotes, online booking, automatic reminders, card-on-file payments, review requests and rebooking messages (see [How to make a booking website](/blog/how-to-make-a-booking-website/)).

## 7. Reviews and referrals

Ask happy clients for Google reviews; reward referrals.

## 8. Commercial contracts

Offices and facilities offer steady weekday work (see [How to start a commercial cleaning business](/blog/how-to-start-a-commercial-cleaning-business/) and [How to find commercial cleaning contracts](/blog/how-to-find-commercial-cleaning-contracts/)).

## 9. Marketing system

Google Business Profile, local SEO pages, a fast website, Nextdoor and targeted ads (see [How to get clients for a cleaning business](/blog/how-to-get-clients-for-a-cleaning-business/)).

## 10. Metrics

Client retention, revenue per cleaner hour, profit per job, cost per new client and review count.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I grow my cleaning business?
Focus on recurring clients, raise prices, choose a niche, hire and train well, automate booking and follow-ups, and get reviews and referrals.

### How do cleaning businesses get more clients?
Google reviews, referrals, local SEO, Nextdoor, ads and commercial outreach.

### How do I scale a cleaning business?
Hire and train teams with checklists, use software for scheduling and payments, and add commercial contracts.

### What is the most profitable cleaning niche?
It varies by market; commercial, post-construction and specialised cleaning can be lucrative.

### How do I keep cleaners from quitting?
Pay fairly, schedule consistently, train well and recognise good work.

### Should a cleaning business use online booking?
Yes; it makes booking easier for clients and reduces admin time.
