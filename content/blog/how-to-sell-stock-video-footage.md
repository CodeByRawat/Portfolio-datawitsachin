<p class="lede">To sell stock video footage: shoot <strong>high-quality, commercially useful clips</strong> (typically 4K, steady, 10–30 seconds, no logos or trademarks), get <strong>model and property releases</strong> where needed, add <strong>accurate titles and keywords</strong>, and upload to <strong>stock agencies</strong> such as Adobe Stock, Shutterstock, Pond5, Getty Images/iStock and Storyblocks-style marketplaces. You earn <strong>royalties</strong> each time a clip is licensed, which can grow into passive income with a large, well-keyworded library.</p>

## Where to sell stock footage

| Platform | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Adobe Stock** | Integrated with Adobe apps; contributor portal |
| **Shutterstock** | Large marketplace; tiered earnings |
| **Pond5** | Video-focused; contributors can influence pricing |
| **Getty Images / iStock** | Large reach; exclusivity options |
| **Storyblocks and subscription libraries** | Different payout models |
| **Your own site** | Direct licensing for niche footage |

Terms, royalty rates and exclusivity rules vary and change; read contributor agreements.

## What sells

- **People doing real activities** (diverse, natural, authentic)
- **Business and technology:** teamwork, remote work, devices, AI concepts
- **Lifestyle:** health, food, family, travel
- **Establishing shots:** cities, landmarks (check property/landmark rules), aerials (follow drone laws)
- **Backgrounds and abstracts**, slow motion, time-lapses
- **Seasonal and trending topics** (see [How to find trending topics](/blog/how-to-find-trending-topics/))

## Technical tips

- 4K resolution, correct exposure, steady shots (gimbal/tripod)
- Clean audio optional (often muted); avoid music
- Natural colour or lightly graded
- Avoid logos, brand names, artwork and recognisable private property without releases
- Trim to useful lengths and remove shaky starts/ends

## Releases and legal

- **Model releases** for recognisable people
- **Property releases** for private property or some locations
- Editorial vs commercial licensing categories

## Keywording and metadata

Descriptive titles, accurate keywords (who, what, where, mood, concept), consistent categories. Good metadata is essential to being found.

## Earnings

Royalties per download vary by platform, licence type and buyer plan. Most contributors earn modest amounts at first; meaningful income usually requires hundreds or thousands of clips and consistent uploads in niches with demand.

## Tips to grow

Shoot in series (many variations of one setup), track best-sellers, fill gaps in niches, upload consistently, and repurpose footage for your own clients (see [How to make a promotional video](/blog/how-to-make-a-promotional-video/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I sell stock video footage?
Shoot high-quality, commercially useful clips, obtain necessary releases, add strong titles and keywords, and upload to stock platforms like Adobe Stock, Shutterstock and Pond5.

### What stock footage sells best?
Authentic people and lifestyle, business and technology, aerials, slow motion, time-lapses and seasonal themes.

### How much can you earn selling stock footage?
It varies widely; earnings grow with library size, quality, niche demand and keywording.

### Do I need a model release for stock video?
Yes, for recognisable people in commercial-use footage.

### Is it better to sell stock photos or videos?
Videos often earn more per licence but take more effort to produce; many contributors do both.

### Can I sell the same footage on multiple sites?
Usually yes with non-exclusive agreements; exclusive programmes may offer higher royalties but restrict other sales.
