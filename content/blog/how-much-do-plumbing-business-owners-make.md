<p class="lede">Plumbing business owner income varies widely. An <strong>owner-operator</strong> typically earns their own labour plus a profit margin, while owners of <strong>multi-truck plumbing companies</strong> can earn significantly more from their teams. Revenue depends on <strong>number of trucks, jobs per day, average ticket, pricing and service mix</strong>, and profit depends on <strong>labour, materials, vehicles, insurance, marketing and overhead</strong>.</p>

## How plumbing company revenue scales

Revenue ≈ trucks × jobs per day × average ticket × working days. Adding trucks, increasing average ticket (better options, larger jobs) and reducing idle time grow revenue.

## Typical costs

| Cost | Notes |
|---|---|
| Labour | Wages, benefits, payroll taxes |
| Materials | Parts, fixtures, water heaters |
| Vehicles | Vans, fuel, maintenance |
| Insurance | Liability, workers' comp, auto |
| Marketing | Ads, SEO, Local Services Ads |
| Overhead | Office, software, dispatch staff |

## Owner-operator vs multi-truck owner

| | Owner-operator | Multi-truck owner |
|---|---|---|
| Income source | Own labour + margin | Team profit |
| Time | In the field | Managing, selling, hiring |
| Risk | Lower | Higher, but scalable |

## What drives plumbing profit

Flat-rate pricing that covers true costs, high-value services (water heaters, sewer, repiping), membership plans, efficient dispatching, low call-backs and steady lead generation.

## How to earn more

Raise prices to match costs, train techs to present options, sell maintenance memberships, add trucks gradually, and invest in marketing (see [How to get plumbing leads](/blog/how-to-get-plumbing-leads/) and [How to start a plumbing business](/blog/how-to-start-a-plumbing-business/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How much do plumbing business owners make?
It varies with trucks, pricing and costs; owner-operators earn labour plus margin, while multi-truck owners can earn substantially more.

### How much does a small plumbing company make a year?
Revenue depends on trucks, jobs per day and average ticket; profit depends on cost control.

### Is owning a plumbing company worth it?
It can be very rewarding financially, but involves management, sales and business risk.

### What is a good profit margin for a plumbing business?
It varies; well-run service companies with flat-rate pricing typically achieve healthier margins.

### How do plumbing companies increase revenue?
Higher average tickets, more trucks, memberships, high-value services and consistent marketing.

### Do plumbing business owners still work in the field?
Many owner-operators do; larger company owners focus on management and growth.
