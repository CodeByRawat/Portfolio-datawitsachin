<p class="lede">To start a charcuterie business, check whether your state's <strong>cottage food laws</strong> allow it (many <strong>don't allow meats and cheeses</strong> made at home), so you'll often need a <strong>licensed commercial kitchen</strong> and <strong>health permit</strong>. Then learn <strong>food safety</strong>, source <strong>quality ingredients</strong>, create <strong>board and grazing table packages</strong>, set prices, and market through <strong>Instagram, events and local partners</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Check food laws

Charcuterie includes perishable meats and cheeses, which most cottage food laws exclude. Many charcuterie businesses:

- Rent a commercial or commissary kitchen
- Get a health department permit and food handler/manager certification
- Get a business licence and sales tax permit

Rules vary by state (questions like starting a charcuterie business in Florida or Texas depend on local food codes). Check with your health department.

## Step 2: Food safety

Keep cold foods cold, use time limits for grazing tables, label allergens, and transport in insulated, temperature-controlled containers.

## Step 3: Products

| Product | Notes |
|---|---|
| Charcuterie boxes | Individual or couples |
| Boards | Small to large party sizes |
| Grazing tables | Events, high value |
| Charcuterie cups | Weddings and corporate events |
| Workshops | Teach board building |

## Step 4: Startup costs

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Kitchen rental | $15 – $40 per hour or monthly |
| Permits and certification | $200 – $1,500 |
| Boards, tools, coolers | $300 – $1,500 |
| Packaging | $200 – $800 |
| Branding and website | $300 – $2,000 |

## Step 5: Pricing

Cost of ingredients + packaging + labour + overhead, then add profit. Boards often price per person; grazing tables per guest with minimums.

## Step 6: Marketing

Instagram photos and reels, local event planners, wineries and breweries, bridal shows, corporate gifting, and a website with online ordering.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a charcuterie business?
Check food laws, use a licensed kitchen if needed, get permits, create products and market online.

### Can I make charcuterie boards at home to sell?
Often not under cottage food laws because of meats and cheeses; check your state.

### How much does it cost to start a charcuterie business?
Often $1,000 to $5,000 plus kitchen rental.

### How do I price charcuterie boards?
Ingredients, packaging, labour and overhead, plus profit, often priced per person.

### Is a charcuterie business profitable?
Yes, especially grazing tables and corporate orders.
