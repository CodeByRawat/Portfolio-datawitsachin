<p class="lede">To start a wedding and event rental business, choose <strong>upscale inventory</strong> (tents, chiavari or cross-back chairs, linens, décor, lighting, dance floors), set up <strong>storage and delivery</strong>, create <strong>packages and clear contracts</strong>, get <strong>insured</strong>, and build relationships with <strong>wedding planners, venues and caterers</strong>. Startup costs often range from <strong>$15,000 to $75,000+</strong>.</p>

## Choose your inventory

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Seating | Chiavari, cross-back, ghost chairs |
| Tables | Farm tables, rounds, cocktail tables |
| Linens | Tablecloths, runners, napkins |
| Tents | Frame and pole tents, sidewalls |
| Décor | Arches, backdrops, centrepieces |
| Lighting | String lights, uplighting |
| Dance floors and staging | Receptions |
| Lounge furniture | Upscale events |

## Startup costs

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Wedding chairs (100) | $3,000 – $10,000 |
| Tables and linens | $3,000 – $10,000 |
| Tent | $3,000 – $20,000 |
| Décor and lighting | $2,000 – $10,000 |
| Box truck or trailer | $5,000 – $40,000 |
| Storage or warehouse | $500 – $3,000 per month |
| Insurance and licences | $2,000 – $8,000 per year |

## Pricing and packages

Price per item plus delivery, setup and teardown fees. Offer packages (for example a "100-guest reception" bundle). Require deposits and signed contracts.

## Contracts

Include dates, items, delivery windows, damage and loss charges, cancellation policy, weather terms and payment schedule.

## Operations

Inventory tracking software, careful cleaning and pressing of linens, set-up crews, and reliable delivery timing.

## Get clients

1. Wedding planners and coordinators
2. Venues (preferred vendor lists)
3. Caterers and florists
4. Wedding shows and styled shoots
5. Instagram and Pinterest
6. A website with inventory gallery and quote requests

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start an event rental business?
Choose inventory, set up storage and delivery, create packages and contracts, and partner with planners and venues.

### How do I start a wedding rental business?
Invest in upscale chairs, linens, décor and tents, and get on venue vendor lists.

### How much does it cost to start an event rental business?
Often $15,000 to $75,000+.

### Is wedding rental profitable?
Yes. Premium items rent at higher prices and pay back quickly.

### How do event rental companies get clients?
Planners, venues, caterers, wedding shows and social media.
