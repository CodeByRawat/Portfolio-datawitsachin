<p class="lede">To sell gutters, <strong>get in front of homeowners</strong> who have a problem (water damage, overflowing or old gutters), <strong>show them the damage and the fix</strong>, offer <strong>good, better and best options with gutter guards</strong>, give a <strong>clear written price</strong> on the spot, and <strong>follow up</strong>. Partnering with <strong>roofers</strong> brings a steady flow of ready buyers.</p>

## Step 1: Get leads

- Google Business Profile and reviews
- A website with photos and "free estimate" booking
- Roofers and remodelers
- Yard signs on finished jobs
- Door hangers after storms

## Step 2: Run a great estimate

1. Walk the property with the homeowner
2. Point out problems: overflow, sagging, rot, foundation water, basement leaks
3. Measure and take photos
4. Explain what causes the damage and how new gutters fix it

## Step 3: Offer options

| Option | Example |
|---|---|
| Good | Seamless 5-inch aluminium |
| Better | 6-inch gutters with bigger downspouts |
| Best | 6-inch gutters with gutter guards and a longer warranty |

Most people choose the middle or top option when they see all three.

## Step 4: Handle objections

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Too expensive" | Compare to the cost of foundation and fascia repairs |
| "I'll clean them myself" | Explain guards and ladder safety |
| "Getting other quotes" | Highlight warranty, reviews and seamless quality |

Offering financing helps with larger jobs.

## Step 5: Follow up

Send the quote by email the same day, then follow up after 2, 7 and 14 days.

## Step 6: Partner with roofers

Roof replacements often need new gutters. Offer roofers a reliable, quick install and fair referral terms.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you sell gutters?
Show homeowners the problem, offer clear options with guards, price on the spot and follow up.

### How do gutter companies get leads?
Google, reviews, websites, roofers and storm marketing.

### Should I sell gutter guards with every job?
Offer them every time; they add value and margin.

### How do I close more gutter estimates?
Give options, a clear price, photos of the problem and quick follow-up.

### Do gutter companies offer financing?
Many do for larger jobs, which helps close sales.
