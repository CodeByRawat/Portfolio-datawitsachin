<p class="lede">To start a website: <strong>decide its purpose</strong>, <strong>choose a domain name</strong>, <strong>pick a platform</strong> (a website builder such as Wix, Squarespace or Framer for the easiest route, WordPress for flexibility, or custom code), <strong>set up hosting</strong> (included with builders), <strong>choose a design or template</strong>, <strong>add your pages and content</strong>, and <strong>publish</strong>. Then connect Google Search Console and share your link. You can do this <strong>for free</strong> with a builder's free plan, and upgrade to your own domain when ready.</p>

## Step 1: Decide what the website is for

- **Business:** get customers (see [How to create a website for your business](/blog/how-to-create-a-website-for-your-business/))
- **Portfolio:** show your work
- **Blog:** share knowledge, build an audience
- **Online store:** sell products
- **Personal or community site:** information, events, resumes
- **Web app/tool:** software in the browser (see [How to build a web app](/blog/how-to-build-a-web-app/))

The purpose determines the platform and pages.

## Step 2: Choose a domain name

A domain is your address (yourname.com).
- Short, memorable, easy to spell
- Avoid hyphens and numbers
- Check social handles match
- Buy from a registrar or through your builder (~$10–$40/year)
- Free plans give a subdomain like yoursite.wixsite.com or yoursite.framer.website

## Step 3: Choose how to build it

| Option | Skill needed | Cost | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Website builder** (Wix, Squarespace, Framer, Webflow, Shopify) | None to low | Free–$50/month | Fast, hosting included, drag and drop | Less flexibility, platform lock-in |
| **WordPress.org** | Low to medium | Hosting $5–$50/month | Huge plugin ecosystem, own your site | Maintenance, security updates |
| **Static site / code** (HTML/CSS/JS, Astro, Next.js) | Coding | Hosting often free (Vercel, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, GitHub Pages) | Fast, full control | Needs development skills |
| **Hire a professional** | None | One-off fee | Done right, custom | Upfront cost |

(Comparing builders: [What is the best website builder?](/blog/what-is-the-best-website-builder/))

## Step 4: Understand hosting (simply)

Hosting is the computer that stores your website and serves it to visitors. **Builders include hosting.** WordPress needs a host (shared, managed WordPress). Static sites can be hosted on free or low-cost platforms. Choose reliable hosting with **SSL (https)**, backups and good speed.

## Step 5: Design and add content

1. Pick a **clean template** close to what you want.
2. Set your **brand colours, fonts and logo**.
3. Create core pages: Home, About, Services/Work/Products, Contact.
4. Write clear headlines that say what you do and for whom.
5. Add **real photos** and compress them for speed.
6. Add a **contact form, email or WhatsApp button**.
7. Check **mobile view** on every page.

## Step 6: Publish

- Click **Publish** (builders) or deploy (code)
- Connect your **custom domain** (update DNS records as instructed)
- Make sure **https** works
- Test every link and form

### How to create a website link
Your website link is its URL. Share it as `https://yourdomain.com`. To link to a specific page, copy the URL from the browser address bar. To create a link on a page, select text in your editor and add the URL (in HTML: `<a href="https://example.com">text</a>`).

## Step 7: Get visitors

- Add the site to **Google Search Console** and submit your sitemap
- Create a **Google Business Profile** if you're a local business
- Share on your **social profiles**, email signature and WhatsApp
- Publish **helpful content** answering questions your audience searches
- Consider small **ads** for quick traffic (see [What is the most effective type of advertising?](/blog/what-is-the-most-effective-type-of-advertising/))

## How do I create a website for free?

1. Sign up for a builder with a free plan (Wix, Webflow, Framer, Google Sites, WordPress.com free tier).
2. Choose a template, edit text and images.
3. Publish on the free subdomain.

**Limits of free plans:** platform branding or ads, a subdomain, storage and feature limits, sometimes no custom domain or e-commerce. For a business, upgrading to a paid plan with your own domain looks more professional. (Costs: [How much does a website cost?](/blog/how-much-does-a-website-cost/))

## Beginner mistakes

- Spending weeks on the logo and not the content
- Too many pages and features at launch
- Not checking mobile layout
- Slow, huge images
- No clear call to action
- Forgetting SEO basics (titles, descriptions, headings)
- Domain registered in someone else's name

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a website?
Choose a purpose and domain, pick a platform such as a website builder or WordPress, set up hosting, choose a template, add your pages and content, and publish, then connect Search Console and share your link.

### Can I create a website for free?
Yes. Website builders like Wix, Webflow, Framer and Google Sites have free plans with a branded subdomain. A custom domain and premium features require payment.

### What is the easiest way to start a website?
A drag-and-drop website builder with a template is the easiest, because hosting, security and design tools are included.

### Do I need to know coding to make a website?
No. Website builders and WordPress let you create sites without code, though coding gives more control and performance.

### How much does it cost to start a website?
From free with limitations, to roughly $10–$50 per month for a domain and builder plan, to a one-off professional fee if you hire someone.

### How long does it take to start a website?
A simple site can be live in a few hours to a weekend; a well-planned business site typically takes a few weeks.
