<p class="lede">A <strong>real estate marketing plan</strong> is a written strategy that defines <strong>who you want to reach</strong> (buyers, sellers, a farm area, a niche), <strong>what makes you different</strong>, the <strong>channels</strong> you'll use (website, Google Business Profile, social video, ads, email, direct mail, events), your <strong>budget</strong>, a <strong>content and campaign calendar</strong>, how you'll <strong>follow up with leads</strong>, and the <strong>numbers</strong> you'll track to hit your sales goals.</p>

## Step 1: Set goals

- Annual **GCI** (gross commission income) or transaction target
- Work backwards: closings needed → appointments → leads per month
- Split between buyer and seller sides

## Step 2: Define your target market

- **Farm area** (neighbourhoods)
- **Niches:** first-time buyers, luxury, relocation, investors, downsizers, a language community
- **Personas:** needs, fears, where they spend time online

## Step 3: Unique value proposition

Why you? Local expertise, results, marketing approach, communication, specialisation. Turn it into a one-line message.

## Step 4: Budget

Allocate across website/tools, ads, content production, direct mail, events and client gifts. Reserve a test budget for new channels.

## Step 5: Channels

| Channel | Tactics |
|---|---|
| **Website** | IDX search, home valuation, neighbourhood pages, blog (see [How to make a booking website](/blog/how-to-make-a-booking-website/) for booking features) |
| **Google Business Profile** | Reviews, posts, photos |
| **Social and video** | Tours, market updates, local spotlights |
| **Paid ads** | Seller valuation ads, listing promotions (see [How to advertise for realtors](/blog/how-to-advertise-real-estate/)) |
| **Email/WhatsApp** | Monthly newsletter, listing alerts (see [How to write a newsletter](/blog/how-to-write-a-newsletter/)) |
| **Direct mail** | Farm postcards, just sold |
| **Events** | Open houses, seminars, client appreciation |
| **Partnerships** | Lenders, builders, attorneys |

## Step 6: Listing marketing plan (for every listing)

Professional photos, video and drone, floor plans, MLS and portals, social video ads, email blast, open house, just listed postcards, weekly seller reports.

## Step 7: Content calendar

Weekly: 2–3 short videos, 1 market post. Monthly: newsletter, neighbourhood guide. Quarterly: home value updates, client event.

## Step 8: Lead follow-up system

CRM, speed-to-lead process, follow-up cadences and automation (see [How to convert real estate leads](/blog/how-to-convert-real-estate-leads/)).

## Step 9: KPIs

Leads by source, cost per lead, appointments, listings taken, closings, GCI, cost per closing, website traffic, review count.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a real estate marketing plan?
A written strategy covering goals, target market, value proposition, budget, channels, content calendar, lead follow-up and KPIs for an agent or brokerage.

### How do I create a real estate marketing plan?
Set income goals, define your market and niche, craft your value proposition, budget, choose channels, plan content and listing marketing, set up follow-up systems and track KPIs.

### What should a listing marketing plan include?
Professional photos and video, MLS and portal syndication, social ads, email, open houses, print and regular seller updates.

### How much should agents spend on marketing?
It varies; many allocate a percentage of GCI and adjust based on cost per closing by channel.

### What marketing works best for realtors?
Referral and sphere marketing, local video content, Google Business Profile reviews and targeted ads, supported by strong follow-up.

### Do realtors need a website?
Yes. A website with search, valuation tools and local content builds credibility and captures leads you own.
