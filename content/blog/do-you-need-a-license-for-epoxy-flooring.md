<p class="lede">Usually you need at least a <strong>business license</strong> to do epoxy flooring for customers, and many states require a <strong>contractor or home improvement license</strong> for jobs above a certain value, sometimes under a <strong>flooring or painting/coating classification</strong>. You should also carry <strong>general liability insurance</strong>, and manufacturer certifications help win jobs.</p>

Rules vary by state and city. Always check with your state contractor board and local licensing office.

## Licenses to check

| License | When it applies |
|---|---|
| Business license | Almost always |
| State contractor license | Many states, often above a dollar limit |
| Home improvement license | Some states for residential work |
| Specialty classification | Flooring, painting or coating categories in some states |
| Sales tax permit | If you sell materials separately |

**Example:** Some states, such as California, require a contractor license for jobs above a small dollar amount and have specific classifications for flooring and painting/coatings.

## Insurance

- General liability (damage to property, slips)
- Workers' comp when you hire
- Commercial auto
- Tools and equipment coverage

Many builders and commercial clients require proof of insurance.

## Certifications

Manufacturer training and certification aren't legally required but show customers you're trained, and may be needed for system warranties.

## Safety rules

Epoxy and some coatings contain chemicals that require respirators, ventilation and following product safety data sheets. Grinding concrete creates silica dust, which is regulated; use dust collection.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do you need a license for epoxy flooring?
Usually a business license, and in many states a contractor license above a job-size limit.

### Do I need insurance for epoxy flooring?
Yes. General liability is essential, plus workers' comp if you hire.

### Is epoxy certification required?
Not legally, but manufacturer certification helps with quality and warranties.

### Can I do epoxy floors without a contractor license?
Only for small jobs in states that allow it; check your state's limit.

### What safety rules apply to epoxy work?
Respirators, ventilation, product safety data sheets and silica dust control.
