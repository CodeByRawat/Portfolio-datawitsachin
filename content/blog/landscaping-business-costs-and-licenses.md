<p class="lede">Starting a landscaping business can cost <strong>a few thousand dollars</strong> for a lean lawn-care start using existing vehicles and basic equipment, to <strong>tens of thousands or more</strong> with a truck, trailer, commercial mowers and hardscaping equipment. You'll typically need a <strong>business registration and local licence</strong>, plus <strong>special licences</strong> for pesticide application, irrigation or larger construction work in many areas. Well-run landscaping businesses can be profitable, especially with <strong>recurring maintenance routes</strong> and higher-margin projects.</p>

## Startup cost breakdown (rough)

| Item | Lean start | Established setup |
|---|---|---|
| Mowers, trimmers, blowers | Residential-grade or used | Commercial zero-turn mowers |
| Truck and trailer | Existing vehicle + small trailer | Dedicated truck and enclosed trailer |
| Hand tools and safety gear | Basic | Full set |
| Insurance | General liability | Liability, commercial auto, workers' comp |
| Licences and registration | Business licence | Plus specialised licences |
| Software | Free/low-cost apps | Field service management suite |
| Marketing | Door hangers, Google profile | Website, ads, branded vehicles |

Costs vary by location and new vs used equipment.

## What licences are needed?

- Business entity registration and local business licence
- Contractor licence for landscape construction above certain project values (some states/regions)
- Pesticide/herbicide applicator licence for chemical application
- Irrigation licence in some places
- Tree work certifications for certain jobs

### How to get a landscaping licence
Check your state/regional licensing board, complete required training and exams, show proof of insurance and pay fees.

## Is a landscaping business profitable?

Profit margins vary widely; maintenance can be steady but competitive, while design/build and hardscaping projects can earn higher margins. Profit drivers:
- **Route density** (less travel time)
- **Accurate pricing** (see [How to price landscaping jobs](/blog/how-to-price-landscaping-jobs/))
- **Crew productivity**
- **Upselling projects and add-ons**
- **Equipment maintenance and utilisation**

## Ways to start lean

Start with mowing and maintenance, buy quality used equipment, rent specialised tools, focus on a tight geographic area, and reinvest profits.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does it cost to start a landscaping business?
A lean start can cost a few thousand dollars; a fully equipped company with trucks and commercial equipment costs much more.

### What licences are needed to start a landscaping business?
Typically a business licence; pesticide, irrigation or contractor licences may be needed depending on services and location.

### How do I get a landscaping licence?
Contact your state or regional licensing board, complete training and exams, provide insurance proof and pay fees.

### Is a landscaping business profitable?
It can be, especially with dense recurring routes, accurate pricing and higher-margin projects.

### What equipment do I need to start landscaping?
Mower, trimmer, blower, hand tools, safety gear and transport; add specialised equipment as you grow.

### Can I start landscaping with no money?
It's difficult, but you can start small with borrowed or used equipment and reinvest early profits.
