<p class="lede">Coaches get clients by being <strong>clear about who they help and the result they deliver</strong>, starting <strong>conversations</strong> (warm outreach, discovery calls), sharing <strong>valuable content</strong> consistently (LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube, newsletters), running <strong>free workshops or webinars</strong>, appearing on <strong>podcasts and in communities</strong>, and turning happy clients into <strong>testimonials and referrals</strong>, with a simple website that makes booking a discovery call easy.</p>

## 15 ways to get coaching clients

1. **Niche positioning:** "I help [who] achieve [result] in [time] without [pain]"
2. **Warm outreach:** tell your network what you do; ask for introductions
3. **Free discovery calls** with a clear booking link (see [How to make a booking website](/blog/how-to-make-a-booking-website/))
4. **LinkedIn** posts and conversations (business/career/leadership coaches)
5. **Instagram/TikTok** short videos (life, health, mindset coaches)
6. **YouTube** in-depth content
7. **Newsletter** with practical tips (see [How to write a newsletter](/blog/how-to-write-a-newsletter/))
8. **Lead magnet:** quiz, workbook, checklist
9. **Free workshops/webinars** with an offer at the end
10. **Podcast guesting** and speaking
11. **Communities:** Facebook groups, Slack/Discord, professional associations (helpful, not spammy)
12. **Partnerships:** therapists (for referrals out/in), HR teams, gyms, accountants
13. **Testimonials and case studies**
14. **Referral programme** for past clients
15. **Corporate contracts** for leadership or wellness coaching

## Life coaching clients specifically

Choose a concrete niche (e.g., career change for nurses, confidence for new mums), share relatable stories, and offer a low-commitment starter session or group programme.

## Discovery call structure

1. Understand their goal and situation
2. Explore obstacles and what they've tried
3. Clarify the cost of not changing
4. Present your programme if it's a fit
5. Confirm next steps and payment

## Track what works

Leads by source, discovery calls booked, close rate, client results and referrals. Automate booking, reminders and follow-ups (see [How to automate your sales process](/blog/how-to-automate-your-sales-process/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I get coaching clients?
Define a clear niche and result, reach out to your network, offer discovery calls, share valuable content, run workshops, guest on podcasts and ask for referrals.

### How do life coaches get clients?
Through a specific niche, relatable social content, communities, workshops, referrals and low-commitment starter offers.

### How do I market my coaching business?
Combine clear positioning, consistent content, a website with booking, a lead magnet and email list, partnerships and testimonials.

### Do coaches need a website?
A simple website with your niche, offer, testimonials and booking link builds trust and converts interest into calls.

### How long does it take to get coaching clients?
First clients often come within weeks from your network; consistent content and referrals build a steady pipeline over months.

### Should coaches offer free sessions?
A short discovery call is common; long free coaching sessions often devalue your service.
