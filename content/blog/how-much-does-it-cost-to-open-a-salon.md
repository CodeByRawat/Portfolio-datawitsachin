<p class="lede">Opening a salon can cost from <strong>a few thousand dollars</strong> (booth rental or a salon suite with your own tools) to <strong>roughly $50,000–$150,000+</strong> for a small full-service salon, and more for large or luxury salons. The biggest variables are <strong>rent and build-out</strong> (especially plumbing for shampoo stations and ventilation for nail services), <strong>equipment</strong>, and <strong>working capital</strong> to cover costs while your client base grows. Costs vary widely by city and design.</p>

## Rough startup cost by model

| Model | Rough startup cost |
|---|---|
| Booth/chair rental | ~$1,000–$10,000 (tools, products, licences, booking software) |
| Salon suite | ~$5,000–$25,000 (deposit, furnishing, products) |
| Home or mobile salon | ~$5,000–$30,000 (compliance, equipment, vehicle setup for mobile) |
| Small hair or nail salon | ~$50,000–$150,000 |
| Full-service/luxury salon or spa | $150,000–$500,000+ |

Illustrative ranges; get local quotes.

## Cost breakdown

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Lease** | Deposit, first months' rent |
| **Build-out** | Plumbing, electrical, ventilation, flooring, lighting, mirrors, signage |
| **Equipment** | Styling chairs, stations, shampoo units, dryers, nail tables, pedicure chairs, sterilisation |
| **Licences and insurance** | Salon licence, business registration, liability and property insurance |
| **Software and tech** | Booking/POS software, website (see [How to make a booking website](/blog/how-to-make-a-booking-website/)), Wi-Fi, sound |
| **Initial inventory** | Professional and retail products |
| **Branding and marketing** | Logo, photos, website, opening promotions |
| **Staff** | Hiring, training, pre-opening wages |
| **Working capital** | 3–6 months of expenses |

## Monthly running costs

Rent, payroll or commission, products, utilities, software subscriptions, insurance, laundry, marketing and card fees.

## Nail salon specifics

Nail salons need **ventilation** for fumes, pedicure chairs (with plumbing), nail tables, UV/LED lamps and strict sanitation. (Starting one: [How to start a nail business](/blog/how-to-start-a-nail-business/).)

## Ways to open for less

- Start with **booth rental or a suite**
- Take over a **former salon** with existing plumbing
- Buy **used equipment**
- **Phase services** (add colour bar or spa later)
- **Pre-sell packages** and memberships
- DIY social media marketing at first

(Steps: [How to start a hair salon](/blog/how-to-start-a-hair-salon/). Income: [How much do salon owners make?](/blog/how-much-do-salon-owners-make/))

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does it cost to open a salon?
From a few thousand dollars for booth rental or a suite to roughly $50,000–$150,000+ for a small full-service salon, and more for large salons.

### How much does it cost to start a hair salon?
A small hair salon commonly costs tens of thousands to over $100,000 depending on location, build-out and equipment.

### How much does it cost to open a nail salon?
Similar ranges to small hair salons; ventilation and pedicure plumbing can add significant cost.

### What is the biggest cost of opening a salon?
Usually rent and build-out, particularly plumbing and electrical work.

### Can I open a salon with no money?
It's hard, but booth rental, salon suites, used equipment, loans and pre-selling services can minimise upfront costs.

### How long until a salon is profitable?
Often months to a couple of years depending on client base, pricing, rent and staffing.
