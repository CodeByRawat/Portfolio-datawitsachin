<p class="lede"><strong>Yes, many moving companies will pack for you</strong> for an extra fee. Options usually include <strong>full packing</strong> (everything), <strong>partial packing</strong> (selected rooms) and <strong>fragile-only packing</strong> (kitchenware, art, electronics), plus <strong>unpacking</strong>. Movers bring <strong>boxes and materials</strong>, wrap furniture, label boxes, load, transport and unload, though most won't move <strong>hazardous items</strong>, and some won't pack valuables, documents or medications.</p>

## What movers do

- Provide boxes, tape, paper and wrap
- Pack and label (if booked)
- Disassemble and reassemble furniture
- Protect floors and doorways
- Load, transport and unload
- Place furniture in rooms
- Unpack (if booked)

## Packing service options

| Option | What's included |
|---|---|
| Full packing | All belongings packed by movers |
| Partial packing | Specific rooms or items |
| Fragile-only | Dishes, glassware, art, electronics |
| Unpacking | Unboxing and placing items, removing debris |
| Crating | Custom crates for high-value items |

## What movers usually won't move

Hazardous materials (flammables, paint, propane, chemicals), perishables, plants (sometimes), and often cash, jewellery, important documents and medications (keep these with you).

## Cost

Packing adds labour hours and materials to the move; full packing for a large home can add significantly to the total (see [How much does a moving company cost?](/blog/how-much-does-a-moving-company-cost/)).

## How to prepare

Declutter, set aside essentials and valuables, label "do not pack" items, empty drawers if requested, and confirm the inventory.

## Questions to ask movers

Is packing included in the estimate? Are materials charged separately? Is the estimate binding? What insurance/valuation coverage applies to packed items?

## Frequently asked questions

### Do moving companies pack for you?
Yes, most offer full, partial or fragile-only packing for an additional fee.

### What do movers do?
They pack (if booked), protect, load, transport, unload and often reassemble furniture.

### Can movers pack for you on moving day?
Some can, but large homes usually need packing scheduled a day or more before.

### What should I pack myself?
Valuables, important documents, medications, essentials and anything movers won't take.

### Is it worth paying movers to pack?
Often, for busy households or fragile items, since professional packing reduces damage and saves time.

### Do movers bring boxes?
Yes, when packing is booked; otherwise many sell boxes and supplies.
