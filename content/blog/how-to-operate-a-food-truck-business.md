<p class="lede">Operating a successful food truck comes down to <strong>great locations and a predictable schedule</strong>, a <strong>fast, focused menu</strong>, efficient <strong>prep and service flow</strong>, <strong>daily marketing</strong> of where you'll be, profitable <strong>catering and events</strong>, and tight control of <strong>food cost, waste and maintenance</strong>. Track sales by location and day to keep only what works.</p>

## Daily operations routine

1. **Commissary prep:** chop, portion, marinate; load ingredients and supplies
2. **Checks:** generator, propane, water tanks, fridges, POS
3. **Drive and set up** at the location; menu boards and signage
4. **Service:** clear roles (order taker, cook, expo)
5. **Close:** clean, record sales and waste, restock list
6. **Commissary:** dump grey water, clean, store food safely

## Locations and schedule

- Office parks for lunch, breweries for evenings, events on weekends
- Rotate a **weekly schedule** customers can rely on
- Track revenue per location and drop poor performers
- Get permits for each spot (see [Food truck permits and licences](/blog/food-truck-permits-and-licenses/))

## Menu for speed and margin

5–8 items, shared ingredients, items that cook in minutes, combos to raise average ticket, specials to test new ideas.

## Staffing and service flow

Cross-trained staff, clear stations, POS with card/tap payments, online pre-orders for pickup windows to reduce queues.

## Catering and events

Corporate lunches, weddings, school and community events, private parties: often higher revenue with guaranteed minimums. A website catering request form helps (see [How to make a booking website](/blog/how-to-make-a-booking-website/)).

## Daily marketing

Post today's location and menu on Instagram/TikTok/Facebook, update Google Business Profile hours, message loyalty customers via WhatsApp/SMS (see [How to schedule a WhatsApp message](/blog/how-to-schedule-a-whatsapp-message/)), and collect reviews.

## Niche trucks

- **Lunch trucks:** target worksites and offices with fast combos
- **Taco trucks:** simple, high-margin menus and late-night locations
- **Coffee/dessert trucks:** events and morning commuters

## Track profit

Daily sales, average ticket, food cost %, labour %, waste, fuel and event ROI. (Costs: [How much does a food truck cost?](/blog/how-much-does-a-food-truck-cost/))

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you operate a food truck business?
Prep at a commissary, follow a reliable location schedule, serve a fast focused menu, market your location daily, add catering and events, and track food cost and sales by location.

### How do food trucks decide where to park?
Using permitted spots, partnerships with businesses and breweries, events, and sales data from past locations.

### How many staff does a food truck need?
Commonly two to four people per shift depending on volume and menu complexity.

### How do food trucks get more customers?
Consistent schedules, daily social posts, Google presence, loyalty programmes, events and catering.

### How do I start a taco truck?
Follow standard food truck steps with a simple taco-focused menu, efficient prep and late-night or worksite locations.

### Is catering important for food trucks?
Yes; catering and private events often provide higher, more predictable revenue than street service.
