<p class="lede">A <strong>photography portfolio</strong> is a curated collection of your <strong>best work in a specific style or niche</strong>, presented to show potential clients, employers or galleries what you can do. Today it's usually a <strong>portfolio website</strong> (plus Instagram and sometimes a PDF), with galleries, an about page, testimonials, pricing guidance and an easy way to <strong>book or enquire</strong>.</p>

## What makes a strong portfolio

- **Focused:** one niche per portfolio (or clearly separated galleries)
- **Curated:** 20–40 of your best images, not everything
- **Consistent:** editing style and quality
- **Relevant:** shows the work you want to be hired for
- **Story:** sequences that show a full session or event
- **Fresh:** updated regularly

## How to build a photography portfolio

1. **Pick your niche** (see [How to start a photography business](/blog/how-to-start-a-photography-business/))
2. **Gather candidates** from all your shoots
3. **Select ruthlessly:** remove near-duplicates and weaker images
4. **Sequence:** strongest first, vary compositions, end strong
5. **Get feedback** from photographers and target clients
6. **Publish** on a fast website and social profiles

## Portfolio website essentials

| Section | What to include |
|---|---|
| Homepage | Hero image, one-line positioning, CTA |
| Galleries | Organised by niche or session type |
| About | Your story, approach and photo of you |
| Experience/process | What clients can expect |
| Pricing | "Starting from" or packages |
| Testimonials | Client quotes and reviews |
| Contact/booking | Simple form or booking with deposit (see [How to make a booking website](/blog/how-to-make-a-booking-website/)) |

## Platforms

Squarespace, Format, Pixieset, SmugMug, Wix, WordPress or a custom fast site (see [What is the best website builder?](/blog/what-is-the-best-website-builder/)).

## SEO and performance

- Compress images (WebP/AVIF), use lazy loading
- Descriptive filenames and alt text
- Location and niche keywords on pages
- Blog posts of real sessions
(See [What is SEO?](/blog/what-is-seo/).)

## Common mistakes

Too many images, mixed niches, slow loading, no clear CTA, outdated work, missing contact information.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a photography portfolio?
A curated collection of a photographer's best work in a style or niche, presented online or in print to win clients or jobs.

### How many photos should be in a portfolio?
Typically 20–40 strong images per niche gallery.

### How do I make a photography portfolio with no clients?
Organise styled shoots and practice sessions with friends or models (with releases) in your target niche.

### What is the best platform for a photography portfolio?
Squarespace, Format, Pixieset, SmugMug, Wix and WordPress are popular; choose for design, speed and booking features.

### Should my portfolio include prices?
Showing "starting from" prices or packages helps qualify leads and saves time.

### How often should I update my portfolio?
Whenever you have stronger work, at least a few times a year.
