<p class="lede">To bid excavation work, <strong>visit the site and study the plans</strong>, calculate <strong>cut, fill and haul quantities</strong>, estimate <strong>machine and labour hours</strong>, add <strong>trucking, disposal, materials, mobilisation and testing</strong>, then add <strong>overhead and profit</strong>. To get more jobs, build relationships with <strong>builders and general contractors</strong>, and watch <strong>public bid boards</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Site visit and plans

Check access, soil, rock, water, slopes, existing utilities and trees. Read the plans and specs carefully, including compaction and testing requirements.

## Step 2: Quantities

- **Cubic yards** = length × width × depth (ft) ÷ 27
- Account for **swell** (soil expands when dug) and **shrink** (compaction)
- Calculate cut, fill, import and export amounts

## Step 3: Equipment and labour

Estimate production (yards per hour) for each machine, then:

**Machine hours × hourly machine cost + operator hours × wage burden**

## Step 4: Other costs

| Cost | Examples |
|---|---|
| Trucking | Loads × cost per load |
| Disposal | Dump or tipping fees |
| Materials | Gravel, pipe, fabric, fill |
| Mobilisation | Moving machines to and from site |
| Testing | Compaction tests |
| Permits | Grading or erosion permits |
| Erosion control | Silt fence, inlet protection |

## Step 5: Overhead and profit

Add a share of overhead (office, insurance, yard) and your profit margin. Include contingency for rock or bad soil, or exclude it clearly.

## Step 6: Write the bid

Scope, quantities, exclusions (rock, dewatering, unsuitable soil), schedule, payment terms and unit prices for extra work.

## How to find excavation work

1. Builders and home builders
2. General contractors and developers
3. Concrete, septic and landscaping companies
4. Public bids from cities, counties and utilities
5. Google Business Profile and a strong website
6. Referrals from engineers and surveyors

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you bid an excavation job?
Calculate quantities and machine hours, add trucking, disposal and other costs, then overhead and profit.

### How do I calculate excavation cubic yards?
Length × width × depth in feet ÷ 27, then adjust for swell and compaction.

### How do I find excavation work?
Builders, general contractors, public bid boards and referrals.

### What should an excavation bid exclude?
Commonly rock, dewatering and unsuitable soils unless priced separately.

### How do I get more excavating jobs?
Build relationships, bid often, finish on schedule and get reviews.
