<p class="lede">Running a bookkeeping business well depends on <strong>repeatable workflows</strong>: structured <strong>onboarding</strong>, a <strong>monthly close checklist</strong>, clear <strong>engagement letters and pricing</strong>, reliable <strong>client communication</strong>, <strong>quality reviews</strong>, and <strong>automation</strong> that frees time. Growth comes from <strong>niche marketing, referrals and hiring</strong>.</p>

## Core workflows

| Workflow | What it includes |
|---|---|
| Onboarding | Agreement, access, chart of accounts, historical cleanup |
| Monthly close | Categorise, reconcile, review, report |
| Client requests | Missing receipts and questions |
| Reporting | P&L, balance sheet, cash flow summary |
| Year-end | Hand-off to tax preparer |

## Pricing and scope

Use **monthly fixed fees** based on transaction volume and services. Review prices yearly and charge extra for scope creep, rush work and catch-up months.

## Communication

Set expectations for response times, monthly deadlines for documents and how questions are asked. Automated reminders reduce chasing.

## Quality control

Use review checklists, reconciliation reports and a second review for larger clients.

## Tools

Accounting software, practice management, client portal, receipt capture, e-signature, password manager and automation tools. See [How to start an online bookkeeping business](/blog/how-to-start-an-online-bookkeeping-business/).

## Scaling

Document processes (SOPs), hire and train bookkeepers, specialise in a niche, and grow through accountant referrals. Related: [How to grow an accounting practice](/blog/how-to-grow-an-accounting-practice/) and [How to get accounting clients](/blog/how-to-get-accounting-clients/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you run a bookkeeping business?
With onboarding processes, monthly close checklists, clear pricing, client communication and quality reviews.

### How many clients can one bookkeeper handle?
It depends on transaction volume and automation level.

### How do I set up a bookkeeping business?
Register, insure, choose software, create packages and engagement letters, and build workflows.

### How do I build a bookkeeping business?
Niche down, collect referrals, document processes and hire.

### What software helps run a bookkeeping firm?
Practice management, cloud accounting, client portals and automation tools.

### How do I handle late client documents?
Set deadlines in your engagement letter and use automated reminders.
