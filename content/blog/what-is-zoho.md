<p class="lede"><strong>Zoho</strong> is a suite of more than 55 cloud business applications (covering CRM, accounting, email, customer support, HR, project management, marketing, analytics and low-code app building) made by <strong>Zoho Corporation</strong>, a privately held software company founded in India in 1996. Businesses use Zoho to run much of their operations on one integrated, affordable platform, often through the <strong>Zoho One</strong> bundle.</p>

Zoho is a quiet giant of business software. In March 2026, on its 30th anniversary, Zoho Corporation announced it had passed **one million paying customer organisations** and **150 million users** worldwide. It's especially popular with small and mid-sized businesses that want broad functionality without enterprise prices. This guide explains what Zoho is, its main apps, Zoho One, AI and automation features, pros and cons, and how it compares with alternatives.

## Zoho, the company

- **Founded:** 1996 (originally as AdventNet) by **Sridhar Vembu** and **Tony Thomas**
- **Headquarters:** global base in Chennai, India, with US headquarters in Austin, Texas
- **Ownership:** privately held and bootstrapped. Zoho is famous for never taking venture capital.
- **Leadership:** Sridhar Vembu moved from CEO to Chief Scientist in 2024; Shailesh Kumar Davey became CEO
- **Group:** Zoho Corporation includes Zoho (business apps), **ManageEngine** (IT management software), Qntrl and TrainerCentral
- **Scale:** over 1 million paying customers and 150+ million users (as of March 2026)
- **Notable traits:** builds most of its technology in-house (including its own data centres), emphasises privacy (no ad-based business model), and invests in rural offices in India

## What is Zoho One?

**Zoho One** is Zoho's all-in-one bundle: access to the majority of Zoho's apps (50+) under a single subscription, priced **per employee** (or per user), with unified administration, single sign-on and shared data across apps. It's positioned as an "operating system for business".

**Why businesses choose Zoho One:**
- One bill and one admin console for dozens of apps
- Data flows between CRM, finance, support, HR and marketing
- Usually much cheaper than buying equivalent tools separately from multiple vendors

**When it's not ideal:** if you only need one or two apps, buying them individually may be simpler and cheaper; and if your organisation is deeply standardised on Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, you may only want specific Zoho apps alongside them.

Zoho's pricing is generally lower than comparable enterprise suites, with free tiers for several individual apps (e.g., Zoho CRM free edition for very small teams, Zoho Mail for small businesses in some regions, Bigin free plan). Check Zoho's pricing pages for current prices in your region, as they vary by country and billing period.

## Zoho CRM in brief

Zoho CRM is the company's flagship app: contact and deal management, pipelines, workflow automation, lead scoring, email and telephony integration, analytics, and **Zia** (Zoho's AI assistant). It competes with Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft Dynamics and Pipedrive, usually at a lower price point, and integrates natively with the rest of Zoho. (CRM basics: [What is a CRM?](/blog/what-is-a-crm/))

## AI in Zoho: Zia

**Zia** is Zoho's AI layer across its apps. Capabilities include:
- **Predictions:** lead and deal scoring, anomaly detection in sales and finance data
- **Generative AI:** drafting emails, summarising records and tickets, answering questions in natural language
- **Conversational assistance:** asking questions about your data ("How many deals closed last month in the west region?")
- **Agents:** Zoho has introduced AI agents and an agent studio for building agents that act across Zoho apps
- **Model choice:** Zoho builds its own models and also integrates with third-party LLMs in some products, with emphasis on privacy and data residency

## Automation and integration in Zoho

- **Workflow rules and blueprints** in Zoho CRM and Desk automate processes (assignments, approvals, field updates, notifications)
- **Zoho Flow** connects Zoho and third-party apps (Slack, Google, Shopify, etc.), similar to Zapier or Make
- **Deluge** scripting for custom logic
- **Zoho Creator** for building custom business apps with low code
- **APIs and webhooks** for custom integrations (see [What is a webhook?](/blog/what-is-a-webhook/))
- **Third-party automation tools** (Zapier, Make, n8n) also have Zoho connectors

## Zoho pros and cons

### Pros
- **Breadth:** one vendor for most business functions
- **Value:** strong functionality at relatively low prices
- **Integration:** apps share data and identity, especially within Zoho One
- **Privacy stance:** no ad-based revenue model; owns its infrastructure
- **Customisation:** powerful workflows, Deluge scripting and Creator
- **Global presence:** data centres in multiple regions

### Cons
- **Learning curve:** many apps and settings; configuration takes time
- **Inconsistent depth:** some apps are best-in-class for SMBs, others less mature than specialist tools
- **UI differences** between apps
- **Ecosystem size:** smaller third-party ecosystem than Salesforce or Microsoft
- **Enterprise fit:** very large enterprises may need deeper capabilities or partner support

## Who should use Zoho?

- **Small and mid-sized businesses** wanting CRM, accounting, support and HR on one platform
- **Cost-conscious organisations** replacing many separate subscriptions
- **Businesses in India, the Middle East, Asia-Pacific and beyond** where Zoho has strong local presence and compliance features (e.g., GST in India)
- **Companies wanting customisation** with low-code tools

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Zoho?
Zoho is a suite of more than 55 cloud business applications, including CRM, accounting, email, help desk, HR, project management, marketing, analytics and low-code app building, made by Zoho Corporation, a private software company founded in India in 1996.

### What is Zoho used for?
Businesses use Zoho to manage sales and customer relationships, marketing, customer support, accounting and invoicing, HR and recruiting, email and collaboration, projects, analytics and custom business apps, often all within the Zoho One bundle.

### What is Zoho One?
Zoho One is an all-in-one subscription that gives a business access to most of Zoho's 50+ applications with single sign-on, unified administration and shared data, priced per employee or per user.

### Is Zoho free?
Several Zoho apps offer free plans for small teams, such as Zoho CRM's free edition, Bigin and Zoho Mail in some regions, but most business use requires paid plans. Zoho is generally priced lower than comparable suites.

### Who owns Zoho?
Zoho Corporation is privately owned by its founders and employees and has never taken venture capital. It was co-founded by Sridhar Vembu and Tony Thomas.

### Is Zoho better than Salesforce?
Zoho is usually more affordable and broader across business functions, making it a strong choice for small and mid-sized businesses. Salesforce offers deeper enterprise CRM capabilities, a larger ecosystem and more extensive customisation for complex organisations.
