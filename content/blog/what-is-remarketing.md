<p class="lede"><strong>Remarketing</strong> (often called <strong>retargeting</strong>) is a marketing strategy that <strong>shows ads or messages to people who have already interacted with your business</strong>: visited your website, viewed a product, added to cart, watched your video, used your app or are on your customer list. Because these people already know you, remarketing typically achieves <strong>higher click-through and conversion rates</strong> than reaching cold audiences, bringing back visitors who left without buying.</p>

## How remarketing works

1. A **tag or pixel** (Google tag, Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, LinkedIn Insight Tag) on your site records visits and actions, subject to user consent
2. Visitors are added to **audiences** (e.g., "viewed pricing page in last 30 days")
3. You run **ads targeted to those audiences** on Google, YouTube, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn and display networks
4. Ads remind, persuade or offer an incentive to return and convert

You can also upload **customer lists** (hashed emails/phones) to target or exclude customers.

## Remarketing vs retargeting

The terms are often used interchangeably. Historically, Google used "remarketing" for its ads product; some marketers use "remarketing" for email re-engagement and "retargeting" for ads. In practice, both mean re-engaging people who've interacted with you.

## Types of remarketing

| Type | How it works | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Website remarketing** | Ads to past visitors | Pricing page visitors see a testimonial ad |
| **Dynamic remarketing** | Ads show the exact products viewed | Shoes viewed appear in the ad |
| **Video remarketing** | Ads to people who watched your videos | YouTube viewers see a follow-up offer |
| **Social engagement** | Ads to people who engaged with your Instagram/Facebook | Profile engagers see a promo |
| **Customer list remarketing** | Target or exclude uploaded lists | Past customers see a new product |
| **Email remarketing** | Automated emails after actions | Abandoned cart email |
| **Search remarketing (RLSA-style)** | Adjust search ads for past visitors | Higher bids for returning visitors |

## Examples

- **E-commerce:** abandoned cart → dynamic product ad + email with free shipping
- **SaaS:** visited pricing → case study ad → demo offer
- **Local service:** viewed quote page → ad with reviews and "Book this week"
- **Education:** watched webinar → enrolment deadline ad

## Setting up remarketing

### Google Ads
1. Install the **Google tag** (or GA4 linked) with consent mode where required
2. Create **audience segments** in Google Ads (website visitors, YouTube users, customer lists)
3. Use them in Display, YouTube, Demand Gen or Performance Max campaigns, or as observation/targeting in Search

### Meta (Facebook/Instagram)
1. Install **Meta Pixel** and **Conversions API**
2. Create **custom audiences** (website, engagement, customer lists)
3. Create campaigns targeting them; use **catalog ads** for dynamic product remarketing

## Audience segmentation ideas

- All visitors (last 30/90 days)
- Product/service page viewers
- Pricing page viewers
- Cart abandoners
- Video viewers (50%+)
- Past customers (for upsell, or exclude)
- Leads who didn't convert

Tailor messages to each segment's stage.

## Remarketing creative best practices

- **Answer the reason they didn't buy:** price, trust, timing, questions
- **Show social proof:** reviews, case studies
- **Use offers carefully** (don't train people to wait)
- **Show the product they viewed** (dynamic ads)
- **Use video and motion** to stand out (see [What are motion graphics?](/blog/what-are-motion-graphics/))
- **Refresh creatives** regularly
- **Cap frequency** to avoid annoyance
- **Exclude converters** from acquisition ads

## Privacy and consent

Follow privacy laws (GDPR, CCPA and local rules), use cookie consent and consent mode, disclose tracking in your privacy policy, and respect platform policies (sensitive categories like health have restrictions). Third-party cookie limits and app tracking prompts reduce audience sizes, so first-party data (email lists, CRM) is increasingly important.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is remarketing?
A strategy of showing ads or messages to people who have previously interacted with your website, app, videos or business.

### Is remarketing the same as retargeting?
Mostly yes; the terms are used interchangeably, though some marketers use remarketing for email and retargeting for ads.

### How does remarketing work?
A pixel or tag adds visitors to audiences based on their actions, and ad platforms show those audiences targeted ads.

### Is remarketing effective?
Often yes, because people already familiar with your brand are more likely to click and convert, especially with relevant messaging.

### What is dynamic remarketing?
Remarketing that automatically shows the specific products or services a person viewed, using a product feed.

### How long should a remarketing window be?
Commonly 7–30 days for high-intent actions like cart abandonment and 30–180 days for broader visitors, depending on your sales cycle.
