<p class="lede">Starting a painting business is <strong>relatively inexpensive</strong>. Most of the budget goes to <strong>tools and a paint sprayer, ladders, insurance, licensing, a vehicle and marketing</strong>. Many painters start with an existing vehicle and basic tools, then add equipment as jobs come in. Paint is usually bought per job and included in the customer's price.</p>

## Startup budget categories

| Category | Notes |
|---|---|
| Hand tools | Brushes, rollers, trays, scrapers, sanders |
| Sprayer | Airless sprayer for speed on larger jobs |
| Ladders and access | Extension ladders, step ladders, planks |
| Protection | Drop cloths, plastic, tape, masking tools |
| Safety | Respirators, harnesses, PPE |
| Vehicle | Van/truck or existing vehicle with racks |
| Licences | Contractor licence and lead-safe certification where required |
| Insurance | General liability, workers' comp when hiring |
| Registration | LLC, local business licence |
| Marketing | Website, Google profile, yard signs, cards |
| Software | Estimating, scheduling, invoicing |
| Working capital | Covers payroll and materials before payment |

## How to start on a small budget

Use your current vehicle, buy quality essentials first, rent specialty equipment, collect deposits to cover materials, and start with interior jobs that need less equipment.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does it cost to start a painting business?
Relatively little compared with other trades; mainly tools, sprayer, ladders, insurance, licensing and marketing.

### What is the biggest startup cost for painters?
Often the vehicle, sprayer and insurance.

### Do I need to buy paint upfront?
Usually you buy paint per job, funded by customer deposits.

### Do painters need insurance?
Yes; general liability is essential and workers' comp is required when you hire.

### Can I start a painting business with little money?
Yes; start with interior jobs, basic tools and your existing vehicle.

### Should I buy a paint sprayer at the start?
It's helpful for larger jobs; some start with brushes and rollers and buy a sprayer once work increases.
