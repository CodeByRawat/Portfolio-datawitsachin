<p class="lede">Custom cakes are usually priced <strong>per serving</strong>. Simple custom birthday cakes often cost <strong>$4 to $8 per serving</strong>, and detailed designer cakes <strong>$8 to $15+ per serving</strong>. A custom birthday cake for 20 to 30 people often costs <strong>$80 to $300+</strong>, depending on <strong>size, tiers, design, fondant or buttercream, and sugar decorations</strong>.</p>

These are rough ranges; prices vary by baker and region.

## What affects the price

| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| Servings / size | More servings cost more |
| Tiers | Stacked cakes need support and time |
| Design detail | Hand-painting, piping, figures |
| Fondant vs buttercream | Fondant often costs more |
| Sugar flowers and toppers | Labour-intensive |
| Flavours and fillings | Premium ingredients cost more |
| Delivery and setup | Extra for tiered cakes |
| Rush orders | Extra fees |

## Example prices

| Cake | Rough price |
|---|---|
| 6-inch custom cake (8–12 servings) | $50 – $120 |
| 8-inch custom cake (20–25 servings) | $90 – $250 |
| Two-tier birthday cake (30–40 servings) | $200 – $450+ |
| Wedding cake | Often $4 – $12+ per serving |

## How long does a custom cake take?

Order **2–4 weeks ahead** for most custom cakes, and **months ahead** for wedding cakes. Bakers need time for planning, baking, cooling, decorating and setting.

## Do you pay tax on cakes? Do you tip?

Sales tax on cakes depends on your state and whether it's prepared food. Tipping isn't expected for cakes, though some people tip for delivery or exceptional work.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much is a customized cake?
Often $4 to $15+ per serving, depending on design.

### How much for a custom birthday cake?
Often $80 to $300+ for 20 to 30 servings.

### Why are custom cakes so expensive?
They take many hours of design, baking and decorating, plus quality ingredients.

### How far in advance should I order a custom cake?
Usually 2–4 weeks; longer for weddings.

### Do you tip for custom cakes?
Not usually required, but appreciated for delivery or exceptional work.
