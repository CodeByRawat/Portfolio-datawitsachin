<p class="lede">To choose a demolition company, check that it's <strong>licensed and well insured</strong>, has <strong>experience with your type of project</strong>, handles <strong>permits, utility disconnects and hazardous material surveys</strong>, has the right <strong>equipment</strong>, explains how it will <strong>haul and recycle debris</strong>, and gives a <strong>detailed written quote</strong>. Get at least three quotes and check references.</p>

## Checklist

| Check | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Licence | Many areas require licensed demolition or contractors |
| Liability insurance and workers' comp | Protects you from damage and injury claims |
| Relevant experience | Houses, commercial or interior work |
| Permits handled | Avoids fines and delays |
| Utility disconnects | Safety |
| Asbestos and lead surveys | Required in many places before demolition |
| Equipment | Right machines for the job |
| Debris plan | Hauling, disposal and recycling |
| Site restoration | Grading and cleanup included? |
| References and reviews | Proof of reliable work |

## Questions to ask

1. Are you licensed and insured? Can I see proof?
2. Will you pull the permits?
3. Who handles utility disconnects?
4. Will you do a hazardous materials survey?
5. What's included in the price (hauling, grading, foundation removal)?
6. How long will it take?
7. How will you protect neighbouring properties?

## Compare quotes carefully

Make sure each quote covers the same scope: foundation removal, driveway removal, tree removal, backfill, grading and disposal.

## Red flags

- No insurance or licence
- Very low quotes without details
- Willing to skip permits or surveys
- Cash-only, no written contract

## Can a general contractor do it?

Often for interior demolition. For whole buildings, a specialist is usually best. More: [What is a demolition company?](/blog/what-is-a-demolition-company/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I choose a demolition company?
Check licensing, insurance, experience, permits, hazardous materials handling and get detailed quotes.

### How do I find a reliable demolition contractor?
Ask for references, check reviews and confirm licensing and insurance.

### Do demolition companies get the permits?
Good ones do, but confirm it in the contract.

### What should a demolition quote include?
Scope, permits, utility disconnects, hauling, disposal, grading and timeline.

### Is an asbestos survey required?
In many places, yes, before demolition.
