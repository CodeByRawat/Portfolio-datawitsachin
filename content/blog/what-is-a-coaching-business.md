<p class="lede">A <strong>coaching business</strong> helps clients achieve specific goals through structured conversations, accountability and guidance, and is paid for programmes, sessions, group coaching, courses or retainers. <strong>Business coaching</strong> focuses on helping owners and leaders improve their business: strategy, sales, operations, leadership and personal effectiveness. A <strong>business coach</strong> asks powerful questions, helps set priorities, holds the client accountable and shares frameworks, while the client does the work.</p>

## Types of coaching

| Type | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Life coaching** | Personal goals, transitions, confidence |
| **Business coaching** | Growing and running a business |
| **Executive coaching** | Senior leaders' performance and influence |
| **Leadership coaching** | Leading teams, communication (see [What is leadership coaching?](/blog/what-is-leadership-coaching/)) |
| **Career coaching** | Job search, transitions, promotions |
| **Mindset coaching** | Beliefs, habits, resilience |
| **Health and fitness coaching** | Nutrition, exercise, habits |
| **Sales coaching** | Sales skills and pipeline |

## What does a business coach do?

- Clarifies goals and priorities
- Reviews numbers and bottlenecks
- Helps build plans for sales, marketing, operations and hiring
- Provides frameworks and accountability
- Develops the owner's leadership and decision-making
- Celebrates progress and adjusts plans

## Coaching vs consulting vs mentoring vs therapy

| | Coaching | Consulting | Mentoring | Therapy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Approach** | Questions and accountability | Expert advice and solutions | Shares experience | Treats mental health |
| **Who does the work** | Client | Often consultant | Client | Client with clinician |
| **Regulated** | Usually not | Usually not | No | Yes (licensed) |

## What defines a good coach?

- Active listening and powerful questions
- Clear structure and goals
- Accountability without judgement
- Relevant experience and credibility
- Ethics and confidentiality
- Measurable client results
- Knows when to refer to other professionals

## How coaching businesses make money

1:1 programmes, group coaching, courses, workshops, retreats, corporate contracts, memberships and speaking (see [How much do coaches charge?](/blog/how-much-do-coaches-charge/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a coaching business?
A business that helps clients achieve specific goals through structured coaching, earning money from programmes, sessions, groups, courses or corporate contracts.

### What is business coaching?
Coaching that helps business owners and leaders improve strategy, sales, operations, leadership and personal effectiveness.

### What does a business coach do?
Clarifies goals, analyses the business, helps create plans, provides frameworks and holds the owner accountable.

### What is the difference between coaching and consulting?
Coaches guide clients to their own solutions with accountability; consultants provide expert solutions and often implement them.

### What defines a good coach?
Strong listening and questioning, structure, accountability, relevant experience, ethics and measurable results.

### What is a mindset coach?
A coach who helps clients change limiting beliefs, habits and thinking patterns to achieve goals.
