<p class="lede">A temp agency (temporary staffing agency) is a company that <strong>hires workers and places them in short-term or temp-to-hire jobs at client businesses</strong>. The agency <strong>pays the worker</strong> and <strong>bills the business</strong> a higher hourly rate, keeping the difference to cover payroll costs and profit. Workers <strong>don't pay</strong> a legitimate temp agency.</p>

## How temp agencies work

1. A business sends a job order (role, hours, pay, start date)
2. The agency recruits, screens and sometimes tests candidates
3. The agency places a worker at the business
4. The worker is on the agency's payroll; the agency pays wages and taxes
5. The business pays the agency a bill rate per hour
6. Some placements become permanent (temp-to-hire)

## Types of staffing agencies

| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| General / light industrial | Warehouses, manufacturing, logistics |
| Office and administrative | Reception, data entry, customer service |
| Healthcare | Nurses, aides, medical assistants |
| IT and technical | Developers, help desk |
| Hospitality and events | Servers, event staff |
| Executive search | Permanent senior roles |

## How temp agencies make money

The agency's **bill rate** is usually the worker's pay plus a **markup** (often 25%–75%). The markup covers payroll taxes, workers' comp, benefits, recruiting and profit. For permanent placements, agencies charge a fee, often a percentage of first-year salary.

## For workers

Temp agencies help you find work quickly, try different employers and sometimes get hired permanently. Learn more: [How to get hired through a temp agency](/blog/how-to-get-hired-through-a-temp-agency/) and [Do temp agencies cost money?](/blog/do-temp-agencies-cost-money/).

## For businesses

Temps help cover busy seasons, absences and projects without long hiring processes, and let you try workers before hiring.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are temporary agencies?
Companies that employ workers and place them in temporary jobs at client businesses.

### How does a temp agency work?
The business pays the agency, the agency pays the worker, and the agency handles recruiting and payroll.

### Who pays you when you work through a temp agency?
The agency pays your wages.

### What is temp-to-hire?
A temporary job that can become permanent if both sides are happy.

### How do staffing agencies make money?
By billing businesses more per hour than they pay workers.
