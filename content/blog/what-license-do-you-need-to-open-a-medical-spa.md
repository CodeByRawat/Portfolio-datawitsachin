<p class="lede">A medical spa usually needs <strong>business registration and a local business licence</strong>, a <strong>licensed physician medical director</strong>, properly <strong>licensed providers</strong> for each treatment, and any required <strong>facility, laser or radiation-device permits</strong>. Rules vary by state, so confirm with your state medical and nursing boards and a healthcare attorney.</p>

## Licences and approvals to expect

| Requirement | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Business entity and local licence | Legal operation |
| Medical director (licensed physician) | Oversight and delegation |
| Provider licences (MD, DO, NP, PA, RN) | Performing medical treatments |
| Esthetician/cosmetology licences | Non-medical skin services |
| Facility or clinic permits | In some states |
| Laser/radiation device registration | Some states regulate lasers |
| Drug and device purchasing rules | For injectables and prescriptions |

## Who can perform which treatments?

Generally, **injectables and medical lasers** must be done by licensed medical providers or delegated under supervision per state rules. **Estheticians** typically perform non-medical facials and peels within their scope. See [Can you open a spa with an esthetician license?](/blog/can-an-esthetician-open-a-med-spa/).

## Ownership

Some states restrict ownership of medical practices to physicians. Read [Who can open a medical spa?](/blog/who-can-open-a-medical-spa/).

## How to check if a med spa is licensed

Look up the medical director and providers on state licensing board websites, ask about supervision, and confirm the business is registered.

## Frequently asked questions

### What license do you need to open a medical spa?
Business licences, a licensed medical director, licensed providers and any state facility or device permits.

### Does a med spa need a medical director?
Yes, when offering medical procedures.

### Can a nurse open a med spa?
In some states nurses can, often with a physician medical director or MSO structure.

### Do you need a license for lasers?
Some states regulate laser operators and devices; check your state rules.

### How much does a medical spa license cost?
Fees vary by state and the number of licences and permits needed.

### How do I know if a med spa is licensed?
Check providers on state board websites and ask about medical oversight.
