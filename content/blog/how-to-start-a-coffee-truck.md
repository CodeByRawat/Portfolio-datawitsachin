<p class="lede">To start a coffee truck, choose a <strong>truck, van or trailer</strong>, outfit it with a <strong>commercial espresso machine, grinder, water and power systems</strong>, get your <strong>health permits, mobile vendor licence and commissary</strong>, build a <strong>simple, fast menu</strong>, and secure <strong>good locations and events</strong>. Startup costs often run <strong>$40,000 to $150,000+</strong>, depending on whether you buy new or used.</p>

## Step 1: Choose your vehicle

| Option | Rough cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Converted van | $30,000 – $80,000 | Compact, easy to park |
| Coffee trailer | $20,000 – $70,000 | Needs a tow vehicle |
| Step van / truck | $60,000 – $150,000+ | Most space, highest cost |

Used builds save money but check equipment and health code compliance.

## Step 2: Equipment

- Commercial espresso machine (2-group is common)
- Commercial grinders
- Fresh and grey water tanks, water pump and filtration
- Generator or battery/inverter system
- Refrigeration for milk
- Hand-wash sink and 3-compartment sink (as required)
- POS system

## Step 3: Permits and licences

Business licence, mobile food vendor permit, health department permit and inspection, commissary agreement, fire inspection (for propane), sales tax permit, and parking or event permits. See [Food truck permits and licenses](/blog/food-truck-permits-and-licenses/).

## Step 4: Startup costs

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Vehicle and build-out | $30,000 – $150,000 |
| Espresso machine and grinders | $8,000 – $25,000 |
| Generator or power system | $2,000 – $10,000 |
| Permits and licences | $1,000 – $5,000 |
| Branding and wrap | $2,000 – $6,000 |
| Initial inventory | $1,000 – $3,000 |
| Insurance | $2,000 – $5,000 per year |

## Step 5: Menu and pricing

Keep it short and fast: espresso drinks, drip or cold brew, a few signature drinks, and simple pastries. Price for a 70%+ gross margin on drinks.

## Step 6: Locations and events

- Office parks and business districts (mornings)
- Farmers' markets and festivals
- Weddings and private events (catering packages)
- Schools, hospitals and gyms
- Corporate coffee bars

Private events often bring the most reliable income.

Smaller start: [How to start a coffee cart business](/blog/how-to-start-a-coffee-cart-business/). Related: [How to start a coffee shop](/blog/how-to-start-a-coffee-shop/) and [How to start a food truck business](/blog/how-to-start-a-food-truck-business/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a coffee truck business?
Choose a vehicle, install espresso, water and power systems, get permits and secure good locations and events.

### How much does it cost to open a coffee truck?
Often $40,000 to $150,000+.

### Is a coffee truck profitable?
Yes. Coffee drinks have high margins, especially at busy spots and events.

### Do I need a commissary for a coffee truck?
Many health departments require one.

### What's cheaper, a coffee truck or a coffee shop?
A coffee truck is usually much cheaper to start.
