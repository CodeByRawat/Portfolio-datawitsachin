<p class="lede">To get into bookkeeping with no experience: learn <strong>accounting basics</strong> (debits, credits, reconciliations), master <strong>cloud accounting software</strong>, take a <strong>course or certification</strong>, practise on <strong>sample or volunteer books</strong>, then land an <strong>entry-level job or first freelance client</strong>. Experience then lets you start your own bookkeeping business.</p>

## Skills to learn

- Double-entry bookkeeping
- Chart of accounts
- Bank and credit card reconciliations
- Accounts payable and receivable
- Payroll basics
- Financial statements
- Spreadsheet skills

## Learning path

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Free or low-cost bookkeeping fundamentals course |
| 2 | Software certifications |
| 3 | Practice with sample companies |
| 4 | Volunteer for a nonprofit or friend's business |
| 5 | Apply for entry-level roles or freelance gigs |
| 6 | Optional professional bookkeeping certification |

## First jobs

Accounts payable/receivable clerk, bookkeeping assistant, office manager, or junior bookkeeper at a firm.

## First freelance clients

Friends' businesses, local small businesses, freelancer platforms and referrals. Start with simple monthly books and grow.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I get into bookkeeping with no experience?
Yes, by learning basics, getting software skills and starting with entry-level work.

### How do I start bookkeeping with no experience?
Take a course, earn software certifications, practise, then find a job or first client.

### How long does it take to learn bookkeeping?
The basics can be learned in weeks to months; mastery comes with practice.

### Do I need a degree to be a bookkeeper?
No; skills and certifications matter more.

### Can I do bookkeeping from home as a beginner?
Yes, once you have basic skills and software experience.

### What is the best first bookkeeping job?
Accounts payable/receivable or junior bookkeeper roles.
