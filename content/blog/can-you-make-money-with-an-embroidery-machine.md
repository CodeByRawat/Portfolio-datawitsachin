<p class="lede">Yes, you can make money with an embroidery machine. Home embroidery businesses often earn <strong>a few hundred to a few thousand dollars a month</strong> part-time, and full-time shops with commercial machines and business clients can bring in <strong>$50,000 to $200,000+</strong> a year. Profit per item is often <strong>$5 to $20+</strong> after blank and thread costs.</p>

These are rough ranges; pricing, niche and machine speed matter most.

## Profit per item (example)

| Item | Price | Blank + supplies | Gross profit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Logo polo (left chest) | $25 | $10 | $15 |
| Embroidered cap | $22 | $7 | $15 |
| Personalised towel | $20 | $8 | $12 |
| Custom patch | $6 | $1.50 | $4.50 |

Digitizing fees and set-up charges add profit on custom jobs.

## Best-selling embroidered products

- Company polos, jackets and caps
- Team and school apparel
- Personalised baby items and blankets
- Towels and robes
- Custom patches
- Tote bags and aprons

## How much can an embroidery business make?

| Business | Rough yearly revenue |
|---|---|
| Hobby / side hustle | $5,000 – $30,000 |
| Full-time home business | $30,000 – $100,000 |
| Commercial shop with multi-head | $100,000 – $500,000+ |

## Ways to earn more

- Win **business accounts** for repeat uniform orders
- Charge **digitizing and set-up fees**
- Offer **bulk pricing** with minimums
- Upgrade to multi-needle or multi-head machines for speed
- Sell personalised products online
- Add screen printing or heat transfers

## Do embroidery businesses make money?

Yes, especially those focused on business clients and bulk orders rather than single low-priced items.
