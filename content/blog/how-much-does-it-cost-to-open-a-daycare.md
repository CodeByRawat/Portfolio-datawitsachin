<p class="lede">Opening a <strong>home daycare</strong> can cost from <strong>a few thousand dollars</strong> (safety upgrades, equipment, licensing, insurance), while a <strong>daycare center</strong> often costs <strong>tens of thousands to several hundred thousand dollars</strong> or more, driven by the <strong>facility lease or purchase, renovations to meet licensing codes, equipment, staffing before enrolment fills, and working capital</strong>. Local rules, size and whether you renovate an existing childcare space make a big difference.</p>

## Rough startup costs by type

| Type | Rough range | Main costs |
|---|---|---|
| Home daycare | Low thousands | Safety items, toys, cots, licensing, insurance |
| Small center | Tens of thousands to $100k+ | Lease, build-out, equipment, staff |
| Large center | $100k–$500k+ | Facility, renovations, playground, staff |
| Franchise | Varies | Franchise fees + build-out |

## Cost breakdown

- Facility lease/purchase and deposits
- Renovations (bathrooms, kitchens, fire safety, accessibility)
- Furniture, toys, learning materials, cribs/cots
- Outdoor play equipment and fencing
- Licensing, background checks, training
- Insurance
- Childcare software and technology
- Marketing and website
- Pre-opening staff wages and working capital

## Monthly operating costs

Staff wages (largest), rent, utilities, food, supplies, insurance, software, maintenance, marketing.

## Funding

Savings, small business loans, grants and childcare stabilisation/expansion programmes where available, investors, and pre-enrolment deposits.

## Profitability drivers

Full enrolment, appropriate tuition, efficient staffing within ratios, waitlists, and controlled overhead. (Steps: [How to start a daycare](/blog/how-to-start-a-daycare/).)

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does it cost to open a daycare?
From a few thousand dollars for a home daycare to tens or hundreds of thousands for a daycare center.

### How much does it cost to start a home daycare?
Usually the lowest-cost option: safety upgrades, supplies, licensing, training and insurance.

### What is the biggest cost of running a daycare?
Staff wages, followed by rent or facility costs.

### Are there grants to start a daycare?
Some regions offer childcare grants or programmes; check local government and childcare agencies.

### Is a daycare business profitable?
It can be with strong enrolment, appropriate tuition and efficient staffing.

### How can I reduce daycare startup costs?
Start home-based, lease an existing childcare facility, buy used equipment and apply for available grants.
