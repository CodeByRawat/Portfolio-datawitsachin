<p class="lede">To start a laser engraving business, choose a <strong>laser</strong> (<strong>diode</strong> for low cost, <strong>CO2</strong> for wood and acrylic, <strong>fiber</strong> for metal), learn <strong>design software</strong>, set up a <strong>safe, ventilated workspace</strong>, pick <strong>products that sell</strong> (personalised gifts, signs, business items), and sell <strong>online, at markets and to local businesses</strong>. Home setups often start at <strong>$1,000 to $10,000</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Choose your laser

| Laser | Rough cost | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Diode | $300 – $2,000 | Wood, leather, some coated items |
| CO2 | $2,000 – $15,000+ | Wood, acrylic, glass, leather, cutting |
| Fiber | $3,000 – $15,000+ | Metal, tumblers, tools, jewellery |

Many businesses start with a CO2 and add fiber for metal.

## Step 2: Software and design

Learn laser software, vector design tools and how to prepare files. Buy commercial-use design licences or create your own designs.

## Step 3: Safety

- Proper ventilation or fume extraction
- Fire safety: never leave the laser running unattended, keep an extinguisher nearby
- Safety glasses suited to your laser
- Never cut PVC or vinyl (toxic gas)

## Step 4: Products that sell

| Product | Customers |
|---|---|
| Personalised cutting boards and tumblers | Gifts, weddings |
| Custom signs | Homes, businesses, events |
| Name tags and awards | Businesses, schools |
| Leather patches and keychains | Retail, brands |
| Wedding décor | Couples, planners |
| Business branding items | Promotional products |

## Step 5: Startup costs

Laser, extraction, materials, software, workspace, business licence and insurance: often $1,000 to $10,000+.

## Step 6: Pricing and selling

Price for material, machine time, design time and profit. Sell on Etsy, your website, markets and directly to businesses for bulk orders.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a laser engraving business?
Choose a laser, learn software, set up a safe space, pick products and sell online and locally.

### What laser is best to start a business?
A CO2 laser is versatile; diode lasers are cheaper; fiber lasers are for metal.

### How much does it cost to start laser engraving?
Often $1,000 to $10,000+.

### Can I run a laser engraving business from home?
Yes, with good ventilation and fire safety.

### What laser engraved products sell best?
Personalised gifts, signs, tumblers and business items.
