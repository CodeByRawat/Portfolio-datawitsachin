Sales and HR share a problem: skilled people spend huge amounts of time on coordination, chasing, and data entry. Both are strong automation territory, as long as the human moments stay human.

## Sales process automation

**Automate:**
- **Lead capture to CRM:** forms, email and ads create enriched, deduplicated records automatically.
- **Routing:** the right rep gets the right lead within minutes.
- **Follow-ups:** reminders and sequences triggered by what the prospect did.
- **Meeting prep and notes:** AI summarises the account before a call and logs the call after it.
- **Reporting:** pipeline reports build themselves.

**Keep human:** discovery calls, negotiation, relationship building. **Artificial intelligence in sales** works best as a prep and admin layer under the salesperson, not a replacement for them.

## HR workflow automation

**Automate:**
- **Onboarding:** one trigger creates accounts, sends documents for signing, schedules orientation, notifies IT.
- **Leave and expenses:** requests route to the right approver, with reminders and a record.
- **Recruiting logistics:** scheduling, status emails, collecting documents.
- **Employee FAQs:** an assistant answers policy questions from your handbook.

**Keep human:** performance conversations, sensitive cases, hiring decisions.

## Frequently asked questions

### What sales tasks can be automated?
Lead capture and enrichment, routing, follow-up reminders and sequences, meeting scheduling, CRM updates, proposal drafting and pipeline reporting.

### What HR processes are best to automate?
Onboarding and offboarding checklists, leave and expense approvals, document collection, interview scheduling and routine employee questions.
