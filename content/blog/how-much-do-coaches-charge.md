<p class="lede">Coaching prices vary widely. Many <strong>life and wellness coaches</strong> charge roughly <strong>$75–$300 per session</strong> or package programmes of a few hundred to a few thousand dollars; <strong>business and executive coaches</strong> often charge <strong>several hundred dollars per hour</strong> or <strong>thousands per engagement</strong>. Rates depend on niche, results, experience, client type (individual vs corporate) and how the offer is packaged. The most sustainable coaching businesses sell <strong>outcome-based packages</strong> rather than single hours.</p>

## Typical coaching price ranges (rough)

| Coaching type | Common pricing |
|---|---|
| Life/wellness/mindset | Per session or monthly packages |
| Health and fitness coaching | Monthly programmes |
| Career coaching | Packages for job search or transitions |
| Business coaching | Monthly retainers or 3–6 month programmes |
| Executive/leadership coaching | Premium engagements, often paid by employers |
| Group coaching | Lower per-person price, scalable |

Rates differ by country and market; use local research.

## Factors that affect coaching rates

- **Niche and outcome value** (e.g., revenue growth vs general wellbeing)
- **Experience and proof** (results, testimonials, credentials)
- **Client type:** corporate budgets vs individuals
- **Format:** 1:1, group, hybrid, access level (messaging support)
- **Duration and intensity**

## Package vs hourly pricing

Packages (e.g., 12 weeks with 6 sessions + messaging + resources) focus clients on outcomes, improve commitment and stabilise income. Hourly pricing is simple but caps earnings and encourages short engagements.

## How to set your prices

1. Define the outcome and its value to the client
2. Decide format and time commitment
3. Calculate your income goal ÷ realistic client capacity
4. Check market ranges
5. Start confidently and raise prices as demand and results grow

## How to make money as a coach (beyond 1:1)

- **Group programmes and cohorts**
- **Online courses and workshops**
- **Memberships/communities**
- **Corporate contracts and speaking**
- **Retreats and intensives**
- **Digital products** (workbooks, templates)
- **Apps or tools** for clients (see [How to create an app and make money](/blog/how-to-create-an-app-and-make-money/))

## Frequently asked questions

### How much do professional coaches charge?
It varies widely: many life coaches charge around $75–$300 per session, while business and executive coaches charge much more per hour or engagement.

### How much should a new coach charge?
Enough to be sustainable while you build proof; many new coaches start with packages at accessible prices and raise rates as results and testimonials grow.

### Do coaches charge per session or per package?
Both exist, but packages focused on outcomes are common and usually better for clients and coaches.

### How do coaches make money?
Through 1:1 and group programmes, courses, memberships, corporate contracts, speaking, retreats and digital products.

### Is coaching a profitable business?
It can be, with a clear niche, strong results, good pricing and scalable offers like groups and courses.

### Who pays for executive coaching?
Often the employer, as part of leadership development budgets.
