<p class="lede">The best plumbing leads come from <strong>people searching urgently on Google</strong>, so plumbers should prioritise a <strong>Google Business Profile with many reviews</strong>, <strong>Local Services Ads</strong> and <strong>emergency search ads</strong>, a <strong>fast website with click-to-call</strong>, and <strong>answering every call immediately</strong>. Add <strong>referral partnerships</strong> (property managers, real estate agents, contractors), <strong>maintenance plans</strong> and <strong>local SEO</strong> to build steady, lower-cost leads over time.</p>

## Top plumbing lead sources

| Source | Why it works |
|---|---|
| **Google Business Profile** | Map results for "plumber near me" (see [How to create a Google Business page](/blog/how-to-create-a-google-business-page/)) |
| **Local Services Ads** | Pay-per-lead, Google-screened badge (where available) |
| **Google Search Ads** | Emergency keywords ("burst pipe repair") |
| **Website + local SEO** | Service and city pages, water heater and drain pages |
| **Referrals** | Past customers, partners |
| **Property managers and landlords** | Repeat work |
| **Real estate agents and inspectors** | Pre-sale repairs |
| **Home service platforms** | Additional leads (quality varies) |

## How to market a plumbing business

1. Website with emergency number, services, prices or "starting from", financing and booking (see [How to make a booking website](/blog/how-to-make-a-booking-website/))
2. Ask every customer for a review
3. Branded vans and yard signs
4. Maintenance plans (water heater flush, drain cleaning)
5. Seasonal tips content (frozen pipes, water heater care)
6. Social proof: photos of jobs, technician intros
7. Email/SMS reminders and follow-ups

## Answer every call

Missed calls are lost jobs. Use call forwarding, an answering service or an AI receptionist, and text back missed callers immediately (see [How to create an AI chatbot](/blog/how-to-create-an-ai-chatbot/)).

## Track cost per job

Track leads, booked jobs and revenue by source to invest in the best channels. (Local marketing ideas: [How to promote your business locally](/blog/how-to-promote-your-business-locally/).)

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I get plumbing leads?
Optimise Google Business Profile, use Local Services Ads and emergency search ads, build a fast website with local SEO, get reviews and partner with property managers and real estate agents.

### How do plumbers get customers?
Mostly from Google searches, reviews and referrals, plus partnerships and repeat maintenance customers.

### How do I market my plumbing business?
Combine Google visibility, a strong website, reviews, branded vehicles, maintenance plans and fast call handling.

### Are Local Services Ads good for plumbers?
Often yes, because they appear at the top of searches and charge per lead, though you must respond quickly and manage disputes.

### How can plumbers reduce missed calls?
Use answering services, call forwarding, AI receptionists and automatic text-backs for missed calls.

### What is a good cost per plumbing lead?
It depends on market and job value; track cost per booked job and revenue per job rather than lead cost alone.
