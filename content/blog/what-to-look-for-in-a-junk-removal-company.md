<p class="lede">Look for a junk removal company with <strong>clear upfront pricing</strong> (flat-rate or by truck volume), <strong>no hidden fees</strong>, <strong>insurance</strong>, strong <strong>recent reviews</strong>, a clear list of what they take, and a <strong>recycling and donation</strong> policy. Get two or three quotes and ask what's included before you book.</p>

## What a junk removal service includes

Usually: loading from anywhere on the property, hauling, disposal fees, sweeping up, and recycling or donating usable items. Some charge extra for heavy items, stairs or same-day service.

## Checklist for choosing a company

| Check | Why |
|---|---|
| Upfront or flat-rate volume pricing | Avoid surprise fees |
| Price in writing before loading | Confirms the final cost |
| Insurance | Protects your property |
| Recent Google reviews | Shows reliability |
| What they won't take | Paint, chemicals, tires and similar |
| Recycling and donation | Less waste going to landfill |
| Availability | Same-day or next-day if needed |
| Uniformed, on-time crews | Professionalism |

## Questions to ask

1. How do you price: by volume, weight or item?
2. Are dump fees and labour included?
3. Are there extra charges for mattresses, appliances or stairs?
4. Are you insured?
5. Do you recycle or donate?
6. Can you give a quote from photos?

## How to research before hiring

Read Google reviews, check that the business is local and insured, compare at least two quotes, and look for clear prices on their website.

## For junk removal business owners

Customers compare you on exactly these points. Show volume pricing on your website, offer photo quotes, show insurance, highlight recycling and donation, and collect reviews after every job. More: [Is junk removal a good business?](/blog/is-junk-removal-a-good-business/).

## Frequently asked questions

### What should I look for in a junk removal company?
Clear pricing, no hidden fees, insurance, good reviews and recycling or donation.

### What is included in junk removal services?
Loading, hauling, disposal and cleanup, sometimes with extra fees for heavy items.

### How do I compare junk removal services?
Get two or three quotes for the same load and compare what's included.

### Do junk removal companies have hidden fees?
Some do. Ask about dump fees, heavy items and stairs before booking.

### What do junk removal companies do with your stuff?
Donate usable items, recycle metal and materials, and dispose of the rest.
