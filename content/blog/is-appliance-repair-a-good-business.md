<p class="lede">Yes, appliance repair is a good business, and it's <strong>not dying</strong>. Appliances are more expensive and complex than ever, and there's a <strong>shortage of skilled technicians</strong>. Owner-operators commonly earn <strong>$60,000 to $120,000+</strong>, with service businesses often keeping <strong>15% to 30% net profit</strong>. The challenges are <strong>electronics, parts delays and keeping up with new models</strong>.</p>

These are rough ranges; your market and pricing matter.

## Is appliance repair a dying business?

No. Some cheap appliances are replaced instead of repaired, but:

- Mid-range and high-end appliances are worth repairing
- New appliances cost more, so people repair longer
- Many older technicians are retiring
- Landlords and property managers need fast repairs
- Sustainability and right-to-repair trends support repair

## Pros

- Low startup cost compared with many trades
- Strong demand and few qualified techs
- Good pay per hour
- Mobile business with no shop needed
- Repeat customers and property manager contracts

## Cons

| Downside | How to manage it |
|---|---|
| Complex electronics | Ongoing training |
| Parts delays | Good supplier accounts, common parts on van |
| Heavy lifting | Proper dollies, a helper for big jobs |
| Low-paying warranty work | Use it to fill gaps only |
| Callbacks | Diagnose carefully, use quality parts |

## How profitable is an appliance repair business?

| Stage | Rough yearly revenue |
|---|---|
| Solo technician | $100,000 – $200,000 |
| Owner + 2–3 techs | $300,000 – $700,000 |

Profit comes from diagnostic fees, labour and parts markup.

## How to make it more profitable

Use flat-rate pricing, specialise in premium brands, limit low-rate warranty jobs, sell maintenance (dryer vent cleaning, fridge coil cleaning) and get direct customers through Google and online booking.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is appliance repair a good business?
Yes. Demand is strong and there's a shortage of technicians.

### Is appliance repair a dying business?
No. Higher appliance prices and fewer technicians keep demand strong.

### How profitable is an appliance repair business?
Solo technicians often bring in $100,000 to $200,000 a year in revenue.

### Is appliance repair hard?
It takes training, especially for electronics and refrigeration, but it's learnable.

### Should I do home warranty work?
It brings volume but pays less; balance it with direct customers.
