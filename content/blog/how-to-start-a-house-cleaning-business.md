## Steps

1. **Choose your model:** solo cleaner, small team or franchise
2. **Services:** standard cleaning, deep cleaning, move-in/move-out, recurring weekly/biweekly
3. **Register the business** and get any local licence (see [What licenses are needed to start a cleaning business?](/blog/what-licenses-are-needed-to-start-a-cleaning-business/) and [How to register a cleaning business](/blog/how-to-register-a-cleaning-business/))
4. **Insurance and bonding:** general liability and a janitorial bond build client trust
5. **Supplies:** vacuum, microfibre cloths, mops, eco-friendly products, caddies
6. **Pricing:** flat rate by home size and service, or hourly; offer recurring discounts
7. **Checklists:** room-by-room standards for consistent quality
8. **Online booking:** instant quotes, scheduling, reminders and card payments (see [How to make a booking website](/blog/how-to-make-a-booking-website/))
9. **First clients:** friends, neighbours, Nextdoor, Facebook groups, Google Business Profile (see [How to get clients for a cleaning business](/blog/how-to-get-clients-for-a-cleaning-business/))
10. **Hire and grow:** background checks, training, quality checks (see [How to grow a cleaning business](/blog/how-to-grow-a-cleaning-business/))

## Pricing tips

Flat rates by bedrooms/bathrooms are easy for clients to understand; recurring clients get lower rates; deep cleans and move-outs cost more.

## Startup costs

House cleaning has low startup costs: registration, insurance, supplies, a website and marketing. A vehicle is needed to travel between homes.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a house cleaning business?
Choose your model and services, register the business, get insured and bonded, buy supplies, set prices, create checklists, add online booking and find clients locally.

### How much does it cost to start a home cleaning business?
Startup costs are low: mainly registration, insurance, supplies and marketing.

### How should I price house cleaning?
Use flat rates by home size and service type, or hourly rates, with discounts for recurring service.

### Do I need insurance to clean houses?
General liability insurance and bonding are strongly recommended and often expected by clients.

### How do I get house cleaning clients?
Google Business Profile, referrals, Nextdoor, Facebook groups and a website with online booking.

### Can I start a cleaning business by myself?
Yes; many owners start solo and hire cleaners as demand grows.
