<p class="lede">Yes, junk removal is a good business for many people: <strong>low startup costs</strong> (a truck or dump trailer), <strong>steady demand</strong> from homeowners, landlords and realtors, and margins often around <strong>30% to 50%</strong>. Single-truck owners commonly bring in <strong>$100,000 to $250,000 a year</strong>. The downsides are <strong>heavy physical work and dump fees</strong>.</p>

These are rough ranges; local prices and dump fees vary a lot.

## Pros

- Start with a pickup and trailer or a used box truck
- No special license in most areas (just business license and insurance)
- Many customer types: homeowners, estate cleanouts, landlords, realtors, contractors
- Easy to explain and sell
- Extra income from scrap metal and resale items

## Cons

| Downside | How to manage it |
|---|---|
| Heavy physical work | Hire a helper, use dollies and ramps |
| Dump fees | Price by volume, recycle and donate |
| Truck costs | Buy used, maintain it well |
| Competition from franchises | Compete on speed, price clarity and reviews |
| Hazardous items | Have a clear list of what you don't take |

## Startup costs

| Setup | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Pickup + dump trailer | $10,000 – $25,000 |
| Used box or dump truck | $20,000 – $50,000 |
| Insurance, licenses, website | $2,000 – $5,000 |

## How junk removal companies price jobs

Most charge by **truck volume** (1/8, 1/4, 1/2, full load), with extra fees for heavy items like mattresses, appliances, tires and construction debris. Many show "flat-rate" volume pricing on their websites.

## How to start

1. Register an LLC and get insurance
2. Get a truck or dump trailer
3. Find local dumps, recycling centres and donation partners
4. Set volume pricing with a minimum charge
5. Market with Google, reviews, Facebook groups and realtor and property manager partnerships
6. Let customers upload photos online for a quick quote

## Frequently asked questions

### Is junk removal a good business?
Yes. Startup costs are low and demand is steady, but the work is physical.

### How much do junk removal businesses make?
Single-truck owners often bring in $100,000 to $250,000 a year in revenue.

### How much does it cost to start a junk removal business?
Often $10,000 to $50,000, depending on the truck or trailer.

### Do you need a license for junk removal?
Usually just a business license; some areas require a waste hauling permit.

### How do junk removal companies make extra money?
Selling scrap metal and reusable items, and offering demolition and cleanouts.
