Reading thousands of documents is slow and expensive, and mistakes carry real consequences. **Document review software** speeds up the work by helping reviewers find what matters first.

## Types of legal document review

- **Disclosure / discovery:** which documents are relevant and which are privileged.
- **Due diligence:** extracting key terms from contracts in a transaction.
- **Contract review:** checking agreements against a playbook.
- **Investigations and compliance:** finding patterns in communications.

## How software streamlines it

1. **Ingest and de-duplicate,** with OCR for scans.
2. **Search and filter** by date, sender, keyword and concept.
3. **Prioritise** using classification so likely-relevant documents come first.
4. **Extract** key fields (parties, dates, termination, liability caps).
5. **Flag** deviations from your standard positions.
6. **Audit and export** with a record of decisions.

## Quality controls to insist on

- **Sampling and validation:** measure recall and precision on a reviewed sample.
- **Privilege protocols:** AI shouldn't be the last check on privileged material.
- **Audit trail:** who reviewed what and why.
- **Security:** encryption, access control, and clarity on whether data is used to train any model.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is legal document review?
Examining large sets of documents for relevance, privilege, confidentiality or issues, typically in litigation, investigations, due diligence and contract analysis.

### Can AI do document review?
AI can prioritise, classify, cluster and extract information much faster than linear reading, but results need sampling and human validation, especially on privilege and key issues.

### How do I choose document review software?
Match it to the task (disclosure, due diligence, contract review), test on a sample of your own documents, check security and audit trails, and measure accuracy rather than trusting claims.
