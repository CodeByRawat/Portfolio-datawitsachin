<p class="lede">For basic <strong>mowing, edging and trimming</strong>, most places only require a <strong>general business licence/registration</strong> and tax ID. But if you <strong>apply pesticides, herbicides or certain fertilisers</strong>, most US states require a <strong>pesticide applicator licence</strong>. Some states also license <strong>landscape contractors, irrigation installers or tree work</strong>. Requirements vary by state and city, so always check locally.</p>

## Licences and registrations to consider

| Requirement | When needed |
|---|---|
| Business licence/registration | Almost always (city/county/state) |
| Sales tax permit | If your state taxes lawn services |
| Pesticide applicator licence | Applying weed/pest control products |
| Fertiliser licence | Some states for commercial fertiliser application |
| Landscape contractor licence | Some states for larger landscaping work |
| Irrigation licence | Installing or repairing sprinklers in some states |

## Insurance

- **General liability** for property damage and injuries
- **Commercial auto** for work vehicles
- **Workers' compensation** once you hire employees (often required)
- **Equipment insurance** for mowers and tools

Many clients, HOAs and commercial properties require proof of insurance.

## How to check the rules

Contact your city/county business licensing office, your state department of agriculture (pesticide licensing) and your state contractor board.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a licence to mow lawns?
Usually only a general business licence; requirements vary by city and state.

### Do I need a licence to spray weed killer for customers?
In most states, yes: commercial pesticide application requires a state licence.

### Do I need insurance for a lawn care business?
General liability insurance is strongly recommended and often required by clients.

### Can a teenager start a lawn mowing business without a licence?
Small informal mowing jobs are common, but local rules vary; check with your city.

### What is a pesticide applicator licence?
A state licence that permits applying pesticides commercially after training and an exam.

### Where do I get a lawn care business licence?
From your city or county business licensing office, plus state agencies for specialised licences.
