<p class="lede">Yes, concrete can be a very good business. Concrete contractors often earn <strong>10% to 25% net profit</strong>, and decorative work can be higher. Small owner-run companies commonly bring in <strong>$250,000 to $1 million+</strong> a year. The trade-offs are <strong>hard physical work, weather delays, equipment costs and the risk of costly mistakes</strong>.</p>

These are rough ranges; your market, crew and pricing matter most.

## Why concrete is a good business

- Constant demand: driveways, patios, foundations, sidewalks
- Jobs are large, so revenue adds up fast
- Decorative concrete commands premium prices
- Repeat work from builders and general contractors
- Hard to DIY, so homeowners hire professionals

## Downsides

| Downside | How to manage it |
|---|---|
| Weather delays | Schedule buffers, winter repair work |
| Physical labour | Build a good crew |
| Mistakes are permanent | Experience and good prep |
| Equipment costs | Rent before you buy |
| Slow-paying contractors | Deposits and progress billing |

## Startup costs

Often $20,000 to $100,000+ for a truck, trailer, tools, insurance and licensing. See [How to start a concrete business](/blog/how-to-start-a-concrete-business/).

## Most profitable concrete work

| Work | Why |
|---|---|
| Stamped and decorative | Premium pricing per sq ft |
| Commercial flatwork | Big jobs, repeat clients |
| Foundations for builders | Steady volume |
| Repair and resurfacing | Small crews, good margins |

## How to be more profitable

Bid accurately with all costs, take deposits, keep your crew busy, upsell sealing and decorative finishes, and get direct homeowner jobs through Google and a strong website.

Pricing help: [How to bid concrete jobs](/blog/how-to-bid-concrete-jobs/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is concrete a good business?
Yes. Demand is steady and profits are good, but it's physical and weather-dependent.

### How much do concrete companies make?
Small companies often bring in $250,000 to $1 million+ a year.

### What is the profit margin for concrete contractors?
Often 10% to 25% net, higher for decorative work.

### How much does it cost to start a concrete business?
Often $20,000 to $100,000+.

### Is concrete work seasonal?
In cold climates yes; many companies do repairs and indoor slabs in winter.
