<p class="lede">To become a demolition worker, start as a <strong>labourer</strong> with a demolition, construction or excavation company, complete <strong>safety training</strong> (OSHA 10 or 30), and learn on the job. To become a <strong>demolition equipment operator</strong>, get <strong>heavy equipment training</strong> (trade school, union apprenticeship or on-the-job) and experience on excavators, skid steers and loaders.</p>

## What demolition workers do

- Remove interior materials by hand (drywall, flooring, fixtures)
- Operate jackhammers, saws and small equipment
- Sort and load debris for recycling and disposal
- Set up barriers and follow safety plans
- Assist equipment operators

## Steps to get started

1. **Apply for labourer jobs** with demolition, construction and excavation companies
2. **Get safety training**: OSHA 10 or OSHA 30 construction
3. **Learn on the job**: tools, methods, safety
4. **Add certifications**: asbestos or lead awareness, confined space, fall protection, first aid
5. **Move into equipment**: train on skid steers, excavators and loaders

## Becoming a demolition operator

| Route | Notes |
|---|---|
| Union operating engineer apprenticeship | Paid training, strong wages |
| Heavy equipment school | Weeks to months |
| On-the-job | Start on small equipment, move up |
| CDL | Helps for hauling equipment and debris |

## Skills that help

Physical fitness, safety awareness, teamwork, mechanical ability, and following plans.

## Pay

Demolition labourers often earn around **$16 to $25 per hour**, while experienced equipment operators often earn more, especially union operators.

## Career path

Labourer → skilled demolition worker → equipment operator → foreman → estimator or demolition contractor.

## How to find demolition work

Apply directly to demolition contractors, general contractors, excavation and environmental (abatement) companies, staffing agencies and union halls.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I become a demolition worker?
Start as a labourer, get OSHA safety training and learn on the job.

### How do I become a demolition operator?
Get heavy equipment training and experience on excavators and skid steers.

### How do I find demolition jobs?
Apply to demolition and construction companies, staffing agencies and union halls.

### Do demolition workers need certification?
Safety training is common; hazmat work needs specific certifications.

### Is demolition work dangerous?
It can be; strict safety procedures and training reduce risk.
