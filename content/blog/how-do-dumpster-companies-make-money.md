<p class="lede">Dumpster companies make money by charging a <strong>flat rental price</strong> for delivery, a set number of days and a <strong>weight allowance</strong>, then adding <strong>fees for extra weight, extra days and banned items</strong>. Profit is what's left after <strong>landfill tipping fees, fuel, driver time and truck costs</strong>. Busy containers that turn over quickly make the most money.</p>

## Revenue sources

| Source | How it works |
|---|---|
| Base rental | Flat price by size, includes delivery, pickup and a rental period |
| Weight overage | Charge per ton above the included weight |
| Extra days | Daily fee after the rental period |
| Swaps | Fee to pick up a full dumpster and drop an empty one |
| Banned items | Fees for mattresses, tires, appliances |
| Trip fees | When a dumpster can't be placed or is blocked |
| Contractor accounts | Steady volume with set pricing |

## The main costs

1. **Tipping fees**: what the landfill or transfer station charges per ton
2. Truck payments, maintenance and fuel
3. Driver wages
4. Container purchase and repairs
5. Insurance
6. Marketing and software

## What drives profit

- **Utilisation**: dumpsters out on rent, not sitting in the yard
- **Route density**: more drops and pickups per day
- **Accurate weight pricing**: so heavy loads don't lose money
- **Repeat contractors**: lower marketing costs

## Example

A 20-yard rental at $450 might cost about $300 in dump fees, fuel, driver time and overhead, leaving around $150 profit. A company doing 10 rentals a day can earn a solid profit.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do dumpster companies make money?
They charge rental fees plus extra for weight, days and banned items, and keep the difference after dump fees and costs.

### What is the biggest cost for a dumpster company?
Usually landfill tipping fees, then truck and fuel costs.

### Why do dumpster rentals have weight limits?
Because the company pays the landfill by weight.

### How much profit is in one dumpster rental?
Often around $100 to $200, depending on size and dump fees.

### How do dumpster companies get customers?
Google, websites with online booking and contractor relationships.
