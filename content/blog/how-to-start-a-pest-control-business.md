<p class="lede">To start a pest control business: get the <strong>state pesticide applicator licence</strong> (and any business/operator licence your state requires), <strong>register the business</strong>, get <strong>liability and pollution insurance</strong>, buy <strong>equipment, PPE and approved products</strong>, choose <strong>residential, commercial or specialty</strong> services (termites, bed bugs, wildlife), sell <strong>recurring quarterly plans</strong>, set <strong>pricing</strong>, use <strong>scheduling and routing software</strong>, and market with <strong>Google, reviews and a fast website</strong>.</p>

## Steps

1. **Training and licensing:** pass state exams for a commercial pesticide applicator licence in the right categories; some states also license the business and require a certified operator
2. **Business plan:** services, target customers, service area, pricing and recurring plan strategy
3. **Register:** LLC or corporation, EIN, business bank account, local licence
4. **Insurance:** general liability, pest control errors and omissions, pollution liability, commercial auto, workers' comp
5. **Equipment:** sprayers, dusters, bait stations, traps, inspection tools, PPE, a vehicle with secure chemical storage
6. **Products:** buy from licensed distributors; follow labels and record-keeping rules
7. **Services:** general pest, rodents, termites, mosquitoes, bed bugs, wildlife exclusion
8. **Recurring plans:** quarterly or monthly service creates predictable revenue
9. **Software:** scheduling, routing, service records, invoicing and auto-pay
10. **Marketing:** Google Business Profile, reviews, Local Services Ads, door-to-door in season, website with booking

## Startup costs

Licensing and exams, insurance, equipment, initial products, vehicle, software and marketing. Starting residential general pest service needs less capital than termite work.

## Pricing

Initial service fee plus recurring plan pricing; one-time treatments cost more; specialty services (termites, bed bugs) are quoted after inspection.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a pest control business?
Get your state pesticide licence, register and insure the business, buy equipment and products, sell recurring plans and market locally.

### Do I need a licence to start a pest control business?
Yes; commercial pesticide application requires state licensing, and many states license the business too.

### How much does it cost to start a pest control business?
Costs cover licensing, insurance, equipment, products, a vehicle and marketing; general pest is the cheapest start.

### How do pest control companies make money?
Mostly from recurring service plans, plus one-time and specialty treatments.

### How do I get pest control customers?
Google Business Profile, reviews, Local Services Ads, door-to-door sales, referrals and a website.

### What pest control services are most profitable?
Recurring general pest plans, termites, mosquitoes and bed bug treatments.
