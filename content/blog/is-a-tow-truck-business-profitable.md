<p class="lede">Yes, a tow truck business can be profitable. Many single trucks bring in <strong>roughly $100,000 to $250,000 a year</strong> in revenue, and well-run towing companies often keep <strong>10% to 25%</strong> as profit. Owner-operators commonly earn <strong>$50,000 to $100,000+</strong>. The money depends on <strong>call volume, the mix of work and controlling insurance and fuel</strong>.</p>

These are rough ranges. Your area, rates and contracts will change them a lot.

## How tow companies make money

- Hook-up fee plus per-mile charges
- Roadside service (jump starts, lockouts, tire changes, fuel)
- Police and accident towing
- Private property impounds plus daily storage fees
- Transport for dealerships, auctions and body shops
- Winching and recovery
- Heavy-duty towing and recovery (highest prices)

## Most profitable towing work

| Work | Why it pays |
|---|---|
| Heavy-duty recovery | High hourly and per-job rates |
| Accident and police rotation | Higher fees plus storage |
| Private property impounds | Storage fees add up |
| Direct customer calls | Full price, no motor club cut |
| Motor club calls | Steady volume but lower rates |

## Biggest costs

1. Insurance
2. Truck payments
3. Fuel
4. Maintenance and tires
5. Driver wages
6. Yard rent and software

## How to make a towing business more profitable

- Get more direct calls through Google reviews and a fast click-to-call website
- Use motor clubs to fill gaps, not as your whole business
- Join police rotation lists and sign private property contracts
- Charge after-hours, winching and storage fees properly
- Keep trucks maintained to avoid downtime
- Use dispatch software to cut empty miles

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a towing business profitable?
Yes, when trucks stay busy and insurance and fuel are controlled. Margins of 10% to 25% are common for well-run companies.

### How much does a tow truck owner make?
Owner-operators often earn $50,000 to $100,000+, and multi-truck owners can earn more.

### How much does one tow truck make a year?
Often $100,000 to $250,000 in revenue, depending on call volume and work mix.

### Is towing a good business?
It can be, with steady demand year-round, but it's 24/7 work with high insurance costs.

### Do motor club calls pay well?
They pay less than direct calls but provide steady volume, especially for new companies.
