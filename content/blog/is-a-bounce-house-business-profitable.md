<p class="lede">Yes, a bounce house business can be very profitable. A commercial bounce house costing <strong>$1,500 to $3,000</strong> often rents for <strong>$150 to $350 a day</strong>, so it can <strong>pay for itself in 10 to 20 rentals</strong>. Part-time owners often make <strong>$15,000 to $50,000</strong> a year, and full-time inflatable rental companies can bring in <strong>$100,000 to $500,000+</strong>.</p>

These are rough ranges; local prices, insurance and weather matter.

## Profit per rental (example)

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Combo unit rental | $250 |
| Delivery labour | −$40 |
| Fuel | −$15 |
| Cleaning and wear | −$15 |
| Insurance and overhead share | −$30 |
| **Profit** | **about $150** |

## How much do bounce house businesses make?

| Business | Rough yearly revenue |
|---|---|
| Weekend side business (3–5 units) | $15,000 – $50,000 |
| Full-time (10–20 units) | $100,000 – $250,000 |
| Large party rental company | $250,000 – $500,000+ |

## Biggest costs

Insurance, labour for delivery and setup, trailers and fuel, storage, cleaning and repairs, and replacing units every several years.

## Seasonality

Spring and summer are busiest, especially water slides. Indoor events, schools and corporate events help in the off-season.

## How to increase profit

- Add water slides and obstacle courses (higher prices)
- Offer packages with tables, chairs and concessions
- Book multiple units per delivery
- Work with schools and HOAs for big events
- Use online booking and deposits
