<p class="lede">Painting company income depends on <strong>number of crews, job size, pricing, close rate and how many months you can paint</strong>. A <strong>solo painter</strong> earns roughly their labour plus margin, while <strong>owners of multi-crew companies</strong> earn from their teams and can make substantially more. Well-run painting companies with accurate estimates often achieve <strong>healthy gross margins</strong> because materials are a small share of the price.</p>

## Revenue drivers

| Driver | Effect |
|---|---|
| Crews | More crews = more jobs at once |
| Average job size | Exteriors, whole-home and commercial jobs are larger |
| Pricing | Pricing to true labour cost protects margin |
| Close rate | Better quotes and follow-up win more work |
| Season | Exterior work is seasonal in colder climates |

## Typical costs

Labour (largest cost), paint and materials, insurance, vehicles, marketing, software and overhead.

## Owner-operator vs crew-based company

| | Solo painter | Multi-crew owner |
|---|---|---|
| Income | Own labour + margin | Profit from crews |
| Time | Painting | Sales, estimating, managing |
| Scale | Limited by hours | Grows with crews |

## How painting companies increase profit

Accurate estimating, production rates per crew, upselling (cabinets, trim, decks), reviews and referrals, follow-up on every quote, and off-season interior and commercial work (see [How to run a painting business](/blog/how-to-run-a-painting-business/)).

## Is starting a painting business a good idea?

Often yes: low startup costs and steady demand. Success depends on estimating, sales and hiring good painters (see [How to start a painting business](/blog/how-to-start-a-painting-business/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a painting company make a year?
It varies with crews, job size, pricing and season; multi-crew companies earn far more than solo painters.

### How much do painting contractors make?
Solo contractors earn their labour plus margin; owners with crews earn from team production.

### What is a good profit margin for a painting business?
Margins vary; accurate estimating and efficient crews are key to healthy margins.

### Is a painting business profitable?
It can be very profitable relative to its low startup costs.

### How can a painting company make more money?
Improve estimating, follow up on quotes, add crews, upsell services and win commercial work.

### Do painting companies make money in winter?
Yes, through interior, cabinet and commercial projects.
