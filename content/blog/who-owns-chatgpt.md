<p class="lede"><strong>ChatGPT is owned by OpenAI</strong>, the San Francisco AI company. Since its October 2025 restructuring, ChatGPT is run by <strong>OpenAI Group PBC</strong>, a for-profit public benefit corporation that is <strong>controlled by the nonprofit OpenAI Foundation</strong>. Its biggest shareholders include the OpenAI Foundation (about 26% at the restructuring), <strong>Microsoft</strong> (roughly a quarter, about 27% as announced at the restructuring), and investors such as SoftBank, plus employees. No single person owns ChatGPT; Sam Altman is OpenAI's CEO.</p>

ChatGPT is one of the fastest-adopted consumer technologies in history, so it's natural to wonder who's behind it. This guide answers the most common questions: who owns ChatGPT, what "ChatGPT" stands for, who developed it, how long it's been around, how it works, and how it compares to alternatives like Claude and Gemini.

## Who owns ChatGPT?

### OpenAI owns and operates ChatGPT
ChatGPT is a product of **OpenAI**, founded in December 2015 as a nonprofit AI research lab with the mission of ensuring artificial general intelligence benefits all of humanity.

### OpenAI's ownership structure (2026)
OpenAI's structure has changed several times:

1. **2015:** founded as a nonprofit.
2. **2019:** created a "capped-profit" subsidiary (OpenAI LP) to raise the large sums needed for computing, while the nonprofit retained control. Microsoft invested $1 billion, then billions more in later years.
3. **October 2025 recapitalisation:** the for-profit arm became **OpenAI Group PBC**, a public benefit corporation. The nonprofit, renamed the **OpenAI Foundation**, retains control by appointing the board, and received an equity stake reported at about 26% (plus a warrant for more if the company's value grows substantially).

**Major shareholders (approximate, based on public reporting):**

| Shareholder | Approximate stake | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **OpenAI Foundation** (nonprofit) | ~26% at restructuring | Controls the company; appoints the board |
| **Microsoft** | ~27% announced at restructuring; later reported around 25% after new funding | Long-term strategic partner; Azure cloud provider |
| **SoftBank** | Significant stake (reported ~13% in late 2026) | Led major funding rounds |
| **Employees and other investors** | Remainder | Including venture firms, Thrive Capital, and others |

These percentages change as OpenAI raises new funding, and OpenAI hasn't published an official, current cap table, so treat them as estimates.

### Does Microsoft own ChatGPT?
**No.** Microsoft is a major investor and partner, not the owner. Microsoft holds a large minority stake in OpenAI, provides cloud computing through Azure, and has rights to use OpenAI's models in its products (such as Microsoft Copilot), but OpenAI controls ChatGPT.

### Does Elon Musk own ChatGPT?
**No.** Elon Musk was one of OpenAI's co-founders and early funders in 2015, but he left OpenAI's board in 2018 and has no ownership of ChatGPT. He later founded a competing AI company, xAI (maker of Grok), and has been in legal disputes with OpenAI.

### Who is the CEO of OpenAI?
**Sam Altman** is OpenAI's CEO. He was briefly removed by the board in November 2023 and reinstated within days after employee and investor pressure.

## Who developed ChatGPT?

ChatGPT was developed by **OpenAI's research and engineering teams**. OpenAI was co-founded in 2015 by a group including **Sam Altman, Greg Brockman, Ilya Sutskever, Elon Musk, Wojciech Zaremba and John Schulman**, among others. ChatGPT builds on OpenAI's GPT series of language models and on research by many people across the AI field, including the **transformer** architecture published by Google researchers in 2017.

## What does ChatGPT stand for?

**ChatGPT = Chat + GPT.**
- **Chat:** it's designed for conversation.
- **GPT = Generative Pre-trained Transformer:**
  - **Generative:** it generates new text (and now images, code and more).
  - **Pre-trained:** it is first trained on a vast amount of text to learn language patterns, then refined for specific behaviours.
  - **Transformer:** the neural network architecture it's built on.

(Searching for "chat gbt" or "chat gtp"? Those are common misspellings of ChatGPT.)

## How long has ChatGPT been around?

ChatGPT was **released to the public on November 30, 2022**, as a free "research preview". It reached an estimated 100 million users within about two months, then the fastest consumer app growth ever recorded at the time. By October 2025, OpenAI said ChatGPT had **800 million weekly users**.

## What is ChatGPT and how does it work?

**ChatGPT is an AI assistant** that understands and generates natural language. You can ask it questions, have it write or edit text, analyse files and data, write code, generate images, search the web, and (with paid features) carry out multi-step tasks.

### How it works, simply
1. **Pre-training:** a large language model is trained on huge amounts of text and code to predict the next word (token). This teaches it language, facts and reasoning patterns.
2. **Fine-tuning:** the model is trained on examples of helpful responses to follow instructions.
3. **Reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) and other methods:** people rate responses; the model learns to produce answers people prefer, and reasoning is strengthened with further training.
4. **Generation:** when you send a message, ChatGPT generates a response one token at a time, based on your message, the conversation history and its instructions.
5. **Tools:** it can use tools such as web search, code execution, image generation, file analysis and connectors to apps, extending what the model alone can do.

### Is ChatGPT an app?
Yes. ChatGPT is available on the **web** (chatgpt.com), as **mobile apps** for iOS and Android, and as **desktop apps** for macOS and Windows. Developers can also use OpenAI's models through the **OpenAI API**.

### Is ChatGPT free?
There's a **free tier** with access to capable models and usage limits, plus paid plans (Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu) with higher limits, more advanced models and features.

### Does ChatGPT use my computer's resources?
Mostly no. ChatGPT runs on OpenAI's cloud servers; your device just sends messages and displays responses. The apps use some memory and network bandwidth like any app, and features that operate your computer or browser use local resources for that control.

## Who owns artificial intelligence?

No one owns "AI" as a technology. It's a field of research and engineering developed by universities, companies and open-source communities worldwide over decades. Specific AI **models and products** are owned by the companies that build them: OpenAI (ChatGPT), Anthropic (Claude), Google (Gemini), Meta (Llama), Microsoft (Copilot, built largely on OpenAI models), xAI (Grok) and many others. Some models are released as **open-weight** models that anyone can download and run under their licence terms. (More: [What does AI stand for?](/blog/what-does-ai-stand-for/))
