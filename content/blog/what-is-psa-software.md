<p class="lede"><strong>PSA stands for professional services automation.</strong> PSA software is an all-in-one platform that helps service businesses (consultancies, agencies, IT managed service providers, accounting and engineering firms) manage the full lifecycle of client work: selling and scoping projects, planning resources, tracking time and expenses, delivering projects or support tickets, billing clients and measuring profitability.</p>

Service businesses sell time and expertise. Their profit depends on keeping skilled people busy on the right work, tracking every billable hour, delivering on budget, and invoicing accurately and quickly. Doing that across spreadsheets, a CRM, a project tool, a time tracker and an accounting system is painful and leaky. PSA software brings it together. This guide explains what PSA is, PSA for MSPs specifically, key features, how PSA compares to other systems, tools, and how AI is changing it.

## What does PSA mean in business?

In a business software context, **PSA = professional services automation**: software for running a professional services organisation. (Outside software, "PSA" can mean other things, such as a public service announcement or a purchase and sale agreement in real estate, but in services and IT, it's professional services automation.)

## Who uses PSA software?

- **IT managed service providers (MSPs)**
- **Consulting firms** (management, technology, engineering)
- **Marketing, creative and digital agencies**
- **Accounting, legal and architecture firms**
- **Software companies' professional services and implementation teams**
- **Research and advisory firms**

## What does PSA software do? Core features

### 1. Sales and opportunity management (CRM-lite)
Track opportunities, scope projects, create estimates and proposals, and convert won deals into projects.

### 2. Project management
Plan projects with phases, tasks, milestones, budgets and dependencies; track progress against plan.

### 3. Resource management
See who is available, with which skills, and when; assign people to projects; forecast capacity and utilisation; avoid overbooking and bench time.

### 4. Time and expense tracking
Capture billable and non-billable hours and expenses against projects and tasks, with approvals.

### 5. Service desk and ticketing (especially for MSPs)
Manage client support requests, SLAs, escalations and recurring maintenance.

### 6. Billing and invoicing
Support fixed-fee, time-and-materials, retainer, milestone and recurring (managed services) billing; generate invoices from approved time and expenses; integrate with accounting.

### 7. Contracts and agreements
Manage service agreements, retainers, SLAs and renewals.

### 8. Reporting and analytics
Utilisation, realisation, project margins, budget vs actual, revenue forecasts, client profitability.

### 9. Client portal
Clients view tickets, project status, invoices and approvals.

## What is a PSA for MSPs?

For **managed service providers** (companies that manage IT for other businesses), PSA is the operational backbone. An MSP PSA typically focuses on:

- **Ticketing and service desk** with SLA tracking
- **Contracts and agreements** for monthly managed services, block hours and projects
- **Recurring billing** based on devices, users or services
- **Time tracking** against tickets
- **Integration with RMM** (remote monitoring and management) tools, so alerts from monitored devices automatically create tickets
- **Integration with documentation, quoting and accounting tools**
- **Client reporting** (SLA performance, ticket volumes, service reviews)

The PSA and RMM together form the core of an MSP's tech stack.

## PSA vs CRM vs ERP vs project management tools

| System | Main focus | Overlap with PSA |
|---|---|---|
| **CRM** | Sales pipeline and customer relationships | PSA includes basic opportunity management; many firms integrate a full CRM (see [What is a CRM?](/blog/what-is-a-crm/)) |
| **Project management** (Asana, Monday, Jira) | Tasks and collaboration | PSA adds resourcing, time, billing and profitability |
| **ERP** | Finance, procurement, inventory, HR across the company | PSA focuses on service delivery operations; integrates with ERP/accounting |
| **Accounting** (QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite) | Bookkeeping, AR/AP, reporting | PSA sends invoices and billing data to accounting |
| **Service desk / ITSM** (ServiceNow, Zendesk) | Support tickets | MSP PSAs include service desk features tied to billing |

The value of PSA is that **the same data flows from sale to delivery to invoice to profitability report** without re-entry.

## Key PSA metrics

| Metric | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Utilisation rate** | % of available hours spent on billable work |
| **Realisation rate** | % of billable value actually invoiced and collected |
| **Project margin** | Revenue minus delivery cost per project |
| **Budget variance** | Actual vs planned hours and cost |
| **Revenue per consultant/technician** | Productivity |
| **Bench time** | Unassigned capacity |
| **SLA compliance** (MSPs) | Tickets resolved within agreed times |
| **Days sales outstanding (DSO)** | How fast clients pay |

## Benefits of PSA software

- **Higher utilisation** through better resource planning
- **Fewer billing leaks:** time and expenses captured and invoiced accurately
- **Faster invoicing and cash collection**
- **Better project delivery:** on time and on budget
- **Visibility** into profitability by client, project and person
- **Less admin:** fewer spreadsheets and double entry
- **Scalability** as the firm grows

## How AI and automation are changing PSA

Modern PSA platforms and integrations increasingly use AI and automation to:

- **Auto-capture time** from calendars, tickets and activity, suggesting timesheet entries
- **Triage and route tickets** by urgency, category and technician skill; draft responses (see [Will AI replace customer service?](/blog/will-ai-replace-customer-service/))
- **Summarise tickets and projects** for handoffs and client updates
- **Forecast resource demand** and flag future over- or under-capacity
- **Predict project overruns** early based on progress and burn rate
- **Draft proposals, statements of work and status reports**
- **AI agents** for MSPs that investigate alerts, gather diagnostics and resolve common issues within defined permissions, escalating to technicians with context

Integrations via APIs, webhooks and automation platforms (n8n, Make, Zapier, Power Automate) connect PSA with CRM, accounting, RMM and communication tools. (See [What is CRM integration?](/blog/crm-integration-and-automation/) and [What is a webhook?](/blog/what-is-a-webhook/))

## How to choose PSA software

1. **Define your business model:** MSP, consulting, agency, implementation team; billing types (T&M, fixed fee, retainers, recurring).
2. **Map your current workflow** from sale to invoice and identify where time and money leak.
3. **List must-have integrations:** accounting, CRM, RMM, calendar, payroll.
4. **Evaluate resource management and reporting depth,** not just project features.
5. **Check usability** for consultants and technicians who must log time daily.
6. **Consider AI and automation capabilities.**
7. **Check scalability and total cost** (per-user pricing, implementation, add-ons).
8. **Run a pilot** with real projects and users.

## Implementation tips

- **Clean your data first:** clients, contracts, rates, service catalogue.
- **Standardise processes:** project templates, ticket categories, approval workflows.
- **Make time entry easy:** mobile apps, timers, AI suggestions and reminders.
- **Integrate accounting early** to close the loop on billing.
- **Train by role:** consultants, project managers, finance and leaders need different things.
- **Review metrics monthly** and adjust.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is PSA software?
PSA (professional services automation) software is an integrated platform for service businesses that manages opportunities, projects, resources, time and expenses, service tickets, billing and profitability reporting in one system.

### What does PSA stand for in business?
In business software, PSA stands for professional services automation. In other contexts it can mean public service announcement or, in real estate, purchase and sale agreement.

### What is a PSA for an MSP?
For a managed service provider, a PSA is the core business system for ticketing, SLAs, contracts, recurring billing, time tracking and client reporting, usually integrated with an RMM tool that monitors client devices.

### What is the difference between PSA and RMM?
PSA manages the business side of an MSP (tickets, contracts, time, billing and reporting), while RMM (remote monitoring and management) monitors and manages client devices and networks. Alerts from RMM typically create tickets in the PSA.

### What is the difference between PSA and project management software?
Project management software focuses on tasks and collaboration. PSA adds resource management, time and expense tracking, billing, contracts and profitability reporting tied to each project.

### What are examples of PSA software?
For MSPs: ConnectWise PSA, Autotask, HaloPSA, SuperOps and Syncro. For consulting and agencies: Kantata, Certinia, Planview Changepoint, Accelo, Scoro and Productive.
