<p class="lede">To start a party planning business: choose your <strong>party types</strong> (kids' birthdays, milestone birthdays, baby showers, bridal showers, themed parties, corporate celebrations), build a <strong>styled portfolio</strong>, <strong>register and insure</strong> the business, create <strong>packages</strong> (planning, décor, full-service), decide whether to <strong>own or rent décor</strong>, partner with <strong>bakers, venues, entertainers and rental companies</strong>, and market heavily on <strong>Instagram and Pinterest</strong> with an easy <strong>online inquiry or booking</strong> page.</p>

## Steps

1. **Pick a niche:** kids' parties, luxury balloon/décor styling, showers, themed events, picnics
2. **Practice and portfolio:** plan parties for friends and family; style shoots with professional photos
3. **Register and insure:** business licence, liability insurance, contracts
4. **Packages:** e.g., Décor Only, Planning + Décor, Full Service (vendors + on-site)
5. **Pricing:** cost of materials + time + travel + profit (see [How much do event planners charge?](/blog/how-much-do-event-planners-charge/))
6. **Inventory:** start renting décor; buy reusable signature pieces over time
7. **Vendor partners:** bakers, entertainers, photographers, rental companies, venues
8. **Marketing:** Instagram Reels of setups, Pinterest, Google Business Profile, local mum groups, referral discounts
9. **Booking:** website with packages, gallery and inquiry/booking form with deposit (see [How to make a booking website](/blog/how-to-make-a-booking-website/))
10. **Grow:** add staff for setups, expand into weddings or corporate events (see [How to start an event planning business](/blog/how-to-start-an-event-planning-business/))

## How to become a party planner

No formal licence is usually needed beyond a business licence; skills come from practice, creativity and organisation. Courses in event planning or balloon/décor styling can help (see [How to become an event planner](/blog/how-to-become-an-event-planner/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a party planning business?
Choose a niche, build a styled portfolio, register and insure the business, create packages and pricing, partner with vendors and market on Instagram and Pinterest with easy booking.

### How do I become a party planner?
Practice by planning parties for your network, build a portfolio, learn décor and logistics, and start offering paid packages.

### Can I run a party planning business from home?
Yes; many planners work from home and store décor inventory there or rent storage.

### How much do party planners charge?
It depends on services and location; packages range from décor-only to full-service planning.

### What do I need to start a party planning business?
A business licence, insurance, contracts, a portfolio, basic décor inventory or rental partners, and a way for clients to book.

### How do party planners get clients?
Instagram and Pinterest content, referrals, Google Business Profile, local parent groups and vendor partnerships.
