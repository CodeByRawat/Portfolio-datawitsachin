<p class="lede">To build backlinks, <strong>create content and assets worth linking to</strong> (original data, tools, guides), then <strong>promote them</strong> to people who write about your topic through <strong>digital PR, guest contributions, partnerships, local organisations, broken link building and reclaiming unlinked mentions</strong>. Focus on <strong>relevant, trusted websites</strong> linking naturally; avoid buying links or link schemes, which violate Google's spam policies.</p>

## What is a backlink?

A **backlink** is a link from another website to yours. Search engines treat relevant links from trusted sites as signals of credibility. Links also bring referral traffic.

## How does link building help SEO?

- Helps search engines **discover** your pages
- Signals **authority and trust** in your topic
- Can **improve rankings**, especially for competitive keywords
- Brings **referral traffic** and brand exposure

Links aren't the only factor: content quality and relevance still come first. (Foundations: [What is SEO?](/blog/what-is-seo/))

## What makes a good backlink?

| Quality signal | Good | Poor |
|---|---|---|
| **Relevance** | Site in your industry or location | Unrelated sites |
| **Trust** | Real business, publication or organisation | Spammy link farms |
| **Placement** | Within content, editorially given | Footer, sitewide, comment spam |
| **Traffic** | Site has real readers | No organic traffic |
| **Anchor text** | Natural (brand, URL, descriptive) | Over-optimised exact keywords |
| **Link attributes** | Followed editorial links carry most SEO value; `nofollow`/`sponsored`/`ugc` are hints | — |

## What is a backlink profile?

Your **backlink profile** is the full set of links pointing to your site: number of linking domains, their quality, anchor texts, followed vs nofollow ratio and growth over time. Tools like Semrush, Ahrefs, Moz and Google Search Console's Links report show it. A healthy profile is diverse and natural.

## 12 white-hat ways to create backlinks

1. **Create linkable assets:** original research, statistics pages, free tools/calculators, templates, in-depth guides.
2. **Digital PR:** pitch newsworthy data or stories to journalists; respond to journalist requests (platforms like Qwoted, Featured, Help a B2B Writer and similar).
3. **Guest contributions:** write genuinely useful articles for relevant industry sites (not low-quality guest post farms).
4. **Partnerships:** suppliers, clients, integration partners and associations often list partners.
5. **Local links:** chambers of commerce, local news, sponsorships, events, schools, community groups (see [How to promote your business locally](/blog/how-to-promote-your-business-locally/)).
6. **Business directories and citations:** reputable and industry-specific ones (see [What are local citations?](/blog/what-are-local-citations/)).
7. **Unlinked brand mentions:** find mentions of your brand without links and ask for a link.
8. **Broken link building:** find broken links on relevant pages and suggest your resource as a replacement.
9. **Resource pages:** get listed on curated "resources" pages in your niche.
10. **Expert quotes and podcasts:** appear as a guest; show notes often link back.
11. **Testimonials:** write testimonials for tools you use; many feature customer websites.
12. **Content repurposing:** turn data into infographics, videos or slides that others embed with attribution.

## Link building for local businesses

- Local sponsorships and charity events
- Local press about community involvement
- Supplier and partner pages
- Industry associations and licensing bodies
- Local "best of" lists and guides
- Collaborations with nearby complementary businesses

## What to avoid

- **Buying links** that pass PageRank
- **Private blog networks (PBNs)**
- **Excessive link exchanges**
- **Automated links,** comment spam and forum spam
- **Low-quality directories** in bulk
- **Over-optimised anchor text**

These violate Google's spam policies and can lead to ranking drops or manual actions.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I build backlinks?
Create valuable content and assets, then promote them through digital PR, guest contributions, partnerships, local organisations, broken link building and reclaiming unlinked mentions.

### How does link building help SEO?
Relevant links from trusted sites signal authority to search engines, help pages get discovered and can improve rankings, as well as sending referral traffic.

### What is a backlink profile?
The complete set of links pointing to your website, including how many domains link to you, their quality, anchor text and link types.

### How many backlinks do I need to rank?
There's no fixed number; it depends on competition. Analyse the top-ranking pages for your keyword and aim for comparable relevant, quality links.

### Is buying backlinks safe?
No. Buying links that pass ranking value violates Google's spam policies and can harm rankings.

### How long does it take for backlinks to work?
Often weeks to a few months after Google discovers and processes the links, depending on site authority and competition.
