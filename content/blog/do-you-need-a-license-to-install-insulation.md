<p class="lede">If you install insulation for customers, you'll usually need a <strong>business license</strong>, and many states require a <strong>contractor or home improvement license</strong>, sometimes with a specific <strong>insulation classification</strong>. Homeowners usually don't need a license to insulate their own home, and many simple retrofit jobs don't need a permit, but <strong>new construction and some remodels require permits and inspections</strong>.</p>

Rules vary a lot by state and city. Check your state contractor board and local building department.

## For insulation businesses

| Requirement | When it applies |
|---|---|
| Business license | Almost always |
| State contractor license | Many states, often above a dollar limit |
| Home improvement license | Some states for residential work |
| Insulation / acoustical classification | Some states (for example California) |
| Spray foam training and certification | Strongly recommended, sometimes required by insurers |
| Lead-safe certification | Work disturbing paint in pre-1978 homes |

## Insurance

General liability, workers' comp (if you hire) and commercial auto. Spray foam contractors often need specialised coverage.

## Do you need a permit for insulation?

- **New construction**: insulation is usually inspected as part of the building permit
- **Remodels and additions**: usually covered by the project's permit
- **Simple attic top-ups or blown-in retrofit**: often no permit needed
- **Spray foam in some areas**: may require a permit or fire-protection coverings

Ask your local building department before starting.

## For homeowners

You can usually insulate your own home without a license, but follow safety and fire codes, and check whether a permit is needed.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do you need a license to install insulation?
For customers, usually a business license and often a contractor license.

### Do you need a permit for insulation?
New construction and remodels usually do; simple attic top-ups often don't.

### Can I install insulation myself without a license?
Usually yes, in your own home.

### Do spray foam installers need certification?
It's strongly recommended and sometimes required by insurers or local rules.

### Who inspects insulation?
The local building department, for permitted projects.
