<p class="lede"><strong>Inbound marketing</strong> is a strategy that <strong>attracts customers by creating valuable content and experiences they're already looking for</strong> (blog posts, SEO, videos, guides, tools, social media and email) instead of interrupting them with cold ads or calls. People find you when they search for answers, you <strong>build trust by helping</strong>, then convert them with relevant offers. "Inbound" is the opposite of <strong>outbound marketing</strong>, where you push messages to people who didn't ask.</p>

## The inbound methodology: attract, engage, delight

| Stage | Goal | Tactics |
|---|---|---|
| **Attract** | Draw the right people | SEO, blog content, YouTube, social media, podcasts |
| **Engage** | Turn visitors into leads and customers | Landing pages, lead magnets, chatbots, email, consultations |
| **Delight** | Make customers successful and promoters | Onboarding, support, education, community, reviews, referrals |

This model (popularised by HubSpot) is often shown as a **flywheel**: delighted customers attract new ones.

## Inbound vs outbound marketing

| | Inbound | Outbound |
|---|---|---|
| **Approach** | Pull: be found | Push: reach out |
| **Examples** | SEO, content, social, email to subscribers | Cold calls, cold email, TV/radio, display ads, trade shows |
| **Audience** | People searching or interested | Broad or targeted lists |
| **Cost over time** | Compounds; content keeps working | Stops when spending stops |
| **Speed** | Slower to start | Faster results |
| **Trust** | Higher | Lower |

Most businesses benefit from both: inbound for sustainable growth, outbound or ads for speed and specific targets (see [What is account-based marketing?](/blog/what-is-account-based-marketing/)).

## Inbound marketing examples

- A web designer publishing "How much does a website cost?" and getting enquiries from readers (see [How much does a website cost?](/blog/how-much-does-a-website-cost/))
- A SaaS company offering a free calculator that captures leads
- A dentist posting short videos answering patient questions
- An accounting firm sending a monthly tax-tips newsletter (see [How to write a newsletter](/blog/how-to-write-a-newsletter/))
- A YouTube channel with tutorials linking to paid courses

## Inbound marketing channels

- **SEO and blogging** (see [What is SEO?](/blog/what-is-seo/))
- **Video** (YouTube, short-form)
- **Social media** (organic)
- **Email marketing and newsletters**
- **Lead magnets:** guides, templates, checklists, tools
- **Webinars and podcasts**
- **Landing pages and chatbots**
- **Reviews and referrals**

## The inbound funnel

| Funnel stage | Content | Conversion |
|---|---|---|
| **Top (awareness)** | Blog posts, videos answering questions | Subscribe, follow |
| **Middle (consideration)** | Guides, comparisons, case studies, webinars | Download, register (lead) |
| **Bottom (decision)** | Pricing, demos, consultations, testimonials | Book a call, buy |

## How to start inbound marketing

1. **Define your ideal customer** and their questions
2. **Research keywords** they search (see [How to choose keywords for SEO](/blog/how-to-choose-keywords-for-seo/))
3. **Create helpful content** consistently
4. **Build conversion paths:** CTAs, landing pages, lead magnets (see [What is a landing page conversion?](/blog/what-is-a-landing-page-conversion/))
5. **Nurture leads** with email automation
6. **Hand off to sales** at the right moment (see [What is inbound sales?](/blog/what-is-inbound-sales/))
7. **Delight customers** for reviews and referrals
8. **Measure and improve**

## Inbound marketing tools

HubSpot, WordPress/Webflow, Semrush/Ahrefs, Google Search Console and GA4, email platforms (Mailchimp, Kit, Brevo), CRM, chatbots and automation tools (see [What is content automation?](/blog/what-is-content-automation/)).

## Inbound metrics

Organic traffic, leads by source, conversion rates, cost per lead, lead-to-customer rate, customer acquisition cost, and revenue from inbound.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is inbound marketing?
A strategy that attracts customers by creating helpful content and experiences they seek, such as articles, videos, SEO, social media and email, rather than interrupting them.

### What is the difference between inbound and outbound marketing?
Inbound pulls customers in with content they find themselves; outbound pushes messages to people through cold outreach and traditional advertising.

### What are examples of inbound marketing?
Blog posts that rank on Google, YouTube tutorials, free tools, downloadable guides, newsletters, webinars and social media content.

### What are the stages of inbound marketing?
Attract, engage and delight, often visualised as a flywheel.

### Does inbound marketing work for small businesses?
Yes. Answering customers' questions online builds visibility and trust affordably, though it takes months to compound.

### How long does inbound marketing take to work?
Typically three to twelve months for meaningful results, depending on content volume, competition and promotion.
