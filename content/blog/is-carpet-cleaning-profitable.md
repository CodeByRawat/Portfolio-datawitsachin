<p class="lede">Yes, carpet cleaning is profitable. Owner-operators often keep <strong>40% to 60%</strong> of revenue as profit before their own pay, and a single truck-mount van commonly brings in <strong>$100,000 to $200,000 a year</strong>. Profit grows with <strong>upsells</strong> (upholstery, rugs, tile, pet treatments) and <strong>repeat customers</strong>.</p>

These are rough ranges; your area and pricing matter most.

## How much carpet cleaning businesses make

| Stage | Rough yearly revenue |
|---|---|
| Part-time, portable machine | $20,000 – $50,000 |
| Full-time, one truck mount | $100,000 – $200,000 |
| Multiple vans | $300,000+ |

## Startup costs

| Setup | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Portable extractor + van you own | $3,000 – $10,000 |
| Used truck mount + van | $20,000 – $40,000 |
| New truck mount + new van | $60,000 – $100,000+ |

## Where the profit comes from

- **Carpet cleaning** (per room or per square foot)
- **Upholstery** (sofas, chairs, cars)
- **Area rugs** (in-plant rug cleaning pays well)
- **Tile and grout cleaning**
- **Pet odour and stain treatment**
- **Protectant (stain guard)**
- **Commercial contracts** (offices, apartments, restaurants)

Upsells often add 20%–50% to each job.

## Costs to control

Fuel, chemicals, equipment repairs, van payments, insurance, marketing and labour.

## How to be more profitable

- Avoid "3 rooms for $X" bait pricing; price by area and condition
- Offer packages with upholstery and protectant
- Build repeat customers with 6–12 month reminders
- Get property manager and apartment turnover contracts
- Use reviews and a booking website to reduce paid lead costs

## Frequently asked questions

### Is carpet cleaning profitable?
Yes. Margins are high because chemicals are cheap and jobs can be priced well.

### How much do carpet cleaning businesses make?
Full-time owner-operators often bring in $100,000 to $200,000 per truck.

### Is carpet cleaning a good business to start?
It can be, with modest startup costs and steady demand from homes and property managers.

### Truck mount or portable?
Portables are cheaper to start and work in apartments; truck mounts are faster and more powerful.

### What is the most profitable carpet cleaning service?
Area rugs, upholstery and commercial contracts often have the highest margins.
