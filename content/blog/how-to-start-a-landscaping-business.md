<p class="lede">To start a landscaping business: choose your <strong>services</strong> (lawn maintenance, landscape design, planting, hardscaping, irrigation, snow removal), register the <strong>business</strong> and get any required <strong>licences</strong> (some services like pesticide application or irrigation need special licences), buy <strong>liability insurance</strong>, get <strong>equipment</strong> and a vehicle/trailer, set <strong>pricing</strong> for recurring maintenance and projects, use <strong>scheduling and invoicing software</strong>, and win clients with <strong>Google, neighbourhood marketing and referrals</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Choose your services

| Service | Revenue type |
|---|---|
| Lawn mowing and maintenance | Recurring, seasonal |
| Landscape design and installation | Project-based, higher ticket |
| Hardscaping (patios, walls) | High ticket, skills and equipment |
| Irrigation | Specialised, licensing in some areas |
| Tree and shrub care | Recurring and project |
| Snow removal | Winter revenue in cold climates |
| Commercial grounds maintenance | Contracts (see [How to get commercial landscaping contracts](/blog/how-to-get-commercial-landscaping-contracts/)) |

## Step 2: Licences and insurance

Business registration, local business licence, contractor licence for larger projects in some areas, pesticide applicator licence if applying chemicals, general liability, commercial auto and workers' comp with employees (details: [Landscaping business costs, licences and profitability](/blog/landscaping-business-costs-and-licenses/)).

## Step 3: Equipment

Mowers, trimmers, blowers, hand tools, safety gear, truck and trailer. Start with what you need for your core service; rent specialty equipment.

## Step 4: Pricing and bidding

Price maintenance by property size and frequency; price projects by materials + labour + equipment + overhead + profit (see [How to price landscaping jobs](/blog/how-to-price-landscaping-jobs/)).

## Step 5: Software and systems

Scheduling, routing, quotes, invoicing and payments in field service software (see [What is field service management software?](/blog/what-is-field-service-management-software/)); checklists for crews.

## Step 6: Get clients

Google Business Profile with before/after photos, a website with quote requests, door hangers and yard signs, Nextdoor, real estate agent and HOA partnerships, referrals (see [How to grow a landscaping business](/blog/how-to-grow-a-landscaping-business/)).

## Step 7: Hire and scale

Build crews with leads, train on quality and safety, add recurring maintenance routes and upsell projects.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a landscaping business?
Choose services, register the business, get licences and insurance, buy equipment, set pricing, use scheduling and invoicing software, and market locally through Google, signage and referrals.

### What do landscapers do?
They design, install and maintain outdoor spaces: mowing, planting, mulching, pruning, hardscaping, irrigation and seasonal clean-ups.

### How much does it cost to start a landscaping business?
From a few thousand dollars with basic equipment to much more with trucks, trailers and commercial equipment.

### Do I need a licence for landscaping?
Usually a business licence; some services (pesticides, irrigation, larger construction) require specific licences depending on location.

### Is a landscaping business profitable?
It can be, especially with recurring maintenance routes, efficient scheduling and upsold projects.

### How do I get my first landscaping clients?
Friends and neighbours, door hangers, Google Business Profile, Nextdoor and offering a first-service discount.
