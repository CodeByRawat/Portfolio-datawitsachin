## Steps

1. Choose services and niche
2. Package: Starter/Growth/Scale retainers with clear deliverables
3. Tech stack: analytics, ad platforms, SEO tools, reporting dashboards, CRM, project management
4. Legal: contracts, data processing agreements, access management
5. White-label partners for development, design or video
6. Website + case studies + booking
7. Client acquisition: referrals, LinkedIn, outreach, webinars, partnerships
8. Onboarding: audits, goals, access, 90-day plan
9. Monthly reporting tied to business outcomes (see [How to make an SEO report for a client](/blog/how-to-make-an-seo-report-for-a-client/))
10. Grow: hire specialists, productise, automate

## Starting a digital marketing business solo

Begin as a specialist freelancer, then add contractors and systems as demand grows (see [How to start a marketing agency](/blog/how-to-start-a-marketing-agency/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a digital marketing agency?
Choose core services and a niche, package retainers, set up tools and reporting, use white-label partners, build a website with case studies and win clients through referrals and outreach.

### What services should a digital marketing agency offer?
Start with one or two you excel at, such as SEO, PPC, social media, email or CRO, then expand.

### How much do digital marketing agencies charge?
Retainers vary widely by service, niche and scope, from hundreds to many thousands per month.

### Can I start a digital marketing agency with no experience?
Gain practical experience and results first through freelancing or projects; clients buy proof.

### What tools does a digital marketing agency need?
Analytics, ad platforms, SEO tools, reporting dashboards, CRM, project management and communication tools.

### How do digital marketing agencies retain clients?
Clear goals, regular reporting on business outcomes, proactive communication and continuous improvement.
