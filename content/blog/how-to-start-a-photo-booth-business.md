<p class="lede">To start a photo booth business, choose a <strong>booth type</strong> (iPad or DSLR open-air, 360 video booth, mirror booth or enclosed booth), buy the <strong>booth, camera, lighting, printer and software</strong>, add <strong>backdrops and props</strong>, get <strong>liability insurance</strong>, create <strong>hourly packages</strong>, and market to <strong>weddings, parties, schools and corporate events</strong>. You can start for about <strong>$2,000 to $15,000</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Choose your booth type

| Booth | Rough cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| iPad booth | $1,000 – $3,000 | Cheapest, digital sharing |
| DSLR open-air booth | $3,000 – $8,000 | Best photo quality, prints |
| 360 video booth | $1,500 – $8,000 | Very popular for parties and events |
| Mirror booth | $5,000 – $15,000 | Interactive, upscale events |
| Enclosed booth | $5,000 – $15,000+ | Classic style |

Many start with an iPad or 360 booth and add a DSLR or mirror booth later.

## Step 2: Equipment and software

- Booth shell or stand
- Camera (iPad, DSLR or 360 platform)
- Lighting (ring light or strobe)
- Dye-sublimation printer and paper (for prints)
- Booth software (templates, sharing, galleries)
- Backdrops and props
- Cases, cart and stanchions

## Step 3: Startup costs

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Booth and camera | $1,000 – $15,000 |
| Printer and media | $800 – $2,000 |
| Software (yearly) | $100 – $1,000 |
| Backdrops and props | $200 – $1,000 |
| Insurance | $300 – $1,500 per year |
| Website and marketing | $300 – $3,000 |

More detail: [How much does it cost to start a photo booth business?](/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-start-a-photo-booth-business/).

## Step 4: Register and insure

Business licence, sales tax where applicable, and general liability insurance (many venues require a certificate of insurance).

## Step 5: Pricing packages

Most booths rent by the hour (often 2–4 hour packages) with an attendant, unlimited sessions, custom templates and digital galleries. Add-ons: extra hours, guest book albums, premium backdrops, branded overlays for companies.

## Step 6: Get bookings

1. Wedding planners and venues
2. Corporate event planners and brand activations
3. Schools (proms, graduations)
4. Google Business Profile and a website with online booking
5. Instagram and TikTok videos from events
6. Bridal shows

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a photo booth business?
Choose a booth type, buy equipment and software, get insured, set packages and market to weddings and events.

### How much does it cost to start a photo booth business?
Often $2,000 to $15,000.

### What photo booth is best for a new business?
iPad and 360 booths are cheap to start; DSLR booths offer better prints.

### Do I need insurance for a photo booth?
Yes. Many venues require liability insurance.

### How much do photo booth rentals charge?
Usually hourly packages, often several hundred dollars for a few hours.
