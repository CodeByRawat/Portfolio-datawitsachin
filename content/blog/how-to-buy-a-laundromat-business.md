<p class="lede">To buy a laundromat, find stores through <strong>brokers, distributors and listings</strong>, value them on <strong>net cash flow</strong>, verify revenue with <strong>utility bills</strong> (since many laundromats are cash-heavy), confirm a <strong>long assignable lease</strong>, inspect <strong>equipment age</strong>, and finance with <strong>SBA or seller financing</strong>.</p>

## Where to find laundromats for sale

Business brokers, laundry equipment distributors, online business marketplaces, laundry owner associations and direct outreach to owners.

## How laundromats are valued

Value is usually based on **net cash flow multiplied by a factor** that reflects lease length, equipment condition, location and growth potential.

## Due diligence checklist

| Area | What to check |
|---|---|
| Revenue | Collections records and utility bills (water usage confirms washer activity) |
| Lease | Length, renewal options, rent increases, assignment |
| Equipment | Age, brand, maintenance records |
| Utilities | 12–24 months of bills |
| Competition | Nearby stores and new openings |
| Demographics | Renter density |
| Repairs | Pending issues, plumbing, venting |

## Red flags

Short lease, old equipment, revenue claims not supported by utility usage, declining neighbourhood, or a new competitor opening nearby.

## Financing

SBA loans, bank loans, seller financing and equipment financing for upgrades.

## After the purchase

Transition customers smoothly, keep attendants, fix cleanliness issues, add card payments and wash-and-fold, and market the new ownership. Next: [How to run a laundromat business](/blog/how-to-run-a-laundromat-business/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I buy a laundromat business?
Find listings, value the cash flow, verify with utility bills, check the lease and equipment, and finance the deal.

### How are laundromats valued?
Mostly on net cash flow, adjusted for lease, equipment and location.

### How do I verify laundromat revenue?
Compare claimed revenue to water usage and collection records.

### Can I get an SBA loan for a laundromat?
Yes, SBA loans are commonly used.

### What lease length should a laundromat have?
Long enough to recover your investment, usually with renewal options.

### Should I buy a laundromat with old equipment?
Only at a price that reflects upcoming replacement costs.
