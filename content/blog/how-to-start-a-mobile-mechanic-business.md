<p class="lede">To start a mobile mechanic business, get solid <strong>repair experience (ASE certification helps)</strong>, pick services you can do <strong>at a customer's home or workplace</strong>, register the business, get <strong>garage liability insurance</strong>, equip a <strong>van or truck with tools</strong>, and get customers through <strong>Google, reviews and online booking</strong>. Startup costs are often <strong>$10,000 to $40,000</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Get experience

Most mobile mechanics work in a shop first. ASE certifications build trust with customers and fleet clients.

## Step 2: Choose your services

| Good for mobile | Usually needs a shop |
|---|---|
| Brakes, pads and rotors | Transmission rebuilds |
| Batteries, starters, alternators | Engine rebuilds |
| Oil changes and tune-ups | Major frame and body work |
| Diagnostics and check-engine lights | Jobs needing a lift |
| Belts, hoses, spark plugs | Alignments |
| Pre-purchase inspections | |
| Fleet maintenance | |

## Step 3: Licenses and insurance

- LLC and EIN
- Local business license
- Some states require auto repair facility registration, even for mobile work
- EPA 609 certification if you handle A/C refrigerant
- Garage liability and commercial auto insurance

Details: [Do you need a license to be a mobile mechanic?](/blog/do-you-need-a-license-to-be-a-mobile-mechanic/)

## Step 4: Van, tools and startup costs

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Used van or truck | $8,000 – $25,000 |
| Hand and power tools | $3,000 – $10,000 |
| Diagnostic scanner | $500 – $5,000 |
| Jacks, stands, creeper, lighting | $500 – $2,000 |
| Insurance and licenses | $2,000 – $5,000 per year |
| Website, booking software, signage | $500 – $3,000 |

## Step 5: Price your work

Charge an hourly labour rate (often a bit below local shops), use labour guides for job times, mark up parts, and add a trip or diagnostic fee.

## Step 6: Running from home

You can usually base a mobile business at home, but most cities don't allow repairs in a residential driveway. Do the work at the customer's location.

## Step 7: Get customers

1. Google Business Profile with reviews
2. A website with online booking and service prices
3. Fleet accounts (landscapers, plumbers, delivery companies)
4. Used car dealers and pre-purchase inspections
5. Office parks and apartment complexes
6. Referrals and repeat maintenance reminders

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a mobile mechanic business?
Get experience, register and insure the business, equip a van and market with Google and online booking.

### What do I need to start a mobile mechanic business?
Skills, a van or truck, tools and a scanner, licenses, insurance and a way to book customers.

### How much does it cost to start a mobile mechanic business?
Often $10,000 to $40,000, depending on your vehicle and tools.

### Can I run a mechanic business from home?
You can base a mobile business at home, but repairs are usually done at the customer's location.

### Is a mobile mechanic business profitable?
Yes. Overhead is much lower than a shop, so margins can be strong.
