<p class="lede">To create an online store: <strong>choose what you'll sell and how</strong> (your own products, dropshipping, print-on-demand, digital), <strong>pick an e-commerce platform</strong> such as Shopify, WooCommerce, Wix or Squarespace, <strong>add products</strong> with great photos and descriptions, <strong>set up payments, shipping and taxes</strong>, <strong>design for trust and conversion</strong>, add legal pages, <strong>test checkout</strong>, and launch with marketing to bring your first customers.</p>

## Step 1: Decide what to sell and your model

| Model | How it works | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Own products / inventory** | Buy or make stock, ship yourself or via 3PL | Control and margins | Upfront cost, storage |
| **Handmade** | Make products yourself | Unique, story-driven | Limited scale |
| **Dropshipping** | Supplier ships directly | Low upfront cost | Low margins, less control |
| **Print-on-demand** | Products printed per order | No inventory | Lower margins |
| **Digital products** | Downloads, courses, templates | No shipping, high margin | Piracy, needs audience |
| **Subscriptions** | Recurring deliveries | Predictable revenue | Retention work |

Validate demand first: talk to customers, check search volume and competitors, sell to a small audience.

## Step 2: Choose an e-commerce platform

| Platform | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Shopify** | Most stores, from first sale to large brands | Easy, huge app ecosystem, monthly fee and possible transaction fees |
| **WooCommerce (WordPress)** | Flexibility and content-heavy stores | Free plugin; hosting and maintenance needed |
| **Wix / Squarespace** | Small stores with simple catalogues | Easy, design-friendly |
| **BigCommerce** | Larger catalogues, B2B | Fewer transaction fees on some plans |
| **Headless/custom** | High-growth brands needing performance or unique UX | Developer required |

## Step 3: Domain and branding

Register a domain (see [How to buy a domain name](/blog/how-to-buy-a-domain-name/)), create a logo, colours and fonts, and write a short brand story.

## Step 4: Add products

- **High-quality photos:** multiple angles, in use, scale, zoom
- **Product videos** or short motion clips (see [How to make a promotional video](/blog/how-to-make-a-promotional-video/))
- **Benefit-led descriptions** with specs, materials, sizing
- **Variants** (size, colour), SKUs, inventory tracking
- **Pricing** that covers costs, fees, shipping and margin
- **Reviews** once you have them
- **SEO:** unique titles and descriptions per product

## Step 5: Payments

Use the platform's payment system (e.g., Shopify Payments) or gateways like Stripe, PayPal, Razorpay, plus wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay). Offer local payment methods for your market. Consider buy-now-pay-later where relevant.

## Step 6: Shipping and fulfilment

- Shipping zones and rates (flat, free over a threshold, calculated)
- Packaging and returns policy
- Carrier integrations and label printing
- 3PL (third-party logistics) as you grow

## Step 7: Taxes and legal

- Configure sales tax/VAT/GST settings for where you sell (consult an accountant)
- **Legal pages:** terms, privacy policy, refund/returns, shipping policy
- **Cookie consent** where required
- Business registration and licences as required locally

## Step 8: Design for trust and conversion

- Clear **navigation and search**
- **Fast loading** and mobile-first layout
- Prominent **Add to cart**, sticky on mobile
- **Trust signals:** reviews, secure checkout badges, clear returns, contact info
- **Simple checkout:** guest checkout, few fields, express payments
- **Abandoned cart emails/WhatsApp** (with consent; see [How to schedule a WhatsApp message](/blog/how-to-schedule-a-whatsapp-message/))

## Step 9: Test and launch

Place real test orders, check emails, taxes, shipping, mobile checkout and analytics (GA4, Meta Pixel/Conversions API, Search Console).

## Step 10: Get your first customers

- Friends, family and existing followers
- Social content and short videos
- Paid social and Google Shopping ads (see [What is the most effective type of advertising?](/blog/what-is-the-most-effective-type-of-advertising/))
- Marketplaces alongside your store (see [How to choose the right marketplace for your business](/blog/how-to-choose-the-right-marketplace-for-your-business/))
- Email list with a welcome discount
- SEO for product and category pages

## How much does it cost to start an online store?

| Item | Typical cost |
|---|---|
| Platform | ~$30–$100+/month (more for advanced plans) |
| Domain | ~$10–$20/year |
| Theme | Free to ~$400 one-time |
| Apps | $0–$200+/month |
| Payment fees | ~2–3% + fixed fee per transaction (varies) |
| Professional store build | ~$3,000–$25,000+ (see [How much does a website cost?](/blog/how-much-does-a-website-cost/)) |

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I create an online store?
Choose your products and business model, pick a platform like Shopify or WooCommerce, add products, set up payments, shipping and taxes, design for trust, add legal pages, test checkout and launch with marketing.

### What is the best platform to create an online store?
Shopify is the most popular all-round choice; WooCommerce suits WordPress users wanting flexibility; Wix and Squarespace suit small catalogues.

### How do I make a website to sell stuff?
Use an e-commerce platform or builder with a store feature, add your products and payment methods, and configure shipping, then promote it.

### How much does it cost to create an online store?
DIY stores can start around $30–$100 per month plus fees; professionally built stores typically cost a few thousand dollars or more.

### How do I start an e-commerce business with no money?
Start with print-on-demand, dropshipping or digital products, use free plans or trials, and market through social content, though some budget for a domain and platform is advisable.

### Do I need a business license to sell online?
It depends on your country and state; many places require business registration and tax registration. Check local regulations or consult an accountant.
