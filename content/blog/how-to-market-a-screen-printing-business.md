<p class="lede">To market a screen printing business, focus on <strong>groups that order shirts repeatedly</strong> (businesses, schools, teams, churches, events, brands), make it easy to <strong>get a quote online</strong>, show <strong>real work and mockups</strong> on social media, collect <strong>Google reviews</strong>, reach out <strong>directly</strong>, and build <strong>reorders and referrals</strong>.</p>

## 1. Know your best customers

| Customer | Why they're great |
|---|---|
| Local businesses | Uniforms and promo items, repeat orders |
| Schools and PTAs | Events, spirit wear |
| Sports teams and leagues | Every season |
| Churches and nonprofits | Events and fundraisers |
| Bands and brands | Merch runs |
| Event organisers | Races, festivals, reunions |

## 2. Website with online quotes

Show products, price examples, turnaround times and a simple quote form or design tool. Fast quotes win orders.

## 3. Local SEO

Set up a Google Business Profile, add photos of finished orders and ask every happy customer for a review.

## 4. Social media

Post videos of printing, finished orders, mockups and customer photos. Instagram, TikTok and Facebook work well for print shops.

## 5. Direct outreach

Email or visit local businesses, coaches, school offices and event organisers with samples and a simple price sheet.

## 6. Referrals and reorders

Offer a referral discount, keep screens and artwork on file, and remind customers before their next event or season.

## 7. Add promotional products

Hats, bags, mugs and embroidery increase order value. Related: [How to start a small embroidery business](/blog/how-to-start-a-small-embroidery-business/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I market my screen printing business?
Target repeat buyers, offer online quotes, post work on social media, collect reviews and reach out directly.

### How do screen printers get customers?
Local businesses, schools, teams, events, Google and referrals.

### Is social media good for screen printing?
Yes. Videos of printing and finished orders attract customers.

### How do I get repeat screen printing orders?
Keep artwork on file and remind customers before events and seasons.

### Should I sell promotional products too?
Yes. It increases order value.
