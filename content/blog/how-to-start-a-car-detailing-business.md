<p class="lede">To start a car detailing business: choose a model (<strong>mobile, home-based or shop</strong>), learn <strong>detailing techniques</strong>, register the business and get <strong>garage keepers/liability insurance</strong>, buy <strong>equipment and quality products</strong>, create <strong>packages and pricing</strong>, add <strong>high-value services</strong> like paint correction and ceramic coating, use <strong>online booking</strong>, and market with <strong>before/after photos, Google reviews and social media</strong>.</p>

## What you need

| Category | Items |
|---|---|
| Washing | Pressure washer, foam cannon, buckets, mitts, drying towels |
| Interior | Vacuum, extractor, steamer, brushes, cleaners |
| Paint | Polisher, pads, compounds, clay, sealants/coatings |
| Business | Registration, insurance, booking software, payments |
| Mobile extras | Van/trailer, water tank, generator |

## Startup costs

Home or mobile detailing can start with modest equipment; a shop requires rent, build-out and more equipment. Mobile setups add water tanks and generators.

## Packages and pricing

Basic wash, interior detail, full detail, paint correction, ceramic coating; price by vehicle size and condition; offer maintenance plans for repeat clients.

## Running from home

Check zoning, water runoff and HOA rules before detailing at home.

## Getting clients

Before/after content on Instagram and TikTok, Google Business Profile and reviews, dealership and fleet partnerships, and a website with online booking and deposits.

## How hard is it?

Entry costs are low, but quality, consistency and marketing determine success; learning paint correction and coatings raises income potential.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a car detailing business?
Choose a model, learn techniques, register and insure, buy equipment, set packages and pricing, add booking and market locally.

### What do you need to start a car detailing business?
Washing, interior and paint correction equipment, products, insurance, registration and a booking system.

### Can you run a car detailing business from home?
Often yes, subject to zoning, HOA and water runoff rules.

### How much does it cost to start a car detailing business?
Mobile or home setups can start small; shops cost more due to rent and build-out.

### Is car detailing a good business?
It can be, with low startup costs and high-margin services like ceramic coating.

### How do detailers get clients?
Before/after social posts, Google reviews, partnerships and online booking.
