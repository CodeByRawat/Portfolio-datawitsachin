<p class="lede">A courier business <strong>picks up and delivers packages, documents and specialty items</strong>, usually <strong>fast, direct and tracked</strong>, for businesses and individuals. Unlike regular mail, a courier often takes the item straight from sender to receiver, often the same day. Courier businesses make money from <strong>per-delivery fees, mileage, rush charges and recurring route contracts</strong>.</p>

## How a courier service works

1. A customer books a pickup (phone, website or app)
2. A driver collects the item and scans or photographs it
3. The item travels directly or through a hub
4. The driver delivers it and captures proof of delivery (signature or photo)
5. The customer is invoiced per delivery or monthly

## Types of courier businesses

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Same-day / on-demand | Rush documents across town |
| Scheduled routes | Daily lab specimen pickups |
| Medical courier | Specimens, prescriptions, equipment |
| Legal courier | Court filings and contracts |
| Last-mile delivery | E-commerce parcels to homes |
| International express | Large global carriers |

## Courier vs delivery vs freight

| | Courier | General delivery | Freight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Size | Small items, documents | Parcels | Pallets, large loads |
| Speed | Fast, often same-day | Days | Scheduled |
| Handling | Direct, tracked | Sorted in networks | Trucking |

## How courier businesses make money

- Per-delivery base fees
- Mileage and zone pricing
- Rush, after-hours and weekend surcharges
- Wait time and multi-stop fees
- Monthly route contracts

## Who uses couriers?

Labs and hospitals, pharmacies, law firms, banks, print shops, auto parts stores, restaurants, florists, retailers and e-commerce sellers.

## What you need to run one

A reliable vehicle, commercial insurance, dispatch and tracking software, and local business clients.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a courier business?
A company that picks up and delivers packages and documents quickly and directly, usually with tracking.

### How do courier businesses make money?
From delivery fees, mileage, rush charges and recurring route contracts.

### What is the difference between a courier and a delivery service?
Couriers are usually faster and more direct, often same-day with door-to-door tracking.

### Is a courier a good business?
It can be, with low startup costs and steady demand from local businesses.

### What do courier drivers deliver?
Documents, packages, medical specimens, prescriptions, parts and other time-sensitive items.
