<p class="lede">To start a gym: <strong>choose a model</strong> (boutique studio, 24/7 access gym, strength/functional gym, personal training studio or franchise), <strong>validate local demand</strong>, write a <strong>business plan with startup costs and membership pricing</strong>, secure <strong>funding</strong>, find a <strong>location</strong> with the right size, parking and zoning, buy or lease <strong>equipment</strong>, handle <strong>licences, insurance and waivers</strong>, hire <strong>coaches</strong>, set up <strong>gym management software, a website and online booking</strong>, and run a <strong>pre-sale campaign</strong> so you open with paying members.</p>

## Step 1: Choose your gym model

| Model | Size | Revenue model | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Boutique studio** (HIIT, cycling, yoga, Pilates) | Small | Class packs, memberships | Strong community, higher price per member |
| **Personal training studio** | Very small | Session packages | Lowest space needs |
| **Functional/strength gym** | Medium | Memberships + coaching | Equipment-heavy |
| **24/7 access gym** | Medium–large | Low-cost memberships at volume | Needs access control and many members |
| **Full-service health club** | Large | Memberships, classes, amenities | High capital |
| **Franchise** | Varies | Brand + systems | Fees and royalties |
| **Online/hybrid** | Minimal | Programmes and coaching | Digital marketing driven |

## Step 2: Research your market

- Competitors within 10–15 minutes: prices, offers, reviews
- Target audience: age, income, goals (weight loss, strength, seniors, women-only)
- Gaps: niche classes, hours, coaching quality, community

## Step 3: Business plan and numbers

- Startup costs (see [How much does it cost to open a gym?](/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-open-a-gym/))
- Membership pricing and target member count
- Monthly costs: rent, staff, utilities, software, marketing
- Break-even point and cash runway
- Profitability expectations (see [Are gyms profitable?](/blog/are-gyms-profitable/))

## Step 4: Funding

Savings, small business loans, equipment financing/leasing, investors, franchise financing, and **pre-sold memberships**.

## Step 5: Location and lease

Visibility, parking, ceiling height, flooring strength, noise considerations, showers/changing rooms, zoning approval and lease terms.

## Step 6: Equipment

Prioritise what your model needs; consider quality used or leased equipment; plan layout and safety space.

## Step 7: Legal, licences and insurance

Business registration, local permits, liability and property insurance, member waivers and contracts, music licensing for public performance where required, and staff certifications.

## Step 8: Staff

Certified trainers and coaches, front desk or automated access, cleaning. Strong coaches drive retention.

## Step 9: Technology

- **Gym management software** (memberships, billing, class booking, check-in)
- **Website** with schedules, pricing, trial booking (see [How to make a booking website](/blog/how-to-make-a-booking-website/))
- **Access control** for 24/7 gyms
- **WhatsApp/email automation** for reminders and follow-ups

## Step 10: Pre-sale and launch marketing

- Founding member offers before opening
- Google Business Profile (see [How to create a Google Business page](/blog/how-to-create-a-google-business-page/))
- Instagram/TikTok progress videos of the build-out
- Local partnerships (physios, nutritionists, corporate wellness)
- Free trial classes and opening events
(Growth: [How to grow a gym](/blog/how-to-grow-a-gym/).)

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a gym business?
Choose a gym model, research your market, write a business plan, secure funding, find a location, buy equipment, get licences and insurance, hire coaches, set up software and online booking, and pre-sell memberships.

### How do I open a gym with little money?
Start small: a personal training or boutique studio, used or leased equipment, shared spaces, and pre-sold memberships to fund the launch.

### Do I need a certification to open a gym?
Owners don't always need one, but trainers should be certified, and you need proper business licences and insurance.

### What is the most profitable type of gym?
Boutique studios and personal training models often have higher revenue per member; profitability depends on rent, pricing and retention.

### How long does it take to open a gym?
Often three to twelve months depending on location, build-out, funding and permits.

### What is a commercial gym?
A gym open to the public on a membership basis, as opposed to a home, hotel or corporate gym.
