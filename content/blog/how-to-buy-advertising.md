<p class="lede">To buy advertising: <strong>define your goal, audience and budget</strong>, choose <strong>channels where your customers pay attention</strong>, then buy either <strong>self-serve</strong> (Google Ads, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, Amazon, programmatic platforms) or <strong>directly from media sellers</strong> (TV, radio, newspapers, podcasts, billboards), or through an <strong>agency</strong>. Understand the <strong>pricing model</strong> (CPM, CPC, CPA or flat rate), prepare <strong>creative in the right specs</strong>, set up <strong>tracking</strong>, and start with a test before scaling.</p>

## Step 1: Set the goal and budget

- **Goal:** awareness, leads, sales, app installs, event attendance
- **Target audience:** who, where, when
- **Budget:** a test budget you can afford to learn with
- **Success metric:** CPA, ROAS, reach, store visits (see [What is LTV?](/blog/what-is-ltv/) for setting allowable acquisition cost)

## Step 2: Choose channels

| Channel | How to buy | Typical pricing |
|---|---|---|
| **Google Search/YouTube** | Self-serve Google Ads | CPC, CPM, CPV |
| **Meta (Facebook/Instagram)** | Self-serve Ads Manager | CPM-based auction |
| **TikTok, LinkedIn, X, Pinterest, Snapchat** | Self-serve ad managers | CPM/CPC auctions |
| **Amazon and retail media** | Self-serve or managed | CPC/CPM |
| **Programmatic display/video/CTV** | DSPs (often via agencies) | CPM |
| **Podcasts** | Networks, marketplaces or direct | CPM or flat rate |
| **Newsletters** | Direct from publishers | Flat rate or CPM |
| **TV and streaming TV** | Media sales teams, agencies, self-serve CTV platforms | CPM/spot rates |
| **Radio and streaming audio** | Station sales teams, audio platforms | Spot rates/CPM |
| **Print** | Publisher ad sales | Flat rate by size/placement |
| **Out-of-home (billboards, transit)** | Media owners, programmatic DOOH | Flat rate per period/CPM |

(Which channel works best: [What is the most effective type of advertising?](/blog/what-is-the-most-effective-type-of-advertising/))

## Step 3: Understand pricing models

| Model | You pay for |
|---|---|
| **CPM** | 1,000 impressions (see [What is CPM?](/blog/what-is-cpm/)) |
| **CPC** | Each click |
| **CPV** | Each video view |
| **CPA/CPL** | Each conversion or lead (affiliate, some platforms) |
| **Flat rate** | A placement for a set period |
| **Sponsorship** | Package of placements/mentions |

## Step 4: Self-serve vs direct vs agency

| | Self-serve | Direct with media sellers | Agency |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Control** | Full | Moderate | Delegated |
| **Minimum spend** | Low | Often higher | Fees or minimums |
| **Expertise needed** | Medium | Medium | Low (for you) |
| **Best for** | Digital campaigns, testing | Local media, premium placements | Larger budgets, multi-channel |

## Step 5: Negotiating direct media buys

- Ask for **rate cards**, audience data and case studies
- Negotiate **added value:** bonus spots, digital add-ons, better placements
- Ask about **remnant or last-minute inventory** discounts
- Commit to frequency for better rates
- Get **proof of performance** (affidavits, screenshots, impression reports)

## Step 6: Prepare creative

Every channel has specs (sizes, lengths, file types). Strong creative is the biggest performance lever: hooks, clear messages, brand cues and calls to action (see [How to make an advertisement](/blog/how-to-make-an-advertisement/) and [What is creative strategy?](/blog/what-is-creative-strategy/)).

## Step 7: Track and measure

- **UTM links and pixels** for digital
- **Unique URLs, promo codes, QR codes and call tracking numbers** for offline
- **Incrementality tests** for larger budgets
- Compare **cost per customer** across channels

## Mistakes to avoid

- Buying channels because a salesperson called
- Spreading a small budget across too many channels
- No tracking or unique codes
- Weak creative adapted poorly from another channel
- Judging too quickly (or too slowly)
- Ignoring frequency and creative fatigue

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I buy advertising for my business?
Set goals and budget, choose channels where your audience is, buy via self-serve platforms, direct from media sellers or through an agency, prepare creative to spec, track results and scale what works.

### Where can I buy ads?
On Google, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, Amazon and other self-serve platforms; directly from TV, radio, podcast, newsletter, print and outdoor media owners; or through agencies and programmatic platforms.

### How much does advertising cost?
It varies by channel, audience and market; digital channels allow small budgets, while TV, outdoor and print often require larger commitments.

### What is the cheapest way to advertise?
Self-serve digital ads with tight targeting, plus free channels like Google Business Profile and social media content, are usually most affordable for small businesses.

### Should I use an advertising agency?
Consider an agency when budgets are large, campaigns span many channels or you lack time and expertise; small budgets often work well self-serve.

### How do I know if my advertising is working?
Track conversions with pixels, UTMs, promo codes and call tracking, and compare cost per customer and return on ad spend across channels.
