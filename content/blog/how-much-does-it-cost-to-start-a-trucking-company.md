<p class="lede">Starting a trucking company commonly costs from <strong>roughly $10,000–$40,000 to get running if you lease or finance equipment</strong>, to <strong>$100,000–$200,000+ if you buy a truck and trailer outright</strong>. Beyond equipment, budget for <strong>insurance down payments</strong> (often a major early cost), <strong>registrations and authority filings</strong>, <strong>ELD and software</strong>, <strong>fuel, maintenance and repair reserves</strong>, and <strong>working capital</strong> because brokers and shippers often pay on 30-day or longer terms.</p>

## Startup cost breakdown (rough)

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| Truck | Used, new, financed or leased; biggest variable |
| Trailer | Depends on type (van, reefer, flatbed) |
| Insurance | Down payment plus monthly premiums; high for new authorities |
| Registrations/authority | USDOT/MC filing, BOC-3, UCR, IRP plates, IFTA (US) |
| ELD and tech | ELD device/subscription, load boards, accounting |
| Permits | State permits, heavy vehicle use tax where applicable |
| Business setup | Entity, legal, accounting |
| Working capital | Fuel, tolls, repairs, payroll until invoices are paid |

## Operating costs (cost per mile)

Fuel, truck payment, insurance, maintenance and tyres, driver pay, permits and fees, tolls, factoring fees and overhead. Know your **cost per mile** to accept only profitable loads.

## Ways to reduce startup costs

- Lease onto an established carrier first
- Buy a well-inspected used truck
- Start with hotshot or box truck niches
- Use factoring to manage cash flow
- Shop insurance carefully

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does it cost to start a trucking company?
From around $10,000–$40,000 with leased or financed equipment to $100,000–$200,000+ when buying equipment outright, plus working capital.

### What is the biggest startup cost in trucking?
The truck and trailer, followed by insurance down payments.

### How much is trucking insurance for a new company?
New authorities often pay high premiums; costs vary by coverage, location, freight and driving history.

### What is cost per mile in trucking?
Total operating costs divided by miles driven, used to price loads and measure profitability.

### Can I start a trucking business with no money?
It's very difficult; leasing onto a carrier or driving for one first is a common lower-cost path.

### How much cash should a new trucking company keep?
Enough to cover several months of fuel, insurance, payments and repairs while waiting for invoices to be paid.
