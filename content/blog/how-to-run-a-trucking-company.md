<p class="lede">The trucking business works by moving freight for <strong>shippers</strong>, usually via <strong>freight brokers</strong> or direct contracts, at a rate per mile or per load. Running a trucking company well means <strong>booking profitable loads</strong>, <strong>dispatching efficiently</strong>, keeping <strong>drivers safe and compliant</strong>, maintaining <strong>equipment</strong>, <strong>invoicing quickly</strong> (or factoring), controlling <strong>fuel and maintenance costs</strong>, and tracking <strong>cost per mile and profit per load</strong>.</p>

## How the trucking business works

1. Shipper needs freight moved
2. Broker or shipper posts the load
3. Carrier negotiates rate and books it
4. Dispatcher plans the route and driver
5. Driver picks up, delivers, gets proof of delivery
6. Carrier invoices (or factors) and gets paid

## Daily operations

| Area | Key tasks |
|---|---|
| Dispatch | Load booking, routing, communication with brokers and drivers |
| Rates | Know cost per mile; negotiate; avoid deadhead |
| Drivers | Hiring, pay, retention, safety |
| Compliance | Hours of service, ELD logs, inspections, drug testing |
| Maintenance | Preventive schedules, repairs, tyres |
| Billing | Rate confirmations, BOL/POD, invoices, factoring |
| Fuel | Fuel cards, route optimisation |

## Technology and automation

TMS (transportation management system), load boards, ELD/telematics, fuel cards, accounting, and automation for invoicing, document capture (BOL/POD scanning), driver updates and broker communication (see [How to automate business processes](/blog/how-to-automate-business-processes/) and [What is supply chain automation?](/blog/what-is-supply-chain-automation/)).

## Growth

Build direct shipper relationships, specialise in lanes, hire reliable drivers, add trucks gradually, and maintain strong safety scores.

## Frequently asked questions

### How does the trucking business work?
Carriers move freight for shippers, often through brokers, earning per mile or per load, then invoice and get paid.

### How do you run a trucking company successfully?
Book profitable loads, dispatch efficiently, stay compliant, maintain equipment, invoice fast and track cost per mile.

### What is a TMS in trucking?
A transportation management system for managing loads, dispatch, drivers, documents and billing.

### What is factoring in trucking?
Selling invoices to a factoring company for immediate cash, minus a fee.

### How do trucking companies find drivers?
Job boards, referrals, competitive pay and home time, and a strong safety culture.

### How can trucking companies reduce costs?
Optimise routes, reduce deadhead, manage fuel, maintain equipment preventively and automate admin.
