<p class="lede">To become a dog groomer: learn <strong>animal handling, breed-specific cuts, bathing, drying, clipping, scissoring and nail care</strong> through an <strong>apprenticeship</strong>, a <strong>grooming school</strong> or <strong>online courses with hands-on practice</strong>, get comfortable with <strong>safety and dog behaviour</strong>, consider a <strong>certification</strong> to show competence, gain experience in a salon, mobile service or vet clinic, and build a <strong>portfolio</strong> before working independently or opening your own grooming business.</p>

## What dog groomers do

Bathing, drying, brushing and de-matting, haircuts and breed trims, nail trimming, ear cleaning, sanitary trims, skin and coat checks, and calm handling of anxious dogs. They also communicate with owners about coat care and any concerns spotted.

## Ways to learn dog grooming

| Path | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| **Apprenticeship/bather role** | Paid, real-world experience | Slower to learn advanced cuts |
| **Grooming school** | Structured, hands-on curriculum | Tuition cost |
| **Online course + practice** | Flexible, lower cost | Needs hands-on mentorship |
| **Vet clinic or kennel jobs** | Animal handling experience | Limited grooming |

## How much is dog grooming school?

Costs vary widely by programme length and location, from a few hundred dollars for online courses to several thousand for full in-person programmes. Check what's included (tools, practice dogs, certification).

## Skills you need

Patience, animal handling, attention to detail, physical stamina, knowledge of breeds and coat types, safety and hygiene, and customer service.

## Certification and licensing

Certification is usually voluntary (from grooming associations/schools) but builds credibility. Licensing of individual groomers is rare in many places, though grooming businesses may need business licences or kennel/animal permits (see [How to start a dog grooming business](/blog/how-to-start-a-dog-grooming-business/)).

## Tools

Clippers and blades, scissors, brushes and combs, dryers, grooming table, tub, nail tools, safety restraints.

## Get experience and grow

Start as a bather, build a before/after portfolio, specialise (doodles, show grooming, cats), and consider mobile grooming or your own salon. Income: [How much do dog groomers make?](/blog/how-much-do-dog-groomers-make/)

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I become a dog groomer?
Learn through an apprenticeship, grooming school or online course with hands-on practice, master handling and breed cuts, consider certification and build experience in a salon.

### How long does it take to become a dog groomer?
Often a few months to a year or more depending on the training path and practice time.

### Do you need a licence to groom dogs?
Individual licensing is uncommon in many areas, but grooming businesses may need business licences or animal permits; check locally.

### How much does dog grooming school cost?
From a few hundred dollars for online programmes to several thousand for comprehensive in-person courses.

### Is dog grooming a good career?
It can be rewarding for animal lovers, with income potential growing through experience, loyal clients and owning a business.

### How do you become a pet groomer for cats too?
Learn cat-specific handling and grooming, often through specialised courses.
