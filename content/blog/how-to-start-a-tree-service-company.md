<p class="lede">To start a tree service company, get <strong>hands-on experience and safety training</strong>, check <strong>state and local licence rules</strong>, buy strong <strong>liability and workers' comp insurance</strong>, invest in <strong>chainsaws, a chipper, rigging gear and a truck</strong>, price jobs by <strong>size, risk and access</strong>, and market through <strong>Google, storm response and referrals</strong>. Safety is the core of the business.</p>

## What you need to start a tree cutting business

1. **Experience**: work for an established tree company first.
2. **Training**: chainsaw safety, climbing, rigging, first aid; arborist certification is a plus.
3. **Business registration** and local licences (see [Do tree services need to be licensed?](/blog/do-tree-services-need-to-be-licensed/)).
4. **Insurance**: general liability, workers' comp, commercial auto, equipment coverage.
5. **Equipment** and a truck.
6. **Pricing and estimates**.
7. **Marketing** and a website.

## Equipment

| Item | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Chainsaws (top-handle and rear-handle) | Cutting |
| Climbing gear and ropes | Access and rigging |
| PPE | Helmets, chaps, eye/ear protection |
| Wood chipper | Brush disposal |
| Dump truck or pickup + trailer | Hauling |
| Stump grinder | Add-on service |
| Bucket truck or crane (later) | Large removals |

Many startups rent large equipment until volume justifies buying.

## Services to offer

- Tree trimming and pruning
- Tree removal
- Stump grinding
- Storm cleanup and emergency removal
- Land clearing
- Tree health assessments (with certified arborist)

## Pricing jobs

Price on tree height and diameter, proximity to houses and power lines, access, rigging difficulty, clean-up and haul-away, and stump grinding. Charge more for emergency work.

## Safety first

Tree work is dangerous. Use trained crews, job briefings, proper PPE, rigging plans and never work near power lines without utility-qualified workers.

## Getting customers

Google Business Profile, reviews, before/after photos, a website with estimate requests, partnerships with landscapers and property managers, and storm-season advertising. If you also do yard work, see [How to start a landscaping business](/blog/how-to-start-a-landscaping-business/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a tree service company?
Gain experience, get trained, register and license the business, insure it, buy equipment and market locally.

### What do you need to start a tree cutting business?
Training, licences, insurance, chainsaws, safety gear, a chipper and a truck.

### How do I start a tree trimming company?
Start with trimming and pruning services, basic equipment and insurance, then add removals as skills and equipment grow.

### How much does it cost to start a tree service?
Equipment and insurance are the main costs; renting larger machines keeps startup costs lower.

### Is a tree service business profitable?
It can be very profitable because jobs are high-ticket, but insurance and equipment costs are significant.

### Do I need to be a certified arborist?
Not always, but certification helps win clients and is required for some work.
