<p class="lede">A laundromat can be a <strong>good investment</strong> because demand is <strong>steady and recession-resistant</strong>, there's <strong>no inventory</strong>, and stores can run <strong>semi-absentee</strong>. But it requires <strong>large upfront capital</strong>, depends on <strong>location and lease terms</strong>, and faces <strong>utility costs and equipment wear</strong>. Profitability comes down to <strong>location, pricing, equipment and added services</strong>.</p>

## Pros

| Pro | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Steady demand | People always need clean clothes |
| Recession-resistant | Laundry isn't optional |
| No inventory or receivables | Customers pay upfront |
| Semi-absentee potential | With cameras and card systems |
| Add-on services | Wash-and-fold, delivery, commercial |
| Long equipment life | Good machines last many years |

## Cons

- High upfront cost
- Utilities can swing profit
- Equipment repairs and replacement
- Lease risk (short leases kill value)
- Vandalism and cleanliness issues
- Competition from new stores or apartments adding in-unit laundry

## Is owning a laundromat profitable?

It is when the store has the right **location**, **fair rent**, **efficient equipment**, **competitive pricing**, and **extra revenue streams**. See [How much can a laundromat make?](/blog/how-much-can-a-laundromat-make/).

## What to check before investing

1. Demographics (renters, households)
2. Competition within the trade area
3. Lease length and renewal options
4. Real utility bills (to confirm revenue)
5. Equipment age and condition
6. Room for wash-and-fold and delivery

## Who should buy a laundromat?

Investors with capital who want **steady cash flow**, can handle **maintenance and oversight**, and want a business that can be **systemised**.

Getting started: [How to start a laundromat business](/blog/how-to-start-a-laundromat-business/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a laundromat a good business?
Yes, for owners with capital, a strong location and a long lease.

### Is a laundromat a good investment?
It can offer steady cash flow and semi-passive income, but upfront costs are high.

### Is owning a laundromat profitable?
Yes, when revenue exceeds rent, utilities and repairs with room for profit.

### Is a laundromat passive income?
Semi-passive; owners still handle maintenance, staff and customer issues.

### What are the risks of owning a laundromat?
Lease risk, utility spikes, equipment failure and competition.

### Are laundromats recession-proof?
They're recession-resistant because laundry is a basic need.
