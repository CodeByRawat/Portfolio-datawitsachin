<p class="lede">Dumpster rental means a company <strong>drops off a large roll-off container</strong> at your property, you <strong>fill it over a set rental period</strong> (often 7 days), and the company <strong>picks it up and dumps it</strong> at a landfill or transfer station. You pay a <strong>flat price</strong> that includes a weight limit, with extra fees for overweight loads, extra days or banned items.</p>

## How to rent a dumpster

1. **Pick a size**: 10, 15, 20, 30 or 40 cubic yards
2. **Get a quote**: ask what's included (days, weight, delivery)
3. **Book**: online or by phone, choose a delivery date
4. **Prepare the spot**: driveway or yard, clear of cars and wires
5. **Get a permit if needed**: for placement on the street
6. **Fill it**: follow the rules on banned items and fill lines
7. **Schedule pickup**: or the company picks up at the end date

## Which size do you need?

| Size | Good for |
|---|---|
| 10 yard | Garage cleanout, small bathroom remodel |
| 20 yard | Roof replacement, kitchen remodel, house cleanout |
| 30 yard | Major renovation, new construction |
| 40 yard | Large demolition, commercial projects |

## What's usually not allowed

Hazardous waste, paint, chemicals, batteries, tires, and often mattresses and appliances (or they cost extra). Ask your company for its list.

## What does it cost?

Prices vary widely by area, often a few hundred dollars for a 10–20 yard dumpster for a week. Extra weight and extra days add to the bill.

## Tips

- Don't overfill above the top edge
- Spread heavy items evenly
- Protect your driveway with boards
- Ask about overweight fees before you start

## Frequently asked questions

### How does dumpster rental work?
The company delivers a container, you fill it during the rental period, and they haul it away.

### How do you rent a dumpster?
Choose a size, get a quote, book a delivery date and prepare a place for it.

### What is dumpster rental?
Short-term rental of a large waste container for cleanouts and projects.

### How long can you keep a rented dumpster?
Often 7 to 14 days, with daily fees after that.

### Do I need a permit for a dumpster?
Usually only if it's placed on a public street.
