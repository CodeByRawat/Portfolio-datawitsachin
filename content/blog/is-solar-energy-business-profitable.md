<p class="lede">Yes, the solar business can be profitable, but margins are <strong>tighter than many people expect</strong>. Residential installers often earn around <strong>10% to 20% gross profit after equipment and labour</strong>, and net profit can be <strong>low single digits to around 10%</strong> because <strong>customer acquisition costs are high</strong>. Profit also depends on <strong>incentives, net metering rules and interest rates</strong>.</p>

These are rough ranges and change with policy and market conditions.

## Where solar companies make money

| Source | Notes |
|---|---|
| System sales and installation | Main revenue, priced per watt |
| Battery storage | Growing, good margins |
| Commercial projects | Larger, can be more efficient |
| Service and maintenance | Recurring revenue |
| Dealer commissions | Selling for installers |

## Biggest costs

1. **Customer acquisition** (leads, sales commissions), often the largest controllable cost
2. Panels, inverters, batteries and racking
3. Labour, electricians and roofers
4. Permits, inspections and interconnection
5. Financing fees
6. Warranty and service

## Risks

- Changes to tax credits and incentives
- Net metering rule changes
- Higher interest rates (most customers finance)
- Supply chain and equipment pricing
- Warranty claims and roof leaks

## How to be more profitable

- Lower acquisition costs with referrals, reviews and strong local SEO
- Add batteries, EV chargers and roofing
- Build service and maintenance revenue
- Focus on markets with good incentives and high power prices
- Control install time and permit delays

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the solar energy business profitable?
Yes, but margins are tight; acquisition costs and policy changes matter a lot.

### How much do solar companies make per installation?
It varies widely; profit often depends on sales costs and system size.

### What's the biggest cost for solar companies?
Equipment and customer acquisition.

### Is solar sales profitable?
Successful solar salespeople and dealers can earn well on commissions.

### Is solar a good business for the future?
Long-term demand is strong, but policy and interest rates create ups and downs.
