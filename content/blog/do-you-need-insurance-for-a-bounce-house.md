<p class="lede">If you <strong>rent out bounce houses</strong> as a business, you need <strong>commercial liability insurance</strong>, and many states, venues and customers require it. If you're a <strong>homeowner renting one</strong> for a party, your homeowners policy <strong>may or may not cover</strong> injuries; many insurers exclude or limit inflatables. The safest choice is to rent from a company that is <strong>insured and can name you as additional insured</strong>.</p>

This is general information, not insurance advice. Check with your insurer.

## For homeowners renting a bounce house

| Question | What to know |
|---|---|
| Does homeowners insurance cover it? | Sometimes; some policies exclude inflatables or trampolines |
| How to protect yourself | Rent from an insured company, ask for a certificate of insurance |
| Additional insured | Ask the rental company to list you on their policy |
| Event insurance | One-day event liability policies are available |

## For bounce house rental businesses

You'll typically need:

- **Commercial general liability** covering inflatables
- **Commercial auto** for delivery vehicles
- **Workers' comp** if you have employees
- **Equipment (inland marine)** coverage for your inflatables
- Sometimes **umbrella** coverage for large events

## Certificates and venues

Schools, parks, churches and venues often require a certificate of insurance naming them as additional insured.

## State rules

Some states require inflatable rental companies to carry minimum insurance, register, and have units inspected regularly.

## Reduce risk

- Commercial-grade units and proper anchoring
- Trained setup crews
- Written safety rules and waivers
- No operation in high winds
- Regular inspections and repairs

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need insurance to rent out bouncy castles?
Yes. Commercial liability insurance is essential and often required.

### Does homeowners insurance cover renting a bounce house?
Sometimes; many policies exclude or limit inflatables, so check your policy.

### What insurance does a bounce house business need?
General liability, commercial auto, equipment coverage and workers' comp if hiring.

### What is additional insured?
Adding a customer or venue to your policy for an event.

### Do bounce houses need inspections?
Some states require regular inspections.
