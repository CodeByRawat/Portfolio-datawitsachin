<p class="lede">To start a coaching business: choose a <strong>specific niche and outcome</strong> (e.g., career transitions for engineers, fitness for busy parents, leadership for new managers), build <strong>credibility</strong> (experience, results, optional certification), design a <strong>clear coaching offer</strong> (sessions + support + resources over a set period), set <strong>pricing</strong>, put <strong>contracts and payments</strong> in place, set up <strong>booking, video and a simple website</strong>, then win first clients through <strong>your network, content, referrals and discovery calls</strong>. Many coaches start online from home.</p>

## Step 1: Choose your niche

Combine **who** you help + **what result** + **how**. Common niches: life, career, business/executive, leadership, health and fitness, relationships, mindset, money, sales, ADHD/productivity. (Leadership coaching: [What is leadership coaching?](/blog/what-is-leadership-coaching/))

## Step 2: Credibility and certification

Coaching is largely unregulated in many places. Credibility comes from experience, results, testimonials and, optionally, certification (e.g., ICF-accredited programmes) for coaching skills and ethics. Don't present yourself as a therapist or financial adviser unless licensed.

## Step 3: Design your offer

- **Outcome-based programme** (e.g., 12 weeks to a new job)
- Weekly or biweekly sessions + messaging support + resources
- **Group coaching** for scale
- **Courses/workshops** as entry offers

## Step 4: Pricing

Price by value and outcome, not per hour alone. Packages (3–6 months) work better than single sessions (see [How much do coaches charge?](/blog/how-much-do-coaches-charge/)).

## Step 5: Legal and admin

Business registration, coaching agreement (scope, confidentiality, cancellation, refunds, disclaimers), payments, bookkeeping, insurance (professional liability), data privacy.

## Step 6: Tools

Scheduling (Calendly, Cal.com), video (Zoom, Google Meet), payments (Stripe, PayPal), CRM, client portal/notes, email newsletter, automation for onboarding and reminders (see [How to create an AI assistant](/blog/how-to-create-an-ai-assistant/)).

## Step 7: Website

Clear niche headline, who you help and outcomes, programmes and pricing guidance, testimonials, about story, free discovery call booking (see [How to make a booking website](/blog/how-to-make-a-booking-website/)).

## Step 8: Get first clients

Network outreach, free discovery calls, LinkedIn/Instagram content, workshops, referrals, partnerships (see [How to get coaching clients](/blog/how-to-get-coaching-clients/)).

## Starting a coaching business online / from home

Everything can be remote: video sessions, online booking and payments. Choose a quiet professional setup and reliable internet.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a coaching business?
Choose a niche and outcome, build credibility, design a packaged offer, set pricing, handle contracts and payments, set up booking and a website, and find clients through your network and content.

### Do I need a certification to be a coach?
Often not legally, but certification can improve skills and credibility; follow local rules and avoid regulated claims.

### How do I start an online coaching business?
Offer sessions via video, use online booking and payments, build a simple website and promote through content and referrals.

### How much does it cost to start a coaching business?
It can be very low: a website, scheduling and video tools, contracts and optional certification costs.

### What is the most profitable coaching niche?
Business, executive and sales coaching often command higher fees, but profitability depends on results and positioning.

### How do coaches get their first clients?
From their network, free discovery calls, content, workshops and referrals.
