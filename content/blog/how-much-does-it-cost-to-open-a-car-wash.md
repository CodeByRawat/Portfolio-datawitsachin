<p class="lede">Opening a car wash can cost from <strong>a few thousand dollars for a mobile detailing setup</strong> to <strong>hundreds of thousands for self-serve or in-bay automatic washes</strong>, and <strong>several million dollars for a new express tunnel</strong> including land and construction. Major costs are <strong>land, building/site work, wash equipment, permits, water systems and working capital</strong>.</p>

## Rough cost ranges by type

| Type | Rough range |
|---|---|
| Mobile detailing | A few thousand to tens of thousands |
| Self-serve (multiple bays) | Hundreds of thousands+ |
| In-bay automatic | Hundreds of thousands to $1M+ |
| Express tunnel (new build) | Often several million including land |
| Full-service | Highest, plus larger staff |

Costs vary greatly by location, land prices and equipment choices.

## Cost breakdown

- Land purchase or lease
- Site work, utilities, building construction
- Wash equipment, dryers, vacuums
- Water reclamation and treatment
- Pay stations, POS, licence plate recognition
- Permits, engineering, architecture
- Signage and branding
- Working capital and marketing

## Financing

SBA/bank loans, equipment financing, investors, or buying an existing wash (see [How much does it cost to buy a car wash?](/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-buy-a-car-wash/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does it cost to open a car wash?
From a few thousand dollars for mobile detailing to several million for a new express tunnel.

### How much does it cost to build a car wash?
Building costs depend on type, size and land; tunnels are the most expensive.

### What is the most expensive part of opening a car wash?
Land and construction, followed by wash equipment.

### Can I finance a car wash?
Yes, through bank and SBA loans, equipment financing or investors.

### Is it cheaper to buy or build a car wash?
Buying can be cheaper upfront and faster but may need upgrades; building allows modern design but costs more.

### How can I open a car wash with less money?
Start with mobile detailing or a smaller self-serve site and expand.
