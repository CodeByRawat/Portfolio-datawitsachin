<p class="lede">To start a car locksmith business, learn <strong>vehicle lockouts, key cutting and transponder key programming</strong>, buy a <strong>key cutting machine and a key programmer</strong>, get licensed (where required), insured and bonded, and market to <strong>drivers on Google, car dealers, used car lots, tow companies and repair shops</strong>. Automotive keys are one of the most profitable locksmith services.</p>

## Services to offer

| Service | Notes |
|---|---|
| Car lockouts | Quick jobs, high volume |
| Key duplication | Mechanical and transponder keys |
| All keys lost | Highest-value jobs |
| Key fob and smart key programming | Push-to-start vehicles |
| Ignition repair | Worn or broken ignitions |
| Broken key extraction | Common add-on |

## Training

Take an automotive locksmith course, practise on many vehicle makes, and learn the programming procedures for common brands. A start in general locksmithing helps.

## Equipment and startup costs

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Lockout kit (wedges, reach tools) | $200 – $800 |
| Key cutting machine | $1,500 – $6,000 |
| Key programmer | $1,500 – $8,000+ |
| Software and update subscriptions | Yearly fees |
| Blanks, fobs and transponders | $1,000 – $5,000 |
| Van or car with organised storage | Varies |

## Licenses and insurance

Some states license locksmiths, including automotive work. Get general liability, commercial auto and a bond. Always verify vehicle ownership (ID and registration) before opening or making keys.

## Pricing

Charge a service call fee plus key and programming fees. "All keys lost" and smart keys cost more. Your price is often well below dealer prices, and you come to the customer, which is your main selling point.

## Getting customers

1. Google Business Profile and a fast mobile website
2. Used car dealers (spare keys for every car)
3. Tow companies and roadside services
4. Auto repair shops
5. Car rental and fleet companies

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a car locksmith business?
Get automotive training, buy a key cutter and programmer, get licensed and insured, and market to drivers and dealers.

### How do I start a car unlocking business?
Start with lockout tools and roadside work, then add key cutting and programming.

### Is automotive locksmithing profitable?
Yes. Key replacement and programming have strong margins.

### How much does it cost to start a car locksmith business?
Often $5,000 to $20,000 for tools and inventory, plus a vehicle.

### Do I need to verify ownership?
Yes. Always check ID and registration before opening a car or cutting keys.
