<p class="lede">To become an appliance repair technician, <strong>learn electrical and appliance basics</strong> through a trade program, online course or on-the-job training, get <strong>EPA 608 certification</strong> for refrigeration work, and start as a <strong>trainee with an appliance repair company or manufacturer service network</strong>. Many technicians later start their own repair business.</p>

## Step 1: Learn the basics

- Electrical theory and using a multimeter
- Reading wiring diagrams and fault codes
- How washers, dryers, dishwashers, ovens and refrigerators work
- Gas safety
- Refrigeration basics

## Ways to learn appliance repair

| Option | Notes |
|---|---|
| Community college or trade school | HVAC/appliance or electrical programs |
| Online appliance repair courses | Flexible, often with videos and manuals |
| Manufacturer training | Brand-specific skills |
| On-the-job training | Ride along with a senior tech |
| Retailer and service company programs | Paid training for new techs |

## Step 2: Get certified

**EPA Section 608** is required to work on sealed refrigerant systems. Optional certifications from appliance service organisations add credibility.

## Step 3: Get hired

Apply to independent repair companies, appliance retailers' service departments and manufacturer service networks. Highlight mechanical ability, customer service and a clean driving record.

## Skills that matter

Troubleshooting, patience, customer communication, careful work around water and gas, and physical ability to move appliances.

## What appliance repair technicians make

Many earn around **$45,000 to $70,000**, and experienced or self-employed technicians can earn more.

## Starting your own business

After a few years, many technicians go out on their own. See [How to start an appliance repair business](/blog/how-to-start-an-appliance-repair-business/) and [Is appliance repair a good business?](/blog/is-appliance-repair-a-good-business/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I become an appliance repair technician?
Learn the basics through a course or job training, get EPA 608 certified, and join a repair company.

### How do I learn appliance repair?
Trade schools, online courses, manufacturer training and riding with experienced techs.

### How long does it take to become an appliance technician?
Basic skills take months; becoming fully skilled often takes one to three years.

### Do appliance technicians need certification?
EPA 608 is required for refrigerant work; other certifications are optional.

### Is appliance repair a good career?
Yes. Demand is steady and skilled technicians are in short supply.
