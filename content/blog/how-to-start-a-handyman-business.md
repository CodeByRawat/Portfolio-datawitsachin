<p class="lede">To start a handyman business: decide which <strong>services</strong> you'll offer, check your state's <strong>licence rules and job value limits</strong> (many states require a contractor licence above a dollar threshold or for electrical/plumbing work), <strong>register the business</strong>, get <strong>general liability insurance</strong>, assemble <strong>tools and a vehicle</strong>, set <strong>hourly or flat-rate pricing with minimums</strong>, use <strong>online booking and invoicing</strong>, and get jobs through <strong>Google, Nextdoor, referrals and realtors</strong>.</p>

## Steps

1. **Services:** repairs, drywall, painting touch-ups, assembly, mounting TVs, doors, fixtures, caulking, small carpentry
2. **Licensing:** learn your state's handyman exemption limit; avoid licensed trades (major electrical, plumbing, HVAC) unless licensed
3. **Register:** LLC or sole proprietorship, EIN, local licence
4. **Insurance:** general liability, commercial auto, workers' comp when hiring
5. **Tools:** drills, saws, levels, stud finder, ladders, hand tools, organised vehicle storage
6. **Pricing:** hourly with a minimum, or flat rates for common tasks (TV mounting, fixture swaps)
7. **Booking and payments:** online booking, reminders, digital invoices and card payments
8. **Marketing:** Google Business Profile, reviews, Nextdoor, Facebook groups, realtors and property managers
9. **Systems:** job checklists, photo documentation, follow-up and review requests
10. **Grow:** add technicians, maintenance plans, or a niche (see [How to start a home maintenance business](/blog/how-to-start-a-home-maintenance-business/))

## Startup costs

Tools, insurance, registration, a reliable vehicle and marketing; many handymen start with tools they already own.

## Is a handyman business profitable?

It can be, with good pricing, minimum charges, efficient routing and repeat customers (see [Are handyman businesses profitable?](/blog/are-handyman-businesses-profitable/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a handyman business?
Choose services, check licence limits, register and insure, gather tools, set pricing, add booking and market locally.

### Do I need a licence to be a handyman?
It depends on the state; many allow small jobs without a contractor licence but require one above a set amount.

### How much does it cost to start a handyman business?
Mostly tools, insurance, registration, a vehicle and marketing.

### How should handymen price jobs?
Hourly with a minimum charge, or flat rates for common tasks.

### How do handymen get customers?
Google Business Profile, reviews, Nextdoor, referrals, realtors and property managers.

### Can I start a handyman business part-time?
Yes; many start on evenings and weekends before going full-time.
