<p class="lede">Most cleaning businesses need a <strong>business registration</strong> (sole proprietorship, LLC or local equivalent), a <strong>general business licence</strong> from the city/county, <strong>tax registrations</strong> (tax ID, and sales tax where cleaning services are taxable), and <strong>employer registrations</strong> if you hire. Specialised services (biohazard, pest control, certain chemical use) may need extra licences. Beyond licences, you'll want <strong>general liability insurance</strong>, <strong>workers' compensation</strong> for employees, and a <strong>janitorial bond</strong> to reassure clients. Requirements vary by country, state and city, so always check locally.</p>

## Typical licence and registration checklist

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Business entity registration** | Sole proprietorship, LLC, corporation (or local equivalents) |
| **DBA / trade name** | If operating under a different name |
| **General business licence** | City or county level in many places |
| **Tax ID** | E.g., EIN in the US if you have employees or form an LLC |
| **Sales tax permit** | Where cleaning services are taxable (varies by state/country) |
| **Employer registrations** | Payroll tax, unemployment insurance |
| **Specialised licences** | Biohazard/crime scene, pest control, some chemical use, waste disposal |
| **Home occupation permit** | If running the business from home (some areas) |

## Insurance and bonding (not licences, but essential)

- **General liability insurance:** property damage or injuries
- **Workers' compensation:** usually required with employees
- **Janitorial/surety bond:** protects clients against theft by employees; often expected by commercial clients
- **Commercial auto insurance** for business vehicles

## How to register a cleaning business

1. Choose a name and check availability
2. Pick a structure and register with the state/government
3. Get a tax ID and open a business bank account
4. Apply for local business licence/permits
5. Register for sales tax if applicable
6. Buy insurance and a bond
7. Set up bookkeeping (see [How to start a bookkeeping business](/blog/how-to-start-a-bookkeeping-business/) for the bookkeeping side)

## Frequently asked questions

### What licenses are needed to start a cleaning business?
Usually a business registration, a local general business licence, tax registrations and employer registrations if hiring; specialised services may need additional licences.

### Do I need a licence to clean houses?
Often only a general business licence is needed, but requirements vary by city and state, so check locally.

### Do cleaning businesses need insurance?
Yes, general liability insurance is strongly recommended and often required by clients; workers' compensation is typically required with employees.

### What is a janitorial bond?
A surety bond that compensates clients if an employee steals from them, increasing trust.

### How do I register a cleaning business?
Choose a name and structure, register with your state or government, get a tax ID, apply for local licences and permits, and set up insurance and banking.

### Do I need to charge sales tax on cleaning services?
It depends on your state or country; some tax cleaning services, others don't. Check local tax rules.
