<p class="lede">A coffee cart is the <strong>cheapest way to start a mobile coffee business</strong>. Buy or build a <strong>cart</strong> with an <strong>espresso machine, grinder, water tanks and sinks</strong>, get <strong>health and vendor permits</strong>, and sell at <strong>events, markets, offices, gyms and indoor venues</strong>. Startup costs often run <strong>$10,000 to $40,000</strong>.</p>

## Coffee cart vs coffee truck

| | Coffee cart | Coffee truck |
|---|---|---|
| Startup cost | $10,000 – $40,000 | $40,000 – $150,000+ |
| Mobility | Needs transport | Drives itself |
| Best for | Events, indoor spots | Street vending, big events |
| Capacity | Smaller | Larger |

## Step 1: Get a cart

Buy a ready-made coffee cart or build one. Many health departments require hand-wash sinks, water tanks and food-safe surfaces. Check rules before you build.

## Step 2: Equipment

- Espresso machine (1–2 group)
- Grinder
- Water tanks or plumbed connection
- Power (venue outlet, generator or battery)
- Small fridge for milk
- POS system

## Step 3: Permits

Business licence, health department permit, mobile vendor permit, commissary or base kitchen if required, and event permits.

## Step 4: Startup costs

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Cart (new or built) | $5,000 – $20,000 |
| Espresso machine and grinder | $3,000 – $15,000 |
| Power system | $500 – $5,000 |
| Permits | $500 – $3,000 |
| Branding and signage | $500 – $2,000 |
| Supplies and inventory | $500 – $2,000 |

## Step 5: Locations

- Weddings and corporate events (charge per guest or per hour)
- Office lobbies, coworking spaces and hospitals
- Farmers' markets and pop-ups
- Gyms and car dealerships
- Schools and colleges

## Step 6: Pricing

Retail drinks at events and markets, and packages for private events (for example 2–3 hours of unlimited drinks for a set number of guests).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a coffee cart business?
Get a compliant cart, espresso equipment and permits, then sell at events, markets and indoor venues.

### How much does a coffee cart business cost?
Often $10,000 to $40,000.

### Is a coffee cart profitable?
Yes. Low overhead and high drink margins make it profitable.

### Do I need a health permit for a coffee cart?
Yes. Health and vendor permits are required.

### Where can I set up a coffee cart?
Events, offices, markets, gyms and other venues with permission.
