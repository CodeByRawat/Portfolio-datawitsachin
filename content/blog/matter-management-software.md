Every firm and in-house legal team runs on matters. **Matter management software** (or **legal case management software** in litigation) is the record of what each matter is, who's on it, what's due and what it costs.

## Core features

- **Matter intake and opening:** standard fields, conflict check, engagement terms.
- **Parties and contacts** linked to each matter.
- **Tasks and deadlines** with automatic reminders and rule-based dates.
- **Documents and email** filed against the matter.
- **Notes and activity timeline** so anyone can pick up a file.
- **Budgets and costs** with alerts before a matter overruns.
- **Reporting:** workload, ageing, profitability, outcomes.

## Litigation-specific needs

Litigation adds court calendars and rule sets, pleadings and bundles, disclosure tracking, hearing preparation and witness management. Check that the tool supports the courts and procedures you use.

## Legal project management

Predictable fixed-fee work needs phases, tasks, budgets and hours tracked against plan. Look for matter templates by type, so every new conveyance or claim starts with the right checklist.

## Where AI helps (and where to be careful)

AI case management features can summarise documents, draft chronologies, suggest next steps and extract key dates. These save time but must be **reviewed by a lawyer**, and you should know where the data goes. Ask any vendor whether client data is used to train models.

## Cloud-based or self-hosted

Cloud-based matter management is the default for most firms: lower upkeep and access from anywhere. If your clients or regulators restrict data location, confirm hosting regions or consider a self-hosted or custom option.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is matter management?
Matter management is tracking each legal matter from opening to closure: parties, documents, tasks, deadlines, communications, costs and outcomes, in one place.

### What's the difference between case management and matter management?
Case management is the term most used for litigation and individual cases. Matter management is broader, covering any piece of legal work, including transactional and advisory matters, and is common in in-house legal teams.

### What is legal project management?
Applying project management methods (scope, budget, milestones, staffing) to legal matters so they're delivered predictably and profitably.
