<p class="lede">To start an appliance store, pick a model: <strong>new appliances</strong> (through a buying group or distributor), <strong>used and refurbished</strong>, or <strong>scratch-and-dent / overstock</strong>. Then register the business, secure a <strong>showroom or warehouse</strong>, line up <strong>suppliers</strong>, offer <strong>delivery, installation and haul-away</strong>, and market locally and online. Startup costs range from about <strong>$20,000 for a used appliance shop to $250,000+ for a new-appliance showroom</strong>.</p>

## Choose your model

| Model | How it works | Startup cost |
|---|---|---|
| Used / refurbished | Buy, repair, clean and resell | Lowest |
| Scratch-and-dent / overstock | Buy damaged-box or returned units in bulk | Low to medium |
| New appliances | Authorised dealer through distributors or buying groups | Highest |
| Online / marketplace | Sell through listings with local pickup | Low |

Used and refurbished stores pair well with an appliance repair business: see [How to start an appliance repair business](/blog/how-to-start-an-appliance-repair-business/).

## Step 1: Register and get licensed

LLC, EIN, business license, sales tax permit and general liability insurance. If you install gas appliances, you may need a licensed installer.

## Step 2: Find suppliers

- Appliance distributors and buying groups (for new)
- Retail return and overstock liquidators (for scratch-and-dent)
- Estate sales, landlords, remodelers and trade-ins (for used)

## Step 3: Location and space

A warehouse-style space with a loading dock keeps costs down. Showrooms in visible retail areas cost more but attract walk-ins.

## Step 4: Startup costs

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Lease deposit and setup | $5,000 – $30,000 |
| Initial inventory | $10,000 – $150,000+ |
| Box truck or trailer for delivery | $10,000 – $40,000 |
| Dollies, tools, repair parts | $1,000 – $5,000 |
| Point of sale and website | $1,000 – $5,000 |
| Insurance and licenses | $2,000 – $5,000 |

## Step 5: Services that boost profit

Delivery, installation, haul-away of old appliances, extended warranties, customer financing and repair services.

## Step 6: Marketing

1. Google Business Profile with photos and reviews
2. A website with your current inventory and prices
3. Marketplace listings for individual units
4. Landlords, property managers and contractors (bulk buyers)
5. Promotions for holiday sale periods

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start an appliance store?
Choose new, used or scratch-and-dent, register the business, find suppliers, get a space and offer delivery and installation.

### How much does it cost to start an appliance business?
From about $20,000 for used appliances to $250,000+ for a new-appliance showroom.

### Is selling used appliances profitable?
Yes, especially if you can repair units yourself and buy cheaply.

### Where do appliance stores get inventory?
Distributors, buying groups, overstock liquidators and trade-ins.

### Do I need a license to sell appliances?
A business license and sales tax permit; gas installations may need a licensed installer.
