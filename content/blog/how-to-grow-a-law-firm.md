<p class="lede">To grow a law firm profitably, increase the <strong>number of good-fit clients</strong> (marketing, referrals, intake conversion) <strong>and</strong> the <strong>profit per matter</strong> (pricing, realisation, collections, efficient delivery). Track <strong>utilisation, realisation and collection rates</strong>, analyse <strong>client and matter profitability</strong>, move suitable work to <strong>flat or value-based fees</strong>, <strong>delegate</strong> to the right level, and use <strong>technology and automation</strong> to remove admin.</p>

## Growth levers

1. **Focus:** stronger positioning in fewer practice areas
2. **Marketing:** website, SEO, content, reviews (see [How to market a law firm](/blog/how-to-market-a-law-firm/))
3. **Referral networks** with professionals and other lawyers
4. **Intake conversion:** faster response, better consultations (see [Legal intake software](/blog/legal-intake-software/))
5. **Client retention and cross-selling** across practice areas
6. **New services:** subscriptions, unbundled services, packages
7. **Hiring and delegation** to scale capacity

## Profitability levers

| Lever | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Utilisation rate** | Increase billable/productive time vs available time |
| **Realisation rate** | Bill what's worked (reduce write-downs) |
| **Collection rate** | Collect what's billed (clear terms, retainers, online payments) |
| **Pricing** | Raise rates; use flat or value pricing where efficient |
| **Leverage** | Right work at the right level (partner, associate, paralegal) |
| **Overhead** | Rent, software and staff efficiency |
| **Automation** | Documents, intake, reminders, billing (see [Legal workflow automation](/blog/legal-workflow-automation/)) |

## How law firms measure client profitability

1. Track **time and costs per client/matter** (including non-billable time)
2. Calculate **revenue collected** per client
3. Allocate **overhead** reasonably
4. **Profit = revenue − direct costs − allocated overhead**
5. Compare by client, practice area, fee type and lawyer

Use practice management and billing data (see [Law practice management software](/blog/law-practice-management-software/), [Legal billing software](/blog/legal-billing-software/)) and dashboards (see [What is business intelligence?](/blog/what-is-business-intelligence/)).

## KPIs to track

New matters, lead-to-client conversion, revenue per lawyer, utilisation, realisation, collection rate, average fee per matter, accounts receivable days, client satisfaction and reviews.

## Using AI and automation

Document drafting, research assistance, intake chatbots, billing reminders and reporting can save significant time (see [AI for lawyers](/blog/ai-for-lawyers/)). Maintain confidentiality, supervision and accuracy checks.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you grow a law firm?
Focus on clear practice areas, market consistently, build referral networks, improve intake conversion, retain clients and add capacity through hiring and technology.

### How can a law firm improve profitability?
Raise utilisation, realisation and collection rates, refine pricing, delegate work appropriately, control overhead and automate admin.

### How do law firms measure client profitability?
By tracking time, costs and collected revenue per client or matter and allocating overhead to calculate profit.

### What are the key KPIs for a law firm?
New matters, conversion rate, utilisation, realisation, collection rate, revenue per lawyer and accounts receivable days.

### Should law firms use flat fees?
Flat fees work well for predictable matters and can improve profitability and client satisfaction when processes are efficient.

### How can technology help a law firm grow?
By automating intake, documents, billing and reminders, freeing lawyers for higher-value work and improving client experience.
