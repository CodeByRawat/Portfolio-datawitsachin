<p class="lede"><strong>SEO (search engine optimisation)</strong> is the practice of improving a website so it <strong>appears higher in unpaid (organic) search results</strong> on Google, Bing and AI-powered search, for the searches your customers make. It involves making pages <strong>easy for search engines to crawl and understand</strong> (technical SEO), creating <strong>helpful content that matches what people search</strong> (on-page and content SEO), and earning <strong>trust signals such as links and reviews</strong> (off-page SEO). Good SEO brings steady, free traffic from people who are actively looking for what you offer.</p>

## What does SEO mean?

**S**earch **E**ngine **O**ptimisation (or optimization in US spelling). "Doing SEO" means improving a website and its presence so search engines show it more prominently for relevant searches. An "SEO" can also refer to a person who does this work.

## How search engines work

1. **Crawling:** bots discover pages by following links and sitemaps.
2. **Indexing:** the search engine stores and understands page content.
3. **Ranking:** for each search, algorithms order results based on relevance, quality, usefulness, authority, page experience, location and more.

SEO helps at each stage: making pages discoverable, understandable and the best answer.

## The main types of SEO

| Type | Focus | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **On-page SEO** | Content and HTML of each page | Titles, meta descriptions, headings, keywords, internal links, images |
| **Content SEO** | Creating pages that answer searches | Service pages, guides, FAQs, comparisons |
| **Technical SEO** | How well search engines can crawl and render the site | Speed, mobile-friendliness, sitemaps, structured data, HTTPS, canonical tags |
| **Off-page SEO** | Reputation beyond your site | Backlinks, mentions, reviews (see [How to build backlinks](/blog/how-to-build-backlinks/)) |
| **Local SEO** | Ranking in local and map results | Google Business Profile, citations, local pages (see [What are local citations?](/blog/what-are-local-citations/)) |
| **E-commerce SEO** | Product and category pages | Product schema, faceted navigation |

## Key SEO ranking factors (simplified)

- **Relevance:** the page clearly answers the search intent
- **Content quality and helpfulness:** original, accurate, experience-based
- **Authority and trust:** links, brand mentions, reviews, author expertise (E-E-A-T)
- **Page experience:** mobile-friendly, fast, secure, not cluttered with intrusive pop-ups
- **Technical health:** crawlable, indexable, no duplicate content issues
- **Location** (for local searches)

## Keywords and search intent

Keywords are the words people type. Search intent is **why** they search:
- **Informational:** "what is SEO"
- **Commercial:** "best website builder"
- **Transactional:** "hire web designer"
- **Navigational:** "Google Search Console login"
- **Local:** "dentist near me"

Choose keywords where your page can satisfy the intent. (Guide: [How to choose keywords for SEO](/blog/how-to-choose-keywords-for-seo/).)

## SEO in the age of AI search

AI Overviews and AI assistants summarise answers, sometimes reducing clicks. What still works:
- Clear, factual, well-structured content that's easy to quote
- Strong brand and reviews
- Pages for specific questions and services
- Structured data and up-to-date business information
- Content with real experience, data and opinions AI can't replicate

## How long does SEO take?

Typically **3–6 months** to see meaningful results for a new site, longer for competitive terms. Low-competition (low keyword difficulty) questions can rank faster. SEO compounds over time.

## How much does SEO cost?

DIY costs time. Freelancers and agencies commonly charge monthly retainers (from a few hundred to several thousand dollars) or project fees for audits and fixes. A website built with SEO in mind from day one avoids costly fixes later (see [How much does a website cost?](/blog/how-much-does-a-website-cost/)).

## Beginner SEO checklist

- [ ] Set up **Google Search Console** and **Analytics**
- [ ] Ensure the site is **mobile-friendly, fast and HTTPS**
- [ ] One page per main service/topic, with a unique **title tag** and **meta description** (see [What is a meta description?](/blog/what-is-a-meta-description/))
- [ ] Clear **H1** and logical headings
- [ ] **Internal links** between related pages
- [ ] Descriptive **image alt text** and compressed images
- [ ] **XML sitemap** submitted
- [ ] **Google Business Profile** for local businesses (see [How to create a Google Business page](/blog/how-to-create-a-google-business-page/))
- [ ] Publish **helpful content** answering customer questions
- [ ] Earn **links and reviews**
- [ ] Track rankings and traffic; improve monthly

## Frequently asked questions

### What is SEO?
SEO, or search engine optimisation, is improving a website's content, technical setup and reputation so it ranks higher in organic search results for relevant searches.

### What does SEO mean in marketing?
In marketing, SEO is the channel focused on earning free traffic from search engines by matching content to what potential customers search for.

### What are the main types of SEO?
On-page, content, technical, off-page and local SEO, plus specialised areas like e-commerce SEO.

### Is SEO free?
Organic clicks are free, but SEO requires time, content, tools and often professional help.

### How long does SEO take to work?
Usually three to six months for noticeable results, depending on competition, website age and effort.

### Is SEO still worth it with AI search?
Yes. Search engines and AI assistants still rely on websites for information, and well-optimised, trustworthy content continues to earn visibility and customers.
