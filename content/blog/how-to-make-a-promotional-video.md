<p class="lede">To make a promotional video: <strong>set one goal and audience</strong>, choose the <strong>type of promo</strong> (product, brand, event, app or offer), <strong>write a short, hook-first script</strong> with a single call to action, <strong>storyboard</strong> it, <strong>produce</strong> the footage or animation, <strong>edit</strong> with music, sound and captions, and <strong>export versions for each platform</strong> (9:16, 1:1, 16:9). A strong promo video is short, focused on the viewer's benefit and designed for where it will be watched.</p>

## Step 1: Define the goal, audience and platform

- **Goal:** sales, sign-ups, event tickets, app installs, awareness
- **Audience:** who they are and what they care about
- **Platform:** Reels/TikTok/Shorts, YouTube, website, LinkedIn, event screens, TV
- **Length:** 6–30 seconds for social ads; 30–90 seconds for website and YouTube

## Step 2: Choose the type of promotional video

| Type | What it shows | Best style |
|---|---|---|
| **Product promo** | Product in action, key benefits | Live action or 3D product animation |
| **App / SaaS promo** | Real UI, features, outcomes | Motion graphics with UI animation (see [What are motion graphics?](/blog/what-are-motion-graphics/)) |
| **Brand video** | Values, story, people | Live action, documentary style |
| **Event promo** | Date, speakers, highlights, urgency | Fast cuts, kinetic text, countdown |
| **Offer / sale promo** | Discount and deadline | Bold motion graphics, short |
| **Testimonial promo** | Customers explaining results | Interviews + B-roll |
| **Teaser / launch** | Curiosity before release | Short, mysterious, dramatic sound |
| **Explainer** | How it works | Animation (see [What is an explainer video?](/blog/what-is-an-explainer-video/)) |

## Step 3: Write the script (hook first)

**Structure for a 30-second promo:**
1. **Hook (0–3 s):** the result, the pain or a striking visual
2. **Problem or desire (3–8 s)**
3. **Product as the solution (8–20 s):** 2–3 benefits shown visually
4. **Proof (20–25 s):** rating, numbers, customer quote
5. **CTA (25–30 s):** one action, with offer or urgency

Keep on-screen text short and write voiceover at about 2.5 words per second.

## Step 4: Storyboard and plan

Sketch each shot, list the footage, screens, graphics and music needed, and plan **transitions** that connect scenes. Approve the storyboard before production, which is where changes are cheapest. (Briefing tips: [How to write a video brief for a product demo](/blog/how-to-write-a-video-brief-for-a-product-demo/).)

## Step 5: Produce the visuals

### Filming (live action)
- Use a modern phone or camera in **4K**, stabilised
- **Lighting:** soft natural light or a key light; avoid harsh overhead light
- **Audio:** lavalier or shotgun mic for speaking parts
- Capture **B-roll:** close-ups of the product, hands using it, environment, reactions
- Film **vertical-safe** framing if social is the main use

### Animation and motion graphics
- Animate product shots, UI screens, text and icons
- Use brand fonts and colours
- 3D renders work well for physical products without a studio shoot
(How-to: [How to make motion graphics](/blog/how-to-make-motion-graphics/).)

### AI-assisted assets
AI can generate B-roll, backgrounds and voiceovers, but use real product visuals for accuracy (see [Which AI tools generate videos from text?](/blog/which-ai-tools-generate-videos-from-text/)).

## Step 6: Edit

- Cut to the **beat of the music**
- Remove anything that doesn't serve the message
- Add **captions** and on-screen text
- **Sound design:** whooshes, clicks, impacts
- **Colour correction** for a consistent look
- Add your **logo** subtly (see [How to add a logo to a video](/blog/how-to-add-a-logo-to-a-video/))
- End on a clear **CTA screen** with URL or button prompt

## Step 7: Export for every platform

| Platform | Ratio | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Reels, TikTok, Shorts, Stories | 9:16 | Safe zones for UI |
| Instagram/Facebook feed, LinkedIn | 4:5 or 1:1 | Captions burned in |
| YouTube, website | 16:9 | Higher resolution master |

## How much does a promotional video cost?

- **DIY with templates:** time plus subscriptions
- **Freelancer:** roughly a few hundred dollars for short social promos up to several thousand for custom animated or filmed promos
- **Agency/production company:** several thousand to tens of thousands for full shoots and high-end animation

## Promotional video examples

- **Café:** 15 s Reel: latte art close-up → new seasonal menu text → "This week only"
- **SaaS:** 30 s motion promo: messy spreadsheet → app dashboard animating → "Start free trial"
- **Gym:** testimonial cut with before/after and "First week free"
- **Event:** speakers' faces flashing to beat, date, "Tickets selling fast"

## Mistakes to avoid

- Slow intro or logo first
- Too many messages
- Unreadable text, no captions
- Poor audio
- One version cropped badly for every platform
- No clear call to action

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I make a promotional video?
Set a goal and audience, choose a promo type, write a hook-first script, storyboard, film or animate, edit with music, captions and a CTA, then export for each platform.

### How long should a promotional video be?
6–30 seconds for social ads, 30–90 seconds for website and YouTube promos.

### What makes a good promotional video?
A strong opening hook, one clear message focused on the viewer's benefit, good visuals and audio, proof, and a single call to action.

### Can I make a promotional video on my phone?
Yes. Modern phones shoot high-quality video, and apps like CapCut let you edit, add text and music and export for social media.

### Should my promo video be animated or filmed?
Animation suits software, abstract services and offers; filming suits physical products, people-driven services and testimonials. Many promos mix both.

### How much does it cost to hire someone to make a promo video?
From a few hundred dollars for simple social promos to several thousand or more for custom animation or professional shoots.
