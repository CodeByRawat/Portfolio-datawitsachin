<p class="lede">To market a dentist office effectively, focus on being <strong>easy to find, easy to trust and easy to book</strong>: a <strong>fast, mobile-friendly dental website with online booking</strong>, an optimised <strong>Google Business Profile</strong> with lots of <strong>recent reviews</strong>, <strong>local SEO</strong> pages for each service, targeted <strong>Google and Meta ads</strong> for high-value treatments (implants, Invisalign, cosmetic dentistry), helpful <strong>video content</strong>, and <strong>automated reminders and follow-ups</strong> that keep patients coming back.</p>

## 1. Build a dental website that converts

Your website is where most new patients decide. It should include:
- **Clear headline** with location and specialities ("Family and cosmetic dentist in Austin")
- **Online booking** and **click-to-call** visible on mobile
- **WhatsApp or chat button** (see [How to create an AI chatbot](/blog/how-to-create-an-ai-chatbot/))
- **Separate pages per service:** implants, Invisalign, whitening, emergency dentistry, children's dentistry
- **Insurance and financing information**
- **Real team photos and bios**
- **Before/after galleries** (with consent)
- **Reviews and ratings**
- **Fast speed and accessibility**

(Building one: [How to create a website for your business](/blog/how-to-create-a-website-for-your-business/), costs: [How much does a website cost?](/blog/how-much-does-a-website-cost/))

## 2. Optimise your Google Business Profile

- Correct name, address, phone, hours, categories (primary: Dentist, plus relevant secondary categories)
- Services, photos of the clinic and team, booking link
- Regular posts and offers
- Answer common questions

## 3. Get more reviews (and reply to them)

Ask every happy patient via an automated text or WhatsApp after visits with a direct review link. Reply to all reviews professionally, without disclosing patient information.

## 4. Local SEO

- Service + city pages ("dental implants in Austin")
- Consistent name, address and phone across directories (Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Yelp, local directories)
- LocalBusiness/Dentist structured data
- Helpful articles answering patient questions ("How do dental implants work?", "Will the ER pull a tooth?")
- Local links: sponsorships, community events

## 5. Google Ads

Target high-intent searches like "emergency dentist near me", "Invisalign cost [city]", "dental implants near me". Send each to a matching landing page (see [What is a landing page?](/blog/what-is-a-landing-page/) and [Good landing page experience](/blog/good-landing-page-experience/)).

## 6. Meta and social ads

Promote new patient offers, whitening specials and Invisalign consultations with short videos, before/after stories and patient testimonials (see [How to create Instagram Stories ads](/blog/how-to-create-instagram-stories-ads/)).

## 7. Video content

- Meet-the-dentist videos
- Treatment explainers (implants, aligners) with animation (see [What is an explainer video?](/blog/what-is-an-explainer-video/))
- Patient testimonial videos (with written consent)
- Clinic tours to reduce anxiety
- Short-form tips for Reels, TikTok and Shorts

## 8. Online booking and fast response

Many patients choose the first practice that responds. Offer online booking, after-hours chat and instant confirmations.

## 9. Automated reminders and recalls

- Appointment reminders via SMS, email or WhatsApp (see [How to schedule a WhatsApp message](/blog/how-to-schedule-a-whatsapp-message/))
- 6-month cleaning recalls
- Treatment plan follow-ups for unscheduled treatment
- Birthday and seasonal messages
(See [Automation in healthcare](/blog/automation-in-healthcare/).)

## 10. Referral programme

Thank patients who refer friends (follow local rules on incentives in healthcare). Make it easy with shareable links.

## 11. Email newsletters

Oral health tips, practice news and offers to existing patients.

## 12. Community marketing

School visits, health fairs, local sponsorships and partnerships with gyms, pharmacies and employers.

## 13. Patient experience

Comfort, punctuality, clear pricing and friendly staff produce reviews and referrals: the most powerful marketing.

## 14. Membership plans

In-house membership plans for uninsured patients can attract and retain patients with predictable revenue.

## 15. Track ROI

Track calls, form submissions, bookings and new patients by source (call tracking numbers, UTM links, CRM). Know your **cost per new patient** and **patient lifetime value**.

## Compliance reminders

Follow health privacy laws (HIPAA in the US or local equivalents), professional advertising rules, and consent requirements for testimonials, photos and messages.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I market a dentist office?
Build a conversion-focused website with online booking, optimise your Google Business Profile, gather reviews, invest in local SEO and targeted ads, create video content, and automate reminders and follow-ups.

### What is the best marketing for dentists?
Local SEO and Google Business Profile combined with reviews, Google Ads for high-intent searches and a strong website usually deliver the best return.

### How can a dental practice get more new patients?
Make it easy to find, trust and book: strong reviews, fast mobile website, online booking, ads for high-value treatments, and referral programmes.

### How much should a dental practice spend on marketing?
Practices often budget a few percent of revenue on marketing, more when growing or launching; track cost per new patient to guide spending.

### Do dentists need a website?
Yes. Most patients research online before booking, and a website with services, reviews and booking converts searches into appointments.

### How do dentists get more Google reviews?
Ask every satisfied patient soon after their visit with a direct review link sent automatically by text, email or WhatsApp.
