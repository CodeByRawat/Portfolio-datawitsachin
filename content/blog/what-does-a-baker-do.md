<p class="lede">A <strong>baker</strong> prepares and bakes breads, pastries, cakes, cookies and other goods: measuring and mixing ingredients, shaping doughs, proofing, baking, decorating and packaging, while following <strong>recipes, production schedules and food safety rules</strong>. Bakers work in retail bakeries, supermarkets, cafés, restaurants, hotels, factories or their own home or shop businesses.</p>

## Typical duties

- Measure, mix and knead doughs and batters
- Shape, proof and bake products
- Monitor ovens, timing and quality
- Decorate cakes and pastries
- Follow recipes and adjust for conditions
- Clean equipment and follow hygiene rules
- Manage inventory and orders
- Package and label products

## Types of bakers

| Type | Focus |
|---|---|
| Bread/artisan baker | Breads, sourdough, laminated doughs |
| Pastry chef | Pastries, desserts, chocolate |
| Cake decorator | Celebration and custom cakes |
| Production/commercial baker | High-volume baking in factories |
| Home baker/entrepreneur | Small business from home (see [How to start a home baking business](/blog/how-to-start-a-home-baking-business/)) |

## Skills

Precision, consistency, time management, physical stamina, creativity (decorating), food safety knowledge and teamwork.

## Work hours

Often early mornings, weekends and holidays, especially in retail bakeries.

## Pay

Pay varies by location, employer and specialisation; pastry chefs, head bakers and bakery owners can earn more than entry-level bakers.

## How to become a baker

1. Learn basics at home or through classes
2. Get an entry-level job or apprenticeship in a bakery
3. Consider culinary/pastry school or certificates
4. Earn food safety certification
5. Specialise and build a portfolio
6. Open your own bakery when ready (see [How to start a bakery](/blog/how-to-start-a-bakery/))

## Frequently asked questions

### What does a baker do?
Prepares, bakes and decorates breads, pastries and cakes, following recipes, schedules and food safety rules.

### What skills do bakers need?
Precision, consistency, time management, physical stamina, creativity and food safety knowledge.

### Do bakers need formal training?
Not always; many learn through apprenticeships and on-the-job training, though culinary school can help.

### What hours do bakers work?
Often early mornings, weekends and holidays.

### What is the difference between a baker and a pastry chef?
Bakers often focus on breads and everyday baked goods; pastry chefs specialise in pastries, desserts and fine decorative work.

### Can bakers start their own business?
Yes, from home bakeries and market stalls to retail shops and cake studios.
