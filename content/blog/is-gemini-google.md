<p class="lede"><strong>Yes, Gemini is Google's.</strong> <strong>Gemini</strong> is the name of Google's family of AI models, developed by <strong>Google DeepMind</strong>, and also the name of Google's <strong>AI assistant app</strong> (formerly called Bard). Gemini models power features across Google products, including <strong>Search (AI Overviews and AI Mode), Gmail, Docs, Android and Google Workspace</strong>, and are available to developers through the Gemini API and Google Cloud's Vertex AI.</p>

## What is Google Gemini?

| "Gemini" can mean | Description |
|---|---|
| **Gemini models** | Google's multimodal large language models that understand text, images, audio, video and code |
| **Gemini app** | The chatbot/assistant at gemini.google.com and on Android and iOS |
| **Gemini in Workspace** | AI features in Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Slides and Meet (plan-dependent) |
| **Gemini API** | Developer access via Google AI Studio and Vertex AI |

Gemini replaced the Bard brand in early 2024 and has gone through several model generations since.

## What can Gemini do?

- Answer questions and explain topics
- Write and edit text, emails and documents
- Analyse images, PDFs and files
- Help with code
- Research with Google Search grounding
- Generate images and (on eligible plans) video with Google's Veo models (see [How long can Veo 3 videos be?](/blog/how-long-can-veo-3-videos-be/))
- Connect to Google apps (Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Maps, YouTube) when you enable it

## Free vs paid Gemini

Google offers a **free Gemini plan** and paid plans (Google AI subscriptions bundled with Google One storage, and Gemini for Google Workspace for businesses). Paid tiers typically unlock more capable models, higher usage limits, longer context, video generation and Gemini in Gmail/Docs. Plan names, features and prices change, so check Google's current offers.

### Why am I not eligible for a Gemini free trial?
Common reasons include: you've already used a trial on that Google account, your account type (some work/school or child accounts) isn't eligible, your country isn't supported for the offer, or you're already subscribed through another plan. Check the offer terms on the Google One/Gemini subscription page.

## Can Gemini access Google Scholar?

Gemini doesn't have a dedicated, guaranteed integration with Google Scholar's database. With search grounding it may find and summarise publicly available academic pages, and Deep Research-style features can browse sources, but always verify citations directly in Google Scholar or the publisher's site, since AI can misattribute or invent references.

## Gemini vs ChatGPT vs Claude

| | Gemini (Google) | ChatGPT (OpenAI) | Claude (Anthropic) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Strengths** | Deep Google ecosystem integration, multimodal, search grounding | Broad features, large ecosystem, image/voice tools | Writing quality, long documents, coding and agentic work |
| **Business suite** | Google Workspace | Microsoft integrations and ChatGPT Business/Enterprise | Claude for Work, integrations via MCP |

Each releases new models frequently; the best choice depends on your tasks and tools. (Background: [What does ChatGPT stand for?](/blog/what-does-chatgpt-stand-for/), [Who owns ChatGPT?](/blog/who-owns-chatgpt/))

## Is Gemini safe and private?

Review Gemini Apps Activity settings, which control whether conversations are saved and may be reviewed to improve services. Workspace business plans have separate data protections. Avoid sharing sensitive information you wouldn't want stored.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Gemini made by Google?
Yes. Gemini is developed by Google DeepMind and is Google's main family of AI models and its AI assistant app.

### Is Gemini the same as Bard?
Yes. Google renamed Bard to Gemini in 2024 and upgraded it with Gemini models.

### Is Google Gemini free?
There is a free version, plus paid plans with more capable models, higher limits and extra features.

### What is the search AI called on Google?
Google's AI features in Search include AI Overviews and AI Mode, both powered by Gemini models (see [Why is AI Mode not showing up on Google?](/blog/why-is-ai-mode-not-showing-up-on-google/)).

### Can I use Gemini for work?
Yes. Gemini for Google Workspace and Google Cloud offer business features with enterprise data protections.

### Is Gemini better than ChatGPT?
It depends on the task; Gemini excels with Google integrations and multimodal tasks, while ChatGPT and Claude have their own strengths. Many people use more than one.
