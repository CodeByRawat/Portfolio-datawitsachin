## What you need to start

| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| Gas pressure washer (4+ GPM) | Concrete, driveways, decks |
| Surface cleaner | Fast, even flat-surface cleaning |
| Soft wash system (12V pump) | Houses and roofs without damage |
| Hoses, reels, nozzles, wands | Reach and control |
| Chemicals (sodium hypochlorite, surfactants, degreaser) | Kill algae and mildew, remove stains |
| Truck, trailer or skid setup | Carry it all |
| Water tank (optional) | For sites without water access |
| Safety gear | Goggles, gloves, boots |

## Step 1: Learn the trade

Too much pressure damages siding, wood and roofs. Learn when to use high pressure (concrete) vs soft wash (houses, roofs, fences). Practise on your own property and friends' homes first.

## Step 2: Register and get insured

- LLC and EIN
- Local business license
- General liability insurance (essential)
- Check local rules on chemical runoff and wastewater

## Step 3: Startup costs

| Setup | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Basic (one machine, surface cleaner, chemicals) | $3,000 – $6,000 |
| Pressure + soft wash skid on trailer | $8,000 – $15,000 |
| Full truck-mounted rig with tank | $15,000 – $30,000+ |

## Step 4: Set your prices

| Service | Common pricing |
|---|---|
| Driveways and patios | Per square foot |
| House washing | By house size or square footage |
| Roof soft washing | By square footage and pitch |
| Decks and fences | Per square foot or per job |
| Commercial / storefront | Recurring monthly price |

Set a minimum job price so small jobs are worth the drive.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a pressure washing business?
Learn the trade, register and insure the business, buy equipment and market with before-and-after photos.

### How much does it cost to start a pressure washing business?
Roughly $3,000 to $15,000, depending on your equipment.

### Do I need a license to pressure wash?
Usually just a general business license, but check local rules on chemicals and wastewater.

### What PSI do I need for pressure washing?
Around 3,000–4,000 PSI with 4+ GPM for concrete; houses and roofs need low-pressure soft washing.

### Can I start pressure washing part-time?
Yes. Many people start on weekends and go full time as bookings grow.
