<p class="lede">A <strong>marketing agency</strong> is a company that plans and runs marketing for other businesses: <strong>strategy, branding, content, SEO, paid advertising, social media, email, creative production and analytics</strong>. Businesses hire agencies to access <strong>specialist skills, tools and capacity</strong> without building a full in-house team, and to grow leads, sales and brand awareness.</p>

## What marketing agencies do

| Area | Examples |
|---|---|
| Strategy | Market research, positioning, campaign planning |
| Branding and creative | Logos, messaging, ads, video, motion graphics (see [What are motion graphics?](/blog/what-are-motion-graphics/)) |
| SEO and content | Website optimisation, articles, link building |
| Paid media | Google, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn ads |
| Social media | Content, community management, influencers |
| Email and automation | Newsletters, nurture flows |
| Web and CRO | Websites, landing pages, conversion optimisation |
| Analytics | Tracking, dashboards, reporting |

## Types of marketing agencies

Full-service, digital marketing, SEO, PPC, social media, content, creative/advertising, PR, branding, performance marketing, B2B, niche-specific agencies, and AI/automation agencies.

## How agencies charge

Monthly retainers, project fees, hourly rates, percentage of ad spend, or performance-based fees.

## Agency vs in-house vs freelancer

| | Agency | In-house | Freelancer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Skills | Broad team | Deep company knowledge | Specialist |
| Cost | Retainer | Salaries + tools | Lower, flexible |
| Capacity | Scalable | Fixed | Limited |
| Speed to start | Fast | Slow to hire | Fast |

## Are marketing agencies worth it?

They can be when you choose one with relevant results, clear goals and transparent reporting, and when the work is tied to measurable outcomes like leads and revenue.

## How to choose a marketing agency

Relevant case studies, niche experience, clear process and reporting, who will do the work, contract terms, cultural fit and references. (Starting your own: [How to start a marketing agency](/blog/how-to-start-a-marketing-agency/).)

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a marketing agency?
A company that plans and executes marketing for other businesses, from strategy and creative to SEO, ads, social media and analytics.

### What do marketing agencies do?
Research, strategy, branding, content, SEO, paid ads, social media, email, websites and reporting.

### Are marketing agencies worth it?
Often, if they have relevant experience, set clear goals and report transparently on business outcomes.

### How much does a marketing agency cost?
It varies widely by scope and services, from small monthly retainers to large contracts.

### What is the difference between a marketing agency and an advertising agency?
Advertising agencies focus on creating and placing ads; marketing agencies cover broader strategy and channels.

### How do I choose the right marketing agency?
Look for relevant results, transparent processes and reporting, clear contracts and good communication.
