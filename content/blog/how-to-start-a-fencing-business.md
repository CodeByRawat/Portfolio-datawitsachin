<p class="lede">To start a fencing business, <strong>learn fence installation</strong> (work for a fence company first), choose the <strong>fence types</strong> you'll install, get a <strong>contractor license where required and liability insurance</strong>, buy or rent <strong>post-hole equipment</strong>, open <strong>supplier accounts</strong>, and win jobs from <strong>homeowners, builders and businesses</strong>. Startup costs are often <strong>$15,000 to $50,000</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Get experience

Work for a fence company to learn layout, post setting, stretching chain link, building wood fences, gates and dealing with slopes and rock.

## Step 2: Choose fence types

| Type | Notes |
|---|---|
| Wood | Popular, many styles |
| Vinyl | Low maintenance, higher price |
| Chain link | Residential and commercial |
| Aluminium / ornamental | Pools and front yards |
| Farm and ranch | Wire, split rail, livestock |
| Commercial / security | Larger jobs, specialised |
| Gates and automatic openers | High-value add-on |

## Step 3: Licenses and insurance

LLC, EIN, business license, contractor license where your state requires it, general liability, workers' comp and commercial auto.

## Step 4: Equipment and startup costs

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Truck and trailer | $20,000 – $50,000 (used) |
| Post-hole digger or auger | $500 – $5,000 |
| Skid steer or mini auger | Rent at first |
| Saws, nail guns, levels, string lines | $1,000 – $3,000 |
| Post driver, concrete mixer | $500 – $2,000 |
| Insurance and licenses | $3,000 – $8,000 per year |

## Step 5: Suppliers

Open accounts with fence distributors for contractor pricing and delivery to job sites.

## Step 6: Price per linear foot

Include materials, labour, gates, removal of old fence, permits, terrain difficulty and concrete. Set a minimum job price.

## Step 7: Permits and utility locates

Always call 811 before digging. Check property lines, HOA rules, height limits and pool fence codes.

## Step 8: Find customers

1. Google Business Profile and reviews
2. A website with photos and free estimate booking
3. Builders, landscapers and pool companies
4. Realtors and property managers
5. Yard signs on finished jobs

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a fencing business?
Learn installation, get licensed and insured, buy equipment, open supplier accounts and market to homeowners.

### How much does it cost to start a fencing company?
Often $15,000 to $50,000, mostly for a truck, trailer and equipment.

### Do I need a license to install fences?
In some states yes; all businesses need a local business license.

### How do fence companies price jobs?
Usually per linear foot, plus gates, removal and difficult terrain.

### Is fencing a good business?
Yes. Demand is steady and jobs have good margins.
