<p class="lede">To buy a vending machine, first <strong>secure or identify the location</strong>, then choose the <strong>machine type</strong> that fits it, decide between <strong>new, used or refurbished</strong>, make sure it supports <strong>card payments</strong>, inspect used machines carefully, and plan <strong>delivery, installation and stocking</strong>.</p>

## Choose the type

| Type | Best for |
|---|---|
| Snack | Offices, schools |
| Drink | Gyms, warehouses, outdoor areas |
| Combo | Small locations |
| Healthy | Gyms, hospitals |
| Micro market | Large workplaces |

## New vs used vs refurbished

| | New | Used | Refurbished |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | Highest | Lowest | Middle |
| Warranty | Yes | Rarely | Often limited |
| Technology | Latest | May be outdated | Updated parts |
| Risk | Low | Higher | Medium |

## Where to buy

Manufacturers and authorised distributors, refurbishing companies, local operators selling machines, business marketplaces and auctions.

## What to check on a used machine

- Refrigeration works and holds temperature
- Coin mech, bill validator and card reader function
- Spirals and motors turn properly
- Lights, display and locks work
- Keys included
- Model is still supported for parts

## Add-ons

Cashless card readers, telemetry for remote sales tracking, and security features.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I purchase a vending machine?
Choose the type for your location, decide new vs used, check card payments, inspect it and arrange delivery.

### Should I buy a new or used vending machine?
New is more reliable; used is cheaper but riskier.

### Where can I buy vending machines?
Distributors, refurbishers, local operators and online marketplaces.

### Should I buy the machine before the location?
Ideally secure the location first so the machine fits it.

### Do vending machines need card readers?
They're optional but usually increase sales.

### Is investing in vending machines worth it?
It can be, if machines are placed in strong locations.
