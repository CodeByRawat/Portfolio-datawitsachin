<p class="lede">To price landscaping jobs profitably, calculate your <strong>true cost per labour hour</strong> (wages, payroll taxes, insurance), add <strong>equipment and overhead</strong> costs per hour, estimate <strong>time and materials</strong> accurately for each job, apply a <strong>materials markup</strong>, then add your <strong>profit margin</strong>. Price recurring maintenance by <strong>property size, frequency and complexity</strong>, and price projects with detailed line items in a clear written bid.</p>

## Step 1: Know your costs

| Cost | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Labour** | Wages + payroll taxes + workers' comp + benefits |
| **Equipment** | Mowers, trimmers, trucks: depreciation, fuel, maintenance |
| **Overhead** | Insurance, software, office, marketing, owner admin time |
| **Materials** | Mulch, plants, stone, soil, irrigation parts |

**Billable rate per labour hour** = (labour cost + equipment cost + overhead per hour) ÷ (1 − desired profit margin).

Example: labour $25/hr + equipment $8/hr + overhead $12/hr = $45/hr cost. For a 20% margin: $45 ÷ 0.8 = **$56.25/hr**.

## Step 2: Estimate time accurately

Measure the property (online maps help), count obstacles, slopes and edging, and use your crew's actual production rates. Include travel and setup time.

## Step 3: Materials

Quantity × cost + markup (commonly 15–50% depending on market and handling), plus delivery and disposal fees.

## Pricing maintenance

- Per visit based on property size and time
- Seasonal or annual contracts (often monthly instalments)
- Adjust for frequency (weekly vs biweekly), complexity and extras

## Pricing projects

Line items for demolition, prep, materials, installation labour, equipment rental, disposal, plus contingency for unknowns.

## Writing a winning bid

- Scope of work with clear inclusions/exclusions
- Timeline
- Materials specifications
- Price breakdown or packages (good/better/best)
- Payment terms (deposit, milestones)
- Warranty and insurance details
- Photos or design sketches
Send fast and follow up (see [How to send an invoice](/blog/how-to-send-an-invoice/) for invoicing after the job).

## Estimating software

Field service and landscaping software can measure properties, store production rates, generate professional quotes and convert them into jobs and invoices (see [What is field service management software?](/blog/what-is-field-service-management-software/)).

## Common pricing mistakes

- Pricing by competitors instead of your costs
- Forgetting overhead and drive time
- Underestimating job time
- No price increases each year
- Not charging for disposal, permits or difficult access

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I price landscaping jobs?
Calculate your cost per labour hour including equipment and overhead, estimate time and materials, add materials markup and your profit margin.

### How do I bid on landscaping jobs?
Measure the property, estimate time and materials, price with your rates and margin, and send a clear written bid with scope, timeline and terms.

### What is a good profit margin for landscaping?
It varies; many aim for healthy double-digit net margins, with projects typically earning more than basic maintenance.

### How do I price lawn mowing?
By property size, time per visit, frequency and difficulty, using your hourly billable rate.

### Should I charge by the hour or by the job?
Most clients prefer job pricing; calculate it from your hourly costs so every job stays profitable.

### How often should landscapers raise prices?
Review annually and adjust for wage, fuel, insurance and material cost increases.
