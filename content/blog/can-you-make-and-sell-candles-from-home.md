<p class="lede">Yes, in most places you can make and sell candles from home. You'll usually need a <strong>business licence</strong>, possibly a <strong>home occupation permit</strong>, a <strong>sales tax permit</strong>, <strong>safety labels</strong> on every candle, and ideally <strong>product liability insurance</strong>. Keep a <strong>safe workspace</strong> and follow <strong>shipping rules</strong> for selling online.</p>

Rules vary by city, state and country. Check local requirements.

## Requirements checklist

| Requirement | Why |
|---|---|
| Business licence | Legally operate |
| Home occupation permit | Some cities require it |
| Sales tax permit | Collect tax on sales |
| Safety labels | Warn customers; often required |
| Product liability insurance | Protects you if a candle causes damage |
| HOA rules | Some restrict home businesses |

## Safety labelling

Include burn instructions and warnings: never leave unattended, keep away from children, pets and flammables, trim the wick, and burn on a heat-safe surface.

## Safe home workspace

- Melt wax with a double boiler or wax melter, never over direct high heat
- Keep a fire extinguisher nearby
- Ventilate the space
- Store fragrance oils safely
- Keep pets and children away from hot wax

## How to make candles at home to sell

1. Choose wax, wicks, fragrance and containers
2. Melt and add fragrance at the right temperature
3. Pour, set wicks and cure (often 1–2 weeks for soy)
4. Test burn each recipe
5. Label, package and photograph

## Shipping

Use sturdy packaging and protect glass jars. Check carrier rules for heat in summer.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I make and sell candles from home?
Yes, in most places with a business licence and proper labels.

### Do I need a licence to sell homemade candles?
Usually a business licence and sales tax permit.

### Do I need insurance to sell candles?
It's strongly recommended; many markets require it.

### How long should candles cure before selling?
Often 1–2 weeks for soy candles.

### Can I sell candles on Etsy from home?
Yes, following tax and labelling rules.
