<p class="lede">To compare garage door installation quotes, make sure each one quotes the <strong>same door</strong> (size, material, insulation R-value, windows), includes <strong>new springs, tracks, hardware and weather seals</strong>, states whether <strong>removal and disposal</strong> of the old door is included, details the <strong>opener</strong> (drive type, horsepower, smart features), and lists <strong>warranties</strong> on parts and labour. Then check <strong>reviews, licensing and insurance</strong>.</p>

## Compare these line by line

| Item | What to check |
|---|---|
| Door model | Brand, size, style |
| Material | Steel gauge, wood, aluminium, composite |
| Insulation | Polystyrene vs polyurethane, R-value |
| Windows and design | Same options in each quote |
| Springs | Torsion springs, cycle rating |
| Tracks and hardware | New or reused |
| Weather seals | Bottom seal and perimeter |
| Removal and disposal | Included or extra |
| Opener | Chain, belt, wall-mount; horsepower; Wi-Fi |
| Permits | If required locally |
| Warranty | Door, springs, opener and labour |

## Garage door opener installation quotes

Compare the drive type (belt is quieter), horsepower for your door's weight, battery backup, smart features, remotes and keypads, and whether old opener removal is included.

## Common mistakes

- Comparing an uninsulated door to an insulated one
- Missing extra costs for springs, tracks or removal
- Ignoring labour warranty
- Choosing the lowest price from an uninsured installer

## Questions to ask

1. Is everything in the quote new?
2. What's the spring cycle rating?
3. How long is the labour warranty?
4. Are you licensed and insured?
5. How long will installation take?

## New construction timing

On new homes, garage doors are usually installed after the garage is framed, roofed and the opening is finished, before final interior work, so the space can be secured.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I compare garage door installation quotes?
Make sure each quote has the same door, insulation, springs, hardware, removal and warranty.

### What should a garage door quote include?
Door details, springs, tracks, seals, removal, opener (if any), permits and warranty.

### How do I compare garage door opener quotes?
Compare drive type, horsepower, features, removal and warranty.

### When is a garage door installed in new construction?
After framing, roofing and finishing the opening, before final interior work.

### Should I choose the cheapest quote?
Not if it leaves out parts, removal or warranty, or the installer isn't insured.
