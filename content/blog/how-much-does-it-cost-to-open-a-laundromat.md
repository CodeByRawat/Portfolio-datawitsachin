<p class="lede">Opening a laundromat is <strong>capital-intensive</strong>: the biggest costs are <strong>commercial washers and dryers</strong>, <strong>build-out (plumbing, venting, electrical, water heating)</strong>, and <strong>lease deposits</strong>. Buying an existing laundromat costs a <strong>multiple of its cash flow</strong> plus equipment value. Ongoing costs are mainly <strong>rent, water, gas, electricity, repairs and staff</strong>.</p>

## Startup cost breakdown (new build)

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| Washers | Several sizes; the largest equipment expense |
| Dryers | Stacked and large capacity |
| Water heater/boiler | Sized to washer count |
| Plumbing, drains and venting | Often a major construction cost |
| Electrical and HVAC | Depends on the space |
| Payment system | Coin, card or app |
| Flooring, lighting, seating, carts, tables | Customer comfort |
| Signage and security cameras | |
| Lease deposit and rent during build-out | |
| Permits, legal and design | |
| Working capital | Cover the ramp-up period |

## Buying an existing laundromat

Price usually depends on **net cash flow**, **lease length and terms**, **equipment age**, and **location**. Older equipment means lower prices but upcoming replacement costs. See [How to buy a laundromat business](/blog/how-to-buy-a-laundromat-business/).

## Ongoing monthly costs

- Rent and common area charges
- Water and sewer
- Gas (water heating and dryers)
- Electricity
- Repairs and parts
- Attendant wages
- Insurance, software, marketing

## Ways to reduce startup cost

- Buy an existing store with good equipment
- Use distributor equipment financing
- Negotiate tenant improvement allowances
- Start self-service and add wash-and-fold later
- Choose energy-efficient machines to lower utilities

## Financing options

Equipment financing, SBA loans, conventional bank loans, seller financing and partners/investors.

Is it worth it? Read [Is a laundromat a good investment?](/blog/is-a-laundromat-a-good-investment/) and [How much can a laundromat make?](/blog/how-much-can-a-laundromat-make/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does it cost to open a laundromat?
It's a significant investment driven by equipment, build-out and location; get quotes from distributors and contractors for your space.

### How much does it cost to own a laundromat each month?
Rent, utilities, repairs, wages and insurance are the main ongoing costs.

### Is it cheaper to buy or build a laundromat?
Buying an older store can be cheaper upfront but may need equipment replacement soon.

### Can you finance laundromat equipment?
Yes, equipment financing and SBA loans are common.

### What is the biggest expense in a laundromat?
Equipment upfront; rent and utilities ongoing.

### How long does it take to open a new laundromat?
Several months for lease, permits, build-out and installation.
