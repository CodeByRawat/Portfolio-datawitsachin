<p class="lede">An <strong>AI assistant for business</strong> (sometimes sold as an AI executive assistant or AI secretary) uses large language models to handle the administrative work that eats a professional's day: triaging email, drafting replies, scheduling, taking meeting notes, preparing research briefs, updating systems and chasing follow-ups. The best results come from connecting the assistant to your email, calendar and tools, running it in "draft mode" first, and expanding its autonomy only where it has proven reliable.</p>

AI assistants have moved from novelty to everyday tool. Thomson Reuters' 2025 Future of Professionals research found professionals expect AI to save around five hours a week within a year, and adoption across knowledge work is high. But many people still use AI as a chat window rather than a real assistant. This guide explains what an AI assistant can do well today, what it still can't, the step from chatbot to "AI employee", how to choose and set one up, how to keep data safe, and how to measure the return.

## What an AI executive assistant or AI secretary can do well

### Email
- **Triage:** sort incoming mail by priority and type; surface what needs you today
- **Summarise** long threads in a few lines
- **Draft replies in your voice,** using context from the thread and your past emails
- **Follow up** on emails that haven't received replies
- **Unsubscribe and declutter** suggestions

### Scheduling
- Propose meeting times based on your calendar and preferences
- Handle the back-and-forth with external contacts
- Add agendas, context and documents to invites
- Protect focus time and flag conflicts

### Meetings
- Transcribe and summarise calls (Zoom, Teams, Google Meet)
- Extract decisions and action items with owners and dates
- Push tasks into your task tool and notes into your CRM
- Draft the follow-up email

### Research
- Compile briefings on companies, people or topics with sources
- Prepare you before meetings (who's attending, their company, recent news, your history together)
- Summarise reports, articles and industry updates

### Admin and operations
- Fill forms, update the CRM, prepare reports, chase invoices
- Draft documents, proposals and presentations from notes
- Organise files and notes
- Manage expenses and receipts

(For the full method of automating tasks, see [How to automate tasks with AI](/blog/how-to-automate-tasks-with-ai/).)

## What it still can't do (well)

- **Judgement on sensitive relationships:** delicate negotiations, difficult conversations, politics
- **Guaranteed accuracy:** it can misread context, invent details or make confident mistakes, so review what matters
- **Read your mind:** without context, preferences and examples, outputs are generic
- **Act safely without guardrails:** an assistant with access to email and accounts can be manipulated by malicious content (prompt injection) or make costly mistakes. (See [Is ChatGPT agent mode safe?](/blog/is-chatgpt-agent-mode-safe/))
- **Take accountability:** you remain responsible for what's sent in your name

## From chatbot to "AI employee"

There's a spectrum of AI assistance:

| Level | What it is | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Chat assistant** | You ask, it answers | "Draft a reply to this email" |
| **2. Connected assistant** | Has access to your email, calendar, files and apps; still waits for you | "What's on my plate today, and draft replies to the urgent emails" |
| **3. Workflow automation** | Runs automatically on triggers, with AI steps | Every inbound lead is researched, scored and logged in the CRM |
| **4. Agent / "AI employee"** | Pursues goals across tools with limited autonomy and approvals | "Handle scheduling for all external meetings and chase anyone who hasn't confirmed" |

Vendors increasingly market "AI employees" or "digital workers". Some are genuinely useful agents; many are rebranded chatbots ("agent washing"). Judge by what it can actually do in your systems, how it handles errors, and what it requires you to approve. (Background: [What is an AI agent?](/blog/what-is-an-ai-agent/))

## How to automate tasks with AI: a simple sequence

1. **Write down your ten most repetitive tasks** and how long they take each week.
2. **Choose one with clear inputs and a clear "done" state** (e.g., "every meeting has notes and actions in Asana within an hour").
3. **Connect the AI to the source and the destination** (email to CRM, form to spreadsheet, call to notes).
4. **Run it in draft mode,** where it prepares and you approve.
5. **Measure accuracy and time saved** for two to four weeks.
6. **Remove the approval step only for the categories where it's been consistently right.**
7. **Move to the next task.**

## Choosing an AI assistant

| Type | Examples | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **General AI assistants (business tiers)** | Claude for Work, ChatGPT Business/Enterprise, Gemini for Workspace | Drafting, research, analysis, with connectors to email, calendar and drive |
| **Built into your productivity suite** | Microsoft 365 Copilot, Gemini in Gmail/Docs | Organisations standardised on Microsoft or Google |
| **Meeting assistants** | Fathom, Otter, Fireflies, Granola, built-in Zoom/Teams/Meet AI | Notes, actions and CRM updates from calls |
| **Email and scheduling assistants** | Superhuman, Reclaim, Motion, Calendly AI features | Inbox speed and calendar management |
| **Automation platforms with AI** | n8n, Make, Zapier, Power Automate | Custom workflows across your tools (see [No-code vs low-code automation](/blog/no-code-vs-low-code-automation/)) |
| **Custom assistants** | Built on model APIs with your data and tools | Specific business processes, private data, deep integrations |

### What to prioritise for business use
- **Data handling:** where does your data go? Is it used for training? What are retention and residency terms?
- **Integrations** with your stack (email, calendar, CRM, documents, chat)
- **Admin controls:** user management, permissions, audit logs, SSO
- **Quality on your tasks:** test two or three options on real work
- **Cost:** per-user licences vs usage-based pricing

If your needs are specific, a custom assistant wired into your own systems tends to beat an off-the-shelf one.

## Setting up an AI assistant that actually helps

1. **Give it context:** a short document about you, your role, your priorities, writing style, key contacts and recurring tasks.
2. **Provide examples:** good emails you've written, report formats you like.
3. **Connect the essentials:** email, calendar, documents, task tool, CRM.
4. **Create reusable instructions** (projects, custom GPTs/Gems, saved prompts) for recurring work.
5. **Define boundaries:** what it may do without asking, and what always needs approval.
6. **Review weekly:** what worked, what didn't, what to automate next.

## Keeping data safe

- Use **business or enterprise plans** with appropriate data terms, not personal free accounts, for company data
- **Limit access** to the accounts and folders the assistant actually needs
- **Approve external actions** (emails, bookings, payments)
- **Treat content from outside** (emails, web pages, documents) as untrusted, especially when the assistant can act
- **Follow your organisation's AI policy** and data protection obligations

## Measuring the return

Track for each automated task: **minutes saved per occurrence × occurrences per month**, minus review time, compared with tool costs. Also track quality (errors caught), speed (response times to clients) and what you did with the time saved. A typical professional who automates email triage, meeting notes and research briefs can realistically save several hours a week.

## Can AI replace an executive assistant?

For a large share of **routine** tasks (scheduling, notes, first drafts, research), yes. For **relationship and judgement** work (managing stakeholders, prioritising in ambiguous situations, handling sensitive matters, representing an executive), human assistants remain valuable, and the best executive assistants are using AI to take on more strategic responsibilities.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best AI assistant for productivity?
For personal use, any major assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot) is capable. For a business, prioritise data handling, integrations with your stack and admin controls, and test a few options on your real tasks. Specialist tools often win for meetings, email and scheduling.

### Can an AI replace an executive assistant or secretary?
It can take over a large share of routine work such as scheduling, notes, drafting and research, but judgement, discretion and relationship management still favour a human. Many teams pair a human assistant with AI tools.

### How do I automate tasks with AI?
Pick a repetitive task with clear inputs and a clear done state, connect an AI tool or automation platform to where the task starts and ends, run it with your approval at first, and remove the approval once it has proven reliable.

### What is an AI secretary?
An AI secretary is an AI assistant that handles administrative tasks such as answering and triaging emails, scheduling meetings, taking notes, answering calls or messages, and reminding you of follow-ups.

### Is it safe to give an AI assistant access to my email?
It can be, with a reputable business-grade tool, minimum necessary access, human approval for outgoing messages and other actions, and awareness that malicious content in emails could try to manipulate the assistant.

### How much time can an AI assistant save?
It depends on your work, but professionals commonly report saving several hours per week by automating email triage, meeting notes, scheduling and research.
