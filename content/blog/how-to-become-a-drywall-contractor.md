<p class="lede">To become a drywall contractor, <strong>start as a drywall helper or apprentice</strong>, learn to <strong>hang, tape and finish</strong> to a high standard, get your <strong>contractor license (if your state requires one)</strong>, buy <strong>insurance</strong>, and start <strong>bidding small jobs</strong> for homeowners, remodelers and builders.</p>

## Step 1: Start as a helper

Look for helper or labourer jobs with drywall companies or general contractors. You'll carry boards, cut, hang and clean up while learning.

## Step 2: Learn the skills

- Measuring and cutting
- Hanging walls and ceilings
- Taping, mudding and finishing levels (1–5)
- Corner bead and texture
- Repairs and patching
- Reading plans
- Estimating

## Step 3: Apprenticeship and training

Union carpenter or drywall finisher apprenticeships, trade schools and manufacturer training all help. Apprenticeships pay while you learn.

## Step 4: Get licensed

Many states require a contractor license for jobs over a certain amount. This may include an exam, proof of experience and a bond. Check your state's rules.

## Step 5: Insurance and business setup

LLC, EIN, general liability, workers' comp (if you hire) and commercial auto.

## Step 6: Get your first jobs

Start with repairs, basements and small remodels. Work for remodelers and handymen who don't do drywall. Take photos and collect reviews.

## Career path

| Stage | Role |
|---|---|
| Helper | Carrying, cutting, cleanup |
| Hanger or finisher | Skilled installer |
| Lead / foreman | Runs jobs and crews |
| Contractor | Bids and owns the business |

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I become a drywall contractor?
Learn the trade as a helper or apprentice, get licensed if required, insure your business and bid jobs.

### Do you need a license to be a drywall contractor?
Many states require one above a job-size limit; check locally.

### How long does it take to become a drywall contractor?
Usually a few years of hands-on experience.

### Can I do drywall without experience?
You can start as a helper; running your own jobs needs experience.

### How do drywall contractors find work?
Builders, remodelers, Google and referrals.
