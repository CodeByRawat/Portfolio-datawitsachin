<p class="lede">To sell online, <strong>decide what you're selling</strong> (used items, products you make or source, digital products or services), <strong>choose where to sell</strong> (marketplaces like Amazon, eBay, Etsy and Facebook Marketplace, social commerce like Instagram and TikTok Shop, or <strong>your own online store</strong>), create <strong>great listings with clear photos and pricing</strong>, set up <strong>payments and shipping</strong>, then <strong>market</strong> your products. Beginners often start on marketplaces; growing businesses add their own website for control and higher margins.</p>

## Where can I sell stuff online?

| Where | Best for | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Facebook Marketplace** | Local, used and bulky items | Free locally, quick | Haggling, no-shows |
| **eBay** | Used, collectibles, electronics, parts | Large audience, auctions | Fees, competition |
| **Amazon** | New products at scale | Massive demand | Fees, strict rules, competition |
| **Etsy** | Handmade, vintage, personalised | Gift buyers | Fees, copycats |
| **Poshmark, Depop, Vinted** | Clothing and fashion resale | Fashion-focused buyers | Fees, shipping |
| **OfferUp, Craigslist, local apps** | Local sales | No shipping | Safety precautions |
| **Instagram / TikTok Shop** | Visual, trending products | Content-driven discovery | Needs regular content |
| **Your own website** | Brands and repeat customers | Control, margins, data | You bring traffic |
| **Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, Etsy digital** | Digital products | No inventory | Need audience |

(Choosing between them: [How to choose the right marketplace for your business](/blog/how-to-choose-the-right-marketplace-for-your-business/).)

## Step 1: Decide what to sell

- **Declutter:** sell used items you own (fastest start)
- **Make:** handmade products, art, crafts
- **Source:** wholesale, private label, resale/retail arbitrage
- **Print-on-demand or dropshipping:** low upfront cost
- **Digital:** templates, courses, presets, ebooks
- **Services:** consulting, design, tutoring sold through your website

## Step 2: Research demand and pricing

Search your product on marketplaces and check **sold** listings (eBay's sold filter), reviews and prices. Factor in fees, shipping and your time.

## Step 3: Create listings that sell

- **Photos:** natural light, clean background, all angles, close-ups of flaws for used items
- **Short videos** showing the product in use (see [How to make a promotional video](/blog/how-to-make-a-promotional-video/))
- **Titles with keywords** buyers search
- **Honest, detailed descriptions:** size, condition, materials, what's included
- **Competitive pricing** and clear shipping costs

## Step 4: Payments and safety

Use platform payments where possible. For local sales, meet in public places, use secure payment methods and beware of overpayment and fake-payment scams.

## Step 5: Shipping

Weigh and measure items, compare carriers, use platform discounted labels, package securely and ship quickly to earn good reviews.

## Step 6: Market your products

- Social media content and short videos
- Email list for repeat buyers
- Paid ads once you know your margins (see [What is the most effective type of advertising?](/blog/what-is-the-most-effective-type-of-advertising/))
- SEO on your own store and product pages
- Bundles, seasonal promotions and reviews

## Selling services online

Service providers sell online by building a website with service pages, pricing, proof and booking (see [How to create a website for your business](/blog/how-to-create-a-website-for-your-business/)), plus content and ads to attract clients.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I sell online?
Choose what to sell, pick a platform (marketplace, social commerce or your own store), create listings with good photos and descriptions, set up payments and shipping, and promote your products.

### Where can I sell stuff online?
Facebook Marketplace, eBay, Amazon, Etsy, Poshmark, Depop, OfferUp, Instagram, TikTok Shop and your own website, depending on what you sell.

### What is the easiest way to sell online?
For used items, Facebook Marketplace and eBay; for handmade goods, Etsy; for building a brand, a Shopify store combined with social media.

### How do I sell stuff online for free?
Facebook Marketplace (local pickup) and some local apps let you list free; most other platforms charge fees on sales.

### What sells best online?
Popular categories include fashion, beauty, home goods, electronics accessories, collectibles and digital products, but success depends on niche, quality and marketing.

### Do I need a website to sell online?
Not to start, since marketplaces and social platforms work, but a website helps you build a brand, own customer relationships and keep more profit.
