<p class="lede">To start a dog grooming business: choose a <strong>model</strong> (storefront salon, mobile grooming van or home-based), check <strong>licences and permits</strong> (business licence, and in some places animal/kennel permits or zoning approval), get <strong>insurance</strong>, buy <strong>equipment</strong>, set <strong>prices by size and coat</strong>, use <strong>online booking with reminders</strong>, write <strong>safety and vaccination policies</strong>, and market to local pet owners through <strong>Google, Instagram before/after posts and vet/pet store partnerships</strong>.</p>

## Choose a model

| Model | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| Storefront salon | Capacity, walk-ins, team | Rent and build-out |
| Mobile van | Premium pricing, convenience | Van cost, fewer dogs/day |
| Home-based | Low cost | Zoning and space limits |

## Do you need a licence to groom dogs?

Usually a **business licence**; some cities/states require **animal care, kennel or grooming facility permits**, and home-based businesses may need zoning approval. Individual groomer licensing is uncommon but check local rules.

## Insurance

General liability, care custody and control (covers animals in your care), property, commercial auto for mobile, workers' comp with employees.

## Equipment and costs

Tub, table, dryers, clippers, blades, scissors, brushes, nail tools, sanitation, kennels; for mobile, a converted van with water and power. Costs range from modest (home) to high (mobile van or salon build-out).

## Pricing

Price by size, breed, coat condition, service level and time; add-ons (de-matting, teeth, nails) and fees for behaviour or matting.

## Booking and policies

Online booking, deposits, automatic reminders (see [How to make a booking website](/blog/how-to-make-a-booking-website/)); vaccination records, matting/shave-down policy, late/no-show policy, aggressive dog policy.

## Marketing

Google Business Profile and reviews, Instagram/TikTok before-and-after Reels, partnerships with vets, trainers, pet stores and doggy daycares, referral rewards, rebooking at checkout.

## Grow

Hire groomers and bathers, add services, open a second van or location.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a dog grooming business?
Choose salon, mobile or home-based, check licences and permits, get insurance and equipment, set prices, use online booking and market locally.

### Do you need a license for dog grooming?
Often a business licence; some areas require animal facility permits or zoning approval.

### How much does it cost to start a dog grooming business?
From a few thousand dollars for home-based setups to much more for mobile vans or salons.

### Is mobile dog grooming profitable?
It can be due to premium pricing and loyal clients, but van and fuel costs must be managed.

### How do groomers get clients?
Google reviews, social media before/afters, vet and pet store partnerships, referrals and rebooking.

### What insurance does a dog groomer need?
General liability with care, custody and control coverage, plus property and commercial auto if mobile.
