<p class="lede">Tutoring rates vary widely. Many private tutors charge roughly <strong>$25–$80 per hour</strong> for general K–12 subjects, while <strong>specialised subjects, advanced levels and test prep</strong> (SAT, ACT, GRE, professional exams) often command <strong>$80–$150+ per hour</strong>. Location, experience, credentials, online vs in-person delivery and package structure all affect what tutors charge and what tutoring costs families.</p>

## Factors affecting tutoring rates

| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| Subject and level | Advanced maths, sciences and test prep cost more |
| Experience and credentials | Certified teachers and proven results charge more |
| Location | Higher-cost cities pay more |
| Format | In-home may include travel premium; online widens competition |
| Session size | Group sessions cost less per student |
| Results and demand | Strong track record supports higher rates |

## Pricing models

- **Hourly:** simple, flexible
- **Packages:** e.g., 10 sessions with diagnostic and progress reports (discounted per hour, better commitment)
- **Monthly memberships:** weekly sessions billed monthly
- **Group classes:** lower price per student, higher total per hour
- **Test prep programmes:** fixed-price courses

## How to set your tutoring prices

1. Research local and online rates for your subject/level
2. Factor preparation time, travel and tools
3. Decide your target hourly income
4. Create packages with clear value (assessment, plans, reports)
5. Raise rates as experience and demand grow

## How much does a math tutor cost?

Math tutoring often ranges from moderate rates for elementary levels to higher rates for calculus and exam prep; online marketplaces and tutoring companies vary in pricing and take fees from tutors.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much should I charge for tutoring?
Research local and online rates for your subject and level, factor your experience and prep time, and consider packages; many general tutors charge around $25–$80 per hour, with specialised tutoring higher.

### How much do tutors charge per hour?
Commonly $25–$80 for general subjects and $80–$150+ for specialised subjects and test prep, varying by location.

### How much does a math tutor cost?
It depends on level and location; advanced maths and test prep usually cost more than elementary maths.

### Should tutors charge per session or per package?
Packages encourage commitment and results, while hourly pricing offers flexibility.

### Do online tutors charge less?
Sometimes, due to wider competition and no travel, but expert online tutors can charge premium rates.

Platform pay varies and changes; check the platform's current tutor pay information.
