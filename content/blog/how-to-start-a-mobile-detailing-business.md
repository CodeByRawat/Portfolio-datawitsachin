<p class="lede">Mobile detailing means bringing the detail to the customer's home or office. To start: set up a <strong>vehicle with a water tank, pressure washer, generator or inverter, hoses and storage</strong>, buy <strong>equipment and products</strong>, get <strong>liability and garage keepers insurance</strong>, set <strong>service areas and pricing</strong> that cover travel time, use <strong>online booking and route planning</strong>, and market with <strong>before/after content and reviews</strong>.</p>

## Rig setup

| Item | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Van, truck or trailer | Transport and storage |
| Water tank | Water when customers can't provide it |
| Pressure washer + hose reel | Washing |
| Generator or inverter/battery | Power for vacuums, polishers |
| Vacuum, extractor, steamer | Interior work |
| Shelving and organisers | Efficiency |

## Pricing

Price by vehicle size, condition and service; add travel fees outside your core area; set minimums; sell maintenance plans.

## Routing and booking

Online booking with deposits, service area limits, clustered appointments and automatic reminders reduce drive time and no-shows.

## How much can mobile detailers make?

Income depends on jobs per day, average ticket and high-value services such as paint correction and ceramic coatings; adding a second van or technician scales income.

## Is mobile detailing a good business?

Yes for many: low overhead (no shop rent), convenience customers pay for, and high-margin services. Challenges include weather, water rules and physical work.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a mobile detailing business?
Set up a vehicle rig, buy equipment, get insured, set pricing and service areas, use online booking and market locally.

### How much does it cost to start a mobile detailing business?
Costs depend on vehicle, water tank, power and equipment; you can start lean and upgrade.

### Is mobile detailing a good business to start?
Yes, thanks to low overhead and high-margin services, though weather and physical demands are challenges.

### How much can you make mobile detailing?
It depends on jobs per day, pricing and premium services like ceramic coating.

### Do mobile detailers need their own water?
Many carry water tanks for independence; some use customers' water with permission.

### How do mobile detailers find customers?
Social media before/afters, Google reviews, referrals and partnerships with offices and dealerships.
