<p class="lede">To start an insulation business, choose your services (<strong>blown-in, batt, spray foam, removal or air sealing</strong>), get <strong>training</strong>, buy equipment (an <strong>insulation blowing machine</strong> is a low-cost start), get <strong>licensed and insured</strong>, and find customers among <strong>homeowners, builders and energy-efficiency programs</strong>. Blown-in and batt businesses can start for <strong>$15,000 to $50,000</strong>; spray foam costs much more.</p>

## Step 1: Choose your services

| Service | Startup cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Blown-in (cellulose, fibreglass) | Low | Attics, retrofit walls |
| Batt and roll | Low | New construction |
| Spray foam | High | Premium, skilled |
| Insulation removal | Low–medium | Old or damaged insulation |
| Air sealing and energy audits | Low–medium | Pairs with rebates |

## Step 2: Training

Learn building science, R-values, ventilation, air sealing, safety and manufacturer installation rules. Energy auditor certifications help with rebate programs.

## Step 3: Equipment and costs

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Insulation blowing machine | $5,000 – $25,000 |
| Removal vacuum | $5,000 – $20,000 |
| Box truck or trailer | $15,000 – $40,000 (used) |
| Ladders, tools, PPE | $1,000 – $3,000 |
| Insurance and licenses | $3,000 – $8,000 per year |

## Step 4: Licenses and insurance

LLC, EIN, business license, contractor license where required, general liability and workers' comp. See [Do you need a license to install insulation?](/blog/do-you-need-a-license-to-install-insulation/).

## Step 5: Pricing

Price per square foot by type and R-value, plus prep, air sealing and removal. Mention available utility rebates and tax credits to help close jobs.

## Step 6: Get customers

1. Home builders (new construction)
2. Homeowners via Google, a website and energy rebate programs
3. HVAC and roofing companies (referrals)
4. Home energy auditors
5. Property managers

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start an insulation business?
Choose your services, get trained, buy a blowing machine and truck, get licensed and insured, and market to homeowners and builders.

### How much does it cost to start an insulation business?
Often $15,000 to $50,000 for blown-in and batt; spray foam costs more.

### Is insulation a good business?
Yes. Energy efficiency demand and rebates keep it steady.

### What insulation business is cheapest to start?
Blown-in attic insulation.

### Do insulation companies need a license?
Often a business license and, in many states, a contractor license.
