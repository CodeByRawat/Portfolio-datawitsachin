<p class="lede"><strong>Inbound sales</strong> is selling to <strong>prospects who reach out to you first or show interest</strong>, for example by filling in a website form, booking a demo, downloading a guide, starting a trial, messaging on WhatsApp or calling after seeing your content. Instead of cold outreach, the salesperson <strong>responds quickly, understands the buyer's needs and advises them</strong> toward the right solution. It pairs with inbound marketing, which generates the interest.</p>

## Inbound vs outbound sales

| | Inbound sales | Outbound sales |
|---|---|---|
| **Who starts** | Prospect | Salesperson |
| **Lead source** | Website, content, ads, referrals, events | Lists, cold email, calls, LinkedIn |
| **Buyer awareness** | Already aware of the problem and you | Often unaware |
| **Conversion rates** | Typically higher | Typically lower |
| **Key skill** | Speed, consultative advice | Prospecting, persistence |

(Outbound explained: [What is sales prospecting?](/blog/what-is-sales-prospecting/))

## The inbound sales process

A common framework (HubSpot's inbound sales methodology):

1. **Identify:** prioritise leads that fit your ideal customer and show buying signals (pricing page visits, demo requests)
2. **Connect:** reach out quickly, personalised to what they did ("You downloaded our checklist on X…")
3. **Explore:** discovery conversation about goals, challenges, timeline and decision process
4. **Advise:** recommend the right solution, tailored proposal, handle objections, close

## Speed to lead: the biggest win

The faster you respond, the more likely you are to connect and win. Many inbound leads contact several providers and choose the first helpful response. Aim to respond within **minutes during business hours**, with automatic instant acknowledgements otherwise.

## Lead qualification

**Lead qualification** decides whether a lead is worth sales time now. Use:
- **Fit:** company size, industry, location, budget range
- **Intent:** actions taken (pricing page, demo request vs ebook download)
- **Frameworks:** BANT (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline), CHAMP, MEDDIC
- **Lead scoring** in your CRM combining fit and behaviour

Route unqualified leads to nurturing email sequences instead of discarding them.

## Inbound sales call outline

1. Thank them and reference their action
2. Ask what prompted them to reach out now
3. Explore goals, current situation and challenges
4. Understand decision process and timeline
5. Share relevant examples and how you'd help
6. Agree clear next steps (proposal, demo, trial)

## How to convert more inbound leads into sales

- **Respond instantly** (automation + alerts)
- **Make booking easy:** calendar links on the website and in emails
- **Personalise** using form answers and website behaviour
- **Follow up persistently** (multiple touches over days)
- **Use proof:** case studies relevant to their industry
- **Nurture** not-yet-ready leads with useful content
- **Track every lead** in a CRM (see [How to use a CRM](/blog/how-to-use-a-crm/))
- **Close the loop with marketing:** which sources produce customers

## Inbound sales metrics

- Speed to first response
- Lead-to-meeting rate
- Meeting-to-opportunity rate
- Win rate and sales cycle length
- Revenue by lead source
- Cost per acquisition

## Frequently asked questions

### What is inbound sales?
Selling to prospects who initiate contact or show interest through your website, content, ads or referrals, using a responsive, consultative approach.

### What is the difference between inbound and outbound sales?
Inbound sales responds to prospects who come to you; outbound sales proactively contacts prospects who haven't expressed interest.

### What is lead qualification?
The process of determining whether a lead fits your ideal customer profile and is ready to buy, using fit, behaviour and frameworks like BANT.

### What are the steps of inbound sales?
Identify, connect, explore and advise: prioritise good-fit leads, reach out quickly, understand their needs and recommend the right solution.

### How fast should you respond to inbound leads?
As quickly as possible, ideally within minutes, with automated instant acknowledgements outside business hours.

### Can inbound sales be automated?
Parts can: instant replies, routing, scoring, booking, follow-ups and CRM updates. Discovery and advice still benefit from human salespeople.
