<p class="lede">A pool cleaning business earns mainly from <strong>recurring weekly service accounts</strong>, so income depends on <strong>how many pools you service, what you charge per month, route density and chemical costs</strong>. Extra profit comes from <strong>repairs, equipment replacement, green-to-clean cleanups and openings/closings</strong>. Solo operators earn from their own routes; owners with technicians can scale substantially.</p>

## How pool businesses make money

| Revenue source | Notes |
|---|---|
| Weekly service | Recurring monthly fee per pool |
| Chemicals | Included or billed separately |
| Repairs | Pumps, filters, heaters, leaks |
| Equipment upgrades | Variable-speed pumps, automation |
| One-time cleanups | Green pools, storm cleanup |
| Openings/closings | Seasonal markets |

## Costs

Chemicals, fuel and vehicle, insurance, equipment, software, and labour if you hire technicians.

## Income factors

Pools per day (route density), monthly price, chemical cost control, repair/upsell revenue and climate (year-round vs seasonal).

## Starting and growing

Learn water chemistry and equipment, get any required licences/certifications, register and insure, buy equipment, build a route through marketing or **buy an existing pool route**, and use routing and billing software. Grow by adding technicians and selling repairs.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a pool cleaning business make?
It depends on number of accounts, monthly pricing, route density and repair revenue.

### How much do pool cleaning companies make per pool?
They earn a recurring monthly fee per pool, plus repairs and extras.

### Is a pool cleaning business profitable?
Yes, especially with dense routes and repair revenue in warm climates.

### Can I buy a pool route?
Yes; buying a route provides immediate recurring customers.

### How many pools can one tech service per day?
It varies by route density and pool size; efficient routes allow more stops.

### Do I need a licence for pool cleaning?
Requirements vary by state; some require licences for chemical handling or equipment repair.
