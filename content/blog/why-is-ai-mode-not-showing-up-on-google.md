<p class="lede">If <strong>AI Mode</strong> isn't showing up on Google, the most common reasons are that it <strong>isn't available yet in your country or language</strong>, you're <strong>not signed in</strong> or using an <strong>ineligible account</strong> (some work, school or under-18 accounts), your <strong>Google app or browser is outdated</strong>, or Google is still <strong>rolling it out gradually</strong>. <strong>AI Overviews</strong> also don't appear for every search: Google shows them only when it decides they're helpful, and rarely for some topics.</p>

## What is Google's search AI called?

- **AI Overviews:** AI-generated summaries that appear at the top of some normal search results
- **AI Mode:** a dedicated conversational search experience (a separate tab or button) for complex, multi-part questions with follow-ups
- Both are powered by Google's **Gemini** models (see [Is Gemini Google?](/blog/is-gemini-google/))

## Why AI Mode isn't showing up

| Reason | What to check |
|---|---|
| **Not available in your region/language** | Google expands AI Mode country by country and language by language |
| **Not signed in** | Some features require a signed-in personal Google account |
| **Account type** | Workspace (work/school) accounts may be restricted by admins; age requirements may apply |
| **Outdated app or browser** | Update the Google app and Chrome |
| **Gradual rollout** | Features reach users in stages even within supported countries |
| **Settings/Labs** | Earlier versions were opt-in via Search Labs |
| **Using a different Google domain or VPN** | Region mismatch can hide features |

## How to fix it (step by step)

1. **Update** the Google app (Android/iOS) and your browser
2. **Sign in** with a personal Google account
3. Check you're on **google.com** or your country's Google domain without a VPN
4. Try **google.com/aimode** (or look for the "AI Mode" tab/button below the search bar) where available
5. Clear cache or try another browser or device
6. Check **Search Labs** (labs.google.com/search) for any relevant experiments
7. For Workspace accounts, ask your **admin** whether AI features are enabled
8. If still missing, it's likely **not yet available** for your account or region

## Why AI Overviews aren't working or showing

- Google only shows AI Overviews when it judges them useful; many queries don't trigger them
- Some sensitive topics have stricter limits
- Short navigational searches ("facebook login") rarely trigger them
- Region and language availability
- Temporary issues or experiments

You generally can't force AI Overviews on. To see only traditional results, use the **"Web" filter** under the search bar.

## Can you turn off AI Overviews?

There's no universal off switch in regular search. Options include the **Web** filter tab, or adding terms like "-ai" to a query (results vary; this is a workaround, not an official setting).

## What AI search means for businesses

AI Overviews and AI Mode summarise information from websites, so businesses should publish clear, accurate, well-structured content and maintain strong Google Business Profiles to be cited. (See [How does SEO work on AI Mode?](/blog/how-does-seo-work-on-ai-mode/))

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is AI Mode not showing up on Google?
It may not be available in your country, language or account type yet, you may not be signed in, your app may be outdated, or Google may still be rolling it out to you.

### How do I get AI Mode on Google?
Update your Google app or browser, sign in with a personal Google account and look for the AI Mode tab or visit google.com/aimode where available.

### Why is my AI Overview not working?
Google only shows AI Overviews for some queries when it deems them helpful; availability also depends on region and language.

### What is the AI on Google Search called?
AI Overviews (summaries in regular results) and AI Mode (a conversational search experience), powered by Gemini.

### Can I turn off AI Overviews?
There's no standard toggle, but the Web filter shows traditional link results without AI summaries.

### Is AI Mode free?
Yes, AI Mode is part of Google Search for eligible users at no cost.
