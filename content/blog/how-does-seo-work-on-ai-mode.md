<p class="lede">SEO for <strong>Google AI Mode and AI Overviews</strong> still starts with <strong>classic SEO fundamentals</strong>: your pages must be <strong>crawlable, indexed and eligible for Search</strong>, and they must be <strong>helpful, trustworthy and clearly structured</strong>. AI Mode breaks complex questions into many sub-searches (often called <strong>query fan-out</strong>), retrieves relevant pages, and <strong>summarises them with links to sources</strong>. Sites that answer specific questions clearly, show expertise and are well-known in their niche are more likely to be cited.</p>

## How AI Mode and AI Overviews choose sources

1. **Understanding the query** and its sub-questions
2. **Fan-out:** issuing related searches to cover each aspect
3. **Retrieval** from Google's index (same index as regular search)
4. **Synthesis** of an answer by Gemini models
5. **Citations:** links to pages supporting the answer

Google has said there are **no special requirements or tags** to appear in AI features beyond being indexed and eligible to show a snippet; standard SEO best practices apply.

## What changes for websites

- **Fewer clicks for simple informational questions** answered directly
- **Visibility via citations** can still drive qualified visitors who want depth
- **Longer, conversational queries** reward pages covering topics comprehensively
- **Brand mentions and authority** matter for being trusted as a source
- **Bottom-of-funnel content** (services, pricing, comparisons, local) remains valuable because people still click to act

## How SEOs are reacting

Many SEOs are shifting from pure ranking reports to **visibility in AI answers**, focusing on **topic depth, original insights, brand building and conversions** rather than raw traffic. Measurement is harder because Google Search Console groups AI Mode data with web search performance.

## How to get cited in AI Mode and AI Overviews

### Content
- **Answer questions directly** near the top, then expand with detail
- **Cover sub-topics** a user would ask next (FAQs, comparisons, steps)
- **Original value:** experience, data, examples, photos, opinions
- **Clear structure:** descriptive headings, lists, tables
- **Keep facts current**

### Trust (E-E-A-T)
- Show **author expertise** and real experience
- **About, contact and policies** pages
- **Reviews and reputation** across the web
- **Citations and sources** for claims

### Technical
- Indexable pages, fast and mobile-friendly
- **Structured data** (Organization, LocalBusiness, Product, FAQ, Article) to clarify entities
- Allow Google crawling; avoid blocking with robots.txt or nosnippet unless intended
- Good internal linking and topic clusters

### Brand and local
- **Google Business Profile** optimisation for local queries (see [How to create a Google Business page](/blog/how-to-create-a-google-business-page/))
- **Mentions and links** from reputable sites (see [How to build backlinks](/blog/how-to-build-backlinks/))
- Consistent entity information across the web

## Measuring AI search visibility

- Search Console performance (AI features included in web totals)
- Manual checks for priority queries
- Third-party AI visibility trackers (Semrush, Ahrefs and others offer AI Overview tracking)
- Referral traffic and conversions from search, branded search growth

## Frequently asked questions

### How does SEO work on AI Mode?
AI Mode retrieves pages from Google's index for many related sub-queries and summarises them with citations, so standard SEO (indexable, helpful, trustworthy, well-structured content) is the foundation for being cited.

### Do I need special optimisation for AI Overviews?
Google says no special markup is required; follow SEO best practices, create helpful content and make sure pages are eligible for snippets.

### Does AI Mode reduce website traffic?
It can reduce clicks for simple questions but can send highly engaged visitors via citations, especially for complex or commercial queries.

### How do I get my website cited by Google AI?
Answer specific questions clearly, cover topics in depth, demonstrate expertise, use structured data, earn mentions and keep content updated.

### How are SEO professionals reacting to AI Mode?
By focusing more on topical authority, original content, brand building, conversions and tracking AI visibility rather than rankings alone.

### Is SEO dead because of AI?
No. AI search still depends on web content; SEO is evolving toward being the trusted source AI systems cite.
