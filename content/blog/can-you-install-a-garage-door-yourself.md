<p class="lede">You <strong>can</strong> install a garage door yourself if you're experienced with tools, but it's <strong>risky</strong>. The biggest danger is the <strong>spring system</strong>: torsion springs hold enormous tension and can cause serious injury if handled wrong. Many homeowners DIY the <strong>opener</strong> but hire a pro for the <strong>door and springs</strong>.</p>

## What installation involves

1. Removing the old door and releasing spring tension safely
2. Installing panels, hinges and rollers
3. Mounting tracks level and plumb
4. Installing and winding torsion springs (or extension springs)
5. Balancing the door
6. Installing the opener, sensors and controls
7. Testing safety reversal

## Why springs are dangerous

Torsion springs store a lot of energy. Winding or releasing them without the right winding bars and know-how can cause severe injuries. Many manufacturers recommend professional installation of springs.

## Tools needed

Winding bars, socket set, drill and drivers, level, clamps, ladders, and at least one helper.

## Time

Professionals often install a door in a few hours. DIY can take a full day or more.

## What homeowners misunderstand about opener installation

- The opener doesn't lift the door; balanced **springs** do. A bad spring makes the opener fail
- Safety sensors must be aligned and the auto-reverse tested
- Heavier doors may need more horsepower
- Old openers may not be compatible with smart features

## When to hire a pro

- Torsion spring replacement or new door installation
- Insulated or heavy doors
- Warranty requirements (some need professional installation)
- If you don't have the tools or experience

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I install a garage door myself?
Yes, if you're skilled, but springs make it risky; many hire a pro.

### Is installing a garage door opener DIY-friendly?
Often yes, if the door is balanced and you follow the instructions.

### Why are garage door springs dangerous?
They store high tension that can cause serious injury if released incorrectly.

### How long does it take to install a garage door?
A pro often takes a few hours; DIY can take a day or more.

### Does DIY installation void the warranty?
Sometimes. Check the manufacturer's warranty terms.
