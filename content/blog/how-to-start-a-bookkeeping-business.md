<p class="lede">To start a bookkeeping business: build skills (and optionally a <strong>bookkeeping certification</strong> and software certifications like QuickBooks or Xero), register your <strong>business</strong> and get <strong>insurance</strong>, choose a <strong>niche</strong>, offer clear <strong>monthly packages</strong>, set up a <strong>cloud tech stack</strong>, use <strong>engagement letters</strong>, and win clients through <strong>referrals, local SEO, software partner directories and niche communities</strong>. It can be started from home with low costs.</p>

## Step 1: Skills and credentials

Double-entry bookkeeping, reconciliations, payroll basics, sales tax/VAT/GST, financial statements. Certifications (e.g., certified bookkeeper programmes, QuickBooks ProAdvisor, Xero Advisor) build credibility.

## Step 2: Business setup

Entity and registration, business bank account, professional liability insurance, data security practices, engagement letter templates.

## Step 3: Niche and services

Niches: trades, restaurants, e-commerce, medical practices, real estate investors, creators. Services: monthly bookkeeping, catch-up/clean-up, payroll, AP/AR, sales tax filings, reporting.

## Step 4: Pricing

Monthly fixed packages by transaction volume and complexity; separate pricing for clean-up projects. Avoid pure hourly billing once processes are efficient.

## Step 5: Tech stack

Cloud accounting (QuickBooks, Xero, Zoho Books), receipt capture, payroll software, client portal, practice management and automation (see [What tools do accountants use?](/blog/what-tools-do-accountants-use/)).

## Step 6: Get your first clients

- Your network and local businesses
- Accountants/CPAs who need bookkeeping partners
- Software partner directories
- Niche online communities
- Google Business Profile and local SEO

## How to market bookkeeping services

1. **Niche website** with services, packages and booking (see [How to create a website for your business](/blog/how-to-create-a-website-for-your-business/))
2. **Google Business Profile** and reviews
3. **Content:** checklists and guides for your niche
4. **LinkedIn** and local business groups
5. **Referral partners:** CPAs, lawyers, bankers
6. **Free bookkeeping health check** as a lead magnet
(More: [How to get accounting clients](/blog/how-to-get-accounting-clients/).)

## Step 7: Scale

Standard procedures, automation (bank rules, document capture, reminders), then hire or contract bookkeepers.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a bookkeeping business?
Build bookkeeping skills, register a business, get insurance, choose a niche, create monthly packages, set up cloud software and find clients via referrals and local marketing.

### Do I need a certification to start a bookkeeping business?
Often not legally required, but certifications build credibility; check local regulations for tax or payroll services.

### How much should I charge for bookkeeping?
Many bookkeepers charge monthly packages based on transaction volume and complexity, with separate fees for clean-up work.

### How do bookkeepers find clients?
Through referrals from accountants and clients, local SEO, software partner directories, niche communities and networking.

### Can I run a bookkeeping business from home?
Yes. Cloud software makes remote bookkeeping common, though check local business and data-security requirements.

### How do I market bookkeeping services?
With a niche website, Google Business Profile, helpful content, LinkedIn, referral partners and a free health-check offer.
