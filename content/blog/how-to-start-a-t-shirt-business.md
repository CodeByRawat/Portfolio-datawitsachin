<p class="lede">To start a t-shirt business from home: pick a <strong>niche</strong> with a clear audience, create <strong>original designs</strong>, choose a <strong>printing method</strong> (print-on-demand for zero inventory, or DTG, screen printing or heat transfer if you produce yourself), source <strong>quality blanks</strong>, set <strong>prices</strong>, create <strong>mockups and photos</strong>, and sell through your <strong>own online store</strong>, <strong>marketplaces</strong> and <strong>social media</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Niche and designs

Hobbies, professions, pets, local pride, humour, causes, fandom (avoid copyrighted characters). Original designs or licensed artwork only; check trademarks for phrases.

## Step 2: Printing methods

| Method | Best for | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Print-on-demand** | Testing designs | No inventory or upfront cost | Lower margins, less control |
| **DTG (direct-to-garment)** | Full-colour, small runs | Detailed prints | Equipment cost if in-house |
| **Screen printing** | Bulk orders, simple colours | Durable, cheap per unit at volume | Setup costs, MOQs |
| **Heat transfer/vinyl/DTF** | Home-based small runs | Low entry cost | Durability varies |
| **Embroidery** | Premium, logos | Upscale look | Higher cost |

## Step 3: Blanks and suppliers

Choose blanks by fabric, fit, sustainability and price. Order samples before committing.

## Step 4: Costs and pricing

Blank + printing + packaging + shipping + platform fees + marketing + profit. POD margins are thinner; bulk printing improves unit costs.

## Step 5: Mockups and photos

Realistic mockups for launch, then real photos on models for trust and conversions.

## Step 6: Sell

- **Your own store** (Shopify, WooCommerce) with POD integrations (see [How to create an online store](/blog/how-to-create-an-online-store/))
- **Marketplaces** (Etsy, Amazon Merch-style programmes) (see [How to choose the right marketplace](/blog/how-to-choose-the-right-marketplace-for-your-business/))
- **Social shopping** (see [What is social shopping?](/blog/what-is-social-shopping/))
- Local events, schools, teams, businesses (custom orders)

## Step 7: Marketing and scaling

Short videos, creator collaborations, limited drops, email list; move best-sellers to bulk printing for better margins.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a t-shirt business?
Choose a niche, create original designs, pick a printing method, source blanks, price for profit and sell online and on social media.

### Can I start a t-shirt business from home?
Yes, with print-on-demand or small-scale heat press or DTF setups.

### How much does it cost to start a t-shirt business?
Almost nothing with print-on-demand; more for printing equipment or bulk screen-printed inventory.

### What is the best printing method for t-shirts?
Print-on-demand for testing, screen printing for bulk simple designs, and DTG for detailed small runs.

### Can I use any design on t-shirts?
No; use original or licensed designs and avoid trademarks and copyrighted characters.

### Where can I sell t-shirts online?
Your own store, Etsy, Amazon programmes and social platforms.
