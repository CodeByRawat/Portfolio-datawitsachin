<p class="lede">When hiring a security company, check that it's <strong>licensed in your state</strong>, carries <strong>strong liability insurance</strong>, <strong>screens and trains its guards</strong>, provides <strong>supervision and real-time reporting</strong>, understands your <strong>type of property</strong>, and offers <strong>clear pricing and contract terms</strong>. Ask for references from similar clients.</p>

## Checklist

| What to check | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| State security license | Required in most states for guard companies |
| Licensed, registered guards | Each guard should hold a state guard card where required |
| General liability and workers' comp | Protects you from claims |
| Background checks and drug testing | Trustworthy staff |
| Training | Site-specific, de-escalation, first aid, report writing |
| Armed vs unarmed options | Match the risk level |
| Supervision | Field supervisors and site checks |
| Reporting technology | GPS patrol tracking and daily activity reports |
| Industry experience | Retail, construction, events, residential, healthcare |
| References | From clients like you |
| Response plan | What happens when a guard calls out sick |

## Questions to ask

1. Are you licensed in this state, and can I see proof of insurance?
2. How do you screen and train guards?
3. Will we have the same guards each shift?
4. How are guards supervised?
5. What reports will I receive, and how often?
6. What's the hourly rate, and what's included?
7. What's your minimum contract length and cancellation policy?

## Pricing

Most guard companies charge per guard per hour. Armed guards, short-notice and holiday coverage cost more. The cheapest quote often means lower guard pay and higher turnover.

## Red flags

No license or insurance proof, vague training, no supervision, high turnover, and unusually low prices.

## For security company owners

Clients compare you on these exact points. Show your license, insurance, training and reporting on your website, and make quote requests easy. ## Frequently asked questions

### What should I look for when hiring a security company?
Licensing, insurance, guard screening and training, supervision, reporting and references.

### How do I choose a security guard company?
Compare licensed, insured companies with experience in your industry and clear pricing.

### Do security companies need a license?
Most states require security guard companies and guards to be licensed.

### Should I hire armed or unarmed guards?
Unarmed guards suit most sites; armed guards are for higher-risk locations.

### How much does hiring a security guard cost?
Usually an hourly rate per guard, with more for armed or short-notice coverage.
