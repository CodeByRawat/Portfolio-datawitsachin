## Hat styles and niches

| Style | Popular with |
|---|---|
| Dad caps | Everyday streetwear |
| Trucker hats | Outdoor, country, retro |
| Snapbacks/fitted | Sports, streetwear |
| Beanies | Winter, outdoor, lifestyle |
| Bucket hats | Festivals, fashion |
| Custom team/business hats | Teams, companies, events |

## Customisation methods

- **Embroidery:** premium, durable (in-house machine or partner)
- **Patches:** woven, embroidered, leather, PVC
- **Printing/heat transfer:** full colour, lower cost
- **Print-on-demand hats:** test designs without inventory

## Suppliers

Blank hat wholesalers for decoration, or custom manufacturers for fully branded hats (MOQs apply). Always sample.

## Costs and pricing

Blank + decoration + labels/packaging + shipping + fees + marketing + margin. Bulk orders and in-house embroidery improve margins.

## Selling

Your own store (see [How to create an online store](/blog/how-to-create-an-online-store/)), Etsy and marketplaces, pop-ups, and **B2B custom orders** for teams, schools, breweries and companies (often the most profitable).

## Marketing

Lifestyle photos, short videos, creator collaborations, limited drops, local partnerships. (More apparel guides: [How to start a t-shirt business](/blog/how-to-start-a-t-shirt-business/), [How to start your own clothing brand](/blog/how-to-start-your-own-clothing-brand/).)

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a hat business?
Pick styles and a niche, choose a customisation method, source blanks or a manufacturer, sample, price and sell online and through custom B2B orders.

### How much does it cost to start a hat company?
Low with print-on-demand; more with embroidery machines or custom manufacturing minimums.

### Where can I get blank hats?
From wholesale apparel distributors and hat manufacturers; sample first.

### Is embroidery or printing better for hats?
Embroidery looks premium and lasts; printing allows detailed full-colour designs at lower cost.

### How do hat businesses get customers?
Social media, creators, limited drops, and custom orders for teams and businesses.

### Can I start a hat business from home?
Yes, especially with print-on-demand or a home embroidery machine.
