<p class="lede">The fastest ways to sell window cleaning services are <strong>walking into storefronts</strong> with a simple weekly or monthly price, <strong>door-to-door and door hangers</strong> near current jobs, and an online presence with <strong>Google reviews and an instant-quote website</strong>. Turn every one-time job into a <strong>recurring plan</strong>.</p>

## 1. Storefront route selling

Walk into shops, restaurants and offices early in the day. Offer a clean, low-commitment price per visit, show insurance proof, and offer to do the first clean right away.

**Simple script:** "Hi, I clean storefront windows in this area every week. I can do yours inside and out for a set price per visit, no contract. Want me to do it today so you can see the result?"

## 2. Residential door-to-door

- Knock or leave door hangers around houses you're already cleaning
- Offer a neighbour discount
- Show before/after photos on your phone

## 3. Online marketing

| Channel | Why |
|---|---|
| Google Business Profile | Local search visibility |
| Reviews | Trust and conversions |
| Website with online quotes | Capture leads 24/7 |
| Social media | Before/after videos |
| Neighbourhood apps | Local recommendations |

## 4. Partnerships

Realtors (move-in/move-out), property managers, house cleaners, builders (post-construction) and pressure washing companies.

## 5. Follow up and convert to recurring

Send reminders based on last service date, offer quarterly or monthly plans, and ask for referrals at every job.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I sell window cleaning services?
Walk into storefronts, go door-to-door near current jobs, and build reviews and an online quote system.

### How do window cleaners get customers?
Storefront visits, door hangers, Google reviews, referrals and partnerships.

### What should I say when selling window cleaning?
Keep it short: what you do, the price, no contract, and an offer to clean right now.

### Do door hangers work for window cleaning?
Yes, especially in neighbourhoods where you're already working.

### How do I get commercial window cleaning clients?
Visit businesses in person and offer recurring service with insurance proof.

### How do I keep window cleaning customers?
Reliable service, reminders and recurring plans.
