<p class="lede">To get more real estate listings, focus on <strong>seller-intent sources</strong>: stay in regular contact with your <strong>sphere and past clients</strong>, <strong>farm a neighbourhood</strong> consistently, run a <strong>home valuation page</strong> with ads, use <strong>"just listed/just sold" marketing</strong>, reach out to <strong>expired and FSBO listings</strong> (within local rules), partner with professionals who meet sellers early, and win appointments with a <strong>strong listing presentation and marketing plan</strong>.</p>

## 15 strategies to win more listings

1. **Monthly sphere touchpoints:** calls, market updates, events
2. **Annual home value reviews** for past clients
3. **Neighbourhood farming:** postcards, door-knocking, local content for one area
4. **Home valuation landing page** with Meta/Google ads (see [What is a landing page conversion?](/blog/what-is-a-landing-page-conversion/))
5. **Just listed/just sold campaigns** around each sale
6. **Expired listings:** show what you'd do differently
7. **FSBO outreach:** offer help with pricing, marketing and paperwork
8. **Open houses** to meet neighbours who may sell
9. **Local video content** that builds authority (see [How to make great YouTube videos](/blog/how-to-make-great-youtube-videos/))
10. **Google Business Profile reviews** from sellers
11. **Partnerships:** divorce and estate attorneys, financial planners, builders, property managers
12. **Seller seminars** (downsizing, relocation, probate)
13. **Absentee owner and landlord outreach**
14. **Referral programme** with past clients
15. **Automated nurturing** of long-term seller leads (see [How to convert real estate leads](/blog/how-to-convert-real-estate-leads/))

## How to get real estate listings fast

- Call past clients and sphere this week
- Target **expireds** daily
- Door-knock around a new listing or sale
- Run a **valuation ad** to a farm area
- Host an open house this weekend
- Ask lenders for pre-approved buyers who need to sell first

## Winning the listing appointment

- **Prepare a CMA** (comparative market analysis)
- Present a **marketing plan:** professional photos, video, social ads, open houses (see [How to advertise for realtors](/blog/how-to-advertise-real-estate/))
- Show **results and testimonials**
- Explain **pricing strategy** honestly
- Show your **communication process**
- Follow up within 24 hours

## Measure your listing pipeline

Track contacts, appointments, listings taken, list-to-sale ratio and source of every listing so you invest in what works.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I get more real estate listings?
Stay in touch with your sphere, farm a neighbourhood, use home valuation landing pages, market every sale, contact expired and FSBO sellers, build partnerships and deliver strong listing presentations.

### How do new agents get listings?
Through their sphere, holding open houses, door-knocking, partnering with experienced agents, FSBO outreach and consistent local content.

### What is real estate farming?
Consistently marketing to one geographic area so homeowners recognise you as the local expert when they decide to sell.

### How can I get listings fast?
Call your sphere and past clients, contact expired listings, door-knock around recent sales and run home valuation ads.

### Do home valuation ads work?
They can generate seller leads, but many are early-stage, so fast follow-up and long-term nurturing are essential.

### What do sellers look for in an agent?
Local expertise, a clear marketing plan, pricing knowledge, communication and proven results.
