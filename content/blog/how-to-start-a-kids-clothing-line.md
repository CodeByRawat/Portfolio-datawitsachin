<p class="lede">To start a kids clothing line: pick a <strong>niche</strong> (organic baby basics, gender-neutral playwear, occasion outfits, matching family sets), learn the <strong>children's product safety rules</strong> that apply where you sell (flammability, small parts, drawstrings, lead and chemical limits, tracking labels and testing/certification), get <strong>sizing and comfort</strong> right, choose <strong>safe, soft fabrics</strong>, work with experienced <strong>manufacturers</strong>, price with margin, and sell through an <strong>online store, marketplaces and boutiques</strong>, marketing to parents with trust-building content.</p>

## Steps

1. **Niche and brand:** values parents care about (organic, durable, inclusive, sustainable)
2. **Safety compliance:** children's apparel often requires specific testing and certification (e.g., in the US, CPSIA requirements such as third-party testing, tracking labels and a Children's Product Certificate; other regions have their own rules). Avoid hazardous drawstrings and small detachable parts for young children.
3. **Sizing:** age-based sizes with measurements; allow room for growth
4. **Fabrics:** soft, breathable, durable, easy-care; certifications (e.g., organic textile standards) build trust
5. **Manufacturers:** experienced with children's wear and compliance; sample and test
6. **Pricing:** include testing and certification costs
7. **Selling:** your own store (see [How to create an online store](/blog/how-to-create-an-online-store/)), marketplaces, baby boutiques, markets
8. **Marketing:** parent creators, real kids in photos (with consent), reviews, gift bundles, email for size-up reminders

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a kids clothing line?
Choose a niche, learn children's product safety requirements, perfect sizing and fabrics, work with experienced manufacturers, price with compliance costs and sell online and through boutiques.

### Are there special rules for children's clothing?
Yes, many countries have strict safety, testing and labelling requirements for children's products.

### What fabrics are best for kids clothes?
Soft, breathable, durable fabrics like cotton and organic cotton blends that are easy to wash.

### How do I market a kids clothing brand?
Parent-focused social content, creator partnerships, reviews, gift bundles and email reminders as children grow.

### Can I sell handmade kids clothes?
Yes, but handmade children's items may still need to meet safety and labelling requirements.

### Is a kids clothing business profitable?
It can be, with repeat purchases as children grow, though compliance and returns must be managed.
