<p class="lede">A mobile bartending business provides <strong>bartenders and bar service for events</strong>, often without owning a bar trailer. Get <strong>bartending and alcohol server certifications</strong>, understand your state's <strong>liquor laws</strong> (most offer <strong>dry-hire service</strong> where the client supplies alcohol), buy <strong>liquor liability insurance</strong>, put together a <strong>portable bar kit</strong>, and sell <strong>hourly packages</strong> to weddings, parties and corporate events. You can start for <strong>$1,000 to $5,000</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Get certified

Bartending skills plus responsible alcohol service certification (often required by venues and insurers).

## Step 2: Liquor laws

Most mobile bartenders serve alcohol the client purchases. Selling alcohol directly usually requires special licences. Rules vary by state. See [Can you get a liquor license for a mobile bar?](/blog/can-you-get-a-liquor-license-for-a-mobile-bar/).

## Step 3: Insurance

Liquor liability and general liability insurance. Many venues require a certificate of insurance.

## Step 4: Equipment kit

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Portable bar or bar front | $300 – $2,000 |
| Shakers, jiggers, strainers, tools | $100 – $300 |
| Coolers and ice bins | $100 – $500 |
| Glassware (or use rentals) | Varies |
| Uniforms | $100 – $300 |

## Step 5: Pricing

Charge per bartender per hour, often with a minimum (for example 4 hours), plus setup and breakdown time. Add-ons: drink menu design, mixers and garnishes, mocktails, glassware rental.

## Step 6: Hire and train

Hire certified bartenders as you grow. Train them on your service standards and how to check IDs and cut off guests responsibly.

## Step 7: Get bookings

Wedding planners, venues and caterers, Google and social media, and a website with packages and inquiry forms.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a mobile bartending business?
Get certified, understand liquor laws, buy insurance and a bar kit, and sell hourly packages.

### Do mobile bartenders need a liquor license?
Not usually when serving client-supplied alcohol, but rules vary by state.

### Do bartending licenses transfer from state to state?
Server certifications usually don't transfer automatically; check each state.

### How much do mobile bartenders charge?
Usually per bartender per hour, with a minimum.

### Do I need insurance as a mobile bartender?
Yes. Liquor liability is essential.
