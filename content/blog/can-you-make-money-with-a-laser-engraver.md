<p class="lede">Yes, you can make money with a laser engraver. Hobby sellers often earn <strong>a few hundred to a few thousand dollars a month</strong>, and full-time laser businesses with B2B clients can reach <strong>$50,000 to $200,000+</strong> a year. Materials are often cheap, so personalised items commonly have <strong>50% to 80% gross margins</strong>.</p>

These are rough ranges; your products and pricing matter most.

## Profit per item (examples)

| Item | Material cost | Price | Gross profit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engraved cutting board | $10 | $35 | $25 |
| Custom tumbler (fiber) | $8 | $30 | $22 |
| Wood sign | $8 | $45 | $37 |
| Keychains (batch of 10) | $5 | $50 | $45 |

## How much can a laser engraving business make?

| Stage | Rough yearly revenue |
|---|---|
| Side hustle | $5,000 – $30,000 |
| Full-time online + markets | $30,000 – $100,000 |
| B2B and bulk orders | $100,000 – $200,000+ |

## Ways to make money with a laser

1. Personalised gifts on Etsy and your website
2. Wedding and event décor
3. Corporate gifts and awards
4. Engraving customers' own items (tumblers, tools, phones)
5. Signs for businesses and homes
6. Laser cutting parts for makers and small businesses
7. Selling digital design files

## How to price laser work

Material cost + machine time (per minute or hour) + design time + overhead + profit. Charge set-up fees for custom one-offs and give bulk discounts.

## Tips to earn more

- Focus on business clients for repeat bulk orders
- Batch production to save time
- Offer rush services at a premium
- Add a fiber laser for metal products
