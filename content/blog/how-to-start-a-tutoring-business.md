<p class="lede">To start a tutoring business: choose your <strong>subjects and students</strong> (e.g., high school maths, SAT/exam prep, elementary reading, languages, coding), decide <strong>online, in-home or a learning centre</strong>, register the <strong>business</strong> (and get <strong>background checks</strong> if working with minors), set <strong>pricing and packages</strong>, use tools for <strong>scheduling, payments and video lessons</strong>, create <strong>lesson plans and progress reports</strong>, launch a <strong>website with booking</strong>, and win students through <strong>parents, schools and referrals</strong>.</p>

## Steps

1. **Niche:** subject + level + goal (e.g., "GCSE maths grade improvement")
2. **Format:** online (wider reach), in-home, library sessions, small groups, or a centre
3. **Credentials:** subject expertise, teaching experience, certifications where useful, background checks
4. **Business setup:** registration, bank account, contracts/policies (cancellations, payments), insurance (professional liability)
5. **Pricing:** hourly or packages; premium for test prep (see [How much should I charge for tutoring?](/blog/how-much-should-i-charge-for-tutoring/))
6. **Tools:** scheduling (Calendly/Cal.com), video (Zoom/Google Meet), whiteboards, payments, CRM/student records
7. **Curriculum:** diagnostic assessment, learning plans, materials, homework, progress reports for parents
8. **Website:** subjects, results/testimonials, tutor bios, pricing guidance, free consultation booking (see [How to make a booking website](/blog/how-to-make-a-booking-website/))
9. **Marketing:** parents' groups, schools and teachers, Google Business Profile, local SEO, referrals (see [How to sell tutoring services](/blog/how-to-sell-tutoring-services/))
10. **Scale:** hire tutors, standardise materials, run group classes, add online courses

## Starting an online tutoring business

Use reliable video and digital whiteboards, target students beyond your city, consider time zones, and market via SEO, YouTube and social content.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a tutoring business?
Pick subjects and students, choose a format, register the business, set pricing, set up scheduling and payments, create lesson plans, launch a website and market to parents and schools.

### Do I need qualifications to start tutoring?
Not always; subject expertise and teaching ability matter most, though certifications and background checks build trust.

### How do I start an online tutoring business?
Choose a niche, use video and whiteboard tools, build a website with booking and promote through SEO and social media.

### Is a tutoring business profitable?
It can be, especially with packages, group sessions, test prep and additional tutors.

### How do I get my first tutoring students?
Through your network, parents' groups, teachers, local listings and a free first consultation.

### How do I start a tutoring company with other tutors?
Standardise curriculum and quality, recruit and vet tutors, manage scheduling centrally and market the brand.
