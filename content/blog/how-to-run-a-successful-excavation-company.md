<p class="lede">Successful excavation companies <strong>bid accurately from real job costs</strong>, keep <strong>expensive equipment busy and well maintained</strong>, put <strong>safety first</strong> (utility locates, trench protection), track <strong>profit on every job</strong>, manage <strong>cash flow</strong> carefully, and build <strong>repeat relationships with general contractors and builders</strong>.</p>

## 1. Bid from real numbers

Know your true hourly cost for each machine (payments, fuel, maintenance, insurance) and each operator. Include mobilisation, trucking, disposal, materials, compaction and testing. Add overhead and profit. See [How to bid excavation work](/blog/how-to-bid-excavation-work/).

## 2. Keep equipment working

| Practice | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Track machine hours and utilisation | Idle machines still cost money |
| Preventive maintenance schedules | Breakdowns kill schedules and margins |
| Rent for rare jobs | Avoid buying machines you seldom use |
| Telematics / GPS | Fuel, hours and location data |
| Replace before repairs get excessive | Control total cost |

## 3. Safety first

- Call 811 for utility locates before every dig
- Trench protection (sloping, shoring, trench boxes) per OSHA rules
- Competent person on site for trenches
- Daily equipment inspections
- Training and toolbox talks

Safety protects your crew, your insurance rates and your reputation.

## 4. Build a strong crew

Good operators are hard to find. Pay competitively, train helpers into operators, and keep crews together.

## 5. Job costing

Track labour, machine hours, trucking and materials on every job. Compare actual vs bid so your next bid is better.

## 6. Cash flow and billing

Excavation has big upfront costs. Use deposits, progress billing, lien rights and clear payment terms. Keep a cash cushion for slow seasons.

## 7. Win repeat work

Show up on schedule, communicate clearly and leave clean sites. General contractors, builders, developers and municipalities hire contractors they trust. More: [How to bid excavation work and get more jobs](/blog/how-to-bid-excavation-work/).

## 8. Grow carefully

Add services (site prep, utilities, septic, demolition, hauling), but grow equipment only when work justifies it. Profit: [How much do excavation companies make?](/blog/how-much-do-excavation-companies-make/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you run a successful excavation company?
Bid accurately, maintain and use equipment well, focus on safety, track job costs and build repeat contractor relationships.

### What makes excavation companies fail?
Underbidding, idle or broken equipment, poor cash flow and safety incidents.

### How do excavation companies keep equipment costs down?
Preventive maintenance, tracking utilisation, and renting rarely used machines.

### Is excavation a good business?
Yes, it can be very profitable, but it requires large equipment investments.

### How do excavators get repeat work?
Reliable schedules, quality work and good relationships with contractors.
