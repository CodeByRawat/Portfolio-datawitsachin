<p class="lede">To hire a chimney sweep, look for a <strong>certified sweep</strong> (for example CSIA certification), check <strong>insurance and reviews</strong>, ask what's included (cleaning, <strong>Level 1 or Level 2 inspection</strong>, photos or camera), get the <strong>price upfront</strong>, and be wary of <strong>high-pressure upsells</strong>. Schedule in <strong>late spring or summer</strong> to avoid the autumn rush.</p>

## Where to find a chimney sweep

- Chimney industry association directories (certified sweeps)
- Google reviews and local recommendations
- Fireplace and stove retailers
- Home inspectors and realtors

## What to look for

| Check | Why |
|---|---|
| Certification | Shows training in safety and codes |
| Insurance | Protects your home |
| Licence if required | Some areas require one |
| Reviews | Real customer experiences |
| Clear pricing | No surprise fees |
| Inspection reports with photos | Proof of problems |

## What a chimney sweeping includes

Protecting the room, cleaning the flue and firebox, removing creosote and debris, checking the smoke chamber and damper, and a basic inspection.

## Inspection levels

| Level | When |
|---|---|
| Level 1 | Annual check for unchanged systems |
| Level 2 | Home sales, after a chimney fire, system changes (camera inspection) |
| Level 3 | Hidden serious problems, may require removing materials |

## Questions to ask

1. Are you certified and insured?
2. What's included in the price?
3. Will you provide photos or video of problems?
4. Do you offer a written report?

## Avoiding scams

Be cautious of very cheap "specials" followed by big repair claims. Ask for photos and get a second opinion on major repairs.

## Do you tip chimney sweeps?

Tipping isn't expected, but a small tip or a good review is appreciated for great service.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I hire a chimney sweep?
Find a certified, insured sweep with good reviews and clear pricing.

### How do I find a good chimney sweep?
Use certification directories, reviews and recommendations.

### Do you tip chimney sweeps?
It's not expected, but appreciated.

### When should I book a chimney sweep?
Late spring or summer, before the busy autumn season.

### What's a Level 2 chimney inspection?
A deeper inspection with a camera, often for home sales or after changes.
