<p class="lede">Running a profitable electrical company comes down to <strong>accurate estimating and job costing</strong>, <strong>pricing that covers overhead</strong>, <strong>healthy cash flow</strong> (deposits and progress billing), <strong>skilled, safe crews</strong>, <strong>software that keeps jobs and invoices organised</strong>, and a <strong>steady pipeline of work</strong> from service customers, contractors and maintenance contracts.</p>

## Estimating and job costing

Estimate labour hours and materials carefully, include overhead and margin, and compare actual vs estimated costs on every job to improve future bids.

## Pricing

Flat-rate pricing for service work; project pricing based on true labour burden, materials, equipment, overhead and profit.

## Cash flow

Collect deposits, use progress billing on larger projects, invoice immediately, and manage retainage and supplier terms.

## People and safety

Hire and develop apprentices and journeymen, follow safety rules and codes, and invest in training on new technologies (EV, solar, controls).

## Software and automation

Estimating, scheduling, dispatch, job costing, invoicing and payments in one system; automate reminders, review requests and estimate follow-ups.

## Steady work

Service customers via Google and reviews, relationships with general contractors and builders, property managers and commercial maintenance contracts.

## How electrical contractors make more money

- Focus on profitable niches (EV chargers, panel upgrades, generators)
- Raise average ticket with good/better/best options
- Reduce non-billable time
- Add maintenance contracts
- Track job profitability and drop poor-margin work

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you run an electrical company?
Estimate accurately, price to cover overhead, manage cash flow, build safe skilled crews, use software and build a steady pipeline.

### How do electrical contractors make money?
From service calls, project contracts and maintenance agreements priced above total costs.

### How can an electrical contractor increase profit?
Focus on profitable niches, improve estimating, raise average tickets and reduce non-billable time.

### What is job costing for electricians?
Tracking actual labour, materials and overhead for each job against the estimate.

### What software do electrical contractors use?
Estimating, field service and accounting software for scheduling, job costing and invoicing.

### How do electrical contractors find steady work?
Through Google and reviews, contractor relationships, property managers and maintenance contracts.
