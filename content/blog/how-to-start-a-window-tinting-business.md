<p class="lede">To start a window tinting business, <strong>get trained</strong> (manufacturer or tint school), choose <strong>automotive, residential or commercial</strong> tinting, learn your <strong>state tint laws</strong>, buy <strong>tools and a film plotter</strong>, partner with a <strong>film manufacturer</strong> for pricing and warranties, choose a <strong>shop or mobile</strong> setup, and market to <strong>car owners, dealers, homeowners and businesses</strong>. Startup costs often run <strong>$5,000 to $30,000</strong> for mobile and <strong>$30,000 to $100,000+</strong> for a shop.</p>

## Step 1: Get trained

Tinting takes practice: heat shrinking film on curved glass, cutting, and installing without dust or bubbles. Take a course and practise on scrap glass and old cars.

## Step 2: Choose your market

| Market | Notes |
|---|---|
| Automotive | Biggest demand, needs a clean bay |
| Residential | Heat, glare, UV and privacy |
| Commercial | Office buildings, storefronts, security film |
| Paint protection film (PPF) | High-ticket add-on |
| Decorative and frosted film | Offices and homes |

## Step 3: Know tint laws

Each state sets limits for how dark (VLT) car windows can be. Follow the law and issue compliance stickers where required.

## Step 4: Tools and costs

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Tint tools (squeegees, knives, heat gun) | $300 – $1,000 |
| Film plotter and software | $3,000 – $10,000 |
| Film inventory | $1,000 – $5,000 |
| Shop lease and setup (if shop) | $20,000 – $80,000+ |
| Mobile van or trailer (if mobile) | Varies |
| Insurance and licences | $2,000 – $6,000 per year |

## Step 5: Pricing

Auto tint is usually priced per vehicle and film type (dyed, carbon, ceramic). Residential and commercial jobs are priced per square foot.

## Step 6: Get customers

1. Google Business Profile with photos and reviews
2. A website with prices and online booking
3. Car dealers, detailers and body shops
4. Homeowners and property managers
5. Social media videos of installs

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a window tinting business?
Get trained, choose your market, learn tint laws, buy tools and film, and market locally.

### How much does it cost to start a tint business?
Often $5,000 to $30,000 mobile; $30,000 to $100,000+ for a shop.

### Do I need a licence to tint windows?
A business licence; some states have extra requirements for auto tint.

### Is a plotter necessary?
Not at first, but it saves film and time.

### What's the best tint to sell?
Ceramic film is popular for heat rejection and higher margins.
