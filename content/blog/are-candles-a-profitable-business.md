<p class="lede">Yes, candles can be a profitable business. A candle that costs <strong>$4 to $8</strong> to make often sells for <strong>$18 to $35</strong> retail, giving <strong>gross margins of 50% to 75%</strong>. Many candle makers earn <strong>a few hundred to a few thousand dollars a month</strong> part-time; strong brands can reach <strong>$100,000+</strong> a year. Success depends on <strong>branding, quality testing and marketing</strong> in a crowded market.</p>

These are rough ranges.

## Cost vs price (example)

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Wax, wick, fragrance | $3.00 |
| Jar and lid | $1.50 |
| Label and packaging | $1.00 |
| **Cost to make** | **$5.50** |
| Wholesale price | $12 |
| Retail price | $24 |

## How much do candle businesses make?

| Stage | Rough yearly revenue |
|---|---|
| Hobby / markets | $2,000 – $15,000 |
| Part-time online + markets | $15,000 – $60,000 |
| Established brand with wholesale | $100,000+ |

## Why candle businesses succeed

- Strong, consistent brand and scent story
- Carefully tested, safe, clean-burning candles
- Great photos and packaging
- Repeat customers and subscriptions
- Wholesale accounts with boutiques

## Challenges

- Crowded market
- Shipping costs and breakage
- Seasonality (most sales in autumn and holidays)
- Rising costs of jars and fragrance

## How to be more profitable

Buy supplies in bulk, raise average order value with bundles, sell refills and wax melts, build an email list, and add wholesale and custom orders.

Get started: [How to start a candle making business](/blog/how-to-start-a-candle-making-business/). Selling: [How to become a candle vendor](/blog/how-to-become-a-candle-vendor/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Are candles a profitable business?
Yes. Gross margins often run 50% to 75%.

### Are candle businesses successful?
Many are, especially those with strong branding and quality.

### How much does it cost to make a candle?
Often $4 to $8 for a typical jar candle.

### Can candle making be profitable from home?
Yes. Many start and stay home-based.

### When do candles sell best?
Autumn and the holiday season.
