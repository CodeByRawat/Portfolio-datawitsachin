<p class="lede">To send an invoice: <strong>create the invoice</strong> with your business details, the client's details, a unique invoice number, date, itemised services or products, totals, taxes, payment terms and payment methods; save it as a <strong>PDF</strong> or create it in <strong>invoicing software</strong>; then <strong>email it</strong> to the right contact with a short, polite message and a <strong>payment link</strong>. Track it and send <strong>reminders</strong> if it's not paid by the due date.</p>

## Step 1: Gather the details

- Your business name, address, contact, tax/registration number (if applicable)
- Client name, company, billing address, contact person, purchase order (PO) number if they use one
- Description of work or products, quantities, rates
- Agreed payment terms (e.g., due on receipt, Net 15, Net 30)

(Full list: [What does an invoice look like?](/blog/what-does-an-invoice-look-like/))

## Step 2: Create the invoice

| Method | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Invoicing/accounting software** (QuickBooks, Xero, FreshBooks, Zoho Invoice, Wave, Stripe Invoicing) | Regular invoicing, tracking, online payments |
| **Templates in Word, Excel, Google Docs/Sheets** | Occasional invoices (see [How to make an invoice template](/blog/how-to-make-an-invoice-template/)) |
| **PayPal / Stripe / Square invoices** | Quick invoices with card payment links |
| **Accounting module in your CRM or ERP** | Integrated businesses |

Give each invoice a **unique, sequential number** (see [What is an invoice number?](/blog/what-is-an-invoice-number/)).

## Step 3: Send it by email

- Send to the **billing contact** (ask clients who handles invoices)
- Attach a **PDF** or include a link from your software
- Use a clear **subject line:** "Invoice #1045 from Bright Studio – due 30 Nov"
- Include a **payment link** and bank details if applicable

### Example invoice email

> Subject: Invoice #1045 – Website design (due 30 November)
>
> Hi Priya,
>
> Thanks again for working with us. Please find attached invoice #1045 for $2,400 for the website design project, due on 30 November.
>
> You can pay online here: [payment link]. Bank transfer details are on the invoice.
>
> Let me know if you have any questions.
>
> Best regards,
> Sam

## Step 4: Track and follow up

- Software shows when invoices are **viewed and paid**
- Send a **friendly reminder** a few days before or on the due date
- Follow up **after the due date** at 3, 7 and 14 days with increasing firmness
- Call for significantly overdue invoices
- Apply late fees only if agreed in your terms

### Example reminder

> Hi Priya, a quick reminder that invoice #1045 ($2,400) was due on 30 November. You can pay here: [link]. If it's already been paid, please ignore this message. Thanks!

## Tips to get paid faster

- **Invoice immediately** when work is done or on milestones
- **Clear payment terms** agreed before starting
- **Multiple payment options** (card, bank transfer, UPI, wallets)
- **Deposits** for larger projects
- **Automatic reminders**
- **Accurate details** (PO numbers, correct contact) to avoid delays in client approval processes

## Automating invoices

- **Recurring invoices** for retainers and subscriptions
- **Auto-reminders** for overdue invoices
- **Generate invoices automatically** from bookings, completed jobs or CRM deals
- **Sync payments** to accounting
- Use automation tools (Zapier, Make, n8n) to connect systems (see [Accounts payable automation](/blog/accounts-payable-automation/) for the payer side and [How to automate business processes](/blog/how-to-automate-business-processes/))

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I send an invoice?
Create an invoice with your details, client details, invoice number, date, itemised charges, totals, terms and payment options, then email it as a PDF or via invoicing software with a payment link.

### How can I send an invoice through email?
Attach a PDF invoice or send it from invoicing software, use a clear subject with the invoice number and due date, include a short message and a payment link.

### What should I write in an email when sending an invoice?
A greeting, the invoice number, amount, due date, what it's for, how to pay (link or bank details) and an offer to answer questions.

### What is the best way to send invoices?
Invoicing software is best for most businesses because it tracks views and payments, offers online payment and sends automatic reminders.

### When should I send an invoice?
Immediately after delivering the work or product, or at agreed milestones or billing dates.

### How do I follow up on an unpaid invoice?
Send polite reminders before and after the due date, then firmer follow-ups and a phone call if it's significantly overdue.
