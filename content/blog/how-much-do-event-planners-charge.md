<p class="lede">Event planners commonly charge using <strong>flat fees</strong>, a <strong>percentage of the event budget</strong> (often around <strong>10–20%</strong>), <strong>hourly rates</strong>, or a <strong>hybrid</strong>. Fees vary widely by location, experience, event size and service level: <strong>day-of coordination</strong> usually costs least, <strong>partial planning</strong> more, and <strong>full planning and design</strong> the most.</p>

## Pricing models

| Model | How it works | Pros/cons |
|---|---|---|
| **Flat fee** | Fixed price per package | Predictable; must scope carefully |
| **Percentage of budget** | 10–20% of total event cost | Scales with event size; clients may question incentives |
| **Hourly** | Billed per hour | Good for consultations; unpredictable for clients |
| **Per guest** | Fee × guest count | Simple for some events |
| **Hybrid** | Base fee + percentage or hourly extras | Flexible |

## Service levels

| Service | What's included |
|---|---|
| **Day-of coordination** | Final weeks' coordination and on-site management |
| **Partial planning** | Help with key vendors, timelines and design |
| **Full planning and design** | End-to-end planning, vendor sourcing, design and management |
| **Corporate event management** | Strategy, logistics, registration, production |

## Factors that affect fees

Location and market, planner reputation, event complexity, guest count, destination travel, timeline, number of vendors and design involvement.

## How to set your prices

1. Estimate hours per service level (meetings, planning, vendor management, event day)
2. Decide your target hourly value and business costs
3. Research local competitors
4. Create clear packages with inclusions/exclusions
5. Require deposits and staged payments
6. Raise prices as demand and portfolio grow

## Frequently asked questions

### How much do event planners charge?
Commonly flat fees, 10–20% of the event budget, hourly rates or a hybrid, depending on service level and market.

### How much does a day-of coordinator cost?
Usually less than full planning since it covers the final weeks and event day.

### Do event planners charge a percentage?
Many do, often around 10–20% of the total event budget, though flat fees are also common.

### Is hiring an event planner worth it?
Often yes, as planners save time, reduce stress and can secure better vendor pricing.

### How do I price my event planning services?
Estimate hours per package, factor costs and target income, research local rates and create clear packages.

### Do event planners get vendor commissions?
Some do; ethical planners disclose any commissions to clients.
