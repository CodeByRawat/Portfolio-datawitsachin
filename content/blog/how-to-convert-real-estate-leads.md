<p class="lede">To convert real estate leads: <strong>respond within minutes</strong> by call and text, <strong>qualify</strong> their timeline, motivation, budget and financing, <strong>set a specific next step</strong> (showing, consultation, valuation), then <strong>follow up persistently</strong> with a planned cadence and <strong>nurture long-term leads</strong> with listing alerts and market updates. A <strong>CRM with automation</strong> ensures no lead is forgotten. Most online leads aren't ready immediately, so consistent follow-up over months is where many deals come from.</p>

## Step 1: Speed to lead

Leads often contact several agents. Calling and texting within **5 minutes** dramatically increases contact rates. Use instant auto-replies outside working hours.

## Step 2: Qualify

| Buyers | Sellers |
|---|---|
| Timeline to move | Timeline to sell |
| Areas and must-haves | Reason for selling |
| Budget | Expected price |
| Pre-approval status | Mortgage/equity situation |
| Current housing situation (renting, need to sell) | Plans after selling |
| Decision-makers | Other agents interviewed |

## Step 3: Set the next step

- Buyers: book showings or a buyer consultation; connect with a lender
- Sellers: schedule a valuation/listing appointment

## Example scripts

**Text:** "Hi Maria, this is Sam with Bright Realty. Thanks for checking out 24 Oak St! Would you like to see it this week, or should I send similar homes?"

**Call opener:** "Hi Maria, I saw you were looking at 24 Oak St. What caught your eye about it?" Then ask about timeline and needs.

## Step 4: Follow-up cadence

| Timing | Action |
|---|---|
| Day 0 | Call + text + email |
| Day 1 | Call + text |
| Day 3 | Call + helpful email (listings/guide) |
| Day 7 | Text check-in |
| Day 14 | Call |
| Monthly | Market update, new listings, personal check-in |
| Quarterly | Home value update (sellers) |

## Step 5: Long-term nurturing

- **Automated listing alerts** matching their criteria
- **Monthly market reports**
- **Educational emails:** buying process, financing, selling tips
- **Personal touches:** birthdays, local events
- **WhatsApp/SMS** check-ins with consent (see [How to schedule a WhatsApp message](/blog/how-to-schedule-a-whatsapp-message/))

## Use a real estate CRM

Track source, stage, notes, tasks and automations (e.g., Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, Lofty, Sierra, HubSpot or a custom CRM). (See [How to use a CRM](/blog/how-to-use-a-crm/).)

## Automation and AI

- Instant lead response and qualification by AI chat/text, then handoff to the agent
- Automatic appointment booking
- Lead scoring based on activity (viewing listings, opening emails)
- Task reminders for calls
- Summaries of conversations into the CRM
(See [How to automate your sales process](/blog/how-to-automate-your-sales-process/).)

## Conversion metrics

Contact rate, appointment rate, lead-to-close rate by source, average days to convert, cost per closing.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you convert real estate leads?
Respond within minutes, qualify needs and timeline, set a clear next step, follow up persistently and nurture long-term leads with automation.

### What is a good real estate lead conversion rate?
It varies by source; online leads often convert at low single-digit percentages, while referrals convert much higher. Track your own rates by source.

### How often should I follow up with real estate leads?
Frequently in the first two weeks, then monthly nurturing for months or years until they're ready.

### What should I say to a new real estate lead?
Reference what they inquired about, ask an open question about their needs and timeline, and offer a specific next step.

### What CRM is best for real estate agents?
Popular options include Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, Lofty and others; choose one with lead routing, automation and integrations.

### Can AI help convert real estate leads?
Yes, by responding instantly, qualifying, booking appointments and automating follow-up, while agents handle relationships and negotiations.
