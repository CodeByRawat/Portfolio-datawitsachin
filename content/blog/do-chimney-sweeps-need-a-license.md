<p class="lede">In most of the US, chimney sweeps need a <strong>business licence</strong> but there's <strong>no special state licence just for sweeping</strong>. However, some states and cities require a <strong>contractor or home improvement licence</strong>, especially for <strong>repairs, masonry or liner installation</strong>. Industry <strong>certification</strong> (such as CSIA) is voluntary but widely expected.</p>

Rules vary by location; check with your state contractor board and city.

## Licence vs certification

| | Licence | Certification |
|---|---|---|
| Who issues it | Government | Industry organisations |
| Required? | Depends on location and work | Usually voluntary |
| Shows | Legal permission to work | Training and knowledge |

## When licences are more likely required

- Chimney repairs and masonry
- Installing liners, inserts or stoves
- Gas appliance work
- Home improvement work above a dollar limit

## Insurance

General liability is essential; sweeps work on roofs and inside homes. Workers' comp is needed when hiring.

## How homeowners can check a sweep

1. Ask for certification number and verify it
2. Ask for proof of insurance
3. Check local licence requirements for repairs
4. Read reviews

## For new sweeps

Get a business licence, liability insurance and certification first. Add contractor licences as you take on repairs and installations. See [How to become a chimney sweep](/blog/how-to-become-a-chimney-sweep/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do chimney sweeps need a license?
Usually a business licence; repairs and installs may need a contractor licence.

### Do you need a license to be a chimney sweep?
Not a special one in most places, but certification is strongly recommended.

### Is CSIA certification required?
Usually not legally, but many customers and insurers prefer it.

### Do chimney sweeps need insurance?
Yes. Liability insurance protects the homeowner and sweep.

### Can a chimney sweep repair chimneys?
Yes, if they meet local licensing rules for repair work.
