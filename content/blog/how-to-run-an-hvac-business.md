<p class="lede">Running an HVAC business well means mastering <strong>scheduling and dispatch</strong>, <strong>flat-rate pricing that covers your true costs</strong>, a growing <strong>maintenance agreement base</strong>, <strong>hiring and training technicians</strong>, planning for <strong>seasonality and cash flow</strong>, using <strong>field service software and automation</strong>, and tracking <strong>key numbers</strong> such as average ticket, close rate and technician revenue.</p>

## Core operations

| Area | Best practice |
|---|---|
| Scheduling and dispatch | Zones, time windows, emergency slots |
| Pricing | Flat-rate price book, good/better/best options |
| Maintenance agreements | Two tune-ups a year, priority service, discounts |
| Inventory | Stocked vans, common parts, supplier accounts |
| Quality | Checklists, photos, call-backs tracked |

## Technicians

Hire for skill and attitude, train on both technical work and customer communication, pay fairly with performance bonuses, and build a career path to retain good techs.

## Seasonality and cash flow

Use maintenance agreements and tune-up campaigns in shoulder seasons, keep a cash reserve, and offer financing on replacements.

## Software and automation

Field service software for booking, dispatch, invoicing and payments; automate appointment reminders, "on my way" texts, review requests, maintenance renewals and unsold-estimate follow-ups.

## Customer experience

On-time arrivals, clean uniforms and vans, clear options and prices before work starts, and follow-up after the job.

## Marketing

Google Business Profile, reviews, Local Services Ads, SEO pages per service and city, and a website with online booking (see [How to market your HVAC business](/blog/how-to-market-your-hvac-business/)).

## KPIs to track

Average ticket, close rate on replacements, revenue per technician, call-back rate, maintenance agreement count, cost per lead and gross margin.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you run a successful HVAC business?
Use flat-rate pricing, grow maintenance agreements, hire and train strong technicians, use field service software and track KPIs.

### How do HVAC companies handle slow seasons?
With maintenance agreements, tune-up campaigns, indoor air quality services and financial reserves.

### What software do HVAC companies use?
Field service management software for scheduling, dispatch, invoicing, payments and customer history.

### How should an HVAC company price services?
Flat-rate pricing based on true labour, parts and overhead costs, with tiered options for replacements.

### What KPIs matter in an HVAC business?
Average ticket, close rate, revenue per tech, call-backs, agreement count and cost per lead.

### How can HVAC businesses use automation?
Automated reminders, review requests, agreement renewals and estimate follow-ups save time and increase revenue.
