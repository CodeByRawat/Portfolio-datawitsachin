<p class="lede">To market a medical spa: define your <strong>signature treatments and ideal clients</strong>, build a <strong>beautiful, booking-focused website</strong>, post <strong>educational and before/after content</strong> (with consent) on Instagram and TikTok, collect <strong>Google reviews</strong>, run <strong>targeted ads</strong> within healthcare ad policies, grow repeat revenue with <strong>memberships and packages</strong>, and stay in touch through <strong>email/SMS</strong>, while following medical advertising rules.</p>

## 1. Positioning

Signature treatments (injectables, laser, skin rejuvenation, body contouring), target audience, price positioning, provider credentials as a trust signal.

## 2. Website

Treatment pages (what it is, results, downtime, pricing ranges), provider bios, before/after galleries with consent, FAQs, financing, and prominent online booking (see [How to make a booking website](/blog/how-to-make-a-booking-website/)).

## 3. Social media

- Short educational Reels: treatment process, myths, aftercare
- Before/after posts with consent and realistic expectations
- Provider and team content
- Client experience videos (with permission)
(See [How to make a promotional video](/blog/how-to-make-a-promotional-video/).)

## 4. Google Business Profile and reviews

Accurate categories and services, photos, and review requests after each visit (see [How to create a Google Business page](/blog/how-to-create-a-google-business-page/)).

## 5. Paid ads

Google search for treatment + city; Meta ads for consultations and events (respect health/beauty ad policies, avoid before/after where restricted, no misleading claims).

## 6. Memberships and packages

Monthly memberships for regular treatments, packages for laser series, loyalty points; increase lifetime value (see [What is LTV?](/blog/what-is-ltv/)).

## 7. Email/SMS

Treatment reminders (e.g., injectable touch-ups), seasonal offers, new treatment launches (with consent).

## 8. Partnerships and events

Bridal shops, salons, fitness studios, local creators; open evenings with demos.

## Compliance

Medical oversight requirements, advertising rules for prescription treatments, consent for images, realistic claims and platform policies.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I market my med spa?
Position signature treatments, build a booking-focused website, post educational and consented before/after content, gather reviews, run compliant ads, and offer memberships and packages.

### What is the best social media for med spas?
Instagram is usually the most important, followed by TikTok and Facebook, with Google Business Profile critical for local search.

### Can med spas post before and after photos?
Generally yes with patient consent, but some platforms restrict them in ads and claims must not be misleading.

### How do med spas get more clients?
Through social content, Google reviews, referrals, events, partnerships and targeted ads with clear consultation offers.

### Do med spa memberships work?
They often increase retention and recurring revenue when tied to regular treatments.

### How do med spas reduce no-shows?
Deposits, automated reminders and easy rescheduling.
