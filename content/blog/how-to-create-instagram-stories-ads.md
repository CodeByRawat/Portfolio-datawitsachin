<p class="lede">To create Instagram Stories ads, open <strong>Meta Ads Manager</strong>, create a campaign with your objective (awareness, traffic, engagement, leads, app promotion or sales), choose <strong>manual placements</strong> and select <strong>Instagram Stories</strong> (or keep Advantage+ placements), set your audience and budget, then upload a <strong>vertical 9:16 creative at 1080×1920</strong> with a strong first second, captions and a clear call to action. You can also <strong>boost an existing story</strong> from the Instagram app for a quick, simpler version.</p>

Stories ads appear full-screen between people's stories, which makes them immersive but also easy to skip with a tap. This guide covers setup, specs, safe zones and the creative choices that make people stop.

## Method 1: Create Stories ads in Meta Ads Manager

1. **Go to Ads Manager** (adsmanager.facebook.com) and click **Create**.
2. **Choose an objective:** Awareness, Traffic, Engagement, Leads, App promotion or Sales.
3. **Set up the campaign:** budget type (campaign or ad set budget), and A/B test if wanted.
4. **Ad set:** conversion location (website, app, messaging, instant form), audience (location, age, interests, custom and lookalike audiences, or broad targeting), schedule and budget.
5. **Placements:** keep **Advantage+ placements** (Meta chooses) or pick **Manual placements** and select **Instagram Stories** (and optionally Facebook Stories and Reels, which share the 9:16 format).
6. **Ad:** choose your Instagram account, format (single image/video or carousel), upload 9:16 creative, write primary text, headline, and choose a call-to-action button and destination URL.
7. **Tracking:** make sure the Meta Pixel / Conversions API is set up for website goals.
8. **Publish** and let it run through the learning phase before making big changes.

## Method 2: Boost a story from the Instagram app

On a professional account, open an active story › **Boost** (where available) › choose a goal (profile visits, website visits, messages), audience, budget and duration › **Boost story**. This is quick but offers fewer targeting, testing and tracking options than Ads Manager.

## Instagram Stories ad specs

| Spec | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Aspect ratio** | 9:16 |
| **Resolution** | 1080×1920 px |
| **Formats** | Image (JPG/PNG), video (MP4/MOV), carousel |
| **Video length** | Keep it short: 5–15 seconds per card performs best; longer videos may be split into cards |
| **Safe zone** | Keep text, logos and key visuals out of roughly the top 14% and bottom 20–35% (account name and CTA area) |
| **Sound** | Design for sound on, but include captions |

Check Meta's current ad specs, as limits change.

## Creative best practices for Stories ads

1. **Win the first second:** movement, a face, the product or a bold question immediately.
2. **Design natively for vertical full-screen,** not a cropped horizontal ad.
3. **Brand early but not loudly:** logo or product in the first 3 seconds.
4. **One message per card;** big text inside safe zones.
5. **Add captions** for sound-off viewing.
6. **Use motion:** animated text, product reveals, quick cuts (see [How to make motion graphics](/blog/how-to-make-motion-graphics/)).
7. **UGC and creator-style footage** often outperforms polished studio ads in Stories.
8. **Clear CTA:** tell people to tap the link or button, ideally with a visual pointer.
9. **Make several variations** of hooks and formats to test.
10. **Match the landing page** to the ad's message and offer.

### Interactive elements
Organic stories can use polls, quizzes, questions, countdowns and link stickers. Some interactive features such as polls have been available for Stories ads in Ads Manager; availability varies by objective and changes over time.

## Instagram Stories ad examples by goal

| Goal | Creative idea |
|---|---|
| **Sales (e-commerce)** | 3-card carousel: product hero → customer review → offer with "Shop now" |
| **Leads (services)** | Founder speaking to camera: problem in 2 seconds, offer of a free audit, instant form |
| **App installs** | Screen recording of the app's best feature with animated taps and captions |
| **Events** | Countdown-style motion graphic with date, speakers and "Get tickets" |
| **Local business** | Before/after transformation video with location and "Book now" |
| **Messaging** | "Send us a message for a quote in 5 minutes" to WhatsApp or Instagram DM (see [How to schedule a WhatsApp message](/blog/how-to-schedule-a-whatsapp-message/) for automating replies) |

## How much do Instagram Stories ads cost?

There's no fixed price; you set a budget and Meta auctions placements. Costs depend on audience, competition, season, objective and creative quality. You can start with a small daily budget, but you need enough spend to collect meaningful data for testing.

## Metrics to watch

- **CPM** (cost per 1,000 impressions)
- **Hook rate / 3-second views**
- **Exits and taps forward** (signals weak creative)
- **CTR (link)** and **cost per click**
- **Conversion rate, CPA and ROAS**
- **Frequency** (ad fatigue above a few views per person)

## Common mistakes

- Using horizontal or square creative stretched into Stories
- Text or logos covered by the profile bar or CTA
- Slow intros
- No captions
- A single creative with no testing
- Ignoring Reels placement, which uses the same 9:16 assets

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I create Instagram Stories ads?
In Meta Ads Manager, create a campaign, choose your objective, set audience and budget, select Instagram Stories in placements (or Advantage+ placements), and upload a 9:16 image or video with a call to action. You can also boost an existing story from the Instagram app.

### What size are Instagram Stories ads?
1080×1920 pixels in a 9:16 aspect ratio, with important text and logos kept away from the top and bottom safe zones.

### How long can an Instagram Story ad be?
Short is best: 5–15 seconds per card. Check Meta's current specs for maximum lengths, which can change.

### Can I run Instagram Stories ads without a Facebook page?
Ads are managed through Meta's advertising tools; you need an Instagram professional account and access to Meta Ads Manager or the boost feature, which may involve connecting accounts in Meta Business settings.

### Are Instagram Stories ads effective?
They can be very effective for awareness, app installs and sales because they're full-screen and immersive, provided the creative is native, vertical and grabs attention immediately.

### Should Stories ads be video or images?
Both work. Video and motion usually hold attention better, while static images are quick to test. Many advertisers use carousels mixing both.
