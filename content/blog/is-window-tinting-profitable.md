<p class="lede">Yes, window tinting is usually profitable. Film is a <strong>small part of the price</strong>, so gross margins often run <strong>60% to 80%</strong> on auto tint. Small tint shops commonly bring in <strong>$150,000 to $500,000</strong> a year, and experienced tint installers often earn <strong>$35,000 to $70,000+</strong>, with owners earning more.</p>

These are rough ranges; prices, location and volume matter.

## Profit per car (example)

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Ceramic tint price (full car) | $400 |
| Film | −$60 |
| Labour (2–3 hours) | −$75 |
| Overhead share | −$60 |
| **Profit** | **about $200** |

## How much do tint shops make?

| Business | Rough yearly revenue |
|---|---|
| Mobile solo tinter | $60,000 – $150,000 |
| Small shop | $150,000 – $500,000 |
| Shop with PPF and commercial | $500,000+ |

## How much do tint installers make?

Often paid hourly or per car; experienced installers commonly earn $35,000 to $70,000+.

## Pros

- High margins
- Low material costs
- Fast jobs
- Strong demand in sunny climates

## Cons

- Skill takes time to master
- Competition and price shopping
- Comebacks for bubbles or peeling
- Need a clean, dust-free space

## How to earn more

- Sell ceramic and premium films
- Add paint protection film (PPF) and ceramic coatings
- Win dealer contracts
- Add residential and commercial work
- Collect reviews to justify higher prices
