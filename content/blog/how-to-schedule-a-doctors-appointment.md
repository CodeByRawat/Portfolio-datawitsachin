<p class="lede">To schedule a doctor's appointment: <strong>choose a doctor or clinic</strong> (and check they accept your <strong>insurance</strong>, if applicable), then book <strong>online</strong> through the clinic's website, a <strong>patient portal</strong> (such as MyChart where offered) or a booking app like Zocdoc, or <strong>call the office</strong>. Have your <strong>ID, insurance details, reason for the visit and preferred times</strong> ready. You'll usually get a <strong>confirmation and reminders</strong>; arrive early for paperwork or complete forms online beforehand.</p>

## Step 1: Find the right doctor

- **Primary care** (family medicine, internal medicine, paediatrics) for general concerns
- **Specialists** may require a referral depending on your insurance plan
- Use your **insurer's directory**, Google Maps reviews, hospital websites or booking platforms
- Check **location, hours, telehealth options and languages**

## Step 2: Check insurance and costs

- Confirm the doctor is **in-network** (if you have insurance)
- Ask about **copays** or self-pay prices
- Check if a **referral or prior authorisation** is needed

## Step 3: Book the appointment

| Method | How |
|---|---|
| **Clinic website** | "Book online" button with available slots |
| **Patient portal** (e.g., MyChart where used) | Log in, choose "Schedule an appointment" |
| **Booking apps** (Zocdoc and others) | Search by specialty, insurance and availability |
| **Phone** | Call the front desk |
| **Telehealth** | Book virtual visits via the clinic or telehealth services |
| **Walk-in/urgent care** | No appointment for urgent but non-emergency issues |

For **emergencies**, call your local emergency number or go to the emergency room.

## What to have ready

- Full name, date of birth, contact details
- Insurance card and ID
- Reason for visit and symptoms
- Current medications and allergies
- Preferred dates/times
- Referral information if required

## How to prepare for the visit

- Complete **intake forms online** if offered
- Arrive **10–15 minutes early** for first visits
- Bring a **list of questions**
- Bring previous test results or records

## Rescheduling or cancelling

Use the confirmation link, portal or call as early as possible; many clinics have cancellation policies and fees for late cancellations or no-shows.

## Do I need an appointment?

Most primary care and specialist visits require appointments. **Walk-in clinics and urgent care** accept patients without appointments; emergency rooms are for emergencies.

## For clinics: make booking easy

Patients increasingly expect to book online. Clinics can:
- Add **online booking** to their website and Google Business Profile (see [How to make a booking website](/blog/how-to-make-a-booking-website/))
- Send **automated reminders** by SMS, email or WhatsApp to reduce no-shows (see [Benefits of online booking](/blog/benefits-of-online-booking/))
- Offer **online intake forms**
- Use an **AI assistant** to answer common questions and book 24/7 (see [How to create an AI chatbot](/blog/how-to-create-an-ai-chatbot/))
- Follow health privacy rules (e.g., HIPAA in the US)

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I schedule a doctor's appointment?
Choose a doctor, check insurance, then book online via the clinic website, patient portal or a booking app, or call the office with your details and reason for visit.

### How do I make a doctor's appointment online?
Use the clinic's online booking page, your patient portal or a booking platform, select a time and confirm with your details.

### What do I need to make a doctor's appointment?
Your name, date of birth, contact details, insurance information, reason for the visit and preferred times.

### Do I need an appointment to see a doctor?
Usually for regular and specialist visits; walk-in clinics and urgent care accept patients without appointments.

### How far in advance should I book?
Routine primary care can take days to weeks; specialists may take longer. Book early and ask to be added to a cancellation list.

### How do I set up a doctor's appointment for the first time?
Call or book online as a new patient, provide personal and insurance details, complete intake forms and arrive early for your first visit.
