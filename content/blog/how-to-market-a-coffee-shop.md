Track transactions per day and how often customers come back.</p>

## 12 ways to market a coffee shop

1. **Google Business Profile:** hours, menu, photos, reviews and posts
2. **Instagram and TikTok:** latte art, behind the bar, new drinks, regulars
3. **Loyalty programme:** digital stamps or points tied to your POS
4. **Online ordering and pickup:** faster service and more morning transactions
5. **Delivery apps** for reach (watch the fees)
6. **Seasonal and limited menus** to create urgency
7. **Local partnerships:** bakeries, gyms, coworking spaces, schools
8. **Events:** open mics, art shows, tastings, workshops
9. **Office catering:** coffee boxes and pastry trays for meetings
10. **Signage and sidewalk boards** with daily specials
11. **Email/SMS:** new drinks, rewards and events
12. **Website:** menu, hours, location, ordering and catering requests (see [How to create a website for your business](/blog/how-to-create-a-website-for-your-business/))

## Track what matters

Transactions per day, average ticket, loyalty sign-ups, repeat visit rate and online order share.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I market my coffee shop?
Optimise Google Business Profile, post on Instagram and TikTok, run a loyalty programme, offer online ordering and host local events.

### How do coffee shops get more customers?
Through visibility on Google, social media, partnerships, events and word of mouth.

### What is the best loyalty programme for a café?
A simple digital programme linked to your POS that rewards frequent visits.

### Should a coffee shop offer online ordering?
Yes; it speeds up service and increases morning rush capacity.

### How can a coffee shop increase sales?
Raise average ticket with food and add-ons, add catering, and drive repeat visits.

### What should a coffee shop post on social media?
New drinks, latte art, behind-the-scenes, staff and customer moments, and events.
