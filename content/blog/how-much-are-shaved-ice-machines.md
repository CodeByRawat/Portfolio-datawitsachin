<p class="lede">Shaved ice machines range from about <strong>$30 to $100 for home snow cone makers</strong> to <strong>$300 to $1,500 for commercial cube ice shavers</strong> and <strong>$1,000 to $3,000+ for commercial block shavers</strong> that make fluffy, Hawaiian-style shave ice. For a business, choose a <strong>commercial machine</strong> with enough <strong>output per hour</strong> for your busiest events.</p>

These are rough price ranges.

## Types of machines

| Type | Rough cost | Texture | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home snow cone maker | $30 – $100 | Coarse | Home use |
| Commercial cube ice shaver | $300 – $1,500 | Snow cone | Stands and events |
| Commercial block shaver | $1,000 – $3,000+ | Fine, fluffy | Shave ice businesses |
| Hand-crank block shaver | $200 – $800 | Fine | Small or no-power setups |

## Features that matter

- **Output**: servings per hour for peak lines
- **Ice type**: cubes vs blocks (block ice needs moulds and freezer space)
- **Power**: plan for your generator or outlet
- **Durability**: stainless steel and serviceable blades
- **Safety**: guards and switches
- **Ease of cleaning**: for health inspections

## Other equipment

Block ice moulds, freezer or ice chests, syrup pumps, cups and spoons, and a hand-wash station.

## Which machine should I buy?

- **Starting small**: a commercial cube shaver
- **Premium shave ice**: a commercial block shaver
- **High volume events**: two machines or a high-output model

## Frequently asked questions

### How much are shaved ice machines?
From $30 for home models to $3,000+ for commercial block shavers.

### What's the best shaved ice machine for a business?
A commercial block shaver for fluffy shave ice, or a cube shaver for snow cones.

### Can I use a home snow cone machine for a business?
It's not recommended; it's too slow and not built for heavy use.

### Do shaved ice machines need block ice?
Block shavers do; cube shavers use regular ice.

### How many servings per hour can a machine make?
It varies by model; commercial machines make many more than home ones.
