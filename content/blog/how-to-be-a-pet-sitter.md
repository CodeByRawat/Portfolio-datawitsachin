<p class="lede">To become a pet sitter, <strong>get experience with animals</strong> (your own pets, friends' pets, shelter volunteering), take a <strong>pet first aid course</strong>, then start on <strong>pet sitting apps or with neighbours</strong>. No license or degree is needed. Add <strong>insurance</strong> and a simple website when you start taking regular clients.</p>

## What pet sitters do

- Feed pets and give fresh water
- Walk dogs and take them out to go potty
- Clean litter boxes and cages
- Give medication
- Play, give attention and send updates with photos
- Watch for illness and handle emergencies
- Look after the home: mail, plants, lights

**Cat sitters** focus on feeding, litter and companionship; cats usually stay home while owners travel.

## Qualifications

There's no required license. Helpful extras:

- Pet first aid and CPR certification
- Professional pet sitter certification courses
- Experience with dogs, cats and other animals
- A background check (many clients and apps ask for one)

## How to get started with no experience

1. Pet sit for friends, family and neighbours (free or cheap) and ask for reviews
2. Volunteer at a shelter or rescue
3. Create a profile on pet sitting apps
4. Take a pet first aid course
5. Get insurance once you take paying clients

## Skills that matter

Reliability, trustworthiness, comfort with dogs of all sizes, calm in emergencies, and good communication with owners.

## Turn it into a business

When you have regular clients, register a business, get insured and bonded, and build a website so clients can book directly.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I become a pet sitter?
Get experience with animals, take a pet first aid course and start with neighbours or apps.

### What qualifications do I need to look after dogs?
None are required in most places, but pet first aid and experience help.

### How do I get into pet sitting with no experience?
Sit for friends and family, volunteer at a shelter, and collect reviews.

### What does a cat sitter do?
Feeds cats, cleans litter boxes, gives medication and keeps them company.

### Do I need insurance to be a pet sitter?
It's strongly recommended once you take paying clients.
