<p class="lede">An <strong>explainer video</strong> is a short video, usually <strong>60–120 seconds</strong>, that explains <strong>what a product, service or idea is, the problem it solves and how it works</strong>, in a simple, engaging way. Most explainers use animation or motion graphics with a voiceover, and they're placed on homepages, landing pages, ads, sales emails and onboarding to help people understand quickly and take action.</p>

When a product is new, technical or hard to describe in a sentence, an explainer video does the work of a long page of text in a minute. This guide covers the types of explainers, structure, length, cost and how they're made.

## What is an explainer video used for?

- **Homepage and landing pages:** help visitors understand the offer quickly
- **Ads:** cut-downs of 15–30 seconds for social and YouTube
- **Sales:** sent to prospects or shown in demos
- **Product onboarding and feature launches**
- **Fundraising pitches and investor decks**
- **Internal communication and training**
- **Customer support:** explaining processes to reduce repetitive questions

## Types of explainer videos

| Type | Look | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **2D animated (character)** | Illustrated characters in simple scenes | Consumer services, storytelling |
| **Motion graphics** | Animated icons, text, shapes and UI | SaaS, tech, finance, abstract concepts (see [What are motion graphics?](/blog/what-are-motion-graphics/)) |
| **Whiteboard** | Hand drawing on a white background | Education, processes |
| **Screencast / product demo** | Real software screens with animation and zooms | Apps and SaaS (see [How to write a video brief for a product demo](/blog/how-to-write-a-video-brief-for-a-product-demo/)) |
| **3D animated** | 3D products or environments | Hardware, medical, engineering, premium brands |
| **Live action** | Real people and locations | Services, personal brands, trust-heavy offers |
| **Kinetic typography** | Animated text with voice or music | Short ads and social |
| **Mixed** | Live action with motion graphics overlays | Modern brand videos |

## How long should an explainer video be?

- **Homepage/landing page:** 60–90 seconds
- **Ads:** 15–30 seconds (with the key message in the first 3 seconds)
- **Product demos and complex B2B:** up to 2–3 minutes
- **Onboarding/feature videos:** 30–90 seconds each

A spoken pace of about **140–160 words per minute** means a 90-second explainer script is roughly 200–230 words. Shorter is almost always better.

## Explainer video script structure

| Part | Purpose | Example (accounting automation) |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | Grab attention with the pain | "Month-end shouldn't mean three nights of spreadsheets." |
| **Problem** | Show you understand the viewer | "Invoices in inboxes, approvals in chats, errors everywhere." |
| **Solution** | Introduce the product | "Meet LedgerFlow: your invoices, captured and approved automatically." |
| **How it works** | 3 simple steps | "Forward invoices. Approve in one click. Synced to your books." |
| **Benefits and proof** | Results and credibility | "Teams close books 3x faster. Trusted by 1,000 firms." |
| **Call to action** | One next step | "Start your free trial today." |

Write in plain, conversational language and focus on **benefits**, not features.

## How an explainer video is made

1. **Brief:** goal, audience, message, CTA, style references (see [What is creative strategy?](/blog/what-is-creative-strategy/))
2. **Script** written and approved
3. **Storyboard:** sketches of each scene
4. **Style frames:** final look of key scenes
5. **Voiceover** recorded (professional voice actor or high-quality AI voice)
6. **Animation**
7. **Music and sound design**
8. **Revisions** (usually 2 rounds)
9. **Delivery:** 16:9 master plus 9:16 and 1:1 cut-downs, subtitles

Typical timeline: **2–6 weeks**, depending on length, style and approvals.

## How much does an explainer video cost?

| Option | Rough cost (USD) | Quality |
|---|---|---|
| DIY templates (Canva, Animoto, Vyond-style tools) | Low (subscription) | Generic but fast |
| Freelance motion designer | ~$1,500–$8,000 for 60–90 s | Custom, varies by talent |
| Specialist studio/agency | ~$8,000–$50,000+ | High polish, full service |

Cost drivers: length, style (3D and character animation cost more), custom illustration, voiceover, music licensing, number of revisions and cut-downs, and deadline.

## Do explainer videos work?

Explainer videos help when people need to understand something before buying. Benefits include:
- **Faster understanding** of complex products
- **Longer time on page** and potentially higher conversion
- **Fewer support questions**
- **Reusable assets** across ads, sales and social

Effectiveness depends on a clear script, a relevant audience and placement where people make decisions. Test with and without the video on landing pages if you can.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Too long, too many features
- Starting with company history instead of the viewer's problem
- Jargon
- Weak or missing call to action
- Generic templates that look like everyone else
- No captions for muted autoplay
- Not creating short cut-downs for ads

## Frequently asked questions

### What is an explainer video?
A short video, usually 60–120 seconds, that explains what a product or service is, the problem it solves and how it works, typically using animation and voiceover.

### What is explainer video marketing?
Using explainer videos on websites, ads, social media, sales outreach and onboarding to help potential customers understand an offer and take action.

### How long should an explainer video be?
60–90 seconds for most homepage videos, 15–30 seconds for ads, and up to 2–3 minutes for complex products.

### What are the types of explainer videos?
2D character animation, motion graphics, whiteboard, screencast/product demo, 3D animation, live action, kinetic typography and mixed styles.

### How much does an explainer video cost?
From the cost of a template subscription for DIY, to roughly $1,500–$8,000 with a freelancer and $8,000–$50,000+ with an agency, depending on style and length.

### How do I make an explainer video?
Write a brief and short script focused on the viewer's problem, storyboard it, choose a style, record voiceover, animate, add music and sound, then export in all formats you need.
