<p class="lede"><strong>Social shopping</strong> (or <strong>social commerce</strong>) is buying products through social media, either <strong>directly inside the app</strong> (like TikTok Shop) or by discovering products via <strong>shoppable posts, videos, live streams and creators</strong> and checking out on a brand's site. It blends entertainment, recommendations and purchasing, so people discover and buy products in the same scroll.</p>

## How social shopping works

1. **Discovery:** a creator video, post, ad or live stream shows a product
2. **Engagement:** comments, reviews, demonstrations and social proof
3. **Purchase:** tap a product tag to buy in-app or on the store
4. **Sharing:** customers post their own content, fuelling more discovery

## Main social shopping channels

| Platform | Features |
|---|---|
| **TikTok Shop** | In-app checkout, shoppable videos and LIVE, creator affiliate programme (availability varies by country) |
| **Instagram/Facebook** | Product tags, shops, Reels, collaborations; checkout options vary by region |
| **YouTube Shopping** | Product tagging in videos, Shorts and live streams |
| **Pinterest** | Shoppable pins and catalogues |
| **WhatsApp/Messenger** | Conversational commerce (see [What is conversational commerce?](/blog/what-is-conversational-commerce/)) |

## Live shopping

Real-time streams where hosts demonstrate products, answer questions and offer limited-time deals. It's big in some markets and growing elsewhere.

## Benefits

- **Discovery** by new audiences through algorithms
- **Social proof** from creators and comments
- **Shorter path to purchase**
- **Creator affiliate reach** without big upfront budgets
- **Rich data** on what resonates

## Challenges

- Platform fees and dependency
- Need for constant fresh content
- Fulfilment speed expectations
- Returns and customer service at scale
- Brand control in creator content

## How to start social shopping

1. Connect a **product catalogue** (Shopify and other platforms integrate with TikTok, Meta, Pinterest, Google)
2. Set up **shops** on chosen platforms
3. Create **short-form product videos**: demos, before/after, unboxing (see [How to make a promotional video](/blog/how-to-make-a-promotional-video/))
4. Recruit **creators/affiliates** with free samples and commissions
5. Try **live shopping** events
6. Run **shoppable ads**
7. Track sales by channel and creator

## Frequently asked questions

### What is social shopping?
Buying products through social media, either inside the app or via shoppable posts, videos, live streams and creator recommendations.

### What is the difference between social commerce and e-commerce?
E-commerce is buying online generally; social commerce is buying through social platforms where discovery and purchase happen together.

### What is TikTok Shop?
TikTok's in-app shopping feature where users buy products from videos, LIVE streams and shop tabs, often promoted by creator affiliates.

### Is social shopping good for small businesses?
Yes. It offers low-cost discovery and creator partnerships, though it requires consistent content.

### What products sell best through social shopping?
Visual, demonstrable and impulse-friendly products like beauty, fashion, gadgets and home items.

### What is live shopping?
Real-time video streams where hosts demonstrate products and viewers can buy instantly.
