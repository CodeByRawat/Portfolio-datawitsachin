<p class="lede">An Airbnb business earns money from <strong>nightly rates × booked nights</strong>, plus <strong>cleaning fees</strong>, minus <strong>platform fees, cleaning, utilities, supplies, maintenance, taxes and rent or mortgage</strong>. Day to day, it runs on <strong>pricing, guest messaging, cleaning turnovers and reviews</strong>, which most hosts <strong>automate</strong> with software.</p>

## How revenue works

| Revenue driver | Notes |
|---|---|
| Nightly rate | Changes by season, day and demand |
| Occupancy | Percentage of nights booked |
| Cleaning fee | Charged per stay |
| Extra guest/pet fees | Optional |
| Direct bookings | Avoid platform fees |

## Costs

Platform fees, cleaning and laundry, supplies, utilities and internet, repairs, insurance, occupancy taxes, software, and rent or mortgage.

## Daily operations

1. **Booking** comes in
2. **Automated messages** send check-in info
3. **Guest stays**, host answers questions
4. **Cleaner turns over** the property
5. **Review** is requested
6. **Prices update** automatically

## Automation tools

Dynamic pricing tools, channel managers (list on multiple sites), smart locks, noise monitors, cleaner scheduling apps and automated messaging. Good automation lets one host manage many listings.

## Business models

Owner-host, rental arbitrage, co-host (percentage of revenue) and full property management.

## Frequently asked questions

### How does an Airbnb business work?
You list a property, guests book nights, and you earn the nightly rate and fees minus operating costs.

### How do Airbnb hosts make money?
Nightly rates and cleaning fees, with profit after costs and platform fees.

### Can an Airbnb business run automatically?
Much of it can be automated with pricing, messaging, smart locks and cleaner scheduling tools.

### What is co-hosting?
Managing a property for an owner in exchange for a share of revenue.

### What is rental arbitrage?
Leasing a property and hosting it on short-term rental platforms with landlord permission.

### Why take direct bookings?
To lower platform fees and build repeat guests.
