<p class="lede">A <strong>consulting firm</strong> (consultancy or consulting agency) is a company of experts that <strong>helps organisations solve problems and improve performance</strong>. Consulting companies <strong>analyse the situation, identify issues and opportunities, recommend solutions and often help implement them</strong>, in areas such as strategy, operations, technology, marketing, finance and people.</p>

## What consulting firms do

1. **Diagnose:** interviews, data analysis, process reviews, benchmarking
2. **Recommend:** strategies, roadmaps, business cases
3. **Implement:** project management, change management, systems rollout
4. **Train and hand over:** build client capability

## Types of consulting

| Type | Focus |
|---|---|
| Management/strategy | Growth, market entry, M&A, organisation |
| Operations | Processes, supply chain, efficiency (see [What is business process management?](/blog/what-is-business-process-management/)) |
| IT and digital | Systems, cloud, data, AI and automation (see [How to automate business processes](/blog/how-to-automate-business-processes/)) |
| Marketing | Brand, digital, go-to-market (see [What is a marketing agency?](/blog/what-is-a-marketing-agency/)) |
| HR | Organisation design, talent, culture |
| Financial | Restructuring, risk, transactions |
| Specialist | Healthcare, legal, sustainability, regulatory |

## How consulting engagements work

Proposal and scope → kick-off → discovery → analysis → recommendations → implementation support → review.

## How consulting firms charge

Daily/hourly rates, fixed project fees, retainers and sometimes success fees.

## What is a consulting agency vs firm?

Mostly the same; "agency" is often used for marketing or creative consulting, "firm" for management and professional consulting.

## When to hire a consulting firm

When you need specialist expertise, an objective outside view, extra capacity for a major project, or proven methods to accelerate change. (Individual consultants: [What does a business consultant do?](/blog/what-does-a-business-consultant-do/).)

## Frequently asked questions

### What does a consulting firm do?
Helps organisations solve problems and improve performance by diagnosing issues, recommending solutions and supporting implementation.

### What is a consulting firm?
A company of experts that provides advice and implementation support to other organisations.

### What do consulting companies charge?
Daily or hourly rates, fixed project fees, retainers or success-based fees depending on scope.

### What types of consulting are there?
Management/strategy, operations, IT/digital, marketing, HR, financial and specialist industry consulting.

### Is a consultancy the same as a consulting firm?
Yes, the terms are generally interchangeable.

### When should a business hire consultants?
For specialist expertise, objective analysis, extra capacity or faster implementation of major changes.
