<p class="lede">Yes, in most US states you can start a cake business from home under <strong>cottage food laws</strong>, which usually allow <strong>cakes that don't need refrigeration</strong> (fillings like cream cheese or fresh cream are often excluded). You'll need to follow <strong>registration, labelling and sales limits</strong>, build a <strong>portfolio</strong>, price <strong>per serving</strong>, and take <strong>deposits</strong>. Home cake bakers often make <strong>$500 to $5,000+ a month</strong>.</p>

Rules vary by state; check your state's cottage food law and local health department.

## Step 1: Check cottage food rules

| Common rules | Examples |
|---|---|
| Allowed foods | Non-perishable cakes, cookies, breads |
| Often excluded | Cream cheese frosting, custards, fresh fruit fillings |
| Labelling | "Made in a home kitchen", ingredients, allergens |
| Sales limits | Annual revenue caps in some states |
| Where you can sell | Direct to customers, sometimes online or stores |

## Step 2: Register the business

Business name, licence or cottage food registration, sales tax permit if required, food handler certificate, and liability insurance.

## Step 3: Kitchen and tools

Turntable, spatulas, piping bags and tips, cake boards and boxes, pans, mixer, and enough fridge or storage space for your allowed products.

## Step 4: Build a portfolio

Bake for friends and family, photograph every cake, and post on Instagram and Facebook.

## Step 5: Pricing

Price per serving based on design complexity. Include ingredients, boxes, labour hours, utilities and profit. See [How much does a custom cake cost?](/blog/how-much-does-a-custom-cake-cost/).

## Step 6: Orders and deposits

Use an order form, a contract with pickup times and policies, and non-refundable deposits.

## Can you make money baking cakes?

Yes. Home bakers often earn $500 to $5,000+ a month depending on orders and prices; weekend custom cakes can be very profitable when priced correctly.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I start a cake business from home?
Yes, in most states under cottage food laws.

### Can you make money baking cakes?
Yes. Home bakers often earn $500 to $5,000+ a month.

### Do I need a licence to sell cakes from home?
Usually cottage food registration or a permit; check your state.

### Do you pay tax on cakes?
It depends on your state's sales tax rules for food.

### What cakes can't I sell from home?
Often those needing refrigeration, like cream cheese or custard-filled cakes.
