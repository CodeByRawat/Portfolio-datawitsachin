<p class="lede">Running a photo booth business well means <strong>easy online booking with contracts and deposits</strong>, a <strong>repeatable event checklist</strong>, <strong>trained attendants</strong>, <strong>custom templates</strong> sent before each event, <strong>well-maintained equipment</strong>, fast <strong>gallery delivery</strong>, and asking every client for a <strong>review</strong>.</p>

## 1. Booking system

Use booking software with availability, packages, contracts and deposits. Collect event details: venue, times, contact, theme and template text.

## 2. Pre-event process

| When | Task |
|---|---|
| 2–3 weeks before | Design and approve custom template |
| 1 week before | Confirm timing, venue access and power |
| Day before | Test booth, charge batteries, pack props and media |

## 3. Event-day checklist

- Booth, camera, lighting, printer, cables, power strips
- Backdrop and stand
- Props and signs
- Extra print media
- Tablet or sharing station
- Insurance certificate if venue requests

Arrive early and set up before guests arrive.

## 4. Attendants

Train attendants on setup, troubleshooting, guest engagement and professional behaviour. A friendly attendant makes the booth busier and creates better photos.

## 5. After the event

Deliver the online gallery within 24 hours, send a thank-you message, and ask for a review.

## 6. Maintenance

Clean lenses and screens, update software, replace worn props and backdrops, and keep spare cables and printer parts.

## 7. Grow

Add more booths and attendants, book multiple events per weekend, add corporate clients, and get on venue vendor lists.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you run a photo booth business?
Use online booking, follow event checklists, train attendants and deliver galleries fast.

### Do photo booths need attendants?
Most packages include one; it improves guest experience.

### How long does photo booth setup take?
Often 30 to 60 minutes.

### How do I get more photo booth reviews?
Deliver galleries quickly and ask every client right after the event.

### How do I grow a photo booth business?
Add booths and attendants, target corporate events and partner with venues.
