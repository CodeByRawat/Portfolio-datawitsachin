<p class="lede">To register a cleaning business: <strong>choose a name</strong> and check availability, pick a <strong>business structure</strong> (sole proprietorship or LLC are most common), <strong>register with your state</strong> (and file a DBA if needed), get an <strong>EIN</strong>, obtain a <strong>local business licence</strong>, register for <strong>sales tax</strong> if your state taxes cleaning, open a <strong>business bank account</strong>, and get <strong>insurance and bonding</strong>.</p>

## Step by step

1. **Choose a name:** check state business records, trademarks, domain and social handles
2. **Choose a structure:**
   - Sole proprietorship: simplest, no liability separation
   - LLC: liability protection, flexible taxes, modest fees
   - Corporation: for larger growth plans
3. **Register with your state:** file LLC articles of organisation, or a DBA for a sole proprietor using a trade name
4. **Get an EIN** from the IRS (free) for taxes, hiring and banking
5. **Local business licence:** city or county
6. **Sales tax permit** if cleaning services are taxable in your state
7. **Business bank account** to separate finances
8. **Insurance and bonding:** general liability, janitorial bond, workers' comp when hiring
9. **Bookkeeping:** track income and expenses; plan for quarterly estimated taxes
10. **Contracts:** service agreements and cancellation policies

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I register a cleaning business?
Choose a name and structure, register with your state, get an EIN, local licence and any sales tax permit, open a bank account and get insured.

### Should my cleaning business be an LLC?
An LLC offers liability protection and is a popular choice; sole proprietorship is simpler but offers no separation.

### Do I need an EIN for a cleaning business?
It's required if you hire employees or form certain entities and useful for banking even when solo.

### Do I need a licence to clean houses?
Usually a general business licence; specialised licences are rare for basic cleaning.

### How much does it cost to register a cleaning business?
State filing and local licence fees vary; sole proprietorships are cheapest.

### Do cleaning businesses charge sales tax?
In some states; check your state's rules.
