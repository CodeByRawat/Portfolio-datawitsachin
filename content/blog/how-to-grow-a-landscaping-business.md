<p class="lede">To grow a landscaping business: get <strong>visible locally</strong> (Google Business Profile, reviews, a quote-ready website, yard signs), build <strong>recurring maintenance revenue</strong>, <strong>upsell projects</strong> to existing clients, tighten <strong>route density</strong>, partner with <strong>HOAs, property managers and real estate agents</strong>, hire and train <strong>reliable crews</strong>, and run the business with <strong>software, automation and weekly numbers</strong>.</p>

## How to market a landscaping business

1. **Google Business Profile:** photos of real projects, services, review requests (see [How to create a Google Business page](/blog/how-to-create-a-google-business-page/))
2. **Website:** services, service areas, project gallery and quote form (see [How to create a website for your business](/blog/how-to-create-a-website-for-your-business/))
3. **Yard signs** at every job and **door hangers** in the neighbourhood
4. **Before/after photos and time-lapse videos** on Instagram/Facebook
5. **Nextdoor and local groups**
6. **Referral programme**
7. **Branded trucks and uniforms**
8. **Local ads** for seasonal services (spring clean-ups, fall leaf removal)
9. **Partnerships** with HOAs, property managers, real estate agents and builders (see [How to get commercial landscaping contracts](/blog/how-to-get-commercial-landscaping-contracts/))

## Build recurring revenue

- Annual maintenance agreements
- Seasonal packages (spring, summer, fall, winter/snow)
- Auto-renewing contracts with autopay

## Upsell and cross-sell

Mulching, planting, irrigation checks, lighting, hardscaping, seasonal colour, aeration and overseeding.

## Run it profitably

- **Route density:** cluster clients to cut drive time
- **Accurate pricing** and annual price increases (see [How to price landscaping jobs](/blog/how-to-price-landscaping-jobs/))
- **Crew productivity:** checklists, training, equipment maintenance
- **Software:** scheduling, routing, quotes, invoicing, payments (see [What is field service management software?](/blog/what-is-field-service-management-software/))
- **Automation:** quote follow-ups, invoice reminders, review requests (see [How to automate your sales process](/blog/how-to-automate-your-sales-process/))

## Metrics to track weekly

Revenue per crew hour, job profitability, close rate on quotes, recurring revenue, customer retention, drive time and accounts receivable.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I grow my landscaping business?
Increase local visibility, build recurring maintenance contracts, upsell projects, improve route density, partner with HOAs and property managers, and use software to run efficiently.

### How do I market a landscaping business?
Google Business Profile and reviews, a website with project photos and quote forms, yard signs, door hangers, social media and partnerships.

### How do I run a landscaping business efficiently?
Use scheduling and routing software, standard checklists, accurate pricing, maintained equipment and weekly financial reviews.

### What is the most profitable landscaping service?
Design/build and hardscaping projects often have higher margins; recurring maintenance provides stable cash flow.

### How do landscapers get more customers?
Through referrals, Google visibility, neighbourhood marketing around jobs and partnerships with property managers and real estate agents.

### When should a landscaping business hire?
When demand consistently exceeds capacity and recurring revenue can support another crew.
