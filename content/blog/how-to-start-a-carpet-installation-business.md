<p class="lede">To start a carpet installation business from scratch, <strong>learn installation under an experienced installer</strong>, buy <strong>carpet tools</strong> (knee kicker, power stretcher, seaming iron), register and insure the business, then get work by <strong>subcontracting for flooring stores and builders</strong> before adding <strong>direct homeowner jobs</strong>. Startup costs are often <strong>$3,000 to $10,000</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Learn the trade

Work as a helper for a carpet installer for 1–2 years. Learn measuring, seaming, stretching, stairs, padding and transitions.

## Step 2: Buy tools

| Tool | Use |
|---|---|
| Knee kicker | Positioning carpet |
| Power stretcher | Proper tension (prevents wrinkles) |
| Seaming iron and tape | Invisible seams |
| Carpet knives and cutters | Clean cuts |
| Stair tools | Stair installs |
| Tack strip cutter, stapler | Prep |
| Van or trailer | Carrying rolls and tools |

## Step 3: Register and insure

LLC, EIN, business license, general liability insurance and a contractor license if your state requires one for flooring work.

## Step 4: A simple business plan

| Section | What to include |
|---|---|
| Services | Carpet install, removal, padding, stairs, repairs and restretching |
| Customers | Flooring stores, builders, property managers, homeowners |
| Pricing | Labour per square yard + add-ons |
| Startup budget | Tools, van, insurance, marketing |
| Monthly goals | Jobs per week and target revenue |

## Step 5: Price your work

Charge labour per square yard (or square foot), plus removal and disposal, stairs, furniture moving and floor prep. Set a minimum job price.

## Step 6: Get work

1. Subcontract for local flooring stores and big-box installation programs
2. Builders and remodelers
3. Apartment complexes and property managers (turnovers)
4. Homeowners through Google, reviews and a website with free estimates
5. Repairs and restretching as add-on income

## Step 7: Grow

Add LVP, laminate and tile installation, hire a helper, and consider selling material for a markup.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a carpet installation business?
Learn the trade, buy tools, get insured and start by subcontracting for flooring stores.

### How much does it cost to start a carpet installation business?
Often $3,000 to $10,000 if you already have a van.

### Do I need a license to install carpet?
It depends on your state; you'll at least need a business license.

### How do carpet installers get work?
Flooring stores, builders, property managers and homeowner referrals.

### Is carpet installation a good business?
Yes for skilled installers; adding LVP and tile helps as carpet demand changes.
