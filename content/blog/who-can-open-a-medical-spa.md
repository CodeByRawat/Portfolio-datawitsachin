<p class="lede">Who can open a med spa depends on <strong>state law</strong>. In states with strict <strong>corporate practice of medicine (CPOM)</strong> rules, only <strong>physicians</strong> (and sometimes certain licensed providers) can own the medical practice, so non-physicians use a <strong>management services organization (MSO)</strong>. Other states are more flexible but still require <strong>medical oversight</strong>.</p>

## Ownership by role

| Owner | Typical situation |
|---|---|
| Physician (MD/DO) | Can own and operate in most states |
| Nurse practitioner | Can own in some states, especially with full practice authority |
| Physician assistant | Often needs a supervising or collaborating physician |
| Registered nurse | Usually needs a physician-owned practice or MSO |
| Esthetician | Can own a non-medical spa; medical services need a medical structure |
| Non-medical investor | Commonly uses an MSO with a physician-owned practice |

## The MSO model

The **medical practice** (owned by a physician) handles medical services. The **MSO** (owned by the entrepreneur) provides management, staff, space, marketing and equipment for a fee. Contracts must follow state law.

## Steps to structure ownership

1. Learn your state's CPOM rules
2. Hire a healthcare attorney
3. Choose physician-owned, NP-owned or MSO structure
4. Recruit a medical director
5. Write compliant agreements
6. Get licences and insurance

## Frequently asked questions

### Who can open a medical spa?
It depends on the state; physicians generally can, others often use an MSO.

### Can a PA open a medical spa?
Sometimes, usually with a supervising or collaborating physician.

### Can a nurse practitioner own a med spa?
In some states, especially those with full practice authority.

### Can a non-medical person own a med spa?
Often through an MSO structure with a physician-owned practice.

### What is an MSO?
A management company that provides non-medical services to a medical practice.

### Do I need a lawyer to open a med spa?
Yes, a healthcare attorney is strongly recommended.
