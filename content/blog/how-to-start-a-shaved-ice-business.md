<p class="lede">To start a shaved ice or snow cone business, choose a <strong>setup</strong> (event stand, kiosk, trailer or truck), buy a <strong>commercial shaved ice machine</strong>, <strong>syrups and cups</strong>, get <strong>health and vendor permits</strong>, pick <strong>high-traffic summer locations and events</strong>, and price for strong margins. A small stand can start for <strong>$2,000 to $10,000</strong>; a trailer or truck for <strong>$15,000 to $80,000+</strong>.</p>

## Shaved ice vs snow cones

| | Shaved ice | Snow cone |
|---|---|---|
| Texture | Fine, fluffy | Crunchy, crushed |
| Machine | Block shaver | Ice crusher |
| Syrup | Soaks in | Sits on top |

Many businesses offer both or specialise in Hawaiian-style shave ice.

## Step 1: Choose your setup

| Setup | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Pop-up tent and stand | $2,000 – $8,000 |
| Kiosk / stand-alone building | $10,000 – $40,000 |
| Trailer | $15,000 – $50,000 |
| Truck | $30,000 – $80,000+ |

## Step 2: Equipment

- Commercial shaved ice machine or ice shaver (see [How much are shaved ice machines?](/blog/how-much-are-shaved-ice-machines/))
- Ice storage (freezer or insulated chest)
- Syrup bottles and pumps
- Cups, spoons and straws
- Hand-wash station (often required)
- POS system

## Step 3: Permits

Business licence, health permit, mobile vendor or temporary event permits, sales tax permit and insurance.

## Step 4: Locations

Parks, pools, sports fields, schools, festivals, farmers' markets, church events and private parties.

## Step 5: Pricing and menu

Offer sizes, a range of flavours, creamy toppings and specialty combinations. Shaved ice has very low ingredient cost, so prices often bring high margins.

## Step 6: Seasonality

Peak season is late spring to early autumn. Book school, church and corporate events, and consider adding hot drinks or other treats in cooler months.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a shaved ice business?
Choose a setup, buy a commercial machine and supplies, get permits and work high-traffic summer locations.

### How much does it cost to start a shaved ice business?
From about $2,000 for a stand to $80,000+ for a truck.

### How do I start a snow cone business?
Same steps, using an ice crusher instead of a block shaver.

### Do I need a permit to sell shaved ice?
Usually a health permit and vendor or event permits.

### Is shaved ice profitable?
Yes. Ingredient costs are very low.
