<p class="lede">In digital marketing, <strong>CRO stands for conversion rate optimization</strong>: the practice of <strong>increasing the percentage of website or app visitors who take a desired action</strong>, such as buying, booking, signing up or requesting a quote. Instead of buying more traffic, CRO gets <strong>more results from the traffic you already have</strong> by understanding visitors and improving pages, offers and user experience. (In other fields, CRO can also mean Chief Revenue Officer or Contract Research Organisation.)</p>

## Conversion rate basics

**Conversion rate = conversions ÷ visitors × 100**

If 2,000 visitors produce 60 leads, the conversion rate is 3%. Raise it to 4.5% and you get 90 leads from the same traffic and ad spend. (More: [What is a landing page conversion?](/blog/what-is-a-landing-page-conversion/))

## Why CRO matters

- **Lower acquisition costs:** more customers for the same budget
- **Higher ROI** from ads, SEO and content
- **Better user experience**
- **Customer insight:** you learn what persuades your audience
- **Compounding gains:** small improvements across the funnel multiply

## The CRO process

1. **Define goals and metrics:** primary conversion, micro conversions, revenue per visitor
2. **Analyse data:** analytics funnels, drop-off points, device and source differences
3. **Qualitative research:** heatmaps, session recordings, surveys, user tests, sales and support feedback
4. **Form hypotheses:** "Because users can't find pricing, adding a pricing section will increase demo requests"
5. **Prioritise:** frameworks like PIE or ICE (impact, confidence, ease)
6. **Test:** A/B or multivariate tests with enough traffic
7. **Analyse and implement** winners
8. **Document learnings** and repeat

## What to test

| Element | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Headlines and value proposition** | Benefit-led vs feature-led |
| **Calls to action** | Text, colour, placement, number |
| **Forms** | Fewer fields, multi-step forms |
| **Social proof** | Reviews, logos, case studies near CTAs |
| **Pricing presentation** | Plans, anchors, guarantees |
| **Page layout** | Order of sections, length |
| **Media** | Product video, explainer animation (see [What is an explainer video?](/blog/what-is-an-explainer-video/)) |
| **Checkout** | Guest checkout, payment options |
| **Speed and mobile UX** | Load time, tap targets, sticky CTAs |
| **Trust signals** | Guarantees, contact info, security badges |

## CRO quick wins

- Make the **primary CTA visible above the fold** on mobile
- **Shorten forms**
- Add **WhatsApp/chat and click-to-call**
- Put **reviews next to CTAs**
- **Speed up** pages (images, scripts)
- **Match landing pages to ads** (see [Good landing page experience](/blog/good-landing-page-experience/))
- **Clarify pricing** or "starting from" ranges
- **Answer objections** with an FAQ
- **Follow up instantly** after conversions (see [How to automate your sales process](/blog/how-to-automate-your-sales-process/))

## CRO vs SEO

| | SEO | CRO |
|---|---|---|
| **Goal** | More qualified visitors | More conversions from visitors |
| **Focus** | Rankings and traffic | User behaviour and persuasion |
| **Work together** | Bring traffic | Convert traffic |

## Measuring CRO

- Conversion rate (by page, source, device)
- Revenue per visitor
- Average order value
- Lead quality and close rate (not just volume)
- Statistical significance of tests

## Frequently asked questions

### What does CRO mean?
In marketing, CRO means conversion rate optimization: improving the percentage of visitors who complete a desired action on a website or app.

### What does CRO stand for?
Conversion rate optimization in digital marketing. In other contexts it can stand for Chief Revenue Officer or Contract Research Organisation.

### How does conversion rate optimization work?
By researching visitor behaviour, forming hypotheses about barriers, testing changes (often with A/B tests) and implementing improvements that increase conversions.

### What is a good conversion rate?
It varies by industry and goal; many websites convert 1–5% of visitors, while focused landing pages often convert higher.

### Do I need a lot of traffic for CRO?
A/B testing needs enough traffic for statistical significance, but smaller sites can still improve conversions using research, best practices and qualitative feedback.

### Is CRO part of SEO?
They're separate but complementary: SEO increases qualified traffic, CRO increases how much of that traffic converts.
