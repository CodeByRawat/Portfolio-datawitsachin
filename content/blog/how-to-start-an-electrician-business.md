<p class="lede">To start an electrician business: earn the <strong>electrical contractor or master electrician licence</strong> your state requires (journeyman experience usually comes first), <strong>register the business</strong>, get <strong>liability insurance, workers' comp and any required bond</strong>, buy <strong>tools, testers and a van</strong>, choose a focus (<strong>residential service, remodels, commercial or new construction</strong>), set <strong>pricing</strong>, use <strong>estimating and field service software</strong>, and win work through <strong>Google, reviews, builders and general contractors</strong>.</p>

## Steps

1. **Licensing:** apprenticeship → journeyman → master/contractor licence (state rules vary); local business licence and permits for each job
2. **Business plan:** niche (panel upgrades, EV chargers, generators, solar, lighting, commercial fit-outs), service area, pricing
3. **Register:** LLC or corporation, EIN, business bank account
4. **Insurance and bonding:** general liability, workers' compensation, commercial auto, contractor bond
5. **Tools and van:** meters, testers, benders, fish tapes, drills, ladders, stocked materials
6. **Supplier accounts** with electrical distributors
7. **Pricing:** flat-rate for service calls, detailed estimates for projects (labour units, materials, overhead, margin)
8. **Software:** estimating, scheduling, invoicing and payments
9. **Marketing:** Google Business Profile, reviews, Local Services Ads, a website with service pages and quote form
10. **Relationships:** general contractors, builders, property managers

## Can you own an electrical company without being an electrician?

In many states you can, if a licensed master electrician or qualified individual is designated for the company. Check your state licensing board.

## Profitable niches

EV charger installs, panel upgrades, generators, smart home and lighting, solar, and commercial maintenance contracts.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start an electrician business?
Get the required contractor licence, register and insure the business, buy tools and a van, choose a niche, set prices and market locally.

### What do I need to start my own electrical business?
A state electrical contractor or master licence, business registration, insurance, tools, a vehicle and marketing.

### Can a journeyman electrician start a business?
Usually a master or contractor licence is needed to pull permits as a business; rules vary by state.

### Can you own an electrical company without being an electrician?
In many states, yes, with a licensed qualifier on staff.

### How much does it cost to start an electrical business?
Costs cover licensing, insurance, tools, a van, materials and marketing.

### What electrical services are most profitable?
Panel upgrades, EV chargers, generators and commercial contracts are often lucrative.
