<p class="lede">To manage daycare communication well: set <strong>clear policies</strong> (channels, response times, what's shared when), use a <strong>childcare app</strong> for daily reports, photos, messages, check-in/out and billing, send a <strong>regular newsletter</strong>, handle <strong>incidents and sensitive topics</strong> promptly and privately, keep <strong>staff aligned</strong>, protect <strong>privacy with photo consent</strong>, and <strong>automate</strong> reminders for payments, closures and forms.</p>

## 1. Communication policy

- Official channels (app, email, phone) and what each is for
- Response times (e.g., within one business day)
- Emergency procedures and contact tree
- Photo and social media consent
- Escalation path for concerns

## 2. Daily reports

Meals, naps, nappies/toileting, activities, mood and milestones, shared through an app or printed sheets.

## 3. Parent communication apps

Childcare apps (e.g., Brightwheel, Procare, HiMama/Lillio, Kangarootime and others) typically offer messaging, daily reports, photos, check-in/out, billing and attendance. Choose based on features, ease of use and data privacy.

## 4. Newsletters and announcements

Weekly or monthly updates: upcoming events, curriculum themes, reminders, staff news (see [How to write a newsletter](/blog/how-to-write-a-newsletter/)).

## 5. Incidents and sensitive conversations

Document incidents immediately, inform parents the same day, speak privately and factually, and follow licensing reporting rules.

## 6. Staff communication

Shift handovers, shared notes on children's needs, team meetings and a consistent tone in parent messages.

## 7. Privacy and consent

Only share photos with consent, avoid other children's identifiable information, and use secure platforms.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do daycares communicate with parents?
Through parent apps with daily reports, photos and messages, plus newsletters, emails, phone calls and in-person conversations.

### What is the best daycare communication app?
Popular options include Brightwheel, Procare and Lillio (HiMama); choose based on features, cost and privacy.

### What should be in a daycare daily report?
Meals, naps, toileting, activities, mood, notes and any incidents.

### How should daycares handle complaints from parents?
Listen privately, acknowledge concerns, investigate, respond with clear actions and follow up.

### Can daycares post photos of children?
Only with written parental consent, following privacy policies.

### How can daycares save time on admin?
Use childcare management software and automate billing, reminders, forms and announcements.
