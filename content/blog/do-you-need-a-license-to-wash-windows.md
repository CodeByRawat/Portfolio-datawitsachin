<p class="lede">Most places don't require a <strong>special trade licence</strong> to wash windows, but you usually need a <strong>general business licence</strong> from your city or county, <strong>business registration</strong>, and in some states <strong>sales tax registration</strong>. Customers also expect <strong>liability insurance</strong>, and high-rise or rope access work has its own <strong>safety rules and training</strong>.</p>

## What you typically need

| Requirement | Why |
|---|---|
| Business registration (DBA or LLC) | Legal name and structure |
| General business licence | Required by many cities/counties |
| EIN | For taxes and hiring |
| Sales tax permit | Some states tax cleaning services |
| General liability insurance | Covers property damage and injuries |
| Workers' compensation | Usually required once you hire |
| Bond | Often requested by commercial clients |

## High-rise and rope access work

High-rise window cleaning involves fall protection rules, equipment inspections and trained workers. Don't take on this work without proper training, certification and insurance.

## Certifications

There's usually no mandatory certification for ground and ladder work, but safety training and industry association membership build trust with commercial clients.

## How to check your local rules

Contact your city or county licensing office, your state's business registration site and the state revenue department. Rules vary, so confirm before starting.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do you need a license to wash windows?
Usually not a trade licence, but most cities require a general business licence.

### Do window cleaners need insurance?
It's strongly recommended, and commercial clients often require proof of liability insurance.

### Is window cleaning taxable?
In some states cleaning services are subject to sales tax; check your state.

### Do I need to be bonded?
Not always, but a bond helps you win commercial and residential trust.

### Do I need a licence for high-rise window cleaning?
High-rise work requires safety training and compliance with fall-protection rules.

### How do I register a window cleaning business?
Choose a structure, register the name with your state, get an EIN and apply for local licences.
