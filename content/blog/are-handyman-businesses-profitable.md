<p class="lede">Handyman businesses can be <strong>quite profitable</strong> because startup costs are low and labour is the main product. Profit depends on <strong>pricing (with minimums), billable hours, travel time, materials markup and repeat customers</strong>. Solo handymen earn their labour plus margin; owners with technicians can earn more but take on management and payroll.</p>

## How handymen make money

Hourly labour, flat-rate tasks, minimum service charges, materials markup and maintenance plans.

## Costs

Vehicle and fuel, tools, insurance, marketing, software, and non-billable time (quotes, driving, shopping for materials).

## What makes a handyman business profitable

- Minimum charges to cover travel
- Flat-rate pricing for common tasks
- Grouping jobs by area
- Bundling multiple small jobs per visit
- Repeat customers and maintenance plans
- Online booking to reduce admin

## How to be a successful handyman

Show up on time, communicate clearly, do clean work, stay within licence limits, ask for reviews and follow up with past customers.

## Becoming a self-employed handyman

Register the business, get insured, set pricing, create a Google Business Profile and website, and start with referrals (see [How to start a handyman business](/blog/how-to-start-a-handyman-business/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### Are handyman businesses profitable?
Yes, with proper pricing, minimum charges and efficient scheduling.

### How much can a handyman business make?
It depends on billable hours, pricing and whether you have technicians.

### How do handymen increase profits?
Flat-rate pricing, minimums, bundling jobs, maintenance plans and route efficiency.

### Is it better to charge hourly or flat rate?
Flat rates for common tasks reward efficiency; hourly works for unpredictable repairs.

### How do I become a successful handyman?
Be reliable, communicate well, do quality work, collect reviews and price properly.

### Can a handyman business grow into a company?
Yes, by hiring technicians, adding maintenance plans and building systems.
