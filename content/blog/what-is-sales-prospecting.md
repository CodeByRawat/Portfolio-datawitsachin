<p class="lede"><strong>Sales prospecting</strong> is the process of <strong>finding and contacting potential customers (prospects) who fit your ideal customer profile</strong>, with the goal of starting conversations and creating sales opportunities. It involves <strong>researching</strong> companies and people, <strong>qualifying</strong> whether they're a good fit, <strong>reaching out</strong> through email, phone, LinkedIn or other channels, and <strong>following up</strong> until they become a qualified lead or opportunity, or are ruled out.</p>

## Prospect vs lead vs opportunity

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Lead** | Someone who has shown interest or whose details you have (may be unqualified) |
| **Prospect** | A person or company that fits your ideal customer profile and is being actively pursued |
| **Qualified lead (MQL/SQL)** | Confirmed fit and interest; ready for sales conversations |
| **Opportunity** | A qualified prospect in an active deal process |

Definitions vary between companies; agree on them internally.

## The sales prospecting process

1. **Define your ideal customer profile (ICP):** industry, size, location, technology, pain points, triggers
2. **Build personas:** roles and decision-makers you'll contact
3. **Research and build lists:** company databases, LinkedIn, directories, events, website visitors
4. **Prioritise:** fit, intent signals (hiring, funding, new website, tech changes), timing
5. **Personalise outreach:** relevant problem, insight or result
6. **Multi-channel sequences:** email, LinkedIn, phone, video messages over 2–4 weeks
7. **Qualify** via conversation (frameworks below)
8. **Hand off or book meetings** and log everything in the CRM (see [How to use a CRM](/blog/how-to-use-a-crm/))

## Inbound vs outbound prospecting

| | Inbound | Outbound |
|---|---|---|
| **How** | Prospects come to you via content, SEO, ads, referrals | You reach out first |
| **Examples** | Website enquiries, webinar sign-ups, content downloads | Cold email, calls, LinkedIn messages |
| **Pros** | Warmer, higher intent | Control over targeting and volume |
| **Cons** | Slower to build | Lower response rates, needs skill and compliance |

Most teams combine both. (Inbound foundations: [What is SEO?](/blog/what-is-seo/))

## Prospecting methods

- **Cold email:** short, personalised, one clear ask
- **Cold calling:** still effective in many industries
- **LinkedIn:** connection requests with context, engaging with posts, InMail
- **Referrals and introductions:** highest conversion
- **Events and communities**
- **Trigger-based outreach:** respond to funding, hiring, expansion or website changes
- **Video prospecting:** personalised short videos
- **Partnerships**

## Qualification frameworks

| Framework | Checks |
|---|---|
| **BANT** | Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline |
| **CHAMP** | Challenges, Authority, Money, Prioritisation |
| **MEDDIC** | Metrics, Economic buyer, Decision criteria, Decision process, Identify pain, Champion |
| **GPCTBA/C&I** | Goals, plans, challenges, timeline, budget, authority, consequences and implications |

## Example prospecting email

> Subject: Faster bookings for [Clinic name]
>
> Hi Dr. Patel, I noticed [Clinic] takes bookings by phone only, and your reviews mention long hold times. We set up online booking + WhatsApp reminders for a clinic in [nearby city] and their no-shows dropped 30%. Worth a 15-minute chat next week?

## Prospecting metrics

- Contacts reached per week
- Open, reply and positive reply rates
- Meetings booked
- Conversion from meeting to opportunity
- Pipeline value created
- Time per prospect

## How AI and automation help prospecting

| Task | Automation/AI |
|---|---|
| **List building** | Databases and enrichment tools (Apollo, Clay, ZoomInfo, Lusha-style tools) |
| **Research** | AI summarises a prospect's website, news and LinkedIn |
| **Personalisation** | AI drafts tailored first lines and messages for review |
| **Sequencing** | Outreach tools schedule multi-step follow-ups |
| **CRM updates** | Automatic logging of emails, calls and stages |
| **Lead scoring** | Prioritise by fit and intent signals |
| **Booking** | Calendar links and auto-reminders |

**Keep it human and compliant:** personalise genuinely, respect anti-spam and privacy laws (CAN-SPAM, GDPR and local rules), honour opt-outs and avoid mass, generic outreach that damages your domain reputation.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is sales prospecting?
Finding and contacting potential customers who fit your ideal customer profile to start conversations and create sales opportunities.

### What is the difference between a lead and a prospect?
A lead has shown interest or been captured; a prospect is a qualified fit that sales is actively pursuing. Usage varies by company.

### What are the main prospecting methods?
Cold email, cold calling, LinkedIn outreach, referrals, events, trigger-based outreach, video messages and inbound content.

### What are the steps of sales prospecting?
Define your ICP, research and build lists, prioritise, personalise outreach, follow up across channels, qualify and book meetings, logging everything in a CRM.

### How can AI help with sales prospecting?
AI can research prospects, enrich data, draft personalised messages, score leads and automate follow-ups and CRM updates, with humans reviewing outreach.

### How many follow-ups should I send?
Commonly four to eight touches across two to four weeks and multiple channels, stopping immediately if the prospect opts out.
