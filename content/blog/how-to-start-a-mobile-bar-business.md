<p class="lede">To start a mobile bar business, choose your <strong>bar setup</strong> (converted horse trailer, vintage truck or bar cart), learn your state's <strong>liquor laws</strong> (many mobile bars use a <strong>"dry hire" model</strong> where the client buys the alcohol), get <strong>liquor liability insurance</strong> and any required licences, hire <strong>certified bartenders</strong>, create <strong>packages</strong>, and market to <strong>weddings, corporate events and parties</strong>. Startup costs often range from <strong>$10,000 to $60,000</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Choose your bar setup

| Setup | Rough cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Bar cart | $2,000 – $10,000 | Indoor events, low cost |
| Converted horse trailer | $10,000 – $40,000 | Most popular look |
| Vintage truck or van | $20,000 – $60,000+ | Eye-catching, higher cost |
| Tap trailer | $15,000 – $50,000 | Beer and wine on tap |

## Step 2: Understand liquor laws

Rules vary a lot by state:

- **Dry hire**: the client buys alcohol; you provide the bar, service and bartenders
- **Licensed service**: you sell or supply alcohol under a caterer's or mobile licence (where allowed)

See [Can you get a liquor license for a mobile bar?](/blog/can-you-get-a-liquor-license-for-a-mobile-bar/).

## Step 3: Licences and insurance

Business licence, liquor liability insurance, general liability, server certification for bartenders, and health permits if serving food or mixers.

## Step 4: Startup costs

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Bar trailer or cart | $2,000 – $60,000 |
| Bar tools, glassware, coolers | $1,000 – $5,000 |
| Branding and décor | $500 – $3,000 |
| Insurance | $1,500 – $5,000 per year |
| Website and marketing | $500 – $3,000 |

## Step 5: Packages and pricing

Charge for bar rental plus bartender hours, often in 3–5 hour packages. Add-ons: signature cocktails, mixers and garnishes, mocktails, champagne walls, extra bartenders.

## Step 6: Get bookings

Wedding planners and venues, corporate event planners, bridal shows, Instagram, and a website with packages and an inquiry form.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a mobile bar business?
Choose a bar trailer or cart, learn liquor laws, get insured, hire bartenders and market to events.

### How much does it cost to start a mobile bar?
Often $10,000 to $60,000.

### Do mobile bars need a liquor license?
It depends on your state and whether you sell alcohol; many use a dry-hire model.

### What is a dry hire mobile bar?
The client buys the alcohol; you provide the bar and service.

### Is a mobile bar business profitable?
Yes, especially with wedding and corporate packages.
