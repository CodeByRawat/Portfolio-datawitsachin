<p class="lede">To handle a customer complaint: <strong>listen fully without interrupting</strong>, <strong>acknowledge</strong> the problem and the customer's feelings, <strong>apologise sincerely</strong> for their experience, <strong>investigate</strong> the facts, <strong>offer a clear solution</strong> (and options where possible), <strong>act quickly</strong>, then <strong>follow up</strong> to make sure they're satisfied. Finally, <strong>record the complaint</strong> and fix the root cause so it doesn't happen again. Handled well, complaints can turn unhappy customers into loyal ones.</p>

## The 7-step complaint handling process

1. **Listen actively:** let them explain; take notes; don't defend yet
2. **Acknowledge:** "I understand how frustrating it is to wait two weeks for an order."
3. **Apologise:** for the experience, even while investigating fault
4. **Clarify and investigate:** ask questions, check records, confirm facts
5. **Resolve:** propose a fair solution; offer choices (refund, replacement, credit)
6. **Act fast:** do what you promised, within a stated timeframe
7. **Follow up:** confirm the issue is fixed and they're satisfied

## Popular complaint handling frameworks

| Framework | Steps |
|---|---|
| **LAST** | Listen, Apologise, Solve, Thank |
| **HEARD** (Disney) | Hear, Empathise, Apologise, Resolve, Diagnose |
| **LEAP** | Listen, Empathise, Apologise, Problem-solve |

## Example responses

### Email
> Hi Maria, thank you for letting us know, and I'm sorry your order arrived damaged. That's not the experience we want for you. I've arranged a replacement that will ship today with express delivery at no cost, and you don't need to return the damaged item. You'll receive tracking within the hour. I'll check in on Friday to make sure it arrived safely. — Sam

### Phone or chat
> "I'm really sorry about this. Let me pull up your account so I can fix it right now… I can see the charge was duplicated. I've refunded $49; you'll see it in 3–5 business days. Is there anything else I can help with?"

### Public online review
> "Thank you for your feedback, Priya. We're sorry your appointment started late. That's not the standard we hold ourselves to. We'd like to make this right; please contact us at [email/phone] so we can help." (Keep it short, polite and move details offline. Never reveal private information.)

## Handling angry customers

- **Stay calm** and don't take it personally
- **Lower your voice and pace**
- **Use their name**
- **Focus on what you can do**, not what you can't
- **Set boundaries** for abusive behaviour and escalate when needed
- **Don't argue about fault** in the moment

## What not to do

- Ignore or delay responses
- Blame the customer or other departments
- Use defensive or robotic scripts
- Over-promise
- Close the ticket without confirming resolution

## Complaint handling policy essentials

- Channels for complaints (email, phone, chat, WhatsApp, forms)
- **Response time targets** (e.g., acknowledge within 1 hour, resolve within 48 hours)
- **Escalation paths** and authority levels for refunds/credits
- **Logging and categorisation** in a CRM or helpdesk (see [What is Zendesk used for?](/blog/what-is-zendesk-used-for/))
- **Root cause reviews** monthly
- **Regulatory requirements** in sectors like finance, healthcare and telecoms

## Track complaints to prevent repeats

Categorise complaints (delivery, billing, quality, staff, product) and track volume, resolution time, satisfaction and repeat rate. Fix root causes: process changes, product improvements, clearer communication.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you handle customer complaints?
Listen, acknowledge, apologise, investigate, offer a fair solution, act quickly and follow up, then log the complaint and fix its root cause.

### What is the best way to respond to a complaint?
Respond quickly, personally and empathetically, take ownership, explain what you'll do and by when, and follow through.

### How do you handle an angry customer?
Stay calm, listen without interrupting, acknowledge their feelings, focus on solutions and escalate if behaviour becomes abusive.

### What is the LAST method?
Listen, Apologise, Solve, Thank: a simple framework for handling complaints.

### Should you respond to negative reviews?
Yes. Respond politely and briefly, apologise for the experience, and invite the customer to continue the conversation privately.

### Can AI handle customer complaints?
AI can acknowledge, triage, draft replies and resolve simple issues, while humans handle complex or sensitive complaints.
