<p class="lede">Contractors often charge around <strong>$4 to $12 per square foot</strong> installed for vinyl siding, including materials and labour, depending on the <strong>siding grade, home size, removal of old siding, trim work and region</strong>. Labour alone is often <strong>$2 to $5 per square foot</strong>. Siding pros often price by the <strong>square</strong> (100 square feet).</p>

These are rough ranges; get local quotes for accurate prices.

## What's included

- Removal and disposal of old siding (sometimes extra)
- House wrap or underlayment
- Vinyl siding panels
- Trim, corners, J-channel, soffit and fascia (sometimes extra)
- Labour and cleanup

## What affects the price

| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| Siding grade (thickness, insulated) | Better grades cost more |
| Removal of old siding | Adds labour and dump fees |
| Number of stories | Taller homes cost more |
| Trim and detail work | Windows, corners, gables |
| Insulated backer | Higher cost, better efficiency |
| Repairs to sheathing | Added if rot is found |
| Region | Labour costs vary |

## Example

A 2,000 sq ft wall area at $6–$9 per sq ft is roughly **$12,000 to $18,000**, before extras like soffit, fascia or sheathing repairs.

## How siding contractors build quotes

1. Measure wall area in squares, subtract windows and doors
2. Add waste (often 10%)
3. Price materials and accessories
4. Add labour per square
5. Add removal, dump fees and repairs
6. Add overhead and profit

## Tips for homeowners

Get three detailed quotes, check licenses and insurance, ask about warranties and look at past work.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much do contractors charge to install vinyl siding?
Often $4 to $12 per square foot installed.

### How much is labour to install vinyl siding?
Often $2 to $5 per square foot.

### What is a square of siding?
100 square feet of wall area.

### Is removing old siding extra?
Often yes, including disposal.

### How long does vinyl siding installation take?
Often a few days to a week for a typical house.
