<p class="lede"><strong>Adobe After Effects</strong> is software for <strong>motion graphics, animation, visual effects and compositing</strong>. It's used to animate text, logos, shapes and illustrations; create explainer videos, social ads, title sequences and lower thirds; and combine layers of footage and graphics with effects such as green-screen keying, motion tracking and particle systems. It's not a video editor for cutting long footage (that's Premiere Pro); it's where short, layered, animated shots are built.</p>

After Effects (often shortened to "AE") has been the industry standard for motion design for decades. This guide explains what it's used for, how it differs from Premiere Pro, its key features, cost, learning curve and alternatives.

## What is After Effects used for?

| Use | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Motion graphics** | Animated infographics, explainer videos, kinetic typography (see [What are motion graphics?](/blog/what-are-motion-graphics/)) |
| **Advertising** | Social video ads, product promos, animated banners (see [How to make an advertisement](/blog/how-to-make-an-advertisement/)) |
| **Logo animation and brand idents** | Intros and outros for YouTube, corporate videos |
| **Titles and lower thirds** | Film titles, TV graphics, event screens |
| **Visual effects (VFX)** | Green-screen compositing, sky replacement, screen replacements, muzzle flashes, light effects |
| **Motion tracking** | Attaching text or graphics to moving objects in footage |
| **Rotoscoping and cleanup** | Isolating subjects with Roto Brush, removing objects with Content-Aware Fill |
| **UI and app animation** | Prototyping interface motion; exporting to Lottie for web and apps |
| **Character animation (2D)** | Rigging and animating simple characters with tools like Puppet Pin or plugins |
| **3D integration** | Working with 3D models and cameras, often alongside Cinema 4D or Blender |

## After Effects vs Premiere Pro

| | After Effects | Premiere Pro |
|---|---|---|
| **Main job** | Creating animated and composited shots | Editing footage into a timeline story |
| **Typical length** | Seconds to a few minutes per composition | Minutes to hours |
| **Strength** | Layers, keyframes, effects, compositing, expressions | Cutting, sequencing, audio, colour, delivery |
| **Workflow** | Build the shot | Assemble the video |

Most professionals use both: edit in Premiere, send shots to After Effects via **Dynamic Link** for animation or effects, and bring them back.

## Key features

- **Compositions and layers:** stack footage, shapes, text, images and adjustment layers
- **Keyframes and the Graph Editor:** precise control over movement and easing
- **Shape layers:** vector shapes animated natively
- **Text animators:** per-character, per-word and per-line text animation
- **Effects library:** blurs, glows, distortions, colour correction, particles, keying (Keylight)
- **Masks and track mattes:** reveal and hide parts of layers
- **Expressions:** small JavaScript-based scripts (e.g., `wiggle()`, `loopOut()`) to automate animation
- **3D layers and cameras**, plus native 3D model support in recent versions
- **Motion tracking and 3D camera tracking**
- **Roto Brush and Content-Aware Fill** (AI-assisted)
- **Essential Graphics templates** (MOGRTs) for reuse in Premiere Pro
- **Plugins and scripts:** ecosystem including Trapcode, Element 3D, Duik, Motion Tools, Overlord

## Is After Effects hard to learn?

**The basics are approachable; mastery takes time.** A typical beginner path:

| Stage | What you learn | Rough time with regular practice |
|---|---|---|
| Interface and basics | Compositions, layers, keyframes, exporting | 1–2 weeks |
| Core motion skills | Easing, Graph Editor, shape layers, text animation, masks | 3–6 weeks |
| Intermediate | Expressions, parenting, track mattes, tracking, sound sync | 2–4 months |
| Professional | Design sense, timing, efficient workflows, client work | 6–12+ months |

The hardest part isn't the buttons but **design and timing**: knowing what to animate, how fast and why. (A hands-on walkthrough: [How to make motion graphics](/blog/how-to-make-motion-graphics/).)

## How much does Adobe After Effects cost?

After Effects is sold by **subscription** through Adobe Creative Cloud: either as a single app or within the Creative Cloud All Apps plan (which adds Premiere Pro, Illustrator, Photoshop and more). Monthly, annual-billed-monthly and prepaid annual options are available, and students and teachers often get large discounts. Prices change and vary by country, so check Adobe's website for current pricing. There's a free trial, but no permanently free version.

## System requirements (in practice)

After Effects is demanding. For comfortable work:
- A modern multi-core CPU (Apple Silicon M-series or recent Intel/AMD)
- **16 GB RAM minimum, 32 GB+ recommended**
- A GPU with good support for GPU-accelerated effects
- Fast SSD storage, ideally a separate cache drive

## Common After Effects questions

### How do I get the Effects panel?
Go to **Window › Effects & Presets** (shortcut Ctrl/Cmd+5). Drag an effect onto a layer, then adjust it in the **Effect Controls** panel (F3).

### How do I add an expression?
Alt-click (Option-click on Mac) the stopwatch next to a property and type the expression, such as `wiggle(2,20)`.

### How do I import an Illustrator file?
File › Import, choose the .ai file and import as **Composition – Retain Layer Sizes**. Right-click a layer › Create › **Create Shapes from Vector Layer** to animate paths natively.

### Can After Effects edit videos?
It can trim and arrange clips, but it's slow for long edits. Use Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve for editing.

## Should you learn After Effects or hire someone?

Learn it if motion design is a skill you want to build or you'll produce content constantly. Hire a motion designer when you need a polished result on a deadline, campaign-level consistency, 3D or complex compositing, or your time is better spent running the business.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Adobe After Effects used for?
After Effects is used for motion graphics, animated text and logos, explainer videos, ads, title sequences, visual effects, compositing, motion tracking and UI animation.

### Is After Effects used for video editing?
Not mainly. It's for creating animated and composited shots; Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve are better for editing longer videos.

### Is After Effects free?
No. It requires an Adobe Creative Cloud subscription, though a free trial is available and students often get discounts.

### Is After Effects hard to learn?
The basics take a few weeks of practice; becoming professional takes months, mostly because good animation depends on design and timing skills.

### What is better, After Effects or Premiere Pro?
They do different jobs: Premiere Pro edits footage into a story, After Effects builds animated graphics and effects. Most video professionals use both together.

### What are free alternatives to After Effects?
DaVinci Resolve's Fusion page, Blender, Cavalry's free tier and HitFilm offer motion graphics and compositing features without paying for Adobe.
