<p class="lede">To become a chimney sweep, <strong>learn on the job</strong> with an established chimney company, study <strong>fireplace and venting safety codes</strong>, earn a <strong>certification</strong> (such as CSIA Certified Chimney Sweep), and get comfortable with <strong>ladders and roofs</strong>. Many sweeps later start their own business offering <strong>sweeping, inspections, repairs and dryer vent cleaning</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Learn the trade

Work as a helper with a chimney company. You'll learn sweeping methods, inspections, liners, caps, crowns, dampers and safety.

## Step 2: Get certified

Certification programs cover codes, venting, inspection levels and safety. Certification builds customer trust and is sometimes required by local rules or insurers.

## Step 3: Skills you need

- Comfort working at heights
- Understanding of fireplaces, wood stoves, gas and oil appliances
- Inspection and camera skills
- Customer communication
- Basic masonry and repair

## Step 4: Licensing

Some states and cities require chimney or contractor licences, especially for repairs and liner installation. See [Do chimney sweeps need a license?](/blog/do-chimney-sweeps-need-a-license/).

## Do chimney sweeps work in winter?

Yes. Autumn and early winter are the busiest. In summer many sweeps focus on repairs, liners and dryer vent cleaning.

## Pay

Employed sweeps often earn around **$35,000 to $60,000**, and business owners can earn more with repairs and inspections.

## Starting your own business

Get insured, set up booking, partner with realtors and home inspectors, and add repairs and dryer vents for year-round income.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I become a chimney sweep?
Train with a chimney company, learn codes and safety, and get certified.

### Do you need a license to be a chimney sweep?
Some areas require one; many require at least a business licence.

### Do chimney sweeps work in the winter?
Yes. It's the busiest season.

### How much do chimney sweeps make?
Often $35,000 to $60,000 employed; owners can earn more.

### Is chimney sweeping a good business?
Yes. Low startup costs and repeat customers.
