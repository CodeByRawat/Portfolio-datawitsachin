<p class="lede">E-commerce sales grow through four levers: <strong>more qualified traffic</strong>, <strong>higher conversion rate</strong>, <strong>higher average order value</strong>, and <strong>more repeat purchases</strong>. The fastest wins are usually on-site: <strong>better product pages and reviews</strong>, <strong>faster mobile checkout</strong>, <strong>free-shipping thresholds and bundles</strong>, and <strong>automated email/SMS flows</strong> (welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase), before spending more on ads.</p>

## 1. Get more qualified traffic

1. **SEO** for product and category pages, plus buying guides (see [What is SEO?](/blog/what-is-seo/))
2. **Google Shopping and Performance Max**
3. **Meta/TikTok ads** with UGC and short videos (see [How to advertise your business on Facebook](/blog/how-to-advertise-your-business-on-facebook/))
4. **Influencers and creators**
5. **Marketplaces** for discovery (see [How to choose the right marketplace](/blog/how-to-choose-the-right-marketplace-for-your-business/))
6. **Social shopping** (see [What is social shopping?](/blog/what-is-social-shopping/))

## 2. Improve conversion rate

7. **Great product pages:** multiple photos, video, benefits, sizing, FAQs
8. **Reviews and UGC** with photos
9. **Fast mobile site** and simple navigation/search
10. **Streamlined checkout:** guest checkout, express pay, fewer fields
11. **Trust signals:** returns policy, secure payment, contact info
12. **Clear shipping costs and delivery dates** early
(More: [What does CRO mean?](/blog/what-does-cro-mean/))

## 3. Raise average order value

13. **Free shipping threshold**
14. **Bundles and kits**
15. **Cross-sells and upsells** on product, cart and post-purchase pages
16. **Volume discounts**

## 4. Increase repeat purchases

17. **Email/SMS flows:** welcome, browse/cart abandonment, post-purchase, replenishment, win-back (see [How to write a newsletter](/blog/how-to-write-a-newsletter/))
18. **Loyalty programme**
19. **Subscriptions** for consumables
20. **Excellent customer experience** (see [How to improve ecommerce customer experience](/blog/how-to-improve-ecommerce-customer-experience/))

## Metrics to track

| Metric | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Sessions by channel | Traffic quality |
| Conversion rate | Orders ÷ sessions |
| Average order value (AOV) | Revenue ÷ orders |
| Cart abandonment rate | Checkout friction |
| Repeat purchase rate | Loyalty |
| Customer lifetime value | Long-term value (see [What is LTV?](/blog/what-is-ltv/)) |
| CAC and ROAS | Ad efficiency |

**Revenue = traffic × conversion rate × AOV**, plus repeat purchases. Small improvements in each multiply.

## Frequently asked questions

### How can I increase ecommerce sales?
Drive qualified traffic, improve conversion with better product pages and checkout, raise average order value with bundles and thresholds, and increase repeat purchases with email/SMS and loyalty.

### How do I increase online sales quickly?
Fix checkout friction, add reviews, launch abandoned-cart emails, offer a free shipping threshold and run promotions to your existing customers.

### What is a good ecommerce conversion rate?
Often around 1–4% depending on industry, traffic source and device; improve against your own baseline.

### How do I get more traffic to my online store?
SEO, Google Shopping, social ads, influencers, marketplaces and content.

### How do I increase average order value?
Bundles, upsells, cross-sells, volume discounts and free-shipping thresholds.

### What emails should every online store send?
Welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase, review request, replenishment and win-back emails.
