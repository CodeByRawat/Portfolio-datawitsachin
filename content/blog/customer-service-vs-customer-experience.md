<p class="lede"><strong>Customer service</strong> is the <strong>help and support</strong> a business gives customers when they ask a question or have a problem, through phone, email, chat, WhatsApp or in person. <strong>Customer experience (CX)</strong> is the customer's <strong>overall perception of every interaction with your brand</strong> across the entire journey: discovering you, visiting your website, buying, using the product, getting support and renewing. Customer service is one important part of customer experience.</p>

## Customer service vs customer experience

| | Customer service | Customer experience |
|---|---|---|
| **Scope** | Support interactions | Entire journey, every touchpoint |
| **Timing** | Usually reactive (when customers reach out) | Proactive and continuous |
| **Owned by** | Support/service team | Whole company (marketing, product, sales, ops, support) |
| **Examples** | Answering a billing question, fixing an order | Easy website, smooth checkout, clear onboarding, helpful support, renewal |
| **Metrics** | First response time, resolution time, CSAT, FCR | NPS, CES, retention, churn, lifetime value |
| **Goal** | Solve problems well | Make the whole relationship easy and valuable |

## What does customer service mean?

Customer service means **helping customers before, during and after a purchase**: answering questions, solving problems, processing returns and making customers feel valued. Good service is fast, friendly, knowledgeable and effective.

## What does great customer service mean to you? (Interview answer guide)

A common interview question. Strong answers cover:
- **Understanding the customer's need** and listening
- **Solving the problem quickly** and completely
- **Empathy and respect**
- **Going a step further** when appropriate
- **Following up**

**Example answer:** "Great customer service means making the customer feel heard and leaving them better off than when they contacted us. That means listening carefully, solving the problem quickly and accurately, being honest about what we can do, and following up so they don't have to chase us."

## What is good guest service?

In hospitality, **guest service** means anticipating guests' needs, personalised warm interactions, cleanliness, responsiveness and solving issues gracefully. It's customer service with an emphasis on hospitality and atmosphere.

## How to improve customer service

1. **Fast responses** on the channels customers prefer
2. **Knowledgeable staff** with good internal documentation
3. **Empower agents** to resolve issues without escalations
4. **Self-service:** FAQs, help centre, order tracking
5. **AI chat and WhatsApp** for 24/7 answers (see [How to create an AI chatbot](/blog/how-to-create-an-ai-chatbot/))
6. **Consistent complaint handling** (see [How to handle customer complaints](/blog/how-to-handle-customer-complaints/))
7. **Measure CSAT** after interactions

## How to improve customer experience

1. **Map the customer journey** from discovery to renewal
2. **Remove friction:** fast website, simple booking and checkout (see [What does CRO mean?](/blog/what-does-cro-mean/))
3. **Clear communication:** confirmations, reminders, status updates
4. **Great onboarding**
5. **Personalisation** using CRM data (see [What is a CRM?](/blog/what-is-a-crm/))
6. **Proactive service:** solve problems before customers notice
7. **Collect feedback** at key moments and act on it
8. **Align teams** around shared CX goals

## Key metrics

| Metric | Measures |
|---|---|
| **CSAT** (Customer Satisfaction) | Satisfaction with an interaction |
| **NPS** (Net Promoter Score) | Likelihood to recommend |
| **CES** (Customer Effort Score) | How easy it was to get something done |
| **FCR** (First Contact Resolution) | Issues solved in one interaction |
| **First response / resolution time** | Speed |
| **Retention and churn** | Long-term loyalty |
| **LTV** | Value of the relationship (see [What is LTV?](/blog/what-is-ltv/)) |

## How AI is changing service and experience

AI agents answer common questions instantly, triage and route tickets, assist human agents with suggested replies, personalise journeys and analyse feedback at scale. The best results combine AI speed with human empathy for complex issues. ## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between customer service and customer experience?
Customer service is the support provided when customers need help; customer experience is the customer's overall perception across every interaction with your brand.

### What does customer service mean?
Helping customers before, during and after purchase by answering questions, solving problems and making them feel valued.

### What does great customer service mean to you?
Listening carefully, solving problems quickly and completely, showing empathy, being honest and following up so the customer leaves better off.

### Is customer experience more important than customer service?
CX is broader and includes service. Great service can't fully make up for a poor overall experience, so both matter.

### Who is responsible for customer experience?
Everyone: marketing, sales, product, operations and support all shape the customer's experience.

### How do you measure customer experience?
With metrics like NPS, CSAT, Customer Effort Score, retention, churn and customer lifetime value, plus qualitative feedback.
