<p class="lede">To start a consulting business: turn your <strong>expertise into a specific niche</strong> (e.g., operations consulting for manufacturers, marketing consulting for SaaS, management consulting for family businesses), define a <strong>clear offer with deliverables</strong>, set up the <strong>business and contracts</strong>, choose <strong>pricing</strong> (projects, retainers, value-based), build <strong>credibility</strong> (website, LinkedIn, case studies), win <strong>first clients from your network</strong>, and deliver results that create <strong>referrals</strong>.</p>

## Steps

1. **Niche:** expertise + industry + problem you solve
2. **Offer:** diagnostic/audit, strategy project, implementation, ongoing advisory retainer
3. **Business setup:** registration, insurance (professional liability), contracts, invoicing
4. **Pricing:** fixed projects or value-based, plus retainers; avoid only hourly
5. **Credibility:** website with services and case studies (see [How to create a website for your business](/blog/how-to-create-a-website-for-your-business/)), LinkedIn content, speaking
6. **First clients:** former employers, colleagues, network, referrals
7. **Proposals:** problem, goals, approach, deliverables, timeline, investment
8. **Delivery:** clear milestones, regular updates, measurable outcomes
9. **Referrals and case studies**
10. **Scale:** associates, productised offers, workshops, online courses

## Starting a marketing consulting business

Specialise in a channel or industry, offer audits and strategy, and partner with agencies for execution (see [How to start a marketing agency](/blog/how-to-start-a-marketing-agency/)).

## Starting a management consulting business

Focus on operational or strategic problems where you have proven experience; diagnostics and implementation support work well for SMEs (see [What does a consulting firm do?](/blog/what-does-a-consulting-firm-do/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a consulting business?
Define a niche from your expertise, create a clear offer, set up the business and contracts, set pricing, build credibility and win first clients from your network.

### How do consultants get their first clients?
From former employers and colleagues, their network, referrals and LinkedIn content.

### How much should I charge as a consultant?
Base pricing on the value of outcomes and your expertise; project and retainer pricing are common.

### Do I need certifications to be a consultant?
Not usually; proven expertise and results matter most, though certifications help in some fields.

### How do I start a marketing consulting business?
Specialise, offer audits and strategy, build case studies and partner with execution agencies.

### How do I start a management consulting business?
Focus on problems you've solved before, offer diagnostics and implementation support, and build case studies.
