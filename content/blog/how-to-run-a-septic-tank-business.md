<p class="lede">To run a septic tank business well, <strong>plan efficient routes</strong>, keep your <strong>pump truck maintained</strong>, control <strong>disposal costs</strong>, price to cover <strong>truck time and dump fees</strong>, sell <strong>inspections, risers and repairs</strong>, use <strong>reminders for repeat pumping</strong>, train for <strong>safety</strong>, and keep <strong>records for compliance</strong>.</p>

## 1. Routing and scheduling

Group jobs by area to cut drive time, and keep space for emergency calls (backups pay well). Use field service software for scheduling, invoicing and job notes.

## 2. Truck maintenance

Your pump truck is your business. Follow maintenance schedules for the vacuum pump, engine, tank and hoses. A breakdown means lost income.

## 3. Disposal costs

Know your cost per gallon at the treatment plant or land application site. Plan routes to avoid extra dump trips.

## 4. Pricing and upsells

| Service | Why add it |
|---|---|
| Inspections | Required for many home sales |
| Risers and lids | Easier future access, one-time sale |
| Filters and baffles | Common repairs |
| Camera inspections | Diagnose problems |
| Maintenance plans | Recurring revenue |

## 5. Customer reminders

Record tank size, location and last pump date for every customer. Send reminders when they're due (often every 3–5 years). This builds repeat business.

## 6. Safety

Never enter septic tanks without proper confined-space procedures. Watch for gases, collapses and traffic. Train every driver.

## 7. Hiring

Hire reliable drivers with the right licence (often a CDL) and train them in customer service and safety.

## 8. Records and compliance

Keep manifests and disposal records, licences and permits current, and follow local reporting rules.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you run a septic tank business?
Route efficiently, maintain your truck, control disposal costs, upsell services and use reminders for repeat work.

### What software do septic companies use?
Field service software for scheduling, routing, invoicing and customer records.

### How often do customers need pumping?
Often every 3 to 5 years, depending on tank size and household.

### What is the biggest cost in a septic business?
The pump truck, followed by fuel, disposal fees and labour.

### Is septic work dangerous?
It can be; confined spaces and gases need strict safety procedures.
