<p class="lede">You can make money welding by <strong>getting certified for high-paying jobs</strong> (pipe, structural, underwater, travel work), running a <strong>mobile welding business</strong>, doing <strong>fabrication and repair side jobs</strong>, or <strong>making and selling welded products</strong> like fire pits, furniture, signs and art from home. Business owners often earn <strong>$60,000 to $150,000+</strong>.</p>

## 1. High-paying welding jobs

| Specialty | Why it pays |
|---|---|
| Pipe welding | High skill, strong demand |
| Structural / certified welding | Codes and certifications |
| Travel / shutdown work | Long hours, per diem |
| Underwater welding | Dangerous, specialised |
| Aerospace and TIG | Precision work |

Getting more certifications usually means more pay.

## 2. Mobile welding

Repair farm equipment, trailers, gates and structures on site. See [How to start a mobile welding business](/blog/how-to-start-a-mobile-welding-business/).

## 3. Side jobs and repairs

Trailer repairs, gates, railings, equipment fixes and custom work for neighbours, farms and small businesses.

## 4. Make and sell welded products

Popular items:

- Fire pits and grills
- Furniture and tables
- Metal signs and wall art
- Garden decor and planters
- Trailer and truck accessories
- Racks and storage

Sell at markets, online marketplaces and social media, or through your own website. Price for materials, consumables, time and profit.

## 5. Welding from home

You can weld from a home shop if local zoning, insurance and safety allow. Start with small products and repair jobs.

## How much do welding business owners make?

Solo welding businesses often bring in $80,000 to $200,000 in revenue, with owners earning $60,000 to $150,000+, depending on rates and how busy they are.

More: [How to start a welding career](/blog/how-to-start-a-welding-career/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How can I make good money welding?
Get certified for high-paying specialties, take travel work, or run your own welding business.

### How can I make extra money welding?
Side repairs, mobile welding and selling welded products.

### Can you make money welding at home?
Yes, by making and selling products or doing small repairs, if zoning allows.

### How much do welding business owners make?
Often $60,000 to $150,000+.

### What welded products sell best?
Fire pits, furniture, signs, garden decor and custom racks.
