<p class="lede">Often, yes. An Airbnb is commonly treated as a <strong>business activity</strong> for licences and local taxes, and hosts usually need <strong>short-term rental permits</strong>, <strong>occupancy tax registration</strong> and proper <strong>insurance</strong>. For income tax, whether it's <strong>rental income or business income</strong> depends on factors like <strong>average guest stay and services you provide</strong>, so get advice from a tax professional.</p>

## Business vs rental activity

| Factor | Points toward |
|---|---|
| Hotel-like services (meals, daily cleaning, concierge) | Business income |
| Short average stays | Can change tax treatment |
| Long stays, minimal services | Rental income |
| Regular, continuous activity with profit motive | Business |

Rules are detailed and change, so confirm your situation with a qualified tax adviser.

## Taxes hosts typically deal with

- **Income tax** on profit
- Possibly **self-employment tax** if treated as a business
- **Occupancy/lodging taxes** (sometimes collected by the platform, sometimes by you)
- **Sales tax** in some states

## Licences and permits

Many cities require a **short-term rental permit or registration**, a **business licence**, safety inspections, and display of a permit number on listings.

## Should you form an LLC?

An LLC can help separate business and personal finances and may offer liability protection. Consider lender rules, costs and insurance, and get legal advice.

## Insurance

Standard homeowner policies often exclude short-term rentals. Look at **short-term rental insurance** or commercial policies, and understand what the platform's host protection does and doesn't cover.

## Record keeping

Track income, platform fees, cleaning, supplies, utilities, repairs, mileage and depreciation with accounting software.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is an Airbnb considered a business?
Often yes for local licensing and taxes; income tax treatment depends on stays and services.

### Does Airbnb count as a business for taxes?
It depends on your services and guest stays; ask a tax professional.

### Do I need a business licence for Airbnb?
Many cities require one, plus a short-term rental permit.

### Should my Airbnb be an LLC?
It can help separate finances and liability; get legal and tax advice.

### Do Airbnb hosts pay self-employment tax?
Sometimes, when hosting is treated as a business with substantial services.

### What insurance does an Airbnb host need?
Short-term rental or commercial coverage, since regular homeowner policies may exclude it.
