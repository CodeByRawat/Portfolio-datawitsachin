<p class="lede">To start a bakery: choose your <strong>model</strong> (retail bakery, cake shop, café-bakery, wholesale, or home/online bakery), write a <strong>business plan</strong>, get <strong>licences and food safety certification</strong>, find a <strong>location</strong> and commercial kitchen equipment, design a <strong>focused menu</strong> with costed recipes and pricing, line up <strong>suppliers</strong>, hire staff, set up <strong>POS and online ordering</strong> for custom cakes and pre-orders, and market through <strong>Instagram, Google and local partnerships</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Choose a bakery model

| Model | Notes |
|---|---|
| Retail bakery | Bread, pastries, walk-in customers |
| Cake shop | Custom/celebration cakes, pre-orders |
| Café-bakery | Coffee + baked goods; higher footfall |
| Wholesale bakery | Supply cafés, restaurants, grocers |
| Home/online bakery | Lower costs; cottage food rules (see [How to start a home baking business](/blog/how-to-start-a-home-baking-business/)) |
| Speciality (vegan, gluten-free) | Niche demand |

## Step 2: Business plan and costs

Rent, build-out, ovens and mixers, refrigeration, display cases, licences, insurance, initial ingredients, marketing and working capital. Commercial bakeries often cost tens to hundreds of thousands to open; home bakeries far less.

## Step 3: Licences and food safety

Business licence, food establishment permit, health inspection, food handler/manager certification, labelling rules (allergens), sales tax registration, signage permits.

## Step 4: Location and equipment

Foot traffic, parking, ventilation, power/gas capacity, and space for production and display. Equipment: deck/convection ovens, mixers, proofers, refrigeration, work tables.

## Step 5: Menu and pricing

Start with signature items; cost every recipe (ingredients + packaging + labour + overhead) and target healthy margins; price custom cakes by servings and complexity.

## Step 6: Suppliers and staff

Flour, butter, eggs, packaging suppliers; bakers, decorators and front-of-house staff with early-morning schedules.

## Step 7: Systems

POS, inventory, production planning, and **online ordering for custom cakes and pre-orders** (see [How to make a booking website](/blog/how-to-make-a-booking-website/)).

## Step 8: Marketing

Instagram Reels of baking and decorating, Google Business Profile and reviews, local partnerships (cafés, wedding planners, offices), loyalty cards, seasonal specials (see [How to increase restaurant sales](/blog/how-to-increase-restaurant-sales/)).

## How much do bakery owners make?

Income varies widely by size, location, pricing and costs; many small bakeries run on thin margins early, while efficient bakeries with strong wholesale or custom cake demand can be quite profitable.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a bakery?
Choose a model, write a business plan, get licences and food safety certification, find a location and equipment, create a costed menu, line up suppliers and staff, set up online ordering and market locally.

### How much does it cost to open a bakery?
From modest amounts for a home bakery to tens or hundreds of thousands of dollars for a commercial retail bakery.

### Do I need a licence to open a bakery?
Yes, typically a business licence, food establishment permit and health inspection; home bakeries may fall under cottage food laws.

### Is a bakery business profitable?
It can be with good pricing, low waste, strong demand and additional revenue from custom orders or wholesale.

### How do I start a cake shop business?
Focus on custom and celebration cakes, build a portfolio, offer online ordering with deposits and market on Instagram and with event planners.

### How much do bakery owners make?
It varies widely depending on sales volume, margins, rent and staffing.
