<p class="lede">To become an exterminator (pest control technician): typically have a <strong>high school diploma</strong> and <strong>driver's licence</strong>, get hired by a pest control company for <strong>on-the-job training</strong>, study for and pass your state's <strong>pesticide applicator exam</strong> in the relevant <strong>certification categories</strong>, then maintain the licence with <strong>continuing education</strong>. Many technicians later become certified operators or start their own business.</p>

## Steps

1. **Basic requirements:** usually high school diploma/GED, clean driving record, background check
2. **Get hired as a trainee/technician** and learn under a licensed applicator
3. **Study:** pest identification, pesticide safety, labels, laws and application methods
4. **Pass state exams:** a core exam plus category exams (general pest, termites, fumigation, lawn and ornamental)
5. **Get licensed/certified** as a commercial applicator or technician per state rules
6. **Continuing education** to renew your licence

## Skills

Attention to detail, customer communication, physical stamina, safety mindset, problem-solving and record-keeping.

## Career path

Technician → senior/termite technician → route or branch manager → certified operator → business owner (see [How to start a pest control business](/blog/how-to-start-a-pest-control-business/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I become an exterminator?
Get hired for training, study pest control and pesticide safety, pass your state's applicator exam and maintain your licence.

### Do exterminators need a licence?
Yes; applying pesticides commercially requires state licensing or registration.

### How long does it take to become an exterminator?
Often a few weeks to months of training before licensing, depending on the state.

### Do I need a degree to be an exterminator?
No; most positions require a high school diploma and on-the-job training.

### What is a certified pest control operator?
A higher-level licence often required to run or supervise a pest control business.

### Can exterminators start their own business?
Yes, once they meet state licensing requirements for business owners or operators.
