<p class="lede">To get into photography: learn the <strong>basics</strong> (the <strong>exposure triangle</strong> of aperture, shutter speed and ISO, plus <strong>composition and light</strong>), start with the camera you have (even a phone), <strong>practise daily</strong> with small projects, learn <strong>editing</strong>, study photographers you admire, get <strong>feedback</strong>, find your <strong>style and niche</strong>, and build a <strong>portfolio</strong> if you want to earn from it.</p>

## The basics of photography

| Concept | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Aperture (f-stop)** | Lens opening: controls light and depth of field (background blur) |
| **Shutter speed** | How long the sensor is exposed: freezes or blurs motion |
| **ISO** | Sensor sensitivity: brighter images but more noise at high values |
| **White balance** | Colour accuracy under different lights |
| **Focus** | What's sharp; autofocus modes and points |
| **Composition** | Rule of thirds, leading lines, framing, simplicity |
| **Light** | Direction, quality (soft/hard), golden hour |

## First camera

Your phone is a great start. When ready, an entry-level mirrorless camera with a versatile lens; a fast prime lens (e.g., 35mm or 50mm) is an affordable upgrade for portraits and low light.

## Learn and practise

- 30-day photo challenges
- Shoot in manual or aperture priority mode
- Study light at different times of day
- Recreate photos you admire
- Join communities and ask for critique

## Editing

Learn basic adjustments (exposure, contrast, white balance, crop) in Lightroom, Snapseed or similar; develop a consistent style.

## Find your niche

Portraits, landscapes, street, events, products, food, pets, sports.

## Turn it into income

Build a portfolio (see [What is a photography portfolio?](/blog/what-is-a-photography-portfolio/)), start with friends and small paid shoots, sell stock (see [How to sell stock video footage](/blog/how-to-sell-stock-video-footage/)), and when ready start a business (see [How to start a photography business](/blog/how-to-start-a-photography-business/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I get into photography?
Learn exposure, composition and light, practise daily with the camera you have, learn editing, get feedback and build a portfolio.

### What are the basics of photography?
The exposure triangle (aperture, shutter speed, ISO), focus, white balance, composition and understanding light.

### Do I need an expensive camera to start?
No. A phone or entry-level camera is enough to learn; skills matter more than gear.

### How long does it take to get good at photography?
Basics can be learned in weeks; developing a strong style and consistency takes months to years of practice.

### What should beginners photograph?
Everyday scenes, people, nature and objects; practise varied light and compositions.

### Can I make money with photography as a beginner?
Yes, through small local shoots, stock photos and helping businesses with product photos once you have a portfolio.
