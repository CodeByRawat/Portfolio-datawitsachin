<p class="lede">To start a marketing agency: build <strong>skills and proof</strong> (results for real clients), choose a <strong>niche and a core service</strong> (e.g., Google Ads for contractors, social media for restaurants, SEO for SaaS), set up the <strong>business and contracts</strong>, choose a <strong>pricing model</strong> (monthly retainers are ideal), assemble <strong>tools</strong>, build a <strong>website with case studies</strong>, win <strong>first clients</strong> through your network and outreach, and create <strong>delivery systems</strong> so you can hire and scale.</p>

## Steps

1. **Skills and proof:** freelance or run campaigns for friends/local businesses; document results
2. **Niche + service:** a clear offer is easier to sell and deliver
3. **Business setup:** registration, bank account, contracts, insurance
4. **Pricing:** retainers, projects, performance bonuses (avoid pure hourly)
5. **Tools:** analytics, ad platforms, SEO tools, scheduling, CRM, project management, reporting dashboards
6. **Website:** niche positioning, services, case studies, pricing guidance, booking (see [How to create a website for your business](/blog/how-to-create-a-website-for-your-business/))
7. **First clients:** network, referrals, partnerships, targeted outreach (see [What is sales prospecting?](/blog/what-is-sales-prospecting/))
8. **Delivery systems:** onboarding checklists, SOPs, reporting templates
9. **Hire:** contractors first, then specialists and account managers
10. **Scale:** productise and build recurring revenue (see [How to build a sellable agency business](/blog/how-to-build-a-sellable-agency-business/))

## Agency types

Full-service marketing, digital marketing (see [How to start a digital marketing agency](/blog/how-to-start-a-digital-marketing-agency/)), social media (see [How to start a social media marketing agency](/blog/how-to-start-a-social-media-marketing-agency/)), SEO, paid ads, content, creative/video, advertising agencies, and AI/automation agencies.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a marketing agency?
Build skills and proof, pick a niche and service, set up the business, price with retainers, assemble tools, build a website with case studies, win first clients and create delivery systems.

### How much does it cost to start a marketing agency?
Often low: a website, software subscriptions, contracts and business registration; costs grow with hiring.

### Do I need experience to start a marketing agency?
Practical results matter more than credentials; get experience through freelancing or small projects first.

### How do new agencies get clients?
Network referrals, partnerships, niche communities, targeted outreach, content and case studies.

### What is the most profitable agency niche?
Niches where clients have high customer value (legal, healthcare, SaaS, home services) often support higher retainers.

### How do I start an advertising agency?
Focus on paid media or creative, build a portfolio of campaigns, and offer management retainers plus creative production.
