<p class="lede">To start a balloon business, <strong>learn balloon décor</strong> (garlands, arches, columns, bouquets), buy <strong>quality balloons and tools</strong> (electric inflator, helium access, frames), <strong>build a portfolio</strong> with practice installs, register and insure the business, set <strong>pricing by size and design</strong>, and market to <strong>parents, event planners, venues and businesses</strong>. Many start from home for <strong>$500 to $3,000</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Choose your services

| Service | Notes |
|---|---|
| Organic garlands and arches | Most popular, great for photos |
| Balloon bouquets | Quick sales, delivery |
| Backdrops and walls | Higher prices |
| Columns and marquee numbers | Parties and events |
| Corporate décor | Store openings, events |
| Balloon "grab and go" garlands | Easy local sales |

## Step 2: Learn the skills

Practise sizing balloons, building clusters, garlands, arches and colour palettes. Online classes and supplier workshops help.

## Step 3: Supplies and tools

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Electric balloon inflator | $30 – $150 |
| Balloon sizer and tools | $20 – $100 |
| Arch frames and stands | $100 – $500 |
| Starter balloon inventory | $200 – $800 |
| Helium (if offering) | Varies |
| Storage bins and transport | $50 – $300 |

## Step 4: Register and insure

Business licence, sales tax permit and general liability insurance (many venues require it).

## Step 5: Pricing

Price by the foot for garlands, per arch size, or as packages. Include materials, labour, design, delivery and setup time, and removal if offered. Take deposits.

## Step 6: Build your portfolio

Do styled shoots and a few discounted installs to get photos. Instagram is a key sales tool.

## Step 7: Get clients

Event planners, venues, party rental companies, photographers, businesses and parents via social media and Google.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a balloon business?
Learn balloon décor, buy supplies, build a portfolio, get insured and market to planners and parents.

### How much does it cost to start a balloon business?
Often $500 to $3,000 from home.

### How do I start a balloon arch business?
Learn garlands and arches, buy frames and an inflator, and post your work on social media.

### Do I need insurance for balloon décor?
Yes. Many venues require liability insurance.

### Can I start a balloon business from home?
Yes. Most do.
