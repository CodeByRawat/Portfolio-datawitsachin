<p class="lede">To market a private practice, make it easy for the right patients to <strong>find you, trust you and book</strong>: define your <strong>niche</strong>, build a <strong>clear website with online booking</strong>, optimise your <strong>Google Business Profile and reviews</strong>, list on <strong>reputable directories</strong> (insurer and specialty directories), publish <strong>helpful content</strong>, build <strong>referral relationships</strong>, and use <strong>targeted ads</strong> for specific services, all within healthcare advertising and privacy rules.</p>

## 1. Define your niche and ideal patients

Conditions you treat best, populations (children, athletes, seniors), languages, insurance or self-pay, telehealth vs in-person.

## 2. Website with online booking

- Clear headline: who you help and where
- Service/condition pages
- Practitioner bios and credentials
- Insurance and fees information
- Online booking, intake forms, maps and hours
(See [How to make a booking website](/blog/how-to-make-a-booking-website/).)

## 3. Google Business Profile and reviews

Complete profile, categories, photos, posts; request reviews ethically and respond without disclosing patient information (see [How to create a Google Business page](/blog/how-to-create-a-google-business-page/)).

## 4. Local SEO

Pages for conditions + city ("sports physiotherapy in Pune"), FAQs, structured data and consistent citations (see [What are local citations?](/blog/what-are-local-citations/)).

## 5. Directories

Insurer directories, Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Psychology Today (therapists), Practo and local equivalents.

## 6. Content and video

Short videos and articles answering common patient questions build trust and search visibility.

## 7. Referral relationships

Physicians, dentists, schools, employers and community groups (see [How to increase referrals in private practice](/blog/how-to-increase-referrals-in-private-practice/)).

## 8. Paid ads

Google Ads for high-intent searches; social ads for awareness of specific services (follow platform healthcare policies).

## 9. Patient experience

Fast responses, easy booking, reminders, short waits and follow-ups turn patients into promoters.

## Attracting new patients to a dental practice

New-patient offers (where permitted), cosmetic and implant landing pages, before/after galleries with consent, family dentistry content and fast online booking (full guide: [How to market a dentist office](/blog/how-to-market-a-dentist-office/)).

## Compliance

Follow privacy laws (e.g., HIPAA), professional advertising rules, consent for testimonials/photos, and platform health ad policies. (Overview: [What is healthcare marketing?](/blog/what-is-healthcare-marketing/))

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I market my private practice?
Define your niche, build a website with online booking, optimise Google Business Profile and reviews, use directories and local SEO, publish helpful content, build referrals and run targeted ads within healthcare rules.

### How do I attract new patients?
Be easy to find on Google, show credentials and reviews, offer convenient online booking and fast responses, and build referral relationships.

### What is the best marketing for a private practice?
Local search visibility (Google Business Profile, reviews, website) combined with referral relationships usually performs best.

### How do I market a dental practice?
Optimise Google, gather reviews, create service pages for cosmetic, implant and family dentistry, run targeted ads and offer online booking.

### Can therapists advertise on social media?
Yes, within professional and confidentiality guidelines; focus on education and avoid sharing client information.

### How much should a private practice spend on marketing?
It varies; start with free and low-cost channels, then budget based on cost per new patient and patient lifetime value.
