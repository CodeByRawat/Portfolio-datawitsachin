<p class="lede">To advertise your business on Facebook: create a <strong>Facebook Page</strong> and a <strong>Meta Business portfolio</strong>, open <strong>Meta Ads Manager</strong>, install the <strong>Meta Pixel and Conversions API</strong> on your website, then create a campaign with the right <strong>objective</strong> (awareness, traffic, engagement, leads, app promotion or sales), choose your <strong>audience, placements and budget</strong>, upload <strong>engaging creatives</strong>, and launch. Ads run on Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and Meta's Audience Network.</p>

## Step 1: Set up the basics

1. **Facebook Page** for your business (name, profile photo, cover, contact info, CTA button)
2. **Meta Business portfolio** (business.facebook.com) to manage pages, ad accounts and people securely
3. **Ad account** with billing and currency
4. **Connect Instagram** to run ads there too
5. **Verify your domain** (for websites receiving traffic)

## Step 2: Install tracking

- **Meta Pixel** on your website
- **Conversions API** (server-side) for more reliable tracking
- Set up **standard events:** Lead, Purchase, Schedule, Contact, AddToCart
- Respect **cookie consent** laws

Without tracking, Meta can't optimise for results.

## Step 3: Choose a campaign objective

| Objective | Use when you want |
|---|---|
| **Awareness** | Reach and recall |
| **Traffic** | Website visits (judge carefully; clicks aren't customers) |
| **Engagement** | Video views, messages, page engagement |
| **Leads** | Instant forms, calls, Messenger/WhatsApp conversations |
| **App promotion** | Installs |
| **Sales** | Purchases and conversions on your site |

For most small businesses: **Leads** (instant forms or WhatsApp/Messenger) or **Sales** with the Pixel.

## Step 4: Define your audience

- **Location** (radius for local businesses)
- **Age and gender** only if truly relevant
- **Advantage+ audience / broad targeting:** often works well with strong creative
- **Custom audiences:** website visitors, customer lists, engagers (see [What is remarketing?](/blog/what-is-remarketing/))
- **Lookalike audiences** based on best customers

Special ad categories (housing, employment, credit, social issues) have targeting restrictions.

## Step 5: Placements and budget

- **Advantage+ placements** let Meta allocate across feeds, Stories, Reels
- Start with a **daily budget** you can sustain for at least 1–2 weeks of learning
- Let the campaign exit the **learning phase** before big changes

## Step 6: Create ads that work

- **Video and motion beat static** for most cold audiences (see [How to make a promotional video](/blog/how-to-make-a-promotional-video/))
- **Hook in the first 1–3 seconds**
- **Vertical 9:16 and 4:5** formats (see [How to create Instagram Stories ads](/blog/how-to-create-instagram-stories-ads/))
- **Captions** for silent viewing
- **Clear offer and CTA**
- **Social proof:** reviews, results, UGC
- **Test 3–5 creatives** with different angles (see [What is creative strategy?](/blog/what-is-creative-strategy/))

## Boost post vs Ads Manager

| | Boost post | Ads Manager |
|---|---|---|
| **Ease** | Very easy | More steps |
| **Objectives** | Limited | Full range |
| **Targeting and testing** | Basic | Advanced |
| **Best for** | Quick engagement on a good post | Leads and sales campaigns |

## Step 7: Measure and optimise

| Metric | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **CPM** | Cost per 1,000 impressions (see [What is CPM?](/blog/what-is-cpm/)) |
| **Hook rate / thumb-stop rate** | % who watch the first 3 seconds |
| **CTR** | Click-through rate |
| **Cost per result** | Cost per lead/purchase |
| **ROAS** | Revenue ÷ ad spend |
| **Frequency** | Average views per person (fatigue) |

Kill weak creatives, scale winners gradually, and refresh creative every few weeks.

## Common mistakes

- No Pixel or conversion tracking
- Optimising for clicks instead of leads/sales
- Too-narrow targeting with small budgets
- One creative only
- Slow follow-up on leads (see [How to schedule a WhatsApp message](/blog/how-to-schedule-a-whatsapp-message/))
- Sending ads to a weak landing page

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I advertise my business on Facebook?
Create a Facebook Page and Meta Business portfolio, install the Pixel, set up a campaign in Ads Manager with the right objective, audience, placements and budget, then upload engaging creatives and launch.

### How much does it cost to advertise on Facebook?
You set the budget; costs depend on audience, competition and creative. Many small businesses start with modest daily budgets and scale what works.

### Is boosting a post the same as a Facebook ad?
Boosting is a simplified ad with limited options. Ads Manager offers full objectives, targeting, testing and tracking.

### What type of Facebook ad works best?
Short, native-looking videos with a strong hook, clear offer and social proof often perform best, but test multiple formats.

### Do Facebook ads also run on Instagram?
Yes. Meta Ads Manager lets you run ads across Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and the Audience Network.

### Why are my Facebook ads not getting results?
Usually due to weak creative, the wrong objective, missing tracking, poor offers or landing pages, or changing campaigns too often.
