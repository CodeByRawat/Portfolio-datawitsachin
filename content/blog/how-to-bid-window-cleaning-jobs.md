<p class="lede">To bid a window cleaning job, <strong>count the panes or windows</strong>, decide <strong>inside, outside or both</strong>, add for <strong>screens, tracks, height, hard-water stains and access</strong>, estimate your <strong>time</strong>, and make sure the price covers your <strong>hourly target and minimum charge</strong>. For commercial work, bid per visit with a recurring schedule.</p>

## Common pricing methods

| Method | Best for |
|---|---|
| Per pane | Residential homes with many small panes |
| Per window | Simple homes with standard windows |
| Per job (flat) | Quick quotes from photos or a walk-around |
| Hourly | Unusual or post-construction work |
| Per visit (recurring) | Storefronts and offices |

## Step-by-step: bidding a residential job

1. **Count windows/panes** (walk around, use photos or satellite view).
2. **Separate ground floor and upper floors**; upper windows take longer.
3. **Choose service**: exterior only, interior + exterior.
4. **Add extras**: screens, tracks, skylights, French panes, hard water.
5. **Estimate time** using your own speed per pane.
6. **Multiply by your target hourly rate** and add travel.
7. **Apply your minimum charge**.
8. **Offer a recurring-plan price** to win repeat work.

## Bidding commercial window cleaning

- Count storefront panes and doors.
- Set a per-visit price for weekly, biweekly or monthly service.
- Price high-rise or rope work only if you are trained and insured for it.
- Include certificates of insurance with the bid.
- Be clear about access times (before opening hours).

## What to add to a quote

- Interior work (moving furniture, blinds)
- Hard-water stain removal
- Post-construction paint and sticker removal
- Ladder or lift work
- Difficult access or no parking

## Tips for winning bids

Respond fast, show reviews and before/after photos, give a clear written quote, offer good/better/best options, and follow up. A website with an online quote form helps you capture leads while you're working. For setup steps, see [How to start a window cleaning business](/blog/how-to-start-a-window-cleaning-business/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I bid on cleaning windows?
Count windows or panes, decide inside/outside, add extras, estimate time and price to your hourly target with a minimum charge.

### How do you charge for window cleaning?
Per pane, per window, per job or hourly; commercial work is usually per visit.

### Should I charge per pane or per window?
Per pane is more accurate for homes with divided panes; per window is simpler for standard windows.

### How do I bid commercial window cleaning?
Count panes, set a recurring per-visit price and include insurance certificates.

### What is a good minimum charge for window cleaning?
One that covers travel, setup and your hourly target even on a small job.

### Can I quote window cleaning from photos?
Yes, for simple homes; confirm on arrival before starting.
