<p class="lede">To start your own plumbing business: get the <strong>plumbing licence your state requires</strong> to run a company (often a master plumber or plumbing contractor licence), <strong>register the business</strong>, get <strong>liability insurance, bonding and workers' comp</strong>, buy <strong>tools and a service van</strong>, choose <strong>service/repair, remodel or new construction</strong>, set <strong>flat-rate pricing</strong>, use <strong>scheduling and invoicing software</strong>, and generate leads with <strong>Google, reviews and a fast website</strong>.</p>

## Steps

1. **Licensing:** journeyman experience, then master or contractor licence as required by your state; local business licence and permits
2. **Business plan:** services (drains, water heaters, leaks, repiping, remodels, commercial), service area, pricing
3. **Register:** LLC or corporation, EIN, business bank account
4. **Insurance and bonding:** general liability, commercial auto, workers' comp, contractor bond
5. **Tools and van:** pipe tools, drain machines, camera, press tools, water heater dolly, stocked shelving
6. **Supplier accounts** for parts and fixtures
7. **Pricing:** flat-rate price book, trip/diagnostic fees, tiered options for larger jobs
8. **Software:** booking, dispatch, invoicing, payments, reminders
9. **Marketing:** Google Business Profile, Local Services Ads, reviews, SEO pages per service (see [How to get plumbing leads](/blog/how-to-get-plumbing-leads/))
10. **Hire:** add plumbers and an office coordinator as call volume grows

## Startup costs

Main costs: tools, van, insurance, licensing, initial inventory, software and marketing. Service and repair plumbing needs less capital than new construction.

## How much plumbing business owners make

It depends on the number of trucks, pricing, average ticket and overhead; owner-operators earn their labour plus profit, while multi-truck owners earn from the team (see [How much do plumbing business owners make?](/blog/how-much-do-plumbing-business-owners-make/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start my own plumbing business?
Get the required licence, register and insure the business, buy tools and a van, set prices, use software and market locally.

### Do I need a master plumber licence to own a plumbing company?
Many states require a master plumber or contractor licence holder for the company; check your state's rules.

### How much does it cost to start a plumbing business?
Mostly tools, a van, insurance, licensing and marketing; costs vary by scope.

### Is owning a plumbing business profitable?
It can be very profitable with good pricing, steady leads and efficient operations.

### How do plumbing companies get customers?
Google Business Profile, Local Services Ads, reviews, SEO, referrals and builder relationships.

### What plumbing services are most profitable?
Water heater replacements, repiping, sewer line work and emergency service are often high-ticket.
