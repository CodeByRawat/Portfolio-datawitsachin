<p class="lede">To start a mobile coffee shop, choose your <strong>setup</strong> (cart, van, trailer or truck), write a simple <strong>business plan</strong>, buy <strong>espresso, water and power equipment</strong>, get <strong>permits</strong>, build a <strong>brand and short menu</strong>, and earn from <strong>daily locations, events and private catering</strong>. Costs range from about <strong>$10,000 for a cart to $150,000+ for a truck</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Choose your setup

| Setup | Startup cost | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Coffee cart | $10,000 – $40,000 | Events, indoor venues |
| Coffee van | $30,000 – $80,000 | Street vending, compact |
| Coffee trailer | $20,000 – $70,000 | Events, festivals |
| Coffee truck | $60,000 – $150,000+ | High volume |

Guides: [How to start a coffee cart business](/blog/how-to-start-a-coffee-cart-business/) and [How to start a coffee truck](/blog/how-to-start-a-coffee-truck/).

## Step 2: Business plan

Define your customers, locations, menu, pricing, startup costs, and how many drinks you need to sell daily to break even.

## Step 3: Revenue streams

| Stream | Notes |
|---|---|
| Daily locations | Offices, commuter spots, markets |
| Events and festivals | High volume, vendor fees |
| Private events | Weddings, corporate (most reliable) |
| Coffee catering | Office meetings, conferences |
| Retail beans and merch | Extra margin |

## Step 4: Permits

Mobile vendor permit, health permit, commissary agreement, business licence and event permits.

## Step 5: Branding and menu

A memorable name, attractive vehicle wrap or cart design, social media presence, and a short menu that's quick to serve.

## Step 6: Marketing

Post your schedule on social media, list in local event directories, partner with event planners and venues, and offer loyalty cards.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a mobile coffee shop?
Choose a cart, van, trailer or truck, get equipment and permits, and sell at locations and events.

### How much does it cost to start a mobile coffee business?
From about $10,000 for a cart to $150,000+ for a truck.

### Is a mobile coffee business profitable?
Yes, with good locations and private event bookings.

### What permits do mobile coffee businesses need?
Health, mobile vendor and business licences, and often a commissary.

### Where do mobile coffee businesses make the most money?
Private events, busy offices and festivals.
