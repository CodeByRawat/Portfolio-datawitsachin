<p class="lede">Accountants use a stack of tools: <strong>cloud accounting software</strong> (QuickBooks Online, Xero, Sage, Zoho Books, Tally in India), <strong>tax preparation software</strong>, <strong>practice management</strong> for workflows and deadlines, <strong>client portals and e-signatures</strong>, <strong>receipt/bill capture</strong>, <strong>payroll</strong>, <strong>Excel/Google Sheets and BI</strong> for analysis, and increasingly <strong>AI and automation</strong> for data entry, reconciliation, document extraction and client communication.</p>

## The accounting technology stack

| Category | Examples | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Cloud accounting / ledger** | QuickBooks Online, Xero, Sage, Zoho Books, NetSuite (larger), Tally (India) | Bookkeeping, reporting |
| **Tax software** | Country-specific professional tax suites | Tax returns and planning |
| **Practice management** | Karbon, Canopy, TaxDome, Financial Cents, Jetpack Workflow | Tasks, deadlines, client communication |
| **Client portal & e-signature** | TaxDome, SmartVault, ShareFile, DocuSign | Secure document exchange |
| **Receipt/bill capture** | Dext, Hubdoc, AutoEntry, built-in AI capture | Extract data from documents |
| **Payroll** | Gusto, ADP, Paychex and local providers | Payroll and filings |
| **Payments & AP/AR** | Bill.com, Stripe, payment links | Collections and payables (see [Accounts payable automation](/blog/accounts-payable-automation/)) |
| **Spreadsheets & BI** | Excel, Google Sheets, Power BI | Analysis, dashboards (see [What is business intelligence?](/blog/what-is-business-intelligence/)) |
| **Communication** | Email, secure messaging, video calls | Client service |
| **Automation & AI** | Zapier, Make, n8n, Power Automate, AI assistants | Workflows, extraction, drafting |

Availability varies by country; choose tools that support your local tax rules.

## What is accounting technology?

Accounting technology is the software and systems that capture, process, analyse and report financial data, increasingly cloud-based, connected through APIs and enhanced with AI. It shifts accountants' time from data entry to analysis and advice.

## How AI and automation help accountants

- **Document extraction:** invoices, receipts, bank statements (see [What are automated financial systems?](/blog/what-are-automated-financial-systems/))
- **Bank reconciliation suggestions**
- **Month-end close checklists and automation** (see [What is record to report?](/blog/what-is-record-to-report/))
- **Client reminders** for missing documents
- **Drafting emails and summaries** of financial results
- **Anomaly detection** in transactions

Keep professional judgement and review in the loop, and protect client data.

## How to choose an accounting tech stack

1. Start with your **core ledger** and tax software for your jurisdiction
2. Add **practice management** to run workflows and deadlines
3. Ensure **integrations** between tools (bank feeds, apps, portals)
4. Prioritise **security** (MFA, encryption, access controls)
5. **Automate** repetitive steps before hiring more staff
6. Train the team and document processes

## Frequently asked questions

### What tools do accountants use?
Cloud accounting software, tax software, practice management, client portals and e-signatures, receipt capture, payroll, spreadsheets and BI, plus automation and AI tools.

### What software do most accountants use?
QuickBooks and Xero are widely used for small businesses, alongside Excel and country-specific tax software.

### What is accounting technology?
Software and systems that capture, process, analyse and report financial data, increasingly cloud-based and AI-assisted.

### Will AI replace accountants?
AI automates data entry and routine tasks, but accountants remain essential for judgement, compliance, advice and client relationships.

### What is practice management software for accountants?
Software that manages client workflows, deadlines, tasks, documents and communication across the firm.

### How can accounting firms automate work?
By using bank feeds, document extraction, workflow templates, client reminders and integrations between ledger, portal and practice management tools.
