<p class="lede">To start a mobile welding business, get <strong>solid welding experience and certifications</strong> (AWS or similar), build a <strong>welding rig</strong> (truck with welder-generator, torches, tools), get <strong>liability insurance</strong>, set an <strong>hourly rate plus call-out fee</strong>, and find customers among <strong>farms, ranches, contractors, trucking companies and homeowners</strong>. Startup costs are often <strong>$15,000 to $60,000</strong> plus the truck.</p>

## Step 1: Experience and certifications

Mobile welders work alone on varied jobs, so you need experience with several processes (stick, MIG, flux-core, TIG) and positions. AWS certifications help win contractor and industrial work.

## Step 2: Choose your customers

| Market | Typical work |
|---|---|
| Farms and ranches | Equipment repair, gates, pens |
| Construction | Structural steel, rebar, stairs, railings |
| Trucking and heavy equipment | Trailer and bucket repairs |
| Oil, gas and industrial | Pipe and maintenance (higher certs) |
| Homeowners | Gates, railings, repairs |
| Fabrication | Custom projects |

## Step 3: Build your rig

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Welder-generator (engine drive) | $4,000 – $15,000 |
| Oxy-fuel torch set | $500 – $1,500 |
| Plasma cutter | $1,000 – $4,000 |
| Grinders, clamps, hand tools | $1,000 – $3,000 |
| Welding bed or truck setup | $5,000 – $25,000 |
| Safety gear | $500 – $1,500 |
| Truck | Varies |

## Step 4: Register and insure

LLC, EIN, business license, general liability, commercial auto, and tools and equipment coverage. Some industrial clients require extra coverage and safety training.

## Step 5: Pricing

Mobile welders often charge **an hourly rate plus a trip or call-out fee**, plus materials and consumables. Charge more for emergencies, after hours and certified structural work.

## Step 6: Find customers

1. Google Business Profile and a simple website
2. Farm supply stores and equipment dealers
3. General contractors and fabrication shops
4. Trucking companies and equipment rental yards
5. Facebook groups and local word of mouth

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a mobile welding business?
Get experienced and certified, build a welding rig, insure the business, set rates and market to farms and contractors.

### How much does it cost to start a welding business?
Often $15,000 to $60,000 plus a truck.

### How much do mobile welders charge?
Usually an hourly rate plus a call-out fee and materials.

### How do I find welding customers?
Farms, contractors, trucking companies, Google and word of mouth.

### Do I need certification to start a welding business?
Not always, but certifications are needed for structural and many commercial jobs.
