<p class="lede">The fastest ways to get lawn care customers are <strong>neighbourhood marketing</strong> (door hangers, yard signs, neighbour discounts), <strong>Google Business Profile with lots of reviews</strong>, <strong>Nextdoor and Facebook groups</strong>, and a <strong>website with an instant quote form</strong>. Long term, <strong>referrals and route density</strong> make lawn care marketing much cheaper.</p>

## 14 ways to get lawn care customers

1. **Google Business Profile:** service area, photos, services, and steady reviews
2. **Website with quote form:** services, service areas, pricing guidance, online booking (see [How to create a website for your business](/blog/how-to-create-a-website-for-your-business/))
3. **Door hangers** around current customers' homes
4. **Yard signs** after each job (with permission)
5. **Neighbour discounts** to build route density
6. **Nextdoor** recommendations and posts
7. **Facebook community groups**
8. **Referral programme:** a free cut for each referral
9. **Local SEO:** pages for each town or neighbourhood you serve
10. **Google Local Services Ads** and search ads in peak season
11. **Truck and trailer signage**
12. **HOAs and property managers** for multiple properties
13. **Commercial accounts:** offices, churches, retail
14. **Seasonal offers:** spring cleanups, aeration and overseeding

## Keep the customers you win

Show up on schedule, communicate weather delays, send automatic invoices and reminders, and offer annual plans. Retained customers make every new customer more profitable.

## Track what works

Ask every new customer how they found you and track cost per customer by channel.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I get lawn care customers?
Use Google Business Profile, door hangers, yard signs, Nextdoor, Facebook groups, referrals and a website with a quote form.

### How do I market my lawn care business for free?
Google Business Profile, Nextdoor, Facebook groups, yard signs and referrals cost little or nothing.

### What is the best advertising for lawn care?
Local channels: Google profile and reviews, Local Services Ads, door hangers and referrals.

### How do I get more lawn care clients in my area?
Target neighbourhoods near existing customers with door hangers and neighbour discounts.

### Do lawn care businesses need a website?
Yes; it builds trust and captures quote requests around the clock.

### How do I get commercial lawn care accounts?
Approach property managers, HOAs and local businesses with professional proposals and insurance proof.
