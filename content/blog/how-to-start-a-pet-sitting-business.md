<p class="lede">To start a pet sitting business, <strong>choose your services</strong> (drop-in visits, overnights, dog walking, cat sitting), register the business, get <strong>pet sitting insurance and bonding</strong>, set clear prices and a contract, then find clients through <strong>Google, neighbourhood groups, vets and groomers, and pet sitting apps</strong>. Startup costs are low, often under <strong>$1,000</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Choose your services

| Service | What it is |
|---|---|
| Drop-in visits | 20–60 minutes: feeding, potty, play, litter |
| Overnight sitting | Staying at the client's home |
| Dog walking | Daily walks while owners work |
| Cat sitting | Visits for cats, often longer trips |
| Boarding in your home | Check local zoning and rules first |
| Extras | Medication, plant watering, mail, pet taxi |

## Step 2: Register the business

LLC or sole proprietorship, EIN, business bank account and a local business license if required.

## Step 3: Get insured and bonded

Pet sitting insurance covers injury to pets and damage to client homes. Bonding protects clients against theft. Both build trust.

## Step 4: Get some training

Pet first aid and CPR certification, plus experience with different breeds, cats and medications. Professional pet sitter associations offer courses.

## Step 5: Set your prices

| Service | Common pricing |
|---|---|
| 30-minute drop-in | Per visit |
| Overnight | Per night |
| Extra pets | Small add-on per pet |
| Holidays | Surcharge |
| Last-minute bookings | Surcharge |

Check local competitors and app prices.

## Step 6: Contracts and meet-and-greets

Always meet the pet and owner first. Use a service agreement, a pet info form (vet, food, meds, emergency contact) and a key handling policy.

## Step 7: Find clients

1. Google Business Profile and reviews
2. A website with online booking
3. Neighbourhood apps and local Facebook groups
4. Vets, groomers, pet stores and dog trainers
5. Pet sitting apps to start, then move clients to direct bookings
6. Referrals from happy clients

## Step 8: Use booking software

Pet sitting software handles schedules, visit reports with photos, invoices and payments.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a pet sitting business?
Choose services, register, get insured and bonded, set prices, and market to local pet owners.

### Do I need a license to pet sit?
Usually just a general business license; home boarding may need extra permits.

### How much does it cost to start a pet sitting business?
Often under $1,000 for registration, insurance and a simple website.

### Should I use pet sitting apps?
They're a good way to get first clients, but they take a cut, so build direct clients too.

### How do I start a dog sitting business?
Same steps, focused on dogs: walks, drop-ins and overnights.
