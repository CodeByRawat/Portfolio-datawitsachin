<p class="lede">To start a septic tank business, choose between <strong>septic pumping</strong> (lower startup cost, recurring work) and <strong>septic installation and repair</strong> (bigger jobs, more equipment), get your <strong>state septic pumper or installer license</strong> and local permits, buy a <strong>vacuum pump truck</strong>, line up a <strong>legal disposal site</strong>, insure the business and market to <strong>rural homeowners, realtors and builders</strong>. Startup costs often run <strong>$75,000 to $250,000+</strong>, mostly for the truck.</p>

## Step 1: Choose your services

| Service | Notes |
|---|---|
| Septic pumping | Every 3–5 years per home, steady demand |
| Inspections | Required for many home sales |
| Repairs | Baffles, pumps, risers, lids |
| Installation | New systems for builders, big jobs |
| Drain field repair and replacement | High-value work |
| Grease trap and portable toilet pumping | Commercial add-ons |

Many start with pumping and inspections, then add repairs and installs.

## Step 2: Licenses and permits

Most states and counties require licenses for septic pumping (waste haulers) and separate licenses for installers, plus inspector certification for real estate inspections. You'll also need permits for hauling and approval to dispose of septage at a treatment plant or permitted site.

## Step 3: Equipment and startup costs

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Used vacuum pump truck | $60,000 – $150,000 |
| New pump truck | $150,000 – $250,000+ |
| Hoses, probes, locators, camera | $3,000 – $15,000 |
| Mini excavator (for installs) | $30,000 – $80,000 |
| Licenses, permits, training | $1,000 – $5,000 |
| Insurance (yearly) | $5,000 – $15,000 |

## Step 4: Disposal

Arrange disposal at a wastewater treatment plant or a permitted land application site. Disposal fees are a key cost. More: [What do septic companies do with the waste?](/blog/what-do-septic-companies-do-with-the-waste/).

## Step 5: Pricing

Septic pumping is often priced per tank based on size, plus extra for digging to find lids, long hose runs or heavy sludge. Inspections and repairs are priced separately.

## Step 6: Get customers

1. Google Business Profile and reviews
2. A website with service area and online booking
3. Realtors and home inspectors (inspections before sale)
4. Builders and excavators (new installs)
5. Reminder system for every customer's next pump date

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a septic tank business?
Choose pumping or installation, get licensed, buy a pump truck, arrange disposal and market to rural homeowners.

### How much does it cost to start a septic pumping business?
Often $75,000 to $250,000+, mostly for the truck.

### Do you need a license to pump septic tanks?
Usually yes. Most states and counties license septic pumpers and installers.

### Is a septic business recession-proof?
Largely. Septic tanks need pumping regardless of the economy.

### Can I start with a used pump truck?
Yes. Many owners start with a used truck to reduce costs.
