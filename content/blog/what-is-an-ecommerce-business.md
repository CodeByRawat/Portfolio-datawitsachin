<p class="lede">An <strong>e-commerce business</strong> sells products or services <strong>online</strong>, taking orders and payments over the internet through a website, app, marketplace or social platform. An <strong>e-commerce website</strong> is the online store itself: product pages, a shopping cart, checkout and payment processing, connected to inventory, shipping and customer accounts.</p>

## How an e-commerce website works

1. Customer browses **products and categories**
2. Adds items to a **cart**
3. Completes **checkout** (address, shipping, payment)
4. **Payment gateway** processes the transaction
5. Order goes to **fulfilment** (warehouse, 3PL, supplier or digital delivery)
6. Customer receives **confirmation and tracking**
7. **Post-purchase:** support, returns, reviews, marketing

## E-commerce business models

| Model | Example |
|---|---|
| **B2C** | Brand sells to consumers |
| **B2B** | Wholesale or business supplies online |
| **D2C** | Manufacturer sells directly, bypassing retailers |
| **C2C** | Consumers sell to each other (marketplaces) |
| **Marketplace** | Platform hosting many sellers |
| **Dropshipping** | Supplier ships directly to customers |
| **Subscription** | Recurring boxes or replenishment |
| **Digital products** | Courses, templates, software |
| **Services** | Bookable or purchasable services online |

## E-commerce platforms

Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Wix, Squarespace, Magento/Adobe Commerce and custom builds (see [What is the best website builder?](/blog/what-is-the-best-website-builder/)).

## Pros and cons

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Sell 24/7 to a wide market | High competition and ad costs |
| Lower overhead than physical stores | Shipping and returns complexity |
| Data-driven marketing | Customer trust must be earned |
| Scalable | Platform/payment fees |

## Will e-commerce die?

No. Online shopping continues to grow as a share of retail, though growth rates fluctuate and channels shift (marketplaces, social commerce, quick commerce). Physical retail and e-commerce increasingly blend (omnichannel).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is an e-commerce business?
A business that sells goods or services online through a website, app, marketplace or social platform.

### What is an e-commerce website?
An online store with product pages, cart, checkout and payment processing, connected to inventory and fulfilment.

### What are the main types of e-commerce?
B2C, B2B, D2C, C2C, marketplaces, dropshipping, subscriptions, digital products and services.

### Is e-commerce a good business to start?
It can be, with a validated product, good margins and a plan for traffic and customer experience.

### Will e-commerce die?
No; online shopping keeps growing, though channels and consumer habits continue to evolve.

### How much does it cost to start an e-commerce business?
From a few hundred dollars for a simple store and small inventory to much more for custom sites, stock and marketing.
