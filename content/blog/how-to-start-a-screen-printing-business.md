<p class="lede">To start a screen printing business, learn the process, buy a <strong>press, screens, an exposure unit, a flash or conveyor dryer and inks</strong>, set up a <strong>ventilated workspace</strong>, register the business, set <strong>pricing by quantity and colours</strong>, and sell to <strong>local businesses, schools, teams, bands and brands</strong>. A small manual setup costs about <strong>$3,000 to $15,000</strong>; a full shop is <strong>$25,000 to $100,000+</strong>.</p>

## What you need

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Manual press (4–6 colour) | $500 – $5,000 |
| Screens and frames | $200 – $1,000 |
| Exposure unit | $300 – $3,000 |
| Flash dryer | $300 – $1,500 |
| Conveyor dryer | $2,000 – $10,000 |
| Washout booth and pressure washer | $500 – $2,000 |
| Inks (plastisol, water-based) | $300 – $1,000 |
| Emulsion, squeegees, tape, chemicals | $200 – $500 |
| Film printer or film | $200 – $1,500 |
| Automatic press (later) | $15,000 – $80,000+ |

## Step 1: Learn the process

1. Prepare artwork and separate colours
2. Print films
3. Coat screens with emulsion and expose them
4. Register screens on the press
5. Print, flash and cure
6. Reclaim screens

Practise on cheap shirts before taking orders.

## Step 2: Workspace

A garage or small unit with water, drainage for washout, ventilation and power for dryers. Check zoning if working from home.

## Step 3: Register and insure

Business licence, sales tax permit and liability insurance.

## Step 4: Pricing

Price per piece based on **quantity**, **number of colours** and **print locations**, plus screen set-up fees for small orders. See [How much does screen printing cost?](/blog/how-much-does-screen-printing-cost/).

## Step 5: Start lean

You can start as a broker, outsourcing printing to a contract printer while you build customers, then bring printing in-house.

## Step 6: Get customers

Local businesses, schools, sports teams, churches, events, bands and clothing brands. Marketing tips: [How to market a screen printing business](/blog/how-to-market-a-screen-printing-business/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a screen printing business?
Learn the process, buy equipment, set up a ventilated workspace, register and sell to local groups and businesses.

### What do I need to start a screen printing business?
A press, screens, exposure unit, dryer, inks, washout area and supplies.

### How much does it cost to start screen printing?
About $3,000 to $15,000 for a small manual setup.

### Can I screen print from home?
Yes, with ventilation, water access and allowed zoning.

### Is screen printing profitable?
Yes, especially on bulk orders.
