<p class="lede">Siding company owners often earn <strong>$60,000 to $150,000+</strong> a year, and small siding companies commonly bring in <strong>$300,000 to $1.5 million</strong> in revenue with <strong>10% to 20% net profit</strong>. Siding installers who work for companies often earn around <strong>$18 to $35 per hour</strong>, or are paid per square of siding installed.</p>

These are rough ranges; location, materials and sales skill make a big difference.

## Revenue by company size

| Company | Rough yearly revenue |
|---|---|
| Owner-installer | $150,000 – $400,000 |
| Small company, 1–3 crews | $400,000 – $1.5 million |
| Larger exterior remodeler | $2 million+ |

## Margins by material

| Siding | Notes |
|---|---|
| Vinyl | Most common, competitive pricing |
| Fibre cement | Higher price, more skill |
| Wood and cedar | Premium, specialised |
| Metal and engineered wood | Growing demand |

## Installer pay

Installers are often paid per square (100 square feet) or hourly. Experienced crew leaders earn more.

## Biggest costs

Materials, labour, sales commissions, marketing, insurance, trucks and equipment, dump fees for old siding.

## How siding companies increase profit

- Sell premium materials and trims
- Bundle siding with windows, gutters and roofing
- Offer financing for bigger jobs
- Get direct homeowner leads (Google, reviews, website)
- Track profit per job

## Frequently asked questions

### How much do siding contractors make?
Owners often earn $60,000 to $150,000+.

### How much do siding installers make?
Often $18 to $35 per hour or per square installed.

### Is siding a profitable business?
Yes, especially with premium materials and bundled exterior work.

### What is the profit margin on siding?
Often 10% to 20% net.

### How do siding companies get customers?
Google, reviews, websites, referrals and storm damage work.
