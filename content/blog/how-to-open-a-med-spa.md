<p class="lede">To open a med spa, first confirm <strong>who can legally own it in your state</strong> and secure a <strong>medical director</strong>. Then form the business, get the <strong>required licences</strong>, choose a <strong>service menu</strong> (injectables, lasers, facials, body treatments), find and build out a <strong>location</strong>, buy <strong>equipment</strong>, hire <strong>licensed staff</strong>, get <strong>malpractice and liability insurance</strong>, and market with <strong>online booking, memberships and before/after results</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Understand the legal structure

Med spas offer **medical procedures**, so many states apply **corporate practice of medicine** rules: only physicians (or certain licensed providers) may own the medical part of the practice. Common structures use a **management services organization (MSO)** for non-medical owners. See [Who can open a medical spa?](/blog/who-can-open-a-medical-spa/). Talk to a healthcare attorney.

## Step 2: Medical director and licences

You'll need a **medical director**, proper **supervision and delegation protocols**, and licences for the business, facility and staff. Details: [What license do you need to open a medical spa?](/blog/what-license-do-you-need-to-open-a-medical-spa/).

## Step 3: Choose your services

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Injectables | Neurotoxins, dermal fillers |
| Laser and energy | Hair removal, skin resurfacing, IPL |
| Skin treatments | Chemical peels, microneedling, facials |
| Body | Body contouring, IV therapy (where allowed) |
| Wellness | Weight management programs (with medical oversight) |

## Step 4: Location and build-out

Pick a visible, upscale area with parking near your target clients. Build treatment rooms with proper plumbing, electrical for lasers, sterilisation areas and a welcoming reception.

## Step 5: Equipment and startup costs

Lasers and energy devices are often the largest cost (many owners lease them). Add treatment beds, skincare inventory, injectable stock, software, furnishings, insurance, legal fees and working capital.

## Step 6: Staff

Nurse injectors, physician assistants or nurse practitioners, estheticians, laser technicians (per state rules), front desk and a manager.

## Step 7: Systems

Practice management and EHR software, online booking, consent forms, photo documentation, memberships and payment plans.

## Step 8: Marketing

Professional website with booking, Google Business Profile, Instagram before/after content (with consent), reviews, referral programs and memberships. More in [How to market a medical spa](/blog/how-to-market-a-medical-spa/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I open a med spa?
Confirm ownership rules, hire a medical director, form the business, get licences, choose services, build out a location, hire staff and market it.

### Can anyone open a med spa?
Not in every state; ownership rules vary, and non-physicians often use an MSO structure.

### How do I start a medical spa with no medical background?
Partner with a physician or use an MSO model, depending on state law.

### Do I need a medical director?
Yes, med spas offering medical treatments need physician oversight.

### What is the most expensive part of opening a med spa?
Lasers and energy-based devices, plus build-out.

### How do med spas get clients?
Online booking, social media results, reviews, referrals and memberships.
