<p class="lede">To become a candle vendor, build <strong>tested, labelled inventory</strong>, get a <strong>business licence and sales tax permit</strong>, apply to <strong>craft fairs, farmers' markets and pop-ups</strong>, set up an <strong>attractive booth</strong> with scent testers, and expand to <strong>boutiques (wholesale)</strong> and <strong>online stores</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Prepare inventory

Have a clear product line (for example 6–10 scents in two sizes), safety labels and enough stock for each event. Bring best sellers in larger quantities.

## Step 2: Paperwork

- Business licence
- Sales tax permit (and temporary event permits where required)
- Product liability insurance (often required by markets)

## Step 3: Apply to events

| Event type | Notes |
|---|---|
| Craft and holiday fairs | Strongest candle sales |
| Farmers' markets | Weekly repeat customers |
| Pop-up markets | Trendy audiences |
| Festivals | High traffic, higher fees |

Apply early; good markets fill up months ahead. Send photos of your products and booth.

## Step 4: Booth setup

- Table, cover and branded banner
- Tiered displays at eye level
- Scent testers (unlidded jars)
- Clear price signs
- Card payments
- Bags and gift wrap

## Step 5: Sell wholesale

Approach boutiques, gift shops, salons and cafés with a wholesale price sheet, samples and minimum order details.

## Step 6: Sell online

Etsy, your own website and social media, with market customers directed to your online store.

## Tips to sell more

Offer bundles, gift sets, seasonal scents, and an email sign-up with a discount for the next order.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I become a candle vendor?
Prepare labelled inventory, get permits and insurance, apply to markets and sell online and wholesale.

### Do I need a permit to sell candles at markets?
Usually a business licence and sales tax permit; some events need temporary permits.

### Do markets require insurance?
Many require product liability insurance.

### How many candles should I bring to a market?
Enough of your best sellers to avoid selling out; many bring 50–150.

### How do I sell candles to stores?
Offer wholesale pricing, samples and a clear minimum order.
