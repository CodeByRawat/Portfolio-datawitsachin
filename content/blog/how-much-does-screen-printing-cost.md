<p class="lede">Screen printing often costs around <strong>$5 to $15 per shirt</strong> for bulk orders, including the shirt, with prices driven by <strong>quantity, number of ink colours, print locations and garment type</strong>. Small orders cost more per shirt because of <strong>screen set-up fees</strong>, and many printers have <strong>minimums of 12 to 24 pieces</strong>.</p>

These are rough ranges; prices vary by shop and region.

## What affects the price

| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| Quantity | More shirts = lower price per shirt |
| Number of colours | Each colour needs a screen |
| Print locations | Front, back, sleeves each add cost |
| Garment | Basic tees vs premium brands, hoodies |
| Ink type | Specialty inks cost more |
| Set-up / screen fees | Per colour, per location |
| Rush orders | Extra fees |

## Screen set-up fees

Many shops charge per screen (per colour), often waived on larger orders. Reorders may save on screen fees if screens are kept.

## Screen printing vs other methods

| Method | Best for |
|---|---|
| Screen printing | Bulk orders, few colours |
| DTG (direct to garment) | Small runs, full-colour designs |
| DTF / heat transfers | Small runs, mixed fabrics |
| Embroidery | Logos on polos and caps |

## For shop owners

Build a price matrix by quantity and colours, add garment cost with markup, set minimums and add charges for extra locations and specialty inks. More: [How to start a screen printing business](/blog/how-to-start-a-screen-printing-business/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does screen printing cost per shirt?
Often $5 to $15 for bulk orders including the shirt.

### Why do small orders cost more?
Screen set-up costs are spread over fewer shirts.

### Do screen printers have minimums?
Many require 12 to 24 pieces.

### Does each colour cost extra?
Yes. Each colour needs its own screen and set-up.

### Is screen printing cheaper than DTG?
For larger orders, usually yes.
