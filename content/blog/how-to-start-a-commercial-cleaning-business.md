<p class="lede">To start a commercial (contract) cleaning business: pick a <strong>niche</strong> such as offices, medical, retail or schools, <strong>register the business</strong>, get <strong>general liability, workers' comp and a janitorial bond</strong>, buy <strong>commercial equipment</strong>, learn to <strong>bid and price contracts</strong> by square footage and frequency, hire <strong>reliable after-hours staff</strong>, follow <strong>safety rules</strong>, and win contracts through <strong>direct outreach, property managers and bid platforms</strong>.</p>

## Steps

1. **Choose a niche:** offices, medical clinics, retail, gyms, schools, warehouses, post-construction
2. **Register:** business entity, tax ID, local licences (see [What licenses are needed to start a cleaning business?](/blog/what-licenses-are-needed-to-start-a-cleaning-business/))
3. **Insurance and bonding:** general liability, workers' compensation, janitorial bond, commercial auto
4. **Equipment:** commercial vacuums, floor machines, carts, microfibre systems, chemicals with safety data sheets
5. **Pricing and bids:** walk-through, square footage, frequency, labour hours, supplies, overhead and margin
6. **Staffing:** reliable evening crews, background checks, training, supervision
7. **Safety and compliance:** chemical training, OSHA-style safety practices, infection control for medical sites
8. **Win contracts:** cold outreach, property managers, local networking, bid platforms (see [How to find commercial cleaning contracts](/blog/how-to-find-commercial-cleaning-contracts/))
9. **Quality systems:** inspections, client check-ins, issue tracking
10. **Grow:** referrals, larger contracts, subcontracting and multiple crews

## Commercial vs residential cleaning

| | Commercial | Residential |
|---|---|---|
| Hours | Mostly evenings/nights | Daytime |
| Contracts | Longer, recurring | Weekly/biweekly |
| Pricing | Per sq ft/monthly | Flat per home or hourly |
| Sales | Bids and proposals | Online booking and referrals |

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a commercial cleaning business?
Choose a niche, register, get insured and bonded, buy commercial equipment, learn bidding, hire crews and pursue contracts.

### What is a contract cleaning business?
A business that cleans commercial facilities under recurring service agreements.

### How do commercial cleaners price jobs?
By square footage, frequency, labour hours, supplies and overhead, plus profit margin.

### Is commercial cleaning more profitable than residential?
It can be, thanks to larger recurring contracts, though bidding is more competitive.

### How do I get my first commercial cleaning contract?
Start with small offices and local businesses through direct outreach and networking.

### What insurance do commercial cleaners need?
General liability, workers' compensation, a janitorial bond and commercial auto.
