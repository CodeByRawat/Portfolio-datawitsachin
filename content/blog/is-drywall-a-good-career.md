<p class="lede">Yes, drywall is a good trade for people who like physical, hands-on work. It has <strong>low barriers to entry</strong>, <strong>steady demand</strong> from construction and remodels, and skilled <strong>finishers earn well</strong>. Many drywallers earn around <strong>$40,000 to $70,000</strong>, and contractors can earn more. The downsides are <strong>physical strain, dust and work that rises and falls with construction</strong>.</p>

Pay varies a lot by region, union status and skill.

## Pros

- No degree needed; learn on the job
- Apprenticeships pay while you learn
- Skilled finishers are in demand
- Clear path to running crews or your own business
- Work in new construction, remodels and repairs

## Cons

| Downside | Notes |
|---|---|
| Physically demanding | Lifting, overhead work, ladders and stilts |
| Dust | Use masks and dust control |
| Construction cycles | Work slows when building slows |
| Piece-rate pay | Income depends on speed |

## Hanging vs finishing

| | Hanging | Finishing |
|---|---|---|
| Work | Cutting and installing boards | Taping, mudding, sanding |
| Skill | Speed and accuracy | Precision and patience |
| Pay | Often piece rate | Skilled finishers often earn more |

## Career path

Helper → hanger or finisher → foreman → contractor or business owner. See [How to become a drywall contractor](/blog/how-to-become-a-drywall-contractor/) and [How to start a drywall business](/blog/how-to-start-a-drywall-business/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is drywall a good career?
Yes. It's in steady demand, has low barriers to entry and can lead to owning a business.

### Is drywall a good trade?
Yes, especially for skilled finishers.

### How much do drywallers make?
Often $40,000 to $70,000, more for skilled finishers and contractors.

### Is drywall hard on your body?
It can be; proper lifting, tools and stilts help.

### Is the drywall business profitable?
Yes, especially remodels, repairs and finishing.
