<p class="lede">A fencing business can be quite profitable. Fence companies often earn <strong>30% to 50% gross margin</strong> and <strong>10% to 20% net profit</strong>. A small company with one or two crews commonly brings in <strong>$300,000 to $1 million</strong> a year, and owners often earn <strong>$70,000 to $150,000+</strong>.</p>

These are rough ranges; your market and pricing make the biggest difference.

## Where the profit comes from

| Source | Why it pays |
|---|---|
| Residential wood and vinyl | Steady volume |
| Ornamental aluminium | Higher price per foot |
| Gates and automatic openers | High value per hour |
| Commercial and security | Large contracts |
| Repairs | Small jobs, quick cash |
| Removal and haul-away | Easy add-on |

## Biggest costs

1. Materials (often 40%–50% of the price)
2. Labour
3. Trucks, trailers and fuel
4. Equipment and rentals
5. Insurance
6. Marketing

## How much do fencing companies make?

| Size | Rough yearly revenue |
|---|---|
| Owner-operator, one crew | $200,000 – $500,000 |
| Two to three crews | $500,000 – $1.5 million |
| Larger regional companies | Several million |

## How to increase profit

- Price per linear foot from real costs, not just competitors
- Take deposits to buy materials
- Sell upgrades: better posts, caps, gates, staining
- Reduce return trips with good planning
- Build direct homeowner leads through Google reviews and a website
- Track profit per job
