<p class="lede">Running a daycare successfully means staying <strong>compliant and safe</strong> every day, keeping <strong>qualified staff</strong> (and keeping them happy), delivering <strong>consistent routines and learning</strong>, maintaining <strong>full enrolment</strong> with a waitlist, <strong>billing and collecting tuition</strong> reliably, communicating well with <strong>parents</strong>, and tracking your <strong>finances</strong> closely, supported by good software and automation.</p>

## Daily operations

- Check-in/out and attendance tracking
- Ratio management throughout the day
- Routines: arrival, activities, meals, naps, outdoor time, departure
- Cleaning and sanitising schedules
- Incident and health logs

## Compliance and safety

Licensing standards, staff training and certifications, emergency drills, safe sleep, allergy management, records for inspections (see [How to get a daycare license](/blog/how-to-get-a-daycare-license/)).

## Staffing and retention

Competitive pay, clear roles, training, planning time, recognition and supportive leadership. Staff turnover disrupts children and families and raises costs.

## Enrolment and waitlist

Tours, online enquiries and enrolment forms, waitlist management, sibling priority, and transition planning as children age up.

## Tuition and finances

Clear tuition policies, autopay, late fees, subsidies paperwork, monthly budgets, and tracking key metrics: occupancy rate, revenue per child, staff cost %, collections and cash reserve.

## Parent communication

Daily reports, newsletters, conferences and quick responses (see [How to manage daycare communication](/blog/how-to-manage-daycare-communication/)).

## Software and automation

Childcare management software for attendance, billing, staff scheduling and reporting; automated reminders for payments, forms and events (see [How to automate business processes](/blog/how-to-automate-business-processes/)).

## Growing enrolment

Google Business Profile and reviews, a website with programme details and tour booking, parent referrals, employer partnerships and community events (see [How to start a daycare](/blog/how-to-start-a-daycare/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you run a daycare business?
Maintain compliance and safety, keep qualified staff, deliver consistent routines, manage enrolment and billing, communicate with parents and track finances.

### What makes a daycare successful?
Safe, nurturing care, stable staff, strong parent relationships, full enrolment and sound financial management.

### How do daycares keep staff?
Fair pay, training, planning time, recognition, manageable ratios and supportive leadership.

### What software do daycares use?
Childcare management platforms for attendance, billing, parent communication and staff scheduling.

### How do daycares fill spots?
Through reviews, referrals, an informative website with tour booking, waitlists and community outreach.

### What are the biggest daycare expenses?
Staff wages, then rent, food, supplies and insurance.
