<p class="lede">To start a CPA firm or accounting practice: confirm your <strong>licensing and firm registration requirements</strong>, choose a <strong>niche and service mix</strong> (tax, bookkeeping, advisory, audit), form a <strong>business entity</strong>, set <strong>pricing and packages</strong>, build a <strong>cloud tech stack</strong> (practice management, tax and accounting software, secure client portal), get <strong>professional liability insurance</strong>, launch a <strong>professional website and Google Business Profile</strong>, and win first clients through your network, referrals and niche marketing.</p>

## Step 1: Licensing and compliance

- CPA licence (or equivalent chartered/certified status in your country) for attest/audit work and use of protected titles
- Firm registration/permit with your state board or professional body where required
- Tax preparer registrations (e.g., PTIN in the US)
- Data security requirements for taxpayer information (written information security plans where required)

## Step 2: Choose a niche and services

| Service | Notes |
|---|---|
| Tax preparation and planning | Seasonal peaks; planning is higher value |
| Bookkeeping and payroll | Recurring monthly revenue |
| Advisory/fractional CFO | High value, relationship-driven |
| Audit and assurance | Licensing, peer review, higher risk |
| Industry niches | Dentists, real estate, e-commerce, non-profits, creators |

A niche makes marketing easier and pricing stronger.

## Step 3: Business setup

Entity choice (PC, PLLC or local equivalent), engagement letters, privacy policy, bank account, accounting for your own firm, and professional liability (E&O) insurance.

## Step 4: Pricing

Move beyond hourly where possible: fixed monthly packages (bookkeeping + tax + advisory), tiered tax return pricing, and value pricing for advisory. (Add-ons: [How to grow an accounting practice](/blog/how-to-grow-an-accounting-practice/).)

## Step 5: Technology

Practice management, tax software, cloud accounting platforms, document portal and e-signatures, payments, and automation (see [What tools do accountants use?](/blog/what-tools-do-accountants-use/)).

## Step 6: Brand and website

Clear niche positioning, service and pricing pages, team bios, client portal link, booking for discovery calls, and Google Business Profile (see [How to create a website for your business](/blog/how-to-create-a-website-for-your-business/)).

## Step 7: First clients

Network, former colleagues, referral partners (lawyers, financial advisers, bankers), niche communities, content and local SEO (see [How to get accounting clients](/blog/how-to-get-accounting-clients/)).

## Step 8: Hiring and scaling

Start with contractors or seasonal staff, document processes (checklists, SOPs), then hire for bookkeeping and client service as recurring revenue grows.

## Buying an accounting practice

Acquiring a book of clients gives immediate revenue; evaluate client retention, fee quality, staff, technology and valuation (often based on a multiple of recurring revenue, with retention-based payment terms).

## Starting a bookkeeping company instead

No CPA licence is needed for bookkeeping in many places, making it a lower-barrier start (see [How to start a bookkeeping business](/blog/how-to-start-a-bookkeeping-business/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start my own CPA firm?
Meet licensing and firm registration requirements, choose a niche and services, form an entity, set pricing, build a cloud tech stack, get insurance, launch a website and win clients through your network and referrals.

### How much does it cost to start an accounting firm?
Lean virtual firms can start with modest costs for software, insurance, licences and a website; offices and staff increase costs.

### Do you need to be a CPA to start an accounting firm?
Not for bookkeeping or many tax services in some jurisdictions, but attest/audit work and the CPA title require licensing.

### How do new accounting firms get clients?
Through personal networks, referral partners, niche communities, content, local SEO and Google Business Profile.

### What is the most profitable accounting niche?
Advisory and specialised niches (e.g., healthcare, real estate, e-commerce) often command higher fees than general tax prep.

### Should I buy an existing accounting practice?
Buying offers immediate clients and revenue but requires careful due diligence on retention and fees.
