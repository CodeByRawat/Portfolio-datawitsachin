<p class="lede">To get commercial landscaping contracts: target <strong>HOAs, property management companies, office parks, retail centres, healthcare and education campuses and government</strong>, meet their requirements (<strong>insurance levels, licences, safety programmes, references</strong>), find opportunities via <strong>direct relationships with property managers</strong>, <strong>RFP and procurement portals</strong> and <strong>networking</strong>, then win with thorough <strong>site walks</strong>, detailed <strong>bids</strong> and reliable communication.</p>

## Target commercial clients

| Client | What they value |
|---|---|
| HOAs | Curb appeal, responsiveness, budget control |
| Property management companies | Multi-site consistency, reporting |
| Office parks and retail centres | Professional appearance, scheduling around tenants |
| Healthcare and education | Safety, compliance, reliability |
| Government/municipal | Formal bids, compliance, pricing |

## Requirements

Higher liability limits, commercial auto, workers' comp, safety programme, licences (e.g., pesticide), W-9/vendor registration, references and sometimes bonding.

## Finding opportunities

1. **Property management companies:** meet portfolio and facility managers
2. **HOA boards and management firms**
3. **RFP sites and government procurement portals**
4. **Networking:** building owner associations, chambers, real estate groups
5. **Website and Google visibility** for "commercial landscaping [city]" (see [What is SEO?](/blog/what-is-seo/))
6. **Subcontracting** for national facility service providers

## Site walk and bid

- Walk the property with the manager; note turf areas, beds, irrigation, trees, snow needs
- Measure accurately; estimate crew hours and materials (see [How to price landscaping jobs](/blog/how-to-price-landscaping-jobs/))
- Bid: scope by service and frequency, schedule, crew plan, communication/reporting, insurance, pricing (monthly or annual)

## Keep commercial accounts

Consistent crews, proactive reports with photos, quick response to issues, enhancement proposals, and annual reviews.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I get commercial landscaping contracts?
Target property managers, HOAs and facilities, meet insurance and compliance requirements, build relationships, respond to RFPs and submit detailed bids after site walks.

### How do I bid on commercial landscaping jobs?
Walk the site, measure areas, estimate labour and materials, and submit a proposal with scope, frequency, schedule, reporting, insurance and pricing.

### What insurance do I need for commercial landscaping?
Typically higher general liability limits, commercial auto and workers' compensation; some contracts require bonding.

### How do I find landscaping RFPs?
Through government procurement portals, bid aggregator sites and property management companies' vendor programmes.

### Are commercial landscaping contracts profitable?
They can offer stable revenue and route density, though pricing is competitive and requirements are higher.

### How do I keep commercial clients?
Reliable crews, proactive communication with photo reports and quick issue resolution.
