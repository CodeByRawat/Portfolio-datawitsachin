<p class="lede">To get hired through a temp agency, <strong>register with two or three agencies</strong> that place people in your field, bring <strong>ID, work authorization and an updated resume</strong>, do well on the <strong>interview and any skills tests</strong>, say yes to early assignments, and <strong>check in regularly</strong>. Many agencies can place you within <strong>days</strong> if you're flexible.</p>

## Step 1: Pick the right agencies

Choose agencies that specialise in your field: warehouse and light industrial, office and admin, healthcare, IT or hospitality. Large national agencies and local agencies both work; registering with a few increases your chances.

## Step 2: Apply and register

Apply online or walk in. Bring:

- Government-issued ID and work authorization documents
- Resume and references
- Certifications (forklift, CPR, licences)
- Bank details for direct deposit

## Step 3: Interview and tests

Agencies interview you like an employer would. You may take typing, software, safety or skills tests and a background check or drug test.

## How fast do temp agencies hire?

Some place people the same week, especially in warehouse, manufacturing and events. Office and skilled roles may take longer.

## Step 4: Stay at the top of the list

- Answer calls and texts quickly
- Accept your first assignments
- Be flexible on shifts and locations
- Check in weekly
- Get good feedback from clients

## Step 5: Turn temp into permanent

Show up on time, learn fast, ask for more responsibility and tell your recruiter you want a permanent role. Many temp-to-hire jobs convert after a set number of hours.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I get hired at a temp agency?
Register with agencies in your field, bring documents, pass the interview and tests, and stay available.

### How fast do temp agencies hire?
Often within days for general labour; longer for specialised roles.

### How do I work for a temp agency?
Register, accept assignments, track your hours and get paid by the agency.

### Do temp jobs become permanent?
Often. Many are temp-to-hire.

### Can I sign up with more than one temp agency?
Yes, and it usually helps you find work faster.
