<p class="lede">To start a social media marketing agency (SMMA): choose a <strong>niche</strong> and <strong>platforms</strong> (Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube Shorts), define <strong>services</strong> (strategy, content creation, short-form video, community management, paid social, influencer campaigns), package <strong>monthly retainers</strong>, build a <strong>content production system</strong>, report on <strong>business results</strong>, and win clients with <strong>case studies, outreach and your own strong social presence</strong>.</p>

## Services

| Service | Notes |
|---|---|
| Strategy and content calendar | Monthly planning |
| Content creation | Graphics, carousels, copy |
| Short-form video | Reels, TikToks, Shorts (see [How to make a promotional video](/blog/how-to-make-a-promotional-video/)) |
| Community management | Comments, DMs, engagement |
| Paid social | Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn ads (see [How to advertise your business on Facebook](/blog/how-to-advertise-your-business-on-facebook/)) |
| Influencer/UGC | Creator sourcing and management |
| Reporting | Reach, engagement, leads, sales |

## Packages and pricing

Tiers by number of posts/videos, platforms, community management hours and ad management; charge setup fees for strategy and branding.

## Content production system

Monthly planning → scripting → shoots/batch production → editing → approvals → scheduling → reporting. Tools: Canva/Figma, CapCut/Premiere, scheduling tools, approval workflows (see [How to automate Facebook page posts](/blog/how-to-automate-facebook-page-posts/)).

## Getting clients

Pick a niche (restaurants, salons, gyms, real estate), show examples, offer audits, use outreach and referrals, and run your own agency socials as proof (see [How to promote a restaurant on social media](/blog/how-to-promote-a-restaurant-on-social-media/)).

## Scale

Hire editors and creators, build templates, use AI for ideas and captions, and focus on retention with clear reporting. (Broader agency guide: [How to start a marketing agency](/blog/how-to-start-a-marketing-agency/).)

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a social media marketing agency?
Choose a niche and platforms, define services, package retainers, build a content production system, report on results and win clients with case studies and outreach.

### What services does a social media agency offer?
Strategy, content creation, short-form video, community management, paid social, influencer campaigns and reporting.

### How much do social media agencies charge?
It varies by platforms, content volume and services; many use monthly retainers with tiered packages.

### What is SMMA?
Social Media Marketing Agency: a business that manages social media marketing for clients.

### How do social media agencies get clients?
Niche positioning, examples and case studies, outreach, referrals and strong social content of their own.

### Do social media agencies need video skills?
Increasingly yes, as short-form video drives reach on most platforms.
