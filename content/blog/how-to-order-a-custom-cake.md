<p class="lede">To order a custom cake, decide on <strong>servings, date and budget</strong>, find a <strong>baker whose style you like</strong>, send <strong>inspiration photos, colours, theme and flavours</strong>, mention <strong>allergies</strong>, get a <strong>quote</strong>, pay the <strong>deposit</strong>, and confirm <strong>pickup or delivery</strong>. Order <strong>2–4 weeks ahead</strong> for most cakes.</p>

## Step-by-step

1. **Know the basics**: event date, number of guests, budget
2. **Find a baker**: local bakeries, home bakers, Instagram, reviews
3. **Check their portfolio**: does their style match what you want?
4. **Share details**: theme, colours, inspiration photos, message on cake
5. **Choose flavours and fillings**: ask about tastings
6. **Mention allergies and dietary needs**
7. **Get a quote**: confirm price, size, design and what's included
8. **Pay a deposit**: to secure your date
9. **Confirm pickup or delivery**: time, address, transport tips

## Can I order a custom cake online?

Yes. Many bakers take orders through websites, order forms or Instagram messages. Some larger bakeries and grocery stores offer online cake designers.

## How long does a custom cake take?

| Cake | Order ahead |
|---|---|
| Simple custom cake | 1–2 weeks |
| Detailed custom cake | 2–4 weeks |
| Tiered or wedding cake | 2–6 months |

## Tips

- Be flexible on exact copies of other bakers' cakes
- Ask how to store and serve the cake
- Transport on a flat surface with air conditioning
- Confirm a week before the event

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I order a custom cake?
Pick a baker, share your design, servings and flavours, get a quote and pay a deposit.

### Can I order a custom cake online?
Yes, through bakers' websites, order forms or social media.

### How long does a custom cake take?
Order 2–4 weeks ahead for most cakes.

### What information does a baker need?
Date, servings, design, colours, flavours, allergies and pickup or delivery.

### Do bakers require a deposit?
Most do to secure the date.
