<p class="lede">To start a laundromat, decide whether to <strong>buy an existing store or build a new one</strong>, pick a <strong>location with renters and few in-home washers</strong>, secure a <strong>long lease</strong>, budget for <strong>commercial washers, dryers, plumbing, venting and utilities</strong>, arrange <strong>financing</strong>, get <strong>permits</strong>, and add <strong>wash-and-fold, pickup/delivery and card payments</strong> to boost revenue.</p>

## Step 1: Buy or build?

| | Buy existing | Build new |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | Immediate, with history | Starts at zero |
| Equipment | May be old | Brand new, efficient |
| Risk | Verify books carefully | Location risk |
| Setup time | Fast | Months of build-out |

See [How to buy a laundromat business](/blog/how-to-buy-a-laundromat-business/) for due diligence.

## Step 2: Choose the location

Look for:

- Dense **rental housing** (apartments without in-unit laundry)
- Visible storefront with **parking**
- Low or outdated **competition** nearby
- Safe area with good lighting
- **Utility capacity** (water, sewer, gas, electric)

## Step 3: Negotiate the lease

Laundromats need **long leases** (often with renewal options) because equipment lasts many years. Negotiate utility responsibilities, build-out allowances and exclusivity.

## Step 4: Equipment and build-out

- Front-load washers in several sizes
- Stacked and large-capacity dryers
- Water heater/boiler, plumbing and drains
- Dryer venting and HVAC
- Card or app payment system
- Folding tables, carts, seating, Wi-Fi, vending, security cameras

## Step 5: Financing

Options include **equipment financing** (often through distributors), **SBA loans**, bank loans and seller financing when buying. Get a full cost estimate first: [How much does it cost to open a laundromat?](/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-open-a-laundromat/).

## Step 6: Permits and setup

Business registration, local licence, building and plumbing permits, health or wastewater permits where required, sign permits and insurance.

## Step 7: Revenue add-ons

- **Wash-and-fold** service (per pound)
- **Pickup and delivery** via an app or website
- **Commercial accounts** (salons, gyms, Airbnb hosts)
- Vending and soap sales
- Dry cleaning drop-off

## Step 8: Marketing

Grand opening promotions, Google Business Profile, signage, loyalty discounts, and a website where customers book pickup. For operations after opening, read [How to run a laundromat business](/blog/how-to-run-a-laundromat-business/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a laundromat business?
Decide buy vs build, find a strong location, sign a long lease, finance equipment, get permits and add services like wash-and-fold.

### How do you open a laundromat with no experience?
Buying an established laundromat, learning from distributors and starting with simple self-service operations helps first-time owners.

### How do I start a coin laundromat business?
The steps are the same; many modern stores add card and app payments alongside coins.

### Is it better to buy or build a laundromat?
Buying gives immediate income; building gives new equipment and a location you choose.

### What is the most important factor for a laundromat?
Location: renter density, visibility, parking and utility costs.

### Can a laundromat be run semi-absentee?
Yes, with cameras, card systems and an attendant or manager, though owners still need oversight.
