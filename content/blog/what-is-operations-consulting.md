<p class="lede"><strong>Operations consulting</strong> helps organisations <strong>run more efficiently and reliably</strong> by improving how work gets done: <strong>processes, supply chains, capacity, quality, technology and automation</strong>. Operations consultants analyse current operations, find bottlenecks and waste, redesign processes and help implement changes that reduce costs, speed up delivery and improve customer experience.</p>

## What operations consultants do

- Map current processes and measure performance
- Identify bottlenecks, waste and quality issues
- Redesign workflows (lean, Six Sigma)
- Improve supply chain and inventory
- Plan capacity and staffing
- Select and implement systems and automation
- Set KPIs and dashboards
- Train teams and manage change

## Results organisations seek

Lower costs, shorter cycle times, higher quality, better capacity utilisation, improved customer satisfaction and scalable growth.

## Skills of operations consultants

Process mapping, data analysis, lean/Six Sigma, project and change management, technology knowledge and communication.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is operations consulting?
Consulting that improves how organisations operate, focusing on processes, supply chain, capacity, quality, technology and automation.

### What does an operations consultant do?
Analyses operations, identifies inefficiencies, redesigns processes and helps implement improvements and systems.

### What is the difference between operations and strategy consulting?
Strategy consulting decides where and how to compete; operations consulting improves how the organisation executes.

### What methods do operations consultants use?
Lean, Six Sigma, process mapping, data analysis, capacity planning and automation.

### When should a company hire an operations consultant?
When costs are rising, delivery is slow, quality is inconsistent or the company is scaling quickly.

### Can automation be part of operations consulting?
Yes; workflow automation, RPA and AI are common tools for improving operations.
