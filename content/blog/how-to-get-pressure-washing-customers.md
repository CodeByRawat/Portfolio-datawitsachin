<p class="lede">The fastest ways to get pressure washing customers are <strong>door hangers and door-to-door around jobs you've done</strong>, posting <strong>before-and-after photos</strong>, a <strong>Google Business Profile with reviews</strong>, and a website with <strong>instant quotes</strong>. For steady income, sign <strong>commercial and recurring contracts</strong>.</p>

## 12 ways to get pressure washing jobs

1. **Neighbour marketing:** after every job, leave door hangers on 20–30 nearby homes
2. **Door-to-door:** knock with a clean "before/after" photo and a same-week price
3. **Google Business Profile:** service areas, photos and regular posts
4. **Reviews:** ask every happy customer
5. **Website with instant quotes:** let homeowners get a price and book online
6. **Facebook and Nextdoor:** share before-and-after posts in local groups
7. **Short videos:** satisfying cleaning videos on Instagram and TikTok
8. **Yard signs:** with permission, at houses you've just cleaned
9. **Commercial accounts:** restaurants (dumpster pads, drive-thrus), gas stations, storefronts, apartments
10. **Realtors and property managers:** listings and move-outs
11. **HOAs:** sidewalks, entrances, pools and common areas
12. **Partners:** window cleaners, painters, roofers and lawn care companies

## Turn one-time jobs into repeat work

Offer yearly house wash plans, monthly commercial cleaning, and bundle deals (house + driveway + windows).

## Best times to market

Spring is the busiest; start marketing in late winter. Market roof and house washing in late summer before fall, and holiday light installs in fall.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I get my first pressure washing customers?
Clean friends' and neighbours' homes at a discount, take photos, then door-hang the surrounding streets.

### Do door hangers work for pressure washing?
Yes, especially near homes you've just cleaned.

### How do pressure washing companies get commercial clients?
Visit restaurants, gas stations and property managers in person and offer recurring service.

### Should I advertise on Google for pressure washing?
A free Google Business Profile with reviews is the most important step; paid ads can help once your prices are set.

### What's the best season for pressure washing?
Spring and early summer are the busiest.
