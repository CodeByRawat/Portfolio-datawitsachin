<p class="lede">A <strong>microsite</strong> is a <strong>small, standalone website</strong>, usually a handful of pages, created for a <strong>specific campaign, product launch, event, audience or story</strong>, separate from a company's main website. It often has its <strong>own domain or subdomain</strong> and its own design, allowing a brand to create a focused, immersive experience without the constraints of the main site.</p>

## Microsite vs landing page vs website

| | Landing page | Microsite | Main website |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Size** | One page | A few to a dozen pages | Many pages |
| **Purpose** | One conversion action | One campaign, product, event or theme | Represent the whole business |
| **Lifespan** | Campaign length | Campaign to multi-year | Permanent |
| **Domain** | Usually main domain path | Own domain, subdomain or subfolder | Main domain |
| **Design** | Conversion-focused | Often unique, immersive | Brand standard |

(See [What is a landing page?](/blog/what-is-a-landing-page/).)

## Microsite examples

- **Product launch:** an interactive site for a new phone, car or app feature with 3D visuals and specs
- **Event:** a conference site with agenda, speakers, venue and tickets
- **Campaign storytelling:** an annual "year in review" or impact report with scroll animations
- **Recruitment:** a careers microsite showcasing culture and roles
- **Sub-brand or new market:** a site for a product line aimed at a different audience
- **Interactive tool:** a calculator, quiz or configurator
- **Partnerships:** co-branded campaign sites

## Benefits of microsites

- **Focus:** one topic without the main site's navigation and clutter
- **Creative freedom:** unique design, motion and interactivity (see [What are motion graphics?](/blog/what-are-motion-graphics/))
- **Speed to launch:** independent of slow corporate website processes
- **Targeted messaging** for specific audiences
- **Easier campaign measurement**
- **Memorable campaign URLs** for ads, print and TV

## Drawbacks

- **SEO split:** a separate domain doesn't benefit from (or add to) your main site's authority
- **Maintenance:** extra sites to update, secure and eventually retire
- **Brand fragmentation** if not managed
- **Cost** of design, build and hosting
- **Abandoned microsites** with outdated info can hurt trust

## Microsites and SEO

- For **long-term SEO value**, a **subfolder** on your main domain (brand.com/campaign) usually helps most, because links and traffic strengthen your main site
- **Separate domains** suit short campaigns, distinct brands, or when you want a memorable URL
- When retiring a microsite, **301 redirect** its URLs to relevant main-site pages to keep link value
- Ensure crawlable content, unique titles and descriptions, and fast performance

## How to build a microsite

1. **Define the goal and audience** (sign-ups, launch awareness, event registrations)
2. **Plan pages and content:** typically home, details, proof/FAQ and CTA page
3. **Choose domain strategy:** subfolder, subdomain or new domain (see [How to buy a domain name](/blog/how-to-buy-a-domain-name/))
4. **Design** for the campaign: bold visuals, interactions, mobile-first
5. **Build** with Webflow, Framer or custom code for performance and animations (see [What is the best website builder?](/blog/what-is-the-best-website-builder/))
6. **Add tracking:** analytics, UTM parameters, conversion events
7. **Integrate** forms with CRM and email
8. **Launch and promote** via ads, email, social and PR
9. **Plan the end:** archive, redirect or fold content into the main site

## How much does a microsite cost?

Simple microsites built with a builder can cost a few hundred to a few thousand dollars; custom, animated or interactive microsites from agencies often cost $5,000–$50,000+. (General ranges: [How much does a website cost?](/blog/how-much-does-a-website-cost/))

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a microsite?
A small, standalone website created for a specific campaign, product, event or audience, separate from a company's main website.

### What is the difference between a microsite and a landing page?
A landing page is a single page focused on one action; a microsite has several pages and offers a fuller experience around one theme.

### Are microsites good for SEO?
Separate-domain microsites don't build your main site's authority. For long-term SEO, a subfolder on the main domain is usually better.

### When should I use a microsite?
For product launches, events, campaigns, sub-brands or interactive experiences that need their own focus and design.

### Can a microsite be on a subdomain?
Yes. Microsites can live on subdomains, subfolders or entirely separate domains depending on goals.

### What happens to a microsite after the campaign?
Either keep it updated, archive it, or redirect its pages to the main website to preserve traffic and links.
