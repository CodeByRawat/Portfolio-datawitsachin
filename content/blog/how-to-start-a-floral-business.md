<p class="lede">To start a floral business, choose your <strong>model</strong> (home studio, retail flower shop, event and wedding florist, or online delivery), build <strong>floral design skills</strong>, set up <strong>wholesale suppliers and a cooler</strong>, register the business, set <strong>pricing that covers flowers, labour and waste</strong>, and market through <strong>Google, social media, weddings and corporate clients</strong>. Startup costs range from about <strong>$2,000 for a home studio to $50,000+ for a shop</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Choose your model

| Model | Startup cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Home studio florist | $2,000 – $10,000 | Delivery and events |
| Retail flower shop | $25,000 – $100,000+ | Walk-ins and orders |
| Wedding and event florist | $5,000 – $25,000 | High-value bookings |
| Online / subscription | $3,000 – $20,000 | Weekly deliveries |

## Step 2: Learn floral design

Floral design courses, working at a flower shop, or mentoring with an experienced florist. Learn conditioning flowers, mechanics, colour theory and wedding work.

## Step 3: Suppliers and equipment

- Wholesale flower markets and distributors
- Local flower farms
- Floral cooler (walk-in or reach-in)
- Buckets, vases, foam alternatives, tape and wire
- Work tables and storage

## Step 4: Register and insure

Business licence, sales tax permit, resale certificate for buying wholesale, general liability insurance, and a commercial vehicle policy for deliveries.

## Step 5: Pricing

Florists commonly price at about 3× flower and material cost, plus a labour or design fee, and delivery charges. Account for waste and seasonal price changes.

## Step 6: Get customers

1. Google Business Profile and a website with online ordering
2. Instagram and Pinterest portfolios
3. Wedding planners and venues
4. Corporate weekly arrangements (offices, hotels, restaurants)
5. Funeral homes
6. Holidays: Valentine's Day, Mother's Day, Christmas

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a floral business?
Choose a model, learn floral design, set up suppliers and a cooler, register and market locally.

### How much does it cost to start a florist business?
From about $2,000 for a home studio to $50,000+ for a shop.

### Do I need training to be a florist?
Not legally, but courses or shop experience help a lot.

### How do florists price arrangements?
Often about 3× flowers and materials, plus labour and delivery.

### Is a floral business profitable?
Yes, especially weddings, events and corporate accounts.
