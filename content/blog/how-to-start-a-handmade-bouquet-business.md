<p class="lede">To start a handmade bouquet business, choose your <strong>bouquet type</strong> (fresh flowers, dried flowers, crochet, ribbon or money bouquets), <strong>learn arranging and wrapping</strong>, find <strong>wholesale suppliers</strong>, set up a <strong>small home workspace</strong>, price for <strong>materials, time and profit</strong>, and sell through <strong>Instagram, TikTok, Etsy, local delivery and pop-ups</strong>. Many start from home for <strong>$300 to $2,000</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Choose your niche

| Bouquet type | Notes |
|---|---|
| Fresh flower bouquets | Local delivery, perishable |
| Dried and preserved flowers | Ship anywhere, long-lasting |
| Crochet or yarn flowers | Handmade, shippable, popular gifts |
| Ribbon / satin bouquets | Trendy, lasting |
| Money or snack bouquets | Graduations, birthdays |
| Wedding and event bouquets | Higher prices |

## Step 2: Learn the skills

Practise colour palettes, spiral arranging, wrapping paper styles, ribbon tying and photography. Online classes and practice bouquets help.

## Step 3: Source supplies

Wholesale flower markets, online wholesalers and local growers for fresh flowers; craft wholesalers for wrapping paper, ribbon, yarn and dried stems.

## Step 4: Startup costs

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Tools (shears, buckets, tape, wire) | $50 – $200 |
| Wrapping paper, ribbon, cellophane | $100 – $300 |
| First flowers or materials | $100 – $800 |
| Cooler or fridge (fresh flowers) | $0 – $500 |
| Business registration | $50 – $300 |
| Website or online shop | $0 – $500 |

## Step 5: Pricing

Many florists price at **about 3× the cost of flowers and materials**, plus delivery. Add a design or labour fee for custom pieces.

## Step 6: Sell your bouquets

- Instagram and TikTok videos of bouquet making
- Local delivery for birthdays and Valentine's Day
- Pop-up stands at markets and cafés
- Etsy for dried, crochet and ribbon bouquets
- Corporate and event orders

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a handmade bouquet business?
Pick a bouquet type, learn arranging, buy wholesale supplies, price well and sell on social media and locally.

### Can I start a flower business from home?
Yes. Many bouquet businesses start from home with local delivery.

### How much does it cost to start a bouquet business?
Often $300 to $2,000.

### How do I price handmade bouquets?
Often about 3× materials, plus labour and delivery.

### What bouquets sell best online?
Dried, crochet and ribbon bouquets ship well.
