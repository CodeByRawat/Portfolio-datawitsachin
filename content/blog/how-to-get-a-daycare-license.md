<p class="lede">To get a daycare licence: contact your <strong>state or local childcare licensing agency</strong>, choose the right <strong>licence type</strong> (family home, group home or center), complete any required <strong>orientation and training</strong> (CPR, first aid, child development), pass <strong>background checks</strong> for everyone in the home or facility, prepare a <strong>safe space</strong> that meets requirements, write <strong>policies and records</strong>, submit the <strong>application and fees</strong>, and pass <strong>health, fire and licensing inspections</strong>. Requirements vary by location.</p>

## Common licence types

| Type | Typical setting |
|---|---|
| Family/home childcare | Small group in a residence |
| Group/large family childcare | Larger group in a residence, with assistant |
| Childcare center | Commercial facility |
| Preschool/early learning | Education-focused programmes |

## Typical requirements

- Minimum age and education/experience of provider or director
- **Background checks** (fingerprinting) for staff and household members
- **CPR and first aid** certification
- Health and safety training, mandated reporter training
- **Space requirements** (indoor and outdoor square footage per child)
- Safety: smoke detectors, fire extinguishers, outlet covers, secure storage, safe sleep
- **Ratios and group sizes**
- Written policies: emergency plans, illness, discipline, meals, pickup
- Records: enrolment forms, immunisations, attendance, incident reports
- Health and fire inspections

## Step-by-step process

1. Contact the licensing agency and attend orientation
2. Complete training and certifications
3. Prepare the space and equipment
4. Write policies and a parent handbook
5. Complete background checks
6. Submit the application and fees
7. Pass inspections
8. Receive licence; keep up with renewals and unannounced visits

## Costs and timeline

Fees vary by state and licence type; total time often ranges from a few weeks to several months depending on training, inspections and facility readiness.

## Staying compliant

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I get a daycare license?
Contact your licensing agency, complete orientation and training, pass background checks, prepare a safe space, write policies, apply and pass inspections.

### How long does it take to get a daycare license?
Often a few weeks to several months depending on location and readiness.

### How much does a daycare license cost?
Fees vary by state and licence type, plus costs for training, background checks and safety upgrades.

### Can I run a daycare without a licence?
Many places allow caring for a very small number of unrelated children without a licence, but limits are strict; check local rules.

### What training do daycare providers need?
Commonly CPR, first aid, health and safety, safe sleep, child development and mandated reporter training.

### Do daycare licences need renewal?
Yes, typically with ongoing training, inspections and updated records.
