<p class="lede">Truck driver pay depends on <strong>how drivers are paid</strong> (cents per mile, hourly, salary, percentage of the load or per load), the <strong>type of job</strong> (OTR long-haul, regional, local, team, specialised such as hazmat or flatbed), <strong>experience</strong>, <strong>miles driven</strong> and the <strong>company</strong>. Many company drivers are paid <strong>per mile</strong>; local drivers are often paid <strong>hourly</strong>; and <strong>owner-operators</strong> earn the load rate minus their own costs.</p>

## How truckers get paid

| Method | Common for |
|---|---|
| Cents per mile (CPM) | OTR and regional company drivers |
| Hourly | Local and some regional drivers |
| Salary | Dedicated routes |
| Percentage of load | Some carriers and owner-operators leased on |
| Per load/stop | Specialised or local deliveries |

Plus: sign-on, safety, fuel efficiency and referral bonuses; detention, layover and stop pay; per diem.

## What affects pay

Experience, endorsements (hazmat, tanker, doubles), freight type (reefer, flatbed, heavy haul), team driving, region, carrier pay scales and miles available.

## OTR, regional and local

- **OTR (over-the-road):** long hauls, weeks away, often more miles (see [What is OTR in trucking?](/blog/what-is-otr-in-trucking/))
- **Regional:** within a region, more home time
- **Local:** home daily, often hourly pay

## Owner-operators

Earn the full load rate but pay all costs (see [How to become an owner-operator truck driver](/blog/how-to-become-an-owner-operator-truck-driver/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How much do truck drivers make?
It depends on pay method, job type, experience, miles and company; specialised and experienced drivers usually earn more.

### How much do truck drivers make per mile?
Company drivers are often paid cents per mile, with rates rising with experience and specialisation.

### How do truckers get paid?
By the mile, hourly, salary, percentage of load or per load, plus bonuses and accessorial pay.

### Do OTR drivers make more?
Often, due to more miles, but with less home time.

### How much does an owner-operator make?
Gross revenue is higher, but net income depends on expenses like fuel, truck payments and insurance.

### Which trucking jobs pay the most?
Specialised roles like hazmat, tanker, heavy haul, team driving and experienced owner-operators often pay more.
