<p class="lede">A med spa (medical spa) is a business that combines a <strong>spa experience</strong> with <strong>medical aesthetic treatments</strong> such as injectables, laser treatments and advanced skin care, performed or supervised by <strong>licensed medical professionals</strong>. Legally it is often treated as a <strong>medical practice</strong>, and it earns money from <strong>treatments, packages, memberships and skincare retail</strong>.</p>

## What does a med spa do?

- Neurotoxin and dermal filler injections
- Laser hair removal and skin resurfacing
- Microneedling, chemical peels, facials
- Body contouring
- Skincare consultations and products

## Med spa vs day spa vs dermatology

| | Med spa | Day spa | Dermatology clinic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Focus | Aesthetic medical treatments | Relaxation | Medical skin conditions |
| Staff | Medical providers + estheticians | Massage therapists, estheticians | Dermatologists |
| Oversight | Medical director | None medical | Physicians |

## Is a med spa a medical practice?

Often yes. Because treatments are medical, many states regulate med spas like medical practices, with **ownership rules, medical director oversight and licensed providers**. See [Who can open a medical spa?](/blog/who-can-open-a-medical-spa/).

## How med spas make money

Per-treatment fees, treatment packages, monthly memberships, skincare retail and repeat injectable clients.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a med spa business?
A business offering medical aesthetic treatments in a spa-like setting under medical oversight.

### What do med spas do?
Injectables, lasers, skin treatments, body contouring and skincare.

### Is a med spa a medical practice?
Often yes, under state law.

### What is the difference between a med spa and a spa?
Med spas offer medical treatments with licensed providers; day spas focus on relaxation services.

### Who works at a med spa?
Medical directors, nurse injectors, NPs/PAs, estheticians and front-desk staff.

### Are med spas profitable?
They can be, thanks to high-value treatments, memberships and repeat clients.
