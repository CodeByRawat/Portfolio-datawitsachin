<p class="lede">Yes, epoxy flooring can be a very good business. Garage and commercial floor coating jobs often have <strong>40% to 60% gross margins</strong>, startup costs are <strong>lower than many trades</strong>, and many jobs take <strong>one or two days</strong>. A small company can bring in <strong>$200,000 to $600,000+</strong> a year. The main risks are <strong>coating failures from poor prep</strong> and <strong>warranty callbacks</strong>.</p>

These are rough ranges; your market and quality matter most.

## Why it's a good business

- High price per square foot vs material cost
- Short jobs: many garages done in a day
- Growing demand for garage makeovers
- Lower startup cost than many trades
- Strong visual before-and-after marketing

## Downsides and risks

| Risk | How to manage it |
|---|---|
| Peeling and failures | Proper grinding and moisture testing |
| Warranty callbacks | Quality systems and clear warranty terms |
| Chemical exposure | Respirators and ventilation |
| Weather and humidity | Schedule carefully |
| Competition from cheap installers | Sell quality and warranty |

## How much do epoxy businesses make?

| Stage | Rough yearly revenue |
|---|---|
| Solo installer | $100,000 – $250,000 |
| One to two crews | $250,000 – $600,000+ |

## How to make it more profitable

- Sell premium systems (flake with polyaspartic topcoat, metallic)
- Upsell stem walls, crack repair and storage
- Partner with builders and garage organisers
- Use before-and-after photos and reviews to sell
- Avoid callbacks with excellent prep

## Frequently asked questions

### Is epoxy flooring a good business?
Yes. Margins are high and startup costs are relatively low.

### How profitable is an epoxy flooring business?
Gross margins often run 40% to 60%.

### How much do epoxy flooring companies make?
Small companies often bring in $200,000 to $600,000+ a year.

### Why does epoxy fail?
Usually poor surface prep or moisture in the concrete.

### Is epoxy flooring hard to learn?
The basics are learnable quickly, but prep and premium systems take practice.
