<p class="lede">A <strong>digital process</strong> is a business process carried out with digital tools and data rather than paper, manual handoffs and spreadsheets. <strong>Digital process automation (DPA)</strong> is the practice of automating those processes end to end (forms, approvals, routing, system updates, documents, notifications and decisions) using low-code workflow platforms, integrations, business rules and increasingly AI, so work flows from start to finish with minimal manual effort and full visibility.</p>

Think of an employee onboarding, a loan application, a purchase request, a customer complaint or an insurance claim. Each involves forms, documents, several people, several systems and rules about who approves what. Digital process automation turns these into a designed, automated flow instead of emails and chasing. This guide explains what DPA is, how it compares with RPA and BPM, examples, tools and how to start.

## What is a digital process?

A process is "digital" when:
- **Inputs are digital:** online forms, emails, uploads, API data instead of paper
- **Steps run in software:** routing, approvals, checks and calculations happen in systems
- **Data flows between systems** without re-typing
- **Status is visible** to everyone involved
- **Records are kept automatically** for audit and analysis

**Digitisation** (scanning paper into PDFs) is not the same as a digital process. A scanned form emailed around for signatures is digitised; a web form that routes itself, validates data, collects e-signatures and updates the HR system is a digital process.

## What is digital process automation (DPA)?

DPA automates the whole process, not just individual tasks:

| DPA capability | Example |
|---|---|
| **Digital forms and portals** | Customers or employees submit requests online |
| **Workflow and routing** | Tasks automatically assigned to the right people in the right order |
| **Business rules** | "Requests over $5,000 need finance director approval" |
| **Integrations** | Read and update CRM, ERP, HR, document management via APIs |
| **Document generation and e-signature** | Contracts and letters created from templates and signed online |
| **Notifications and SLAs** | Reminders, escalations and deadlines |
| **Case management** | Handling exceptions and complex, variable work |
| **Analytics** | Cycle times, bottlenecks and workloads |
| **AI** | Extracting data from documents, classifying requests, drafting responses, recommending decisions |

## DPA vs RPA vs BPM vs workflow automation

| | Digital process automation (DPA) | Robotic process automation (RPA) | Business process management (BPM) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Focus** | Automating end-to-end processes and customer/employee journeys | Automating individual repetitive tasks in existing application interfaces | The discipline of designing, managing and improving processes |
| **How** | Low-code workflows, forms, integrations, rules, AI | Bots mimicking clicks and keystrokes | Methodology plus BPM software (BPMS) |
| **Best for** | Redesigning processes for speed and experience | Legacy systems without APIs, quick wins | Organisation-wide process governance and improvement |
| **Relationship** | Often built on BPM platforms; can orchestrate RPA bots | Often a step within a DPA workflow | DPA is a modern, low-code evolution of BPM |

(More: [What is business process management?](/blog/what-is-business-process-management/), [What is robotic process automation?](/blog/what-is-robotic-process-automation/), [What is hyperautomation?](/blog/what-is-hyperautomation/).)

**Workflow automation** is a broader, everyday term for automating sequences of steps; DPA emphasises **end-to-end, cross-department processes** and digital experiences. (See [What is a workflow?](/blog/what-is-a-workflow/))

## Examples of digital process automation

### Human resources
**Employee onboarding:** offer accepted → digital forms and documents → e-signatures → IT accounts and equipment requested → payroll set up → training assigned → manager checklist. (See [What is HR automation?](/blog/what-is-hr-automation/))

### Finance
**Purchase-to-pay:** purchase request form → budget check → approvals by amount → PO created in ERP → invoice matched → payment scheduled. (See [Accounts payable automation](/blog/accounts-payable-automation/).)

### Banking and lending
**Loan origination:** online application → document upload with AI extraction → identity and credit checks → risk rules → underwriter review for exceptions → digital offer and e-signature. (See [Banking and financial process automation](/blog/banking-and-financial-process-automation/).)

### Insurance
**Claims:** first notice of loss online → photos and documents → AI triage → automatic approval for simple claims → adjuster for complex ones → payment. (See [Insurance claims automation](/blog/insurance-claims-automation/).)

### Customer service
**Complaints and requests:** web form or email → AI classifies and routes → SLA tracking → resolution workflow → customer updates → satisfaction survey.

### Public sector
**Permits and licences:** online applications, document checks, inspections scheduling, approvals and digital certificates.

### Legal
**Contract requests:** intake form → template selection → clause approvals → e-signature → repository. (See [Legal workflow automation](/blog/legal-workflow-automation/).)

## Benefits of DPA

- **Speed:** processes that took weeks finish in days or hours
- **Better customer and employee experience:** self-service and status visibility
- **Fewer errors:** validation and integrations replace re-typing
- **Compliance:** rules enforced consistently, full audit trails
- **Transparency:** know where every request is and where bottlenecks are
- **Scalability:** handle more volume without proportional headcount
- **Agility:** low-code tools let teams change processes quickly

## Challenges

- **Automating a bad process:** redesign first
- **Integration with legacy systems** (sometimes needing RPA)
- **Change management:** people used to email and paper
- **Governance** of citizen-developed apps
- **Exceptions:** real processes have many variations; design case handling

## How to start with digital process automation

1. **Pick a process** that is high-volume, slow, error-prone or painful for customers or staff.
2. **Map the current process** (as-is): steps, people, systems, documents, delays, exceptions.
3. **Redesign it** (to-be): remove unnecessary steps and approvals, design for self-service.
4. **Define rules and data** needed at each step.
5. **Choose a platform** fitting your systems, skills and scale.
6. **Build iteratively:** start with the core path, then add exceptions.
7. **Integrate systems** to remove re-typing.
8. **Add AI** for documents, classification and drafting where it adds value.
9. **Test with real users** and real cases.
10. **Launch, measure** cycle time, errors and satisfaction, and **improve**.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a digital process?
A digital process is a business process carried out using digital forms, systems and data, with steps, routing, approvals and records handled in software rather than on paper or through manual handoffs.

### What is digital process automation?
Digital process automation (DPA) uses low-code workflow platforms, integrations, business rules and AI to automate end-to-end business processes across people and systems, improving speed, accuracy and experience.

### What is the difference between DPA and RPA?
DPA automates and redesigns entire processes with workflows, forms and integrations. RPA automates individual repetitive tasks by having software bots mimic user actions in existing applications. DPA workflows often include RPA steps.

### What is an example of digital process automation?
An online employee onboarding process that collects documents and e-signatures, requests IT accounts and equipment, sets up payroll and assigns training automatically after an offer is accepted.

### Is DPA the same as BPM?
They're closely related. BPM is the broader discipline of managing and improving processes, while DPA focuses on automating processes with modern low-code tools; DPA is often seen as an evolution of BPM software.

### What are digital process automation tools?
Pega, Appian, Microsoft Power Platform, ServiceNow, Nintex, Bizagi, Kissflow and OutSystems, often combined with integration tools such as Workato, MuleSoft, n8n or Make and AI services.
