<p class="lede">To buy a domain name: <strong>choose a short, memorable name</strong>, <strong>check availability</strong> on a domain registrar (such as Cloudflare Registrar, Namecheap, Porkbun, Squarespace Domains or GoDaddy), <strong>add it to your cart</strong> with the extension you want (.com, .co, .in, .io…), <strong>create an account in your own name</strong>, <strong>turn on WHOIS privacy and auto-renew</strong>, and pay, usually <strong>about $10–$20 per year</strong> for a .com. Then <strong>point the domain's DNS</strong> to your website host and email provider.</p>

## Step 1: Choose a good domain name

- **Short and easy to spell** (say it out loud: would someone type it correctly?)
- **Brandable** rather than stuffed with keywords
- **Avoid hyphens and numbers**
- **Match your business name** and social handles where possible
- **Check trademarks** to avoid legal problems
- Local businesses can include the city if the brand name is taken (e.g., brightsmileaustin.com)

## Step 2: Choose an extension (TLD)

| Extension | Best for |
|---|---|
| **.com** | Default choice, most trusted globally |
| **Country codes** (.in, .co.uk, .ca, .com.au) | Local businesses targeting one country |
| **.co, .io, .ai** | Startups and tech (often pricier) |
| **.org** | Non-profits and communities |
| **.store, .shop, .studio, .agency** | Descriptive brand names when .com is taken |

## Step 3: Check if the website name is available

Type the name into any registrar's search box. It shows:
- **Available:** register it now
- **Taken:** see alternatives, or check whether it's for sale
- **Premium:** available but priced higher

You can also check ownership details via **WHOIS/RDAP lookup** tools (often hidden by privacy services).

## Step 4: Where to buy a domain

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| **Cloudflare Registrar** | At-cost pricing, no markup on renewals | Requires Cloudflare DNS |
| **Namecheap, Porkbun** | Low prices, free privacy, simple | Some upsells |
| **Squarespace Domains, GoDaddy** | Well known, many TLDs | Renewal prices and upsells can be higher |
| **Through your website builder** (Wix, Shopify, Squarespace) | Easiest connection | Sometimes higher renewal prices; harder to move later |
| **Country registrars** | Required or best for some country domains | Varies |

**Tip:** compare **renewal prices**, not just first-year deals.

## Step 5: Register it properly

1. Create the account with **your business email** (not a freelancer's)
2. Enter accurate owner details (required by registry rules)
3. Enable **WHOIS privacy** (free with many registrars)
4. Enable **auto-renew** and keep a valid payment method
5. Turn on **two-factor authentication** for the account
6. Choose 1–10 years; longer registration avoids accidental expiry

## How much does a domain name cost?

- **.com:** typically ~$10–$20/year
- **Country domains:** ~$5–$40/year
- **Premium domains or aftermarket purchases:** hundreds to thousands of dollars
- Add-ons you usually **don't need**: paid privacy (often free elsewhere), "SEO tools", website builders bundled by the registrar

## Buying a domain someone already owns

1. Check if the site is active or parked (see [What is this website built with?](/blog/what-is-this-website-built-with/))
2. Look for "for sale" pages or listings on marketplaces (Sedo, Afternic, Dan.com-style marketplaces)
3. Contact the owner via WHOIS contact forms
4. Use an **escrow service** to pay safely
5. Consider whether a different name or extension is better value

## Step 6: Connect the domain to your website

| Where your site is hosted | What to do |
|---|---|
| Website builder (Wix, Squarespace, Shopify, Framer, Webflow) | Follow the builder's "connect domain" steps (change nameservers or add A/CNAME records) |
| Vercel, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages | Add the domain in the dashboard, then add the given DNS records |
| WordPress host | Point nameservers to the host or add an A record |

DNS changes usually work within minutes to a few hours (up to 48 hours). Make sure **HTTPS** is active after connecting.

## Step 7: Set up email on your domain

Use Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Zoho Mail or similar. Add **MX records**, plus **SPF, DKIM and DMARC** records so emails don't land in spam.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Letting a designer or agency register the domain in their name
- Forgetting renewal and losing the domain
- Choosing a hard-to-spell name
- Buying many unneeded add-ons
- Not securing the registrar account with 2FA

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I buy a domain name?
Search for your desired name at a domain registrar, choose an extension, create an account in your own name, enable privacy and auto-renew, and pay the yearly fee.

### How do I check if a website name is available?
Enter it into a registrar's domain search. It will show whether it's available, taken or premium, and suggest alternatives.

### Where is the best place to buy a domain?
Low-cost, transparent registrars such as Cloudflare Registrar, Namecheap and Porkbun are popular. Compare renewal prices and included privacy.

### How much does a domain name cost?
A .com typically costs about $10–$20 per year; some extensions and premium names cost more.

### Can I buy a domain name permanently?
No. Domains are registered for 1–10 years at a time and must be renewed, though you can renew indefinitely.

### Do I need hosting to buy a domain?
No. You can buy a domain on its own and connect it to hosting or a website builder later.
