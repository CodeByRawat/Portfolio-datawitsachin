<p class="lede">The best pest control marketing combines <strong>Google visibility</strong> (Business Profile, reviews, Local Services Ads and SEO pages for each pest and city), a <strong>website that books inspections</strong>, <strong>seasonal campaigns</strong>, <strong>door-to-door sales</strong> in peak season, <strong>referrals</strong> and <strong>commercial outreach</strong>. Because revenue is recurring, focus on <strong>converting one-time calls into service plans</strong> and keeping customers.</p>

## Channels

1. **Google Business Profile:** services, service area, photos and steady reviews
2. **Local Services Ads:** pay-per-lead with Google screening badge
3. **SEO:** pages for each pest (termites, ants, rodents, bed bugs, mosquitoes) and each city
4. **Website:** clear plans and pricing guidance, online booking, click-to-call, trust badges
5. **Seasonal campaigns:** mosquitoes in summer, rodents in fall, termites in swarm season
6. **Door-to-door:** effective for recurring residential plans in dense neighbourhoods
7. **Referrals:** credits for customers who refer neighbours
8. **Commercial outreach:** restaurants, property managers, warehouses, healthcare
9. **Email/SMS:** renewal reminders and seasonal add-ons

## Convert and retain

Offer plans at the first call, explain the value of prevention, use auto-pay, communicate before each visit and follow up after service.

## Track results

Cost per lead and per customer by channel, plan conversion rate, retention and lifetime value.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I market my pest control business?
Use Google Business Profile, reviews, Local Services Ads, SEO, a booking website, seasonal campaigns, door-to-door and referrals.

### How do pest control companies get customers?
Mostly from Google search and maps, ads, door-to-door sales and referrals.

### Is door-to-door effective for pest control?
Yes, especially for selling recurring residential plans in peak season.

### What should a pest control website include?
Services by pest, service areas, plan options, reviews, online booking and click-to-call.

### How do I get commercial pest control contracts?
Approach restaurants, property managers and facilities with professional proposals and compliance documentation.

### How do pest control companies keep customers?
Recurring plans, auto-pay, reliable service and proactive communication.
