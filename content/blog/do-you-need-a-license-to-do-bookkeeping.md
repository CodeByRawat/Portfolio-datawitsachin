<p class="lede">In the US, you generally <strong>don't need a professional licence to be a bookkeeper</strong>. You do need standard <strong>business registration</strong> and any <strong>local business licence</strong>. Certifications are <strong>optional</strong>. If you <strong>prepare tax returns for pay</strong>, federal rules require a <strong>PTIN</strong>, and some services (like attest work) are reserved for <strong>CPAs</strong>.</p>

## Bookkeeper vs CPA

| | Bookkeeper | CPA |
|---|---|---|
| Licence required | No (generally) | Yes, state-licensed |
| Typical work | Recording transactions, reconciling, reports | Audits, attest, tax, advisory |
| Can call themselves CPA | No | Yes |

## What you'll typically need

- **Business registration** (sole proprietorship, LLC)
- **Local business licence** where required
- **EIN** for hiring and banking
- **PTIN** if you prepare tax returns for compensation
- **Professional liability (E&O) insurance**
- **Engagement letters** defining scope

## Optional certifications

Bookkeeping certifications and software certifications build credibility and help you appear in partner directories.

## Rules to respect

Don't present yourself as a CPA, avoid services reserved for licensed accountants, and follow state rules if you offer tax services.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do you need a license to do bookkeeping?
Generally no professional licence is required, but you need normal business registration.

### Do I need a business license to do bookkeeping from home?
Many cities require a general business licence or home occupation permit.

### Do bookkeepers need to be certified?
No, certification is optional.

### Can a bookkeeper do taxes?
They can prepare returns with a PTIN, but some tax representation and attest services require credentials.

### Do bookkeepers need insurance?
Professional liability insurance is strongly recommended.

### What's the difference between a bookkeeper and an accountant?
Bookkeepers record and reconcile; accountants and CPAs analyse, audit and advise.
