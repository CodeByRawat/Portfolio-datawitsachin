<p class="lede">To start a dog boarding or daycare business: decide between <strong>home-based boarding</strong> and a <strong>dedicated facility</strong>, check <strong>licences, kennel permits and zoning</strong>, get <strong>pet care insurance</strong>, design a <strong>safe facility</strong> (secure fencing, separate areas, easy-clean surfaces, ventilation), set <strong>vaccination and temperament policies</strong>, hire and train <strong>staff with safe dog-to-staff ratios</strong>, set <strong>pricing</strong>, use <strong>online reservations with deposits</strong>, and market through <strong>Google, vets and groomers</strong>.</p>

## Steps

1. **Model:** home boarding (small numbers), daycare only, boarding + daycare facility, add grooming/training
2. **Licences:** business licence, kennel/animal facility permits, zoning approval, inspections
3. **Insurance:** liability with care-custody-control, property, workers' comp
4. **Facility:** secure double-gated entries, separate play groups by size/temperament, kennels/suites, outdoor area, cleaning stations, climate control
5. **Policies:** vaccinations, spay/neuter rules for group play, temperament evaluation, emergency vet plan
6. **Staff:** trained in dog behaviour, first aid and cleaning protocols; appropriate ratios
7. **Pricing:** daycare day rates and packages, overnight boarding, peak season premiums, add-ons
8. **Software:** reservations, deposits, vaccination record tracking, check-in/out, report cards and photos (see [How to make a booking website](/blog/how-to-make-a-booking-website/))
9. **Marketing:** Google Business Profile and reviews, Instagram daily photos, vet/groomer partnerships, first-day trials

## Dog daycare specifics

Temperament tests, playgroup management, rest periods, daily report cards and photos, and monthly packages for regulars.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a dog boarding business?
Choose a model, secure licences, permits and zoning, get insurance, prepare a safe facility, set policies, hire trained staff, set pricing and use reservation software.

### Do I need a licence for dog boarding?
Often yes: business licences plus kennel or animal facility permits and zoning approval, depending on location.

### Can I board dogs from home?
In many places, with limits on numbers and zoning approval; check local rules.

### How do I start a dog daycare business?
Follow similar steps with a focus on group play safety, temperament testing, staff ratios and daily reporting.

### How much do dog boarding businesses charge?
Rates depend on location, services and accommodation type; peak holidays usually cost more.

### Is dog daycare profitable?
It can be with consistent attendance, packages, efficient staffing and add-on services like grooming.
