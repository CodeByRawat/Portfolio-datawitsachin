<p class="lede">Buying an existing car wash can cost from <strong>tens of thousands for small self-serve operations</strong> to <strong>several million dollars for modern express tunnels with land</strong>. Prices depend on the <strong>type of wash, whether real estate is included, location and traffic, car counts, membership base, profitability and equipment condition</strong>. Valuations are often based on <strong>cash flow (EBITDA/SDE) multiples</strong> plus real estate value.</p>

## What affects the price

| Factor | Impact |
|---|---|
| Car wash type | Tunnels > in-bay automatic > self-serve |
| Real estate | Land ownership raises price |
| Location | Traffic and visibility |
| Car counts and revenue | Proven demand |
| Memberships | Recurring revenue adds value |
| Equipment | Age and condition |
| Competition | Nearby washes |

## How car washes are valued

Cash flow multiples for the business, plus property appraisal if real estate is included; recurring membership revenue and modern equipment support higher valuations.

## Due diligence checklist

Financial statements and tax returns, car counts by month, membership numbers and churn, equipment age and maintenance records, utility bills, environmental compliance, leases, permits, staff and competition.

## Financing

SBA/bank loans, seller financing, equipment loans and investors.

## How to own a car wash

Buy an existing wash, build new (see [How much does it cost to open a car wash?](/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-open-a-car-wash/)), or start mobile and grow. Run it with memberships and strong local marketing (see [How to start a car wash business](/blog/how-to-start-a-car-wash-business/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does it cost to buy a car wash?
From tens of thousands for small self-serve washes to several million for express tunnels with land.

### How are car washes valued?
Typically on cash flow multiples plus the value of any real estate.

### Is buying a car wash a good idea?
It can be, with careful due diligence on car counts, memberships, equipment and location.

### Can I finance buying a car wash?
Yes, through bank or SBA loans, seller financing and equipment loans.

### What should I check before buying a car wash?
Financials, car counts, memberships, equipment condition, environmental compliance, leases and competition.

### How do I own a car wash with no experience?
Buy a well-run wash with training from the seller, or partner with experienced operators.
