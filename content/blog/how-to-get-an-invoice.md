<p class="lede">To get an invoice, <strong>check your account or order history</strong> on the seller's website or app (most online services let you download invoices as PDFs), look in your <strong>purchase confirmation emails</strong>, or <strong>ask the seller directly</strong> with your order number, date, amount and the <strong>billing details</strong> (company name, address, tax ID) you need on it. For business expenses, request a proper <strong>tax invoice</strong> (VAT/GST invoice) where applicable.</p>

## Getting invoices from popular services (typical locations)

| Service | Where to look |
|---|---|
| **Amazon** | Your Orders › select order › Invoice (business accounts can add tax details) |
| **Apple** | reportaproblem.apple.com or purchase history in Settings/App Store; emailed receipts |
| **Google** | Google Play order history; Google Workspace/Cloud billing › Documents |
| **Microsoft** | Microsoft account › Order history; Microsoft 365 admin › Billing › Invoices |
| **Uber/ride apps** | Trip history › request or download invoice/receipt; business profiles add details |
| **Software/SaaS** | Account › Billing › Invoices/Billing history |
| **Utilities, telecoms** | Online account › Bills/Statements |
| **Freelancers/suppliers** | Ask directly |

Menus change over time; search the service's help centre for "invoice" if you can't find it.

## How to request an invoice from a company or freelancer

Include:
- **Order/booking reference**, date and amount
- **Your billing details:** legal company name, address, tax ID (VAT/GST/EIN where relevant), PO number
- **Delivery email**
- Any **format** requirements (PDF, e-invoice)

### Example request

> Subject: Invoice request – Order #A-5531 (12 Nov)
>
> Hello, could you please send an invoice for order #A-5531 placed on 12 November for $480? Please address it to Acme Clinic LLC, 45 Oak Ave, Austin TX, PO #7781, and email it to accounts@acmeclinic.com. Thank you!

## Invoice vs receipt

If you've already paid, you may receive a **receipt**; some businesses need a **tax invoice** for expense claims or tax credits. (Difference explained: [Is an invoice the same as a receipt?](/blog/invoice-vs-receipt/))

## Tips

- Add **business details to your account** before purchasing so invoices are correct
- Use a **dedicated billing email** for all invoices
- **Store invoices** in a shared folder or accounting software
- Ask for **corrections** quickly if details are wrong (sellers often issue a corrected invoice or credit note)

## For businesses: make invoices easy to get

Customers asking for invoices costs support time. Automatically email invoices on purchase, provide a self-service billing portal, and allow customers to add tax details (see [How to send an invoice](/blog/how-to-send-an-invoice/) and [How to automate business processes](/blog/how-to-automate-business-processes/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I get an invoice?
Check the seller's account order history or confirmation emails, or request one directly with your order details and billing information.

### How do I ask a company for an invoice?
Email or contact them with your order number, date, amount, the billing details you need on the invoice and where to send it.

### Can I get an invoice after paying?
Yes. Most businesses can issue or resend invoices after payment; many online accounts let you download them anytime.

### How do I get an invoice with my company name?
Add your business name and tax details to your account before purchasing, or ask the seller to issue the invoice with those details.

### What is the difference between an invoice and a receipt?
An invoice requests payment; a receipt confirms payment received. Some documents serve both purposes when payment is immediate.

### Why do I need an invoice?
For expense claims, accounting, tax deductions or credits, warranties and proof of purchase.
