<p class="lede"><strong>Leadership coaching</strong> is a structured partnership in which a coach helps a leader (from new managers to executives) improve <strong>self-awareness, communication, decision-making, influence and team performance</strong>. Through regular conversations, feedback (often <strong>360-degree assessments</strong>), goal setting and accountability, the leader changes behaviours that matter for their role. An <strong>executive coach</strong> does this with senior leaders, often linking personal development to business results.</p>

## How leadership coaching works

1. **Intake and goals:** with the leader and often their manager/HR
2. **Assessment:** 360 feedback, personality or leadership assessments, interviews
3. **Development plan:** 2–3 focus areas with measurable behaviours
4. **Regular sessions:** typically every 2–4 weeks over 3–12 months
5. **Practice and feedback** between sessions
6. **Review:** progress against goals, follow-up feedback

## Common leadership coaching goals

- Delegation and empowering teams
- Communication and executive presence
- Giving feedback and handling conflict
- Strategic thinking and prioritisation
- Leading change
- Managing former peers
- Emotional intelligence and resilience
- Stakeholder influence

## What does an executive coach do?

Acts as a confidential thinking partner for senior leaders, challenges assumptions, provides honest feedback, helps navigate politics and transitions, and aligns leadership behaviour with organisational goals.

## Benefits

For leaders: clarity, confidence, better relationships and performance. For organisations: stronger teams, retention, succession readiness and smoother change.

## Leadership coaching vs training

| | Coaching | Training |
|---|---|---|
| **Format** | Individual or small group, personalised | Group, standard curriculum |
| **Focus** | Specific behaviours and context | General knowledge and skills |
| **Duration** | Months | Hours to days |

## How to choose a leadership coach

Relevant leadership experience, coaching credentials (e.g., ICF), assessment tools, references, chemistry call, clear process and confidentiality agreements.

## Cost

Varies widely by coach experience and seniority of the client; executive coaching programmes often cost thousands per engagement, while group leadership coaching costs less per person.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is leadership coaching?
A structured partnership where a coach helps a leader improve self-awareness, communication, decision-making and team performance through feedback, goals and accountability.

### What does an executive coach do?
Works confidentially with senior leaders to challenge thinking, provide feedback and develop leadership behaviours tied to business results.

### How long does leadership coaching take?
Typically three to twelve months with sessions every two to four weeks.

### Is leadership coaching worth it?
Often yes when goals are clear and the leader is committed; benefits include better performance, retention and team results.

### What is the difference between leadership coaching and mentoring?
Coaching uses questions, feedback and accountability; mentoring shares the mentor's experience and advice.

### How much does leadership coaching cost?
It varies widely, from affordable group programmes to premium executive engagements.
