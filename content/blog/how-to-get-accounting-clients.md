<p class="lede">Accountants get the best clients by <strong>specialising</strong> (an industry or client type), building <strong>referral partnerships</strong> (lawyers, financial advisers, bankers, real estate agents), being <strong>easy to find online</strong> (website, Google Business Profile, reviews, local SEO), sharing <strong>helpful content</strong> that shows expertise, and making it <strong>easy to start</strong> with clear packages and online booking. Existing happy clients are often the best source of new ones.</p>

## 15 ways to get accounting clients

1. **Pick a niche** (e.g., dentists, contractors, e-commerce, creators)
2. **Referral partners:** attorneys, financial planners, bankers, insurance agents, real estate agents
3. **Client referral programme**
4. **Website** with services, pricing ranges, niche pages and booking (see [How to create a website for your business](/blog/how-to-create-a-website-for-your-business/))
5. **Google Business Profile** and reviews (see [How to create a Google Business page](/blog/how-to-create-a-google-business-page/))
6. **Local SEO:** "tax accountant in [city]", "[niche] accountant"
7. **Content:** tax deadlines, deductions, guides for your niche
8. **LinkedIn** thought leadership and outreach
9. **Webinars and workshops** for small business owners
10. **Tax season campaigns** to past and prospective clients (how to get more tax returns)
11. **Partnerships with software providers** (e.g., accounting app partner directories)
12. **Business networking groups** and chambers
13. **Free reviews or health checks** as a lead magnet
14. **Upsell existing clients:** bookkeeping, payroll, planning, advisory (see [How to grow an accounting practice](/blog/how-to-grow-an-accounting-practice/))
15. **Fast, professional onboarding** with portals and e-signatures

## Getting more tax clients

- Start marketing before tax season
- Clear pricing for return types
- Easy document upload and e-signature
- Reminder campaigns to last year's clients
- Referral rewards
- Year-round planning offers to convert one-time filers into monthly clients

## Convert leads into clients

Respond quickly, offer a short discovery call, send a clear proposal with packages, and automate onboarding (see [How to automate your sales process](/blog/how-to-automate-your-sales-process/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do accountants get clients?
Through referrals from clients and professional partners, niche marketing, a strong website and Google presence, content, networking and webinars.

### How can I get more accounting clients quickly?
Contact past clients and referral partners, run a tax-season or year-end campaign, and offer a free review to targeted prospects.

### How do I get tax clients?
Market early in tax season, offer clear pricing and easy online document upload, remind past clients and encourage referrals.

### Is niche marketing good for accountants?
Yes. Specialising makes marketing more targeted and lets you charge more for expertise.

### Do accountants need a website?
Yes. Prospective clients check credentials, services and reviews online before reaching out.

### How do accounting firms get new clients without cold calling?
Referral partnerships, SEO, content, webinars, LinkedIn and client referrals.
