<p class="lede">To start a spray foam insulation business, get <strong>trained and certified</strong> (manufacturer and industry training), buy or build a <strong>spray foam rig</strong> (trailer or box truck with a proportioner, generator, compressor and heated hoses), invest in <strong>PPE and safety</strong>, get <strong>licensed and insured</strong>, and win work from <strong>builders, contractors and homeowners</strong>. Startup costs are often <strong>$60,000 to $200,000+</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Get trained

Spray foam is chemistry: wrong temperatures or mix ratios cause failures and odours. Take manufacturer training and industry certification courses and learn safety, equipment and building science.

## Step 2: Open-cell vs closed-cell

| Type | Uses |
|---|---|
| Open-cell | Interior walls, attics, sound control, lower cost |
| Closed-cell | Basements, crawl spaces, metal buildings, roofs, higher R-value per inch |

## Step 3: Equipment and startup costs

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Spray foam rig (trailer or truck) | $50,000 – $150,000+ |
| Proportioner, hoses, spray gun | Often included in rig |
| Generator and air compressor | Often included in rig |
| Fresh-air respirators and PPE | $2,000 – $8,000 |
| Initial chemical sets | $3,000 – $10,000 |
| Training and certification | $1,000 – $5,000 |
| Insurance (yearly) | $5,000 – $20,000 |

Used rigs reduce costs but need careful inspection.

## Step 4: Licenses and insurance

LLC, EIN, business license, contractor license where required, general liability (spray foam insurance can be costly), workers' comp and commercial auto. See [Do you need a license to install insulation?](/blog/do-you-need-a-license-to-install-insulation/).

## Step 5: Pricing

Spray foam is priced per **board foot** (1 sq ft × 1 inch thick) or per square foot at a set thickness. Include chemicals, labour, prep, masking, cleanup, trip costs and profit.

## Step 6: Get customers

1. Home builders and general contractors
2. Metal building and pole barn builders
3. Homeowners (attics, crawl spaces, energy upgrades)
4. Commercial roofers
5. Google Business Profile, a website and energy rebate programs

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a spray foam insulation business?
Get trained, buy a spray foam rig, invest in safety gear, get licensed and insured, and market to builders.

### How much is a spray foam machine?
A complete rig often costs $50,000 to $150,000+; used rigs cost less.

### Is spray foam insulation a good business?
Yes. Margins can be strong, but equipment and insurance costs are high.

### Is spray foam dangerous to install?
It requires proper respirators, ventilation and training.

### How is spray foam priced?
Per board foot or per square foot at a set thickness.
