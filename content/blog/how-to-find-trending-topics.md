<p class="lede">To find trending topics online, combine <strong>search trend tools</strong> (Google Trends, keyword tools like Semrush and Ahrefs, Exploding Topics), <strong>platform trend signals</strong> (YouTube and TikTok trending pages, TikTok Creative Center, Instagram Reels audio, X and Reddit), and <strong>news and industry sources</strong>. Then filter trends for <strong>relevance to your audience</strong> and act quickly with content: short videos, posts or articles. A weekly routine (and simple automation) makes trend-spotting consistent.</p>

## Free and paid tools to find trending topics

| Tool | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Google Trends** | Interest over time, rising queries, trending searches by country |
| **Google autocomplete and "People also ask"** | Emerging questions |
| **YouTube** | Trending/Explore, search suggestions, competitor outliers |
| **TikTok Creative Center** | Trending hashtags, songs, creators and ads |
| **Instagram Reels** | Trending audio indicators |
| **X (Twitter)** | Trending topics and conversations |
| **Reddit** | Rising posts in niche communities |
| **Exploding Topics** | Fast-growing topics before they peak |
| **Semrush / Ahrefs** | Trending keywords, volume changes, questions (see [How to choose keywords for SEO](/blog/how-to-choose-keywords-for-seo/)) |
| **Google News, Feedly, newsletters** | Industry news |
| **AI assistants with search** | Summarising what's changing in a niche |

## How to tell if a trend is worth it

- **Relevance:** does it connect to your audience and offer?
- **Timing:** rising, peaking or fading?
- **Longevity:** fad vs lasting shift (use Google Trends' 5-year view)
- **Competition:** can you add a unique angle?
- **Brand fit and safety:** avoid sensitive or controversial trends unless appropriate

## Types of trends

| Type | Lifespan | Best content |
|---|---|---|
| **Viral/meme trends** | Days | Short-form video, social posts |
| **News trends** | Days to weeks | Commentary, explainers |
| **Seasonal trends** | Predictable yearly | Planned campaigns and SEO pages |
| **Rising topics** | Months to years | Pillar articles, guides, products |

## A weekly trend-spotting workflow (30–45 minutes)

1. **Monday:** check Google Trends rising queries for your niche terms
2. Scan **TikTok Creative Center** and YouTube outliers in your category
3. Check **Reddit and X** for new questions and complaints
4. Review **keyword tool** trending/new questions
5. Shortlist **3–5 ideas** with a reason each matters to your audience
6. Produce **one quick-response piece** (short video or post) and plan **one evergreen piece** if the trend has longevity
7. Log results to learn which trends work

## Turning trends into content

- **Short-form video:** quick takes, tutorials, reactions (see [How to make great YouTube videos](/blog/how-to-make-great-youtube-videos/))
- **Blog posts:** explainers for rising questions
- **Newsletters:** "what changed this week" (see [How to write a newsletter](/blog/how-to-write-a-newsletter/))
- **Ads:** trend-inspired hooks (carefully, brand-safe)
- **Products/services:** new offers for emerging needs

## Automating trend monitoring

- **Google Alerts** for keywords and competitors
- **RSS feeds** into a reader or Slack
- **Automation workflows** (n8n, Make, Zapier) that pull Google Trends/Reddit/RSS data into a sheet and send a weekly digest
- **AI summaries** of collected items for quick review

## What are the top Google searches?

Google publishes **Year in Search** and real-time **Trending Now** in Google Trends. Top searches are dominated by news, sports, entertainment and everyday utilities (weather, translate), which is why businesses should focus on trends **within their niche**.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I find trending topics online?
Use Google Trends, YouTube and TikTok trend tools, X and Reddit, Exploding Topics and keyword tools, then filter for relevance to your audience.

### How do I find trending keywords?
Check Google Trends' rising queries, keyword tools' trending or new keywords, and autocomplete suggestions for your niche terms.

### What is the best free tool to find trends?
Google Trends is the most popular free tool, complemented by TikTok Creative Center and YouTube search suggestions.

### How do I know if a trend will last?
Look at long-term Google Trends data, whether multiple platforms show growth, and whether it reflects a real change in needs rather than a meme.

### Should businesses follow social media trends?
Selectively. Trends that fit your brand and audience can boost reach, but forced or off-brand trend content can hurt credibility.

### Can I automate trend research?
Yes. Alerts, RSS feeds and automation tools can collect trend signals into a weekly digest, with AI summarising them for review.
