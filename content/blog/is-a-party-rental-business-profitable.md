<p class="lede">Yes, party rental can be very profitable because items are <strong>rented many times</strong> and often <strong>pay for themselves in a season</strong>. Small home-based rental businesses often make <strong>$20,000 to $100,000</strong> a year, and established companies with tents, inflatables and event services can bring in <strong>$250,000 to $1 million+</strong>. Net margins are often <strong>20% to 40%</strong>.</p>

These are rough ranges; local demand and pricing matter.

## Payback examples

| Item | Cost | Rental price | Rentals to pay back |
|---|---|---|---|
| Folding table | $60 | $10 | ~6 |
| Folding chair | $20 | $2 | ~10 |
| 20×20 tent | $2,000 | $300 | ~7 |
| Bounce house | $2,500 | $200 | ~13 |

After payback, most rental income is profit, minus delivery and upkeep.

## How much do party rental businesses make?

| Business | Rough yearly revenue |
|---|---|
| Side business, weekends | $10,000 – $50,000 |
| Full-time home-based | $50,000 – $150,000 |
| Established company | $250,000 – $1 million+ |

## Most profitable rentals

- Tents (high price per rental)
- Inflatables and water slides
- Packages (tables + chairs + linens + tent)
- Dance floors and lighting for weddings
- Concession machines (cheap to buy, rented often)

## Costs and risks

Delivery labour and fuel, cleaning and repairs, storage, insurance, damaged or lost items, weather cancellations and seasonality.

## How to increase profit

- Sell packages and add-ons
- Charge delivery and setup fees
- Require deposits and damage waivers
- Maximise weekend bookings with online booking
- Work with planners and venues for repeat bookings

How much to start: often $5,000 to $25,000 small. Full guide: [How to start a party rental business](/blog/how-to-start-a-party-rental-business/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a party rental business profitable?
Yes. Items pay for themselves quickly and are rented many times.

### How much does a party rental business make?
From $20,000 a year part-time to $1 million+ for established companies.

### How much does it cost to start a party rental business?
Often $5,000 to $25,000 to start small.

### What party rental items make the most money?
Tents, inflatables and event packages.

### Is party rental seasonal?
Yes. Spring through autumn is busiest in most areas.
