<p class="lede">To grow a salon or barber shop: <strong>make it easy to find and book you</strong> (Google Business Profile, Instagram, online booking), <strong>show your work</strong> with before-and-after photos and Reels, <strong>collect reviews</strong> after every visit, <strong>rebook clients at checkout</strong>, run a <strong>referral programme</strong>, and use <strong>automated reminders and win-back messages</strong>. Growing clientele is as much about <strong>keeping clients</strong> as attracting new ones.</p>

## Attract new clients

1. **Instagram and TikTok:** before/afters, transformation Reels, stylist personalities
2. **Google Business Profile:** photos, services, prices and a booking link (see [How to create a Google Business page](/blog/how-to-create-a-google-business-page/))
3. **Reviews:** automated review requests after each visit
4. **Online booking** on your website and social profiles (see [How to make a booking website](/blog/how-to-make-a-booking-website/))
5. **Referral programme:** discounts for referrer and friend
6. **Local partnerships:** bridal shops, photographers, gyms, boutiques
7. **Local ads** for new client offers (see [How to advertise your business on Facebook](/blog/how-to-advertise-your-business-on-facebook/))
8. **Events:** bridal trials, workshops, charity cut days

## Increase clientele through retention

- **Rebook before they leave** (the most powerful habit)
- **Reminders** by SMS/WhatsApp (see [How to schedule a WhatsApp message](/blog/how-to-schedule-a-whatsapp-message/))
- **Loyalty points and memberships**
- **Personal notes:** preferences, formulas, birthdays
- **Win-back messages** for clients who haven't returned in 8–12 weeks
- **Consistent, excellent service**

## How to promote a barber shop

- Fresh-cut Reels and TikToks with music
- Barber personality and shop culture content
- Google reviews and walk-in wait times
- Partnerships with schools, sports teams and gyms
- Membership plans for regular cuts
- Online booking plus walk-in options

## Increase revenue per client

Add-on services, treatments, product recommendations, packages and premium time slots.

## Track growth

New clients per month, rebooking rate, retention rate, average ticket, chair utilisation, reviews and no-show rate. (Income: [How much do salon owners make?](/blog/how-much-do-salon-owners-make/))

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I grow my salon business?
Optimise Google and Instagram, show before/afters, offer online booking, collect reviews, rebook at checkout, run referrals and automate reminders and win-backs.

### How do I increase salon clientele?
Combine new client attraction (social media, Google, referrals, partnerships) with retention (rebooking, loyalty, follow-ups).

### How do you promote a barber shop?
Post fresh-cut videos, collect Google reviews, offer online booking and memberships, and partner with local schools, teams and gyms.

### What is the best marketing for a salon?
Instagram content plus Google Business Profile reviews and easy online booking usually deliver the best results.

### How do salons reduce no-shows?
Automated reminders, deposits for long appointments and clear cancellation policies.

### How often should a salon post on Instagram?
Several times a week, mainly Reels and before/after posts, plus daily Stories if possible.
