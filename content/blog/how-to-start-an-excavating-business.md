<p class="lede">To start an excavating business, get <strong>experience as an equipment operator</strong>, choose your services (<strong>site prep, foundations, utilities, septic, grading or land clearing</strong>), get <strong>licensed and well insured</strong>, start with a <strong>compact or mid-size excavator and skid steer</strong> (buy used or rent), and win work from <strong>builders, contractors and landowners</strong>. Startup costs often range from <strong>$50,000 to $250,000+</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Get experience

Operate equipment for an excavation company. Learn grading, trenching, reading plans, soil types and safety. Experience also helps with insurance.

## Step 2: Choose your services

| Service | Notes |
|---|---|
| Residential site prep and foundations | Steady builder work |
| Utilities (water, sewer, electric) | Skilled, well paid |
| Septic systems | Often needs a separate license |
| Grading and drainage | Common residential need |
| Land clearing | Rural and development work |
| Driveways and ponds | Good for small operators |
| Demolition and hauling | Add-on services |

## Step 3: Licenses and insurance

LLC, EIN, contractor license (many states require one), septic installer license if needed, DOT number and CDL for heavy trucks, general liability, workers' comp, commercial auto and inland marine (equipment) insurance.

## Step 4: Equipment and startup costs

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Used mini or mid-size excavator | $30,000 – $120,000 |
| Skid steer or compact track loader | $25,000 – $70,000 |
| Truck and equipment trailer | $30,000 – $80,000 |
| Dump truck (or hire trucking) | $40,000 – $120,000 |
| Attachments, lasers, compactor | $5,000 – $20,000 |
| Insurance and licenses | $5,000 – $20,000 per year |

Many start with one excavator, a skid steer and a trailer, and hire trucking.

## Step 5: Pricing

Price by the hour for small jobs, or by the job for larger work. Make sure rates cover machine costs, operator pay, fuel, mobilisation and profit. See [How to bid excavation work](/blog/how-to-bid-excavation-work/).

## Step 6: Get your first jobs

1. Builders and general contractors
2. Concrete, septic and landscaping companies
3. Landowners and farmers
4. Google Business Profile with job photos
5. A website with services and an estimate form

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start an excavating business?
Get operator experience, choose services, get licensed and insured, buy or rent equipment and market to builders and contractors.

### How much does it cost to start an excavating business?
Often $50,000 to $250,000+, depending on equipment.

### Should I buy or rent excavators?
Buy what you use every week; rent specialised equipment.

### Do I need a license for excavation?
Many states require a contractor license; septic and utility work often need more.

### Can a small excavation business be profitable?
Yes. Owner-operators with one or two machines can earn well.
