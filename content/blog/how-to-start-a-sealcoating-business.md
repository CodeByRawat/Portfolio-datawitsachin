<p class="lede">To start a sealcoating business, buy <strong>sealcoating equipment</strong> (a skid or trailer spray rig, or start with squeegees and a small tank), add <strong>crack-filling tools</strong>, get <strong>insured and licensed where required</strong>, open a <strong>sealer supplier account</strong>, and market to <strong>homeowners with asphalt driveways and commercial parking lots</strong>. You can start small for <strong>$5,000 to $15,000</strong>, or <strong>$20,000 to $60,000</strong> with a full spray rig and truck.</p>

## What you need to start

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Sealcoat skid or trailer unit (spray or squeegee) | $3,000 – $25,000 |
| Crack filling machine or pour pots | $500 – $15,000 |
| Blower, edger, wire brushes, squeegees | $500 – $2,000 |
| Truck (or use your own) | Varies |
| Line striping machine (for parking lots) | $1,500 – $8,000 |
| Sealer, sand and additives | $1,000 – $5,000 |
| Insurance and licenses | $1,500 – $5,000 per year |

Many start with driveways using a small skid unit and add striping and parking lots later.

## Step 1: Learn the process

1. Clean (blow, edge, remove oil spots)
2. Fill cracks
3. Prime oil stains
4. Apply sealer in thin, even coats (often two)
5. Block off until cured

Supplier training and working with an experienced sealcoater help a lot.

## Step 2: Register and insure

LLC, EIN, business license, general liability and commercial auto. Some areas need a contractor license for paving-related work.

## Step 3: Pricing

Residential driveways are often priced per square foot or per driveway; commercial lots per square foot, plus crack filling per linear foot and striping per stall or per line. See [How much does sealcoating cost?](/blog/how-much-does-sealcoating-cost/).

## Step 4: Seasonality

Sealcoating needs warm, dry weather. Many sealcoaters work spring to fall and do snow removal in winter. Related: [How to start a snow removal business](/blog/how-to-start-a-snow-removal-business/).

## Step 5: Get customers

1. Door hangers in neighbourhoods with asphalt driveways
2. Google Business Profile with before-and-after photos
3. A website with an estimate form
4. Property managers, HOAs, churches and businesses
5. Paving companies (referrals)

## Frequently asked questions

### What do I need to start a sealcoating business?
A sealcoating unit, crack-filling tools, cleaning tools, sealer supply, insurance and a truck.

### How much does it cost to start a sealcoating business?
From about $5,000 small to $60,000 with a full rig and truck.

### Is sealcoating a good business?
Yes. Startup is low and margins are good, but it's seasonal.

### Do you need a license to sealcoat?
Usually a business license; some areas need a contractor license.

### When is sealcoating season?
Spring through fall, in dry weather above about 50°F.
