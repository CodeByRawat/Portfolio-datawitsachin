<p class="lede">To start a concrete business, get <strong>hands-on experience with a concrete crew</strong>, pick a <strong>niche</strong> (flatwork, decorative, foundations or repair), get your <strong>contractor license, insurance and bond</strong>, buy or rent <strong>equipment</strong>, and win jobs from <strong>homeowners, builders and general contractors</strong>. Startup costs often run <strong>$20,000 to $100,000+</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Get experience

Concrete is unforgiving: mistakes are permanent. Work on a crew to learn forming, rebar and mesh, pouring, finishing, curing and cutting control joints.

## Step 2: Choose your niche

| Niche | Examples |
|---|---|
| Residential flatwork | Driveways, patios, sidewalks |
| Decorative | Stamped, stained, exposed aggregate |
| Foundations and footings | New homes, additions |
| Commercial | Parking lots, slabs, curbs |
| Repair and resurfacing | Lifting, patching, overlays |

## Step 3: Licenses, insurance and bonding

- LLC and EIN
- State or local contractor license (many states require one above a job-size limit)
- General liability and workers' comp
- Commercial auto
- A contractor bond (often required for licensing or permits)

## Step 4: Equipment and startup costs

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Truck and trailer | $20,000 – $60,000 |
| Forms, stakes, rebar tools | $2,000 – $5,000 |
| Finishing tools (bull float, trowels, edgers) | $1,000 – $3,000 |
| Power trowel, plate compactor | $2,000 – $8,000 (or rent) |
| Concrete saw | $1,000 – $3,000 |
| Skid steer | Rent at first |
| Insurance, licenses, bond | $3,000 – $10,000 per year |

## Step 5: Price your jobs

Price per square foot, including excavation and grading, base, forms, reinforcement, concrete delivery, finishing, sealing and cleanup. Details: [How to bid concrete jobs](/blog/how-to-bid-concrete-jobs/).

## Step 6: Build a crew

Concrete needs several people on pour days. Start with one or two reliable finishers and add labourers.

## Step 7: Get clients

1. Google Business Profile with photos of finished work
2. A website with an estimate request form
3. Builders and general contractors
4. Landscapers and pool companies
5. Reviews and yard signs on finished jobs

Is it worth it? [Is concrete a good business?](/blog/is-concrete-a-good-business/). Career path: [How to become a concrete contractor](/blog/how-to-become-a-concrete-contractor/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a concrete business?
Get experience, choose a niche, get licensed and insured, buy equipment and market to homeowners and builders.

### How much does it cost to start a concrete company?
Often $20,000 to $100,000+, depending on equipment.

### Do I need a license to do concrete work?
Many states require a contractor license above a certain job size; check locally.

### What do you need to start a concrete business?
Experience, a truck and trailer, tools, a license, insurance and a crew.

### Is concrete work profitable?
Yes. Decorative and commercial work have especially good margins.
