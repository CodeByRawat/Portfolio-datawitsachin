<p class="lede">To open a flower shop, write a <strong>business plan</strong>, choose a <strong>visible location</strong> near your customers, design a shop with <strong>coolers, work areas and displays</strong>, register and get <strong>licences</strong>, open <strong>wholesale accounts</strong>, hire <strong>designers and delivery drivers</strong>, and market for <strong>everyday, event and holiday orders</strong>. Opening a retail flower shop often costs <strong>$25,000 to $100,000+</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Business plan

Define your customers, competitors, services (walk-in, delivery, weddings, sympathy, corporate), pricing, and how many orders you need to cover rent.

## Step 2: Location

Look for good visibility, parking, nearby offices, hospitals, funeral homes and residential areas. Delivery reach matters too.

## Step 3: Shop design and equipment

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Walk-in or display cooler | $5,000 – $20,000 |
| Work tables and shelving | $1,000 – $5,000 |
| Displays and décor | $2,000 – $10,000 |
| POS and order system | $500 – $3,000 |
| Delivery vehicle | $10,000 – $30,000 (or use own) |

## Step 4: Startup costs

Lease deposit and build-out, equipment, first inventory, licences, insurance, branding, website and marketing: often $25,000 to $100,000+.

## Step 5: Licences

Business licence, sales tax permit, resale certificate, signage permit, and insurance.

## Step 6: Inventory and suppliers

Order from wholesalers several times a week, rotate stock, and plan for big holidays well ahead.

## Step 7: Staffing

Floral designers, a shop manager or front-of-house staff, and delivery drivers, plus extra holiday help.

## Step 8: Marketing

Google Business Profile and reviews, a website with online ordering, social media, local business accounts, wedding marketing and holiday promotions.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I open a flower shop?
Plan, choose a location, set up coolers and displays, get licences, line up suppliers and staff, and market.

### How much does it cost to open a flower shop?
Often $25,000 to $100,000+.

### How do I own a flower shop without experience?
Hire an experienced designer or take courses before opening.

### Is owning a flower shop profitable?
It can be, especially with weddings, events and corporate accounts.

### What's the busiest time for flower shops?
Valentine's Day, Mother's Day and the winter holidays.
