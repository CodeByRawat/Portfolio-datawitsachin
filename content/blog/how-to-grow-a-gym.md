<p class="lede">To grow a gym, combine <strong>visibility</strong> (Instagram/TikTok content, Google Business Profile and reviews, local ads), <strong>conversion</strong> (easy free trials, fast follow-up, structured intro sessions) and <strong>retention</strong> (great onboarding, coaching check-ins, community and challenges). For the gym's Instagram account, post <strong>short coach-led videos, member transformations and community moments</strong> consistently, use <strong>location tags and collaborations</strong>, and give every post a reason to <strong>book a trial</strong>.</p>

## Growing your gym's Instagram (and TikTok) account

### Content ideas
- **Coach tips:** 15–30 second form fixes and exercise demos
- **Member spotlights and transformations** (with consent)
- **Class highlights** with energetic music
- **Behind the scenes:** opening routine, new equipment
- **Challenges and events**
- **Myth busting and nutrition tips**
- **Day-in-the-life** of coaches
- **Trends** adapted to your gym (see [How to find trending topics](/blog/how-to-find-trending-topics/))

### Growth tactics
- Post **4–5 Reels per week** and daily Stories
- **Hook in the first second;** captions on screen
- **Location tags** and local hashtags
- **Collab posts** with members, local businesses and creators
- **Reply to every comment and DM** quickly
- **Clear CTA:** "DM 'TRIAL' for a free class"
(Filming tips: [How to make a promotional video](/blog/how-to-make-a-promotional-video/).)

## Getting more members

1. **Google Business Profile** with photos and reviews (see [How to promote your business locally](/blog/how-to-promote-your-business-locally/))
2. **Referral programme:** rewards for members who bring friends
3. **Free trials or intro offers** with a simple booking page (see [How to make a booking website](/blog/how-to-make-a-booking-website/))
4. **6-week challenges** with before/after goals
5. **Local ads** on Meta targeting a few kilometres (see [How to advertise your business on Facebook](/blog/how-to-advertise-your-business-on-facebook/))
6. **Corporate partnerships** with nearby offices
7. **Partnerships** with physios, nutritionists, sports clubs
8. **Community events:** charity workouts, bring-a-friend days

## Converting leads into members

- **Respond within minutes** to enquiries
- **Structured intro session:** goals, tour, workout, membership recommendation
- **Follow up** after trials with personal messages
- **Automated reminders** and WhatsApp follow-ups (see [How to schedule a WhatsApp message](/blog/how-to-schedule-a-whatsapp-message/))

## Retention: the real growth engine

- **Onboarding plan** for the first 30–90 days
- **Coach check-ins** and progress tracking
- **Community:** groups, events, recognition
- **Notice drop-offs:** contact members who haven't visited in 10–14 days
- **Variety and goals:** programmes and challenges

Reducing churn often grows a gym faster than adding new leads. (Profitability: [Are gyms profitable?](/blog/are-gyms-profitable/))

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I grow my gym's Instagram account?
Post short coach-led Reels, member stories and community moments consistently, use location tags and collaborations, reply to comments and DMs, and include clear calls to action.

### How do I get more gym members?
Optimise Google Business Profile and reviews, run referral programmes, offer free trials and challenges, use local ads, partner with businesses and follow up quickly.

### What should a gym post on social media?
Exercise tips, member transformations, class highlights, events, coach stories and behind-the-scenes content.

### How do gyms reduce member churn?
Strong onboarding, regular coach check-ins, community events, progress tracking and reaching out to members who stop attending.

### Do gym ads on Facebook and Instagram work?
They can, especially with authentic video, a clear offer like a free trial and fast follow-up on leads.

### How fast can a gym grow?
It depends on market, offer and systems; consistent marketing plus strong retention compounds growth month by month.
