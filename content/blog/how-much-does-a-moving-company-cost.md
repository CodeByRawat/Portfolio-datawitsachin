<p class="lede">Moving company costs depend mainly on <strong>distance, home size (or weight), number of movers and hours, packing services, and the time of year</strong>. <strong>Local moves</strong> are usually charged <strong>by the hour</strong> for a crew and truck (often several hundred to a couple of thousand dollars for apartments and small homes), while <strong>long-distance moves</strong> are priced by <strong>weight/volume and distance</strong> and commonly cost <strong>several thousand dollars or more</strong>. Always get written estimates.</p>

## How movers price moves

| Move type | Pricing basis |
|---|---|
| Local | Hourly rate × crew size × hours, plus travel/truck fees |
| Long-distance | Weight or cubic feet + distance, plus services |
| Flat-rate | Fixed price based on inventory |
| Labour-only | Movers load/unload your rental truck |

## What affects moving costs

- Distance and home size
- Amount of belongings (inventory)
- Packing and unpacking services
- Stairs, elevators, long carries
- Specialty items (pianos, safes, art)
- Storage needs
- Season and day (summer and weekends cost more)
- Insurance/valuation coverage

## Common extra fees

Packing materials, stair or long-carry fees, shuttle trucks, storage, fuel surcharges, and disassembly/reassembly.

## Do moving companies pack for you?

Yes, many offer full or partial packing services for an extra fee (see [Do moving companies pack for you?](/blog/do-moving-companies-pack-for-you/)).

## How to get accurate quotes

Get 3+ written estimates, ideally with an in-home or video survey; ask whether estimates are binding; check licences (e.g., USDOT for interstate moves in the US), insurance and reviews.

## Tips to save

Declutter, move off-peak, pack yourself, get flexible dates, compare quotes, and consider labour-only help.

## For moving company owners

Clear, instant online quotes and easy booking win more customers (see [How to start a moving company](/blog/how-to-start-a-moving-company/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a moving company cost?
It depends on distance, home size, services and timing; local moves are hourly and long-distance moves are priced by weight and distance.

### How much does it cost to hire movers for a local move?
Typically an hourly rate for the crew and truck multiplied by hours, often totalling hundreds to a couple of thousand dollars.

### How much do long-distance movers charge?
Usually several thousand dollars or more, based on weight/volume, distance and services.

### Do movers charge by the hour?
Local movers usually do; long-distance movers usually price by weight or volume and distance.

### Should I tip movers?
Tipping is customary in many places for good service; amounts vary.

### How can I lower moving costs?
Declutter, move off-peak, pack yourself, compare written quotes and be flexible with dates.
