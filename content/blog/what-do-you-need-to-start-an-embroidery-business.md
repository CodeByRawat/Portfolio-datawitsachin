<p class="lede">To start an embroidery business you need an <strong>embroidery machine</strong> (ideally multi-needle), <strong>hoops and a cap frame</strong>, <strong>digitizing software</strong> or a digitizing service, <strong>stabilisers, threads and needles</strong>, <strong>blank garments</strong>, a <strong>workspace</strong>, and the basics of a business: <strong>registration, pricing and a way to sell</strong>.</p>

## Equipment checklist

| Item | Rough cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Embroidery machine | $5,000 – $20,000 | Multi-needle for business |
| Hoops (various sizes) | $100 – $500 | Often some included |
| Cap frame and driver | $200 – $800 | For hats |
| Magnetic hoops | $50 – $150 each | Faster hooping |
| Digitizing software | $0 – $3,000 | Or outsource designs |
| Threads (starter set) | $200 – $500 | Polyester is durable |
| Stabilisers (backing, topping) | $100 – $300 | Cutaway, tearaway, water-soluble |
| Needles, bobbins, scissors | $100 – $200 | |
| Hooping station | $100 – $500 | Speeds production |
| Heat press | $300 – $1,500 | Patches and vinyl add-ons |
| Blank garments | Varies | Buy from wholesale suppliers |

## Workspace

A spare room or garage with good lighting, a sturdy table, power and storage for blanks and threads.

## Business setup

- Business name and registration
- Sales tax permit (for selling goods)
- Home occupation permit if needed
- Liability insurance
- Accounting and invoicing tools

## Selling setup

- A website with product pages or quote forms
- Etsy or marketplace listings
- Social media
- Price list for businesses and teams

## Skills to learn

Hooping and stabiliser choice, thread tension, digitizing basics, and fixing thread breaks and puckering.

## Frequently asked questions

### What do I need to start an embroidery business?
A machine, hoops, digitizing, stabilisers, threads, blanks, a workspace and business registration.

### Do I need digitizing software?
Not at first; you can outsource digitizing, but learning it saves money long term.

### What's the most important purchase?
A reliable multi-needle embroidery machine.

### Where do I buy blank shirts and hats?
From wholesale apparel suppliers.

### Do I need a heat press?
It's optional but useful for patches and mixed products.
