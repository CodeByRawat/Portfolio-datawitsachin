<p class="lede">To market a construction business, show <strong>proof of quality and reliability</strong>: a <strong>website with a detailed project portfolio</strong>, <strong>case studies and testimonials</strong>, an optimised <strong>Google Business Profile</strong>, and <strong>video/drone footage</strong> of completed work. Build <strong>relationships with architects, developers, property managers and (for restoration) insurance adjusters</strong>, participate in <strong>bidding platforms and industry associations</strong>, and follow up every lead and bid consistently.</p>

## Positioning

Residential, commercial, industrial or public sector? Specialities (custom homes, tenant improvements, design-build, restoration)? Geography and project sizes? Clear positioning makes every channel more effective.

## Online marketing

1. **Website:** services, project galleries with details, team, safety record, certifications, testimonials, quote/bid request form (see [How to create a website for your business](/blog/how-to-create-a-website-for-your-business/))
2. **Google Business Profile** with project photos and reviews
3. **Local SEO** for services and locations (see [What is SEO?](/blog/what-is-seo/))
4. **Case studies:** challenge, solution, timeline, budget, results
5. **Video and drone:** progress timelapses and finished walkthroughs (see [How to make a promotional video](/blog/how-to-make-a-promotional-video/))
6. **LinkedIn** for commercial relationships

## Relationship marketing

- Architects, engineers and designers
- Developers and property managers
- Real estate agents and investors
- Insurance agents and adjusters (restoration)
- Suppliers and subcontractors
- Industry associations and builders' exchanges

## How to market a restoration company

Water, fire and mould restoration depends on speed and trust: 24/7 phone answering, Google Business Profile and emergency search ads, relationships with insurance agents, plumbers and property managers, and clear explanations of the insurance claims process (see [Insurance claims automation](/blog/insurance-claims-automation/)).

## Marketing to contractors and general contractors

If you sell to contractors (as a supplier or subcontractor), focus on reliability, pricing clarity, fast quotes, trade shows, associations and direct relationship building (see [How to start a contracting business](/blog/how-to-start-a-contracting-business/)).

## Bids and follow-up

Track bids in a CRM, follow up promptly, analyse win/loss reasons, and keep in touch with past clients for repeat work (see [How to use a CRM](/blog/how-to-use-a-crm/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I market my construction business?
Build a portfolio website, optimise Google Business Profile, publish case studies and video, network with architects, developers and property managers, and track bids and follow-ups.

### How do construction companies get clients?
Through referrals, relationships with architects and developers, bidding platforms, Google search and reputation from completed projects.

### How do you market a restoration company?
Offer 24/7 response, optimise for emergency searches and Google Maps, build relationships with insurers and property managers, and explain the claims process clearly.

### Is social media useful for construction companies?
Yes, especially project videos, before/after photos and team stories on Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and LinkedIn.

### What should a construction website include?
Services, a detailed project portfolio, testimonials, certifications and safety information, service areas and a quote request form.

### How do I market to general contractors?
Build relationships directly, join associations, attend trade events and provide fast, reliable quotes and service.
