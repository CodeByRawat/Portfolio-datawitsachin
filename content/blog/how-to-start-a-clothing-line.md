<p class="lede">To start a clothing line: choose a <strong>niche and target customer</strong>, build a <strong>brand identity</strong>, design a <strong>small first collection</strong>, create <strong>tech packs</strong>, choose a <strong>production model</strong> (print-on-demand, blank apparel + decoration, private label or cut-and-sew manufacturing), order <strong>samples</strong>, set <strong>prices</strong> that cover costs and margin, launch an <strong>online store</strong>, and market through <strong>social media, creators and pre-orders</strong>.</p>

## Step 1: Niche and customer

Streetwear, athleisure, sustainable basics, plus-size, modest fashion, kids, workwear, faith-based, sports fan apparel. Know who you're designing for and why they'd choose you.

## Step 2: Brand identity

Name, logo, story, values, visual style and tone. Check trademark availability.

## Step 3: Design the first collection

Start small (3–8 pieces). Sketch designs, choose colours and fabrics, and test ideas with your audience.

## Step 4: Tech packs

Specifications for manufacturers: flat sketches, measurements, materials, trims, stitching, labels and graded sizes.

## Step 5: Production model

| Model | Upfront cost | Control | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Print-on-demand | Very low | Low | Testing designs (see [How to start a t-shirt business](/blog/how-to-start-a-t-shirt-business/)) |
| Blank apparel + screen print/embroidery | Low–medium | Medium | Graphic-led brands |
| Private label | Medium | Medium | Customising existing styles |
| Cut-and-sew manufacturer | High (MOQs) | High | Original garments |

## Step 6: Sampling

Order samples, check fit and quality, revise, and approve before bulk production.

## Step 7: Pricing and costs

Cost per unit (materials, labour, trims, packaging, shipping, duties) + overhead + margin. Common retail pricing aims for strong margins to support marketing and wholesale.

## Step 8: Launch online

Shopify or similar store, great product photos, size guides, returns policy (see [How to create an online store](/blog/how-to-create-an-online-store/)).

## Step 9: Marketing

Instagram/TikTok content, creator seeding, pre-orders, launch drops, email list, pop-ups (see [How to increase e-commerce sales](/blog/how-to-increase-ecommerce-sales/)).

## Step 10: Grow

Reorder best-sellers, add seasonal drops, wholesale to boutiques, and scale production.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a clothing line?
Choose a niche, build a brand, design a small collection, create tech packs, pick a production model, sample, price, launch an online store and market on social media.

### How much does it cost to start a clothing line?
From very little with print-on-demand to thousands or more for custom manufacturing with minimum order quantities.

### Do I need to know how to sew to start a clothing line?
No; designers often work with pattern makers and manufacturers using tech packs.

### How do I find a clothing manufacturer?
Directories, trade shows, referrals, sourcing platforms and local production houses; always sample first.

### What is a tech pack?
A detailed specification document that tells manufacturers exactly how to produce a garment.

### How many pieces should my first collection have?
Usually a small capsule of 3–8 pieces to test demand and manage costs.
