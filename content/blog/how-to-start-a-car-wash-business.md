<p class="lede">To start a car wash business: choose a <strong>type</strong> (self-serve bays, in-bay automatic, express exterior tunnel, full-service, or mobile detailing), research <strong>traffic and competition</strong>, secure a <strong>high-visibility location</strong> with proper zoning, decide to <strong>build or buy</strong>, obtain <strong>permits</strong> (including water discharge/environmental rules), buy <strong>equipment</strong>, arrange <strong>financing</strong>, set up <strong>POS and an unlimited membership programme</strong>, and market to drivers locally.</p>

## Types of car washes

| Type | Startup | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile detailing | Low | Van/trailer with water and equipment; premium service |
| Self-serve bays | Medium | Low labour, coin/card operated |
| In-bay automatic | Medium–high | Often paired with gas stations |
| Express exterior tunnel | High | High volume, membership-driven |
| Full-service | High | Interior cleaning, more staff |

## Location factors

Traffic counts, visibility, easy entry/exit, nearby residential density, competition, zoning and utility access (water, sewer, power).

## Build or buy

Buying an existing car wash gives immediate revenue (see [How much does it cost to buy a car wash?](/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-buy-a-car-wash/)); building lets you choose modern equipment and layout (see [How much does it cost to open a car wash?](/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-open-a-car-wash/)).

## Permits and regulations

Business licence, zoning/building permits, environmental and water discharge permits, water reclamation requirements in some areas, signage permits.

## Equipment

Wash systems, dryers, water reclaim systems, vacuums, pay stations, licence plate recognition for memberships, chemicals.

## Memberships

Unlimited monthly wash clubs create recurring revenue; use licence plate recognition and an app or website for sign-ups and billing.

## Marketing

Google Business Profile and reviews, grand opening offers, membership promotions, fleet accounts, local partnerships, and a website with pricing and membership sign-up.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a car wash business?
Choose a car wash type, research location and competition, build or buy, get permits, install equipment, secure financing, set up POS and memberships, and market locally.

### What is the cheapest car wash business to start?
Mobile detailing usually has the lowest startup costs.

### Do I need permits to open a car wash?
Yes, typically zoning, building, business and environmental/water discharge permits.

### What are car wash memberships?
Monthly subscriptions for unlimited washes, providing recurring revenue.

### How long does it take to open a car wash?
Building can take many months to over a year due to permits and construction; buying is faster.

### Is a car wash a good business?
It can be with a strong location, efficient equipment and recurring memberships.
