<p class="lede">To bid snow plowing jobs, <strong>measure the area</strong>, estimate <strong>how long it takes to plow, shovel and salt</strong>, multiply by your <strong>hourly equipment rate</strong>, then choose a pricing model: <strong>per push, seasonal or per inch</strong>. Always quote salting and sidewalks separately and set a clear trigger depth.</p>

## Step 1: Visit and measure the site

Note the square footage, obstacles (islands, curbs, light posts), where snow can be piled, sidewalks and steps, and when the property must be clear.

## Step 2: Estimate time

| Equipment | Rough plowing speed |
|---|---|
| Pickup with 8-ft plow | about 20,000–30,000 sq ft per hour |
| Skid steer with pusher | faster on big open lots |
| Snow blower | sidewalks and walkways |
| Shovel | steps and tight spots |

Speeds change with snow depth and lot layout, so time your first few jobs.

## Step 3: Set your hourly rates

Include truck payment, fuel, insurance, labour, wear on the plow and profit. Check what local companies charge.

## Step 4: Choose a pricing model

| Model | Example | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| Per push | Fixed price each visit | Driveways, small lots |
| Seasonal | One price for the winter, paid monthly | Steady income for you, fixed cost for client |
| Per inch / depth tiers | 2–4", 4–8", 8–12" pricing | Commercial lots |
| Hourly | Per machine per hour | Very large lots, heavy storms |

Seasonal contracts often include a cap on visits or an extra charge after a set number of storms.

## Step 5: Price add-ons

- Salting or ice melt per application
- Sidewalk and step shoveling
- Snow hauling or relocation
- Return visits after drifting

## Step 6: Write a clear contract

Include trigger depth, service hours, priority, what's included, salting terms, payment terms and damage and liability terms.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you bid a snow plowing job?
Measure the area, estimate time, apply your hourly rate and choose per push, seasonal or per inch pricing.

### How do I bid seasonal snow removal?
Estimate average storms per winter times your per-push price, then adjust for risk and add salting separately.

### Should I charge per push or seasonal?
Seasonal gives steady income; per push pays more in heavy winters. Many companies offer both.

### What is a trigger depth?
The snow depth (often 1–2 inches) at which you start plowing.

### Is salting included in snow plowing?
Usually not. Charge it per application.
