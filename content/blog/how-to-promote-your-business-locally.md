<p class="lede">To promote your business locally, make sure people nearby can <strong>find you</strong> (Google Business Profile, local SEO, a fast website), <strong>trust you</strong> (reviews, photos, community presence) and <strong>contact you easily</strong> (click-to-call, WhatsApp, online booking). Then add <strong>local ads</strong> on Google and Meta, <strong>partnerships and referrals</strong>, <strong>community events</strong> and <strong>follow-up systems</strong> that turn first-time customers into regulars. The cheapest growth usually comes from reviews, referrals and repeat customers.</p>

## Get found online

1. **Google Business Profile:** complete, verified, with photos, services and posts (see [How to create a Google Business page](/blog/how-to-create-a-google-business-page/))
2. **A fast, mobile-first website** with service and location pages (see [Why does a business need a website?](/blog/why-does-a-business-need-a-website/))
3. **Local SEO:** target "[service] in [city]" keywords (see [What is SEO?](/blog/what-is-seo/))
4. **Consistent local citations** on Apple Maps, Bing, Yelp and industry directories (see [What are local citations?](/blog/what-are-local-citations/))
5. **Reviews:** ask every happy customer, reply to all

## Advertise locally

6. **Google Search and Local Services Ads** (where available) for high-intent searches like "plumber near me"
7. **Meta (Facebook/Instagram) ads** targeted by radius, with short videos (see [How to create Instagram Stories ads](/blog/how-to-create-instagram-stories-ads/))
8. **Nextdoor and local community platforms**
9. **Local radio, newspapers and newsletters** for older or community-focused audiences
10. **Flyers, signage and vehicle wraps** with a QR code to your booking page

(Choosing channels: [What is the most effective type of advertising?](/blog/what-is-the-most-effective-type-of-advertising/))

## Build relationships in the community

11. **Partner with complementary businesses** (a gym and a physiotherapist, a wedding venue and a photographer)
12. **Sponsor local teams, schools and events**
13. **Host workshops or open days**
14. **Join the chamber of commerce** and networking groups
15. **Local PR:** pitch stories (anniversaries, charity work, new services) to local media

## Turn customers into a customer base

16. **Referral programme:** reward customers who refer friends
17. **Loyalty programme:** stamps, points or member pricing
18. **Email and WhatsApp lists** (with consent) for offers and reminders (see [How to schedule a WhatsApp message](/blog/how-to-schedule-a-whatsapp-message/))
19. **Automated follow-ups:** thank-you messages, review requests, rebooking reminders (see [How to automate your sales process](/blog/how-to-automate-your-sales-process/))
20. **Exceptional service:** the source of reviews and word of mouth

## How to build a customer base from scratch

1. **Define your ideal customer** and where they spend time
2. **Launch offer:** introductory price or free consultation
3. **Tell your network** personally
4. **Collect contact details** from every customer (CRM; see [What is a CRM?](/blog/what-is-a-crm/))
5. **Ask for reviews early**
6. **Show up consistently** online and in the community
7. **Measure** which channels bring customers and double down

## How to expand your customer base

- Add **new services** existing customers ask for
- Target **new neighbourhoods** with location pages and ads
- Reach **new segments** (businesses, seniors, students)
- **Online booking and delivery** to widen your radius
- **Partnerships** that introduce you to other businesses' customers
- **Content marketing** answering local questions

## Measure what works

- Ask every new customer "How did you hear about us?"
- Use **tracking numbers, UTM links and promo codes** per channel
- Track **cost per new customer** and **customer lifetime value**
- Review monthly and shift budget to the best channels

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I promote my business locally?
Optimise your Google Business Profile, build a fast website with local SEO, collect reviews, run targeted local ads, partner with other businesses, join community events and use referral and follow-up systems.

### How do I advertise a small local business on a budget?
Focus on free and low-cost channels: Google Business Profile, reviews, social media, partnerships, referrals and community involvement, then add small, tightly targeted local ads.

### How do I build a customer base?
Define your ideal customer, make a strong launch offer, use your network, collect customer contacts, ask for reviews and referrals, and follow up consistently.

### How do I expand my customer base?
Offer new services, target new areas or segments, add online booking or delivery, partner with complementary businesses and create local content.

### What is the best free way to promote a local business?
A fully optimised Google Business Profile with lots of recent reviews is one of the most effective free tools for local visibility.

### Do local businesses need social media?
It helps with awareness and trust, especially with photos, videos and reviews, but it works best alongside a website and Google Business Profile.
