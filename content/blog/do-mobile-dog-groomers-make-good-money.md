<p class="lede">Mobile dog groomers can make <strong>good money</strong> because customers pay a <strong>premium for at-home convenience</strong>. Income depends on <strong>price per groom, dogs per day, route efficiency and van costs</strong>. Mobile groomers do <strong>fewer dogs per day</strong> than busy salons and carry <strong>van payments, fuel and maintenance</strong>, so pricing and scheduling are key.</p>

## How mobile grooming income works

**Income = dogs per day × price per groom − van, fuel, supplies and other costs**

## Mobile vs salon

| | Mobile | Salon |
|---|---|---|
| Price per dog | Higher (convenience) | Lower |
| Dogs per day | Fewer | More (with staff) |
| Overhead | Van, fuel, generator | Rent, utilities |
| Client experience | One-on-one, low stress | Kennel waiting |

## Costs

Van purchase or conversion, water and power systems, grooming equipment, fuel, maintenance, insurance (commercial auto + grooming coverage), supplies and booking software.

## How to make more as a mobile groomer

1. Charge a convenience fee or premium pricing
2. Group appointments by neighbourhood
3. Book multiple dogs at the same home
4. Pre-book every 4–8 weeks
5. Add teeth brushing, de-shedding and nail add-ons
6. Use online booking and reminders to avoid no-shows
7. Add a second van with a hired groomer

## Frequently asked questions

### Do mobile dog groomers make good money?
Yes, with premium pricing, efficient routes and full schedules.

### How many dogs can a mobile groomer do per day?
Fewer than a salon, usually depending on travel time and breed sizes.

### Is mobile grooming more profitable than a salon?
It can be per dog; salons scale more easily with staff.

### What does a mobile grooming van cost?
Costs vary widely between new, used and self-converted vans.

### How do mobile groomers price services?
By breed, size, coat and service, often with a convenience premium.

### How do mobile groomers get clients?
Online booking, local social media, vet referrals and neighbourhood marketing.
