<p class="lede">Most security companies charge a <strong>bill rate that's about 1.4 to 1.8 times the guard's hourly wage</strong>. In the US, unarmed guards are often billed at around <strong>$25 to $45 per hour</strong>, and armed guards at <strong>$35 to $65+</strong>, depending on location, risk and contract length. Build your rate from <strong>wages, payroll costs, insurance, overhead and profit</strong>.</p>

These are rough ranges; local wages and competition change them a lot.

## How to calculate your bill rate

| Cost per hour | Example |
|---|---|
| Guard wage | $18.00 |
| Payroll taxes and workers' comp (~15–25%) | $3.60 |
| Liability insurance, training, uniforms | $1.50 |
| Supervision and admin | $2.00 |
| Overhead (office, software, vehicles) | $1.50 |
| **Cost** | **$26.60** |
| Profit (10–15%) | $3.00 – $4.00 |
| **Bill rate** | **about $30 per hour** |

Use your real numbers.

## Typical rates by service

| Service | Typical pricing |
|---|---|
| Unarmed guard | ~$25 – $45 per hour |
| Armed guard | ~$35 – $65+ per hour |
| Event security | Hourly with a minimum shift (often 4 hours) |
| Mobile patrol | Per visit or monthly |
| Overtime, holidays, short notice | 1.5× rate |

## Factors that change price

- Local wage levels
- Armed vs unarmed
- Number of hours per week (bigger contracts get lower rates)
- Risk level and special training
- Contract length

## How to quote a contract

Visit the site, list duties, set post hours, price per guard hour, add supervision and reporting, and state overtime, holiday and cancellation terms.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much should I charge for security services?
Often 1.4 to 1.8 times the guard's wage, around $25 to $45 per hour for unarmed guards in many US areas.

### How much do private security companies charge?
Usually an hourly rate per guard, more for armed guards and events.

### How do I price mobile patrol?
Per visit or a monthly fee based on visits per night and drive time.

### Do security companies charge for holidays?
Most charge time-and-a-half for holidays and short-notice shifts.

### Why are some security quotes so cheap?
Usually lower guard pay, less supervision or minimal insurance.
