<p class="lede"><strong>Zendesk is used for customer service.</strong> It's a cloud platform that lets businesses receive, organise and resolve customer requests from email, chat, messaging apps, phone, social media and web forms in one place, as <strong>tickets</strong>. It also powers self-service help centres, AI agents that resolve common questions automatically, and reporting on support performance. Many companies also use it for internal help desks (IT and HR).</p>

Zendesk is one of the best-known customer service platforms in the world, used by companies from startups to large enterprises. This guide explains what Zendesk does, its main features (including its AI agents), who uses it, pricing and free options in 2026, pros and cons, and alternatives.

## What does Zendesk do?

### 1. Ticketing (the core)
Every customer request becomes a **ticket** with a status (new, open, pending, solved), priority, assignee and full conversation history. Agents work from shared views and queues, collaborate with internal notes, and use macros (saved responses and actions) to work faster.

### 2. Omnichannel support
Zendesk brings channels into one workspace:
- **Email**
- **Messaging and live chat** on your website and app
- **WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Instagram, LINE, Telegram** and other messaging apps
- **Voice** (phone support with call routing and recording)
- **Social media**
- **Web forms and APIs**

Customers can switch channels while agents see the whole history.

### 3. Help centre and knowledge base
A self-service website with articles, FAQs and community forums, which reduces ticket volume by helping customers help themselves. Content also powers AI answers.

### 4. AI agents and automation
- **AI agents** handle conversations end to end for common requests (order status, returns, account questions), grounded in your help centre and connected to your systems, and hand off to humans when needed
- **Copilot** for human agents: suggested replies, ticket summaries, tone adjustments, and recommended next actions
- **Intelligent triage:** detecting intent, language and sentiment to route tickets
- **Triggers and automations:** rule-based workflows (assign, notify, escalate, close)
- **SLA policies** to track response and resolution targets

(For how AI agents work in support, see [Will AI replace customer service?](/blog/will-ai-replace-customer-service/))

### 5. Reporting and analytics
Dashboards for ticket volume, response and resolution times, customer satisfaction (CSAT), agent performance, SLA compliance and AI resolution rates.

### 6. Workforce management and quality assurance
Forecasting and scheduling agents, and reviewing conversation quality (including AI-assisted QA across all conversations).

### 7. Integrations and APIs
A marketplace with over a thousand apps (Salesforce, HubSpot, Shopify, Slack, Jira and more), plus APIs and webhooks for custom integrations. (See [What is CRM integration?](/blog/crm-integration-and-automation/) and [What is a webhook?](/blog/what-is-a-webhook/))

## Who uses Zendesk?

- **E-commerce and retail** brands handling order and returns questions
- **SaaS and technology companies** supporting users
- **Financial services, travel, gaming and telecoms**
- **Internal help desks:** IT, HR and facilities teams use Zendesk for employee service
- **Small businesses to large enterprises:** it scales from a few agents to thousands

## Zendesk pricing (2026) and can you use it for free?

**Is Zendesk free?** There's **no permanent free plan**. Zendesk offers a **14-day free trial**, and qualifying startups may be eligible for its startup programme (which has offered free usage for a period). Some nonprofits may get discounts.

**Seat-based plans:** Zendesk's main bundles are the **Suite** plans (Team, Growth, Professional, Enterprise), priced per agent per month. As of 2026, published annual-billing prices are roughly:
- **Suite Team:** about $55 per agent/month
- **Suite Professional:** about $115 per agent/month
- **Suite Enterprise:** about $169 per agent/month

Monthly billing costs more. Lighter "Support" plans exist for email-focused ticketing.

**AI pricing:** Zendesk's AI agents are priced largely **per automated resolution**, with an allowance included in plans and additional resolutions billed via committed packs or pay-as-you-go. The **Copilot** add-on for human agents is priced per agent per month. AI pricing has changed several times, so confirm on Zendesk's official pricing page before budgeting.

**Tip:** estimate total cost = (agents × seat price) + (expected AI resolutions × resolution price) + add-ons (Copilot, workforce management, QA, advanced data privacy).

## How Zendesk works: a simple flow

1. A customer messages on your website: "Where's my order #4412?"
2. The **AI agent** identifies the intent, checks the order system via an integration, and replies with tracking details. Resolved, with no human needed.
3. Another customer emails angrily about a damaged item. Zendesk detects negative sentiment and high priority, creates a ticket and routes it to the returns team.
4. An agent opens the ticket; **Copilot** summarises it and suggests a reply and next steps (issue replacement).
5. The agent approves and sends; the ticket is solved; a CSAT survey follows.
6. Reports show resolution time, CSAT and how many conversations AI resolved.

## Zendesk pros and cons

### Pros
- **Mature, reliable and widely adopted**
- **Strong omnichannel support** including messaging apps
- **Scales** from small teams to large enterprises
- **Large integration marketplace**
- **Advanced AI agents and agent assist**
- **Good reporting and workforce tools** at higher tiers

### Cons
- **Cost** can be high, especially with add-ons and AI usage at scale
- **Complexity:** configuration and admin can take significant effort
- **Pricing changes** to AI and plans require careful monitoring
- **Some features locked to higher tiers**

## How to get the most from Zendesk

1. **Fix your help centre first:** AI agents and Copilot are only as good as your knowledge content.
2. **Analyse ticket reasons** and automate the top intents with AI agents and macros.
3. **Use SLAs and triggers** to ensure nothing falls through the cracks.
4. **Integrate order, billing and CRM data** so agents (human and AI) have context.
5. **Review AI conversations weekly** to improve accuracy.
6. **Make human escalation easy.**
7. **Track CSAT, resolution rate and cost per resolution,** not just deflection.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Zendesk used for?
Zendesk is used for customer service: managing customer requests from email, chat, messaging apps, phone and social media as tickets, providing self-service help centres, automating answers with AI agents, and reporting on support performance. It's also used for internal IT and HR help desks.

### What does Zendesk do?
Zendesk provides ticketing, omnichannel communication, a knowledge base and help centre, AI agents and agent assist (Copilot), workflow automation, SLA management, workforce management, quality assurance, analytics and integrations for customer and employee service.

### Can I use Zendesk for free?
Zendesk does not offer a permanent free plan. It offers a 14-day free trial, and eligible startups may qualify for a startup programme. Freshdesk and some other alternatives offer free plans for small teams.

### How much does Zendesk cost?
In 2026, Zendesk Suite plans cost roughly $55 to $169 per agent per month on annual billing, with higher monthly prices. AI agents are largely charged per automated resolution beyond included allowances, and Copilot is an add-on. Check Zendesk's pricing page for current rates.

### Is Zendesk a CRM?
Zendesk is primarily a customer service platform, not a full sales CRM, though it stores customer profiles and interaction history. Zendesk also offers a sales CRM product, and it integrates with CRMs such as Salesforce and HubSpot.

### What is the difference between Zendesk and Intercom?
Both are customer service platforms with AI agents. Zendesk is known for robust ticketing, omnichannel and enterprise-scale support operations; Intercom is known for conversational, in-app messaging and its Fin AI agent, popular with SaaS companies.
