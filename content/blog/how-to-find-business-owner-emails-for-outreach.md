<p class="lede">To find business owner email addresses for outreach, start with <strong>public business sources</strong>: the company <strong>website</strong> (contact, about, team pages), <strong>Google Business Profile</strong>, <strong>LinkedIn</strong>, social profiles and <strong>industry directories</strong>. Use <strong>email finder tools</strong> (such as Hunter, Apollo or Snov) to find and <strong>verify</strong> professional emails, then send <strong>personalised, relevant outreach</strong> that complies with <strong>anti-spam and privacy laws</strong> and includes an easy opt-out.</p>

## Where to find owner and decision-maker contacts

| Source | What you'll find |
|---|---|
| **Business website** | Contact page, about/team page, footer emails |
| **Google Business Profile** | Website, phone, sometimes messaging |
| **LinkedIn** | Owner/manager names and roles |
| **Instagram/Facebook** | Contact buttons and emails on business profiles |
| **Industry directories and associations** | Member listings |
| **Local chambers of commerce** | Business member contacts |
| **Domain WHOIS** | Usually privacy-protected now |

For restaurants specifically: website "contact/catering/events" emails, Instagram contact buttons, local restaurant associations, and LinkedIn for multi-location groups.

## Email finder and verification tools

- **Hunter.io:** domain search and email patterns
- **Apollo, Snov.io, RocketReach, Lusha:** contact databases and finders
- **Clay:** enrichment workflows combining sources
- **Verification:** NeverBounce, ZeroBounce or built-in verifiers to reduce bounces

**Email patterns:** many businesses use firstname@domain.com or firstname.lastname@domain.com; verify before sending.

## Writing outreach that gets replies

- **Personalise:** mention something specific about their business
- **Lead with value:** a problem you noticed and a quick idea
- **Keep it short:** 50–125 words
- **One clear ask:** a short call, or permission to send an idea
- **Proof:** a relevant result for a similar business
- **Easy opt-out**

### Example

> Subject: Online orders for Spice Garden
>
> Hi Ravi, I ordered from Spice Garden last week (the butter chicken is excellent). I noticed online orders go through apps that take a big commission. We helped a restaurant nearby set up direct ordering on their website and moved 30% of repeat orders off the apps. Open to a 10-minute chat? If not relevant, just let me know and I won't follow up.

## Compliance and ethics

- **CAN-SPAM (US):** accurate headers and subject, physical address, clear opt-out, honour unsubscribes promptly
- **GDPR/UK GDPR (EU/UK):** B2B outreach may rely on legitimate interest with proper assessment; provide transparency and easy objection; some countries have stricter rules for emailing individuals
- **CASL (Canada):** stricter consent requirements
- **Local laws** elsewhere (check your market)
- Don't scrape personal data in violation of site terms; don't buy dubious lists

## Deliverability tips

- Use a **separate sending domain/subdomain** and warm it up
- Set up **SPF, DKIM, DMARC** (see [How to make a business email](/blog/how-to-make-a-business-email/))
- Send **small volumes** with personalisation
- Verify emails to keep bounce rates low
- Monitor replies and spam complaints

## Automating prospecting responsibly

Automation can find businesses (e.g., from Google Maps data where permitted), enrich contacts, draft personalised first lines with AI for review, schedule follow-ups and log everything in a CRM. Keep humans reviewing messages and volumes reasonable. ## Frequently asked questions

### How do I find a business owner's email address?
Check the business website, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn and social profiles, then use email finder and verification tools for professional addresses.

### How can I find restaurant owner emails for outreach?
Look at restaurant websites (contact, events, catering pages), Instagram contact buttons, LinkedIn for owners or managers, and local restaurant associations, then verify emails.

### Is it legal to cold email business owners?
In many countries B2B cold email is allowed with conditions such as honest content and easy opt-out; some jurisdictions require consent. Check local laws.

### What is the best email finder tool?
Hunter, Apollo, Snov.io and RocketReach are popular; accuracy varies, so verify emails before sending.

### How do I avoid my cold emails going to spam?
Authenticate your domain, warm it up, send low volumes, personalise, verify addresses and avoid spammy language.

### How many follow-ups should I send?
Commonly two to four polite follow-ups over a couple of weeks, stopping immediately if they ask you to.
