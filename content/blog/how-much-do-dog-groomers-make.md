<p class="lede">Dog groomer income varies widely with <strong>location, experience, pay structure and business model</strong>. Employed groomers are often paid <strong>hourly or on commission</strong> (commonly a percentage of grooming fees) plus <strong>tips</strong>; experienced groomers with loyal clients, <strong>mobile groomers</strong> and <strong>salon owners</strong> can earn considerably more than entry-level bathers.</p>

## Pay structures

| Structure | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Hourly** | Fixed wage, common for bathers and beginners |
| **Commission** | Percentage of each groom (often substantial share) |
| **Hourly + commission** | Base plus incentive |
| **Booth/chair rent** | Pay salon rent, keep your fees |
| **Self-employed/mobile** | Keep revenue minus costs |
| **Salon owner** | Profit from team and services |

## Factors affecting income

- Location and local pricing
- Experience and speed (dogs per day)
- Specialisation (doodles, show cuts, cats)
- Client retention and rebooking
- Tips
- Business model (employee vs owner)

## How much does dog grooming cost customers?

Prices depend on size, breed, coat condition and service (bath, full groom, de-matting, add-ons) and location. Mobile grooming usually costs more for convenience.

## How groomers earn more

1. Build speed and quality
2. Rebook every client at checkout
3. Specialise in high-demand breeds
4. Add services (teeth brushing, de-shedding, spa treatments)
5. Go mobile or open a salon (see [How to start a dog grooming business](/blog/how-to-start-a-dog-grooming-business/))
6. Online booking and reminders to reduce no-shows (see [How to make a booking website](/blog/how-to-make-a-booking-website/))
7. Collect reviews and referrals

## Frequently asked questions

### How much do dog groomers make?
It varies by location, experience and pay model; experienced commission-based, mobile and self-employed groomers often earn more than entry-level hourly staff.

### Do dog groomers get tips?
Yes, tips are common and can add meaningfully to income.

### Do mobile groomers make more money?
Often they charge higher prices, but have van, fuel and equipment costs.

### How much do pet groomers make per dog?
Commission groomers earn a percentage of each groom price; self-employed groomers keep the fee minus costs.

### How much does dog grooming cost?
It depends on size, breed, coat condition, services and location.

### How can a dog groomer increase income?
Rebook clients, specialise, add services, raise prices with demand and consider owning a business.
