<p class="lede">A food truck usually needs several permits: a <strong>business licence</strong>, a <strong>mobile food vendor permit</strong>, a <strong>health department permit and inspection</strong>, <strong>food handler/manager certifications</strong>, <strong>fire safety inspection</strong> (especially with propane), a <strong>commissary agreement</strong>, <strong>parking/zoning permits</strong> for where you operate, <strong>event permits</strong>, <strong>sales tax registration</strong> and <strong>commercial vehicle registration and insurance</strong>. Costs and rules vary widely by city, county and state.</p>

## Common food truck permits and licences

| Permit/licence | Issued by (typical) |
|---|---|
| Business licence | City/county |
| Mobile food vendor permit | City/county or health department |
| Health permit and inspection | Health department |
| Food handler cards / food manager certification | Health department-approved programmes |
| Fire department inspection | Fire marshal (propane, suppression systems) |
| Commissary letter/agreement | Licensed commissary kitchen |
| Parking/zoning/vending location permits | City transportation or zoning |
| Special event permits | Event organisers/city |
| Sales tax permit | State/tax authority |
| Vehicle registration and commercial insurance | DMV/insurers |
| Employer registrations | If hiring |

## How to get a food truck licence

1. Contact your city/county health department and business licensing office
2. Form your business and get tax IDs
3. Secure a commissary agreement
4. Build or buy a truck that meets health and fire codes
5. Pass health and fire inspections
6. Obtain vendor and parking permits for zones you'll operate in
7. Keep certifications and renewals up to date

## How much does a food truck permit cost?

It varies widely: some permits cost tens of dollars, others hundreds or more, and you may need multiple permits across jurisdictions plus annual renewals. Budget a meaningful amount for permits and inspections in your startup plan (see [How much does a food truck cost?](/blog/how-much-does-a-food-truck-cost/)).

## Tips

- Ask other local food truck owners and associations
- Keep digital copies of permits on the truck
- Track renewal dates (automated reminders help)
- Check rules for each city you plan to operate in

## Frequently asked questions

### What permits do I need for a food truck?
Typically a business licence, mobile food vendor permit, health permit, food safety certifications, fire inspection, commissary agreement, parking/zoning permits, sales tax registration and vehicle registration/insurance.

### How much is a food truck permit?
It depends on your city and state; costs range from small fees to several hundred dollars or more per permit, with renewals.

### How do I get a food truck licence?
Contact local health and licensing departments, secure a commissary, pass inspections and apply for vendor and parking permits.

### Do food trucks need a commissary?
Many jurisdictions require one for food prep, storage, water and waste disposal.

### Can I operate my food truck in different cities?
Often yes, but you may need permits for each city or county.

### How long does it take to get food truck permits?
Anywhere from a few weeks to several months depending on inspections and local processes.
