<p class="lede">To find a deck contractor, look for <strong>dedicated deck builders</strong> or experienced <strong>carpenters and general contractors</strong> through <strong>reviews, referrals and supplier recommendations</strong>. Choose one that's <strong>licensed and insured</strong>, pulls <strong>permits</strong>, shows a <strong>portfolio of similar decks</strong>, explains <strong>materials</strong> clearly, and gives a <strong>detailed written quote and warranty</strong>.</p>

## Who builds decks?

| Option | Notes |
|---|---|
| Deck building companies | Specialists, often best design and experience |
| Carpenters | Many build decks, especially smaller ones |
| General contractors | Good for decks as part of bigger projects |
| Landscaping companies | Some build decks with outdoor living projects |

## Where to find deck contractors

- Google reviews and local recommendations
- Lumber yards and composite decking suppliers (installer lists)
- Neighbours with decks you like
- Home shows

## How to choose

1. **Licence and insurance**: verify both
2. **Permits**: the builder should pull them
3. **Portfolio**: decks like yours
4. **Materials**: pressure-treated wood, cedar, composite, PVC
5. **Design help**: layout, railings, stairs, lighting
6. **Detailed quote**: footings, framing, hardware, decking, railings, removal
7. **Timeline and payment schedule**
8. **Warranty**: workmanship and materials

## Questions to ask

- How long have you been building decks?
- Who will be on site?
- How do you handle footings and ledger flashing?
- What's included and what's extra?

## Red flags

Large upfront payments, no permits, no insurance, vague quotes and pressure to sign today.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I find a deck contractor?
Use reviews, referrals and supplier installer lists, then compare licensed, insured builders.

### Who do you hire to build a deck?
A deck builder, carpenter or general contractor.

### Do carpenters build decks?
Many do, especially smaller and standard decks.

### How do I choose a deck contractor?
Check licence, insurance, permits, portfolio, detailed quotes and warranties.

### Does a deck need a permit?
Most attached or raised decks need a permit.
