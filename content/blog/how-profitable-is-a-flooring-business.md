<p class="lede">A flooring business can be quite profitable. Installers who sell their labour often keep <strong>20% to 40%</strong> after costs, and flooring stores typically earn <strong>30% to 50% gross margin</strong> on material with <strong>5% to 15% net profit</strong>. Owners commonly earn <strong>$50,000 to $150,000+</strong>, depending on crew size and job volume.</p>

These are rough industry ranges, not guarantees.

## Where the money comes from

| Source | Margin |
|---|---|
| Installation labour | Highest margin per hour, limited by crew time |
| Material markup | Good margin on every job if you supply |
| Removal and disposal | Easy add-on |
| Subfloor prep and leveling | Often underpriced; charge for it |
| Stairs, trims and transitions | High value per hour |
| Refinishing hardwood | Strong margins, little material |

## Installer vs store

| | Installer | Flooring store |
|---|---|---|
| Startup cost | Low | High |
| Overhead | Low | Rent, showroom, staff |
| Gross margin | High on labour | High on material |
| Net profit | Good for owner-operators | 5%–15% typical |
| Scale | Limited by crew time | Bigger revenue |

## Why flooring companies need working capital

You often buy material before the job starts, pay crews weekly and wait for builders or insurance companies to pay. Without working capital, a growing flooring business can run out of cash even while it's profitable. Take deposits (often 30%–50%) and invoice quickly.

## How to increase profit

- Charge for removal, prep, moving furniture and stairs
- Supply material instead of install-only
- Focus on higher-margin types like tile and hardwood
- Reduce callbacks with careful prep and moisture testing
- Take deposits to fund materials
- Get direct homeowner jobs through Google and a quote-booking website instead of relying only on low-rate subcontracting

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a flooring business profitable?
Yes. Installers often keep 20%–40% and stores typically net 5%–15% after overhead.

### How much do flooring business owners make?
Commonly $50,000 to $150,000+, depending on crews and volume.

### What is the markup on flooring?
Many stores mark up material 30%–50% or more over cost.

### Why do flooring companies need working capital?
They buy material and pay crews before customers or builders pay them.

### What flooring work is most profitable?
Tile, hardwood refinishing and add-ons like stairs and subfloor prep.
