<p class="lede">To open a preschool: understand how a <strong>preschool differs from a daycare</strong> (education-focused programmes for roughly ages 3–5, often with shorter school-day schedules), research <strong>local demand and licensing rules</strong>, write a <strong>business plan</strong>, choose a <strong>curriculum approach</strong>, prepare a <strong>safe, licensed facility</strong>, hire <strong>qualified early childhood teachers</strong> to meet ratios, set <strong>tuition and schedules</strong>, and market to families with <strong>tours and an easy online enrolment process</strong>.</p>

## Preschool vs daycare

| | Preschool | Daycare |
|---|---|---|
| Ages | Often 3–5 | Infants to school age |
| Focus | Early education, school readiness | Care with learning activities |
| Schedule | Half-day or school-day programmes | Full day, year-round |
| Staff | Early childhood education qualifications common | Varies by licence |

(Daycare guide: [How to start a daycare](/blog/how-to-start-a-daycare/).)

## Steps

1. **Research:** waitlists, competitors, family needs, local regulations
2. **Business plan:** costs, tuition, enrolment targets, staffing
3. **Licensing:** childcare/early education licence, background checks, inspections (see [How to get a daycare license](/blog/how-to-get-a-daycare-license/))
4. **Curriculum:** play-based, Montessori, Reggio Emilia, Waldorf, academic readiness, or a blend
5. **Facility:** classrooms, child-sized furniture, learning centres, outdoor play, safety features
6. **Teachers:** early childhood qualifications, ratios, training
7. **Policies:** parent handbook, health, drop-off/pickup, behaviour guidance
8. **Tuition:** half-day/full-day options, registration fees, sibling discounts
9. **Systems:** enrolment, billing and parent communication software (see [How to manage daycare communication](/blog/how-to-manage-daycare-communication/))
10. **Marketing:** website with programme details and tour booking, Google Business Profile, open days, parent referrals

## Costs

Similar drivers to daycare centres: facility, renovations, materials, staffing and licensing (see [How much does it cost to open a daycare?](/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-open-a-daycare/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I open a preschool?
Research demand and rules, write a business plan, get licensed, choose a curriculum, prepare a safe facility, hire qualified teachers, set tuition and market with tours and online enrolment.

### What is the difference between a preschool and a daycare?
Preschools focus on early education for older toddlers and preschoolers; daycares provide broader care, often from infancy and for longer hours.

### Do preschool teachers need qualifications?
Requirements vary, but early childhood education credentials are common and often required.

### How much does it cost to open a preschool?
Costs depend on facility, renovations, staffing and location, similar to daycare centres.

### Which preschool curriculum is best?
Play-based, Montessori and Reggio Emilia are popular; choose one aligned with your philosophy and community.

### How do preschools attract families?
Open days, tours, a clear website, reviews, referrals and community involvement.
