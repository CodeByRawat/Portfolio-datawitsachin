<p class="lede">A <strong>video brief for a product demo</strong> is a one- to two-page document that tells the video team <strong>why the video exists, who it's for, the single message it must deliver, which features to show, what proof to include, the call to action</strong>, and the practical details: length, formats, style references, brand assets, budget and deadline. A good brief prevents the most expensive problem in video production: discovering after animation that the video says the wrong thing.</p>

Whether you're briefing an in-house editor, a freelancer or an agency for a SaaS demo, app promo, product launch or explainer, the template below will get you a better video, faster, with fewer revision rounds.

## Why the brief matters

- **Alignment:** everyone (marketing, product, founders) agrees on the message before money is spent
- **Fewer revisions:** most expensive changes come from unclear goals, not bad animation
- **Better creative:** constraints and context help video makers propose stronger ideas
- **Accurate quotes:** producers can price and schedule correctly

## What to include in a product demo video brief

### 1. Background
One paragraph on the company, the product and why you're making this video now (launch, new feature, low conversion on the landing page, sales enablement).

### 2. Objective and success metric
Example: "Increase demo bookings from the pricing page; success = 15% more bookings in 60 days." Or: "Ad to drive trial sign-ups at under $30 CPA."

### 3. Target audience
Role, industry, sophistication level, pains and objections. Is it someone who's never heard of you (needs context) or a warm lead (needs depth)?

### 4. The single key message
One sentence the viewer should remember. "Invoices get approved and paid without anyone chasing them."

### 5. Features to show (prioritised)
List no more than **three** must-show features and how each links to a benefit. Demo videos fail when they try to tour every button.

| Feature | Benefit to the viewer | Must / nice to have |
|---|---|---|
| Auto-capture invoices from email | No manual data entry | Must |
| Approval rules | No chasing managers | Must |
| ERP sync | Books always up to date | Nice |

### 6. Proof
Customer logos, testimonials, metrics, awards or ratings you can legally use.

### 7. Call to action
"Book a demo", "Start free trial", "Download the app". One CTA.

### 8. Length and formats
- **Ad cut:** 15–30 s, 9:16 and 4:5
- **Landing page / sales video:** 60–120 s, 16:9
- **Feature clips:** 10–20 s each for social and onboarding
- Captions burned in? Voiceover language(s)?

### 9. Style and tone
Reference videos you like (and why), style (2D motion, 3D UI, live action, screen recording with motion graphics, UGC), tone (premium, playful, technical). (Style options: [What are motion graphics?](/blog/what-are-motion-graphics/))

### 10. Assets available
Logo files (vector), brand guidelines, fonts, product access or a demo account with clean sample data, high-resolution screenshots, Figma files, existing footage, approved music.

### 11. Mandatories and constraints
Legal disclaimers, claims that need approval, things never to show (customer data, unreleased features), accessibility needs.

### 12. Budget, timeline and approvals
Budget range, deadline, review rounds, who gives final approval.

## A proven product demo script structure

| Section | Time (60 s video) | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | 0–5 s | Name the pain or show the result immediately |
| **Problem** | 5–12 s | Make the viewer feel understood |
| **Solution intro** | 12–18 s | Introduce the product in one line |
| **Demo of feature 1–3** | 18–45 s | Show, don't tell: real UI, each tied to a benefit |
| **Proof** | 45–52 s | Customer result, logos, rating |
| **CTA** | 52–60 s | One action, with the URL or button |

About 140–160 spoken words fit in 60 seconds.

## Example brief (short)

- **Objective:** more trial sign-ups from Meta ads, target CPA under $35
- **Audience:** owners of 5–50 person agencies who lose hours to client reporting
- **Key message:** "Client reports build themselves every Monday."
- **Features:** auto data pull from ad platforms; branded templates; scheduled email delivery
- **Proof:** "1,200 agencies", 4.8 rating
- **CTA:** Start free trial
- **Deliverables:** 20 s (9:16, 4:5), 60 s (16:9), captions on
- **Style:** clean 3D UI motion, upbeat but not childish; references attached
- **Assets:** Figma UI kit, demo account with dummy data, brand guide

## From brief to finished video

1. **Brief** approved
2. **Script** written and approved
3. **Storyboard / style frames** approved
4. **Voiceover and music** recorded or licensed
5. **Animation / edit**
6. **Review rounds** (typically two)
7. **Final exports** for every platform

Approving script and storyboard before production is what keeps timelines and budgets under control. (Making the animation: [How to make motion graphics](/blog/how-to-make-motion-graphics/).)

## Common brief mistakes

- No clear objective or metric
- Trying to show every feature
- "Make it like Apple" without explaining which qualities you mean
- Missing assets (no clean demo data, low-res logos)
- Too many approvers, giving contradictory feedback
- Changing the message after animation has started
- No plan for cut-downs and aspect ratios

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you write a video brief for a product demo?
State the objective and success metric, describe the audience, write one key message, list up to three features with their benefits, add proof, a single CTA, deliverables and formats, style references, assets, mandatories, budget and timeline.

### What should a product demo video include?
A hook, the problem, a short product introduction, a focused demo of the top features tied to benefits, proof such as customer results, and a clear call to action.

### How long should a product demo video be?
Ads usually work best at 15–30 seconds; landing page and sales demos at 60–120 seconds; longer walkthroughs belong in onboarding or help centres.

### What is the difference between a creative brief and a video brief?
A creative brief sets the message and strategy for any marketing asset. A video brief adds video-specific details: length, formats, script structure, style, voiceover, music and production assets.

### Who writes the video brief?
Usually the marketer, product marketer or founder who owns the goal, with input from product and sales, before handing it to the video team.

### How much does a product demo video cost?
It depends on length and style. Simple screen-recording edits cost little; custom motion graphics or 3D UI demos from professionals typically range from about $1,500 to $10,000 or more.
