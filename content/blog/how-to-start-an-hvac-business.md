<p class="lede">To start an HVAC business: get the <strong>experience and licences</strong> your state requires (usually an HVAC/mechanical contractor licence plus <strong>EPA Section 608</strong> certification to handle refrigerants), <strong>register the business</strong>, get <strong>liability insurance, bonding and workers' comp</strong>, buy <strong>tools, a van and starter inventory</strong>, choose <strong>residential or commercial</strong> work, set <strong>flat-rate pricing</strong>, use <strong>field service software</strong>, sell <strong>maintenance agreements</strong>, and market with <strong>Google, reviews and a fast website</strong>.</p>

## Step by step

1. **Experience:** most owners work years as technicians first; many states require documented experience for a contractor licence
2. **Licences and certifications:** state HVAC/mechanical contractor licence, EPA 608, local business licence, permits for installations
3. **Business plan:** services (repair, replacement, maintenance, ductwork, commercial refrigeration), service area, pricing and targets
4. **Register:** LLC or other entity, EIN, business bank account
5. **Insurance and bonding:** general liability, commercial auto, workers' compensation, contractor bond
6. **Tools and van:** gauges, vacuum pump, recovery machine, leak detector, meters, ladders, van shelving, starter parts
7. **Suppliers:** open accounts with distributors for equipment and parts
8. **Pricing:** flat-rate price book for repairs, quoted installs with good/better/best options, financing for replacements
9. **Software:** scheduling, dispatching, invoicing, payments, customer history (see [How to run an HVAC business](/blog/how-to-run-an-hvac-business/))
10. **Maintenance agreements:** recurring revenue and steady seasonal work
11. **Marketing:** Google Business Profile, Local Services Ads, reviews, website with online booking (see [How to market your HVAC business](/blog/how-to-market-your-hvac-business/))

## Startup costs

The main costs are tools, a service van, insurance, licensing, inventory, software and marketing. Starting with repair and maintenance needs less capital than large installation work.

## How long does it take?

Becoming a licensed contractor can take years of field experience; once licensed, registering, insuring and equipping the business can take weeks to a few months.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start an HVAC business?
Get experience and licences, register and insure the business, buy tools and a van, set flat-rate pricing, use field service software and market locally.

### What licences do I need for an HVAC company?
Typically a state HVAC or mechanical contractor licence, EPA 608 certification and a local business licence; rules vary by state.

### How much does it cost to start an HVAC business?
It depends on tools, vehicle, insurance and inventory; starting with service and repair is the least expensive route.

### Should I start my own HVAC business?
If you have strong technical skills, licences and are ready to handle sales and admin, it can be very rewarding.

### How do HVAC businesses get customers?
Google Business Profile and reviews, Local Services Ads, referrals, a website with booking, and maintenance plans.

### What is the most profitable part of HVAC?
System replacements and maintenance agreements are typically the biggest profit drivers.
