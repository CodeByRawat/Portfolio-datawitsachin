<p class="lede">To become an independent courier driver, you need a <strong>reliable vehicle, a valid license and clean driving record</strong>, the right <strong>insurance</strong>, and a smartphone. Start by <strong>contracting with courier companies or delivery apps</strong>, then add <strong>your own direct business clients</strong> for better pay. You'll work as a 1099 contractor and handle your own expenses and taxes.</p>

## Requirements

- Driver's license and a clean driving record
- Reliable car, SUV, cargo van or box truck
- Proof of insurance (often commercial auto for courier companies)
- Background check
- Smartphone with GPS and courier apps
- Some companies require a minimum age (often 21+)

## Where to find courier work

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery apps | Easy to start | Low pay per delivery |
| Local courier companies (contractor) | Steadier routes | Company takes a share |
| Medical courier companies | Regular routes, better pay | Training required |
| Your own direct clients | Highest pay | You find and keep clients |

## Larger vehicles pay more

Cargo vans and box trucks can do bigger B2B and freight-style deliveries, which pay more per job than car deliveries.

## Pay and expenses

Pay depends on your vehicle, niche and market. Track fuel, maintenance, insurance, phone and mileage: as a 1099 contractor these are often tax-deductible, and you'll need to set money aside for self-employment taxes.

## Getting direct clients

Visit labs, law firms, print shops and parts stores. Offer reliable daily pickups at a fixed price. Once you have several, register an LLC and you're running a courier business.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I become a courier with my own vehicle?
Get insurance, pass a background check, and sign up with courier companies or delivery apps.

### Do I need special insurance to be a courier?
Usually yes. Personal auto policies often exclude business deliveries.

### Are courier drivers independent contractors?
Many are 1099 contractors who use their own vehicles.

### How much do independent courier drivers make?
It varies widely by niche and vehicle; medical and B2B routes generally pay more than app deliveries.

### Can I be a courier with a car?
Yes. Many document, medical and small-package couriers use cars.
