<p class="lede">A dog grooming business makes money from <strong>dogs groomed per day × average price</strong>, plus <strong>add-ons and retail</strong>. Owner income depends on <strong>business model (home, mobile, salon), number of groomers, pricing and rebooking rates</strong>. Self-employed groomers keep the full price minus costs; salon owners earn from groomers' work after paying commissions or wages.</p>

## Revenue formula

**Revenue = dogs per day × average ticket × working days**

Raise any of those three and income grows. Rebooking clients every 4–8 weeks keeps the schedule full.

## Income by business model

| Model | Income drivers | Main costs |
|---|---|---|
| Home-based | Low overhead, solo output | Equipment, insurance |
| Mobile van | Premium prices, convenience | Van loan, fuel, maintenance |
| Salon | Multiple groomers, retail, daycare | Rent, wages/commission, utilities |

## Costs

Rent or van payments, groomer pay (often commission), supplies, insurance, utilities, software, marketing and equipment repairs.

## Are dog grooming businesses profitable?

Yes, when pricing covers time per dog, schedules stay full, and costs are controlled. Mobile groomers often charge more per dog; salons scale through more staff.

## Do mobile dog groomers make good money?

Mobile groomers can, because customers pay for convenience, but they do fewer dogs per day and carry van costs.

## How to earn more

- Raise prices for breeds that take longer
- Charge for matting, de-shedding and nail grinding
- Sell rebooking at checkout
- Add teeth brushing, spa packages and retail
- Hire groomers and bathers to increase capacity
- Use online booking to reduce no-shows

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a dog grooming business make?
It depends on dogs per day, prices, groomers employed and costs.

### How much do dog grooming salon owners make?
They earn the business profit after rent, groomer pay and supplies.

### How much does a self-employed dog groomer make?
They keep the full grooming price minus expenses, so income depends on volume and pricing.

### Are dog grooming businesses profitable?
Yes, with full schedules, good pricing and controlled costs.

### Do mobile dog groomers make good money?
Often, thanks to premium pricing, though they groom fewer dogs per day.

### How many dogs can a groomer do per day?
It depends on breed and service; full grooms take longer than baths.
