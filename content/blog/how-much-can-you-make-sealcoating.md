<p class="lede">A one-person sealcoating business can make roughly <strong>$40,000 to $100,000</strong> in a season, and companies with crews and commercial contracts can bring in <strong>$250,000 to $1 million+</strong>. Sealcoating often has <strong>40% to 60% gross margins</strong> because sealer is cheap compared with what customers pay. Seal coaters working for companies often earn <strong>$15 to $25 per hour</strong>.</p>

These are rough ranges; your area, prices and weather matter.

## Profit per job (example)

| Line | Driveway |
|---|---|
| Price (1,000 sq ft) | $300 |
| Sealer, sand, crack filler | −$60 |
| Fuel and equipment | −$20 |
| Labour (2 people, 1.5 hours) | −$60 |
| **Profit** | **about $160** |

Small crews can do several driveways a day.

## Income by business size

| Business | Rough yearly revenue |
|---|---|
| Solo, driveways only | $40,000 – $100,000 |
| Small crew, driveways + small lots | $100,000 – $300,000 |
| Commercial with striping | $300,000 – $1 million+ |

## What do seal coaters make?

Employees often earn $15 to $25 per hour, with crew leaders earning more.

## Ways to earn more

- Add **crack filling** (high margin per foot)
- Add **line striping** for parking lots
- Win commercial contracts (bigger jobs)
- Offer annual maintenance plans
- Add snow removal in winter

## Costs to watch

Sealer and sand, crack filler, fuel, equipment, labour, insurance and marketing.
