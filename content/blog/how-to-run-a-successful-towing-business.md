<p class="lede">A successful towing business <strong>answers every call fast</strong>, gets to customers quickly, <strong>prices every extra service</strong>, gets <strong>more direct calls than motor club calls</strong>, keeps trucks running, and adds trucks only when the current ones are busy. Safety and good drivers protect your insurance rates.</p>

## 1. Answer every call, 24/7

Missed calls go to the next tow company. Use a dispatch line, call forwarding or an answering service, and reply to web leads within minutes.

## 2. Fast response times

Quote an honest arrival time and beat it. Fast, friendly service earns police rotation standing, motor club ratings and Google reviews.

## 3. Hire and train good drivers

- Clean driving records (this affects your insurance)
- Training on safe loading, scene safety and customer service
- Clear pay structure (hourly, per call or commission)
- Photos before every hook-up to avoid damage claims

## 4. Price correctly

| Fee | Example |
|---|---|
| Hook-up | Base charge per tow |
| Mileage | Per loaded mile |
| After-hours / holidays | Surcharge |
| Winching / recovery | Per hour or per job |
| Storage | Per day |
| Dollies, go-jacks, extra labour | Add-on |

## 5. Balance your work

| Source | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| Motor clubs | Steady volume | Lower rates |
| Police rotation | Higher fees, storage | Rules and paperwork |
| Private property | Recurring contracts | Customer complaints |
| Direct (Google, referrals) | Full price | Needs marketing |
| Dealers and body shops | Repeat business | Price pressure |

## 6. Keep trucks on the road

Follow a maintenance schedule, keep a spare-parts budget and track cost per truck. A truck in the shop earns nothing.

## 7. Manage cash flow

Motor clubs and insurers can pay slowly. Invoice the same day, track receivables and keep a cash reserve for insurance renewals.

## 8. Build reviews and a strong website

Ask happy customers for Google reviews and make your website load fast on phones with a big call button and your service area.

## 9. Grow carefully

Add a truck when you're turning down calls regularly. Consider adding storage, heavy-duty or transport work as you grow.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I make my towing business successful?
Answer every call, respond fast, price every service, build direct customers and maintain your trucks.

### How do towing companies get more calls?
Google reviews, a mobile-friendly website, police rotation lists, private property contracts and body shop relationships.

### Should I work with motor clubs?
They're good for steady volume when you start, but direct calls pay more.

### When should I add another tow truck?
When you're regularly turning down calls or customers wait too long.

### What software do towing companies use?
Towing dispatch software for calls, drivers, invoices, photos and impounds.
