<p class="lede">To market a balloon business, show off your work with <strong>photos and videos on Instagram and TikTok</strong>, build a <strong>Google Business Profile</strong> and a <strong>website with packages</strong>, partner with <strong>venues, event planners, photographers and party rental companies</strong>, approach <strong>local businesses</strong> for openings and promos, and collect <strong>reviews and referrals</strong>.</p>

## 1. Social media

- Post finished installs, time-lapses and before/after videos
- Use local hashtags and location tags
- Show behind-the-scenes and colour palettes
- Tag clients, venues and photographers

## 2. Portfolio and styled shoots

Collaborate with photographers and other vendors on styled shoots to create high-quality portfolio images.

## 3. Google and website

| Tool | Why |
|---|---|
| Google Business Profile | Shows up in local searches |
| Website with packages | Makes booking easy |
| Online quote form | Captures leads 24/7 |
| Reviews | Builds trust |

## 4. Partnerships

| Partner | How they help |
|---|---|
| Venues | Preferred vendor lists |
| Event planners | Steady bookings |
| Party rental companies | Bundle décor with rentals |
| Photographers | Shared content and referrals |
| Bakeries and florists | Cross-referrals |

## 5. Local businesses

Offer grand opening arches, seasonal displays and promo décor to shops, gyms, dealerships and restaurants.

## 6. Promotions

Holiday specials, grab-and-go garlands, and referral discounts. Run giveaways with partner vendors.

## 7. Reviews and repeat clients

Ask every client for a review and photos, and remind them before next year's birthdays and events.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I market my balloon business?
Use social media, a Google profile, a website, vendor partnerships and reviews.

### How do I promote a balloon business on Instagram?
Post installs, videos, and tag clients, venues and vendors.

### How do balloon businesses get clients?
Social media, planners, venues, parents and local businesses.

### Should I partner with party rental companies?
Yes. Bundling balloons with rentals brings steady bookings.

### Do balloon businesses need a website?
A simple website with packages and a quote form helps convert leads.
