<p class="lede">To become a trucker: meet <strong>age and driving record requirements</strong>, get a <strong>DOT medical card</strong>, obtain a <strong>commercial learner's permit (CLP)</strong>, complete <strong>CDL training</strong> (required entry-level driver training in the US), pass the <strong>skills and road test</strong> to earn your <strong>CDL</strong>, add <strong>endorsements</strong> if needed, and apply for your <strong>first job</strong>, often OTR or regional with a carrier that hires new drivers.</p>

## Steps (US example)

1. **Requirements:** age minimums (interstate typically 21+), valid licence, clean record
2. **DOT medical exam** and medical card
3. **Commercial learner's permit (CLP):** knowledge tests
4. **Entry-level driver training (ELDT)** from a registered provider
5. **Practice** with the CLP holding period
6. **Skills test:** pre-trip inspection, basic control, road test
7. **CDL issued** (Class A for tractor-trailers)
8. **Endorsements:** hazmat, tanker, doubles/triples as needed
9. **First job:** carriers with training programmes, OTR/regional entry roles
10. **Grow:** specialise, move to local/regional, or become an owner-operator (see [How to become an owner-operator truck driver](/blog/how-to-become-an-owner-operator-truck-driver/))

## CDL school vs company-sponsored training

| | CDL school | Company-sponsored |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | You pay tuition | Low/no upfront cost |
| Commitment | Free to choose employer | Often contract to drive for the company |

## Skills that help

Safety focus, patience, time management, basic mechanics, communication and comfort with long hours.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I become a trucker?
Meet requirements, get a medical card and learner's permit, complete CDL training, pass the skills test and apply for driving jobs.

### How long does it take to become a truck driver?
Often a few weeks to a few months depending on the training programme.

### How much does CDL training cost?
CDL school tuition varies; company-sponsored programmes reduce upfront costs but may require a work commitment.

### Do I need a Class A CDL?
For tractor-trailers, yes; smaller trucks may require Class B or no CDL.

### Can new truck drivers get jobs?
Yes, many carriers hire new CDL holders, often for OTR or regional routes.

### What is the best way to start a trucking career?
Get solid training, start with a carrier that supports new drivers, keep a clean safety record and specialise over time.
