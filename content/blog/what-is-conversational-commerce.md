<p class="lede"><strong>Conversational commerce</strong> is buying and selling through conversations: customers discover products, ask questions, get recommendations, place orders, pay and get support through chat and voice channels such as WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook Messenger, website live chat, SMS and AI assistants, handled by people, chatbots or AI agents. <strong>Conversational marketing</strong> is the marketing side of the same idea: engaging prospects in real-time, two-way conversations instead of static forms and one-way campaigns.</p>

Customers already message friends all day; they increasingly expect to message businesses the same way. In many markets, especially India, Latin America, Southeast Asia and parts of Europe, WhatsApp is a primary channel for shopping and customer service. AI has made it possible to offer instant, personal conversations at scale. This guide explains how conversational commerce works, examples, channels, metrics, tools and how to start.

## The term

The phrase was popularised by Chris Messina (also known for inventing the hashtag) in 2015, describing the convergence of messaging apps and shopping. Since then, messaging APIs, payments inside chat and generative AI have turned it into a mainstream sales channel.

## Conversational commerce vs conversational marketing

| | Conversational marketing | Conversational commerce |
|---|---|---|
| **Goal** | Engage and qualify prospects, generate leads, nurture | Sell, transact and support customers through conversation |
| **Examples** | Website chat asking visitors what they need, booking demos, Instagram DM campaigns | Product recommendations, ordering, payment, order updates, returns in chat |
| **Owner** | Marketing and sales | E-commerce, sales and customer service |

In practice they overlap: a conversation can start as marketing and end as a sale and support.

## How conversational commerce works

1. **Entry point:** a customer clicks a "Chat on WhatsApp" button, replies to an Instagram story, scans a QR code, clicks an ad that opens a chat, or opens the website chat.
2. **Conversation:** a chatbot or AI agent greets them, understands what they want and asks clarifying questions.
3. **Discovery:** it recommends products, shares images, catalogues, prices and availability.
4. **Purchase:** the customer adds items to a cart in chat, gets a checkout link or pays in-chat where supported.
5. **Updates:** order confirmation, shipping and delivery notifications in the same thread.
6. **Support:** returns, exchanges and questions handled in the conversation, with handoff to humans.
7. **Re-engagement:** opt-in messages for restocks, offers and reorders.

| **WhatsApp Business Platform** | Huge reach in many countries, catalogues, rich messages, payments in some markets, high open rates |

| **Voice assistants and phone AI** | Hands-free ordering and support |

## Examples of conversational commerce

- **Fashion retailer:** Instagram ad opens a DM; AI asks about style and budget, shows three options, shares a checkout link, sends delivery updates on WhatsApp.
- **Restaurant:** customers order via WhatsApp from a menu, pay by link, and receive pickup notifications.
- **Clinic or salon:** chatbot answers questions, books appointments and sends reminders (see [How to create an AI chatbot](/blog/how-to-create-an-ai-chatbot/)).
- **B2B SaaS:** website chat qualifies visitors and books demos with the right sales rep (see [How to automate your sales process](/blog/how-to-automate-your-sales-process/)).
- **Grocery and pharmacy:** reorder previous baskets in chat.
- **Real estate:** chat qualifies buyers and schedules viewings.
- **Travel:** booking changes, check-in and boarding info through messaging.

## Conversational marketing tactics

- **Click-to-chat ads** that open WhatsApp or Messenger
- **Comment-to-DM automations** on Instagram ("Comment GUIDE to get it")
- **Website chat** that greets high-intent visitors (pricing page) with tailored questions
- **Lead qualification** by chat instead of long forms
- **Quizzes and product finders** in chat
- **Opt-in broadcasts** for launches and offers (with consent)
- **Abandoned cart recovery** messages

## Benefits

- **Higher conversion:** answering questions at the moment of decision removes friction
- **Faster responses:** AI replies instantly, 24/7
- **Personalisation:** recommendations based on what the customer says and their history
- **Higher engagement:** messaging often sees much higher open and response rates than email
- **Customer insight:** conversations reveal objections, questions and demand
- **Lower support costs:** routine questions handled automatically
- **Retention:** ongoing relationship in a channel customers use daily

## Challenges

- **Consent and spam rules:** WhatsApp and SMS require opt-in; respect message template and frequency rules
- **AI accuracy:** bots must not invent prices, stock or policies; ground them in live data
- **Integration:** catalogue, inventory, orders, CRM and payments need connecting
- **Human handoff** for complex or emotional situations
- **Privacy and payment security**
- **Measuring attribution** across chat and other channels

## Metrics to track

- Conversation volume and response time
- Conversation-to-lead and conversation-to-order conversion rate
- Revenue influenced by conversations
- Average order value in chat
- Automation (containment) rate and handoff rate
- Customer satisfaction (CSAT)
- Opt-in growth and opt-out rate

## The AI shift: agentic commerce

Two big changes are under way:
1. **Businesses deploy AI agents** that hold sales conversations, check inventory, apply policies and process orders autonomously within guardrails.
2. **Consumers use AI assistants** that research products and increasingly complete purchases for them, which requires secure ways for agents to pay (see [How AI agents make secure payments](/blog/how-ai-agents-make-secure-payments/)).

Businesses that structure product data well and offer conversational channels are better prepared for both.

## How to start with conversational commerce

1. **Pick one channel** where your customers already are (often WhatsApp or Instagram, or website chat for B2B).
2. **Choose 2–3 high-value journeys:** product questions, ordering, order status, booking.
3. **Prepare data:** product catalogue, FAQs, policies, stock and order system access.
4. **Set up the channel** officially (WhatsApp Business Platform, Messenger API) and collect opt-ins.
5. **Build the assistant:** AI for understanding and answering, structured flows for checkout and payments, human handoff.
6. **Integrate** with your store, CRM and help desk.
7. **Launch with entry points:** website buttons, QR codes, click-to-chat ads, social bios.
8. **Measure and improve** weekly using conversation reviews and metrics.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is conversational commerce?
Conversational commerce is selling and buying through chat and voice conversations on channels such as WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, website chat and SMS, where customers can ask questions, get recommendations, order, pay and get support.

### What is conversational marketing?
Conversational marketing engages prospects through real-time, two-way conversations, using chatbots, AI and live chat to answer questions, qualify leads and guide buyers instead of relying on static forms.

### What is an example of conversational commerce?
A customer clicks an Instagram ad that opens WhatsApp, an AI assistant asks a few questions, recommends products, sends a payment link, and later delivers order and shipping updates in the same chat.

### Is conversational commerce the same as a chatbot?
No. Chatbots are one tool used in conversational commerce. The strategy also includes human agents, messaging channels, catalogues, payments and integrations.

### Which channels are best for conversational commerce?
It depends on your customers. WhatsApp is dominant in many countries, Instagram and Messenger suit social-led brands, website chat suits B2B and high-consideration purchases, and SMS works for universal notifications.

### Does conversational commerce need AI?
Not strictly, but AI makes it scalable, enabling instant 24/7 answers, recommendations and order handling, while humans focus on complex sales and support.
