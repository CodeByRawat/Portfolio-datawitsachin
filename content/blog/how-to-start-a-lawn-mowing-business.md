<p class="lede">A lawn mowing (grass cutting) business is one of the easiest service businesses to start: you need a <strong>reliable mower, trimmer and blower</strong>, a way to <strong>transport them</strong>, <strong>basic registration and insurance</strong>, clear <strong>pricing</strong>, and <strong>customers close together</strong>. Start with weekends or evenings, build a dense route of recurring clients, then upgrade equipment and hire help as demand grows.</p>

## Starting on a small budget

- Use a good residential mower to begin; upgrade to a commercial mower when you're mowing daily
- Add a string trimmer and leaf blower for a finished look
- Use your existing vehicle with a small trailer if possible
- Keep marketing local and low-cost

## Pricing mowing jobs

Price per cut based on lot size, obstacles, slopes and travel time; offer weekly or biweekly recurring plans and minimum charges (see [How much does lawn care cost?](/blog/how-much-does-lawn-care-cost/)).

## Build route density

Focus on one or two neighbourhoods; customers close together mean less driving and more mowing per hour. Offer neighbour discounts.

## Registration and insurance

Register the business, open a business bank account and get general liability insurance to cover property damage (flying rocks, broken windows).

## Finding customers

Door hangers, Nextdoor, Facebook groups, a Google Business Profile and a simple website with a quote form (see [How to get lawn care customers](/blog/how-to-get-lawn-care-customers/)).

## Scheduling and payments

Use lawn care software or a simple calendar with automatic invoicing and card/auto-pay to avoid chasing payments.

## From side hustle to mowing company

Add services (edging, cleanups, aeration), hire a helper, buy a commercial mower and trailer, and formalise systems (see [How to start a lawn care business](/blog/how-to-start-a-lawn-care-business/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a lawn mowing business?
Get basic equipment, register and insure the business, set prices, find nearby customers and schedule recurring cuts.

### How much should I charge to mow a lawn?
Price by lot size, complexity and travel time, with a minimum charge per visit.

### Can I start a mowing business with no money?
You can start with minimal investment using a residential mower and existing vehicle, then reinvest profits.

### Do I need insurance to mow lawns?
General liability insurance is strongly recommended to cover property damage.

### How do I get mowing customers fast?
Door hangers, Nextdoor, Facebook groups, neighbour referrals and Google Business Profile.

### When should I buy a commercial mower?
When you're mowing enough lawns that speed and durability pay for the upgrade.
