<p class="lede">A table and chair rental business is one of the <strong>easiest party rental businesses to start</strong>. Buy <strong>folding tables and chairs</strong> in popular styles, get a <strong>truck or trailer</strong> and <strong>storage</strong>, set <strong>per-item prices plus delivery fees</strong>, take <strong>online bookings</strong>, and market to <strong>parents, churches, schools and event planners</strong>. You can start with <strong>$3,000 to $10,000</strong>.</p>

## Choose your inventory

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| 6 ft and 8 ft rectangular tables | Most popular |
| 60-inch round tables | Weddings and banquets |
| Cocktail tables | Receptions |
| Kids' tables and chairs | Birthday parties |
| White folding chairs | Most rented |
| Chiavari / cross-back chairs | Weddings, higher price |
| Linens | Add-on income |

Start with enough for common party sizes (for example 10 tables and 80 chairs).

## Startup costs

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| 10 tables + 80 chairs | $2,000 – $3,500 |
| Chair and table carts | $300 – $800 |
| Trailer | $2,000 – $6,000 |
| Linens | $300 – $1,000 |
| Insurance and licences | $500 – $2,000 per year |
| Website and booking | $300 – $2,000 |

## Pricing

Charge per item (for example per table and per chair), add delivery fees by distance and setup fees, and offer packages like "10 tables + 80 chairs". Set a minimum order for delivery.

## Operations

- Count items out and back
- Clean and inspect after every rental
- Store on carts to save space and time
- Charge for damaged or missing items

## Get customers

1. Google Business Profile and reviews
2. A simple website with packages and online booking
3. Churches, schools and community centres
4. Event planners and caterers
5. Facebook groups and local parents

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a table and chair rental business?
Buy folding tables and chairs, get a trailer and storage, set prices and market locally.

### How much does it cost to start table and chair rentals?
Often $3,000 to $10,000.

### How many tables and chairs do I need to start?
Enough for a typical party, such as 10 tables and 80 chairs.

### How do I price chair rentals?
Per chair per event, plus delivery and setup fees.

### Is table and chair rental profitable?
Yes. Items pay for themselves after a few rentals.
