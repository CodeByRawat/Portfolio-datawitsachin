<p class="lede">To start a web design business: <strong>build solid design and build skills</strong> on one or two platforms, <strong>choose a niche</strong> (e.g., dental clinics, restaurants, SaaS startups), <strong>create a portfolio</strong> with real or self-initiated projects, <strong>set up the business legally</strong>, <strong>package and price your services</strong> (including monthly care plans), then <strong>win clients</strong> through referrals, targeted outreach, local networking, content and partnerships. Recurring revenue from maintenance, SEO and automation is what turns freelancing into a stable business.</p>

## Step 1: Skills and tools

- **Design:** layout, typography, colour, UX (see [What does a web designer do?](/blog/what-does-a-web-designer-do/))
- **Building:** Webflow, Framer, WordPress, Shopify, or code (HTML/CSS/JS, React/Next.js)
- **Copywriting basics:** headlines and CTAs
- **SEO and performance basics**
- **Business skills:** sales, communication, project management
- **Bonus services:** automation, chatbots, booking integrations, motion graphics

## Step 2: Choose a niche

Specialising makes marketing easier and lets you charge more.
- **By industry:** dentists, law firms, restaurants, real estate, coaches
- **By platform:** Shopify stores, Webflow sites
- **By outcome:** landing pages for ads, high-speed sites, conversion optimisation

Pick a niche where businesses **value customers highly** (each client worth hundreds or thousands), so a website clearly pays for itself.

## Step 3: Build a portfolio

- Redesign 3 real businesses' sites as concepts
- Build sites for friends, non-profits or local businesses at a discount in exchange for testimonials
- Show **before/after**, your process and results (speed, leads)
- Create a fast, polished website for your own business

## Step 4: Set up the business

- Register a business entity or sole proprietorship as appropriate
- Business bank account and invoicing tool
- Contracts and proposal templates
- Tax registration (consult an accountant)
- Tools: project management, file sharing, password manager

## Step 5: Package and price your services

| Package | Example contents | Example pricing approach |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | 3–5 page site, template-based, basic SEO | Fixed price |
| **Growth** | Custom design, 8–12 pages, copy help, booking/CRM integration | Fixed price |
| **Premium** | Custom design and build, conversion copy, animations, automations | Fixed price or value-based |
| **Care plan** | Hosting, updates, backups, small edits, reports | Monthly retainer |
| **Add-ons** | Landing pages, SEO content, chatbots, motion graphics | Per project |

Typical market rates: [How much does a website cost?](/blog/how-much-does-a-website-cost/) Price on **value and scope**, not hours alone, and always define revisions and exclusions.

## Step 6: Get web design clients

1. **Your network:** tell everyone you know; ask for introductions
2. **Local businesses:** find ones with outdated, slow or missing websites (see [What is this website built with?](/blog/what-is-this-website-built-with/))
3. **Personalised outreach:** short email or video audit showing 2–3 specific improvements, never mass spam
4. **Partnerships:** marketing agencies, SEO consultants, photographers, copywriters, accountants
5. **Content and SEO:** write articles your niche searches for (like this one)
6. **Social proof:** case studies and reviews on Google and LinkedIn
7. **Platforms:** Upwork, Contra, platform expert directories
8. **Referral incentives** for past clients

## Step 7: Proposals and contracts

Include scope, timeline, deliverables, payment milestones (e.g., 50% upfront), revisions, ownership terms and support. (Client-side view: [How to hire a web designer](/blog/how-to-hire-a-web-designer/).)

## Step 8: A repeatable delivery process

Discovery → sitemap → wireframes → design → build → content → testing → launch → training → care plan. Use checklists and templates so every project runs smoothly.

## Step 9: Scale

- **Recurring revenue:** care plans, SEO, content, ads management
- **Productise:** fixed packages for your niche
- **Automate** onboarding, invoicing, reporting (see [How to automate business processes](/blog/how-to-automate-business-processes/))
- **Subcontract** or hire for development, copy or design
- **Raise prices** as demand and results grow
- **Build client relationships** (see [How to build relationships with clients](/blog/how-to-build-relationships-with-clients/))

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a web design business?
Develop design and build skills, choose a niche, build a portfolio, set up the business legally, package and price your services, then win clients through referrals, outreach, partnerships and content.

### How do I get web design clients?
Start with your network, target local businesses with weak websites using personalised outreach, partner with agencies and marketers, publish helpful content and collect reviews.

### How much should I charge for website design?
Many freelancers charge about $1,000–$6,000 for small business sites, more for custom or complex work, plus monthly care plans. Price based on scope and value.

### Do I need a degree to start a web design business?
No. Clients care about your portfolio, results and professionalism more than formal qualifications.

### Is web design a good business in the age of AI?
Yes, if you focus on strategy, conversion, integrations and quality. AI speeds up building, but businesses still need experts who deliver results.

### How can a web design business make recurring revenue?
Offer hosting and maintenance plans, SEO and content, conversion optimisation, ads landing pages and automation services on monthly retainers.
