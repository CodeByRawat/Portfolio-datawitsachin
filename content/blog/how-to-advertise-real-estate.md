<p class="lede">Realtors get the best results advertising where buyers and sellers already look: <strong>Facebook and Instagram</strong> (video tours, lead ads), <strong>Google Search</strong> (high-intent searches like "sell my home [city]"), <strong>YouTube</strong> (neighbourhood and home tour videos), <strong>real estate portals</strong> (Zillow, Realtor.com and regional sites), plus local channels like <strong>direct mail, signs and community sponsorships</strong>. Strong <strong>video and photography</strong>, a clear offer and fast follow-up make the difference.</p>

## Where to advertise real estate

| Channel | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Facebook/Instagram ads** | Listings, open houses, seller leads, branding | Video and carousel; lead forms; Special Ad Category (housing) limits targeting |
| **Google Ads** | Sellers and buyers searching now | "Sell my house fast [city]", "[neighbourhood] homes for sale" (see [Are Google Ads worth it?](/blog/are-google-ads-worth-it/)) |
| **YouTube** | Tours, market updates, personal brand | Long-term trust |
| **Portals** | Buyer leads | Premier agent-style programmes; cost varies by zip code |
| **Google Business Profile** | Local discovery | Free; reviews matter (see [How to edit your Google Business listing](/blog/how-to-edit-my-google-business-listing/)) |
| **Direct mail** | Farming and seller leads | Just listed/just sold postcards |
| **Signs and open houses** | Local visibility | QR codes to listing pages |
| **Local media and sponsorships** | Community brand | Schools, sports teams, events |
| **Email/WhatsApp** | Past clients and leads | Listings, market updates (with consent) |

## Real estate ad ideas

- **Video home tour** (30–60 s vertical) with captions and price
- **"Just listed / just sold"** with result highlights
- **Home valuation offer:** "What's your home worth in today's market?"
- **Neighbourhood spotlight** videos
- **Client testimonials**
- **Market update** in 30 seconds
- **First-time buyer workshop** registration
- **Behind-the-scenes** of the agent's process

(Creative principles: [How to make an advertisement](/blog/how-to-make-an-advertisement/), [How to make a promotional video](/blog/how-to-make-a-promotional-video/).)

## Video tips for property ads

- Start with the **best shot** (view, kitchen, exterior at golden hour)
- **Vertical 9:16** for Reels/Stories
- Smooth gimbal movement or drone footage (follow drone regulations)
- On-screen text: price, beds/baths, location
- Music plus agent voiceover or face-to-camera intro
- End with a clear CTA (book a viewing, call, message)

## Budgets and tracking

Start small per listing or campaign, track **cost per lead and cost per appointment**, and use separate landing pages or forms per campaign. Know your average commission to set a sensible cost-per-closing target (see [What is LTV?](/blog/what-is-ltv/)).

## Fair housing and compliance

Housing ads must not discriminate. On Meta, housing ads fall under a **Special Ad Category** with limited targeting options. Follow fair housing laws, MLS rules and brokerage branding requirements.

## Frequently asked questions

### How should realtors advertise?
With a mix of Facebook/Instagram video ads, Google Search ads, YouTube content, portals, Google Business Profile, direct mail and local sponsorships, supported by fast follow-up.

### Where is the best place to advertise real estate?
Facebook/Instagram and Google are top digital channels; portals provide buyer leads; direct mail and signs work well locally.

### How do I advertise a property for sale?
Use professional photos and video, list on the MLS and portals, run social video ads and open house promotions, and share with your email/WhatsApp list.

### How much should a realtor spend on advertising?
It varies; many set budgets per listing plus a monthly lead-generation budget and track cost per closing.

### Can I target ads by age or zip code for housing?
Housing ads on Meta have restricted targeting under the Special Ad Category; follow platform rules and fair housing laws.

### What makes a good real estate ad?
Great visuals, a clear hook, key property details or a compelling offer, and a simple call to action.
