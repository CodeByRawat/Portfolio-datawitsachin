<p class="lede">To start a home daycare: check your area's <strong>family childcare licensing rules</strong> (child limits, training, inspections), <strong>childproof and set up</strong> your home for care, complete <strong>CPR, first aid and background checks</strong> for everyone in the household, write <strong>policies and parent contracts</strong>, set <strong>tuition and hours</strong>, plan <strong>routines, meals and activities</strong>, get <strong>home daycare insurance</strong>, use simple <strong>software for records and parent updates</strong>, and market through <strong>local parent networks and Google</strong>.</p>

## Steps

1. **Licensing rules:** how many children you can care for, ages, and whether a licence is required (see [How to get a daycare license](/blog/how-to-get-a-daycare-license/))
2. **Home setup:** dedicated play area, safe sleep space, childproofing, fire safety, outdoor area
3. **Training and checks:** CPR, first aid, safe sleep, background checks for adults in the home
4. **Policies:** hours, fees, sick policy, holidays, pickup, discipline, emergencies
5. **Tuition:** daily/weekly rates, deposits, late fees, sibling discounts
6. **Daily routine:** play, learning activities, meals/snacks, naps, outdoor time
7. **Insurance:** home daycare liability coverage (homeowners policies often exclude business use)
8. **Records:** enrolment forms, immunisations, attendance, incidents
9. **Parent communication:** app or daily sheets (see [How to manage daycare communication](/blog/how-to-manage-daycare-communication/))
10. **Marketing:** local parent groups, Google Business Profile, a simple website with photos and tour booking, referrals

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I open my own daycare at home?
Check local licensing rules, childproof your home, complete training and background checks, write policies, set tuition, get insurance and market to local families.

### How many children can I watch at home without a licence?
It depends on your state or country; limits are often very small, so check local regulations.

### Do I need insurance for a home daycare?
Yes; homeowners insurance typically doesn't cover business use, so get home daycare liability coverage.

### How much can you make running a home daycare?
It depends on local rates, number of children and hours; full enrolment and sound policies improve income.

### What supplies do I need for a home daycare?
Age-appropriate toys, cots or cribs, high chairs, safety equipment, first aid kits, art supplies and outdoor play items.

### How do I find families for my home daycare?
Local parent groups, referrals, Google Business Profile, community boards and a simple website with tour booking.
