<p class="lede">To make money from an app, <strong>solve a problem people or businesses will pay for</strong>, choose a <strong>monetization model that fits how users get value</strong> (subscriptions and freemium are the most common today), build a <strong>small MVP</strong> quickly, then focus on <strong>retention and distribution</strong>. Most app revenue comes from <strong>subscriptions and in-app purchases</strong>; ads work mainly for apps with very large audiences. B2B apps that save businesses time or money often earn faster than consumer apps.</p>

## Step 1: Pick an idea that can pay

- **Painful, frequent problems** (saves time or money, earns money, reduces stress)
- **Clear target user** with budget
- **Existing spending:** people already pay for alternatives
- **Distribution advantage:** you can reach users (community, audience, industry)
- **B2B niches:** booking apps for clinics, field service apps, internal tools, client portals

## Step 2: Choose a monetization model

| Model | How it works | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Subscription** | Monthly/annual fee | Ongoing value (fitness, productivity, SaaS) |
| **Freemium** | Free core, paid premium features | Broad apps that convert a % of users |
| **In-app purchases** | Buy items, credits, content | Games, AI credits, content |
| **One-time purchase** | Pay to download | Utilities, niche tools |
| **Ads** | Earn from impressions/clicks | Apps with very high usage |
| **Marketplace/commission** | Fee per transaction | Booking, services, e-commerce apps |
| **B2B licensing** | Businesses pay per seat/location | Operational and industry apps |
| **Sponsorships/partnerships** | Brands sponsor features/content | Niche communities |
| **Lead generation** | Sell qualified leads to businesses (with consent) | Comparison and service apps |

## Step 3: Understand app store fees

Apple and Google take a commission on digital purchases and subscriptions sold in-app (commonly 15–30% depending on programmes, revenue level and subscription duration, and subject to regional rules). Physical goods and services typically use external payment processors like Stripe. Check current policies.

## Step 4: Build an MVP cheaply

- No-code or AI app builders for prototypes (see [How to make an app](/blog/how-to-make-an-app/))
- Cross-platform frameworks to cover iOS and Android
- Ship the **one feature people pay for** first
- Charge early (pre-sales, paid beta) to validate

## Step 5: Get users

- **App store optimisation:** keywords, screenshots, reviews
- **Content and short-form video** showing the app solving the problem
- **Communities and partnerships**
- **Paid ads** once you know LTV (see [What is LTV?](/blog/what-is-ltv/))
- **Referral programmes**
- **A website/landing page** for the app (see [What is a landing page conversion?](/blog/what-is-a-landing-page-conversion/))

## Step 6: Improve retention and revenue

- Onboarding that delivers value in the first session
- Push notifications that are useful, not spammy
- Pricing experiments (annual plans, trials)
- Paywalls placed after users experience value
- Measure retention cohorts, conversion to paid, churn and ARPU

## How much money can an app make?

Most apps earn little; a minority earn significant income. Results depend on problem value, pricing, distribution and retention. B2B apps with tens of paying businesses can outperform consumer apps with thousands of free users.

## Costs to budget for

Development, developer accounts, hosting/backend, AI API costs, design, marketing, support and ongoing updates.

## Frequently asked questions

### How can I create an app and make money?
Solve a problem people pay for, choose a model like subscriptions, freemium or in-app purchases, build an MVP, acquire users efficiently and improve retention.

### What is the best way to make money from an app?
Subscriptions and freemium models generate most app revenue; B2B licensing and marketplace commissions also work well for the right ideas.

### Do free apps make money?
Yes, through ads, in-app purchases, premium upgrades, sponsorships or commissions.

### How much does Apple take from app sales?
Commonly 15–30% of digital sales and subscriptions depending on programmes and duration, subject to current policies and regional rules.

### Is it profitable to make an app?
It can be, but most apps don't earn much. Profitability depends on solving a valuable problem and acquiring users cost-effectively.

### Can I make money from an app with no coding?
Yes. No-code tools can produce sellable apps, especially niche or B2B tools, though scaling may later require developers.
