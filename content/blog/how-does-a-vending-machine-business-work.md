<p class="lede">A vending machine business works by <strong>placing machines in host locations</strong> (often for free or a small commission), <strong>stocking them with products bought at wholesale</strong>, and <strong>collecting cash and card sales</strong>. The owner runs a <strong>restocking route</strong>, maintains machines, pays <strong>sales tax</strong>, and adds locations to grow.</p>

## The business model

1. **Owner buys machines** (new, used or financed)
2. **Location agrees to host** the machine via a placement agreement
3. **Owner stocks** products bought at wholesale
4. **Customers buy** with cash, card or phone
5. **Owner restocks and collects** on a schedule
6. **Location may receive a commission**

## Placement agreements

Define who supplies power, commission rates (if any), service expectations, product types and how either party can end the agreement.

## Restocking and routes

Owners visit machines weekly or as needed. **Telemetry** shows what's selling so you bring the right items and skip unnecessary visits.

## Payments

Modern machines accept **cards and mobile wallets**, which raises sales. Card deposits arrive in your bank; cash is collected on visits.

## Maintenance

Clean machines, fix jams, replace failed coin mechanisms or readers, and keep refrigeration working for drinks.

## Accounting and taxes

Track sales and inventory per machine, pay sales tax as required, and keep records for income tax.

## Growing

Add machines at existing strong locations, win new locations near your route, and hire a route driver when you have enough machines. ## Frequently asked questions

### How does a vending machine business work?
You place machines in locations, stock them, collect sales and restock regularly.

### Do you pay locations for vending machines?
Sometimes; some locations accept free service, others ask for a commission.

### How often do you restock vending machines?
It depends on sales; busy machines may need weekly visits.

### Who pays for electricity?
Usually the location, as part of the placement agreement.

### Do vending machines need to accept cards?
Not required, but card readers usually increase sales.

### How do vending owners track sales?
With telemetry systems and per-machine records.
