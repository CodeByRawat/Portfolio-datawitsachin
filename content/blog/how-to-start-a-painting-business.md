<p class="lede">To start a painting business: choose <strong>residential, commercial or specialty</strong> painting (cabinets, exteriors, epoxy), check <strong>contractor licence</strong> requirements, <strong>register the business</strong> and get <strong>liability insurance</strong>, buy <strong>professional equipment</strong>, learn to <strong>estimate and price jobs</strong> accurately, build a <strong>portfolio and reviews</strong>, and market with <strong>Google, a quote-generating website and referrals</strong>. Startup costs are relatively low, which makes painting one of the easier trades to launch.</p>

## Steps

1. **Niche:** interior/exterior residential, cabinets, commercial repaints, new construction, specialty coatings
2. **Licences:** many states require a contractor or home improvement licence above certain job values; lead-safe (RRP) certification for pre-1978 homes in the US
3. **Register:** LLC, EIN, business bank account, local licence
4. **Insurance:** general liability, workers' comp when hiring, commercial auto
5. **Equipment:** brushes, rollers, sprayer, ladders, drop cloths, masking tools, sanders, vehicle
6. **Estimating:** measure surfaces, estimate labour hours, paint and materials, prep work, overhead and margin
7. **Pricing:** per job with clear scope; offer good/better/best paint options
8. **Portfolio:** before/after photos from early jobs
9. **Marketing:** Google Business Profile, reviews, yard signs, website with quote form, referrals, realtors and property managers
10. **Hiring:** add painters or subcontractors as demand grows

## Startup costs

Low compared with most trades: tools, sprayer, ladders, insurance, registration and marketing (see [How much does it cost to start a painting business?](/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-start-a-painting-business/)).

## Is starting a painting business a good idea?

Yes for many: low startup costs, steady demand and good margins on well-estimated jobs. Challenges are seasonality for exteriors, competition and finding reliable painters (see [How much does a painting company make?](/blog/how-much-does-a-painting-company-make/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a painting business?
Pick a niche, check licences, register and insure, buy equipment, learn estimating, build a portfolio and market locally.

### Do I need a licence to start a painting business?
Many states require a contractor licence above certain job sizes and lead-safe certification for older homes.

### How much does it cost to start a painting business?
Relatively little: mainly tools, a sprayer, ladders, insurance and marketing.

### Is a painting business profitable?
It can be, with accurate estimating, efficient crews and steady leads.

### How do painters get clients?
Google reviews, yard signs, referrals, realtors, property managers and a website with a quote form.

### Can I start a painting business with no experience?
It's better to gain experience first or partner with skilled painters while you handle sales.
