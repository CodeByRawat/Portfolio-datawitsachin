<p class="lede">To increase website traffic organically: make sure Google can <strong>find and index</strong> your site, publish <strong>helpful content targeting what your audience searches</strong>, optimise <strong>titles, headings and internal links</strong>, <strong>refresh and expand</strong> pages that are close to ranking, earn <strong>backlinks and mentions</strong>, and <strong>distribute</strong> content through email, social media, YouTube and communities. Organic traffic grows steadily when you publish consistently and improve what already works.</p>

## First: make your website show up on Google

1. Add your site to **Google Search Console** and verify it
2. Submit your **XML sitemap**
3. Use **URL Inspection** to request indexing of key pages
4. Make sure pages aren't blocked by **robots.txt** or **noindex**
5. Ensure the site is **mobile-friendly, fast and HTTPS**
6. Create a **Google Business Profile** for local businesses (see [How to create a Google Business page](/blog/how-to-create-a-google-business-page/))

## SEO and content strategies

7. **Target low-competition questions** your audience asks (see [How to choose keywords for SEO](/blog/how-to-choose-keywords-for-seo/))
8. **One strong page per topic** that fully answers the search intent
9. **Optimise titles and meta descriptions** for clicks (see [What is a meta description?](/blog/what-is-a-meta-description/))
10. **Improve "striking distance" pages** ranking in positions 8–20 (see [How to get on the first page of Google](/blog/how-to-get-on-the-first-page-of-google/))
11. **Refresh old content:** update facts, add sections, improve structure
12. **Internal links** from high-traffic pages to important pages
13. **Topic clusters:** groups of related articles linking to a pillar page
14. **Structured data** for rich results (FAQ, Product, Review, Event)
15. **Earn backlinks** with linkable assets and PR (see [How to build backlinks](/blog/how-to-build-backlinks/))

## Distribution channels

16. **Email newsletter** sending readers to new content (see [How to write a newsletter](/blog/how-to-write-a-newsletter/))
17. **Social media:** native posts and short videos with links where possible
18. **YouTube:** videos that link to related articles (see [How to make great YouTube videos](/blog/how-to-make-great-youtube-videos/))
19. **Communities:** Reddit, LinkedIn groups, Quora, forums (help genuinely, follow rules)
20. **Partnerships and guest content** with complementary brands

## Technical improvements that increase traffic

- Fix **crawl errors** and broken links
- Improve **Core Web Vitals** and page speed
- Consolidate **duplicate or thin pages**
- Use clean **URL structures** and breadcrumbs
- Ensure **canonical tags** are correct

## How to improve site traffic quickly (paid options)

Organic growth takes time. For faster traffic:
- **Google Ads** for high-intent keywords
- **Social ads** with engaging creative (see [How to advertise your business on Facebook](/blog/how-to-advertise-your-business-on-facebook/))
- **Remarketing** to bring back visitors (see [What is remarketing?](/blog/what-is-remarketing/))
- **Sponsored newsletters and influencers**

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I increase website traffic organically?
Publish helpful content targeting what your audience searches, optimise on-page SEO, improve pages close to page one, build internal links and backlinks, and distribute content via email, social and video.

### How do I make my website show up on Google?
Verify it in Google Search Console, submit a sitemap, request indexing, ensure pages aren't blocked, and create content that matches searches.

### How long does it take to increase organic traffic?
Often three to six months for noticeable growth, depending on competition and publishing consistency.

### What is the fastest way to get website traffic?
Paid ads, email to an existing list and social promotion are fastest; SEO builds sustainable traffic over time.

### Does updating old content increase traffic?
Yes. Refreshing and expanding existing pages that already rank on pages one or two is one of the quickest organic wins.

### Why is my website not getting traffic?
Common reasons include not being indexed, targeting overly competitive keywords, thin content, poor titles, technical issues and lack of promotion.
