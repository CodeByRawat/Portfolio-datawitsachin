<p class="lede">Lawn care cost depends on <strong>lawn size, terrain, frequency, services included and your location</strong>. Companies typically charge <strong>per visit</strong> for mowing, <strong>per hour</strong> for labour-heavy work, or offer <strong>monthly/seasonal plans</strong>. Add-ons such as fertilisation, aeration, overseeding and leaf cleanup are priced separately. Always get a quote based on your property.</p>

## Common pricing methods

| Method | Used for |
|---|---|
| **Per visit** | Recurring mowing, edging and blowing |
| **Per hour** | Cleanups, trimming, irregular work |
| **Per square foot / acre** | Large properties, fertilisation, aeration |
| **Monthly or seasonal plan** | Recurring service with predictable billing |

## What affects lawn care prices

- Lot size and obstacles (beds, trees, playsets)
- Slopes and terrain
- Grass height and condition
- Frequency (weekly costs less per visit than occasional)
- Services included
- Local labour costs and competition
- Travel time

## Typical add-on services

Fertilisation and weed control, aeration, overseeding, dethatching, leaf removal, hedge trimming, mulching and spring/fall cleanups.

## How lawn care businesses should price

Know your cost per hour (labour, fuel, equipment, insurance, overhead), estimate time per property, add profit margin, set a minimum charge and review prices yearly (see [How to price landscaping jobs](/blog/how-to-price-landscaping-jobs/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How much is lawn care?
It depends on lawn size, terrain, frequency, services and location; most companies quote per visit or monthly.

### How much does lawn care cost per hour?
Hourly rates vary by region and service; labour-heavy jobs like cleanups are often billed hourly.

### How much do lawn services cost per month?
Monthly plans depend on visit frequency, lawn size and services included.

### Is weekly mowing cheaper than biweekly?
Per visit, weekly is often cheaper because the grass is easier to cut, though the monthly total is higher.

### What is included in a basic lawn service?
Usually mowing, edging, trimming and blowing clippings off hard surfaces.

### How do lawn care companies set prices?
By calculating costs per hour, estimating time per property, and adding margin and minimum charges.
