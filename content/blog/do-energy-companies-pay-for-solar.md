<p class="lede">Often, yes, but usually as <strong>bill credits</strong> rather than cash. Under <strong>net metering</strong>, your utility credits you for the excess solar electricity you send to the grid, often at or near the <strong>retail rate</strong>. Many areas have moved to <strong>net billing</strong>, where exports are credited at a <strong>lower rate</strong>. A few programs pay <strong>cash</strong> through feed-in tariffs or annual payouts.</p>

Rules depend on your state and utility, and they change. Always check your utility's current program.

## How it works

1. Your panels produce electricity
2. Your home uses what it needs
3. Extra power flows to the grid through a two-way meter
4. The utility credits you for that power
5. At night or on cloudy days, you draw from the grid and use your credits

## Types of programs

| Program | How you're paid |
|---|---|
| Net metering | Credits at or near the retail rate |
| Net billing | Credits at a lower export rate |
| Feed-in tariff | Fixed rate per kWh, sometimes cash |
| Annual true-up | Leftover credits paid out or reset yearly |

## How much do energy companies pay for solar?

It depends on the program: from full retail value under classic net metering to a fraction of it under net billing. Utilities often pay leftover annual credits at a lower "avoided cost" rate.

## Do batteries help?

Under net billing, storing your solar in a battery and using it at night can be worth more than exporting it at a low rate.

## Why it matters for solar companies

Export rates heavily affect customer savings and payback, so solar companies must explain them clearly. More: [How do solar companies work?](/blog/how-do-solar-companies-work/) and [Is the solar energy business profitable?](/blog/is-solar-energy-business-profitable/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do energy companies pay for solar?
Usually through bill credits under net metering or net billing; some pay cash.

### How much do energy companies pay for solar energy?
From retail rate down to a much lower export rate, depending on the program.

### What is net metering?
A billing system that credits you for excess solar power sent to the grid.

### Can I get a check from my utility for solar?
Sometimes for leftover annual credits, usually at a lower rate.

### Do I need batteries to benefit from solar?
No, but batteries can increase savings where export rates are low.
