<p class="lede">To start a small clothing business from home: choose a <strong>low-overhead model</strong> (print-on-demand, reselling/thrift flipping, handmade pieces, boutique wholesale or small private-label runs), register the <strong>business</strong>, set a <strong>budget</strong>, source <strong>products</strong>, shoot <strong>photos at home</strong>, price for profit, sell through an <strong>online store and social media</strong>, and handle <strong>packaging and shipping</strong> from home until you scale.</p>

## Home-friendly clothing business models

| Model | Startup cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Print-on-demand | Very low | Design-led (see [How to start a t-shirt business](/blog/how-to-start-a-t-shirt-business/)) |
| Reselling/thrift flipping | Low | Sourcing skills, unique items |
| Handmade/sewing | Low–medium | Time-intensive, premium pricing |
| Boutique wholesale | Medium | Buy from wholesalers, resell |
| Small private label | Medium | Your branding on existing styles |

## Steps

1. Choose a niche and customer
2. Register the business, get any resale/sales tax permits
3. Budget for inventory, packaging, photography and marketing
4. Source products and order samples
5. Set up a simple home photo area (natural light, plain backdrop, mannequin or model)
6. Price: product + shipping + fees + marketing + profit
7. Launch an online store (see [How to create an online store](/blog/how-to-create-an-online-store/))
8. Sell on Instagram, TikTok, marketplaces and local pop-ups (see [How to sell online](/blog/how-to-sell-online/))
9. Branded packaging and efficient shipping routines
10. Track best-sellers and reinvest

## Starting an online clothing business

Your website is your shop window: great photos, size guides, clear returns, reviews and fast checkout (see [How to start an online clothing business](/blog/how-to-start-an-online-clothing-business/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I start a small clothing business from home?
Choose a low-overhead model, register the business, source products, photograph at home, price for profit and sell online and on social media.

### What is the cheapest way to start a clothing business?
Print-on-demand or reselling require the least upfront investment.

### Do I need a licence to sell clothes from home?
Usually a business registration and possibly a sales tax/resale permit; check local rules.

### How do I take clothing photos at home?
Use natural light, a plain background, steady camera, and show items on a model or mannequin plus detail shots.

### Can I run a fashion business from home long-term?
Yes, many small brands do, especially with outsourced fulfilment as they grow.

### How do I find clothing wholesalers?
Wholesale marketplaces, trade shows and directories; always verify quality with samples.
