<p class="lede">To generate sales leads, combine <strong>inbound methods</strong> that bring interested people to you (a lead-generation website, SEO content, lead magnets, ads, social media) with <strong>outbound and relationship methods</strong> (referrals, partnerships, LinkedIn and targeted outreach), then <strong>capture, qualify and follow up</strong> every lead quickly with a CRM and automation. The cheapest high-quality leads usually come from <strong>referrals, search and helpful content</strong>.</p>

## Inbound lead generation strategies

1. **Build a lead-generation website:** clear offer, service pages, proof, forms, booking and WhatsApp on every page (see [How to create a website for your business](/blog/how-to-create-a-website-for-your-business/))
2. **SEO:** rank for problems and services your buyers search (see [What is SEO?](/blog/what-is-seo/))
3. **Content marketing:** blog posts, videos and guides answering buyer questions (see [What is inbound marketing?](/blog/what-is-inbound-marketing/))
4. **Lead magnets:** checklists, templates, calculators, audits in exchange for email
5. **Landing pages** for each offer and campaign (see [What is a landing page conversion?](/blog/what-is-a-landing-page-conversion/))
6. **Google Ads** on high-intent keywords
7. **Social media ads** (Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok) with lead forms or landing pages
8. **Webinars and workshops**
9. **Google Business Profile** for local leads (see [How to create a Google Business page](/blog/how-to-create-a-google-business-page/))
10. **Chatbots and WhatsApp** capturing and qualifying leads 24/7 (see [How to create an AI chatbot](/blog/how-to-create-an-ai-chatbot/))

## Outbound and relationship strategies

11. **Referral programme** for customers and partners
12. **Strategic partnerships** with complementary businesses
13. **LinkedIn:** helpful posts plus personalised outreach
14. **Targeted cold email and calling** to a defined ICP (see [What is sales prospecting?](/blog/what-is-sales-prospecting/))
15. **Account-based marketing** for high-value B2B accounts (see [What is account-based marketing?](/blog/what-is-account-based-marketing/))
16. **Events, trade shows and networking**
17. **Directories and marketplaces** in your industry
18. **Reactivate old leads and past customers**

## How to get sales leads for free

- Google Business Profile and reviews
- SEO and blogging
- LinkedIn and community posts
- Referrals and partnerships
- Networking groups and local events
- Answering questions in forums and groups (genuinely helpful, not spammy)
- Reactivating your existing contact list

## How to increase inbound leads

- Add **CTAs on every page** and in blog posts
- Offer **multiple contact options** (form, booking, call, WhatsApp, chat)
- **Shorten forms**
- **Improve page speed and mobile UX**
- Add **social proof** near CTAs
- Create **bottom-of-funnel content** (pricing, comparisons, case studies)
- **Retarget** website visitors

## Lead qualification

Not every lead is ready. Score by **fit** (industry, size, location, budget) and **intent** (requested a quote vs downloaded a guide). Use frameworks like BANT, route hot leads to sales immediately and nurture the rest. (More: [What is inbound sales?](/blog/what-is-inbound-sales/))

## How to convert leads into sales

1. **Respond within minutes**
2. **Personalise** using what they told you
3. **Book a call or demo** quickly
4. **Follow up** several times across channels
5. **Send relevant proof** and clear proposals
6. **Nurture** with email for leads not ready yet
7. **Track** in a CRM (see [How to use a CRM](/blog/how-to-use-a-crm/))

Automation makes this consistent (see [How to automate your sales process](/blog/how-to-automate-your-sales-process/)).

## How to measure lead generation

| Metric | Why |
|---|---|
| Leads by source | Where leads come from |
| Cost per lead (CPL) | Efficiency of paid channels |
| Lead-to-customer rate | Lead quality |
| Customer acquisition cost (CAC) | Profitability |
| Speed to lead | Sales responsiveness |
| Pipeline and revenue by source | True ROI |

## Is lead generation sales or marketing?

Both. Marketing typically generates and nurtures leads; sales qualifies, converts and closes. The best results come when they share definitions, goals and a CRM.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I generate sales leads?
Use a lead-generation website, SEO, content, lead magnets, ads and social media to attract interested people, plus referrals, partnerships and targeted outreach, then capture and follow up quickly.

### How can I get sales leads for free?
Optimise your Google Business Profile, publish helpful content, post on LinkedIn, ask for referrals, partner with complementary businesses and network locally.

### How do I get more inbound leads?
Add clear CTAs everywhere, offer several contact options, shorten forms, improve speed and mobile UX, publish bottom-of-funnel content and retarget visitors.

### What is the best lead generation strategy?
It depends on your business; referrals and search typically produce the highest-quality leads, while ads and outreach scale faster.

### How do I qualify leads?
Assess fit and intent, use frameworks like BANT, apply lead scoring in your CRM, and route hot leads to sales immediately.

### How do I convert leads into customers?
Respond fast, personalise, book meetings, follow up consistently, share relevant proof and nurture leads that aren't ready yet.
