Claims are where insurers spend the most manual effort and where customers feel the experience most. **Insurance claims automation** removes the repetitive handling so adjusters spend time on judgement, not data entry.

## The claims workflow, step by step

| Stage | Manual today | Automated |
|---|---|---|
| **Intake (FNOL)** | Phone calls, emails, paper forms retyped | Web/chat/email intake creates a structured claim record |
| **Document handling** | Staff open attachments and key in fields | AI extracts and validates fields from forms, invoices, reports, photos |
| **Coverage & rules check** | Adjuster looks up policy manually | Rules engine checks policy status, limits, exclusions |
| **Triage & routing** | Queue assigned by hand | Claim scored by complexity and routed to the right adjuster |
| **Fraud & anomaly flags** | Spot checks | Pattern checks flag unusual claims for review |
| **Communication** | Status calls and emails | Automatic updates to claimant at each stage |
| **Payment & closure** | Re-entry into finance systems | Approved claims pushed to payment and policy systems |

## Where the value is

Most of the gain comes from three places: **faster first response** (intake and triage), **less rekeying** (document extraction), and **cleaner handoffs** (everything the adjuster needs on one screen). Straight-through processing for simple claims is a bonus, not the starting goal.

## Guardrails that matter in insurance

- **Human review for anything consequential.** Denials, large payouts and suspected fraud need a person.
- **Audit trail.** Every automated action should be logged and explainable.
- **Data protection.** Claims contain personal and medical data; control where it's processed and who can see it.
- **Accuracy tracking.** Measure extraction accuracy on your own documents before you scale.

## How to start

Choose one line of business and one claim type with decent volume, such as motor glass or travel delay. Automate intake and document extraction first, keep adjusters approving everything, then expand. That gives measurable results quickly with low risk.
