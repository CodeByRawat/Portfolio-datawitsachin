Documents are a firm's work product and its biggest confidentiality risk. A solid **document management system (DMS)** keeps them findable and protected.

## What a law firm DMS should provide

- **Matter-centric filing,** so each matter has one home for documents and emails.
- **Version control:** know which draft is current and what changed.
- **Access control:** restrict sensitive matters and apply ethical walls.
- **Search:** full-text and metadata search, including scanned documents via OCR.
- **Audit trail:** who opened, edited or shared what, and when.
- **Secure sharing** with clients and third parties instead of loose email attachments.
- **Retention and disposal** policies applied consistently.

## Securing and tracking legal agreements

Once signed, agreements need to be **managed**, not forgotten. The tools in this space handle:

1. **Storage** with access controls and encryption.
2. **Signature** capture and a clear record of execution.
3. **Key-data extraction:** parties, dates, notice periods, renewal terms, liability caps.
4. **Alerts** for renewals, expiries, and obligations.
5. **Reporting** on exposure across your portfolio.

## How to choose

Check security certifications and hosting location, ask how data can be exported, test search on your own documents, and confirm integration with email and your matter or practice system.
