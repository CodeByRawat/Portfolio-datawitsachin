Privacy Policy – Bhulekh Uttarakhand – Khatauni

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Not a government app. Bhulekh Uttarakhand – Khatauni is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the Government of Uttarakhand, its Revenue Department or the National Informatics Centre (NIC). Land record data is fetched from the public government website https://bhulekh.uk.gov.in/.

Information we collect

None. The app has no account, login or sign-up, and we do not collect your name, phone number, email, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

How searches work

When you search, the details you enter (district, tehsil, village, Khasra/Khata number or the owner name you type) are sent directly from your phone to the government website bhulekh.uk.gov.in to fetch the public record. They are not sent to us or to any other server. The government website's own policies apply to that request.

Data stored on your device

Records you save (⭐), your recent searches and your language choice are stored only on your phone. You can remove a saved or recent item by long-pressing it, or remove everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.

PDF sharing

When you tap “Share PDF”, the PDF is created on your phone and shared only with the app you choose.

Permissions

The app only uses Internet access, to reach bhulekh.uk.gov.in.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
