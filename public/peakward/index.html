Peakward: Trek & Camp

Survive the real Himalaya.

Peakward is a single-player mountain survival game set in real Uttarakhand. The terrain is built from real elevation data at true scale. Walk with trekking poles, light a campfire, row a boat, fly a paraglider, climb ice with two axes and photograph rare wildlife for your agent.

The game is free, works offline, has no ads, no account and no in-app purchases.
