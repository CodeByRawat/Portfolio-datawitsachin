How to play

  • Walk: touch and drag on the left half of the screen. Look: drag on the right half.
  • Run: press RUN. Running spends energy, and it drains faster when you are hungry.
  • GEAR bar: holds the camera, the bow and the paraglider.
  • Warmth, energy, food: eat with EAT, warm up by a campfire, and stay out of deep water.
  • MAP: opens the map of Uttarakhand. Choose a starting place from the title screen.
  • PHONE: shows the coordinates of your current photo assignment.
