Privacy Policy

Peakward: Trek & Camp · Last updated: 28 November 2026

In short: Peakward collects no data about you. It works offline, has no account, no ads and no tracking.

Information we collect

None. The game has no sign-up or login, and we do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos from your library, or device identifiers. The game contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software, and it does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

Your progress (where you are, your warmth, energy and food, the time of day, the gear you wear and the assignments you have finished) and the pictures you take with the in-game camera are saved only inside the game on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the game removes them.

Permissions

The game asks for no permissions. It does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photo library. The “camera” in the game is a part of the game world.

Real-world locations

Places in the game are based on real map data, but the game is entertainment and not a navigation aid. Do not use it to plan a real trek.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
