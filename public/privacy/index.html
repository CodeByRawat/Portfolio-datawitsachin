Privacy notice

This notice covers the enquiry forms on datawithsachin.com. It was last updated on 24 November 2026.

Who is responsible

Sachin Rawat, web developer. Contact: hello@datawithsachin.com.

What is collected, and why

The name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, website and message you type into a form, plus the address of the page you sent it from. It is used to reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote, at your request.

Where it is kept

Enquiries are stored in a Google Sheet, and replies are sent by email or WhatsApp. This means your details may be handled outside the UK by Google, by WhatsApp and by Sachin.

Who sees it

It is not sold or passed to advertisers. The services above process it to store and deliver messages.

How long it is kept

Enquiries that do not become a project are deleted after 12 months, or sooner if you ask.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your details, or for them to be corrected or deleted, by email. If you are in the UK you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
